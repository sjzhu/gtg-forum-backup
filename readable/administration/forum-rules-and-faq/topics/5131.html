At Gen Con 2011, Chicago Comic Con 2011 and PAX 2011, we gave away a Young Legacy promo card (an alternate character card that can be used with the Legacy deck). Unfortunately, that (and future promo cards) are printed in very limited quantities and are for the most part only available at conventions. However, it is possible that we will have a few promo cards available for fans in other ways (contests, promotions, etc), so keep an eye out!
