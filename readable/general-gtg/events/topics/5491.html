A fun time was had by all this afternoon.

We had four people and randomly decided to play against Dawn.

Then a fifth person showed up, so we decided to play against Dawn on Advanced Mode.

Then a sixth person showed up. Rather than split into two groups, we decided to play against Dawn on Advanced Mode, but switch to playing against the Matriarch as soon as Dawn was defeated.

As expected, having the chance to get built up made a huge difference in facing future villains. Even so, we lost two people to Matriarch. It would have been far worse, I fear, if we hadn’t elected to have continue in turn order such that a few heroes got a shot at the Matriarch and destroyed her Mask before she got a turn.

Grease Gun for the win! With about twenty villain cards in play we were able to ignore all the damage done by killing Fowl (both Carrion Fields in play!) and just pummel The Matriarch. Double extra bonus points for Visionary delivering the coup de grace while incapacitated.

Next get together is Sun April 8th at Comic Carnival.
