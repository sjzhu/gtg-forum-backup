I've lived in various parts of the Triangle area in NC for about 5 years.  If you're in Raleigh itself, Game Theory will most likely be your closest local store.  It's got a great game selection and seems to have a pretty active community.  I believe they do an open board game night once a week and frequently do demoes of various games.  Nearby, I also know of Gamer's Armory (my home store) in Cary, which is smaller but has really friendly staff and a pretty good community as well, and Event Horizon in Garner.  I don't know much about the community at Event Horizon, but (at least as of the last time I was there) they do have a pretty nice selection of older RPG books if that's your thing.  I also recommend checking out Atomic Empire, which is in Durham.  They're a really nice comic/game store with a huge selection and beer on tap.

 

If you want to meet up once you get out here and settled in, please feel free to PM me.  My husband and I are always happy to pull more cool people into our gaming group. :)
