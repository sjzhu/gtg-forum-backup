Greetings once again!

In about six weeks, Greater Than Games will be traveling to Australia for PAX Aus (31 Oct-2 Sep, Melbourne, VIC, AUS), and we are still looking for volunteers! In addition, in the first half of 2015, we will be attending two moe shows shows: PAX South (23-25 January, San Antonio, TX, USA) and PAX East (6-8 March, Boston, MA, USA). We are currently looking for volunteers for all three of these conventions. GTG Booth Volunteers spend the majority of their time running demos of our various games. In exchange, we pick up your badge and treat you to dinner and drinks with the other volunteers the evening before the show. If you or anyone you know is interested in volunteering, here's what we offer:

  • You work 50% of the expo hours (which run 10-6 Friday through Sunday for both shows) running GTG games
  • We get you:
    • an exhibitor badge
    • a tshirt
    • dinner and drinks Thursday evening

Send me an email at paul@greaterthangames.com if you are interested, and, as always, feel free to cross-post!
