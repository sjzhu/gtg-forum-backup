Who's Yer Con is over! Long live Who's Yer Con!

 

Thanks for organizing, Steve!

 

I ran six games of Sentinels. Lots of good times. Troy from Arsenal sold everything he brought (admittedly just a few each of EE, RC, and IR).

 

The uptick in recognition of Sentinels continues. Last year Jeff and I talked a few people into playing, but no one had heard of the game. This year there were people trying to sign up, but couldn't because the time slot was full, and some people would come by and say they'd heard of the game and were glad to have a chance to try it. There were still plenty of people, though, who'd never heard it. Everyone who played seems to at least like it and in many cases love it. Per normal, it looked like at least half the people who played the game once would stick around to play it again immediately.

 

At least one person remarked to me that they would have been a lot more excited by the raffle if there was a copy of Sentinels in it.

 

I saw that the people who had won a copy of Sentinels the weekend before at IndyCon were playing it in the open gaming area, too.
