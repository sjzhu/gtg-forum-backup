If you really think IREP loses more from the pick-2 rule than it gains from your own draws gaining that rule, I can rule it differently in future.  But I would still discourage this; in essence, IREP is allowing you to remove the worst of two options, and leaving the other as one of two options, when without IREP the villain would play the best of the original two cards - essentially it turns a 50/50 choice into a 75/25 or a 33/66 or something like that.  So it's a question of how often you think the third card of the deck will be better than either of the other two.  I think that if the IREP "locked" the villain deck, it'd be too much of a "we win" card, and would result in games as pathetic as the Insula/Matriarch one.
