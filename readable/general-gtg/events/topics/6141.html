Are you planning to be in northern California this November? If not, are you looking for an excuse to be in northern California this November?

If so, you’re in luck: Thanks to Christopher’s awesomeness, I’m happy to announce that I will be running demos of Sentinels at Convolution 2012!

Convolution is a brand-spankin’-new sci-fi and fantasy convention here in the Bay Area. I’m trying to help them have as good a first year as possible, and trying to help spread the word about Sentinels, and I figured, why not combine the two into one massive double-decker sandwich of awesome?

Basic Info for Convolution: The con is from Friday November 2nd to Sunday November 4th, at the Hyatt in Burlingame, California. Full price of a badge for the whole weekend is currently $55, not sure what their pricing for day passes will be yet. Further info at their website, http://www.con-volution.com.

I will demo-ing Sentinels in the gaming room on Sunday, noon to three.

I’ll also be at the con the whole weekend, Friday night until whenever I leave Sunday and I’d love to meet up with forum folks and chat or play a game or two of Sentinels, my schedule permitting; I’ll have info on my badge name and where to find me.

Hope to see some folks there!
