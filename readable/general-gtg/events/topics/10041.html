Hanabi's pretty cool - it's a co-operative game where you have to collectively play cards in order from 1 to 5 in each of five colours. You get three "lives" so you can't make many mistakes. The difficulty is in that you are not allowed to see your own cards, and can only communicate with others about their cards with limited information, e.g. "Those three cards are red," or "you have no fours". It's really hard, but interesting.

Having played both Dominion and Ascension a fair bit against AI on my phone, I can say that Dominion has much less theme, but less swinginess since the cards you can buy are in 10 piles of 10 rather than an ever-shifting central row, and there's only one kind of currency. I like both, but I think Dominion is the better game - many people disagree with me though!
