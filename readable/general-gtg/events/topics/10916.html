The best people to ask that question are the players. From my perspective as organizer it was a lot of fun. 

All told, we had 15 players in three games. Games were often coming down to the wire on time so and there were some last minute wins. All three games reached "happy" endings so I guess I need to make it tougher if I do an event like this in the future.

Special thanks go out to Paul Bender for taking time out of a absurdly busy con to help me out with the decks needed to run the event and for providing me with promo cards as participation prizes.
