Odin:

Also, does initialize allow me to draw two and put one on the bottom of my deck?

Yes it does…any normal "draw" effect (except for drawing four at the start of the game) gains the benefit of the de-randomization rule, as does any "play" of villain or environment cards from the top of the deck.  (I'm debating whether automatically playing a card from the top of a hero deck, as with Atlantean Font of Power, Incapacitated Set, or eventually Setback's main power should qualify as well…other similar effects such as Timeshift and DDA, which involve any degree of choice on the hero's part, definitely do not.)  Anything which lets you gain cards in some fashion other than the word "draw" is not affected by this rule.

jffdougan:

I don't recall if there's a ruling about how Wraith's Trust Fund or Tachyon's Research Grant work, but they're the only ones that have that structure here.

You perform each draw individually as a take-two-keep-one, then you perform all required discards as normal.  So in performing Trust Fund on an empty hand, you don't get to see eight cards and reject six of them; instead you bottom one from two, repeat three times, and then discard two of the four you kept.  Research Grant is the same principle: pick one of two, then pick one of two, then put the picks in your hand, then discard any card in your hand.
