So people wanted something that doesn't involve playing a ton of games, so here we go. I am starting this now, becuae it might take a week or two to do everything.

First everyone who wants to participate should sign up here. Whether teams are made randomly or chosen is something we can decide upon. Depending on how many people we get, there will be 3 or 4 teams.

Each team will start out with the full roster of 18 heroes. There will be 4 different matches, one against each difficulty of villain. Each match will use 4 heroes, and we will start with the difficulty 1, and move right up. However, each hero can only be used once. You can decide on which heroes you are using after finding out about the villain and environment, but you can't reuse any heroes.

Depending on how many people are interested, will depend on how many games are played for each match. For example, each team might need to play 3 different games of the 4 heroes against that villain. I will give a few days between each match to give poeple enough time to play the game. The matches can be played live, with Google+, or on the forum, it doesn't matter.

Each win will give the team points equal to the difficulty of the villain. The team with the most points after all the games are played is the winner.
