Okay, today was insane.

First, I forgot to mention we sold out of Rook City Friday morning, and a few hours later we were out of Infernal Relics. When I say sold out, I mean sold out - there are no more copies of these available to >G. (Some local stores and distributors might still have some, of course.) However, they're planning to reprint them, so we should hear more about that sometime in the near future…

Today was all kinds of crazy. Being Saturday, it was costume day, and the SotM fans were no exception! We had some great costumes! If I'm remembering correctly (someone please chime in with anything I missed!) there were Spikes, Wraiths, NightMists, Unities, and one each of Baron Blade, Legacy, Apostate, Argent Adept, The Scholar, and Ra. (Unfortunately, I didn't have my camera - long story - so no pictures from me. Hopefully some others are able to share…)

Lots more fun with folks playing Sentinels Tactics, today playing heroes versus villains - with the villains run by designers Kevin and Luther!

I was very heads-down in the booth, working behind the counter with the exception of half a demo game I facilitated, so I didn't catch a lot of what was going on, but apparently there was a demo of Galactic Strike Force Christopher gave to some game reviewers. Appeared to go pretty well, from what I've heard (third/forth hand).

We've had a great Gen Con! Lots of buzz, lots of love, lots of people, and lots of fun.

Sorry to the folks who couldn't make it. Hopefully you'll be able to make next year and/or do something at a local Con. If you have a local con and there's no official participation, feel free to see about setting up something with the con organizers! A few folks here on the forums have done that, and it's gone really well. (Arenson9 might have started a thread on that, but I'm completely Con-brained right now and need to go coma…)

I just wanted to say, I wandered by a number of times and was pulled into an open seat on demos (knowing folks working the booth who knew I knew the game and liked it helped with that).  Killed Baron Blade so many times on Thurs/Fri.  :-)  Some things in particular I REALLY liked about the demos, and wanted to point out as a "nice" for whomever came up with the nuances for it:

 

1. In the demo games I saw, it was always against Baron (easiest villan), and the person running the demo always played Legacy (great support).  This let the new players try the game with a villan who was neither complicated nor hard, while being able to feel awesome and see the effects of Legacy's boosts, since it's the sort of thing that can feel boring for a new player who doesn't realize how powerful the support is.

 

2.  The Sentinels points gave an incentive for people who already had the game to stop by and play.  Bonus points for each new player in the game, and more bonus points for using stuff from an expansion.  This allowed the demos to display some non-base heroes to help sell the expansions, and also have someone on hand who a) knew the game to help field questions, b) could play a more complicated hero that might be a bit overwhelming for someone new to the game (I swear, had I not shuffled myself I'd think that Unity was stacked to easily get out 3 raptors and stealth bot, smoothest early hand I've had with her for that 4-5 turn Baron kill), and c) have someone at the table who was enthusiastic about the game.  It really helps sell a game people don't know to have more people than just the person paid to be there sitting down having fun with it.

 

3. I swear there was a 3 when I started this post...

Thanks, Huttj! Most of the details were evolved by the >G folks. We used to do "half-games", but realized it was going to be easier to just do a game and let folks see the whole thing, feel the threat of potential lose (occasionally), etc. (If a new-comer is pressed for time, we'll still do that on occasion.)

And playing Legacy can be challenging, as we usually try to make sure we're not stealing the thunder from the other players (although there are different opinions on this). For example, if I have a choice between doing a Flying Smash or a Bolster Allies, I'll almost-always go with Bolster Allies as it will help everyone else be awesome. If I'm trying to accellerate the demo, I'll make acceptions, but generally I don't have Legacy do damage during a demo to allow the other heroes to have the fun of beating BB down.

FYI: The folks running the demos aren't actually paid - we are volunteers! (I try to remember to tell folks that; as you said, it really does make a difference to know we love this game so much we're willing to give up some/all of our con to be there. )

Nothing much special to report about today - more similar things going on. The con went really well!

Definitely volunteer. First, have fun! Being flexible to help out is always good, but also realize that demos are the area of biggest need. Enjoy the game enough that you can do Baron Blade repeatedly, because he really is a great villain to show off many of the mechanics of the game while being easy enough for most newcomers to beat him. Grab people who look interested, because they probably are. Mostly, explain thoroughly and be patient, because the game will sell itself if you set it up well.

First and formost, make sure you enjoy the game. Otherwise it’s going to be a long weekend. Be paitent and delibrate with your demos, and be ready to feild a lot of questions. Don’t be afraid to offer new players advice, but don’t play their turns for them. That energy will quickly transfer to the players who will soon be enjoying the game.
