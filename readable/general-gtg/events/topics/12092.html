=======
Effects:

- Reduce damage dealt to Manifestation cards by 2 (Challenge Mode)

- At the start of the Villain turn if there are 5 or more Manifestations Cards in play Flip Infinitors Villain Character Cards (Infinitor)

- Reduce damage dealt to villain targets by 1 (Infinitor)

- At the end of the villain turn Infinitor deals the Hero Target with the Highest HP 4 Energy damage (Infinitor)

- At the end of the Villain Turn, Play cards from the top of the villain deck until at least 6 Manifestation cards are in play (Infinitor Advanced)

- Whenever Twisted Miscreation would be dealt damage reduce that damage to 1 (Twisted Miscreation A,B and C)

- At the End of the Villain Turn, Twisted Miscreation deals the Hero Target with the Highest HP 5 Energy damage (Twisted Miscreation A,B and C)

- At the End of the Villain Turn, Recalescent Hellion deals the two Hero Targets with the Highest HP 3 Fire damage each (Recalescent Hellion A,B and C)

- At the End of the Villain Turn, Lambent Reaper deals the Hero Target with the Lowest HP 2 Energy damage and 2 Psychic damage (Lambent Reaper A,B and C)

- Sky-Scraper (volunteered) may not play cards. At the start of that heros turn, this card deals that hero then itself 1Psychic and 1 Energy damage (Crushing Cage)

- Whenever this card or any other manifestation card is destroyed reveal the top card of the villain deck. If the revealed card is a Manifestation put it in play. If not, discard it and this card deals each hero target 1 energy damage (Ocular Swarm)
