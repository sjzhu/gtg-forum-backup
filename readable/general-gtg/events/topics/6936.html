Definitely "Are" :)

We intend to continue using Sentinels Points at conventions for the forseeable future. How it works is, you come by our booth and play one of our games. You earn some points just for playing, and more for winning, playing with a new player (someone who has never played before), and playing a more difficult scenario. These points can then be redeemed for promos (mini-expansions, promo cards, even tshirts!
