With the reprint of RC&IR coming, i thought it would be appropriate to have an even around them. What I need is some challenges that use RC or IR. It doesn't matter what the challenge is. It could be just using a hero, beating someone, a full scenerio, it doesn't matter. The only requirement is that it has to have something from either RC or IR.

Just so we don't have one person giving 100 different challenges, each player can give up to 3: one easy, one medium, and one hard. Please list them in this thread, as well as here.

If you want to try the challenges, simply put your name on top of the spreadsheet, and indicate which challenges you have completed.

Becauase different people will be getting the game at different times, this event will go until January 2nd, so you have plenty of time to play everything.

Have fun everyone!
