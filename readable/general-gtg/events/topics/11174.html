Announcing Sentinels of the Multiverse Organized Play! 

 

Games By James: Southdale Center- October 2, 2014 @ 6:00PM 
Games By James: Burnsville Center- October 9, 2014 @ 6:00PM


Event Fee: $5/player


Prize Support: PROMO CARDS!!!

Yes, that's right, we're giving away sets of the Dark Watch promo cards to winning teams. So grab your friends and get ready to defend the multiverse! 
 


Format and Rules...

At least one player for a team must have a copy of The Sentinels of the Multiverse (or the Enhanced Edition). Copies of heroes, decks, environments, or villains will not be provided. 

Players will seat themselves at tables with no more than four players to a team. If you do not have four people, you may be asked to seat someone from another group to fill out your numbers. 

Each player can select a hero of their choice- hero promo cards and proxies of hero promo cards will be considered to be playable if they are accurate. There may be no more than one of any hero at a given table. A player may only use one hero at a time- so no two-handed playing. Players may select heroes from expansion sets. 

Players will be playing two rounds with this group of heroes. The heroes will heal back to full health at the start of each round. 

The TO will roll a die to determine the environment and the villain that they will face each round. Villains and environments will only be drawn from the base set of Sentinels of the Multiverse. 

If your table wins either round, you receive a set of the Dark Watch promo cards! No more than one set will be given out per team. Limit of 10 sets per event.  

See you there, heroes!
