Time for a new depth chart game! This time, we are going to use all the content currently available in the video game (although it doesn't have to be played on the video game). So here is how it works: out of the 20 heroes available, you will pick 15 to create your roster (no variants). You will set it up that you you have a starting team of 5 heroes, and each hero has 2 backups in order. If during a game one of your heroes is incapped, for the next game, you will use the 1st reserve hero.

For example, let's say my starting five is the Freedom Five, and behind Legacy I put Mr. Fixer and NightMist. If Legacy is incapped during a game, then Mr. Fixer, the 1ist reserve hero for him, will take his spot. If you lose all three heroes for a spot, then you keep playing as long as you can field 3 heroes for a game. Once you can't your time is finished.

Each round is pre-determined based on randomly assigning a villain with an environment. No environment repeats until all are used. If you complete all the current villains, I will post more in advanced mode (right now everything is normal).

After each game, please sumbit your results. You do not have to win to move on. If you lose, go on to the next round with the new heroes. Each round should only be played once, unless you use a lifeline (see below). If you lose because of a special environment condition, it is as if all your heroes where incapped.

If you can, please post what happens in the game to this thread. Screenshots are welcomed but not required.

Rewinds are allowed to fixed "honest mistakes." An "honest mistake" is where you meant to do A, but did B instead. For example, destroying Localized Hurricane isntead of playing it, or playing the wrong card. It is not called an "honest mistake" if you meant to play the card, but didn't realize the consequence. For example, you meant to play a damage dealing card but forgot you couldn't deal damage.

In addition, each team will get 3 lifelines, and each lifeline can be used twice:

Mulligans: This allows you to restart a game before your first hero takes his second turn. In other words, if after seeing the first round, you don't like how it is going.

Redoes: After a game completely finishes, you can use a redo, to replay the game and keep the new results.

Skips: This allows you to completely skip a round and play the next one.

It is possible to use mutiple lifelines in the same round.
