We had planned to do a series of game days at several stores in the Indianapolis area this week and early next week. Unfortunately, we are now forced to cancel them - we are very sorry for this.

Currently, we are in the midst of development of both Galactic Strike Force and Sentinels of the Multiverse: Vengeance, and the next two weeks are very important design and creation time. Development and art for Galactic Strike Force is taking us longer than anticipated, which is good as it means the game will look awesome and be an even better gameplay experience, but the direct result is that we must focus fully on that work, at the cost of the scheduled events. We apologize to the game stores, the individuals who had planned on coming out to the events, and especially to Andy for all his hard work setting these up.

I always enjoy the events and fully intend on making it out to Indianapolis later this year, both for Gen Con and hopefully for additional game day events. However, if we do not focus now completely on these upcoming games, we will be hurting ourselves as a company, and you as the people who hope to someday play these games. In order for Greater Than Games to continue to produce the quality games which you have come to expect from us, it is absolutely necessary that we focus on this now so that we can move forward and continue to grow as a company. 

Again, I humbly apologize to everyone involved, and I hope that you can trust us enough to know that this decision was made neither lightly nor happily. 

Please, feel free to contact me with any questions, either by posting here or by e-mailing me at christopher@greaterthangames.com

Thank you for your understanding.
