I love how this game lets you imagine what’s happening in the fight while you are playing.

Last night I had perhaps the coolest game I’ve ever played. (right up there with Blade getting eaten by a T-Rex).

It was Ra, Expat, and Haka up against Plague Rat at Insula Primalis.

Due to certain circumstances, Ra and Expat both got ded, with Haka at 1 HP. Haka had all 3 dominions, Mere, and Ta Moko. the environment was 2 obsidian fields.

After the other 2 died, the fight devolved to Haka and Plague Rat just going at it, whaling on each other with no regard for their own safety. With Ra and Expat both giving Haka an extra power, he got to use Mere 3 times a round, so with that and the 3 dominions he was drawing about 7 cards a round. Whenever he got low on HP he’d just pop a Haka of Restoration, drop about 12 cards, and keep fighting. He’d Ground Pound every now and then, but it was mostly them just hitting each other repeatedly.

I imagine it as Haka going into a Berserker Fury after seeing his friends injured, and completely disregarding anything that wasn’t directly related to making Plague Rat hurt as much as possible. Occasionally he’d get attacked by a T-Rex, but he just shrugged it off and let Ra take care of it.

It was pretty cool.

I LOVE THIS. A game I played just last night against The Organization ended pretty dramatically.

We had Haka, Fanatic, Ra, and Legacy. Legacy had started with Inspiring Presence, so that helped us get a good +2 damage rolling every turn with Galvanize. Then, Haka picks up Savage Mana pretty quickly, and starts swallowing up all the thugs he can get to. Our environment, Atlantis, was surprisingly docile for a few turns, but eventually started messing us up a bit more. We finally get the third underboss in the trash so we can start hitting The Chairman and BOOM Haka unleashes a good 20-24 damage on The Operative, bringing her down to 10 HP. The rest of us just wail on The Chairman a bit more and that’s when our luck was golden.

The next environment turn, it played Pillars of Hercules (a card that lets us double our power, play, and draw phases if we allow the Villain deck to play another card on each Hero turn). I was controlling Legacy and there it was… Take Down. For one glorious turn, the Villain deck couldn’t do a damn thing while we took two turns each (essentially). We smoked the Chairman and let the Kraken eat the Operative. MAN what a fun game that was.
