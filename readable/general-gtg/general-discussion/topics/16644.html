Things are going very well here at GTG! We are making a ton of progress on many different goals, which feels great. I am sure that for many of you, judging our progress is tricky; from an external perspective, it is basically a binary "is this game released yet" question. However, internally, we are clearing things off of our "to do" list at a fantastic rate. Due to the vagaries of the production process, it now looks like we'll be delivering Spirit Island, Exoplanets, AND Lazer Ryderz all at around the same time in late June.

Here is a quick summary of things we accomplished here at GTG in the past month!

  1. We announced a new opening for an Inventory Manager, accepted resumes, interviewed candidates, and hired Matt!
  2. Mara attending the PHD Speed Gaming event in Dallas.
  3. Trevor, our videographer, came to St. Louis for a week to take new product photos and start setting up for our upcoming live stream of the Sentinel Comics RPG! More details coming soon.
  4. We set a Letters Page record of 27,486 downloads in April!
  5. Mike Mullins and Darrell Louder, designers of Bottom of the 9th, were in town for a weekend demo of Bottom of the 9th at local awesome board game bar Pieces.
  6. Tons of playtesting and tweaking of the OblivAeon scenario.

Here is the status of all of our works in progress!

In Stock Since Last Month

  1. Nothing this month.

Shipping to Backers

  1. Nothing this month.

In Transit; On A Train In New Mexico

  1. Viva Java
  2. Viva Java: The Dice Game

In Mass Production

  1. Lazer Ryderz (scheduled shipping 18 May)
  2. Exoplanets (scheduled shipping 15 May)
  3. Spirit Island (scheduled shipping 18 May)
  4. Fate of the Elder Gods (July delivery estimate)
  5. Bottom of the 9th: Clubhouse (July delivery estimate)

At Factory; Pre-Press Work

  1. SotM: Ultimate Collector's Case
  2. SotM: Stuntman, Benchmark, & Void Guard
  3. SotM: Complete Hero Variant Collection
  4. SotM: 5th Anniversary Foil Villain Collection

Current WIP Focus by GTG

  1. Completing OblivAeon
  2. Sentinel Comics: The Role Playing Game starter kit
  3. Prime War
  4. A new and improved website.

I'll see you next month!
