ok... lets assume the breaking into 2 groups isn't an option or desirable.

In the past I was a paid game reviewer (and reviews editor) for the gamingreport.com (before being disbanded by F&W), Scry, Game Trade and others under the pen name “Marc Shayed”. I have also have consulted with several game companies on game design... as thus I have a pretty epic game room with 100s of games on my shelves. I have a 100+ year old restored oak library table (3’ x 8’) to game on and wing back chairs. My players/gamers come to my game nights to interact and be social to play games with their friends, spouses and significant others because I can accommodate a large group and we can all get in on the same games. I would like to be able to have SotM to remain our go-to game, however with 6 players the game tends not to be enjoyable.

We have played Advanced rules... still 6 players makes it too easy

We have tried putting a +1 to all "H"s on top of the advanced rules... villains tend to crush the heroes

We have tried 2 villains... 1 starting the round (before player 1), and 1 villain half way through the round (between players 3 &4)... had mixed results...it’s either a slaughter (villains wrecking the heroes in just a few rounds) or it’s a hard fought slug-fest that still ends up with the villains winning.

We have tried 2 teams taking on 2 villains in 2 environments... where if the heroes beat 1 villain, they can leave that site and join in the other's battle... where BOTH villains have an H of 6...  (this was ok... but you have to keep the turns synced and that just makes the game timing a pain)

What I think would be great is a sheet of villain specific rules for 6 players ... ie... (not actual suggestions)
Gloomweaver : at the end of turn, Gloomweaver pulls 1 zombie out of the trash and puts it back into play
Baron Blade : At the start of the turn Baron Blade flips 2 cards from the villain deck and starts with 2 minions in play
The Dreamer : Whenever a Projection is destroyed, the Dreamer deals each Hero 2 psychic damage

so on and so forth... 

I don't think a 1 rule fits all approach will work... but a downloadable and updateable sheet with a rule for each villain may work well. Thoughts?
