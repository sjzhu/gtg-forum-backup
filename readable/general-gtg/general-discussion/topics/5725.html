A suggestion.

There may be a need for counters to track hard to remember order of played cards.

For instance, people might forget Close Quarters Combat or Imbue Fire was played earlier.

My suggestion is to have 10 chits. 5 of them red on 1 side, yellow at the back. The other 5 blue on 1 side and green at the back. Then for each set of 5 chits, on the same coloured side, they are numbered 1 to 5, while on the back, they are numbered in reverse 5 to 1.

That is, a Red 5 will be opposite a Yellow 1, Red 4 opposite Yellow 2, and Red 3 opposite Yellow 3.

This way, the 10 chits can be used to track up to 4 orders of play up to 2 or 3 card sequences.

Summary,
Chit-Front-Back
#1-Red1-Yellow5
#2-Red2-Yellow4
#3-Red3-Yellow3
#4-Red4-Yellow2
#5-Red5-Yellow1
#6-Blue1-Green5
#7-Blue2-Green4
#8-Blue3-Green3
#9-Blue4-Green2
#10-Blue5-Green1
