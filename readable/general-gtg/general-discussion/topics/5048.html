I’ll say everything I like about each Villian.

Baron Blade - His mechanic where he has fifteen cards in the trash is an automatic loss because he succeeded in pulling the moon to the Earth is awesome. The flip mechanic is incredible, probably my favorite of the flips, since it is used to represent his machine getting destroyed so he hops into his mechanical suit to start lashing out the whoopings. He has a lot of interesting cards in his deck, one of my favorites are his Elemental Redistributor, because it is very annoyig depending on who your playing, especially Tempest if he as Electrical Storm out or even Absolute Zero with Cold Snap, since they will constantly attack each round with elemental damage.

Citizen Dawn - Look a little further back and you’ll see that.

Grand Warlord Voss - The fact that he is essentially invicible when he has at least three minions out makes him a very tough cookie, I mean there aren’t too many heroes that can do more than six damage at once, and not at the beginning either when Voss starts out with his four minions. And if his near invincibility wasn’t bad enough, if on his Conquering Alien Warlord side and there are ten minions out it means an automatic loss. There is also the fact you don’t really want to kill too many minions either, because if he draws Forced Deployment, then there will be some trouble because if there are more than ten inside the trash you’ll have a heck of a time to beat them. On his flip side he has the ability to do manage damage two the highest and lowest HP hero targets.

Omnitron - His flip mechanic is probably my second favorite, since it is him flipping every turn, and thus causing a lot a chaos, by bringing back Drones and Components that are probably something to fear, to bringing new things that you haven’t had the honor of destroying yet. I also like how all his Drones are based on how many drones there are in play can either make them extremely fierce or something to giggle about. The fact he is also able to destroy equipment cards makes a few heroes weaken, and he even destroy ongoing cards while dealing five damage to all. Then there is the card that allows him to be immune to the last type a damage that had hit him, which can be really annoying depending on who is in the battle and the turn order. It’s even worse when you have Ra and had played Imbue Fire, but at least he can use his power turn to destroy it, just hopefully he isn’t the last in turn.
