Personally, I'm looking for villains that provoke interesting choices. Not necessarily just "which of my powers/cards will I use to hurt them". More innate in the villain, such as effects that say I can let effect A happen or I can let effect B happen, where both are Bad Things™ with not-insignificant downstream impacts, and our decision will guide our chance of success. When a villain does that, I'm much more interested and invested in the game, because it's my decisions that are making a difference. (And I'll acknowledge that having interesting targets creates that kind of decision making, as you have to decide where to focus, but we already have lots of those kinds of villains.)

But that's me.  In no way am I saying their bad, just that they're not my preference - and we all have our preferences!
