Even though I was only there for the one day (which was yesterday) it was highly exciting. I spent most of my time (probably only twenty minutes of the time I was there was somewhere else) at the Sentinels of the Multiverse’s table. Got answers to some awesome questions and even a few hints as to what to expect in the next few expansions. So i’m going to be doing some heavy duty research today before I have to prepare for work. I’ll probably start talking about my findings when the next expansion has been announced, so that’ll probably be in a few more months. Also learned that the next game on >G is going to be co-op aswell which will be a nice touch.

Sadly since KantCon is a extremely small convention there wasn’t too many people coming around to play the game, most people where to busy at some other tables role-playing. Guess that isn’t all bad since I wouldn’t of been able to play as many games as I did or probably ask as many questions either. Though the best game I played would of had to of been the one where I played with Christopher’s parents, his younger brother, and Christopher himself. There were even a few people that showed up that had played before which allowed us to take on the villians like Plague Rat and The Chairman, which all ended in victory for the heroes.

Funny thing is is how many people are still buying the core game even after the are pleaded not to since the reprint will be out in about a month. But most of them said they will go ahead and buy the reprint as well they just really wanted to be able to take it home and play it again.

Also! Absolute Zero’s nemesis was showed to me. He is on Tempest’s card Aquatic Correspondence. I’m so positive about it that i’ll take any bets.

Just met Christopher at Kantcon and I can’t thank him enough for the time he spent demoing the game to me and my family… We played a full game and had blast talking with him and his assistant and enjoying some sweet dinosaur sounds For a guy with a hit game and some very impressive Kick Starter Campaigns he could not have been more humble and down to earth - plus he was so enthusiastic it was infectious… If I did not know better I would have sworn this was his first con!
