Okay guys, I'm looking for some simple house-rule ideas to common questions.  Let me reinforce the idea: simple ideas.  Here are some ideas I think work but I'd love to hear other ideas:

1. Making Ambuscade Better:  Double his HP and the amount of cards he plays every turn. 

2. Two Hero Variant game:  Increase both heroes HP by 15, each turn one hero gets an extra power and the other draws an extra card, the following turn they switch.  

3. Playing against multiple villians (other than V5):  Very tough because of the diversity amongst the villians.  I know HP isn't everything, but my best guess is to divide the individual villian's HP by the total number of villians.

 

For the designers who may happen to read this thread, I want to say this isn't meant to be critical as SotM is by far my favorite game.  That's why I called the thread "simple ideas" not "simple solutions."  I don't think the game has problems, but sometimes people may want to just play it a little differently!

Anyone else have some ideas?  Anyone willing to try these (or some other ideas that may be posted) and share thier experience?  I've already tried the two-hero variant and its worked great.  I'd really like to hear some other ideas.
