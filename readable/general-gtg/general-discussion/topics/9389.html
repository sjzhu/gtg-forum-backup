jsz2, Imbued Fire only boosts fire damage, not all damage.  So all of his cold-healing is only at +5, one less than what you've listed for each step.  This means that he'd actually die during the self-damage step of the first Backfist Strike, so you'll have to lose some damage somewhere either for more healing or by using TtE to subtract rather than add.

Also, Scholar would need to have taken damage during the first villain turn to be able to get damage out of MFtE.  The on-healing trigger won't occur if he's already at full health, since his HP won't change.  It's still an impressively large amount of damage, but it's not quite as high as you've listed.
