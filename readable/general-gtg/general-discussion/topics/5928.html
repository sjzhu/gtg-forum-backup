#2

Things are about to change, but as it currently stands:

Ambuscade will be the new 1. Depending on if he gets “combos” off (or not), he can be a chump or moderately dangerous (nothing to worry about).

Baron Blade is getting changes and is more dangerous now. (!!!)

If Apostate is kept as he is, he will be the new 10. He is horribly, horribly strong. A guy I play test with was joking that he’d win vs the Organisation and the Matriarch united against him!

Akash’Bhuta is like an epic boss battle you’d find in a RPG. You have to keep going and going and going, whittling her slowly. And just when you think you have her under control, BOOM! boss changes form and becomes more dangerous, spouting 8 tentacles like some Final Fantasy game! I played 2 games against her today, and it nearly took me 4 hours! (of course, I was taking copious notes, so that’d account for some time) If you have AoE, she’s slightly easier (normal: 6~7ish). If you have no AoE, she’s tough (advanced: 10). (edit: forgot to mention: she depends on the environment slightly more than other villains, so that can change difficulty as well)

Ennead are solid. They’re very flavourful villains and lots of fun to fight. I’d reckon they’re 6~7 normal / 8 advanced.

I’ve not played against Gloomweaver yet.

The Tomb of Anubis environment is very flavourful and ADDS GAMEPLAY TO THE GAME. NEW MECHANICS! Yeah, it will totally change the way you look at environments.

I haven’t ventured into the Planes of Discord yet.

Nightmist is fun to play, never really runs out of options. Nightmist does a little bit of everything. Utility with decks, environments, ongoings; damage, healing; drawing cards, etc. She is a jack of all trades.

Argent Adept is a more nuanced hero and resembles a bard. I think he suffers a bit when he faces villains who destroy equipment and/or ongoings. He has some really great cards (and absolutely rules the environment), but his damage is lacking. Imagine someone like Visionary, except with sustained heals (!) and more complicated cards.

Unity is a botmistress. Spawn bots, more bots and even more bots. She suffers when the villains throw out AoEs or do lots of damage to Lowest HP Hero targets (naturally). But even in the worst of cases, you can see her dealing consistent damage. From the few times I play tested her, I tended to run out of cards to play in my hand (esp. when my bots were being destroyed willy-nilly).

Well, now that I've played it all, here is my review.

 

NightMist: Perhaps my favorite character. Lots of damage redirecting, card management, and jack-of-all-trades-ing. She can destroy environment and ongoings, deal LOTS of damage, heal a lot, can control the villain AND environment (or even hero decks) with the one card that's basically infrared eyepiece, she can draw cards (and have others draw cards as well).
 

Argent Adept: I've had a bit of trouble getting his combos out, but he almost always has something at least marginally helpful to do, and usually better.

 

Unity: OP. She's easy to use, fun to play, and has hilarious cards, but she almost makes the game too easy to be any fun. ALMOST.

 

Ambuscade: Surprisingly tough. The whole bit with him shuffling his deck every turn is kind of annoying. He's fun to play, though. The traps are creative, and his flip mechanic is great. (I was going to do basically the same thing for the villain contest, but obviously had to abort that.)

 

Akash'Bhuta: Probably the easiest of the new ones. I like how it's tied in with the environment deck, and the large amount of hit points make it seem like a long, RPG-like battle, which is great.

 

Apostate: I've only played him once, but he's neat. He mirrors Fanatic perfectly. His flip mechanic is a little confusing at first, but easy enough once you figure it out.

 

Gloomweaver: My favorite, thematically. The pins are a huge nuisance, as well they should be. The cultists turning into zombies is a great touch, and the Power of the Grave card makes them a fearsome sight to behold. Also, I like how he's the only villain with an alternate heroes WIN condition, rather than an automatic heroes lose like Voss or Baron Blade. Granted, taking out the relics is barely easier than taking him out (unless you use Cedistic Dissonant) but the option is nice.

 

Ennead: The hardest of the new set, easily. Especially on advanced. The way they play like an opposing group of heroes is great, and the way the deck dictates what extra actions they take is extremely creative. It must have been hell making them balanced.

 

Tomb: Awesome environment. I found it significantly easier than Rook City. The fact that the cards target ALL non-villains rather than just heroes like RC helps. The treasures are pretty neat and helpful -- temporary powers FTW -- but there are only 2, so I felt they were underused.

 

Plane of Discord: Awesome. I like the creative things they can do (and did do) with an alternate universe with wonky physics. Imbued Vitality is pretty hilarious. WE GOING TO GO FIGHT CRIME?

 

Overall, I give it 7/7 asses.
