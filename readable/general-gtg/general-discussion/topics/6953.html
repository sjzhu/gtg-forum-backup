Hello all,

I'm reaching out to both the designers and the amazing community here for Sentinels of the Multiverse. My girlfriend of over a year and I play the game on a nearly daily basis- it is by far our favorite game out of our growing collection. I have decided that I want to spend the rest of my life with her- she is both the most wonderful woman I have ever met and my best friend. I want to make my proposal special for her and for that I would like to request the help of the Sentinels team! 

I would like to ask from the developers to help me create a card I can insert without her knowledge into the deck of her favorite character (Expatriette) and be on one knee when she draws it. 

My idea for the card was "Engaged in Combat" perhaps with a picture of Expatriette kissing or about to kiss a member of the male cast (or future hero, whatever!). I would like the quote at the bottom of the page to be "Will you Marry me?".

I know this is a great deal to ask- especially with Shattered Timelines coming out-but I would gladly pay any associated price for the art and production etc (I can't find any art that can match Adam's in quality and style). 

From the community I would like to ask:

What do you think the card should do for mechanics?

Who should be her soon-to-be fiance (my favorite character is Absolute Zero- but her lips might get stuck to his faceplate!). Maybe at some point in the future it could be adopted into the plot.. but now I'm just talking crazy.

 

I do hope to hear from you guys at Greater than Games; I've had nothing but good encounters with you in the past and I cannot wait for the new expansions!

 

In any event, everyone have an amazing New Year's and please continue to be the amazing community you are, 

-James Hamilton
