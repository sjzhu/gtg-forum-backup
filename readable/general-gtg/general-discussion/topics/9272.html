I remember playing a MMO similar to SotM in that it had archtypes (and I know there's been many others), or different classifications of heroes. 

 

Here they were in that game:

TANKS:  Damage absorbers.  They could redirect damage to themselves, and while they could punch a little, there was virturally no area-of-effect damage.  Superman without nearly as much offensive potential.

BLASTERS:  Ranged damage dealers, heavy damage and heavy on the area-of-effect damage.  They had glass jaws though, and would go down fast without some protection.  Human torch.

CONTROLLERS:  Crowd control.  They couldn't do much damage and they they weren't tough, but they could keep huge crouds wandering around in a daze or slipping on ice or something.  They could buff and debuff a little.  I'm thinking maybe Dr. Strange, I'm having a hard time thinking of one.

DEFENDERS:  Great for dealing out buffs and debuffs, as a secondary they could blast a little.  Good healers.   

SCRAPPERS:  Tough melee damage dealers with very little AOE.  Not as tough as tanks, not as much damage as blasters.    

 

Anyways, would these five archtypes fit SotM?  If so, where would you put the heroes?  It seems to me that Legacy is a tank; Ra, Fanatic, and Chrono-Ranger would be blasters; Visionary is a controller; Haka is a scrapper.  Am I wrong on these?  Where would you put the others?

I know, probably a noob question, but I'm just trying to better understand the game.

As Foote pointed out, each hero has the capacity to fall across many categories, if not all, to some degree.  What's extra fun about the promo cards is that you can see just how much their role is allowed to shift simply by changing their base power and HP.  Young Legacy for example gives up an incredibly strong support buff and some base HP for the strongest base power in the game.  This can completely change how Legacy plays, going from a buffing support to a primary damage dealer.
