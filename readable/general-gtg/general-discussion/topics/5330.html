I think it will be fun to start listing what the perceived “best” hero assemblies would be. I think it would be fair to even in other threads break this out to villain specific line ups.

For me I find that the best all around hero is Tempest or The Wraith.

Both have crowd control elements, big spot removal, some damage mitigation/healing, and do not start at the top or bottom of the HP tree.

Tempest plus side is the ability to heal the group might put him over Wraith based upon that card alone.

The Wraith can really dish out the damage and gets to eventually set up multiple powers per turn that can help to manipulate the villain deck.

These two also seem to be able to pull it all together with less specific cards needed to be able to be a productive member of the super team.

Other heroes seem to need specific cards to be useful or can pull dead cards that are much more narrow in use.

So, what are your thoughts?

We’ve discussed elsewhere that what really works best is a combination of different types of heroes that can supplement each other, but putting that aside for a moment …

I played a game with The Wraith on a team against Dawn when the infrared eye piece was stuck near the bottom of her deck and Devastating Aurora came out a couple of times. I played another game set on the Mars base where the red dust wiped out her equipment a couple of times. The Wraith, being reliant on her equipment, can take time to be very effective and is more vulnerable than most to losing her abilities. That being said, I generally agree with you that The Wraith is quite versatile and powerful. She’ll make it on to most teams I choose by virtue of the infrared eye piece alone. Most of the rest of what she does is gravy.

I also agree that Tempest is very effective, and in fact agree that he is perhaps the best solo character, though a bit vulnerable to villains with minions that have damage resistance since his base attack does only one damage.

The Hero I always want to have on my team, though, is Legacy. Galvanize, Motivational Charge, Inspiring Presence, and the Legacy Ring – the Holy Quartenary!
