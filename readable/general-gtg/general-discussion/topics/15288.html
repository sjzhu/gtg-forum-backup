The value for original promos is also currently going up, not down. At the last PAX charity auction, Ra went for $200. Young Legacy (Christopher: “We have…like…single digits of these left!”) was more. Like Arcanist said, it’s the collector’s value that drives the price, so I don’t think the new foil/variant packs will cause those numbers to drop. Maybe a few years after OblivAeon ends the Multiverse, interest will wane and the prices will go down. Maybe.

I would say that if it’s between you and your friend, you two alone get to decide how much the promos are worth to each other. The rest of eBay is kind of irrelevant. You each could always consider it from the economist’s marginal cost point of view: Are you willing to trade Seven Wonders for Young Legacy? What about that plus Dominion? What about both of those plus Zooloretto? …you keep considering questions like those until you find your breakpoint.
