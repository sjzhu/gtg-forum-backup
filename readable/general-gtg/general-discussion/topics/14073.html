Out of curiosity, did you pay for FedEx SmartPost or FedEx Home Delivery when you preordered?  There are several people that paid extra for Home Delivery but received SmartPost shipping. https://greaterthangames.com/forum/topic/shipping-fulfillment-issues-8109

Yes, they will. As a delivery driver, that happens all the time. As long as there isn’t another street with the same name but the other qualifier in the zipcode it will plot it to the street that’s closest. And we deliver drivers generally know our way around.
