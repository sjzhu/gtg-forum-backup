Everything is moving along well here at GTG in spite of a few hiccups and annoying developments. Here is a quick summary of things we accomplished in the past month:

  1. We finished fulfilling every outstanding Kickstarter except for OblivAeon!
  2. We got the starter kit for Sentinel Comics: The Role Playing Game to the printer!
  3. We planned out all of the Sentinel Comics RPG line of books and story!
  4. We got Christopher and Adam 2 more 4'x6' whiteboards for a total of 4 (this is related to #3)
  5. We set up the Inventory Management System in the warehouse!
  6. We nearly completed work for the Legends of Sleepy Hollow Kickstarter, coming October 17!
  7. Mara and I attended the Alliance Distribution and GTG Distribution business conferences.
  8. We had a bunch of in-depth meetings and progress on the new website!
  9. We began planning for PAX Unplugged in November.

@Jeysie

We are working on that! It is one part of the massive RPG project.

@MindWanderer

It means that the factory has the bulk of the files and is working on laying them out and getting ready to print. We are still going back and forth on proofs and making sure that everything works / is getting made the way we want. Generally for more complex things (like OblivAeon, the giant box, and Spirit Island, etc.) this process takes a long time, especially the first time they are printed, whereas printing a mini-expansion (even if it is new) is something that we have down pat. Books (for the RPG, etc) are currently time consuming in this phase, but I suspect will become quite quick as we get more experience.
