The big difference between Sentinels and any CCG is that in a CCG you look at the card lists to see what cards work with your deck, and get ideas for your new decks.

 

If a Magic expansion was a box of decks that you did not customize they wouldn't post the cards, they'd post pictures of the deck's theme, and give a description, maybe a card or two to highlight the deck.

 

In SotM they post hero bios.  You get a feel for what the deck will be like, and it builds excitement to play the hero, or you say, "I don't think I'll like that hero, but this one over here sounds fun."

 

A complete card list helps a few situations:  

1.  Someone has a toddler named William, and that Toddler gets into their box and now Parse has 39 cards and they have no clue where that card went, or what card is even missing.

2.  Reference when talking about cards online and you decks are harder to access than an online list.

3.  When you think of something really cool you want to try with Hero A and Hero B and you can't remember the exact text of the cards and you are at work so you can't get out your cards, or even focus because it is driving you crazy.

 

It will not help promote the game.

It will not be a big part of getting new players into the game.

It will be convenient for current players, and those waiting for their game to get to them and are desperate for spoilers.
