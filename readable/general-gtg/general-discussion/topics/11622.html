Thanks for the feedback everyone, whether you like the idea of Sentinel Conflict or not! I expected that there would be a thread like this when we launched the Kickstarter, and I am glad that it has proceeded in a civil manner :) Here are my answers to some of the questions that came up in this thread:

Q) Who is "Story War: Sentinel Conflict" for?

A) It is for:

*Fans of party/story telling games who want to play one with comic book heroes.

*Fans of Sentinel Comics who want to play a story telling game with characters they love.

*People with casual/non-gamer friends who they want to introduce to Sentinel Comics and eventually convince to play SotM or Tactics.

*Parents who want to introduce Sentinel Comics to their young children.

*Fans of Sentinel Comics who really want a bunch of small art prints with new character art.

 

Q) Why is GTG splitting up the story of Sentinel Comics across multiple different games?

A) We love the world of Sentinel Comics, and believe that there are a few different types of game that work very well with the stories of that universe and, if possible, we would like to make those games and through them tell more stories from Sentinel Comics. Each game is going to appeal to a different core audience, though there will be a lot of overlap.

However, we realize that people are also invested in the story and will want to stay up to date with it. That was one of the motivating factors behind setting Tactics at a different point in the Sentinel Comics timeline; the stories of SotM and Tactics are related, but distinct. With Sentinel Conflict, the game does not provide critical advancement to either story, or contain its own story arc. Instead, it acts as an introduction to the characters and world of Sentinel Comics. We don't want anyone to feel like they have to buy it just for the story.

 

Q) Why did GTG choose Story War as the basis for this party/story telling game?

A) We have played a number of party/story telling games with the "subjectivity mechanic" (popular examples including Apples to Apples and Cards Against Humanity), and we believe that Story War is the best of the genre. (On an amusing note, the back of the original Story War box actually has a quote from Max Tempkin, creator of Cards Against Humanity, saying that Story War is better than CAH). In addition, we felt that the characters, locations, and items format translated very well to the Sentinel Comics universe.
