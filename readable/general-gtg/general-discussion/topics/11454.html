I first picked up Sentinels with the Infernal Relics KS.  As a result, I have always had access to at least 15 heroes and I simply don't know the base set heroes as well as I should.  One of the things I love about the SotM Video Game is being (temporarily) limited to the first 10 heroes so I get to learn their decks as well as I should.

I've been using Ameena's Sporcle quizzes to test my knowledge on the names of cards.  Today, I was having trouble remembering the name of Wraith's card that heals you for 3 HP.  I could picture the art and knew I had something to do with suturing.  I finally came up with the name and was thinking about it:

Was it called "Suture Self" as a reference to old RPGs where many spells/powers had different versions for self and other?   Was it a reminder that it only works on Wraith?  After about 20 seconds of thinking about it, I finally realized it's a play on "suit yourself".  I felt so dumb for not realizing this earlier that obviously my first reaction was to come to the boards and make public my secret shame.

Many people here who have met me know that I can be a bit bossy and demanding... sure, we'll go with that. Anyway, if accused of telling others what to do or how to do it, I would often respond by telling a long and involved story of when I went to the doctor to get stitches and how I spent the entire time correcting the doctor's methods until the doctor exasperatedly threw the needle and thread at me, yelling, "Fine! Suit yourself!"

(If you still don't get it, say it out loud.)

At this point, my audience realizes the entire story is a joke and groans at both how much time I spent telling this tall tale, as well as how awful the pun is.
