Your first games will take longer naturally.

Normally it takes about 1 minute a turn for my group.  Others seem to have much slower gameplay.

I'd say it depends on your group and how long they take to make decisions about what to do on their turns.

My Sister, B-i-L and I have run the Baron Blade and Omnitron starting scenarios in ~20 minutes.  The second ones take a bit longer.

 

Once your players are used to the game I think you could do it.  At first I'd run some skirmishes just to get the rules down and the turn time reduced.
