Wow are we busy right now! Here is a quick summary of things we accomplished in the past month:

  1. All of the remaining OblivAeon files were sent to the printer!
  2. Sentinel Comics: The Role Playing Game Starter Kit arrived and was sent out to everyone who pre-ordered, and also distributors!
  3. We attended PAX Unplugged, to great success!
  4. We hired a new Social Media Manager (update about her coming soon - she starts in two weeks)
  5. We launched a new web store!
  6. We successfully funded the Legends of Sleepy Hollow Kickstarter!
  7. We received and shipped out reprints of Spirit Island and Lazer Ryderz! Spirit Island is already sold out again...
  8. We had an in-depth meeting on Prime War, and scheduled several next-step meetings!
  9. We completed layout/design for several sections of the SC:RPG Core Rule Book!
  10. We worked on a pile of additional things; some public (e.g. Letters Page), some private (e.g. playtesting for new Spirit Island expansion), and some secret (e.g. [REDACTED])

Here is the status of all of our works in progress!

In Stock Since November

  1. Spirit Island reprint (which is now out of stock again!)
  2. Lazer Ryderz reprint
  3. Sentinel Comics: The Role Playing Game: Starter Kit

Shipping to Backers

  1. Nothing!

In Transit; On A Boat

  1. Spirit Island reprint (yes, another one)
  2. Spirit Island: Branch & Claw reprint
  3. Various SotM expansion reprints

Completed Mass Production; In China

  1. SotM: Stuntman, Benchmark, & Void Guard
  2. SotM: Complete Hero Variant Collection

In Mass Production

  1. SotM: Ultimate Collector's Case
  2. SotM: 5th Anniversary Foil Villain Collection
  3. Spirit Island: Branch & Claw reprint
  4. SotM: OblivAeon

At Factory; Pre-Press Work

  1. New Bedford reprint
  2. Compounded: Geiger reprint

Current WIP Focus by GTG

  1. Sentinel Comics: The Role Playing Game core rulebook (Christopher, Jenn, Critical Hits)
  2. Prime War (Christopher)
  3. Legend of Sleepy Hollow (Chris Kirkman + Jenn)
  4. A new and improved website (Jenn, Paul, Joel, etc)
  5. A cool Sentinel Comics related thing for 2018

I'll see you next month!

I mentioned in my State of GTG post today that we had a major meeting about Prime War last month. We also have a week of intensive work on the system scheduled for this month.
