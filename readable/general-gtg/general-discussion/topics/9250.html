Okay!  Just played my fourth games and my daughter and I had a great time!  I played Scholar and she played Fanatic (with a support deck, H=3) and we took on Ambuscade on Insula Primis, and it was definitly memorable!  I can't remember the exact play-by-play, but it went something like this:

1. The game started out with us getting our butts kicked by the devices that Ambuscade started with.  Then he kept putting more of them out there and it was NOT looking good.  I was thinking to myself, Crap, maybe I should be playing a third hero here...

2. My daughter figgured out that the immedite threat was not Ambuscade's shield but rather his devices that were attacking us (she's only 7, but I let her figure a lot of stuff on her own and she enjoys it more that way).  So she (Fanatic) started attacking the devices and not the shields.  Oh, and she pulled her relic sword out and the environment was buffing everyone's damage.

3. I (Scholar) started to get something going as well.  The damage that Ambuscade was dealing kept going to the highest hit point hero, which was me at that time (we were going back and forth on HP until I just happened to have more).  Well, I started putting down cards that made it to where I absorbed zero damage.  We felt like we were starting to get the situation under control.

4. My little girl pulls out End of Days and Ambuscade said, "Oh snap."  The board was wiped clean: no shields, no devices, nothing for anyone.  It hurt Ambuscade a lot more than it hurt us. 

5. Ambuscade couldn't really recover and Fanatic was down to 15 points when she pulled Wrathful Retribution (I think that's the name of it).  It took him down 15 hit points, the difference between her current HP and her max HP.  Anyways, at that point we pretty much had him beat.

6. We won! 

It was great because we thought we were toast and then it all came together.
