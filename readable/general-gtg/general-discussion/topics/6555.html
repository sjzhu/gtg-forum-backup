NOTE: This is not an official update or post on the current ST expansion. This post is an idea thought up by UXM266 (me). Please take it as a fun new thing players can try.

 

I just thought of a cool new feature you can do with the heroes, based on the Shattered Timeline expansion set. Take this scenario.

FLAVOR: "The worlds are colliding. Universes are getting mixed up, and no hero is stable in the current timestream. Heroes known for their cheerful demeanor are being swapped for a version known, or their brutal style, in both personality and combat. The only way the members of the Freedom Five can prevent this unstable time loop is if they defeat the foe pitied against them”.

Essentially, consider using both the promo and regular hero card. Every turn you change the hero from the Core Edition Member to the Shattered Timeline Edition. The deck stays the same, but the power will be in constant fluctuation. It’s as if the worlds collided, and only one version can be in “Existence” at once!

Of course this can only be done with the Freedom 6 and Visionary characters…. (as of now OCTOBER 31st).

 

What do all of you think?
