But, if you go back to the website, click on Customize Your Delivery, then login w/ your new account information, it should take you to a page where you can see any pending deliveries, get the tracking number, etc.

At least this worked for me. I can't guarantee it will work for everyone… "

Got my package of Sentinel Tactics awesomeness last Thursday, and haven't really stopped playing it yet.  Getting a lot of skirmishes in, trying out all the characters and getting a solid feel for range probabilities, line of sight, and movement.  The only reason it gets put away at night is so the kitten won't decide it's a playground in the middle of the night and I have to spend half of every morning finding the pieces.

My copy was, thankfully, 100% complete.  The only hang-up was that some of the miniatures didn't quite fit onto their bases (mainly The Wraith, Swift Bot, and a few Citizens), or some wouldn't hold on to the base at all without glue (all the single-peg figures).  Gave me an excuse to pick up a file set and some model glue, which will come in handy in a few months since I Kickstarted an extremely miniatures-heavy game that should be arriving soon.

I considered leaving them off the bases in case I ever get them painted, but reality won out: I have no skill at painting miniatures, I sure as Hell don't want to use these to practice on, and I'll never be able to afford to pay someone to paint them.  They'll just have to look monochromatically awesome, which they do.
