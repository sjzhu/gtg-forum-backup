Just received EE and IE today and began happily transfering all my 1st Ed. card sleeves to the new edition.

However, I must say I was disappointed when I noticed that the EE box couldn't even hold a single card straight in the sleeves  (they must remain at an 20º-30º angle as someone else mentioned). I didn't really expect it to hold all the cards (I use high quality sleeves which are thicker), but I expected to store at least some cards there.

I can understand that it can be hard to accomodate all the possible variations (unsleeved, cheap sleeves, high quality sleeves), but it's a shame because it has been done quite successfully before and I sort of expected that it here too. Thunderstone has pretty much the same system, (the dividers are actually the same size than the ones in sentinels) and everyone (both sleevers and non sleevers) seem quite happy with it.

To check it out, I took my Thunderstone boxes out, and lo an behold, the sentinels cards fit snugly there. The thunderstone box is quite similar except that it is 5 mm wider than the EE box. Who said size doesn't matter?... May I suggest an increase in box width of the same magnitude in future expansions/editions?

Also, I'd advise the guys saying they are using tight "Pro" sleeves (i image you mean "ultra pro" brand sleeves), you're probably using either yu-ghi-oh or custom boardgame size sleeves, which are slightly different from than standard size used by SotM. I'd advise against that, because it can damage your cards. In the short run, there's an increased risk of damaging  your cards while sleeving (by bending and creasing or damaging the card by nicking or tearing the border as in enters the sleeve). In the long run, the top is exposed and naturally damaged by shuffling as if unsleeved and each card may end up bending forward or backwards because of the very tight longitudinal fit.

Any way, I'm obviously happy to have purchased EE (envioronment art rules) and can't wait to finally try IE.

Thanks GtG for all the great work!

Cheers

I did something similar but instead of removing the long sides I removed half of the inner divider. I did this as my sides were fairly secured to the box. I took a razor and carefully cut along the creased fold of the middle divideron both sides so that I could take the entire divered and short sides out of the box. Then I cut the divider in half along the creased fold and the butterfly taped the bottom back into the bottom of the box. I did this because if you don't recenter the divider you end up with one half still too narrow and it will cause the one side to bulge. I resecured the short side back to the sides from the half I put back into the box. With the other half that I cut out, I cut the short sides off and then resecured those back into place. I'll try to take pics of the final product tomorrow and post in the evening.

I have both penny sleeves and the just the right size Ultraprp sleeves and they both fit just fine. Now to get more sleeves and see if there's enough room lengthwise.
