Officially, no. Even if you do, you run into some rule problem. You can only have one of each Limited card on the table, not per characters. So, if you play with three Legacy’s, you could only have 1 Legacy Ring, etc., which can really hinder the deck. However, you can easily add some house rules to make it playable. Have fun!
