Ok, since I'm not one to necro a semi old thread like http://sentinelsofthemultiverse.com/forum/topic/sotm-fonts I was curious if anyone could help me identify the fonts for the expansion Heroes (and Unity)  I've been able to find either the art on Blambot OR I've been able to extract the Title Names from some of the PDF art.

The reason, if you must know, is that I've been trying to design some boxes for sleeved Sentinels Cards.  I also wanted to try to find some fonts for some of the upcoming heroes as well.

So far I've figured out the following from Blambot...

Expatriette is just Armor Piercing with Italics and the X in her name is custom

NightMist is "Vaporized" but with some playing around with the taper and wave settings in the font. As well as outer glow

Mister Fixer is made up of two fonts the first I think is Midnight Snack. The second I thought was Raging Red Locus due to it at least having a lower case set.

Argent Adept's is the worst of the bunch.  While I haven't looked at the script of his name (Virtuoso of the Void) just the whole "The Argent Adept" is killing me. I can tell this isn't a blambot font. I've thought of trying to remove it via Photoshop...however there is just so much going on with the design that...ugh.

Unity is another I can't peg...

And that's before I wait for more art for Omnitron-X, Chrono Ranger, and The Scholar (whose font already is making me scream a little)

I really want to do a really nice job on these.  I've made a few Villian ones, and the Villian ones I've sort of cheated and used the Armor Piercing Font uniformly for each villian - the only thing I've changed are the colors.

 

Oh and props where Props are due - I got the idea from Spiff - he made some for unsleeved cards. I thought...well mine are sleeved! Can they be this good? After making a few I realized how much easier they make setting up and breaking down the game. I'll attache two as an example.

Two things:  I've spent a lot of time either trying to match the card's fonts or explicitly going in a different direction when I thought that would be better.  Towards the beginning (core set, for sure) I think all the fonts were from Blambot's site.  The newer stuff (Argent Adept, Scholar, etc.) isn't from Blambot, but since I've been able to find substitutions that I liked better in some cases, I haven't bothered to ask >G what their new font source is.  If you want to shoot me an email at spiffworld@gmail.com, I can look up the names of the fonts I know of and/or tell you the names of the fonts I've used as substitutes.
