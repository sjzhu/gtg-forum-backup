HikariStarshine:

This's more a question looking for clarification re: the 'don't post custom stuff in the forum' rule than anything else…Is there a similar rule regarding showing custom content to the >G guys at a con, so I'd have to keep anything I came up with on my own limited to other players and any future villain contests that happen?

Not any sort of official response here, but for legal reasons I'm going to bet the answer will be "thanks, but no thanks." One of the big issues is, say you've got some cool custom hero that has some gimmick where you add and remove tokens to a resource pool for various effects. Christopher sees this while working on Vengance. Then Setback come along. You could make the argument that Christopher used your design, and you're entitled to compensation for that. Sure, they can probably prove that they didn't steal it from you, but now there's all these legal hoops that could have just been avoided by not looking at outside submissions in the first place.
