Hello and welcome!

While I'm not aware of a subscription to forum threads exactly, if you go to My Account, under the tab for "My Content" there is a list of threads you've created and you can see the number of replies and, i think, it tags it with 'new' if there are new replies since your last login.

Conversely, when you come to the forums, go to the tab that says 'new and updated' and it shows you a list of recently updated threads and just scan the list for topics you participated in.
