I would like to apologize for missing March. I was gone at PAX East and then GAMA for the first half of the month and am only just now catching up on things. In retropsect, I should have prepped a brief post for while I was gone - I'll try to do that in the future so that I don't run into problems in June and August.

Here is a quick summary of things we accomplished here at GTG in the past two months!

  1. We attended PAX East in Boston and the GAMA trade show in Vegas. Both shows were quite productive, and it was awesome to see the people who came in person to PAX! If you did, you saw a preview of our Gen Con booth setup, complete with super fancy road-case shelves that made several other vendors envious. Working on our booth setup is something that we are all spending a bit of time on on the side these days.
  2. We submitted all of our Gen Con events, arranged for all of our demo and booth space at Gen Con, etc. That takes a huge amount of time, and we're only just now (mostly) done! I am so excited.
  3. We launched, and then cancelled, the Kickstarter for Dubai: Rebuild the Ruins. I really enjoy that game and was especially sad to see it cancelled. However, there were a lot of factors that went into it not funding, we have gotten some great feedback and learned some valuable lessons, and it will join the list of products that we thought were cool but that I'm glad we didn't spend money or time to make.
  4. The Letters Page podcast continues and is going well!
  5. We are deeper into planning all of the launch/preview stuff for the Sentinel Comics RPG. We should hopefully have some things to announce on that next month.
  6. We posted a job opening today for an Inventory Manager! This is a somewhat unusual position, but we have determined that it is critical if we are going to catch up, and stay caught up, with our project backlog.

Here is the status of all of our works in progress! A lot of stuff has moved along in the pipeline.

I'll see you next month!
