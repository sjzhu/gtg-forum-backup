Hi,

I was wondering if it's common amongst the players to read the flavor text outloud when you're playing. When it come to it, Sentinels is basically heavily-based on maths and I think it lights up the game a little when you read the flavor text. I think it reinforce the feeling of actually being the character and it always provides some funny moments. 

When I play with my group of friends, we always read the flavor text outloud and it's fun to see how everyone thinks Legacy should talk or Tempest.

Currently, were even recording ourselves doing the flavor text on the cards so that when this card enters plays we can, for example, run the audio of Legacy saying: «For liberty...for freedom...for justice...for AMERICA». I usually play with the same groupe of 5 people and we developped some affinities with certain heroes. For example, I'm known to be the voice of Legacy and Omnitron-X, while my friend always read for Tempest. 

If we're not the only one to do it, I was wondering if some of you might find interest in recording voices and sharing them inside the community. It would be so much fun to play audio recordings of someone I don't know doing the voice of The Scholar or Unity. 

 

Let me know what you think about it!
