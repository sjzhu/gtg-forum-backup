Hey guys, I'm going to try a 2 hero variant, but I wanted to bounce the idea off of you to see what you thought.  Here's my line of thought:

Okay, so what does a 3rd hero bring when you boil it down to the basics?  Four things:

1: About 25-30 more HP to soak up damage.

2: Another power to use on his turn.

3: Another card to play.

4: Another card to draw.

 

So here's my 2 hero variant idea:

1: Each hero starts with his maximum HP raised by 15.

2: Every round that the heroes play one hero gets to use an extra power, and the other hero gets to draw an extra card.  Then the next round they switch.

3: H=3

It seems real simple and it might work.  I know it doesn't account for getting to play an extra card and it doesn't account for the synergy of having another teammate, but two should be harder than three - right?

Tell me what you think.  

Thanks!

Another way that was suggested if you really want to play only two heroes (I think Paul himself may have suggested this) is to play two extra hero cards flipped over and pick one of their incapped abilities on their "turns".  It's not balanced, but it's better than changing too many numbers/rules that are likely to get lost in the mix.

Overall, play the game how you want.  It's your experience and you have the freedom to toy with it how you wish.  That's the beauty of a coop game; house rules are completely fine as long as everyone agrees to them.  That being said, I have to agree with Envisioner; it's probably better to just play with a third hero or 4 heroes split amongst two people.  That's how the game creators designed the game to be balanced.
