McBehrer:

I wonder how you can do that, though. I mean, you could get 2-turn killed with a kraken, and have no say in it. … Unless she was immune to environment damage. Never mind.

It would make sense that the The Dreamer is immune to environment damage, wouldn't it?  After all, she's in a coma, presumably in a hospital bed somewhere, not in the thick of the fighting.  In fiction when something like this occurs, the heroes always learn early on who is responsible for these terrible manifestations, and the tension comes from finding a way to stop the summoner from inflicting horrible damage on the surrounding populace without hurting the summoner him- or herself.

Here's an ugly thought: What if the strain of her powers damages her, the way The Visionary burned out a part of her brain when she hurled herself back through time?  Mechanically, some of her projections or ongoings attack The Dreamer herself, rather than the heroes, but forcing the heroes to lose nonetheless when The Dreamer runs out of HP.

-CSR
