In regards to Wrath of the Cosmos being sold at a few FLGS, here's what happened. We received a container with the expansion (but no mini expansions or promo cards) and immediately sent off a small amount to some of our retail partners, in anticipation of being able to send out the expansion along with mini expansions and promo cards within a day or two of doing so. Unfortunately, it was not until after doing this that we learned that it would be weeks rather than days. Our options became to either immediately package and mail out 5000 copies of the expansion and then do that again when the rest of the product arrived, thereby doubling our time and money spent, or to get in contact with the retailers and ask them to hold the product that we have sent them until all of our preorder customers have received theirs. Yesterday, we contacted each of the stores we'd shipped Wrath of the Cosmos to and asked them to pull the expansion from their shelves and hold on to them until we sent them the mini-expansions and promos. That way, they will be able to put them back on their shelves at the same time that you'll be getting your copies.

All of the new products are currently in transit to our warehouse, and we expect a mid-December delivery, baring delays due to weather or other issues.
