To put an entire reply into one word: Ok.

Besides expansions for Sentinels of the Multiverse, we are working on various other Top Secret projects, and as long as we are able to make more games, there is a non-zero chance that at least one of them will be in this “Multiverse”. The only limiting factor, really, is money. As soon as we get enough money from sales of Sentinels of the Multiverse, we will be printing the first expansion. It’s already fully written, and Adam’s well under way with the art. It needs more playtesting, but we could do that in record time, were we near the print point. Sadly, we are not, yet. So, if you’re eager for more Sentinels products, or even to see more out of Greater Than Games, the most important thing is to support us. Obviously, telling you guys here to go buy the game is silly, but I wanted to at least let you know why you don’t already have more >G content. We’d love to get it out there, and will do so as soon as we have enough funds to go to the printer again.

Thanks!
