Just wanted to toss an idea out there and see what responses I get:

I was thinking of doing something that would require people at different tables. This would of course require enough players, which I do not think will be a problem. And it would mean enough decks which considering the amounts of heroes/villains/environments we currently have(in addition to the playtest materials I have ;D).

  1. I was thinking we start with all players in one room. Each player picks their Hero via whatever system we decide on(random, choose, draw 2 keep 1, etc)
  2. From here the organizer(s) announce news reports of various bad deeds going on. Something like:

[list]
[]Plague Rat has been seen in the jungles of Insula Primalis[/]
[]Energy readings have detected that an enormous amount of power is being drawn to the Pike Industrial Complex. Something that could power a Terralunar Implosion Beam[/]
[]Etc[/][/list]
3. From there the heroes break up into whatever groups they choose and the are directed to the appropriate table. From here the organizers will inform them that there has been some type of communications breakdown and that they may only contact the other tables once a turn in a note with 10 words or less.
4. At the end of each environment turn players may send heroes to other tables. Any player that moves takes all their cards with them and and they retain their current position(their hand stays the same, discard, cards in play, HP remains, etc) and they will be seated to go last in the hero turn.
5. Obviously the goal is to take out all the villains.

It will be important to have all Villain turns start at the same time. This way all messages and switches of heroes take place at the same time.

I know this might seem like rambling, but I think it could be fun. Ideas? Suggestions? Questions? Obvious things I’m missing?
