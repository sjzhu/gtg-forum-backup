As you may have already seen, we made a big announcement today about a merger between Greater Than Games and Dice Hate Me Games. We are incredibly excited about this development, and wanted to post here so that we could talk about the merger in more detail with you, our most dedicated fans. Here are the notable effects of the merger:

  1. Christopher will remain the “Game Design Director” of Greater Than Games.

  2. Chris Kirkman, founder of Dice Hate Me games, is now the “Game Development Director” of Greater Than Games. He will help spot new awesome games to publish, help refine games prior to publication, and help out with game marketing and promotion (among all sorts of other things). He is also on the forums now as an admin with the name “dicehateme”!

  3. Darrell Louder, former Marketing & Logistics Coordinator for Dice Hate Me Games, is now a Logistics Consultant for GTG and an Admin on the forum (user name Darrell)! In addition to helping with behind the scenes stuff, he’ll also be taking point on answering questions on the DHM section of the forum. He is a cool guy who is also the designer of the DHM game “Compounded” (which is super fun and will be back in print in a few months), and “Bottom of the Ninth”, which will be on Kickstarter on 1 March!

  4. Development of Sentinel Comics games (SotM, Tactics, future cool stuff) will remain constant or speed up slightly due to improved cashflow and better division of labor.

  5. Development of new Dice Hate Me games will accelerate due to the dramatically streamlined logistics of GTG (having our own warehouse, relationships with various printers, etc).

  6. Development of new thematic scifi/fantasy games will be streamlined and gain focus now that we have the Fabled Nexus imprint. Spirit Island will likely be the first new game released under this imprint.

  7. Our engagement with the board game community will increase now that we have both Craig and Chris Kirkman to focus on that aspect of our company.

Let me know if you have any thoughts or questions!

I'm answering so many questions! Here we go!

What does this mean for your presence at Cons? Will you have a booth twice as big or two seperate booths? Will there be cons where it's only DHM peoples or will there be representatives from "both" companies at them?

At most conventions, we will have a large combined booth space. It will differ from show to show, though. For instance, at PAXs, GTG will have similar space as in previous shows, but with DHMG stuff mixed in. At Origins, DHMG will have a booth, and Craig and I will be there to help out and do other things, but it'll be mostly a DHMG booth. At Gen Con this year, it'll be a huge crazy booth with both companies doing lots of stuff. But there should always be representatives from GTG who can answer questions concerning each imprint.

So will the company overall be known as Greater Than Games or will there be a new name for the merged company?

Greater Than Games is the name of the umbrella company that publishes Sentinel Comics games, Dice Hate Me games, and Fabled Nexus games. The games you've come to think of as Greater Than Games products still are, they're just under the Sentinel Comics imprint. (And, in fact, they have had the Sentinel Comic logo on them since the start!)

You list Darrell louder as a consultant.  Does that mean he is only staying on board temporarily?

Darrell is a consultant, in that he has another job that is his "primary employment" and all that. But he is definitely not just a temporary part of the company. We hope to expand his role in coming years!

Will this change the labels on the game boxes for the who published it?

Sentinel Tactics has the Sentinel Comics logo AND the Greater Than Games logo. So, that's already using the logo convention we'll be sticking with going forward.

What does this mean for playtesters? Are we about to get overworked? 

Yes. Ok, no, not really. I'm going to keep throwing everything I design at you guys. I love you all. Chris Kirkman and I will talk about whether he wants to have you all feast on DHMG designs as he's working on them, but you will at the very least be working on everything I am. And probably more!

If only this had happened before I preordered!

As phantaskippy mentioned, if there's anything that's now in the GTG Store that you want to add to your order, just e-mail Craig at Craig@greaterthangames.com and he'll help you make that happen!

Whew! Questions: answered!
