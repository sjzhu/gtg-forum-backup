#140

These are just what printing companies call “digital proofs” (even though, as you can plainly see, they are not digital at all but are actual physical pieces of paper). What they mean though by “digital” is that they are printed out using a digital printing process (like a normal computer printer) just so that we can make sure all of the colors look good, and that they figured out how many of each card to print, and what card goes with what card back, etc. When they do the actual printing, it will probably be laid out differently, and it will be printed in even higher quality by fancy professional printers that are evidently not digital at all (in the somewhat strange parlance of printing companies).

However, in summary, there still is some extra space, which this time we are mostly using to print the Wraith promo card!
