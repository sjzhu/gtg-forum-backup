This update will be really short since my last was so recent! Also, this is the slowest time of year for a game publisher - it is the Chinese New Year, so no production is happening, and sales are slow after the holiday rush. In the past few weeks, we:

  1. Launched the Kickstarter for the Sentinel Comics RPG!
  2. I wrote a long post about OblivAeon.
  3. We commissioned a ton of art for Sentinel Comics: The Roleplaying Game and Spirit Island: Jagged Earth!
  4. We wrote and edited a ton of RPG stuff.
  5. We got Homebrewers and Legends of Sleepy Hollow *incredibly* close to complete. Printing should start once the CNY ends and the factories reopen.

Here is the status of all of our works in progress.

In Stock Since mid-January

  • Nothing

Shipping to Backers

  • Nothing

  • Bottom of the 9th reprint
  • Villains of the Multiverse reprint
  • Spirit Island (7th printing)
  • Spirit Island: Branch & Claw reprint
  • Spirit Island: Promo Spirit Pack 1 reprint

At Factory; Pre-Press Work

  • Homebrewers
  • Microbrewers

Current WIP Focus by GTG

  • Sentinel Comics: The Role Playing Game core rulebook (Christopher, Jenn, Critical Hits)
  • Prime War (Christopher, Chris Burton, Lore Forge)
  • Spirit Island: The Jagged Earth (Paul, Christopher, Jenn, Erik)
  • Sentinels of Freedom video game (Christopher, Adam, Underbite Studio)
  • The History of Sentinel Comics (Christopher, Adam)
  • Homebrewers (Chris Kirkman, Chris Burton, SaRae)
  • Medium (Jenn, Chris Burton, Christopher)
  • Untitled Cheapass Games Project (Jenn, Chris Burton, James Ernest)

  • Fate of the Elder Gods: Agents of Arkham (Chris Kirkman)
  • Some other games (Chris Kirkman, Chris Burton)
  • A new and improved website (Jenn, Paul, Alex, Joel)

I'll see you next month!

Lore Forge will be handling the lion's share of development news until Prime War becomes our main spotlight focus. The reason for that is that, if GTG starts posting updates for a Sentinel Comics property, it will be Christopher posting those updates. Right now, development is wrapping up on the core rulebook for the RPG while we run the Kickstarter, and Christopher's "post excitedly about SC stuff" time is taken up by those. Once the core rulebook development wraps up, and especially once it is sent out to backers, Prime War will be at the absolute top of his list of projects, and you'll also start getting Christopher updates as well as Lore Forge updates. Right now, our "realistic if everything goes well" Prime War release date is Gen Con 2020.
