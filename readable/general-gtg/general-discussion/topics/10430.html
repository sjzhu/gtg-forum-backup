But also relies on the environment being one that will be able to kill the villain, unless of course you have Demoralization as The Visionary.

With Plague Rat, he does have Bestial Vitality, which will heal him by 3 and gives him DR1. He also has Sewer Fiend, which will straight out make him unable to be harmed by the environment. Meaning, it could be a never ending game!

Baron Blade, only having optional destruction will be hard for this method as well. Since his front side has the time limit, odds are the environment won't kill him in time. If the heroes are able to get him to flip, he also has 2 Living Force Fields, along with Flesh Repair Nanites. Giving him a DR2 and the occasional healing.

Grand Warlord Voss only has 1 ship the can destroy ongoings, so it is possible to defeat him. But heavily dependant on the Environment, The Block and Ruins of Atlantis are ones that can make easy work of Voss' minions.

GloomWeaver, is king of a toss up. He can get a DR2 thanks to 2 Profane Zealots. Their low HP can result in environment targets that target the lowest for a lot of damage (Kraken) will have a good shot. The Pouch of Bones can provide some healing when a Zombie enters play.

Ambuscade, he can also get the DR2 with Reactive Plating, but each of those devices only have DR1. So killing the lowest targets are key in this game, but Ambuscade has a lot of targets with lower HP, making it difficult. Also Quick Stim Patch can provide him some healing. Charged Attacks could speed up dealing him damage, but it may also kill Environment targets, giving him a chance to heal up thanks to the break in damage.
