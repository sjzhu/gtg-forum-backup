Two more great events yesterday. We weren’t sure we’d have anyone at all at Downtown Comics in the middle of the day, but we had some regulars drop by AND a handful of new players. It was great. Then, at Arsenal, things were really hopping – three tables playing simultaneously with more people waiting for their turn. Also, for those of you who don’t follow Christopher’s Tweets (@SentinelsOfTheMultiverse), we had both myself (The Deputy) and one of the Enforcers, so Chris shared a pic of us holding our cards.

Normally for game days and demos we’ll play abbreviated games using the simpler-to-play heroes, but one of the games we played last night was five players versus a fully HP’d Spite. Took a loooong time, but was an epic win: Four of five heroes were incapacitated and Mr. Fixer was down to 3 HP when Expatriette finished off Spite with one of her Incapacitated abilities.
