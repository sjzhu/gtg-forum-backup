(I’m assuming a definition as per Meriam-Webster: “awkward or difficult to handle because of many small parts or details”.)

I can see folks thinking there are a lot of things going on, a lot of things to remember, that might or might not trigger. I'd say it's a little fiddly, yeah.

Is it the 17th most fiddly game in the entire BGG database? I have no idea, but I doubt it.

Now, is it one of the games with a lot of comments? Well, the only other game on the "fiddly" list that beat it was Loius XIV, and it was 1330 to 1339, and the next closest is at 675, so I'd say yeah. And I think that makes a big difference.

My biggest issue with these ratings is (as stated) it doesn't differentiate good or bad uses. If someone says "Sentinels isn't as fiddly as X", it counts. That's not useful or even interesting to me, primarily because people can get into an argument about it, which will ratchet up the count as responses quote and contradict the folks saying it is fiddly. On games with fewer comments, those "fiddly" posts probably won't get much response, if any, so there are fewer instances.

I suppose it's an interesting conversation starter, but it's not very scientific.
