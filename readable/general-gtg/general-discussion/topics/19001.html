An on time State of GTG post! Unprecedented in 2019! Things are going very well as we gear up for the summer/fall convention and release season. We've also ironed out a number of internal processes that will really help us going forward. Here's some stuff we did:

  1. Funded and concluded the Kickstarter for the Sentinel Comics RPG!
  2. Seriously, we unlocked so many books! Wow!
  3. Created a production schedule for the next three years; that's right, we know roughly what we'll be working on every month through 2021.
  4. Commissioned writing for all of the unlocked SC:RPG books.
  5. Moved most of the core SC:RPG rulebook chapters into final editing.
  6. Met with the creators of Medium, a very fun party game we'll be publishing this year.
  7. Commissioned a ton of Jagged Earth art and began layout on the bulk of the expansion.
  8. Organized our warehouse and got caught up on freight orders for the first time since May.

Here is the status of all of our works in progress.

I'll see you next month!
