arenson9:

 

BlueHairedMeerkat wrote:
Yeah, I'll add my kingdom to that. Two kingdoms for a games con in London!  
 Perhaps you can organize one.

It has been tried and aborted a few times now. As you can easily get to a game club on pretty much every night of the week in London there hasn't been any drive to create large scale gaming without the attraction of support from publishers, designers, and retailers.The problem is that publishers, even small ones, have a lack of interest in attending as they prefer to focus on the games expo in Brimingham, or to put their investment into Essen and Nuremburg. Large events usually locate to the midlands due to lower costs and a larger draw due to the central location being more convenient for the bulk of the population than coming into London.

There is however a tabletop presence in some larger scale London based events such as Nine Worlds, which looks to be a more general fantasy con, Game Camp which is focused on game design, or Dragonmeet for roleplaying. Nine Worlds is probably the best chance to sow the seeds for a true London games con and it will be very interesting to see how this develops. At the same time there are smaller 'local' events going on like Wallycon, LoBsterCon, the Epsom weekend events, Tringcon, and so on. I wouldn't be surprised if there are many more that I have not yet heard of! I once made an attempt to tie Wallycon together with LoB to create a bigger event but it didn't get very far, largely for the reasons mentioned above.

All I can suggest for gamers who want to see a London based games con is to support Nine Worlds if you can afford it, or if not then to get involved in some of the many weekend and occasional events that are going on. Maybe we can start to network these events together a bit more to grow something bigger. Maybe a Kickstarter project somewhere down the line if the cost of venue hire (which, in London, is truly extortionate) needs to be de-risked by getting people to buy tickets up front.
