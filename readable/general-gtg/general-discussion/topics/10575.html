Yep, that's the idea. Suppose you were in this situation on the table top. You would just ignore the hero targets, not even considering doing them damage, even though you could. Since our screen real estate is limited, we want to make the most of it by focusing on the actions you're most likely to want to do. And if you do want to damage a hero, you still can, by showing all targets and selecting one.

MI
MigrantP
2014-06-25 20:23 UTC
#6

Multiple targets are most likely to be sequential "choose a target" steps, because the available targets can change in between damage dealing. It would also be more difficult to show the calculations/effects for many targets at once.

If you're damaging all targets, or all targets of a certain class (e.g. all non-hero targets), then you're essentially choosing the order in which they will be damaged. In that case, there will be an "I don't care" button (or similar) that just does all the damage in whatever order. Most of the time that's ok, but some times you'll want to carefully choose the order.
