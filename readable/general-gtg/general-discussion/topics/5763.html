I believe they said the Enhanced Edition box was designed to fit everything in the box while sleeved with Mayday Premium sleeves.

There was some concern that everything would fit, but just barely, when sleeved after Ambuscade and Unity are included.

The Mayday Premium sleeves are 90 microns thick. Dragonshield and FFG sleeves are 100 microns thick. The extra 10 microns / card of FFG or Dragonshield would add about 2 inches to the height if all of the cards were stacked, so I don’t think there is any way FFG or Dragonshield sleeved cards would all fit in the Enhanced box.

Mayday Premium may be the way to go for this game if you want heavier sleeves. The 10 micron loss compared to FFG will not seriously impair the ability to protect your cards, but everything (should) fit in the EE box.

Mayday Standard will certainly work fine and fit in the box. However, I have encountered two problems with Mayday Standard sleeves:
1.) Too thin - they do nothing at all to protect the sleeved card from bending or folding
2.) Albedo - Mayday Standard sleeves are so reflective that it is almost impossible to read the sleeved card at any angle other than straight-on. This can be a problem in games where you need to be able to read cards that are not directly in front of you.

Hopefully the Mayday Premium sleeves are more like the FFG/Dragonshield in their reflectivity…
