Inspired by a previous post that discussed epic moments during the game, I thought I would revive it with a new thread. Especially since we will be getting new moments from the Rook City Expansion.

This past weekend my friends and I were playing. We faced Voss in Atlantis with Legacy, Visionary, Absolute Zero, and Ra. The Pillars of Hercules came out and we left it the entire game. Legacy was able to control it a few turns. Then at the end it looked like we were going to lose because of Forced Deployment but The Pillars stopped that. It allowed the last card of the Villain deck be played (another Forced Deployment) before we had to bring back all the minions. This caused the Villain trash to be shuffled and form the Villain deck and so there were no minions to be brought forth. We beat Voss that turn too.

Sorry but I think you are wrong. We had to go through the rule book to find out exactly what happens. This is what we found: “Additionally, if any deck ever runs out of cards, simply shuffle that deck’s trash and put it into play” (p 6 under Gameplay).
