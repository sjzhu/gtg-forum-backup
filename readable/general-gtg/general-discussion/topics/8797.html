Just a quick update:

This thread covers two topics: The "inifinite loop" and the "hey! you're robot!" 

Infinte Loop: This can happen when the title of a forum topic is edited too many times and in the right way. Long story short, if the topic title is A, and you rename it to B, (and C, D, or E) and then back to A, it can create this infinite loop thing. Fortunately the website should catch it, but apparently IE doesn't play too nicely. Andy contacted me this time, but if any of you ever see that message, just drop me a PM and I'll fix it. it's fast. 

Hey! You're a robot!: We often make changes to the site to make it more usable... and stuff. And we're trying out a new captcha system and I'd misconfigured it. Again, I heard pretty quickly and was able to fix it as soon as I heard about it. Definitely an odd error, though. It shouldn't have happened. (whatever "shouldn't" means.)

In any case, for egregious things that are preventing you from posting, I'll usually hear about them sooner or later, but I don't spend a lot of times on the forums (If you do see me in the forums list, then it merely means I am logged into the site, but not actively reading them.)
