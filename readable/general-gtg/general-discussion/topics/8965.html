A while back, I asked people about how frequently they check the GTG website for new news/content, and the general response was "rarely to never". This makes sense, since we've been really infrequent in our content updates and news posts.

Well, as of today, there are several changes that should help mitigate that problem, increasing both our ability to communicate with you awesome people (and a bunch of other people that want to follow along with GTG but not visit this wonderful forum). I made a recent News Post about it on the site (www.greaterthangames.com/news), but here is the summary:

1) Posts to the GTG News blog are now emailed out to our mailing list, and available as an RSS feed.

2) New News Posts and Official Tweets from @GTGamesLLC both now appear on the main site.

3) There is a new Blog, written principally about me, called the GTG Business Blog. It will typically cover general board game industry stuff, and will update every Tuesday and Thursday.
