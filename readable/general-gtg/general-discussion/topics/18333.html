Wow! Gen Con just happened! That is such an incredibly busy and work intensive show. This year we all went *into* the show exhausted after shipping around 4000 OblivAon parcels, but that can't be helped. We also received a ton of other stuff. Here's a look at some of the things we did in the past month:

  • Maggie and Chris Kirkman attended Dice Tower Con.
  • Christopher attended Con in the Castle and Geekly Con.
  • We all went to Gen Con.
  • Jenn and the graphic design department designed and printed a ton of things for Gen Con.
  • Chris Burton, Twuana, and SaRae assembled a ton of prototype games.
  • Jenn, Sarah, SaRae, and Maggie made a ton of decorations for the Gen Con party on Thursday night.
  • Trevor moved to St. Louis and started full time!
  • We finished the first season of Sentinel Comics LIVE on Twitch.
  • Jodie, Matt, and the warehouse team moved 150% of the maximum capacity of our warehouse through our warehouse while it was already 120% full.
  • Matt worked to improve Gen Con organization and setup/teardown.
  • Alex and I standardized our terms for warehousing and fulfillment for other publishers.
  • Craig got a new kilt.

Here is the status of all of our works in progress. There are a ton of changes!
