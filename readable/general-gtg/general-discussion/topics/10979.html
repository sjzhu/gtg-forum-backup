The Sentinels figures are made from the same material as the Reaper Bones, and I've painted the reaper bones (including heavy drybrushing and washes/stains) without having to prime them.

The trick is to wash the figures with soap and water very thoroughly beore you start. The oils from your skin transfer to the figures very well and will disrupt the paint. Let the figures dry over night and you should be fine for painting them without priming.

I have pictures of some Reaper Bones in the process of being painted on my blog, and I'll have some of the Freedom 5 and Omnitrom up later next week after I get more paint on them.
