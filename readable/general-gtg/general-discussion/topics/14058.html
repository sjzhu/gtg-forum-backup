There have been a number of complaints/questions about shipping in various threads on the forum, so I wanted to provide updates/answers to the comments I have seen, as well as provide a single place for people to comment (and me to check) if they have shipping issues. As you might imagine, everyone here is much busier than normal, so this will help to take something off of Craig's plate. In no particular order:

  • Web store orders are now, and have been since mid-December, being processed in the normal way (i.e., they are being printed and shipped in the morning of the next business day after the orders are placed, and the order status is updating in the web store). If you placed a web store order for promo cards (or anything else) in the last month, the shipping status has not updated, and you have not received your order, please send me an email with your order number at paul@greaterthangames.com and let me know what is going on.
  • Web store orders placed during the Holiday Promo Sale prior to mid-December have all shipped, but their status and tracking information has not been and will not be updated. Unfortunately, given the way in which our web store is written, when we receive a large number of orders, we can either process them as efficiently as possible and never update the status, or take three times as long to process them while updating the status. This is something that I really wish were fixed, but we do not have the resources to do so in the short to medium term.  If you placed a web store order for promo cards (or anything else) in the last month, the shipping status has not updated, and you have not received your order, please send me an email with your order number at paul@greaterthangames.com and let me know what is going on.
  • Villains of the Multiverse is now in the process of being shipped. We have everyone working on the ship-out as fast as possible. and my very rough guess right now is that everything will be shipped out in three weeks.
  • For the EU, all of your orders will be shipped out on the same day (probably in approximately 1-2 weeks), and, if past experience is any guide, will take approximately 1 week to arrive. You will definitely not pay any VAT/import/etc fees on delivery.
  • Copies of Villains of the Multiverse will not start heading to North American distributors until after all North American preorders have shipped, and will not start heading to EU distributors until all EU preorders have shipped.
  • Because Battle for Broken City has been delayed longer than expected, people who bundled Villains of the Multiverse with Battle for Broken City will have their Villains of the Multiverse portion of the order shipped once the rest of the Villains of the Multiverse preorder has shipped.

I hope that answers the majority of the questions, but if you have any others on this topic, please post them here!

So i work for fed ex, as a courier. (Shhh)

 

Its not too bad. Its not to great. UPS isn't much better, from what my customers tell me (many of them prefer FedEx). Internationally I can only imagine.

 

When it comes down to it? All of them are the same. DHL (what little it still does), FedEx, UPS, International Air Mail, whatever - they are all surprisingly good, when you consider the small fact that for my terminal alone (one of two in the city) we easily go through 30,000 boxes a day. Thats one terminal, in one city, in the US. FedEx has terminals in every major city in the country, and internationally. Millions upon millions of boxes a day. I deliver 150-250 boxes a day, in what is basically box that is about 25miles on a side. I pick up another 100 or so (more during the month between thanksgiving and christmas!) 

 

When you consider those numbers, 99% successful service rate... still leaves hundreds of thousands of boxes that didn't get delivered properly. The terminals strive for (i think) 99.5 or something. It might even be 99.8 (I don't know, i'm not in that side of the business) and even such things as 'I got to the business and they were closed today, unexpectedly' hurt that number. 

 

So, while it sucks that you didn't get your package ( and I totally feel for you - i really do, even I, in the business of shipping on the personal end, dealing with people who missed their box yesterday - don't like it when my box doesn't show up on friday and I have to wait even till monday. Waiting weeks cause of a customs delay where some S.O.B. forgot to send the paperwork... I'd be british levels of annoyance too!) please do realize that it doesn't really matter what company GtG ships with. They all could have had the same thing happen to them, and even with a 99.9% success rate, thats still hundreds of thousands of boxes that don't get properly delivered a day. 

 

GtG prefers FedEx because of past experiences. Is this experience enough to make them switch services? Maybe. But no matter what service they use, someone somewhere is going to be unhappy. Something is going to go wrong somewhere. Some box is going to get crushed in the conveyor belts and not sent on. Some frieght truck with a couple hundred of them is going to get in an accident and everything catches fire. Some crate is going to get stuck in customs. It sucks, but its the facts.

 

I just wanted to write that out, not in defense of FedEx (Lord knows I have my issues with what little i deal with every day) but in defense of the realities of the shipping industry, especially in this day and age of Amazon Prime, every big site offering free shipping, and huge precentages of everyday shopping moving to online. Take it as you will!
