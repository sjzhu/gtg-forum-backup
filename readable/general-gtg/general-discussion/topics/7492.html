They are not random. Christopher specifically chose those values to create interesting choices while ensuring a specific distritubution of the numbers. For example, you'll notice that no card has a repeat of numbers for that card; you won't see a card that has two instances with the same spell number. This gives you the opportunity to consider which cards you want to cast/discard, if you have more than one in your hand or know there are more to come...
