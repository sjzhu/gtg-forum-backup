Megalopis isn't too painful, but I am not a big fan of it as it stops so many card plays, draws, and power uses. Insula Primalis, Ruins of Atlantis, or The Block would be my recommendations. Possibly Pike Industrial Complex.

Not sure how big your group is, but this is a team I would suggest for five players. Tempest, Ra, Haka, Fanatic, and Wraith fighting in Insula Primalis against Baron Blade. Though if you have less, removing any of those is also fine.

Heroes to avoid for a few games; Absolute Zero, NightMist, Argent Adept, and Omnitron-X.

 

Edit: For a second game, I recommend Voss. Using any hero aside for the four I mentioned to avoid. Environments to not use for it would be Megalopolis, Wagner Mars Base, Rook City, Realm of Discord, Tomb of Anubis, and Time Cataclysm. Wow... I could of listed which ones to use to make a smaller list.

For a third game, you now have enough experience that you all fully understand most ins and outs of the game, make very little errors! Now play any hero, villain, environment that appeals the most. Though probably still avoid using those four heroes.

Playing two heroes makes it harder to get each one down your first game.

 

I would definitely suggest each of you grabbing a lvl 1 hero, like Legacy or Haka.

Wraith and Tachyon are also pretty easy to figure out while playing a second hero.

It is easy to miss some of the less obvious interactions when playing two heroes each.

 

Legacy, Wraith, Tachyon, Haka are great first game heroes as you get a good variety of mechanics wihtout anything being complicated enough that you'd have trouble playing them first game with another hero.

 

After that, you should have a pretty decent grasp of the fundamentals, then do whatever, I'd suggest Voss as a second villain, some of them get pretty complex pretty quick, and Voss is a totally different experience from Blade, they are polar opposites.

I'd suggest Blade Primalis, then Voss Megalopolis.  That should give you a pretty good start.

Welcome to the community! 

 

I would offer a few pointers on what to avoid for your first few games. Absolute Zero, Argent Adept, The Ennead, Megalopolis, and Nightmist. The heroes are a bit more complicated and would not be ideal for getting the basic rules down and getting a feel for the game in general. Megalopolis has a few cards that prevent you from playing parts of the game, and also is counter productive to learning the game. The Ennead are problably the most complicated villain to run (imo) as they have various triggers that could trigger more triggers, so again not good when you are trying to get started. All of those mentioned are fun and can be a delightful challenge, just not something for people trying to learn the basics. All of this is IMHO, of course.
