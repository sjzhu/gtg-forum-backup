My FLGS is Armored Gopher Games in Urbana, IL. They include SotM among many other fine products, and the owner knows both of my kids (ages 8 and 4) by name. He's an awesome dude, and they even started carrying the playmats after seeing them at GenCon.

It's fairly common for me to see somebody having at least purchased a SotM product during his monthly "Dammit, I bought this game and I'm gonna play it" events. Such an event was where I played it for the first time, and the gentleman whose copy we played later loaned me his 1st edition printing so that I could figure out whether it was over the Grasshopper's head (it wasn't).

The Grasshopper is even more addicted to trying new heroes/villains/environments than I am. When we're playing with newer players (which is often), I try to steer him toward people whose turns don't need lots of explaining to the other players, which often results in whining about not being able to try Setback, the Scholar, or the Argent Adept (all of whom were requested today before finally letting him use Fixer for the first time that deck has been used).
