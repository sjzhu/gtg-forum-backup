Here is a quick update on all of the publicly-known projects we are juggling! There have been a few changes from the last update as some projects have been shifted around.

Remember, once something is sent to the printer, there is a 3-4 month turn-around time before we start shipping stuff to Kickstarter/preorder backers.

Finished by Printer; On a Boat

1) Guise/Omnitron-IV reprints (Note: There was an issue with the Wager Master reprints, which will be happening later)

2) Sentinel Miniatures: Minions and Sentinel Tactics Location Maps

3) Deck Building: The Deck Building Game and Unpub: The Unpublished Game

4) Compounded and the Compounded: Geiger expansion

Completed by GTG; At the Printer

1) Bottom of the Ninth

Nearly Completed by GTG; To Printer within ~2 Weeks

1) Villains of the Multiverse and associated mini-expansions

2) New set of Oversized Villain Cards

3) Isle of Trains, Diner, and Brew Crafters: The Travel Card Game reprints

In Focus by GTG

1) New Bedford (this was actually farther along than anticipated, and should be on Kickstarter with glorious new art over Gen Con).

2) Monster Truck Mayhem

On Deck for Intense GTG Focus in Late Summer

1) Spirit Island (as we started work on this, we realized that this needed a bit more work to be the best it can be)

2) Sentinel Tactics: Battle for Broken City and For Profit

3) Don't Get Eated
