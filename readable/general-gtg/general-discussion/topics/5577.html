With the remaining four heroes i have a hard time putting them in groups of 2 thematically to tie into another expansion together. Unless we see a Haka, Bunker or AZ from the future.

All these back story connections and character interweaving is one of my favorite things about this game. The greater world they have created. I look forward to seeing how this continues to be unvieled moving forward. I just really really hope that when >G feels the game has reached its capacity for expansions and such that there won’t be any loose ends in the nemesis department.
