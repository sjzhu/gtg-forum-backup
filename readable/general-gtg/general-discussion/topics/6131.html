Hello everyone,

I’m Joel, the web monkey overseeing the re-development and migration of the Greater Than Games / Sentinels of the Multiverse website. The site is coming along nicely and we hope that you will enjoy the look and feel of the new site as much as we do.

We are planning to start the migration on Saturday August 11th and the new site will come back online sometime after that. This means that on the morning of August 11th, we will take the Forums offline to begin their migration. This is a multiple-step operation and will take us (me) at least a couple of hours to export, import, export again, import one more time (really, I’m not kidding here), and do a whole bunch of testing before the Forums finally appear in Drupal.

The features that we are sure to move are the forums and posts (of course), user accounts, private messages, badges, avatars and attachments. (Avatars and Attachments may come at a later date as they are the most challenging) I think that your passwords will be brought over, too, but we’ll have to see after the move. If passwords are not converted, we will provide detailed instructions on how to initiate a reset. (In the meantime, please be sure your email address is correct in your Account Settings.)

We are still investigating maintaining of the features that you currently have, such as “Notify me of Replies”, and such enhancements may continue to appear after the site is switched.

So now that we have that out of the way, I have a question for you, the users of the forum:

When it comes to composing posts and replies, do you prefer using the BBCode technique for styling your posts (e.g. using the square bracket codes to make [bold] or [italic] or similar) or would you prefer a real WYSIWYG (what you see is what you get) editor that shows you what your post will look like as you are composing it?

Are there other things you wish you had when composing a post? (Ok, I know this is two questions…)

Thanks!
–Joel
