Alright! Gen Con is over, and I'm only a day late with this post! Not bad! Anyhow, here's some stuff!

  1. Gen Con went more smoothly than it ever has in the past!
  2. We ran a bunch of product through our warehouse for GTG Operations.
  3. We did a lot of math and ordered various reprints of products for the fall (notably Spirit Island and some Sentinels expansions).
  4. We were out of the office for the bulk of the past two weeks as a result of Gen Con.
  5. The response to our new releases at Gen Con was fantastic! Medium sold out faster than any game ever has at the show, including Spirit Island, and Homebrewers sold out Saturday (which I think is third or fourth fastest?)
  6. We ran a bunch of panels at Gen Con, including Letters Page Live, State of Greater Than Games, Illustrating Board Games, and Freight & Shipping Logistics.
  7. We met with the show runners for UK Games Expo and planned out our booth for that show for next year.
  8. We had several very exciting meetings at Gen Con about secret things; we shall see if they turn into actual, real things or not (the juries are still out).
  9. We fulfilled the Homebrewers Kickstarter!

Here is the status of all of our works in progress.

  • Spirit Island: The Jagged Earth (Paul, Christopher, Jenn, Erik)
  • Sentinels of Freedom video game (Christopher, Adam, Underbite Studio)
  • The History of Sentinel Comics (Christopher, Adam)
  • Untitled Cheapass Games Project (Jenn, Chris Burton, James Ernest)
  • Milkman (Chris Kirkman, Chris Burton)
  • Fate of the Elder Gods: Agents of Arkham (Chris Kirkman)
  • Some other games (Chris Kirkman, Chris Burton)
  • A new and improved website (Jenn, Paul, Alex, Joel)

I'll see you next month!
