So these forums are getting pretty big and popular, and with christmas and a kickstarter just having passed, I'm certain there will be an influx of new posters coming to look for information about their new favorite game. :)

 

So I figured this would be a good post to start - Links to some of the best things around Sentinels of the Multiverse, in one easy to find place! Seninel fans, feel free to suggest anything I forgot in here! 

 

Sleeving Cards - Want to Sleeve your cards? here are the best suggestions

Oversize Villian Sleeves? Thank you Rabit!

How many Cards? 1,779 including everything up through Vengeance, but not counting oversized cards.

 

Storing all the Cards! - When you start sleeving, and now with Shattered Timelines filling up the EE box, there are a lot of people looking for new ways to store their cards. check out here for lots of great solutions!

 

Looking for copies of Promo Cards? - This thread has downloads of high quality images of all the promo cards (the Shattered Timelines ones still need to be updated) - You can also find versions of them on Spiff's site (next link). Here's a link as of 20-Nov-2014 to a collection at copy.com

 

Spiff's Resources - (And the Forum Thread) - A collection of great print outs, trackers, card boxes, and custom work for SoTM, plus web based Randomizer for choosing games. Spiff has also collected all the Errata and Clarifications in a single file, AND the ...

 

Achievement Tracker - The SotM Story Challenges are here - that is little objectives to try and accomplish. So far only with the EE heroes and villains, but one day they should be updated for more!

 

The Sentinels Wiki - Our own fan grown wiki. Look in, Join up, and if you find some interesting information posted it up and lets find some great little easter eggs about the world of SotM. 

 

Sentinels Statistcs Tracking Thread - The thread for discussion about the statistics project. View the Current months Statistics here

Statistics Input Form - For inputing your games for inclusion into the above statistics project. If you want to see the Raw Data, send a PM to Lynkfox

 

Flamethrower49's Strategies - Flamethrower is slowly (but steadily!) working his way through all the heroes and villains and posting strategies. This is the collected volume of his works, plus a few others whom have made various strategy guides.

 

Realm of Discord  discord server. Very active and somewhat diverse fan run discord server for Sentinels, Spirit Island, and other related games.

Google+ Hangout - Want to find a game online? Google+ or Skype can create a great way to find playing partners when you got no one around. Check out the G+ hangout here to make events with others. - Also: The Skype Thread - This thread for more information on such meetups!

Vassal Mod - In that same idea as the Skype and Google+, here is the mod for Vassal that shows you cards. Vassal is a system for playing table top games online, allowing various mods to represent the game in a virtual environment. However, due to the nature of card games, there are only a limited number of heroes and villains to play with, per >G Request.

Playing Sentinels on Steam? - Check out this thread for other Sentinels on Steam!

 

 

 

Koga's Templates and Forge of the Multiverse Software - Want to make your own cards? Check out these templates and program to help in creating your own Hero decks and Villains!

 

 

 

Those Funky Badges - Forum Nominations Awards! - Some forum goers have some funky special titles under their avatars. Intersted in where those came about? Here is where you can find out.

 

Full Card Spoilers - Full Text Spoilers of all the Cards

----

 

 

Sentinels Fans - Feel free to suggest additional posts to go into this one. I know a lot of these are already stickied on their various forums, but when you first jump into here, there are a lot of forums and a LOT of posts, and it can get a bit overwhelming. Something nice and forward and easy for people to find will be handy

Total: 1,779
 
EE - 578 (41x10 + 27x4 + 15x4 = 410 + 108 + 60)
 
RC - 222 (41x2 + 27x4 + 2, Operative + 15x2 = 82 + 108 + 2 + 30)
 
IR - 228 (41x2 + 27x4 + 8, Ennead + 15x2 = 82 + 108 + 8 + 30)
 
ST - 220 (41x2 + 27x4 + 15x2 = 82 + 108 + 30) 
 
Mini-expansions - 166 (41x2 + 27x2 + 15x2 = 82 + 54 + 30)
 
Promo Heroes - 18 (YL-1, RCW-1, Ra-1, Fanatic-1, DV-1, EH-1, F6-6, DWatch-4, GIL/B-2)
 
Promo Villains - 8 (Omni, BB, Spite, Gloom, 2 each)
 
Vengeance - 339 (41x5 + 4, Sentinels +20x5 + 15x2 = 205 + 4 +100 + 30)
 
 
Note: The Vengeance villains do not have standard sized cards, but rather come by default with oversized cards. There are also oversized cards available for all of the other villains, including the promo villains. The two most recent promo villains may not yet have oversized villain cards available, but I _think_ they will become available in future versions of the oversize villain pack.
 
There are thus a total of 27 oversized cards.
EE - 4 (Omnitron, Baron Blade, Voss, Dawn)
RC - 4 (Plague Rat, Spite, Matriarch, Chairman)
IR - 4 (Gloomweaver, Akash'Bhuta, Apostate, Ennead)
ST - 4 (Kismet, Dreamer, La Capitan, Iron Legacy)
Mini-expansions - 2 (Ambuscade, Miss Information)
Promos - 4 (Mad Bomber Blade, Cosmic Omnitron, Zombie Spite,
                Skinwalker Gloomweaver)
Vengeance - 5 (Baron Blade, Ermine, Proletariat, Friction, Fright Train)
