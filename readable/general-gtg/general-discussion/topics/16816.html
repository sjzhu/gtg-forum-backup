We've had yet another fantastically productive month! We're definitely making a bunch of exciting progress through our massive project backlog, in spite of being in the middle of a ton of Gen Con prep. Here is a quick summary of things we accomplished:

  1. We got in all of the new furniture for our convention booth setup in plenty of time for Gen Con!
  2. We got a list of "items we need for Gen Con" created four weeks earlier than we ever have before.
  3. Tons more Gen Con prep work. Also, our logo is on our booth in the expo hall map this year! Check it out if you haven't yet!
  4. We received Spirit Island, Exoplanets, and Lazer Ryderz from the factory!
  5. We had a giant, incredibly productive meeting about project flow for the Sentinel Comics RPG.

Speaking of Gen Con, we are going to be sponsoring a number of panels in addition to all of the demos and other things that we'll be running! Here is a list in case any seem interesting to you and you want to check them out!

  • Thu, 12pm - Illustrating Board Games
  • Thu, 2pm - Board Games & Intellectual Property Law
  • Fri, 12pm - The Business of Being a Game Artist
  • Fri, 4pm - The State of Greater Than Games
  • Sat, 11am - Costume Contest
  • Sat, 12pm - Cost Conscious Board Game Design
  • Sat, 3pm - The Letters Page LIVE!
  • Sat, 6pm - Bringing Diversity and Accessibility Into Games
  • Sun, 11am - Freight & Shipping Logistics (aka "The Most Boring Panel Ever")

If you are going to be at Gen Con and have interest in any of these topics, come check us out!

Anyway, here is the status of all of our works in progress!

  1. Lazer Ryderz
  2. Exoplanets
  3. Spirit Island

  2. A bunch of reprints (Compounded, SotM stuff, etc)

In Mass Production

  1. Bottom of the 9th: Clubhouse (shipping was delayed; scheduled for 10 July)
  2. SotM: Stuntman, Benchmark, & Void Guard
  3. SotM: Complete Hero Variant Collection
  4. SotM: Ultimate Collector's Case
  5. SotM: 5th Anniversary Foil Villain Collection

At Factory; Pre-Press Work

  1. nothing this month

Current WIP Focus by GTG

  1. SotM: OblivAeon (Christopher)
  2. Sentinel Comics: The Role Playing Game: Starter Kit (Critical Hits + Jenn + Christopher)
  3. Prime War (Christopher - after #1 & #2)
  4. Legend of Sleepy Hollow (Chris Kirkman + Jenn)
  5. A new and improved website.

You'll notice that everything *except* the OblivAeon stuff is in mass production. That is because the Collector's Case is going to take so long to assemble that it won't be ready to ship for several months yet. That meant that we have more time before we need to send OblivAeon itself to the factory if we want them to be done at the same time, and we have chosen to take advantage of that extra time to do some internal testing and refining of the OblivAeon scenario itself just to make sure that it is the best it can be.

I'll see you next month in the midst of a giant pre-Gen Con panic storm!
