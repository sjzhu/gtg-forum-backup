Paul
2012-11-01 15:08 UTC
#18

Greonhal is correct. While the promo character cards are limited-run, the mini-expansions are not. While they are given away free as stretch rewards to our Kickstarter backers, they are fairly major game content that we don't want to limit only to people who know about / back us when we run a Kickstarter campaign.

Also, as I mentioned in yesterday afternoon's Kickstarter update, we are likely to reprint an "unlimitied edition" run of all of the promo cards in several years so that people who want access to those characters can get them. The cards will have the same powers / game effect as the original promo cards, but will be visually distinct. The main reason that we are waiting is that we want to get all of the promo cards that we ever make into that pack, so we have to wait until Sentinels of the Multiverse is complete as a game, which won't be for a couple of years. The secondary reason though is that it rewards our early-adopter fans by giving them some minor game content that others won't have access to for a couple of years.
