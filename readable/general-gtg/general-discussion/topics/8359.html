More Vengeance info, including pre-orders, will happen next week.

We have been working on catching up from conventions and getting various out of print products and nearly out of print products (Enhanced Edition, Rook City, Infernal Relics, etc) off to the printer so that we have restocks before Christmas. We've also been working on a few unannounced cool things that will be mentioned in due time.

We definitely believe that communication is important, and realize that it has been a bit sparse the past few months. We have unfortunately had to choose between getting things done, and talking about what we are doing. It is a rough trade off to have to make, but ultimately we belive that you'll be pleased with the results!

On the up side, this episode of extreme business has really helped us to streamline our workflow, which should make future projects much easlier for us (and more communication-filled for you!) For all of you programmers out there, we have essentially spent time doing the project management equivalent of refactoring our code base to be more maintainable and extensible. We still have a bit of a backlog (Vengeance and GSF most notably), but the light at the end of the tunnel is clearly visible.

Anyhow, I hope that helps explain things somewhat. And stay tuned for Vengeance bios and pre-orders next week!
