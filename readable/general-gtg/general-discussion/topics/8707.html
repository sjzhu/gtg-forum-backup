While I realize Sentinels presents a ton of possibilities for mixing and matching decks to create unique games (even if some result in oddball matchups like facing the chairman in a ancient tomb or a drug-using serial killer in space), I've been thinking about more ways to create more custom fun without going to the length of creating new cards. 

Has anyone experimented with mixing decks?  The obvious choice here would be mixing various environment decks, but maybe other possibilities exist which I havent thought of.  It's kind of fun to think about the ways certain mechanics would be broken or enhanced with different combinations. A few starters:

Time Cataclysm x Realm of Discord: These just seem well suited thematically to me.

Putting minions from other decks into Final Wasteland for even more target-intensive fun.

Cannibalizing Time Cataclysm to place all the cards which are clearly "from" other environments back into those environments.

 

Thoughts?

Yeah, but there's also the aspect of theme to think about. True, there are 4, but they are very different; they aren't just 4, bland heroes. They each have their own roles, abilities, and disadvantages. If you made them each customizable, it would kind of betray the theme of both this hero group AND the game as a whole; the heroes are not supposed to be customizable. They are pre-made, with their own personalities. As I'm sure people have figured out, Medico is the healer of the group. All cards associated with him heal, and that's about it. If you could give Mainstay all the healing, then make Idealist the tank of the group, and make Medico the striker -- or worse, give one hero ALL of the abilities -- then what's the point of having 4 of them?

Plus, that would kind of go against the mechanics of the entire game. You don't play equipment cards ON heroes; you just play them, and their effect HAPPENS to target that specific hero, because it either mentions them by name (increase damage dealt by Ra by 1) or addresses the player directly (YOU may use an additional power during your power phase.) Cards are played by players, not heroes. Powers are used by players, not heroes. It just doesn't fit the mechanics of the game.

 

Actually, that may be kind of cool for a later hero. Maybe like a Gundam-themed robot or something, with switchable parts. Then you could give it shields, an auto-repair, and a gun, or maybe scrap the first two and go ALL GUNS.
