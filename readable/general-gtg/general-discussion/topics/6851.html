The DR's going to be off the charts considering that you're playing 1-4 components every single round. Most heroes have a hard time even cutting through 2 DR without specific combos, and you need to do damage to Omnitron to get rid of them. If you fail to destroy all of the components every round, the DR's going to stack up into the 4-6 range. Which is game over unless you have End of Days, Irreducible damage, or multi-target ongoing destruction. In general I don't really feel like you should lose for having one dead round.

Also, note that you're unlikely to play many drones once Omnitron flips. They should all be in the trash or in play from before, and he'll melt fast under the additional damage from killing drones. I suppose you could purposefully stall or something, but it seems like you'd normally have plenty of deck left by the time it flips. This also feeds into my first point. If all of the drones are in the trash, over a third of Omnitron's remaining deck is components. This means it's not unlikely that you're adding an extra component to the H-2 each round, making the DR even more insane.

I would drop the component damage reduction, or change it to something like "If there is a component in play, reduce damage done to Omnitron by H-2." This can be in addition to reducing damage per drone, but remember that you're not all that likely to see many drones post-flip.

Another option is you could completely rework the flipside to pull Drones instead of components. Also this way it could pull a mess of them (like H-1), which would force the heroes to deal with the drones before they dealt with Omnitron itself. The thought of having to cut through its swarm to get to it is appealing thematically, and it would feel pretty different from other villains.
