I know this will not be a typical result, but I just had a very fun (and very close) 1-player game using only Vengeance cards. 

The matchup was The Sentinels in Freedom Tower against Vengeful Baron Blade. It wasn't easy, and I probably played slightly conservatively, but I managed to defeat VBB with Writhe @ 13hp and Dr. Medico @ 4hp.

 

Because none of the environments and villains that come with Vengeance utilize the (H) mechanic, there was no need to scale or adjust anything. Certainly, having 4 different hero targets to choose from and the ability to revive them when necessary helped the process as well. Also, during the game, the environment played 4 entry points which allowed VBB to deal 5 dmg (6-1 because of Genetically Fused Physique) to himself each turn (then heal 2) also helped considerably.

 

Just thought I'd share. Looking forward to trying some other scenarios. :)

Has anyone else tried a solo game of vengeance? How did it go?
