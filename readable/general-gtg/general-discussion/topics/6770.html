What is the ratio of cards / dollar for all cards released up to and including Infernal Relics?

1104 / $85 = 13.0 [1]

What is the ratio of cards / dollar for all cards that will be released for Shattered Timelines and Vengeance?

682 / $50 = 13.64 [2]

 

The above does not include shipping and assumes that one got all promotions possible during conventions and/or Kickstarters. All prices are rounded up to the nearest dollar.

 

[1] 1104 = EE cards (578: 10 hero decks x 41; 4 villain decks x 27; 2 env decks x 15) + Rook City (222: 2 hero decks; 4 villain decks; 2 env decks; 2 extra cards for The Operative) + Infernal Relics (228; 2 hero decks; 4 villain decks; 2 env decks; 8 extra cards for The Ennead) + Young Legacy, Alt Wraith, Cosmic Omnitron (2 cards), Mad Bomber Blade (2 cards), Unity, and Ambscade

$85 = $40 for EE + $20 for Rook City + $20 for Infernal Relics + $5 for Unity

 

[2] Based on standard deck sizes. 

     Shattered Timelines - 220: 2 hero decks; 4 villain decks; 2 env decks

     Vengeance - 355: 5 hero decks; 5 villain decks; 1 env deck

     Dark Visionary, The Freedom Six, The Assistant, Silver Gulch, The Scholar, Eternal Haka, Greatest Legacy, Final Wasteland

$50 = $20 for Shattered Timelines plus $30 for Vengeance
