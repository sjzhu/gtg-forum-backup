Worldbuilders is the name of the group of authors that Patrick Rothfuss rounds up to donate stuff for his charity raffle & auction.  The proceeds from those are donated to Heifer International, a nonprofit that distributes animals, along with agricultural training, to families in need around the world as a means of providing self-sufficiency. 

They're a pretty rescpected charity organization.  You can read about them at: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heifer_International
