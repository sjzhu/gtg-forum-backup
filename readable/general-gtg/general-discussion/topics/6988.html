Well, some promo cards are available on the secondary market from people who have gotten them in the past. We may also occationally release some of the very, very small number of promo cards we have left for charity events (like we did for the Jack Vassel Memorial Fund Auction this winter).

Additionally, we have made the images for all of the promo cards available on Board Game Geek, so you can always download and print them off from there so that you can play with those rules.

Finally, at some point in the future, we will be releasing an "unlimited edition" pack of all of the promo character cards to-date, and it will be widely available for purchase.
