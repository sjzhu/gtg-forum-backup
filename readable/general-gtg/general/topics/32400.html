The Letters Page folks have the following confirmed as the primary universe heroes represented by the characters on Principle cards:

  • Blank Slate: Haka
  • Compassion: Lifeline
  • Crime: Tachyon
  • Defender: Alpha
  • Equality: Ra
  • Fire: NightMist

  • Guidance: Guise
  • Honor: Greazer
  • Inner Demons: Omnitron-X
  • Invulnerable: the Visionary
  • Levity: Darkstrife and Painstake
  • Loner: Setback
  • Longevity: Tempest
  • Magic: the Wraith
  • Prophecy: Sky-Scraper
  • Rage: Mr. Fixer
  • Self-Preservation: Young Legacy
  • Shadow: Fashion
  • Sidekick: Expatriette
  • Speed: the Naturalist
  • Strength: Unity
  • Tactician: Legacy
  • Time Travel: Benchmark

  • Undead: K.N.Y.F.E.
  • Whispers: the Scholar
  • Wild Card: the Argent Adept
  • Zealot: Chrono-Ranger

And these actual character names have been confirmed:

  • Fire: NightLight
