Hello everyone here on the GTG forum! It has been many years since I posted on a regular basis, but I’m back. It has come to my attention that this forum and community could benefit from some more interaction with GTG leadership, and I finally have the time once again to devote to such a pursuit! I have two comments of note:

  1. Please be kind to each other and strive to put the best construction on everything. Internet communication is challenging, and not everyone has the neurotypical skills necessary to accurately infer tone from written communication.

  2. As part of coming back to the forum, I thought I’d do a quick AMA (Ask Me Anything) style post! I’ll periodically check the forum throughout the day and answer most of the questions anyone posts!

It is good to see you all again!

@TheWyrmSuperior

@skywhale

There are no specific plans to that effect, but you never know! Now that Christopher and I are doing our own monthly podcast, that soaks up a lot of my available podcasting time.

@Powerhound_2000

@Faralis

Nothing on SMDE, but yes on SCRPG!! Ulysses Spiel began working on a German version over the winter, and we’re very excited for it to come out! We don’t have any specific timelines, however.

@TakeWalker

@Godai

Irrevenant:

When will we be getting some meaty expansions that provide detail on things like: the state of the world post-OblivAeon

I assume that the upcoming Urban Settings Book will provide some details on that, and if not, the future G.L.O.B.A.L book likely will.

Irrevenant:

how magic works in the setting

There’s a chance that the Dark Watch book will cover that, as we know it will contain Pinion and Zhu Long. The Diamond Book of Monsters / xxtz’Hulissh Adventure is another possibility. I also vaguely recall C&A mentioning on the podcast about just a “Magic Book,” but I’ve no clue how likely or when that would be. There’s also a possibility for a Virtuosos of the Void book, although that’s just pure speculation.

Irrevenant:

what are some known alien races

It’s confirmed that we’ll be getting a Void Guard (and Captain Cosmic and Lifeline) book eventually, which will likely address this.

Irrevenant:

what sort of technology is available in SCRPG Earth

Frankly, I don’t think any product has been announced or hinted at that will address this. Maybe the Paradigms book, but that seem unlikely.

However, there are a couple of general answer to all these questions: the Sentinel Comics Universe is Like Reality Unless Noted, and writers (i.e. GMs and players) are pretty much free to do whatever they’d like.

I do realise that those answers are fairly unsatisfying, though, so maybe I’ll think about collecting some of the information that’s been given in various products and the podcast into some dossiers. Maybe. We’ll see.
