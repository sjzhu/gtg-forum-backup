There has been a Greater Than Games forum since 2011, when we first started the company.

The first iteration, built into our original website, was pretty janky. But it existed! And as people discovered Sentinels of the Multiverse, many of them found their way there. The forums grew rapidly alongside our website, our games, our fandom, and the company itself.

A lot happened in those digital halls.

There were rules questions and strategy discussions, wild speculation and elaborate theories, running jokes and ridiculous goofs. Friendships formed. Games got organized. People who might never otherwise have met found each other because they all cared about the same weird little card game.

And, behind the scenes, an enormous amount of playtesting happened there, too. Sentinels of the Multiverse and many other Greater Than Games projects were developed with the help of our playtesting community in a super-secret Playtester Only section of the forums. That functionality — and, much more importantly, the people using it — was genuinely important to the success of GTG.

Then the 2020s brought a lot of changes.

First, we rebuilt our website because the old backend was… NOT good. That meant changing the forum software as well. There were ups and downs. The transition wasn’t perfectly executed. But the community persevered, and the forums continued to be a gathering place for GTG fans.

Then, as the company itself changed, GTG staff became less and less present on the forums, and eventually our ability to administer them became extremely limited. Over the last couple years, the forums have become much quieter than they once were.

Which brings us to today.

The Greater Than Games community is still strong. At every convention we attend, we meet dedicated, enthusiastic people who have been playing our games for years alongside people who are discovering them for the first time. There are active communities gathering online around The Letters Page, the Order of the Standard Table, and elsewhere. We even have a new secret playtesting area, carrying on one of the oldest traditions of the GTG forums.

But these forums themselves are now a shadow of the bustling place they once were.

We have finally regained enough access to them to make this post, but restoring and maintaining the forums properly would require substantial expense and ongoing resources that simply don’t make sense for what they have become.

And so, with a heavy heart, it is time to close the doors.

Farewell, ye faithful halls of olden times!

You were always greater than the sum of your software. You gave thousands of conversations a home. You helped people meet one another, share ideas, make friends, build games, tell stories, argue about card interactions, concoct increasingly improbable Multiverse theories, and have a tremendous amount of fun together.

The forums are going away.

The community they helped create isn’t.

We’ll remember the good times had here for a long, long time.

Thank you for being here.

Thank you for playing greater.

And thank you for being part of the Multiverse.

(The GTG forums will be deleted on Monday, September 14th. We wish we could keep them around longer, but unfortunately, that just isn’t possible. We look forward to seeing and interacting with all of you elsewhere as we continue to play greater together throughout the wide GTG Multiverse!)

This is truly the end of an era. To quote Doctor Who: “Everything ends, and it’s always sad. But everything begins again too, and that’s always happy. Be happy.” And hey, 15 years isn’t a bad run.

For anyone wishing to preserve all of your posts from here, I’ve found that you can go to Preferences > Account > Export your data > Request archive, which I believe should cause the system to send you a message, from which you can download a zip file, which contains, among other things, a CSV file of all your posts. It does not, of course, include the posts of any other users, but such is life.

For myself, anyone who wishes to can find me on the unofficial Realm of Discord Discord server. I know Discord isn’t everybody’s cup of tea, but it’s something, and it’s entirely free.

It goes without saying that I’ve had a great deal of fun and made a great deal of fond memories over the years with all of you here. I have undoubtedly enjoyed the honour of being a part of this community, of talking about all these silly little games with you all, of sharing stories and creations.

You’ve all been absolutely fantastic.
