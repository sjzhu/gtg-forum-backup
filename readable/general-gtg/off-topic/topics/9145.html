GAME TWO!

So that first game was pretty fun, huh? Here is where the SECOND game of 1KBWC will begin!

The Rules

  • Your turn begins when I send you a link to a card via PM.
  • During your turn, play a card by posting an image of it to the thread and doing what it says.
  • Unless otherwise stated, a card can be played on any one player, and any mention of "you" means "the player this card is played on."
  • If a card requires you to do something, you must do it by your next turn.
  • If you do not play a card within 24 hours of your turn, you lose your chance and the next player gets their card.
  • Alternatively, after recieving a card you may skip the rest of your turn to get two more cards.
  • At the end of the round, I post a recap of what happened and provide an update of the current game state.
  • If we run out of cards or (equally likey) completely break the game, it ends and the player with the most points wins, provided no cards have changed the win condition by then.
  • If you have a blank card, you may create a new card and PM it to me. I will add it to the deck and send a random card back to you.
  • Submitted cards should be .pngs about 300×400 or 400×300.
  • I may create other rules during the game in response to new elements your cards add.
  • Make the game as fun as you can! That's what it's all about!

To Join

Just send me four cards of your own design in a PM and I'll add them to the deck! We use these to get started. Turn order will be determined by the order I recieve the players' cards in. Cards need a picture, a title, and a description of what they do. If the card has an ongoing effect( like giving someone a special ability or adding a new rule to the game), it stays in play and its effect ends when the card goes away. If it's just a one-time thing( like drawing a card or losing some points) the card is discarded after use. Usually.

Some general guidelines on reversing card effects:

  • The opposite of drawing a card (moving it from the deck to your hand) is shuffling back a card (moving it from your hand to the deck)
  • The opposite of discarding a card (moving it from your hand to the discard pile) is drawing a random discarded card (moving it from the discard pile to your hand)
  • The opposite of destroying a card (moving it from a play area to the discard pile) is playing a random discarded card (moving it from the discard pile to a play area)
  • The opposite of playing a card (moving it from your hand to a play area) is putting a played card in your hand (moving it from a play area to your hand)

Keep in mind that in the regular course of you turn, you still draw and play as usual. This is just for card effects causing you to do these things.
