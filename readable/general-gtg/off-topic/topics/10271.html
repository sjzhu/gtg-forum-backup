A few weeks ago I was lucky enough to playtest a new game by WIBAI Games (Wouldn't It Be Awesome If) named Transylvania: Curses and Traitors.  They're from Kansas, like myself, and I had an absolute blast playing their game.  You and your friends take on the role of adventurers traveling to Transylvania to solve the curse of three great monsters: The Vampire, The Zombie, and The Werewolf.  As the game progresses (on modular tiles that ensure the gameboard changes each game) you gather powerful weapons and ancient knowledge to help you solve the curse.  But there is also danger of turning into the monsters yourself.  The game has three ways of winning:  Either a player solves the curse first, a player kills a monster (one of the other players after they have transformed), or should you become a monster you must kill half of the other players on the board.  It allows the game to be very diverse with a lot of options for travel, victory, and gameplay.

For a horror game, this games mechanics are very forgiving to players.  It can occasionally seem to act against theme, but I honestly really appreciate the fact that it does its best to keep players in the game and not have them fall behind too harshly.  The monsters are epic and provide powerful new mechanics, but they're still beatable.  The game has a lot of layers as far as player interaction.  At the start you are all working toward a similar goal without necessarily attempting to undermine one another.  If one of the players becomes a monster then you must either work together with the rest of the group to kill the monster, boost yourself up and make the other players more appealing targets, or delve into dark powers and become a monster yourself!  As a monster, your power level dramatically increases, but other players are actively trying to harm you, so you no longer have the quiet truce the players share when they're trying to solve the curse.

The creators are nice folks who were great recipients of my and my playtesting groups criticisms, and I'm excited to see how it has changed since I had a chance to play it.  Also, the video in which they address the funders in front of a bunch of shelves covered in games is at the hobby store I work at, Gatekeeper Hobbies!

Check it out!

It's been a few months since I've played, but if anyone has any questions I'll try to answer them!

The dice are d6s composed of 1, 2, 3, and three sides of a wolf howling at a full moon.  Those sides are zeroes for every character but The Werewolf.  It makes the challenges considerably more difficult when you have a 50% chance of any die rolling a zero.
