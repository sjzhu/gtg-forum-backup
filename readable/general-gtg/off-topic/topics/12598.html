its 2-6 players, competitive, but with centralized decks.  Not a deck builder, but it borrows from some concepts that deckbuilders use (central row, resource management)

 

Players have pick resources off Location cards (in a center row), which are used to play Operations cards from their hands (Drawn from a single community deck) - these cards either are events or parts, and the goal is to get the most points out of your parts by the time someone completes a Machine. Machines are completed by having 1 of each of the 5 needed parts in play. 

 

and Donner, this is in such very early phases of testing, that it's gonna be going on for a long while. You can always bug me at a later date when you have the resources to print it out :)
