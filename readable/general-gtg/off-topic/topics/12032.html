Nightcrawler was probably the year's best, or The Imitation Game, but I don't think either will be showered with awards.

I'd like to see Cumberbatch get the Actors gong, just because, well, Cumberbatch, but I think you may be right about Steve Carrell, he's been threatening some proper peer credibility for some time now. Anyhoo, the important ones for me are writing and cinematography as they are the most vital ingredients in a good movie and I cannot personally argue with your choices. I have a feeling that Emmanuel Lubezki for Birdman will get passed over for Robert Yeoman, they do love their sweeping vistas and extreme close ups in the Oscars, but Lubezki's work was technically brilliant and well deserving.

In fact it seems weird to see Grand Budapest Hotel in the running, I could have sworn that movie was much older and should have been in last years awards, but that film has "give me all the awards" written over it even though it was a lazy and meandering effort - almost like Wes Anderson was doing a parody of himself.
