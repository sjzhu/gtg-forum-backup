Sorry to disappoint, folks...I had no intention of arguing this statistics thing, because it doesn't really pertain to this issue.  A game of SOTM is not a roulette wheel...heroes' HP starts the game at a predetermined amount, so there are relatively narrow ranges of possible outcomes.  In a sufficiently large number of SOTM games, it's extremely probable that Voss will end up double-punching a hero at some point...it's built into the game's mechanics, especially with characters like the Scholar that are built for tanking, and cards like Smoke Bombs that encourage players to stay at the same HP total.  The text of the cards, not exclusively luck, will be what determines whether this situation arises...if you fight Voss more than twice or thrice, it's extremely likely you'll see this happen, or at least be able to tell that it could have.
