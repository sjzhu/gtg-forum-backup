My problem with GL was not the effects (which were quite decent I thought, though not the best I could hope for), nor even the story (which, again, was okay).  It was that they completely missed the point in characterization by having Ryan Reynolds be a self-absorbed, insecure jerk instead of a square-jawed, steadfast idealist.  He even flat-out says in the film that he is not "without fear", which is exactly the #1 qualification required of any Green Lantern.  It'd be like having a Batman that's totally unprepared and soft on criminals, or a Superman who angrily rejects his family and vocally sympathizes with America's enemies.  Hollywood simply cannot believe that moviegoing audiences will like a character who is morally pure and upright, unless that character is named Superman, and even then they're scared stiff that Joe Blow won't find him relatable enough.  They always have to try to pull This Loser Is You, and this was one property where that was the most inappropriate trope imaginable.
