Sooo I just found out about this game the other day (subject line of this thread is the game name) when my partner told me about it, then saw a YouTube vid yesterday on one of the channels I check every day and it looks super-cool. I wondered if anyone here was familiar with it - it's only been out a week or two, I think, and can be got on Steam and probably one or two other places as well.

The game is that one person has a bomb that they need to defuse, and five minutes in which to do so (at least, in the plays I've seen of it). They are the only person who can see the bomb - any other players, whether they're in the same room or on the Net, have no idea what the bomb-defuser is looking at and instead have the bomb defusal manual to look at (this is available as a free PDF download on the website, while the game itself is £10.99, at least on Steam). The person with the manual is the "expert" and it's the expert(s) job to give the bomb-defuser the right instructions to defuse the bomb. And the person with the bomb needs to give a good enough description of what they're seeing so that the expert(s) can give them the right instructions to defuse the bomb without it blowing up. It's sort of like solving a bunch of little mini-game things, wherein there may be certain symbols or some numbers, or different-coloured wires and stuff like that, and each module must be successfully "solved" (usually by having the right bit of it clicked on, and in the right order if it has more than one thing that needs clicking) for its light to go green. Solve all modules and the bomb is defused - congratulations, level complete, and you can move onto a harder one.

Obviously this game can't be played single-player so I was wondering if anyone on here was familiar with it/planning on getting it, and if so I'll actually get it myself and we can play it :D.
