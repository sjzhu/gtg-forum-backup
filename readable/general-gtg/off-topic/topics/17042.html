Thanks!

It was a plastic bat that I spray-painted with a layer of white and then a layer of slightly yellowish glow-in-the-dark paint to give it a bit of a shimmer.  The con was fine with me bringing it in.

Generally lightweight or plastic props are fine (think about all of the Harly Quinn's that carry giant mallets), but an actual wood or alumninum bat would be frowned upon and possibly confiscated at the prop check.  My daughter's staff was a light-weight wooden dowel with a foam headpiece (spray-painted gold).

Of course, prop rules vary from convention to convention, so always check first.
