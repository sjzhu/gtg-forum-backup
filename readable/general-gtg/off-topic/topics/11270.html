The game in question is called Dwelvers. The developer, a guy called Rasmus, put it together as a homage to both the Dungeon Keeper and Settlers games - you're the master of a dungeon, you get your imps to dig out tiles so you can build rooms. You recruit creatures and have to provide stuff like food, beer, and gold to keep them happy so they don't rebel while you train them up and explore further, digging out dungeons inhabited by "wild" creatures and portals to the surface guarded by knights and stuff. The Settlers part comes in because you can set up production chains and stuff to make sure all the stuff you need gets, well, produced :).
