Since I recently had some time to dedicate to this project and I am excited that it is still fairly popular with you all (even with all the delays), I decided to post an update on my progress. 

Sadly, there has been little to update and with the frsh look I have had at it I see some major flaws with where I was headed with the original concept. So, I decided to go back to basics and rework that which I had so diligently hammerer out previously. The mechanics of the game remain sound just the application of Powers and interactions was starting to get messy. In an effort to remain as simple and elegant the rules would allow for, I decided to see what you all would like to see in this game.

Would you like a comprhensive list of powers to choose from?

Would a more abstract approch be preferred?

What do you look for in a Super Hero role-playing game that keeps you going back to it over and over again?

For those of you wondering there will be no dice in this version of the game it will be all based on a standard playing card deck of 52 cards and both jokers. Each player (including the Mastermind or GM if you will) would be required to have their own deck to represnt the randomness of the game. Does this sound familiar to some of you? Probably, but i am hoping that the rules I have in place will be different enough that it will not seem like anything else you may have seen.

Thanks for all your patience and hopefully I will have something for you all very soon.

Maybe you could provide a list of suggested powers, but also allow for people to create their own if they want to - the list of suggestions can then double up as a list of example powers as part of a guide in how to create your own. So that it doesn't risk getting too overpowered, you could let people only choose one power for themselves, perhaps, and outline at creation what specific kinds of things their power will let them do. For example, if you're super-strong, what's the biggest weight you can lift? If you're telepathic, is there a maximum range or number of people you can speak to at once? Maybe you could have some kind of scoring system…say, every power has a rating for stuff like Range, Number of Targets, Size/Weight of Target(s) Affected, Duration, or something, with a scale from 1-5 or 1-10, and then players have maybe 10-15 or so points to spend in them. So you could give your power a big range but then wouldn't have many points left to spend in making it affect a bigger target (or more targets). Of course, this is a bit of a problem in that some powers would only affect yourself, such as flight or shapechanging. Maybe then you could decide that Range and Number of Targets are irrelevant. Or something? I don't know, lol. I think I'm waffling uselessly here ;). Maybe just providing a standard list would be better, then, but allow people to "edit" each power to some extent to allow for some customisation…

Wow, so no love for Legacy, Bunker or Absolute Zero huh? 

For those that think that writing a totally new set of mechanics for an RPG from and existing world is a simple process, I say thee NAY. The game has gone there no less than a dozen permutations and it seems that it is in the state of flux once again. In an effort to make the game simple and easy to understand and at the same time elegant and superheroic, is by no means a small feat. 

As the sole person that is writing everything and adapting the Heroes, Villains and Environments it seemed a daunting task at first until I realized that it really wasn't. All I had to do was build a structure that was both simple in its form but could very easily create any number of heroes in any number or possible combinations. As such, it is taking an increasingly longer period of time to write with life continually working against me. The structure hass many branches that need to be filled in but with luck it won't take as long as it appears currently.

I will post the Freedom Five when I have them completed and you may not understand all the mechanics that is associated with them but at least you will see what the last year or so has been leading up to.

I know I have promised things sooner and I am sorry for the delays but to be honest I think it might all have been worth it in the end. I hope to have a completed Adventure with a Villain attempting to do what all villains do and the players of the Freedom Five trying to stop them by the summer but no later then GenCon.

Enough of my rambling, back to the Dreamscape for me and back to saving the Multiverse for you.
