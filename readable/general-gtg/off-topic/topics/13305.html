Episode 7

 

 

Well, it was a good run.

 

One thing amused me, though.  When Matt was studying, one of the factoids he mentioned was that 'Humphry Davy discovered oxygen'.  This is amusing because although three different scientists can lay claim to the title of 'Oxygen Discoverer', Davy is not even close to being one of them.

 

Carl Scheele discovered oxygen by 1773, but never gets credit for anything*, Joseph Priestly discovered oxygen in 1774 but published first, and   Antoine Lavoisier coined the term 'oxygen' in 1777, finally discrediting phlogiston theory.  Humphry Davy was born in 1778.

 

 

*A Short History of Nearly Everything, by Bill Bryson puts it the best:

Scheele… discovered eight elements - chlorine, fluorine, manganese, barium, molybdenum, tungsten, nitrogen, and oxygen - and got credit for none of them.  In every case, his finds were either overlooked or made it into publication after someone else had made the same discovery independently.  He also discovered many useful compounds, among them ammonia, glycerin, and tannic acid, and was the first to see the commercial potential of chlorine as a bleach - all breakthroughs that made other people extremely wealthy.

arenson9:

How do you feel about it?

Weeelllll…

On the one hand, it was a fantastically enjoyable three weeks. We did all sorts of weird and cool things, plus spending time with so many wonderful people was great. On the other hand, it was tiring having to be 'on' for sixteen hours a day, and having to deal with producers (not all of them, but enough) trying to manufacture drama and such. On the third hand, I have made a bunch of really good friends out of it, and watching in on television was incredibly surreal and entertaining.

As for the show itself? I enjoyed watching it, certainly. I think it was decent fluff television. Its view of geek culture is a bit reductionist, certainly, but I think ultimately it's a net positive. Not that it didn't squander a lot of opportunities, but geekdom was genuinely celebrated rather than just mocked.
