jagarciao:

Just curious if anyone that backed this has received it and had a chance to play it. I got mine, but haven't gotten it to the table yet. Looks good though. 

Yes I got this. I have found it to be a very simple but satisfying game. Mechanically its very simple at least, but the mind games that emerge in the meta-game for your group is pretty awesome. Lot of bluffing and predicting. Theres bidding phases and stuff. It really has a lot to like about it. Eric has a pretty decent of well designed games and this is not an exception.

Some teams are flat out better than others I think. There is not a great balance when talking every possible 1v1 matchup. Trolls vs Ogres for example, Trolls have such a distinct advantage in that matchup. Ogres will have to pay an arm and a leg to get on even footing through ability/ringer bidding, and I'd imagine Ogres would be happy to let them pay, take cheaper upgrades/ringers, and have a field day with cheat cards during a match.  Barbarians vs Daemons? Yup. Barbarians will win that match every time given equal player skill. No question. Goblins vs Amazons? Again, Goblins stand little chance.

2v2 matches, however, are very fun and feel much more balanced, but thats expected. 4vAll is a bit of a mess since everyone is bashing everyone, but there are a lot of team abilities that are just more effective with more teams in the game. 

Overall I'm very happy with the purchase.
