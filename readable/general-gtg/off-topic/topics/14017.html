Our Christmas traditions (atheist family but we celebrate in a cultural kind of way): hot turkey sandwiches the evening of Christmas Eve, present-opening around midday Christmas Day, big turkey lunch about 2 (usually turkey, pork, mash, roasted potatoes, pigs in blankets, stuffing, various steamed veggies, roasted parsnips, Yorkshire puddings, gravy, cranberry sauce, followed by Christmas pudding, cheesecake, and whatever desert I feel like making). Then I bully family into playing a few board games. Boxing Day we eat cold meats, salad, and baked potatoes. Then my parents go to the Carary Islands and I take leftovers home.

Also my brother and I gently irritate each other and make each other laugh, and I prevent mini-arguments between him and my parents about a hundred times over the holiday.
