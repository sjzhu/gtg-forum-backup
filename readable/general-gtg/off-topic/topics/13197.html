https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/shadowrift/shadowrift-2nd-edition

Shadowrift is a game I've played many times with my gaming group, it's a fun cooperative deck building game. It recently had a kickstarter for an expansion and now has one for a 2nd edition of the base game. The 2nd edition will make it possible for the game to have more expansions. One big change is instead of the monsters killing off a specific person in town, they'll go after a certain type/class of townfolk, which opens the game up to add more. It's also getting a complete overhaul on the card design and artwork.
