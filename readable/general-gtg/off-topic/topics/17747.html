Hi, welcome to the forum :). Wow, that's a lot of links. I don't really have the time or inclination to go through them right now, but I have played Path of Exile. Last time was probably a couple of years ago, though - I know the game has changed a lot since then. It was good fun, though the end game stuff got pretty ridiculous - I was playing through with a friend and we did most of the late-game bosses by just zerging it, running in and getting off maybe one or two attacks before the boss would vaguely glance in our direction and insta-kill us. Repeat for, like, an hour and a half :P. And the exp loss on death at Merciless difficulty made it almost not worth attempting to level any more - you could spend an hour carefully grinding your way up the exp bar, and then lose all of it (and then some) to one single death. Not fun.

But apart from that, good game :). I like the way the skills and stuff work.
