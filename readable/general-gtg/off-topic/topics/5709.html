Welcome to the Corrupt-A-Wish Foundation!
This was a fun little forum game that I first heard about on the Kingdom Hearts TCG Forums.

How it works is that one poster makes a wish, the second poster grants and corrupts it, and then wishes for something themselves!

Example:
Poster 1 - I wish that I had a million dollars!
Poster 2 - Granted, but it is all shredded! I wish I could live forever!
Poster 3 - Granted, but you are forever paralysed!

And so on and so forth.
