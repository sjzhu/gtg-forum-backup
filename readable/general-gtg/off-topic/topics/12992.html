That was a lot of fun!

I have read Daredevil, not all, probably not a lot, but enough and the series I just watched felt a lot like the more recent comics, and that was goooooood.

I actually liked the characters, I can't think of a single one that felt like a waste or was acted badly, not even the bit parts.

There were character deaths that actually mattered, huzzah for that (even if I didn't want them to die) and (until the end) scenes showing Fisk as not just a stereotypical bad guy from the comics, and very little over-the-top comic book influence.

So many nods to what has come before though, not invasive but prominent enough to be noticed by those looking.

 

Throughout the whole of the series there were only two bum notes for me, the cliffhanger ending to the 1st of the of 2-part Russian brothers' episode (why the hell do you need a cliff hanger in a show that has no schedule or advertising?) and the final fight with Fisk. All the other fights had impact when fist met body, this just sounded like wet noodles in comparison.

 

For next season the only thing I want is more interaction between Foggy and Daredevil (as opposed to Matt), I want to see a more open relationship between them, not having to sneak around behind Karen's back, that usually reduces itself to farce far to quickly.

 

EDIT: Actually, next season I'd like NO Fisk, move on to a completely different thing, and then third season do an adaptation of Brubaker's Daredevil in prison arc, with Fisk inside at the sametime, I think that would be a lot of fun.
