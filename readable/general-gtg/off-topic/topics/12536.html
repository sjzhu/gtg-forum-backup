I can help out with some shops...

Forbidden Planet Megastore on Shaftesbury Avenue for geeky toys, comic books, books etc.

Orbital Comics on Great Newport Street or Mega City Comics on Inverness Street in Camden for comic books and graphic novels.

Orcs Nest on Earlham Street for a boardgames and RPG quick fix, or Leisure Games on Ballads Lane in Finchley more of the same- definitely worth it if you can make it there.

 

I also recommend the Science Museum and/or British Museum. They both very geeky in their own ways.

Ahh, if you want an unusual museum that is kid friendly then you absolutely can't go wrong with the Horniman Museum. Greenwich is OK if you are interested in the maritime museum, otherwise there is a steep walk up a hill to the meridian line that is worth taking but it gets ludicrously crowded up there. The best thing about Greenwich is the view which is quite spectacular but not as awesome as you'll get from either the Shard or the Eye. Also, you get to ride on the driverless Docklands light rail which snakes between all the swanky skyscrapers to get there. Oh yeah, there is also this really cool building somewhere around the university complex that has the most amazing interior decor. I can't remember the name of it but it's easy to find as it's in the Christopher Wren buildings by the riverside.

If you like late medieval/early modern British history, Hampton Court Palace is absolutely top-notch. It is about a half-hour train ride away from London, and is one of the best museums I've ever visited. It is much less touristy than something like The Tower, and whoever is the currator has done an amazing job.

If you like theater and will be there in late April or after, go see a Shakespeare play at The Globe in Southwark. It looks like they have The Merchant of Venice and Romeo and Juliet this spring.

If you like British pubs and cask ale, I recommend my favorite pub in London, Ye Olde Mitre. It is a tiny pub built in 1547 and tucked away into an alley that makes it genuinely difficult to find, even with GPS directions. The beer will be fantastic, they will have pub food-type snacks, and there will be no tourists. It is only open on weekdays, and gets quite busy in the evening.
