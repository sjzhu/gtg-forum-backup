I've played Heroes of Metro City.  It has some great ideas, but some things I find to be poor implementations.  For example, the card Tactical Teleport shoots itself in the foot, making it less than useful.  (It keeps resource cards in your deck for one part and wants power cards in your deck for the second, making the first part bloat the deck so the second part is nigh useless)  I tried many games to get it to be useful, to no avail.  There are other cards that are just likely to have much better options.  And the fact that the villains and the other players are all fighting each other for cards means if the heroes don't plan things out in the beginning, they lose.   It's an alright game.  It just needs a bit more work IMO.

Blue Kae – We're going to meet in person at Comic Carnival tomorrow, right? The designers of Heroes Wanted have regularly playtested it at Game Preserve Fashion Mall Commons at a monthly game designers meet up. It is quite good in my opinion. The part about joining the top and bottom is a bit of fun, but it's mostly about replayability, as you get a far wider range of heroes to play. Yes, from a theme perspective, and particuarly from a story perspective, it is much weaker. Heroes Wanted is nowhere near like the awesome story/theme of Sentinels of the Multiverse, BUT the gameplay itself is very cool – small group tactics with constant interesting choices about using resources now versus later, taking risks or not taking risks, scoring points in different ways, all while determining the best strategy against your particular adversaries and against this particular villain.
