One of my good friends, Dave Fooden, recently launched a Kickstarter about being a barber in the great white north! Your job is to help lumberjacks find the perfect beard, and bears with their perfect hairdo. It's a fun, lightweight game (fits in pretty nicely with DHM's Rabbit line of games or with the announced Jellybean games so far) and takes between 15 and 20 minutes to play.

I helped him to playtest it over the years and I'm excited to finally see it launch. If any of the above sounds good to you, take a look at the link below!
