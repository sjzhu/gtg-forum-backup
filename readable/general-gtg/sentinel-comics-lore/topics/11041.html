Money is grand. However, there are other fantastic motivators in the universe of Sentinels.
 
 
Proletariat is driven by a desire to serve "the people." He may be speaking of a greater audience than himself. He may actually be fighting to bring balance to the world.
 
 
Citizen Dawn is collecting the crystals/shards. Who says she is working in opposition to the figure? Backstabbing and misinformation are very plausible among villains.
 
 
Ambuscade has a desire for the crystals. And, the Operative is much more complex than being motivated by simple greed.
 
 
These villains are complex characters, motivated by ideals and desires. Who says this mysterious shadow cannot provide something to all of them?
 
In the image on Foresight, we see shadow tendrils around The Seer's face. It's possible the figure is draining his powers. Maybe to give them to Ambuscade as payment for his aid.
 
The information thus far is VERY limited. There is a mystery here and we cannot simply assume that money is motivation enough for these characters.
 
The Operative has much more than money at her disposal with her new crew's control over Megalopolis crime. Ambuscade has the ability to destroy superheroes, money is a stepping stone--not his end goal. Proletariat is more of an anti-hero than a villain, he seeks an agenda much larger than financial success.
 
This shadowy figure could need the crystals for something that will distort the balance of powers in the game. Ambuscade wants power, the Operative has acquired power and likely seeks more, Proletariat wants "the people" to be strong and not be oppressed, and Citizen Dawn seeks the crystals with her meta-human family. It is possible, the shadow figure will use the crystals to nullify the powers of the heroes and grant great strength to the villains.
 
OR! It could be Cthulhu... NightMist draws in some great Lovecraft stuff. It could be a way to open a portal to some great tentacled monstrosities that rival the cosmic force in NightMist's visions from SotM...
 
OR! You guys are all right and it's just the president of Revocorp... But, that seems like a kind of a lame follow-up story to "The Mulitiverse is going to be destroyed by a massive cosmic force that's been screwing with the timeline and granting sentience to robot factories." 
 
Only time will tell.

3.  I think that Revenant is Setback's nemesis because Setback is a successfully augmented human and Revo Corp wants to reverse engineer him to make more.  They wanted the secret of Omnitron, and I think they have Voss as well, either as prisoner, subject or an important leader (depending on how Earth-centric they are).  The Lazarus Chambers would be of incredible value to them, esp. if they could reverse-engineer Blade's work.  Revo-Corp could very well be involved in Gloomweaver finding a host and Spite being reborn.
