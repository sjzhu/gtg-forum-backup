I feel that EE and DE SOTM are starting to turn into a Mortal Kombat situation. The game grows organically for a while, then it’s forcibly restarted and refined and has a much shorter lifespan with major expansions and upsets but also some missteps. MK then resets again, and SOTM might do so again. MK had seven primary releases prior to the reset, eight if you count the dubiously canonical MK vs DC, not counting several spin off games (Mythologies, Special Forces, Shaolin Monks, plus things like UMK3 and Gold). Sentinels EE also had four main releases, some mini expansions, compilation boxes that loosely equate to the UMK3 situation, and then after the reboot both universes get three releases. It’s a really interesting parallel.

The best parallel for Stuntman among real world actors is probably William Shatner. He was such a fan phenom and had so much ego about it, up until having a rude awakening which then led to him working to improve his treatment of others.
