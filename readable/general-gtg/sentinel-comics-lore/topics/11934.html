Hey everyone.  So, I'm no Sentinels lore junkie.  I've always wanted to get into the lore and understand it better, and I've been trying to piece together tidbits from the card art and flavor text, but it's been difficult finding absolute information out there to help me connect all the dots.  The wiki helps a lot with a few references, but I haven't found a good source for getting up to date with the lore or finding past info, other than going through backlogs upon backlogs of forum posts.

So I apologize in advance if any of this information has already been discovered or thought up.  I just got my Wrath of the Cosmos and have been reading through the cards; this is what I've found and what my thoughts are on the direction the Multiverse is headed.

Revelation 1: Captain Cosmic and Infinitor got their powers from Progeny.

On the Progeny description in the rulebook, it says that a cosmic object arced through space and drove into the heart of the planet Earth.  In Captain Cosmic's bio, the description says that he and his brother, stargazers, saw a "brilliant purple energy in the sky" (much like the purple aura and eyes of Progeny) which then crashed into the hillside.  Growing in its wake, they came across the crystals that would give them their power.  Notice as well, they described it as a brilliant purple energy; not a comet or a meteorite.  Being stargazers, they would be able to recognize what the object was, but instead, they call it a purple energy.

Revelation 2: Progeny is the precursor to Cosmic Entity's arrival (I know I'm pointing out the obvious here).

The word Progeny means a "descendant" essentially and we know that Progeny is a "creation" of its master.  It's entire purpose is to become the harbinger of destruction and prepare for its masters arrival.  I'd also assume that Progeny was sent to the earth years ago, but lay dormant beneath the surface for quite some time until "cosmic events transpired" which sparked it to life.  Whatever these "cosmic events" are, they are referenced many times through Deadline, but more on that later.

Revelation 3: The Enclave of the Endlings is an order/realm that protects and preserves the last of extinct species

This shouldn't be a surprise, considering the word Endling means "something of the last of its kind/species".  Their "leader" or mother figure, the Terminarch (another term for last of its kind), says that she has a soothing prescense to all of the endlings.  We also know that the Enclave protects these creatures from all dangers, including Time itself. It is a realm that exists outside of any threat and remains so in the name of preservation.  In the Hall of the Terminarch card, the flavor text says "Jansa vi Dero knew she would have a time with this young race, but not yet..."  as she talks with a young Legacy and Black Fist.  This could be either foresight i.e. the end is nigh and she knows of Cosmic Entity's arrival.  Or it could be her simply observing the innevitable; that eventually all species will become extinct at some point.

Revelation 4: Deadline is an endling, and one who has suffered the same fate as the one that seemingly approaches the Milky Way Galaxy and Earth.

It says that he notices the Cosmic Events circling around the Milky Way and instantly knows it's another extinction event.  Here are the interesting tidbits that have been drawing many questions:  For one, he seems to think that the destruction of the Planet (he's not directly killing people, but is focused on destroying the planet itself through extreme courses of nature) is what will slow, if not stop, the Cosmic Events.  Here are my thoughts:  Either (a) he knows that Progeny is hidden somewhere beneath the Earth's surface, lying dormant and awaiting its master's arrival, and his destruction of the Earth is to draw out or kill Progeny.  Or (b) Cosmic Entity is some sort of planet eater, like Galactus, a being that exists to consume planets and their energy.  Perhaps he thinks that destroying the planet will disuade Cosmic Entity from consuming it?  Either way, Deadline knows a lot more about it than we do.

Another interesting tidbit are the objects Deadline carries with thim.  There is a metal ring and a few different sized metal balls along with (gasp) a Crystal!  I'm wondering if this Crystal is a remnant of the destruction of his own planet.  It seems to possess amazing destructive abilities; he can summon hurricanes, raise mountains into the city, erupt volcanos, and even reverse the planet's polarity.  These crystals are definitely tied to the "end" that's to come, which he hints at in the flavor text.  "To save the multiverse, I have become my greatest fear."  He is using the very Crystal that helped destroy his own planet to pre-emptively end another planet.  He has literally become the destroyer that haunts him; the one that killed off his entire race.

One more object in question is the Pandemonium Key.  All we know is that there is a picture of the galaxy and some strange symbols on it.  The flavor text is from the Terminarch who says "The truths seen in the key are not yet fully true..."  Does this mean that the Pandemonium key is what allowed Deadline to see the "future" of what is going to happen to the Milky Way?  Also, does the quote from the Terminarch mean that this future is preventable?  That there is still time to stop what the Key seems to be predicting?

Interesting Connection 1: The Time Crazed Prisoner seems to know of these Cosmic Events; what else does he know?  What is F.I.L.T.E.R.'s role in all of this?

The following is written on the walls of the Time Crazed Prisoner's cell:  "No way out...it will be soon..."  "All will end as one"  "The future is gone"  "It sends its servant" "The Guards know" "Concentric Worlds"  "Each will fall" and of course "The Child is the center".

Frist off, "It sends its servant" is now an obvious reference to Progeny being sent by the Cosmic Entity to prepare the world for destruction.  It's only purpose is to serve and destroy.  Second, what does "The Guards know" mean?  The guards are agents of FILTER and we know that the nemesis of Progeny is KNYFE an agent (ex agent?) of FILTER.  There's a connection here that I'm not quite making.

Concentric Worlds is interesting as well.  Concentric means two circular objects that share the same center, and we have seen a lot of reference to the cosmic events "circling" the multiverse, as if following a pattern.  Last, The Child is the center could be referring to the Dreamer.  The shattering of the timelines (if I remember right) was caused by Visionary going back in time and essentially helping to change the future.  Could it be that the disruption of the Timelines is what is drawing Cosmic Entity to the milky way galaxy?

Interesting Connection 2: Multiple timelines means multiple ways for the world to end.

I think there are multiple ways the Multiverse can come to an end, and these are seen in Nightmist's cards.  The first isd Astral Premonition, which shows a silhouette of Apostate, surrounded by fire.  The text says "I will not allow you to ravage this world as you have others."  This points to Apostate being a possible cause of the end of the world (also as evidenced in his Apocalpyse card).

In the card Mists of Time, events seem to transpire in different ways.  The first panel shows Voss, but all gray with a stoney appearance, with glowing purples eyes.  This is him returning from the Mists he was trapped in with new found abilities perhaps?  Could he be another way the world is ended?  Also, we see a panel of the entire Earth with a giant Akash'Bhuta face essentially composed of the entire planet.  Another end could be Akash finally overcoming the Vituosso's attempt at subduing her and sending the entire world into chaos.  Last, we see a being composed entirely of the stars, reaching out for a planet.  I assume this is Cosmic Entity and is the way this current timeline is slated to end.

What does this all mean?

Well, I haven't the slightest.  Like I said, my knowledge of the lore isn't very deep, but I'm trying to understand it all better and need help connecting the dots.  I also would love for anyone to correct me on anything I said that was probably wrong.  But regardless here is my speculation:

The Final Boss will be Cosmic Entity.  His Nemesis?  Everyone.  All of the villains in the last SotM will be different villain decks for Cosmic Entity; think of this as a multiple part final battle that's drawn out over multiple phases, progressively getting harder and harder.

Also, I think a few Villains will be coming back as Promo cards, particularly Voss, Akash'Bhuta, and Apostate

I also don't really know what I'm talking about... so take this all with a grain of salt.

Some various thoughts:

If Infinitor is the time-crazed prisoner (which I find very likely), then everything he says probably has double (or triple) meanings. I also think that it is filtered through a VERY pessimistic worldview.  Remember- Infinitor spent countless hours floating with nothing but the whispers of oblivion and despair- so whatever knowledge he might have gleaned in the process of going mad, it is from a biased source!  Not only that, but I'm not sure his grasp on linear time is really there anymore- its all circles and it could be that he just sees everything (past, present, future, alternate futures...) as "now". 

"No way out", "it will be soon", "all will end as one" and "it sends its servant":  I think that these are references to his physical imprisonment (in that the Block is suspended in an alternate dimension), to the sealing of the multiverse (we know that the Visionary becomes trapped in Tactics, and that the multiple universes are sealed from one another), and that he has seen no ways to prevent what is to come (I think that part of what drove him insane was seeing future upon future that ended in oblivion- essentially, he got to see all the possible futures where everything ended, and no good ones). In terms of "the future is gone" and "it sends it servant", note how Progeny's cards are all about the hero's losing, and how none of them make it look like they succeed against him...

"The guards know": While I believe that this is a reference to FILTER, I could also see this as a reference to the Prime Wardens.  Both have a lot mor knowledge than we have seen- FILTER has their own agenda, and is playing their hand close so far.  However, I think that the Wardens have a LOT of untapped knowledge that they have yet to access (and since I think that Infinitor basically sees all times as "now", he might not differentiate between "The guards know" and "the guards will know" or "can know".  Think about what the Wardens represent:  Haka is an immortal, and quite possibly the first human to be touched by some otherworldly power (since we know very little about why he comes back from the dead stronger).  Tempest is one of the last Maerynians- and it seems that he may have a connection to Atlantis, or some elder race.  Fanatic is the bearer of angelic power, and has a connection to the as-yet-unexplained Host.  Captain Cosmic has traveled the starlanes and fought alien empires- even without the possibility that his contact with the crystal gave him untapped potential knowledge (The other side of the coin from Infinitor, perhaps?  Knowledge of futures where it could work out...), all of those alien technologies and sources of information may yet yield valuable insights about what is coming. Finally, we have the Argent Adept- and I'm pretty sure that the instruments each accrue knowledge from their owners as time goes one (I have this theory that this is why the Adepts seem to become disconnected over time- see the AA's treatment of the Natualist- the job itself leeches off their humanity until there is nothing left except the power and remnants of the Adept's knowledge and insights in the instrument, and then it moves on to the next host). So Its entirely possible that the Adept has some inklings about what is happening, or could have some inklings if he played the right tune on the right instrument. The Scholar and the Ley-Lines

For what we understand about all of the heroes, I'd say the person we know the least about is The Scholar.  Well, and maybe Guise, considering he just seems to make-up his entire backstory, but I think The Scholar has more content to speculate on.

So, we honestly don't know much about John Rhodes; he's just a guy who showed up some day and lives on a houseboat in a swamp, chilling and occasionally dishing out some noble wisdom.  But, we do know that he wields the mysterious Philosopher's Stone and that he's the only hero that doesn't have a Villain Nemesis yet.

Let's look at some parts of his bio:

John had always had an affinity for altering the very threads of existence. He could feel them as they stretched around him, connecting everything to everything else. So, when those lines drew him to a mysterious, glowing stone, he knew it was right and proper to take it with him. The stone allows him to twist the lines, rather than just perceive them, and he can now easily transmute objects into other materials.

I finally got a copy of Guise and Wager Master. Going to point out some things.

What do we know about our mysterious Cosmic Entity?

1) Created Cosmic Omnitron, because "it deserved another chance". Another chance at what? Survival? No, that would have involved taking it off Earth. Destroying the Earth? Then why not make it much more powerful? It made Progeny, after all. It enhanced Cap Cosmic and Infinitor. Surely it could give Cosmic Omnitron a place to exist without humans bungling around it, or strengthen it to a far more malicious threat. It moved Progeny cosmic distances, so it could move Omnitron.

2) Progeny's story is less clear. It came to Earth from the cosmos, but we don't know when, so we don't know how long it has waited here -- billions of years or weeks. It was also awoken. We don't know if the crash that created Cap Cosmic and Infinitor was Progeny arriving, or Progeny awakening and he was placed here long ago. The interesting thing to note here is that Progeny has two missions. Destroy is one. Prepare for the arrival of the progenitor which we presume is the CE is the second directive. Destruction isn't the preparing, so prepare how? All villain decks attempt to present a style commensurate with motives and abilities of the villain. La Capitan, the pirate, steals your stuff and makes you steal it back before you can hurt her. Infinitor the insane builds then destroys his own creations. Matriarch the self-centered gets angry if you destroy her flock of birds. But Progeny displays nothing I could point at to demonstrate the second motive. Progeny is the one Beat-em Up villain, which only matches the first directive. There is no hint of the preparation that should be vital.

3) Infinitor and Cap Cosmic stumble onto the crystal that either was created on Progeny's landing, or awakened him. One comes away good and sane, one evil and insane, but the evil one abandons Earth. He isn't given Progeny's directive, nor inspired to stay and fight as Omnitron did. The CE either created them, or they were incidentally created, but if incidental, the Ce doesn't seem to care much about conferring power to the locals it has reason to destroy.

4) Deadline sees the warnings of the CE's coming. What warnings? We can dismiss that question, actually. A suitably advanced science will appear as magic to a less advanced civilization. If we followed this train of thought, we might conclude that the CE shattered the timelines and detecting that brought Deadline, but as much as I like that, there was space to say it in Deadline's history blurb. Either he doesn't say... or he doesn't know. The important point is that he hasn't seen this in hundreds of years.

Now, get out Guise's history card. Who created him? "A cosmic entity known as Wager Master". Wager Master is a cosmic entity.

What does Wager master do? Check his own history. "Goes for centuries without showing up." matches Deadline's text. "Appears to a race of Sentient creatures." Again, matches Deadline. "Destroys everything as they inevitably lose its games." Happened to Deadline's world.

Everything can now slide into place. Wager Master is a candidate for Cosmic Entity number One.

Omnitron was given another chance because it was an amusing game to watch the locals play with a violent AI. But it was not given enough power to win, because that would have spoiled later games. It was not given its own place in the galaxy to start, because the conflict itself was the source of amusement.

Progeny is sent to destroy in order to swing the game in Wager Master's favor. WM is cheating to ensure he wins. He only cares about the Champions, not about the rank and file of humanity, who might get annoying, so genocide to get the ones incapableof playing good games out of his hair.

Captain Cosmic and Infinitor are opposites because games are easier to balance that way. And how amusing to a twisted gamer is putting two brothers at each others' throats? A cosmic game ending in mutual annihilation, just as the Wager master's reputation demands.

Guise is just a whimsical joke: another game with a poor sap that is not allowed to die, and someone to mess with the Heroes' sense of reason.

It is not 100% certain the CE is Wager Master, of course. There will not be enough information forthcoming for that. Wager Master's reputation as a destroyer might be unearned. Something may be driving him ahead of the real danger, possibly with WM not even knowing he is a herald of its coming.

There is one other card to consider. Partial Omni-Drone's fluff quote at the bottom. Omnitron-X does not remember fighting inside Omnitron-IV. The CE seems to be capable of editing itself out of memory. Deadline notices the signs of imminent destruction, but does not seem to identify the threat itself, nor feel inclined to name the threat to the local champions, nor consider training the locals how to combat what destroyed his world. He seems unable to forewarn about the threat, and instead takes the insane action of attempting partial destruction ahead of the CE's coming... when that is not far off Progeny's mission, so perhaps he does not remember the CE's agents as well. He is an Endling... are all the other Endlings from civilizations affected by the CE? Are all of them twsited into immortality as a grand cosmic joke? If the CE does edit memory, then Wager master would be remembered as the final event before destruction and gather credit for the destruction, when he is in fact the penultimate threat and himself unaware of his role of being at bat while the CE warms up.

So the next question would be, "So why are all these powerful people showing up?" The Parsons family appears to be unique in that it has the illusion of a genetic legacy that strengthens with each generation. Aata getting up and getting stronger as he is wounded. Psychics, good and evil, cropping up. ancient buried relics all unearthed in a short time and conferring power they may never have had in the past. Huge leaps in science creating beings of power. We have a CE coming. But is a third party already on Earth and prepared humanity to end the CE's rampage?
