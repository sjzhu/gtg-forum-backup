I like how, even though the whole character arc is planned from the very start, the way it's set out makes it really feel like in Sentinel Comics Publishing World she was just this complete throwaway villian of the week for an annual that was really about Absolute Zero where they added a family connection to Tachyon so ITS PERSONAL, and then because they did that they felt like they had to keep her around as a side character in Tachyon's stories, and then years later the Nightmist team decided to have her pick up an apprentice and decided Lillian would be a an interesting person to have as the apprentice. I've always found it interesting how there's this dual layer of sometimes things happen because it's the comics way of doing things and having characters fall by the wayside and back in even though in reality it's part of the plan since day 1. There's been a lot of effort put into the fictional "middle-man" world as well as the actual world in a way that feels very authentic.
