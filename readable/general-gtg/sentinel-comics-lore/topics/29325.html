PPPS: not to Americasplain the mythology of Japan, but my understanding is that yokai really aren’t anything close to demons, they’re more like what various D&D editions might call “monstrosity”, “magical beast”, or “monstrous human”, perhaps best translated as “creature”. The Japanese equivalent to “demon” as best I understand it is “bakemono”, so I find it odd that term isn’t the one they used for the thing that became Akari and which Akari now occasionally becomes under duress. (Legend of the Five Rings mostly uses “Oni” for demon while “bakemono” is treated as “monster”, but this is pretty clearly wrong, since the original mythical Oni were pretty specifically described as these tiger skinned giants with horns that are either red or blue…I think their closest equivalent in a western myth-cycle might be the Scandinavian trolls, which are likewise very definitely physical and humanoid, though they lean a little more nature-spirity. I think demon should pretty much always imply being “not of this world” in addition to fearsome or malevolent or otherwise monstrous.)
