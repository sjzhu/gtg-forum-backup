Thoughts On Ra:

I did a Footenotes piece on Beard Ra and what the historical context was behind the name. Long story short is that there is a death/resurection theme and a specific journy Ra takes into the underworld to die only to return reborn. Ra's initial defeat at the hands of the Ennead was a figurative death for him which sent him away for many years wandering the desert. Beard Ra was the resurection. A mucher stronger version of the Ra we previously knew. But what should interest everyone is the middle part that's missing, the details of the journey that led from the metaphoric death and resurection.

My current hypothesis on this journey is that like in the history books, our Ra also travels to the underworld (which we know exists in the Sentiels Universe thanks to the Tomb of Anubis environment deck. Anubis is seen with Ammit and the scales which weigh a pure heart). To become reborn into something more powerful than he was to save his friends Blake Washington likely had to give something up. A trade, if you will. Resurection is never without cost, a theme Christopher has played around with multiple times with Nightmist, Fixer, Operative, Pike, Spite, ect. The question becomes, "what did Blake have to give up?"

 

Thoughts on Dead Heros:

1. Blake Washington (Ra): I think this is the "gimmi" of the group.

2. Nightmist: The miststorm in Tactics and the Dark Watch bios do not make it sound like Nightmist made it out unscathed.

3. Scholar: The short sentence bio for the new Scholar of the Infinite promo is fairly damning of Johns fate. 

 

Now, Ronway might be correct that the quote wasn't that "3 heros will die from the card game" but that it's "3 characters from the base set will die". I do not remember the podcast well enough. But if he is, the list is as follows:

1. Blake Washington (Ra): Again, this is a "gimmi"

2. Voss: We know he was banished from this realm by Nightmist. Does that count as a death? Not sure, but it's the closest thing we have that we know about from the base set.

3. Haka: The only character from the base set to never be mentioned or seen in any current Tactics art/flavor text. If Ronway is right about the nature of the quote, then this will have to be true as a confirmed death (but again, I'm not sold that it is)
