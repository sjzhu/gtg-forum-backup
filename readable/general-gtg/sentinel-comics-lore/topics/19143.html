He keeps telling people trying to drag him off state that no, no, he's just fine, see, the bullet barely touched him, let him show you... 

So in real-life physics, nothing can exceed the speed of light. One of the physical manifestations of that fact is that it takes infinite energy to push an object with mass up to the speed of light. At low speed, putting 1 unit of energy into an object increases its speed by 1 unit - but as speed builds up, it takes more than 1 unit of energy to add just 1 unit of speed. This provides an easy explanation to why Tachyon has no fundamental speed limit, but going too fast would kill her - she'd burn herself up for energy before getting near the speed of light.
