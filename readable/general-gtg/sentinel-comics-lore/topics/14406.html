Heres a few of the old and new plot threads that have been rattling around in my head as we all prep for Oblivaeon.

 

1. The Fate of Fyodor Ramovat:

If you read Baron Blade's bio, you'll notice that it never explicitly states that (Grandpa) Legacy kills Blade's father. The actual words are:

"Weakened by the fighting, the factory collapsed in on itself, and Legacy was the only one seen escaping from the burning wreckage"

Now, I tend to think that Christopher chooses his words carefully, and thats pretty telling.  Especially since earlier on in the bio, it was revealed that Fyodor had just finished work on an orbital shrink ray!  Its a very interesting idea to me that Fyodor was shrunk (willingly or unwillingly) and taken prisoner, where his mechanical genius could be used in service of the USA (remember- it was the height of the cold war).  We know that in our history, scientists were prized at the time- think about Operation Paperclip- so to gain the technical expertise of one of their greatest minds while denying them the same would be quite a coup.  And again... this is Legacy we're talking about.  Sure, Grandpa Legacy was a soldier in WW2, and carried a gun...but would he kill an enemy trapped in a burning command center?

I kind of think that Fyodor is currently at FILTER, designing their tech, or Fort Adamant working on the bunker project (remember- one of his greatest successes was making a walking tank), and that when Baron Blade killed Grandpa Legacy, he killed the only person who could have told him the truth.

 

2. Revenant

I've been stuck on this one for a while. Who IS this mysterious person?

I asked Christopher, and he said "Which Revenant"?  

My current theory is that their are two Revenants- one during the events of SotM, and a different one during the events of Sentinel Tactics.

Revenant 1.0: I'm pretty sure that the Revenant we see doing things is SotM is the Idealist's father, or what's left of him.  We know that the Idealist's mother was trying to bring him back from the dead, and that in the process, Miranda Fischer's mind was wiped. Well, that kind of superscience and cavalier disregard for safety sounds EXACTlY like Revocorp.  They are suspects in ALL sorts of things- the facility that gave Setback his strength (and killed lots of people in the process), Plague Rat's "taming" (which killed lots of people in the process), Ambuscade's powers (which scarred him "horribly" in the process, and he then killed lots of people).  Its entirely likely that they were involved in making Fright Train bigger and badder.   

Now, he's back from the dead, but maybe not quite all the way.  Its entirely possible that he needs the suit to survive- either because he's a cloud of infernal energy (much as doctor medico is an energy form), or because the body he came back to is slightly less than fresh.  Does he remember he has a daughter? 

 

Revenant 2.0: I'm pretty darn sure that the Revenant we see in Tactics (in silhouette) is the Idealist. I think that she went bad.  Maybe she thinks she's doing it for the right reasons, maybe (prior to the mindwipe) she wasn't a terribly nice person.  Maybe she's trying to harness the power of the Oblivion Shards to get Dr. Medico, Writhe, and Mainstay back from the dead.  Maybe the new Revenant is just an evil version- all of her positivity went into her physical form, and the bad side got shuffled off.  

 

3. Maia and Tyler:

They are totally dating. In the plot arcs revealed by the Oblivaeon ARG, they sure seemed awfully flirty.  

According to Christopher, "Maia and Tyler definitely have things going on", which could be taken several different ways. I prefer to think that it means they are dating. It could also explain why Freedom Six Wraith went so dark- her first boyfriend getting beaten caused her to become the Wraith- think about what it would mean if another one died while she WAS the Wraith?  That might cause her to take steps like getting rid of the Chairman and Operative, and sampling some of the drugs they were using in an effort to transcend her human limitations...

 

4. Chokepoint and Writhe:

We know that Eugene Wilkinson initially produced the cloak by way of techology, and in the "Assault on Fort Adamant" section of the ARG, it specifically states that Writhe used his "shadow tech" to gather the intel and upload it.   We know that Chokepoint is all about absorbing tech.  Why couldn't she absorb Writhe's tech?

We've seen a back, spiky, fanged thing standing over Writhe and attacking Setback.  It could be related, and several other people like Foote have compiled excellent threads on the subject.  Maybe Chokepoint couldn't take it because it was alive.  This may well be additional proof that the cloak is more of a living thing than even Writhe suspects.

About Unleased Visionary...

So Visionary finally detached herself from her Darker self, a dark passenger she caught while travelling between time and space. She now has full control of herself. But where did this dark passenger go...?

On the Genocidal page of the ARG, we see Dark Visionary flying beside Legacy. She teleports away. On the Scion's Aid page of the ARG, normal Visionary is seen in the lower left corner. The Scion talks about how OblivAeon found him(her?) beyond space and time, how it was offered a collar. It is also important to note how intimately the Scion seems to know our heroes.

Ok, theory time:

Dark Visionary is the Scion seen in the Scion's Aid page of the ARG. A few evidences:

-Dark Visionary is present at the beginning of the OblivAeon event, but it is normal Visionary who is in charge toward the end of the events. As powerful as she is (banishing Gloomweaver with nothing else than a dismissive gesture is... impressive to say the least...) why would Dark Visionary give up at that precise moment? Which leads to...

-Why would Dark Visionary teleport away the moment it appears that OblivAeon is coming? Perhaps she knows how to free herself from Visionary and chose this moment to take action by herself?

-Some grammatical clues here and there... Dark Visionary was lingering beyond time and space when she hoped on Visionary's back for a ride. The Scion tells the heroes that OblivAeon found her beyond time and space. Coincidence? Maybe... Also:

-OblivAeon offered the Scion a collar. Could this collar be Visionary herself? After all, Dark Visionary seems to be tied to Visionary in some ways and it looks like she cannot fully control Visionary...

-Dark Visionary, from her bio, is said to be a version of Vanessa who made very different choices. Being an herald of a cosmic entity wishing to destroy everything seems enough radically different than trying to save the world. Maybe that's just me...

-We know that Dark Visionary remembers the heroes when she takes over Visionary's body in the fight against Gloomweaver. That could explain why she seems to know personally every heroes already.

-''Sweet child''. This is how the Scion answers to the Visionary when she enquires why they should help her. It does sound intimate to me, at least...

-Last but not least: Fixed Point. Vanessa Long has to exist in every timelines. If she doesn't, then she couldn't become the powerful Scion who aided defeat the Multiverse ultimate foe...

 

I might be forgetting some things...

Just a theory...
