Since the topic of costumes has come up various times as of late in the podcast and streams, what are your thoughts on the various outfits our heroes and villains wear in the Sentinelsverse?

A few of the ones I particularly like:

I love that they came up with a way for Legacy to look patriotic without the same stars n' stripes motif most America patriotic characters wear. The idea of using Paul Revere's lantern is both unique and goes with Legacy's gift for leadership and hope.

I also love Grandpa Legacy's superheroic WWII uniform look.

Super-Scientific Tachyon's outfit makes her look like a true adventurer scientist.

Scholar of the Infinite also has a very sharply stylish adventuring look to him.

Captain Cosmic's Prime Wardens style makes him look like we should call him Admiral Cosmic. Love that snappy jacket.

I feel like in Tactics Argent Adept's finally found an outfit that's just the right combo of stylized and practical; love the scarf especially. Also it gives him a sort of desert adventurer look.

Over on the villains side, I want to shop wherever Matriarch shops for clothes. I've always loved the classy Goth look.

Baron Blade looks great in the RPG. Like the platonic ideal of an aristocratic mad scientist.

And The Operative looks gorgeous in her green/blue minidress getup in Tactics.
