Not at all. It's a fact of life that the loss of an eye comes witht the loss of a great deal of depth perception. There is a reason so many species have two or more eyes, having only one can be a serious hinderance, especially for a species designed to have two. Our brains are wired to recieve input from two eyes, so that we can tell not just what something looks like, but how far away it is, how fast it is moving, etc. I would think that having only one eye would pose a serious challenge to a master marksman. Contrary to what film and comic books would tell us (looking at you Nick Fury), losing an eye does have consequences besides making you look like a badass. In real life, it can seriously impair us. The fact that Expattriete does what she does despite this handicap makes her all the more impressive. I just thought her level of skill might actually be somewhat superhuman rather than just exceptional.

Binocular depth perception is actually not as important as you'd think. There are a whole lot of other cues your brain can use to determine how far away something is, and you can learn to compensate for the lack of an eye and be (almost) as good at depth perception as you were before.

I have no experience at all with guns but I've shot with a shortbow before, and only the eye on your dominant side is used for sighting. I'm actually right-handed but slightly left-eyed, and I had to overcome this and learn to ignore my left eye. I assume that shooting a pistol or rifle or whatever is the same - if you were to look at the sight with both eyes you'd get a double image of the target, and vice versa.

I reckon Expatriette would have no trouble at all shooting with one eye. She'd have no problem sighting, and presumably she learned to shoot instinctively (i.e. "from the hip" or without sighting) before she lost the eye so that ability's still there. Even if it isn't, it's absolutely learn-able, especially to someone who's naturally gifted as she appears to be.
