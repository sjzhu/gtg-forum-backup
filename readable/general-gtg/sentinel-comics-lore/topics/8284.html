On previous expansions, the heroes have appeared fighting the villains, heroes on the left and villains on the right. On this expansion, all figures on the box stand together, presenting a united front. They look very much like they're all working together. Plus, like you say, several are people we thought were villains, and some of them ooze evil. This is, after all, the 'villain' expansion, so the villains really do take centre stage.

It was stated by Christopher (before a second environment was added to the game) that Vengeance would feature '7 or 11' decks, depending on how you counted. This does really strongly imply that the Vengeful Five are the only villains. Furthermore, as it has been stated on many occasions that Vengeance felt like a whole different game, it would be a little strange to then turn around and put normal villains into the expansion as well.

The whole point of Vengeance is that Blade is gathering together all these villains who have grudges against the heroes. Given Blade's MO (hiding away and forcing the heroes to fight their way to him), having a deck filled with minions would not be surprising. Plus, those people we think are villains have would-be nemeses who themselves already have nemeses, but they could be minion-nemeses, which we've seen happen before.
