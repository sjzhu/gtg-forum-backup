Oh boy! Time to get some errata ( and hopefully answers ) for the SCRPG!

Format for my notes

Timestamp:

  • Q:
  • A:

15:50

  • The Auburn Audience asks:
    – Q: If an ability doesn’t specify a location, does the ability only work in that location, or does it work across the scene? Does that include if the power used makes sense to be used across locations?
    – A (from Dave): It can work across the scene. The higher the die size, the more powerful and flexible it is. It was left purposefully vague so that it can be determined by the narration of a hero.
    – A (From Chistopher): The game in general only cares about location of things if the story and scene cares about locations.
    – Q: If there is a conflict between the players that comes up about how an ability is worded, and therefore how it functions, does the GM have final say about how it works?
    – A (From Christopher): If you’re not sure how something works, discuss it at the table. Given the collaborative nature of the game, just make sure that everyone is on board with how the game is being played.
    – Q: Would you prefer the GMs interpret Rules As Written for their table, or focus on Rules As Intended?
    – A: Focus on Rules As Intended, but to a greater degree, use how they work at your table. They are also hoping to put up an Errata and Rules page on the Greater Than Games website, and it is currently being put together (still a few months out).
    – Q: When a Reality Shaper uses Never Happened.

Never Happened (R): When a nearby enemy would create a bonus or penalty, you may remove it immediately.

– Q (Continued): The players are wondering if this can stop a bonus or penalty would be applied to multiple targets with a single use of the ability. Does it negate all of the modifiers, or just one? Can you use multiple reactions to stop multiple modifiers?
– A (From Chistopher): It will only affect one mod, not multiple, with a single use. With multiple reactions, you could use it multiple times to cancel out multiple modifiers.

Apparently Dave has the power to fire Christopher?

  • Steve Kaylor Asks:
    – Q: Do minions summoned by a hero still act when that hero is OUT?
    – A (From Dave): It depends on the narrative defining what the minions are.
    – Q: Is there a limit to how many minions a hero can create or control?
    – A (From Dave): There is no limit.
    – Q: Summoned Allies and similar abilities state that you chose which basic actions the minions can take. Are you required to choose the targets at summoning as well? What if they were set to Attack, but the only targets remaining are friendly?
    – A: The creator of the minions chooses who they target each turn, but cannot choose to not take a basic actions on their turn.

  • Brian Jewet Asks:
    – Q: The Armored Ability reduces physical and energy damage.

Armored (I): Reduce physical and energy damage taken by 1 in the Green Zone, 2 in the Yellow Zone, and 3 in the Red Zone.

– Q (continued): How is energy damage defined? Does it include things like a bolt of lightning or heat beam?
– A (From Dave): If it’s damage could be described using an Elemental/Energy power, then it’s energy damage. Generally, it’s meant to be non-physical things, non-mental things.
– A (From Adam): Figure out what energy types your character can work with. Elemental/Energy types to damage are intended to be picked out by the player when characters are generated.
– Q: The Frontline Fighting ability.

Frontline Fighting (A): Attack using [Quality]. The target of that Attack must take an Attack action against you as its next turn, if possible.

– Q (Continued): Does this mean they must only attack you, or does it count if you are included a multiple target attack?
– A: It counts if you are included in a multiple target attack.
– Q: Galvanize and several other abilities provide a bonus to every hero taking a certain kind of action. Flowing Fight allows you to make an extra attack for each bonus you have. Does the bonus from Galvanize count as a bonus you have?
– A: Yes. It is one bonus for purposes of eliminating one bonus. Unlike other bonuses, the Galvanize bonus would still apply to all attacks made as part of Flowing Fight.

  • The Auburn Audience Asks:
    – Q: The Red Ability Push Your Limits.

Push Your Limits (I): You have no limit on amount of Reactions you can take. Each time you use a Reaction after the first one each turn, take 1 irreducible damage or take a minor twist.

– Q (continued): Does this mean you get your normal reaction at the start of your turn, an additional one at the start of each other actors turn, and then even more at the cost of damage and/or twists? Or was it not intended to give you an additional free reaction each turn.
– A: You get one free reaction as normal, but all reactions beyond that free one require you to take damage or a minor twist.
– Q: Is adding an extra d10 or d12 Lieutenant a good way to represent an extra hero?
– A: Whenever you need to include an NPC hero or ally in the scene, represent them as a Lieutenant.

  • catdreaming asks:
    – Q: If you have Push Your Limits and Untouchable

Untouchable (I): When you would be dealt damage, roll a d4 in the Green Zone, a d6 in the Yellow Zone, or a d8 in the Red Zone. Reduce the damage taken by the value rolled. Attack another target using that roll.

Can you react to the damage dealt to yourself from Push Your Limits with Untouchable, triggering a chain of reactions to unload a great deal of damage? Also, is Untouchable purposefully the only reaction that reduces damage rather than taking the Defend action?
– A: It doesn’t work. Irreducible damage does not interact with Defends, and cannot trigger reactions to Defend. Untouchable does stack with Defends.

  • Twitson Asks:
    – Q: Can an Action ability that let’s you remove a penalty be used to remove the penalty before it would affect your rolling?
    – A: No. You always do the ability text in the order it is written.

  • Bartkf Asks:
    – Q: What counts as physical damage?
    – A: Materials powers, physical things that effect your physical body.

  • Axel Arcos Asks:
    – Q: Does the bonus made from By My Command apply to all rolls made by minions?

By My Command (A): Boost using [quality] for all your minions until the start of your next turn.

– A: Yes. There are three durations for Boosts and Hinders: Single-use, until next turn, and Persistent. Bonuses with the ‘until next turn’ duration are not used up while they remain.
