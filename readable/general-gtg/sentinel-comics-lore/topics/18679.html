In a sense, he created both.  Yes, the one with the time rewind he initiated is the Sentinel Comics universe.  But because he caused a fork, OblivAeon was in only one of the two.  Without Setback, the canon universe would have been one in which Rook City was destroyed but OblivAeon was defeated.  Because the heroes succeeded in that timeline, super-powered individuals wouldn't be on the outs with the general population, and because Voss would have been there, the Mist Storm wouldn't have been possible.  So it would have been somewhat darker than the Comics Universe timeline, but much less dark than the Mist Storm one.

But yes, the simple answer is that his action led to what happened in the RPG timeline, and the one in which he didn't act was the Mist Storm timeline.
