  • Super-strength or FISS (flying/invulnerability/strength/speed)
  • Speedster
  • Blaster (energy attacks)
  • "Normal" (martial artist, guns, gadgets, etc.)
  • Mystic/psychic
  • Suit (Bunker, Benchmark, etc.)
I know that's 6, but I'm not confident enough to narrow it down to 5.  And I know mystic and psychic aren't really the same, but you don't usually see both at once.  There are lots of other superhero archetypes, but they're less common and more niche.  Oh, and I'm betting exactly one will be nonhuman, probably an alien.
