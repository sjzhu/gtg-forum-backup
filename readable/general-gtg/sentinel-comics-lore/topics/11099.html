Here's a v1.0 prototype and some thoughts:

1) I shrank the template by 50% because I didn't want it to take up as much table space as regular-sized dice towers.

2) Because of the smaller size, that means it can't handle a gigantic handful of dice at a time, but as you can see from the pic, it can handle up to 8 dice pretty comfortably.  Given that you're usually rolling between 3 and 6 dice in Tactics, I think that should be acceptable.

3) Also because of the smaller size, I took out one of the internal ramps.  Dice towers usually have the dice bounce off of two ramps before hitting the final ramp at the bottom.  I'm thinking two total ramps inside should be sufficient.

4) I'm going to change the exit arch from an arch to a hex, because that'd be cooler.

5) I am seriously considering getting rid of the ramparts at the top and going with something simpler.  The ramparts do make it look like a little medieval tower, which is cute, but it unnecessarily complicates the construction process, so if I can get to something I like without the ramparts, I will.

Anyone have any feedback I should incorporate into the design?  I'd hate to end up with something people won't use because I missed something important.
