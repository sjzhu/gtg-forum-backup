Interlude episode just released explains that Kismet does exist in the history of Tactics as the RPG and Tactics have the same SotM background.  However, what they meant by no Kismet is that she hasn't been seen in the Tactics storyline since leaving the Block.   An additional piece is that the Block exists only in the Tactics timeline due to the actions of Kismet when she left and it can travel anywhere in the Tactics timeline but not anywhen like it used to.   So in the RPG timeline the Block is no longer there.
