Welcome to another entry in my Footenotes series. Hope you brought your reading glasses.

Nightmist's deck holds so many bits and pieces of future content. We have picked a lot of them out, but everytime I revist her deck, something new jumps out at me. And then my head starts having thoughts (scarry I know).

Out of all of the villains and heros we have been introduced to through SotM, we know the least about Apostate. Seriously, we know a great deal about every other character except him. We dont know what he is, who he is, where he came from, why he has a sister sword to Fanatics, what his connection to Fanatic is as a whole...we know basically nothing. His bio is unhelpful, being all of a few sentenses and half of them deal with Fanatic going into hiding pre-redeemer status.

There a very important question here. Why. Why do we know so little about him? Why did Christopher choose to present him in this way while all the other villains get cool backstories and have all the fun.The only answer that makes sense to me is that all the answers to the questions above are somehow very important to the conclusion of the of the major narrative.

"It sends its servant" - Time Crazed Prisoner

Could Apostate be the servant of the Cosmic Entity and ultimate bad guy?

Consider the following. Nightmists deck contains a card call Astral Premonition. Flavor Text: "I shall not allow you to ravage this world as you have others". Others? He has brought chaos to other worlds as well?  Going back to the picture of Time Crazed Prisoner, there is a picture of orbit paths with big X's over the planet on each orbit. The name contains "Astral", so an astral premonition could be directly interpreted as getting a bad feeling from the stars (or outter space in general).

Oh yeah, lets also not forget that his front side is called Infernal Emissary. 

Emissary: Noun.

       1. a person who is sent on a mission to represent another person or organization

Man, Christopher kinda spoon fed us that under our noses! So is this person/organization that he is representing the Cosmic Entity? Take a look at his scant bio one more time. Group Affiliation: The Host. That sound familiar to you? Good, me neither. But the picture is starting to come together.

https://greaterthangames.com/comment/56932#comment-56932

Christopher putting "Demons" in quotes like that is more than likely significant to all of this in some way. Previously refered to them as nega-demons in the same thread. Putting both of those together, the conclusion is that these things are not Demons, at least not in the traditional sense of Heaven/Hell/Angel/Demon dynamic. They are something else all together.

The back art of Apostate's deck is incredibly strange and I am surprised it has not come up more on the forums. The thing in the center seems to be a planet on fire, at least thats how I'm seeing it now. The two females(?) bracketing that destruction though is quite a puzzle. Could one symbolise Fanatic, and if so, does she have a twin sister somewhere? That might even be too far a stretch for me. So lets stretch it further but in a different direction.

https://greaterthangames.com/forum/topic/footenotes-the-matriarch-and-the-red-herring-4239

This thread has a bunch of great stuff from a lot of different people. But one of the threads of thought that I really enjoyed thinking about was the loose connection we were making between Matriarch, the Cosmic Entity, and the Irish Morigan mythos.  The Morigan, just to quickly get to the point of this, is commonly depicted as a trio of sisters.

Well, this duo of sisters is rather interesting looking at it through that lense. Going back to my original thought there, maybe the one IS supposed to be Fanatic. Maybe the other is the Matriarch? Why Matriarch? Well, if any of my broad stroke conclusions from both of these threads is anywhere close to the truth, then Matriarch is very much tied in with the Cosmic Entity in a significant way. Now since it looks like Apostate is also the herald of the comming Appocalypse, both he and Matriarch could be connected in a significant way as well.

The Time Crazed Prisoner speaks of 'All will end as one' or something to that effect. "The Host". What if this Cosmic Entity is going from world to world trying to incorporate it into his own "realm" of existence? This could be the whole reason why the timelines and different universes are bleeding together, as a way to condense everything down before being absorbed/eaten/whatever it is it does. In a sense, he "hosts" all these worlds in his own realm to rule over and do as he pleases (or whatever motivation an all powerful cosmic dude might have). Re-reading KNYFE's bio, FILTERs interest in a particular set of realities is troubling. It seems to hint that there is one timeline that would allow this Cosmic Entity/Event/Whatever to consume the rest. FILTER doesn't mess with timelines "ending or begining", but KNYFE knows thats a lie. We are talking about possibly them aiding the Ending to all timelines, which would itself be the begining of a true singular universe. Why would this be good for FILTER? No idea, but considering the very nature of the Block and its inhabiting the gray areas of space/time, I would think they have the means to evade the fate of the rest of the multiverse. 

The bottom left corner of Time Crazed Prisoner shows one line that branches off to 2, which branches off to 4, which branches to 8, ect ect. This is pretty much how timelines get born in a sense, one action can yeild different outcomes depending on the factors of Fate and Chance (Nightmist brings these up as essential elements in her decks flavor text). Those all branch into unending possibilities and realities (ever see the end of Bio-Shock Infinite?). The Cosmic Entity, or whatever Cosmic Event that has KNYFE so worried, might have the reverse effect?
