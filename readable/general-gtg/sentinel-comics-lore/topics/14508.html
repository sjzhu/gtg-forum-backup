Reading Foote's excellent thread on the Void got me thinking about Fort Adamant.  Recent materials have really pushed it as both creepy and important.  What's the deal there?  I took a look at everthing we know surrounding that event, some time shortly after Progeny's assault.  Here's what I was able to reconstruct:

 

The fight against Progeny played out across Freedom Five #763-767.  We don't actually get the Southwest Sentinels numbering for that timeframe, but we do know that "after responding" to it, they spend several days in training (which is probably a montage taking a single issue or less), and then get attacked by Chokepoint across Southwest Sentinels #30-36.  Depending on whether the suspiciously low-numbered Southwest Sentinels came out monthly or bi-monthly, and whether there was an issue between Progeny's attack and Chokepoint's arrival, that puts us at Freedom Five somewhere from #774-783.  Wait a minute...

In Freedom Five #783, Unity built Omnitron-U, because she "finally" had what she needed, "plundered from the twisting back alleys of the Omnitron IV structure".  The finally part implies to me that this is a recent event.  And that's really interesting because of Termi-Nation's Incapacitated sides.  Zero is having his tech torturously stripped away by Chokepoint, of course (since he doesn't wear his suit as Termi-Nation AZ, maybe that's actually the "origin" of his new design?), but Unity and Bunker have a different problem.  Unty's card shows the creation of Golem Unity (whose skull-face is already hidden with "skin" here, but visible on Golem Unity's Incap side), whilst Bunker's has a piece of armor being examined by... Omni-Tron!?

So, Termi-Nation heads out to examine this shady research facility, which has hovertanks and glowy energy panels and so on.  They face threats from Omnitron, and someone builds an Omnitron-style skull-faced robot.  And within a few issues, a member of Termi-Nation has spare Omnitron parts.

Fort Adamant is Omnitron.  Maybe it literally transforms into Omnitron IV, maybe it was merely being run or manipulated by an Omnitron MCP.  But in either case, its true purpose was to gather powerful technology which 'Tron could use to rebuild lost power.
