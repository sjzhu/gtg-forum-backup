Greetings, fellow Multiverse savers!

I was doing a bit of research regarding our beloved heroes and the comics from which they come. Pouring over the flavor text of the hero text, I got multiple titles in the Sentinel Comics line, and think I put together some of their themes/storylines.

A Day in the Life - one-shots showcasing what our heroes average day looks like.
Absolution - Fanatic's origin
America's Finest Legacy - Legacy's line
Arcane Tales - fairly long Ra storyline (98 issues)
Baptism by Fire - Fanatic and Ra crossover
Battle Unending - Haka storyline
Burying the Blade - Side story where Baron Blade may be dead. (Lots of Blade Battalions. No Blade himself)
Cosmic Challenge - ??
Cosmic Concurrence - probably will know more with next expansion
Double-Tap - An Expatriette line
Draw! - Chrono-Ranger one-shot
Emigrant's Song - Expatriette either entering or leaving Insula Primalis
Engine Block Blues - Mr. Fixer story
Exordium - ??
Fabric of Depair - a Gloomweaver event?
Fanatic - Fanatic's main line
Freedom Five - At least 932 issues!! One of the main lines of Sentinel Comics
Freedom Five, Annual - 30 years of mega cross-overs, needing larger teams of heroes
Hero of the Arena - Parse??
Into the Sunset - Story involving the Citizens of the Sun 
Justice Comics - One of the main lines of Sentinel Comics, primarily featuring Legacy
Legacy Independence Day Spectacular
Lightspeed - Tachyon line
Mind over Matter - Visionary's main line?
Moonfall - Baron Blade tries to bring down the moon
Mother Earth One-shot - Probably a pre-issue for the Akash'buta event
Mystery Comics - One of the main lines of Sentinel Comics, primarily featuring the Wraith
New Memories - The Visionary's origin
Nightmare World - The Dreamer cross-over event
Nightmist - Nightmist's main line
One Man Army - A Bunker series
Out of Time - Short series featuring KNYFE
Prime Wardens - Adventures of this group
Ra: God of the Sun - Ra's main line
Rebirth of Ra One-shot - Ra's origin story
Rook City Renegades - The Chairmen cross-over event
Run of Luck - Setback's line?
Science & Progress One-shot - one shot of our science heroes
Singularity - Omnitron-X storyline, including a possible sacrifice (issue 8)
Southwest Sentinels - The Sentinels main line
Stranger in a Strange World - Tempest's come to Earth story
Sunrise - Citizen Dawn cross-ver event
Temporal Targets - Chrono-Ranger's line
Terminal Ballistics - Expatriette's line
Terminus - ??
The Curse - Nightmist's origin story, trained by someone called "The Master"
The Deadliest Game - Ambuscade targeting different heroes
The Final Legacy - Iron Legacy cross-over event
The Ice-Man Cometh - Absolute Zero's solo line?
The Indestructible Bunker - Bunker's main line
The Last Hunt - Chrono-Ranger hunts Plague Rat, who somehow makes it back to Rook City
The Savage Haka - Haka's main line
The Sentinels - probably a line for the Sentinels team, but main line seems to be Southwest Sentinels
Tides of Time - La Capitan's battle
Time and Again - Omnitron-X's line
Time Cataclysm - one-shot w/ Chrono-Ranger
Toll of Destiny - Argent Adept's origin story
Tomb of the Bizarre - Seems to be everywhere, but includes Naturalist's origin story (24-26)
Transmission of Honor - A Mr. Fixer storyline
Vagabond's Tale - Scholar's origin story
Vengeance - A six issue storyline detailed Baron Blade's Vengence (latest expansion)
Virtuoso of the Void - Argent Adept's main line

 

I've just poured through the hero decks as of right now. I plan on going through the villian decks as well. What do you all think? Am I missing anything?
