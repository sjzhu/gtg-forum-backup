Hello all!

I'd like others' opinions/thoughts on the idea of robots in Sentinel Comics. Other than the obvious Omnitron making drones and Baron Blade making the occasional robot for a single task (like self-impersonation), is there any other villains who heavily rely on, or even frequently use, robots for tasks or fighting? I can't think of any.

I ask because I have a hero idea that I'm carrying over from my old City of Heroes days. The concept was a hero who built a suit out of broken robots that heroes had smashed, thereby allowing her to be a hero too. In that game, the main design was based on the Clockwork robots, but I also later made costumes created from Mek Men and Jump-bots. So for SCRPG, I'd like to use robots based on existing in-setting lore, rather than creating everything from nothing.

My initial thought was to use smashed drones but I feel like Omnitron tech, even drones with little to no Omnitron code, would be under strict control and cleaned up pretty quickly. Please, correct me if I'm wrong.
