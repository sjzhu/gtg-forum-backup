Things we find out in Villains  Copy this quick before a mod deletes it!

Legacy didn't like peas until he was 22.

Unity has a pet Raptor Bot that she won't take into battle, she calls it Charlie, it loves to hide in Bunkers unused suits.

La Capitan for a short while went under the title Maria the Mariner, which she ditched after they traded Ken Griffey Jr.

Miss Information similarly played around with the name Innacurate Data, but no one took her menacing letters to the editor seriously.

Baron Blade keeps trying to destroy the Freedom Tower because, "It looks hideous, it doesn't fit the skyline at all, I mean doesn't Megalopolis have a city planners office?"

The Chairman disagrees with this assessment, and is actually a big fan of the tower's design.

Chrono Ranger isn't actually a time-traveller, he made up the story and pretends to talk to Con all the time to hide that he's listenning to Smooth Jazz during battles.

Expatriette doesn't like kittens, but she thinks Turtles are the cutest things ever.

Tachyon may be the fastest woman on Earth, but she's never placed higher than third at interplanetaries.

The government continues to refuse permits for Tachyon to build a big ramp that would let her build up speed and "Run to Mars", a decision Tachyon claims could save weeks off the time it takes to communicate with Wagner Mars Base.
