TakeWalker:

I have hated the name (to use my intuitive assumption of how it was spelled) Tyrannorak ever since it first came up. Baal’Tyrannotrak was better, but it still came across as an intentionally dumb and generic name for a weird alien tyrant monster. Despite also being a dumb joke, your comment has saved it. I’ll be spelling it Tyr’Anorak from now on. That’s so much better.

There’s an image somewhere “in comics”, I have no recollection of where exactly I saw it, but it was branded forever on my consciousness…the New Gods comics had mentioned a Source Wall, but in this comic we actually saw it, and there were all these godlike alien figures who had tried to rule the universe, failed in the attempt, and been mortared into the Source Wall as consequence (either sentenced by God as a punishment, or simply automatically getting absorbed by this thing they unsuccessfully grasped tried to control but couldn’t). I think Sentinel Comics also has a version of that, and the question of what realities do or do not split is somehow answered by that wall. I don’t quite remember the brainwave I had when I started writing here, but I think the half complete idea is enough, given what a vast cosmic unknown it relates to.

I want a VOTM deck for Tyr’Anorak, where he’s a non-target.
