It’s Gloomweaver’s throne, not the Realm of Discord’s throne. Whereas the Throne of Aeturnus is always that of Aeturnus, regardless of exactly who sits in it. You can take the position, but it’s not yours.

A part of the lore they’re not talking about at all, but which seems obviously important, is that Le Rheaume d’Iscorde is connected enough to all human minds that some people just go there during a nightmare. If Aeturnus took it over, they could much more directly access people in their sleep; they wouldn’t just experience random disorienting imagery that disturbs them after they wake up but is ultimately inconsequential. They’d probably have direct access to the human soul, and that could quickly result in Aeturnus taking the Central Plane. A similar problem exists with the Graey. Uniquely, Discord is the only realm that can have that dream connection without it being a huge problem, because humans have adapted to it. Chaos and madness don’t destroy us the way torment and apathy can.

Biomancer himself hasn’t been to Aeternus, but “Biomancer” certainly has. He’s definitely sent several fleshchildren there, some being of himself. The question is, if Aeturnus torments a fleshchild, are they actually suffering, or do they just think they are?
