So, I was just perusing the new bios I unlocked for Captain Cosmic and the other Prime Wardens when I came across this:

 

Captain Cosmic: Power Source: Cosmic Energy, The Potency half of an Oblivion Shard.

 

Never heard that before, so I went looking

 

Infinitor: Power Source: Cosmic Energy: Cosmic Energy, The Ego half of an Oblivion Shard

 

So, it seems that Cosmic and Infinitor each recieved only half the power of a single Oblivion Shard. That could be part of why Inifinitor was driven mad, he only had half the power, Cosmics power of potency is just the ability to affect the world, while the power of Ego indicates it was the part held in check by Cosmics power.

 

What else can I find...

 

Haka not only teaches the young but takes care of the old, which makes him even better. You know, I don't as much want to see a Haka comic of him fighting evil as I do seeing a "Day in the Life of Haka" where he startsteaching Kindergarten, walks Tempest's Dog with him as they talk about humanity, an then he goes and helps out at an Old Folk's home.... I bet one of Haka's stories would end up involving a person he taught as a child, saved as an adult, and then comforted as they passed.

 

Fanatic seems to have broken her sword fighting Apostate, but we see her fighitng Truth.... I wonder if in Fighting Apostate the Citizens including Truth interfered. That could be cool.

 

Tempest, a few things. It talks a lot about loss ( I wonder if each of the Prime Wardens is represented by an emotion, cause Haka talks a lot about Joy, and Fanatic I would bet purpose... I digress) and includes this "Even the loss of self control in the face of a terrible nemesis is a loss felt well by Tempest". So, at one time when fighting Voss Tempest losses control, probably ends up brutalizing Voss quite a bit or maybe even harming the gene-bound Maerynians.

 

It also talks about him and some other making an expidition to Atlantis, which is where he found the sword we see him with. It "would become an extension of the Maernian's power and will." "M'kk Dall'ton could channel command of the elements and ionized air along the length of the blade, and felt a deep connection to the blade."

 

The sword is obviously an artifact of some sort, but I don't know if I want to say it is a Maerynian artifact. Could be as simple as a blade wielded by someone with similiar powers... I don't know what to make of this. What were our conclusions on the sword before, I don't quite remember.
