One thing that sets Nightmist apart from AZ is while AZ has one way to recoup his HP, Nightmist has several -- the Starshield Amulet, Master of Magic and Mist-Fueled Recovery.

While having the Amulet out is her ideal state, she's perfectly playable and useful without it, thanks to those three. As the guide points out -- the only meaningful HP value is zero, so I'm perfectly comfortable using spells right off the bat in the first couple rounds until I see the Amulet or one of the above cards. I've had whole games where I didn't get Starshield, Amulet or MoM out (or couldn't keep them out), but a late-game MFR got me back in health to keep contributing.
