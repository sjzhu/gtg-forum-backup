The problem with sidekicks in SotM is that they're hard to do from a game perspective.  As a sidekick, they should logically be weaker than the regular heros, but that makes them less desirable as a playable character.  If you add them in as a second character card for the hero (like the Sentinels), then you have a card which probably has less health and is less useful the the main hero, and you get a problem like with Spite where it's not stragically sound to try and keep the sidekick alive.

I guess it would depend on what you mean by a sidekick.

 

If you simply want someone who admires the hero or helps from afar then that's one thing. For example, if memory serves Expat was a Bounty Hunter for a while (from her Bio I think) so she could logically have a younger individual running databases and grabbing files for her back at the home base (where would Expat stay actually?)

 

However, if you want someone to fight beside the hero... I doubt anyone would last long. The sentinels heroes seem to be more of the type to tell the kids to go home because it is too dangerous. Exceptions being Unity, Young Legacy and The Idealist because they are simply too powerful to actual stop them from tagging along if they choose to do so.

 

I just don't see many of them having the patience or disregard for allowing signifigantly weaker individuals to enter a battlefield with Spite, Gloomweaver, Apostate, Grand Warlord Voss, ect.
