I thought we could have a little fun but seeing who knows the most about the SotM multiverse. However, instead of me writing all the questions, I wanted to leave it open to the forum. If you have a multiple choice question you want to go on the quiz (three per forumite please), please fill out this form. As soon as we have 40 questions, I will create and post the quiz for people to take, and we can finally see who knows the most about SotM!

For the first quiz, in honor of the Release of SotM:TVG, all questions should be about the story (not mechanics) of the core game heroes, villains, and environments. Your source can be the cards (name, art, and flavor text), rulebook, online bio, or even something that Christopher said on the forums or on a podcast (I may ask for the source of the info if it isn't obvious).

So, let's get started!

EDIT: We only have a few questions in, but I like them, so I am going to open this up to any questions about any hero, villain, and environment. It can be about the story or mechanics.
