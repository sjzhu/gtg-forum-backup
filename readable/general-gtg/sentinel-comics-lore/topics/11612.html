The quote is "Dawn's ability to manipulate life forces has obvious combat applications" (it's on Luminous Leadership).  Mad Bomber Baron Blade's first appearance is FF #258 at the end of the Sunrise story-arc.  (As an aside, I've actually long suspected that the names of the comics listed are not the titles of the comics themselves, but of the story arcs, but that might be one of those things that I'm just the last person to realize.  The quote in the other thread from Christopher seems to confirm that.)  

Most, if not all, of the quotes from Citizens in Dawn's deck are from issues of Sunrise.  So let's assume that Sunrise starts in FF #239 and ends 20 issues later with the first apperance of Mad Bomber.  Twenty issues would certainly qualify as a "major story arc" and it makes sense because the quote from Channel the Eclipse sounds like a last stand ("WIll my powers kill me?  Certailnly, but they will surely kill you first!") comes from Sunrise #14.  The very next issue has a quote from Return with the Dawn ("Like the dawning sun, rise again and bring light to this darkened world!") which implies a mass second effort on the part of Dawn and Co. which could certainly last another several issues.

My point in all this is that I wondered why Tachyon had intel on Dawn and who she is sharing it with in that quote.  When I wrote my original post I was thinking that maybe Tachyon is giving a briefing because the F5 were planning a military action against Dawn.  This being before her first appearance, it would seem pretty diabolical for the F5 to attack her without motivation (presumably in Insula Primalis), unless Christopher was making a direct analogy to the real life search for WMDs in Iraq.  That seems unlikely so I've reconsidered:  What if Tachyon was briefing the rest of the F5 with intent to RECRUIT Dawn, and it wasn't until they got to Insula Primalis that they found she had already recruited her own followers and consolidated a power base.  She probably wouldn't be too welcoming to government interference in her plans...

OH!  I just noticed that Visionary has a quote in Dawn's deck too.  Maybe Dawn turned out to be a hero in Visionary's timeline and it was her knowledge of the future that provided the intel to Tachyon to seek out Dawn in the first place!  They could have been recruiting people for the inevitable attack of Iron Legacy or something.  Plus, IIRC, Citizens Truth and Dare are alumni of Project Cocoon.  Maybe Visionary knew of them from her timeline and thought she would find allies in them as well.
