I was trying to think of ways to represent solo battles (say, Legacy vs. Blade) and I think with heavy scenario modifications, it could work.  For example:

 

1) Start the hero stronger.  Have him start with some number of cards in play, perhaps.

2) Weaken the villain.  Only one of the Ennead, or Baron Blade is pre-flipped, or the Chairman doesn't have the Operative along.

3) Incapacitated heroes.  Fill out the team with additional heroes, but set them to incapacitated.  This represents the hero fighting stronger in the solo battle.

4) Extra base power. This is mostly to round out a hero, to give support characters a damage power ("Legacy does 2 melee to one opponent") or to give damage characters a self-support trick ("Fanatic is immune to the next melee damage she would receive")

 

Not sure how *fun* that game would be, but for scenarios about reenacting solo battles, it might work.
