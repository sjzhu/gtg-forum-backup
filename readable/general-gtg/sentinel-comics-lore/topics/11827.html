The art print for Halls of the Terminarch showed us some little details that were impossible to see on the card.

The nunchucks and, more importantly, the eye patch.

Mr. Fixer used to go by the name "Black Fist". A clear shout out to the blaxsploitation characters of the 1970's. The man with Legacy on HotT has an afro and bell bottoms. Staples of 1970s fashion. Nunchucks point to him being a martial artist and the eye patch says he has impaired vision. Mr Fixer is also a vision impaired martial artist. I think the connection here is solid. That is Black Fist standing with Legacy.

But the question I now have is - was Fixer always as blind as we see him in Rook City? If not, what caused him to wear that eye patch? How did he loose his eye? Will I have to wait until >G does the Letters Page podcasts to find out?
