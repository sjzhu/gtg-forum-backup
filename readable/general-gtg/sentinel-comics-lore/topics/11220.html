First off, welcome to the world of Sentinels!  Glad to have another person whom enjoys the game!  To answer your question, I think it is safe to say that we don't know, but more than likely when the card game ends, that will be the end of SotM in that form.  However, GtG has/will be releasing a tactical minis game set after the events of SotM called Sentinel Tactics. For those whom backed the kickstarter, they probably have received their copy already, but the game will hit store shelves sometime this month or next from what we hear.  There are many whom are currently playing the game and the feeling seems to be (from what I've experienced and read) that you will either love it more than SotM or it won't be quite as good, but very enjoyable.  ST does open up more possibilities with the game in terms of story and if you enjoy the game and want to accessorize, there are minis available with ST as well.

The plan is to make different games.

Sentinels Tactics is set in the same Multiverse, continuing from where the card game will leave off and they are hopeful that it will also have a good run.

If that is successful there will be more games continuing the saga of Sentinels Tactics, but at the end of the card game there will no longer be anymore card game expansions, or a new storyline with the same card game mechanics.

However there is a pretty good fan community that is already making their own expansions, so hopefully that will continue.
