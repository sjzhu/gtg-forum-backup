The Kvothe as Argent Adept promo thread got me thinking about what alternate dimensions might exist in the Sentinels Multiverse.  Shattered Timelines touched on this classic comic book trope a bit with Iron Legacy, but that's really more of an alternate possible future than a full alternate reality (ditto for The Visionary's backstory).  Given that the game is called Sentinels of the Multiverse, we really need some true inter-dimensional versions of the characters.

So, here are some of the alternate universe versions of SotM characters that I'd want to see, starting with the F5:

Victorian steampunk Wraith

Sir Bunker of the Round Table

Absolute Zombie

The Council of Tachyons (perhaps they're the launching *point* for the dimensional *crisis*)

and, of course, no Multiverse would be complete without... the anthropomorphic rabbit version of Legacy

Bonus: We can't skip the dimension in which Baron Blade is the only hero in a world of villains.

 

P.S. I LOVE that the SotM universe is so well designed, that I'm already putting these stories together in my head and can totally picture what the comics would look like.
