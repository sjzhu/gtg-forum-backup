I have a theory that the Idealist isn't "real" in the conventional sense.  I think that she might be a construct who sincerely belives that she is a little girl- enough so that she remains one.  Literally, she is an "idea".  

The incap might be when she figures it out...but it might not be permanent!

 

1. Her power source (in her bio) is listed as "unknown"- NOT telekinesis.  

2. In all of her artwork (except Team communication), her constructs are connected to her eyes.  I think they are more "extensions" of herself than constructs.  Her construct form is basically changing shape- the little girl remains solid, but an extension of her forms another shape.  but they are still one thing.

3. Team communication is the ONLY art where her construct is a separate thing.  I can't help but htink that this is after she figures it out and goes to pieces... and then re-forms with a new awareness.  After all, you can't keep an idea down forever!  I bet as soon as they give permission, that head will coalesce into her normal shape.

4. Her mother (who tried power a machine using her) might know the truth- that she never existed before the machine, and it brought her INTO existance.  Instead of stealing her memories, she simply never had any before that moment.
