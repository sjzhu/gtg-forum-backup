For those who are wondering how I get the different dances, during character creation you are able to set a character's Personality. This defines their stance and a few of their emotes, the most obvious being /dance. Some of the others that change are /wave and /rest, but they're far less fun.

Ra has the Powerful Personality, Absolute Zero doesn't dance because he is Serious, and Apostate is Primal.

Some of the dances also are affected by gender and personality together, but I don't remember which ones.
