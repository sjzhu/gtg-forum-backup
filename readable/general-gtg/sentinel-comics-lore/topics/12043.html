With SotM hitting the end of the line, there are only a handful of potential opportunities left for Heroes, Villains, and Environments left to explore.  

Given SotM's overall premise, I think it's same to assume that, even despite the creative differences, nearly all characters have their roots in some already existing superhero comics character.  Bunker may not be Iron Man, but the similarities are pretty substantial.  Same thing with Sky-Scraper and Ant-Man/Wasp.  Baron Blade's story arc is certainly different than Doctor Doom's, but there's a pretty sizable resemblance.

With that...what comics archetypes hasn't SotM touched on yet?

 

For one, we're really missing the "monster-turned-hero" concept, in the vein of Swamp Thing, Ghost Rider, or Etrigan the Demon.  Fanatic brushes past these ideas a little, but she's hardly a 'monster' in any sense of the word.  We do have Man-Grove as a villain (in Vengeance), but having an actual hero in this style could be really cool.

For villains, we're still missing a Joker or Riddler-style character.  Miss Information is close to this idea, isn't quite in the same vein.  Further, a "fear" based character--someone like Scarecrow or the like--could be really cool.  I'm still holding out hope for a classic monster to fight, in the vein of Dracula or Frankenstein's monster, which would fit with a "monster-turned-hero" character.

 

How about you guys?  Any ideas?  What's left in the superhero-comics genre that hasn't been seen yet?
