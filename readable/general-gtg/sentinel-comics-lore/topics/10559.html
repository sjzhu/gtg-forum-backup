Last night on the History Channel they had a show called "Ancient Ink." The first segement was dedicated to Ta Moko of the Maori. Here are some interesting facts about it:

  • It is disrepectful for any non-Maori to get a facial tattoo.
  • There are specific patterns for specific families.
  • The way it was done was to actaully carve scars into the face, let them heal, carve the scars over again, then fill them with pigments.
  • The Maori used to mummify the heads.
  • They stopped getting facial tattoos when explorers would come and decapitate the people so that they could bring the head home and show it off.
  • Recently, they have begun the practice again.
