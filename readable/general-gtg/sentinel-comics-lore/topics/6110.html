I’ve really been digging into the pulp roots of comics for a while now, and a major overlap between “pure pulp” and superhero earths is the existence of a Hollow Earth, or something like it. I’m curious about this on Sentinels Earth. Insula Primalis certainly hints that something like that could exist. Are there mole men and lava monsters and stranger things that hail from beneath the ground? Have people been stumbling into the Hollow Earth for ages and intermingling with the indigenous cultures? I’d love to see some relic characters and places from the Golden Age show up. It’s certainly a trope in comics; why not here?
