One of my favorite things about Sentinels of the Multiverse are the minor details. Hero Character Cards, as the Font of the Hero's name typically has minor changes on their variants, while other look almost completely different. I was curious about how different each one is and decided to make a post that way I can keep record of it. Now, I am not going to go into the what the exact Font is, because I do not have the ability to recognize them all. 

Absolute Zero

Base Absolute Zero: The "Absolute" part is plain, white text with a blue border. The "Zero" is blue, with a bit of an electronic look to it.

Freedom Six Absolute Zero's: The "Absolute" is now gradiant, white to a blue and even the border is gradiant. The "Zero" has acquired some ice sickles hanging off of it.

Termi-Nation Absolute Zero: The "Absolute" is now plain, black text with a blue border. The "Zero" looks lighter and is frozen solid. The lighter look is probably just because of it being frozen. "Termi-nation" is above his name

Bunker

Base Bunker: It looks to be gradiant, with the bottom color looking similar to the color of his suit. The text looks damaged. "The Idestructible" is above his name.

Freedom Six Bunker: It retains its gradiant look, with the bottom color still being that of the current suit. The text appears to have the same damage as the previous. "Engine of War" is below his name.

GI Bunker: The color is now solid and looks like steel rather than the color of his suit. The damage continues to look the same. "GI" is to the left of his name.

Termi-nation Bunker: The color is solid and matches his suit's color. The damage looks similar to the previous versions, but is far more severe. "Termi-nation" is above his name

Fanatic

Base Fanatic: It's black text, with a red border. Also a shadow cast beneath it.

Redeemer Fanatic: As far as I can tell, it looks the same as Base Fanatic.

Prime Warden Fanatic: The text is smaller, but still look the same. "Prime Wardens" is above her name.

Haka

Base Haka: It looks like it might be dirt with cracks. The edges are very rough. "The Savage" is added above his name.

The Eternal Haka: Similar to Base Haka, but now looks like stone. Some of the cracks are glowing red. The edges are still very rough "The Eternal" is above is name.

Prime Wardens Haka: The text is shorter and more straight. There are still minor cracks within and slightly rough edges. "Prime Wardens" is above his name.

Legacy

Base Legacy: White text, with a blue and red border. "America's Finest" is above is name.

Young Legacy: White text, with a blue and red border. "America's Newest" is above her name.

Jerk Legacy: Beige text, with a mystery color border. "America's Greatest" is above his name.

Ra

Base Ra: The color is yellow to red, infront of a little design. "The Mighty" is above and "God of the Sun" is below his name.

Horus Ra: The font is the same, but now has a look like it is popping out and has the color and texture of Sandstone. "Horus of Two Horizons" is glowing below his name.

Tachyon

Base Tachyon: Red to white gradiant text. With a glowing, white border. "The Quickest woman on Earth!" is below her name.

Freedom Six Tachyon: Red to white gradiant text, possibly a darker red than Base Tachyon.

Super-Scientific Tachyon: Red text with a white stripe down the middle. "The Super-Scientific" is above her name.

Tempest

Base Tempest: Blue text, with a lighter blue down the middle. "The Inhuman" above its name.

Freedom Six Tempest: The text color is hard for me to tell, but is lighter than Base Tempest.

Prime Wardens Tempest: It is white and appears to be lightning. "Prime Wardens" is above its name.

The Visionary

Base The Visionary: "Visonary" is green to purple text. "The" is very plain, black text.

Dark Visionary:  "Visionary" is purple to green. "The" is completely absent.  "Dark" above her name, looks to be either a deep purple or black to a lighter purple text.

The Wraith

Base The Wraith: "The" is black. "Wraith" is blue , the most of the text appears to be in a shadow. "Mystery Comics" is above her name.

Rook City The Wraith: "The" is black. "Wraith" is similar to Base The Wraith, but shadows seem to have gotten smaller. "Mystery Comics" is above her name.

Freedom Six The Wraith: "The" is dark blue. "Wraith" is completely shrouded in shadows, with the bottom being exceptionally dark. "Price of Freedom" is below her name.

Expatriette

Base Expatriette: Mostly black text with a white border. The "X" is a red crosshair.

Dark Watch Expatriette: Still black text with white border. The crosshair is now white. "Dark Watch" is above her name.

Mister Fixer

Mister Fixer: "Mister" is gradiant white to black. Very solid looking letters, with scratches. "Fixer" is black to orange, looks to have a Japanese writing style to it.

Dark Watch Mister Fixer: Nearly identical to the Base Mister Fixer, aside from the orange becoming white. "Dark Watch" is above his name.

The Argent Adept

Base The Argent Adept: Text is silver, with very stylised As. "Virtuoso of the Void" is below his name.

Kvothe Six-String: "The Argent Adept" is takes back stage with plain, silver text. "Kvothe Six-String" is below his name, in big, shiny silver letters. "Virtuoso of the Void" is below that.

Prime Wardens Argent Adept: "The" is absent. "Argent Adept" is silver, with middle being lightest and the bottom being darkest. "Prime Wardens" is above his name. 

NightMist

Base NightMist: Glowing, white to black text, almost mist like.

Dark Watch NightMist: The text loses a lot of the glow, but still the same as Base NightMist. "Dark Watch" is above her name.

Chrono-Ranger

Base Chrono-Ranger: "Chrono-" is silver. "Ranger" is gold. The text is slanted and shiny

Best of Times Chrono-Ranger: "Chrono-" is fairly basic Orange text. "Ranger" is similar to the original but is now purple and no longer slanted. "The Best of Times" is below his name.

Omnitron-X

Base Omnitron-X: "Omnitron-" is blue. "X" is yellow. Both have a bit of a shine to them.

K.N.Y.F.E.

Base K.N.Y.F.E.: Gradiant, silver text.

K.N.Y.F.E Rogue Agent: Loses the periods inbetween her name. Text is italics now and black. Her name is underlined. with "NYFE" being overlined. "Rogue Agent" is below her name and has cracking.

The Naturalist

Base The Naturalist: Very unique looking, black text. Over a background that has triangles that likely represent trees. A silhouette of a Crocodile above his name. a silhouette of a Rhino and Gazelle below his name.

Desperate Prey The Naturalist: His name is identical. Above his name is a circle, seperated in three swirling parts. With a silhouette of Crocodile, Gazelle, or Rhino in each of them.

Parse

Base Parse: White, pixeleted text.

The Sentinels

Base Docter Medico: Fairly plain, yellow text/

Adamant Doctor Medico: Very similar to the base, but possibly green hue added. Hard for me to tell.

Base Mainstay: Name appears to be a rusted metal. Has chains looping around his name.

Adamant Mainstay: Still made of metal, but no longer rusty. Green border around his name.

Base The Idealist: "The" is a dark blue and is above "Idealist". "Idealist" is a lighter blue and has a dark blue border, it is also curved. The dot on the first I in "Idealist" is a light bulb.

Adamant The Idealist: "The" is still above "Idealist" but is now green. "Idealist" is now straight, and a gradiant dark blue to white.

Base Writhe: Black text, with tendrills coming from it.

Adamant Writhe: Same, but has a green glow around the border.

Setback

Base Setback: Cannot determine the colors in his name. The A in "Setback" is an orange spade. "A Change from Better to worse" is below his name.

Dark Watch Setback: His name is now gradiant, white to black. The Spade A is now white. "A Change from Better to Worse" is below his name. "Dark Watch" is above his name.

Captain Cosmic

Base Captain Cosmic: Yellow text. the O in "Cosmic" and I in "Captain" is made up of a star burst.

Prime Wardens Captain Cosmic: Similar to the Base, but the Star burst was replaced with the actual letters. "Prime Wardens" is above his name.

Sky-Scraper

Base Tiny Sky-Scraper: Her name is mostly purple, with the bottom left looking pink (I think?). "Sky-" is above "Scraper".

Base Normal Sky-Scraper: Identical to Base Tiny.

Base Huge Sky-Scraper: Mostly like her other two, but the "Scraper" looks like it is falling because of how big she is.

Guise

Base Guise: The text is yellow, with a spray pinted appearance.

Santa Guise: Text is red and written in cursive. "Santa" is above his name in green. The "S" in Santa is big and very decorated for Christmas.

The Scholar

Base The Scholar: His name looks to be a nameplate with a light beaming on it.

The Scholar of the Infinite: His name is mostly black text. A Black Spatter covers a portion of his name, with the text within it being white. His name also looks like it had been written in cursive and written in a liquid. "Of the Infinite" is below his name in black text, aside for the portion in the splatter which is white.

Unity

Base Unity: Pink text.

Freedom Six Unity: Text has now made from metal.

Termi-Nation Unity: Text is pink tinted metal, with circuits running all around it. "Termi-Nation" is above her name.

Foote:

"Redeemer Fanatic" wasn't an official title change for the character however. It's just the way we chose to denote the promo varient using the name of her new power, so this makes sense to me.

Even with that, the "Fanatic" as far as I can tell are identical in all three of her cards. With the exception being Prime Wardens being slightly smaller. The only other ones to have no change in the actually name is Base Legacy and Young Legacy,but even Jerk Legacy gains a different color. To me, there seems to be a deeper meaning considering the minor changes most other Character Cards have. Take Absolute Zero, his Base card is pretty standard. The Freedom Six version has ice sickles, which typically form without anyone's control, much like the ice on Absolute Zero on the card. He doesn't have much, if any, control over the ice forming, which leads to his incap side where he has absolutely zero control. However, take his Termi-nation card and notice how the ice is in the perfect shape of his name. For ice to take such a shape it would require someone to alter the shape. Looking at Absolute Zero he does seem to have control over his powers in that image. 

Back to Fanatic, her name unchanging may repesent her unchanging persona that she puts on. Of course I may be over thinking the Character Card angle a bit much.
