I could easily see a Count Barzakh deck as something similar to Chokepoint's, where each time you strike him, he steals the top card from your deck, coupled with the additional rider that if any hero deck empties, that hero is automatically incapacitated.  

His cards would then center on either peeling more cards away from hero decks (representing him stealing a hero's power and vitality) or expanding that power, dealing damage to a hero or destroying hero cards based on how much he's already stolen (utilizing that stolen power).

It'd make playing a "nova" character like Tachyon or Bunker much more risky, but potentially rewarding-- they run the risk of being incapped more easily, but would have less cards stolen-- while also making 'ping' heroes like Chrono Ranger or Harpy fight a battle of attrition... neat tactical level choices, either way.
