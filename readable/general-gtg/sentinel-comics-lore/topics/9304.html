I'm super excited about this game and playtesting has indeed been awesome.  What I'm going to post has probably already been considered, but in the interest of this game attracting as many people as possible (something I'm sure we all want) I just wanted to mention a couple of things based on previous Kickstarters, primarily GSF.

 

1. I hope GTG is planning to spread the word in other outlets before Tuesday. Newsletter, website, Twitter, BGG, etc....I typically don't get the email update with announcements from GTG until the day after it posts on the website so it might be a good idea to have that ready before the launch.  Building anticipation should ensure a strong first day, which will lead to people wanting to jump on if they see an already successful project likely to reach more strech goals.

2. The video: Please, please have a demo video done before the campaign starts that has been viewed/edited/gotten feedback from multiple people.  With the ruleset still in playtesting, the demo is all people will have ot go on from a gameplay perspective.  No ofense to GTG, but the quality of the demos in GSF was lacking compared to many other Kickstarter projects and was a complaint by many throughout the campaign.

3. Speaking of GSF, be prepared to address questions/comment from folks who are unhappy that you are launching another Kickstarter before GSF ships.  I'm not saying there is anything that can be done, I would just anticipate said reactions and have a response ready.

 

Again, these are just my opinions based on observing a lot of Kickstarter projects over the last couple of years.  I can't wait for this launch!
