While listening to this episode, I remembered how much C&A (Adam in particular) want a fighting game based on Sentinels, and thought how Uncontrollable Flock would be a perfect special attack in a game like Marvel vs. Capcom.  Then I remembered Setback and his goose, and got the mental image of Harpy opening a portal, unleashing a stream of birds that does a 50+ hit combo, and finishes with this goose that comes out and whacks the opponent a few times.  Then I thought it would be even more hilarious if there was like a 5% chance that instead of the goose, you get a guy in a chicken costume that combos the opponent with a spinning fried chicken sign.

Although in some ways I feel like Harpy has the same problem as Setback (they're supposed to want to balance high and low birds/Unluck but in practice you often just want them to be high), I do feel like they captured Harpy's frustration well in the game.  I always start off trying to be careful and take advantage of high magic, and inevitably end up saying, "Eh, this is too hard.  F* it, BIRDS!"
