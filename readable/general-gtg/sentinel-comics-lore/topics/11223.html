While I was at PAX, I started thinking that Parse sure had fought a wide variety of creatures. She's directly fought Spite, Ambuscade, Miss Information, Numerous creatures, investigated the site of Skinwalker Gloomweaver's emergence, stood up to Iron Legacy, aided in the combat of Akash'Bhuta....

Thats a really wide variety!  Most hero decks don't have that range, and I can't think of any other hero that has come into direct contact with so many different villains!

So I decided to dig a bit deeper and look at Chrono-Ranger's bounties.  And sure enough, each one (with the exception of Plague rat) was a villain that Parse had either directly faced, or coordinated the fight against during the events of Vengeance. This led me to start thinking about CON, and to believe that Parse is a signficant component of that entity.  After all, who better to become an AI (or something close to) than someone who ALREADY has a brain like a computer, and has already merged/been altered by a piece of Omnitron?

And if you think about it, Parse already puts together data to try and predict outcomes and find weak points.  Who better to try and re-write history by moving around key components and altering key pieces of the timeline?

But wait!  Theres more!

Heres where it gets really interesting.

So, if you postulate that Parse is a part of Con, then each of the bounties is trying to accomplish some sort of goal.

This brings me to Citizen Truth.  and the fact that Con SPECIFICALLY instructs that there be no executions.  

 

now... go ahead and look at Critical Multiplier.  With the theory that Con/Parse is trying to keep Citizen Truth alive, maybe we've been looking at it all wrong.  Maybe her reference to "things adding up" ISN'T about Fanatic.  Maybe its about Citizen Truth being in the right place at the right time to BLOCK Fanatic!  What makes more sense?  Fanmatic breaking hewr sword somehow bringing about victory, or someone being saved by that sword being broken?

Heck, maybe Parse is accomplishing multiple objectives- maybe she is trying to send Fanatic a Wake-up call by putting her in a position to have her sword (on of the symbols of her faith) shattered, AND save someone in the process by using Citizen Truth to do it.

Now, this led me further down the rabbit hole.

Citizen Truth is NOT a primary target.  He's a defender. According to the bio from Tacics, He joined the Citizens to Prtotect his brother. Before that, he was cooperating with the guards and waiting for the moment to get free (before Dare fouled it up).

I think the BIG question here is "Who is Citizen Truth guarding?"  I asked Christopher, and in a rare gesture of giving an absolute statement, he said that Citizen Truth is ALWAYS protecting someone. 

there are a LOT of ways to protect someone.  Keeping secrets, for instance. 

Citizen Truth has a history of going along with the people in power, until he sees the right moment to break out. He has a history of playing his cards very close to his chest, and keeping secrets.

I'm wondering if he is protecting someone by keeping their existence secret.  Which would make his name even more interesting, wouldn't it.

I kind of wonder if Expatriette has a twin sister.  Being born into project Coccoon, Citizen Truth knows about being born for powers intimately.  Maybe he conspired with Expatriette's father to spirit away one of the girls... the one with power.

Of course, that is pure speculation, even for me.

But I'm dead certain that Parse knows something about it, and is trying to keep him alive and protecting.
