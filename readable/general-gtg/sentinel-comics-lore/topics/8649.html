I've trying to work out a Proletariat hack that I like, but really, he's the hardest of the V5 to convert.

None of them are easy per say, since you need a lot more cards - you can't just double everything, because before you do that, you take out 4-5 Nemeses.  And having 4x each of a couple of One-Shots is often awkward.  Factor in their lack of Ongoings, and a few other things, and the only one I can see as not just designing a new Hero from the group up is Friction... and only her because we already have that Hero.

But Proletariat is in a league of his own.  Extra targets which deal repeatable damage are very strong - there's a good reason why Unity has to jump through hoops to get them out, and even then, against villains who are weak on global damage, she frequently makes the game a joke.  Proletariat, however, has an even harder to balance gimmick - his own health, similar to Golem Unity.  Getting that to a place where it works as a Hero while not playing too much like the Golem is tough.  I haven't had an idea I really like yet.

On the plus side, a V5 deck minus the Nemeses (and therefore not Blade) can work on its own as an addition to a team of Heroes - its plays remain random (villains are inconsistent allies, after all), you increase H by one to account for the help, and see what happens.

Flavorfully, though, I do still hope for a full redemption for poor Proletariat.

Some random ideas:

Baron Blade "Redemption": Time traveling version of Baron Blade that returns from a time where he has "won" but his victory left the Earth a smoldering wasteland. It is only when he was the last person alive did he realize how blind his mania made him. He goes back in time to stop himself from creating that horrible time line. Possible game mechanics could include time slipping equipment for use so long as they go into the trash and never return after a certain number of turns ("stealing his own devices from the future") or a desperation mechanic that lets him play more turns if he steadily damages himself ("growing more manic as he slowly slips back into his time").

 

Refugee Prince Voss: Voss as a conscientous objector to the military action of the Thorathian Army. Eventually when he sees that his planet's army is pursuing Tempest to Earth, he can remain silent no longer. Turning traitor to the Thorathian Sovereign, he arrives to our planet early, offering our Heroes whatever help he can. I see a personality much closer to Beast of the X-Men: still hulking with superhuman strength and a genuis at genetics, but much more mild and educated. I see his gameplay and deck being a nearly even split between direct damage dealing with his impressive strength and technology and providing buffs to damage, card draw and extra actions in the form of voluntary gene therapy serums. Because what monster would restructure with another living thing's genetic structure without express permission and informed consent?

 

Skald of the Ages Matriarch: Almost the exact same back story with the Matriarch, except when Lillian acquires the Mask she has a moment of expanded empathy as she sees the entire world through the eyes of the flock. Raised up from her adolescent melodrama, she saw the suffering of orphans, the sick and dying from all corners of the globe as birds fly overhead and acted as her eyes. She saw the cruelty of those who would take advantage of the weak with shocking clarity. While in the esctacy of this revelation, two compatriots perched upon her shoulder and whispered into her ears the ageless tales of triumph and tribulations that all the former wearers of the Mask lived through. Her sensitive heart was moved liked it never has been in her young life and she dedicated herself to fighting all manners of injustice. Wherever avian eyes spy evil, she would not be far behind. Her sense of drama is still there, mind you, but at least her poetry has improved greatly. As for gameplay...I actually am not so sure. I see a lot of deck control and multi-attacks in one turn I guess? Somewhere between Chrono Ranger and Visionary?

 

Oathbound Demon Nightgloom: Time can lead us all to strange, unexpected places. Even demon lords may find themselves in very strange alliances. In his near eternal life, Gloomweaver had waited patiently for a chance to enter our realm and his opportunity had finally arrived in the form of inexperienced occultist, Faye Diamond. This young sorceress had cast a spell (The Mists of R'lyeh) that left her mind and soul vulnerable to possession by Gloomweaver. Immediately upon forcing herself into Faye's body did he realize it was a trap set by her and her mystical tutor, the Master. Faye was not only waiting for Gloomweaver to enter her, she was ready and able maintain her free will and had the mystical strength to harness Gloomweaver's power for her own ends! Once a demon of near infinite malice, Gloomweaver and his mortal host, Faye Diamond, are now inextricably bound. What's more to his horror, the longer he resides within Faye's mind, the more he can feel a new emotion gestate and begin to take root within his ageless soul: empathy! The two-in-one mystical entity now known as Nightgloom risks using the foulest and darkest of mystic arts for the (hopefully) purest goals. For gameplay, I could see a card that is Gloomweaver possessing Faye's shadaow and stepping slightly outside of her to cast spells along side her (kind of like a high HP Unity Golem) and many of the spells in the deck having riders that offer bonuses if Gloomweaver is out. Perhaps a defensive ongoing that lets them split incoming damage between them.
