I think the reason he chooses now to end everything is because he realized that the amount of, and the cumulative power of, the super-powered beings on earth had nearly begun to rival his own power.  And thus he would eventually be less and less all-powerful in relation to the rest of the cosmos as time went on.  So he plans to destroy everything and start things over again.  He commanded Progeny to weaken earth's heroes, and soften them up in preparation for his arrival.  What he didn't expect was for the various superpowered beings of earth to decode his signal to and from Progeny.  This, in addition to a rebellious Scion sharing information with the heroes of earth, led to them realizing that the only way they stand a chance is if they drastically increase their power-base.

 

Thus, Nightmist opens a portal to... EVERY universe in the multiverse, pulling through multiple versions of themselves, increasing the relative strength of their side exponentially.

 

This also explains why Deadline wanted to destroy everything on earth.  No more life on earth means no more consolidated power-base that could rival OblivAeon's own. And thus He wouldn't choose to destroy everything... yet...
