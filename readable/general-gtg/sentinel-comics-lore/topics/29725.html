I usually hate awkward portmanteaus, like “voidcano”, but when they started describing the ghost Yakuza guy, and made a joking reference to him having a motorcycle, I immediately think of the Bosozoku, the Japanese version of what we in the USA would call “bikers” (though rather different for numerous obvious reasons). Given what the Japanese do to the English language, I don’t think I’m out of line to have mentally renamed this guy “Ghostozoku”.

The description of the multiple demon lords has me envisioning an Aeternus villain deck which has no character card, just a setup card like the Ennead, but contains BOTH a 25-card villain deck AND a deck of potentially that many character cards…or maybe go the Kaargra Warfang route and have a total of 27 cards, but 15 of them are the shuffled deck and 12 are characters. Either way, the characters are randomized and played from beneath the setup card, but unlike the Ennead, there’s no completing the set…some will actually permanently remove others from the selection! They spend as much time maneuvering to thwart each other as they do attacking the heroes, allowing them to be ridiculously powerful and requiring a lot of effective teamwork to defeat. Definitely someone I’d want to be a Difficulty 4 if not 5 villain (on the EE scale), close to OblivAeon in their complexity and dangerousness, though less over-the-top and nowhere near as long of a game (those familiar with the Cauldron are also invited to compare to Celadroch).

The descriptions of Lucithar and Soulviathan (or whatever her name was) definitely made me think of the Deadly Sins, although I don’t think that’s the vibe Aeternus goes for as a whole. I mean, she definitely suits Envy and he’s obviously Pride, but I would want there to be other demons there which don’t fit into the mold of the Seven.
