Something I’ve always thought would make for an interesting discussion in the Letters Page (but I’ve never really know which episode exactly to ask it) is why do the super hero teams of Sentinel Comics seem a lot more static than ‘real world’ comics?

I mean, some Marvel teams like the Avengers or Xmen have been through huge team changes over the years, but I can pretty much sum up the line up changes to the major Sentinel comics teams in a few lines each:

Freedom 5

- Line up change (more of a reboot than a change of characters) at the end of the Golden Age

- Change of name from Freedom 4 when Absolute Zero joins the team (really early)

 

Prime Wardens

- Broke up for a few years

 

Dark Watch

- Mr Fixer Joins early after the first 3 form the team

- Harpy joins the team

- Nightmist leaves the team

 

South West Sentinels 

- Changed name to Void Guard

 

So why are these teams so stable? Did no new creative team come in and want to shake up these decades old line ups?!

I'd like to hear C&A comment on this, but until then there's my take:

 

I think this is a result of the way the Sentinels franchise was created and developed. Even though the team made efforts to simulate comic-book excentricities and long backstory into the universe, it's still a franchise managed by a small team for some games that is younger than a decade (compare to various teams working on more than 30 titles for multiple decades). So things are bound to be simpler than if Sentinel Comics was a real company, not only teams would've changed more but we'd have way more teams (At least three Freedom 5s working at the same time, with a "Teen team" around way before Daybreak).
