My question is, where does the Legacy-less Freedom Six fit in to all this?  The Freedom Six must have fought Iron Legacy, considering he is a nemesis to all of them (The original four non-legacy members + Tempest and Unity). I think I might know why, but I want to check.  The Freedom Six are from Iron Legacy's timeline, not the primary one.  He fought them there and defeated them, but when he came to the primary timeline he still considered the primary timeline versions to be his Nemeses.
