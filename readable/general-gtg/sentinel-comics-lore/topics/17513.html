I'm listening to old podcasts that I missed before I started listening, and was surprised to learn that Omnitron became sentient/destructive partly because of being connected to the internet.  This surprised me, because I thought of Omnitron as an older villain, and Sentinel Comics predates the internet (or at least general knowledge of the internet) by about 50 years.  So I started trying to figure it out, knowing that Omnitron's first appearance is in Freedom Five #305, but I'm getting confused.

Assuming Freedom Five is a monthly magazine, I looked for known reference points.  Wager Master's first appearance is in the early/mid-1970's, with Freedom Five #174 (according to the video game).  That's a difference of 131 issues, which would put Omnitron's first appearance in the mid 1980's, well before anyone other than a handful of computer scientists and defense contractors knew there was such a thing (and wasn't called "The Internet" yet).  The RPG starts with Freedom Five #801; if we assume Omnitron was introduced in 1995, which is when I recall "the internet" entering common parlance, at one issue a month that would put #801 in the mid 2030's, which seems unlikely.

Citizen Dawn, Visionary, La Capitan, Fright Train, and Citizens Hammer and Anvil also first appear in Freedom Five (not the Annual), but we weren't told when those characters were invented in their podcasts.  Same applies to Voss and Kaargra, whose podcasts we haven't heard yet.  I also couldn't find a whole lot of known years for significant events--I was looking for Vengeance specifically, being an easy one to cross-reference since it's in nearly every story, and which I thought wasn't all that late since the Villains stories take place quite a bit after.

Anyone have any insight?

I believe it was mentioned in one of the Interludes that Sentinel Comics has a “rolling continuity”, similar to that of Schmarvel Comics. In such a continuity, stories get retconned to fit their appropriate era.

As such, Gold-Titanium Alloy Man may have had his first appearance during the Vietnam War, but in later issues, comics may reference his first appearance as being during Afghanistan. So it is for SC characters… how long was Wraith a college student again? Can’t treat time linearly… the timelines are shattered, after all!
