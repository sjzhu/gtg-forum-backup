Finally got caught up!  (Takes me a while, I only listen to podcasts during my commute.)

I'm pretty sure Dark Watch would be considered B-list.  They're not central to most of the main story arcs, but they are peripheral to the main story arcs and central to several secondary ones.  Not counting the late-introduction heroes (the ones from OblivAeon and its mini-expansions), I think the C-list easily includes Guise, Scholar, Omnitron-X, and Sky-Scraper.  Chrono-Ranger, KNYFE, Parse, and Naturalist seem borderline B/C, with their popularity levels going up and down; all of them were given big things to do in OblivAeon or thereabouts but weren't major characters before then.  I get the feeling Ra ranges all the way from A to C and back to A again over the comic history.

I'm definitely looking forward to an RPG Twitch stream, or better yet, a podcast of that Twitch stream.  I'd certainly listen to any games run by Princess Cool or the other Letters Page regulars, but the real gem would be adventures Christopher ran, since one could consider them canon.  Although it would be hilarious to see a game in which Christopher and Adam were mere players, subject to the direction some other person decided to take their campaign world.
