Some observations and thoughts on Sentinels Comics following the receipt of WotC...

 

- Cpt. Cosmic's deck, "Harsh Offense": There is a new character there. I might be wrong but I have a strong feeling that she got her powers the same way Captain and his brother got theirs... Cpt. Cosmic flies into space, has yellow glowing eyes, has a yellow 4-pointed star on his chest and creates yellow constructs of energy. Infinitor flies into space, has green glowing eyes, has a green 4-pointed star on his chest and creates green constructs of energy. That lady on "Harsh Offense" flies into space, has blue glowing eyes and has a blue 4-pointed star on her chest. Now, we don't know if she's able to create blue constucts of energy, but I still see some pattern there... Also, does anybody know why both Cpt. Cosmic and Infinitor designed their costume with this star on the chest? Did they chose this design independently or did one of them copied the other?

 

- Super Scientific Tachyon incapacitated side: Ok, this card is available since a while already, but looking again at it, I get a strong feeling that the scene might be taking place at the enclave of the endlings. The style and background is oddly reminiscent of the entire deck of the Enclave of the Endling... For example, if you look at the card "The Endling Refuge", you'll notice that the same red dots and yellow lines are there on the walls... Not sure what it implies though...

 

- Progeny's deck, "Hour of Reckoning": Progeny looks like he's been beating the crap out of Legacy for some time already and you get Dark Visionary standing there, quite close to Progeny actually, and not inside her cocoon... Looks really evil to me :(... Thoughts?

 

- Kaargra Warfang's deck, "Orrim Hiveminded" and PW AA incapacitated side: This eye-thingy with tentacles looks like it's the same on both cards... Red mildly-glowing eye, a set of tentacles with protuding spikes... Although they're not the same size, I'm pretty sure they're at least of the same species...

 

- I was looking for another card and fell on this card : "Fixed Point" in Time Cataclysm deck. Quote: ""This event must occur in every reality across time." Omnitron-X posited. The Visionary just hung her head. Not one Vanessa Long could be safe." On the card, there's a young Vanessa in hospital clothes... It sounds interesting to me...

 

Now I've got some theory, but you're all so much better at formulating those (and I greatly enjoy reading all this crazy stuff on the forum...!). Is it all old news or does anybody finds something interesting?
