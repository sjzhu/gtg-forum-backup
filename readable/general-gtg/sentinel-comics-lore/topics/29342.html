From the original notes we have on him from episode 115:

Mr. Hideous was a guy in a spooky ghost mask who used poisonous gas to confront people with their greatest fears, etc. He got revamped quite a bit in the ’80s as a victim of his own crimes - overexposure to his own gas left him horribly scarred, so he stopped wearing the mask (his own face being pretty bad on its own now) and and started wearing tacky suits. His gimmick went from being “I will terrify you” to “I will show you how terrible I am”

About the Oblivion infinity stuff: I don’t think any chain reaction that starts with a finite number of destructions can “get rolling” enough to ever be infinite. However I also don’t believe that the multiverse is infinite, or rather there are two multiverses, a finite one which OblivAeon is destroying, and an infinite one which is all Sentinels games ever. I like to think of this as the 5th and 6th dimensions of time; just as there’s a finite amount of 4D time since the universe began, there’s also a finite number of split timelines in 5D, but there’s an effectively if not literally infinite number of 6D versions of each of those 5D timelines. (This also neatly addresses the sandwich bag; there is a 5D loaf of bread split into finite slices, one of which was bagged, and there are an infinite number of different RPG campaigns in that one slice of bread.) All of the timelines in the canon multiverse are 5D, and there are a specific number of them, because the same forces that gave rise to the singular entities also constructed the multiverse, with its single Inversiverse and Xtremiverse and Magic Freedom Fiveiverse, instead of infinite ones of each, its single Ur-Space, and its single copy of each singular entity (plus OblivAeon, minus Wager Master post bagging).
