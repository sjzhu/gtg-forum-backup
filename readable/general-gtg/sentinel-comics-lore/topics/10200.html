Well there are two more SotM expansions planned (one of which is a "mega-expansion"), plus some mini-expansion decks (like Unity, Ambuscade, etc) so there's plenty more in the next few years or so.

Also I can't help but be a pedant, but there's no deck-building in SotM. The decks are fixed and pre-made. Deck-building is when you construct a deck yourself in some way from a larger selection of cards, either before play like in Magic: The Gathering, or during play like in Dominion.

HikariStarshine:

Where did this 'two expansions' thing come from, anyways? I thought that the statement was that Vengeance is the halfway point, which would indicate three more small expansions and either another mega expansion or a full-blown standalone set at the end, right?

Since just about everyone here knows how much I love speculation, I'll take a shot at this.

Honestly, I can't remember exactly where that notion originated from or why it formed in the first place. But its been around for some time during my first year here on the forums.

Rook City –> Infernal Relics –> Shattered Timelines (how the F did I forget that) –> VENGEANCE –> Wrath of the Cosmos –> ???

Lets look at the numbers:

We know WotC will be a standard format expansion. In other words, 4 Villains, 2 Heros, 2 Environments. We also can resonably guess that the 2 Heros will be Captain Cosmic (our 5th Prime Warden), and Skyscrapper (our large Thorathian girly). 

When we start to count currently known Heros who are lacking a "proper" nemesis encounter, we get Captain Cosmic, Skyscrapper, Unity, Scholar, Naturalist, Bunker, AbZero, KNYFE, and Guise. Thats 9 known heros without dedicated nemesis's.

I'm going to go out on a limb and say Guise will be a mini expansion hero. Why? Christopher mentioned that the next mini expansion hero would some sort of hillarious (you can dig through the SotM Quiz thread I believe is where that came from). That quote along with Guise's nemmy symbol seems to fit well, so I'll run with it. Another Mini expansion villain wouldn't seem to be out of the cards with a mini expansion hero, so lets just say its Guise's nemmy (not that it matters who really). So that brings us to 8 known Heros without nemmys.

WotC as I said will introduce 4 villains, bringing us down to 4.

So now we have 1 or 2 expansions left to account for, with 4 villains to account for to match up with every known hero. If there is 2 expansions after WotC, it would most likely be a standard size and a final Mega. Another standard would acount for those 4 villains, but add 2 new heros, bringing our total to 2. But whats left for that final Mega? At least with current information, not much, but those numbers could always be addressed through a bunch of Mini expansions to even things out.

If there is only a Mega expansion left after WotC, we are possibly looking at 4 villains (to account for missing nemmys) and the Galactus-esque Big Bad. Now that is worthy of a Mega expansion I'd say. The issue there is there would be no hero decks. Or, in my mind, no new nemesis symbols. What do I mean by that? I mean there could be new hero decks for this theoretical Mega expansion that use existing Nemmy symbols. This could very well mean we get hero decks for a few old Villains to help fight against our Big Bad (as they all have just as much to loose as the heros I'd imagine). Matriarch, Proletariat and Ambuscade instantly jump to mind as totally possible candidates for this (and the art in Parse's deck strongly hint at Matriarch joining the forces of good in my estimation). Thats a Mega expansion for you.

So, there you have my entire personal speculation into the whole thing. Do with it as you will. Do I make a fair amount of assumptions? Yes would be putting it lightly. But working with what little information we have, this is my best guess as to how it all plays out. Do I expect >G to throw a lot of awesome curve balls my way? If I didn't, I wouldn't be such a big fan of what they do.
