imprimis5:

I hold to the theorem that Visionary's temporal jaunt actually created a new universe. If one subscribes to a certain brand of multiverse theory, that may well be the only way to travel in time.

Logically speaking, this pretty much has to be true.  For anyone who doesn't travel in a police box, temporal paradoxes are just a dead stop to any attempt at conceptualizing traveling back in time within your own history.  I mean, "the butterfly effect" isn't just a fuzzy conceptualization - the moment you stepped out into the past, the air you exhaled would have bacteria in it which had survived a few generations of battling with your immune system, and those would now be in the past, spawning new generations of offspring that were more resistant.  You couldn't possibly not alter your history enough to create a paradox; just physically occupying a space that was previously empty would alter too many molecular-level interactions for the result not to affect your future self.  And those alterations would be compounded when your future self traveled back in time just as you originally did, and then they'd compound again and again until the results were macroscopic, and catastropic.  At which point, once you've killed your future self, he no longer travels back in time and the entire thing didn't happen.  So there's just no possible way.  There is simply no part of the universe that isn't connected to the gigantic clockwork mechanism which is "all matter and energy ever"; nothing can ever not affect all other things to a microscopic extent, and those changes inevitably add up.
