So, in the livestream today, I asked who Dawn was reviving on the tactics version of Return with the Dawn.

I expected the name of a minor character or something similarly cool but pointless.

I did not expect the answer, "Expatriette's father."

For a long time the name of Expat's father has been speculated, but now we have something very nearly complete to work off of. The gravestone Expat's dad is rising out of broken, but it still has half of a name on it. It reads CITIZ-  PAI-... I think it's safe to assume the first word is Citizen and the word that immediately comes to mind with the letters P, A, and I is Pain. Citizen Pain. It works with the naming scheme too. Citizen Pain and Citizen Gain.

Well that's my bit of speculation, anyway. What's yours?
