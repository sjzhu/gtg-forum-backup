So, I was thinking more about Twitchy today

I have it in my mind that he is a F.I.L.T.E.R. agent. I have seen other people express the same sentiment. Where did we get this from?

Is it only because of the emblem on his chest resembles the holding cells in the Block? Like the one Unity finds herself in on her incap side? If this is the case, let me pose an alternate theory (if its not, oh well. it was good while it lasted like my freedom wraith = operative idea)

 

I do not think the emblem on his chest is a FILTER prison cell. You know the card in Kismets deck where she is punching Twichy and we see a bunch of Casino/gambling related art?  I think his emblem is a die. This die only has one side with a value on it, and thats what he rolled. He rolled a 1. Snake Eyes.  

Him rolling snake eyes fits thematically with the popular rumor that he is probably just naturally unlucky. This fits in with him getting unluckily zapped by Shock Rounds and also fits within the potential Nemesis dynamic where Kismet is super lucky and Twitchy can't roll anything but the figurative "snake eyes". If he can not help but be unlucky, then we see another Nemesis dynamic take shape in the form of Infinite Possibilities vs Fixed Outcomes. Kismet's luck can potentially make anything happen if she just gets "lucky" enough. With Twitchy it is the exact opposite. His outcomes, possibly his entire destiny, are set in stone, fated to happen the same way every time. When he rolls a die, it will always be 1.

Plot wise, this puts him in a very unique situation as it pertains to "the multiverse" and the time cataclysm, as he could act as a common thread between them all, potentially being a key to preventing the possible dark futures of Iron Legacy and The Final Wasteland. I also now picture him as having a very bad gambling addiction before gaining his powers
