It’s definitely something we’ve talked about in regards to flavor, specifically in character bios or possibly on-card flavor text, but as of now there are no plans to implement love interests as an additional mechanic. Something else you may like to try is storytelling through gameplay. We’ve worked very hard to create a set of complex, interesting characters, and a lush world they inhabit, but the adventures are just beginning. We encourage you to explore the interactions between characters and the way they relate to each other, and post the scenarios you’ve created here on the forum!

Just because I’m watching this spiral out of control, and I do hate to rain on everyone’s parade: Tachyon and Unity are absolutely NOT romantically involved.

Tachyon had already been married for years by the time she invited Unity to come work for her lab. They butted heads at first, due to their differing ways of approaching issues and Tachyon’s love for order vs. Unity’s love for “just doing things” and not having a thought-out process.
