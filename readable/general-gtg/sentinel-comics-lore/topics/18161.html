1. That's pretty much it.  The Letters Page is the main source of lore, and the summaries go into the wiki.

2. Tactics is hard to recommend right now.  GtG is in the process of revamping the whole ruleset because of certain balance and flow issues with the original, and they're still a long way away from releasing that revision (in the form of Prime War).  Also upcoming are the Sentinel Comics RPG and the Sentinels of Freedom video game, but we're looking at 2019 or later for all that stuff.  Obviously you know what the physical card game is like, so if you have friends you want to play with in person, and can work through doing all the resolutions manually, you'll have no surprises there.
