I really enjoyed myself with this episode! I feel like Fanatic is one of the least ‘normal life’-y of the heroes - Haka teaches kids, Tachyon’s a scientist, Ra probably stops burning stuff sometimes to do some archaeology, but Fanatic gives off a vibe of All Smiting All The Time. It’s nice to explore other sides to her personality!

The Haunted Fanatic story about her ghostly offsider was excellent, and I like the concept it introduced of the ghost, uh, unlife-cycle. Sort of similar to the one presented in Bleach, but with more room for non-violent resolutions. And I would love to read the story, watching Stevie be overcome more and more by her dark side, it sounds very tense.

I also liked Sea Envy’s letter/full plot concept, and I’d love to read the full thing! It sounds like it could tackle very interesting concepts of belief, reclaiming yourself and your heritage from oppression, and also get some conflict with the more radical side of the freedom fighters. They might not be entirely down with the idea of submitting their will to a perfect, untouchable higher power!

I might have to end up writing that. Once I get through some of my current WIPs, maybe.
