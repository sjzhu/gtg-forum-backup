The current Count Barzakh count is set at 2.

This episode is so much ado about literally nothing initially. I like the meta aspect of saying the comic writers basically answered a reader question with something akin to “it comes from midichlorians shut up” and then somebody else canonized it and made it s whole big thing.

That character I mentioned a while ago, who watches videos of spells being cast and realizes they can do it? For the first time today, I think their nemesis is Count Tolvazh.

As the episode evolves, I see a Count Barzakh deck taking shape. It’s a mini-villain, designed to play similarly to Bugbear mixed with Biomancer mixed with Ermine a little bit. Starts the game with the indestructible Hand of Glory at like 25 HP, it loses 1 a round, if it’s on 0 HP he plays extra cards, he damages other villain targets when it’s safe to do so, that heals the hand, and he’s immune to damage if another villain is in play. Not sure about the rest.

Very obviously, the singular entity that S&D Barzakh steals power from is Malavox. That doesn’t happen, but in the place where impossible things happen, he’s one of those things. Malavox is a weird character, because he exemplifies Conquest as a concept, but he’s not conquering anything. So it makes sense to say that anyone who is a conquerer IS ruled by Malavox.
