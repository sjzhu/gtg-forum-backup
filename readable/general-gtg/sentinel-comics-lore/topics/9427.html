I've found it in other places (such as tourism guides), but the phrase "From the center to the sea" could refer to a poem (seen here).  The Shan Van Vocht (translation: The Poor Old Woman) describes the Irish rebellion of 1798.

Her name (Lillian Merle Corvus) is largely of latin derivation, but that doesn't rule out an Irish angle. Lily (her nickname, and what Lillian means) is an important flower in Irish culture (though in fairness, its important in a lot of cultures).

This brings me to the Morrigan.  

she is associated with a LOT of stuff- some good, some bad.  Things like war and death, but also fertility, creativity, and poetry. she's associated with druids, bards, and circles.  She's often associated with crows, or depicted as one.

If she does become a hero, I could see her taking the Morrigan name.   

 

Onto reveal the flaws

In Reveal the Flaws, the Matriarch is being held by the silver creature.  Her eyes are glowing purple- as are its eyes.  Prisoner?  controller?  both being held in the grip of a larger force acting through the mask?

It could well be that she kept seeking more and more power, and something is now using her...
