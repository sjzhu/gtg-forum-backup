In the XTREME World what do you think the nemeses of the Prime Wardens are like?

 NOTE I want to keep this forum to the MAIN Nemeses of the Prime Wardens, we can get the other ones later. 

  1. Akash'bhuta
  2. Infinitor
  3. Apostate
  4. Ambuscade
  5. Voss
 
How do you think these nemeses will differ? Power set, personality, look, etc… 
 
Note: Nothing here is canon. it's my fun thoughts. 
 
Akash'bhuta --> Akash'REBOOTA or Cache'bhuta, maybe Rerooted. 
 
  • Brief Bio: I think Akash'bhuta will go into a route (hehehe) of the cyber world. After spreading across the world, she faces will the old remnants of an Omnitron Facility, though defunct and polluted, has a small spark of Omnitron in it.  The anarchic nature (HA!) of her entity mixes with the cold calculating technology of Omnitron.  . Now she is able to spread everywhere, faster, and more efficiently, 
  • Look: She looks like her current version, but with metal plates, and cybertec (blue shiny cybertech) with wires pouring out of her. Her hair tendrils become wires, and one set of her eyes glows an Omnitron red instead of her monstrous one. Just add TONS of wires, like anything from bad cabling images on the internet. 
  • Power Set: She will deal electricity and energy damage, destroying equipment and ongoing. 
  • Personal Notes: I am aware it's conflicting things, but cyber stuff was pretty big in the Xtreme world. Hacking the net and whatnot.

 

Infinitor --> Infamex

  • Brief Bio: This version of Infinitor is a lot a darker . He came back from the war (what war? You never heard of the Battle of Mordengrad, Lithuania. He came back, wrong. The massive hordes of robots and mind-controlled Blade Battalions. Prone to anger, and hate, he fought with everyone and everything. Then the incident happened, and he got infused with the cosmic power. 
  • Look: He will just have chains, and spikes everywhere. Floating around him in constant spiral whirlwind of shredding vengence. His face will be pierced up, and he won't have any eyeholes in his mask. Basically a black faceplate. Imagine something like Penence mixed with Spawn. When he communicates it's telepathy.
  • Power Set: He is more reactionary. Damaging everything when a manifestation comes out, and whenever he takes damage he strikes back. 
  • Notes: Nothing much to add.
 
Apostate -> Saint Satan
  • Brief Bio: He is actually a version of Satan coming from one of the demonic realms.  Gets mixed up with Lucifer, Beelzebub, etc… He came up one day to do what  anyone wants to do, "Take over the world". Sometimes simple is best. 
  • Look: Varies. Goes from chic business attire, to Spikey armor and helmet. Shoulder pads, flowing cape. Fire sprouting from all the joints and spaces. Carries a big giant hammer, which he calls, Appeasement 
  • Power Set: Does more damage to more people instead of just focusing it one person.  Harder to hit, and does a lot more fire damage.
  • Notes: Simple. Cliched. But still works. Fits the theme of XTREME
 
Ambuscade  --> Le Gunshow
  • Brief Bio: Ambuscade was an angry man, he needed to be bigger, stronger, faster. After an indent of roid rage, he killed a producer on his current movie, and destroyed half the set. Too rich to jail, and too scary to confront, they just ended the contract, and took away his money. Needing a way to keep his habit going, he just stole from everyone and everything.  Anything to get that fix. 
  • Look: Imagine a version of strong guy, but with more pouches, and guns strapped everywhere. So Cable. He wears a bandana over his face, and has some sort of extreme goggles with an extra eye piece.  
  • Power Set: Guns, never ending amount of guns. Also punching. Once again, sometimes simple is best.
  • Notes: I had a hard time with this one. But, I thought why not just get something like Cable.
 
Voss - > Memorial
  • Brief Bio: After a failed attempt at taking over the world, Voss could not be exiled, could not be stopped, and was too powerful to move. The only thing that could have been done was to take all the powers combined and freeze him into Mechchem-X a material so unbreakable it makes vibranium look like adamantium.  Unable to move the casing or statue, he remained there. Part as a warning, part as a place to worship.  Suddenly, any creature tainted by the touch of Voss's men started to go erratic, and flock to the statue clawing away at it. They became stronger, faster, harder to kill. Voss was about to be released after a hibernation, with so much power built-up nothing can escape. 
  • Look: It's him in a screaming pose of agony and defeat, but frozen in a gem-like substance, that glows a blue/green/yellowish shiny tinge. Of course, his look includes big armor, shoulder pads, over the top muscles, and weapons. 
  • Power Set: a. The statue irradiates energy making anything around him violent, and powerful. It has a strange feeling, from cold, to heat, to simple pain. Senses go mad, where you may go blind, deaf, dumb, or begin to start feeling weird things. 
  • Notes: This was the hardest. I really couldn't think of something. I went for something akin to Onslaught, kind of.
