I'd liken it more to Marvel's "Acts of Vengeance" storyline, which was specifically about various villains teaming up and trading off to taken down the Avengers one-by-one.  

A similar storyline took place in the recent animated film Justice League: Doom, which dealt with very similar themes.  It was based loosely on the "Tower of Babel" storyline, replacing Ra's al-Ghul with Vandal Savage.
