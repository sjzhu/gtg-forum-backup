I’ve been tossing songs that remind me of various characters into play lists and I finally have a couple that are full enough to be reasonable CD length. Some of the songs are things that character would listen to, and some are just things where the themes or lyrics seemed appropriate.

I polished the Tachyon list up a bit today and figured I’d post it for anyone who’s interested. Her’s isn’t in any particular order, other than trying to avoid jarring transitions. The first 10 are her listening list, the last six are thematic.

  2. Bulletproof by La Roux

  3. Music is My Hot Hot Sex by Cansei de Ser Sexy

  4. Hit Me by The Sounds

  5. Shut Up and Let Me Go by The Ting Tings

  6. She’s so Lovely by The Butchies

  7. Sharp Dressed Man (cover) by Dragonette

  8. Sexy and I know it by LMFAO

  9. Remedy by Little Boots

  10. Around the World by Aqua

  11. Imperfectly by Ani Difranco

  12. Gold by Lamb

  13. Send Me On My Way by Rusted Root

  14. She’s a Genius by Jet

  15. Drive by Melissa Ferrick

  16. Music Box by Decoder Ring

They’re all available on Grooveshark, so you can listen for free once you’ve signed up there.
