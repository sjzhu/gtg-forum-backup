So, I saw a rumor (well, wild speculation really) that one of the upcoming decks would be multiple heroes.

If that's true, I think it will be Miss Information's nemesis.  Specifically, I think Miss Information's nemesis is a bunch of kid superheroes (or sidekicks)

 

I have a few reasons for thinking this:

1) Miss Information's nemesis symbols is a pair of brackets, which in math is used to denote a set.

2) Much of Miss Information's danger comes from the fact that the Freedom Five don't see her as a threat.  I think children are more likely to catch on, even if the adult heroes don't take the kids' warnings seriously.

3) A lot of Miss Information's cards have very basic sounding threats (old lady in the street, cat in the tree).  That sounds less like a job for Bunker, and more of a job for lil'turret (or, you know, whatever his kid version would be).

4) Of the various four-color superhero tropes we've seen so far, the kid sidekick or scrappy kid hero has been missing.

 

But this might be wishful thinking on my part, and really wanting to see a hero deck with Ennead-like mechanics.
