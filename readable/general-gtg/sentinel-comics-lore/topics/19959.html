I have had an idea kicking around for a little while. I'm ashamed to say that it's the sort of thing that wouldn't ever happen if I didn't just sit down and do it, but that I never get around to it. So, rather than letting this idea fall away, I'm going to put it out here for anybody to pick up.

Between the Writer's Room series, the OblivAeon event narrative, and the various self-contained stories we've heard in character episodes...Christopher and Adam have described the outlines for a lot of comics. Enough that I was thinking we could create wiki pages for them!

I had in mind a page for each comic issue that Christopher and Adam have told us something about, listing as much of the following as we have:

  • The title, issue number, and date
  • The cover
  • Characters
  • Locations/environments
  • A plot summary
  • Crossover events the issue is involved with
  • Notable continuity events (costume changes, scars, friendships, first appearances, ...)

We could also make an index page, linking to all the individual issue pages, of course.

Like I said, I had this idea and thought it would be straightforward to go through the Writer's Room episodes, listen to the brief summary that C&A gave at the end of the creative work, and take quick notes. But it turns out that they don't actually give a summary for most of the Writer's Room issues! Figuring out the final issue content will take listening through the whole episode, making sure to record only the final decisions.

Since several Letters Page listeners seem to be making their way through a second or third listen of all the episodes, I figured - why not just toss out the sketch of the idea? At the very least, it would be a great way to collect together all Adam's amazing covers on the Sentinels wiki.
