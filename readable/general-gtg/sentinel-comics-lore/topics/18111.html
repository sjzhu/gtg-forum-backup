What Powerhound said. The "Death of Legacy" story where Paul VII died was in Justice Comics #101 in September of '48 with Paul VIII taking over the Leagcy role the following month in issue #102. The Wraith's first appearance in Mystery Comics #27 coincided with that latter issue of JC (along with Haka's first appearance in Arcane Tales #14). The first issue of the Freedom Four book wasn't until May of 1950 (with the "team-up" aspect coming in in April '52 with FF #24).
