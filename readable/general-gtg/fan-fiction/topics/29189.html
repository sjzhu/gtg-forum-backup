[details=“SCP-#### - “The Dreamer””]
Item #: SCP-####

Object Class: Keter

Special Containment Procedures: SCP-#### Is uncontainable as of this writing as the Foundation is unable to ascertain her location. Once SCP-#### is located, she is to be transported to a Foundation Facility and placed in a standard humanoid containment chamber. Four Scranton Reality Anchors are to be placed around the cell. Great care will be taken to avoid exposing SCP-#### to any potential causes of stress for fear of reawakening her Reality Warping abilities.

Description: SCP-#### (Footnote 1: Real name Vanessa Long) is, or was, a 6 year old chinese girl with black hair. She is capable of manifesting her and others dreams and nightmares into reality, often to frightening effect. So far this has only been observed to happen while she is asleep but there are theories that significant stress could cause her to lose control of her abilities.

SCP-#### came to the Foundation’s attention on #/#/####, when her reality warping abilities first manifested, turning the town of ####### into what can only be described as a twisted, nightmarish hellscape (See Incident Report 1-D). As far as the Foundation is aware this was unintentional as SCP-#### was in a coma at the time.

Incident Report 1-D:

The Foundation sent MTF-Delta-18 (“Dreamcatchers”) to assess the situation and, if possible, contain the anomaly causing it. The Task Force was able to enter the nightmare where they were immediately set upon by nightmares (Footnote 2: A list of known nightmares, dubbed SCP-####-A through -I, is included below). Instead, SCP-####’s nightmares were ended by the Group of Interest known as the Freedom Five and the Person of Interest known as the Visionary (Footnote 3: Requests to reclassify the Visionary as an SCP are currently under review).

Following the incident, an attempt was made to contain SCP-#### which resulted in failure when the Mobile Task Force instead handed her over to the unknown woman (Footnote 4: When interviewed about this later, the Task Force claimed to be unable to recall what had happened during that period of time). Subsequent attempts to locate SCP-#### have met with failure.

Known Nightmare Entities:

[details=“SCP-#### - “The Sun God””]
Item #: SCP-####

Object Class: Euclid

Special Containment Procedures: SCP-#### is to be kept in a standard humanoid containment chamber. The furnishings of the chamber are to be fireproof and the walls are to be reinforced.

SCP-####-A is to be kept in a high security vault with an anti-summoning circle surrounding it. SCP-#### and SCP-####-A are to be kept separate all times.

Description: SCP-#### appears to be an adult male with bronze skin. He dresses in a manner resembling ancient Egyptian pharaohs. He has repeatedly claimed to be Ra, the Egyptian god of the sun.

SCP-#### has demonstrated the ability to control fire and conjure fire from nothing. This includes flame spikes, blasts of fire from both his hands and eyes, and fire tornados. He is also immune to fire.

SCP-####-A is a wooden staff topped with a gold ornament. At the center of the ornament is a red gem of unknown origin. Tests on the staff indicate that it is centuries old, dating back to the Old Kingdom (Footnote 1: about 2,700-2,200 B.C.E) of Egypt. SCP-#### has demonstrated the ability to summon SCP-####-A from anywhere, hence the anti-summoning circle in its containment procedures.

It is theorized that SCP-#### has some connection to the disappearance of renowned archaeologist Dr. Blake Washington Jr. but the Foundation is unsure how.

Addendum R-1 Interview Log:

Audio Version

Interview Performed By: Dr. Hayden Garland

[BEGIN LOG]

SCP-####: Release me, mortal!

Dr. Garland: I’m afraid I can’t do that. Please sit down.

SCP-####: You dare give orders to a God!

Dr. Garland: [sighs] Very well. Continue to stand if you like. Please state your name for the record.

SCP-####: I am Ra, God of the Sun!

Dr. Garland: Please state your real name.

SCP-###: That is my true name!

[Dr. Garland shakes his head]

Dr. Garland: Let’s move on. Do you have any connection to Dr. Blake Washington Jr?

SCP-####: Yes, you could say we are… intimately connected.

Dr. Garland: How so?

SCP-####: I will say no more. This conversation, if you can even call it that, is finished.

[END LOG]

Addendum R-2 Incident Report 3-R-F:

On #/#/##, SCP-#### breached containment with the assistance of an unidentified woman with white wings. Along with SCP-####, SCP-####-A was stolen. Research into this woman’s identity is currently ongoing. The location of both SCP-#### and the unidentified woman are currently unknown.

Dr. Blake Washington Jr. reappeared soon after the Containment Breach. He denies having any knowledge of SCP-#### or what happened to him during his disappearance. The Foundation will conduct close surveillance on him in the future.
[/details]

Item #: SCP-####

For defensive capabilities, it has heavy armor plating that adapts to become resistant to any damage it receives (Footnote 4: In layman’s terms, if you attack it with fire it becomes immune to fire). It is capable of invading almost any technology connected to the internet.

Dr. Kim Howell along with her hand picked research team are currently studying the code of SCP-#### to determine what caused it to become self-aware and wether or not it can even be considered anomalous.

The surviving scientists did not know out how it had gained sentience.

The AI was neutralized by the Freedom Five and several other Persons of Interest, most notably Omnitron-X, supposedly a version of the Omnitron AI from the future. The remains of the Omnitron AI were subsequently recovered by the Foundation and dubbed SCP-####.

During the incident, several MTF Squads were deployed. Though they were largely ineffective against the AI they were able to prevent many Civilian Casualties.

Addendum 2 List of Drones

SCP-####-B S-84 Automaton Drone Humanoid, silver drones resembling human skeletons. The drones are capable of energy attacks. They are also capable of strong melee attacks.
SCP-####-C S-85 Repair Drone A drone resembling an insect with a large thorax. The drones are incapable of attacking, only serving to repair SCP-####.

SCP-#### - "Parse"

Item #: SCP-####

Object Class: Thaumiel

Special Containment Procedures: SCP-#### will continue working for the Foundation. Personnel with Clearance below Level 4 are not to know that SCP-#### has anomalous properties.

Should containment be required, a Standard Humanoid Containment Chamber has been prepared and checked over for weaknesses by SCP-#### herself. Any access to objects small enough to use as a projectile will be denied.

Description: SCP-#### is Dr. Kim Howell, a researcher working in the Foundation’s anomalous tech department. Following an incident involving SCP-#### - “Cosmitron” (Footnote 1: See that file for details) in which she was exposed to large amounts of an unknown energy, SCP-#### developed anomalous abilities, notably the ability to process and analyze data at a speed faster than any normal human.

SCP-#### is able to not only pinpoint weaknesses in both structures and people but also see the connection between events that would otherwise seem unrelated and perceive the after affects certain actions might have. These abilities allow her to essentially see the future, though she can not always see an exact outcome. Additionally, her anomalous properties have enhanced her already not-inconsiderable prowess at archery.

Since gaining anomalous abilities, SCP-#### has been more valuable to the SCP Foundation than ever. In addition to continuing to be our top analyst, she has pointed out several flaws in Site-##’s security system previously overlooked and prevented at least # Containment Breaches.

[details=“SCP-#### “The Legacy Line””]
Item #: SCP-####

Object Class: Euclid Keter (Footnote 1: Following the Iron Legacy Event, see Incident Report I-L-1, SCP-#### has been upgraded from Euclid to Keter.)

Special Containment Procedures: SCP-####-A is currently an agent of the United States Government and thus cannot be contained without causing significant tension between the Foundation and the United States. SCP-####-B will be kept under constant surveillance and kept alive at all costs. No attempts to contain SCP-####-B are to be made unless strictly necessary.

Should circumstances conspire that it is required to contain either of these individuals, two Humanoid Containment Chambers have been prepared. Both chambers are comprised of a special alloy designed by the Foundation to resist the incredible strength of both SCP-####-A and SCP-####-B as well as SCP-####-B’s “Atomic Glare.” Each chamber will be held in separate, remote locations. At least ten guards each will be on active duty around the chambers at all times. Guards will be equipped with guns full of the “Regression Serum” recovered from Ivan Ramonat.

Should either SCP-####-A or SCP-####-B breach containment and the guards be unable to contain them within ten minutes, both sites are to be simultaneously nuked from orbit.

Description: SCP-#### is the collective designation for the Parsons, a family line stretching back at least four centuries. Members of the Parsons family have been known to fight in every major conflict that the United States of America has been involved in since its inception with the exception of the Vietnam War. Though the Parsons line can be traced back to the American Revolutionary War, the name Legacy has only been in use since World War I.

For the past four centuries, every firstborn Parsons has been in possession of anomalous abilities. It is unknown why this is, though members of the Parsons line with anomalous abilities have been known to give off an unknown energy signature. Currently, there are two living Parsons with known anomalous abilities, dubbed SCP-####-A and SCP-####-B.

SCP-####-A is Paul Parsons VIII also known as America’s Finest Legacy. SCP-####-A is a blonde, blue eyed, heavily built man in his late 40s. He is described by those around him to be excessively caring and supportive of others. He is known to give inspirational speeches that leave the listener(s) feeling rejuvenated and hopeful (Footnote 2: Though there is no evidence that these speeches are anomalous in nature, research is ongoing). SCP-####-A’s known anomalous abilities include increased strength, speed, constitution, and eyesight, durability, the ability to negate a single powerful attack, the ability to sense imminent danger, and flight.

SCP-####-B is the daughter of SCP-####-A, Pauline Felicia Parsons also known as Felicia Fields and America’s Newest Legacy. SCP-####-B, much like her father, she is blonde and has blue eyes. She is around 18 years of age and is known to be brash and impulsive, with a strong desire to forge her own identity. SCP-####-B shares the same anomalous abilities as her father with the addition of what she calls an “Atomic Glare” (Footnote 3: Despite its name, the “Atomic Glare” does not actually give off any atomic radiation, instead giving off the same energy as SCP-####-B herself) which acts akin to a strong physical force and can punch through almost any material.

Addendum 1 Incident Report I-L-1

On #/#/####, an interdimensional portal manifested in the City of Megalopolis. Out of it stepped an alternate version of SCP-####-A. Foundation MTFs were the first on the scene as the Department of Multiversal Investigations had predicted the event’s occurrence, though not what would actually happen, 5 days earlier. Once the alternate version of SCP-####-A stepped out of the portal, MTF leader ##### attempted to communicate with him and was subsequently killed. The alternate version of SCP-####-A then proceeded to kill most of the remaining MTF units with only # surviving.

Respond by the Group of Interest known as the Freedom Five was swift after this and after a fierce battle the alternate version of SCP-####-A was pushed back to his own universe via a portal Foundation researchers swiftly constructed with assistance from the Person of Interest known as the Visionary.

Addendum 2

Though the portal was destroyed after the alternate SCP-####-A left through it, enough data was collected that the Department of Multiversal Investigations was able to do further research into the universe the alternate came from. The following document contains a summary of their findings.

I liked this latest entry a lot, but I have a couple nitpicks.

First, this may be a bias from only mostly reading the early entries on the wiki, but I feel like it just isn’t an SCP article without at least a little pointless redacting, especially of proper names. The references to Ivan Ramonat are pretty contrary to the Foundation’s general policy of not clearly identifying anybody; censoring part of the name, while leaving us at least one full name or two initials would be enough that Sentinels fans would get the joke, while SCP fans would still recognize the article as “up to spec” for how the Foundation usually writes their documents.

Second, the Foundation is unlikely to “nuke from orbit” their containment sites, as these usually have a nuclear warhead onsite for self-destruct purposes. And if they didn’t want to do that and did need to rely on an orbital strike (which is far too slow for their purposes most of the time, but we’ll go with it for comic booky reasons), the official documentation again needs to be written more formally. An agent might say “nuke from orbit” in an interview transcript or exploration log, but the actual SCP entry is going to say something more like “activate protocol Alabaster-7 (codename “nuke from orbit”)”, or “initiate a sustained and targeted orbital thermonuclear bombardment with a temperature of not less than twelve thousand Kelvin”.

(While they were busy deleting my second, and so far final, attempt at writing for the site, one of the editors did compliment me on having a pretty good grasp on the technical writing style they like to use, even if they don’t like other aspects of how I do things.)
