#15

Used to run 3 player battles and they were crazy fun.  We went by incaps eliminations style, with limited respawns, when you ran out of respawns your characters leave play when incapped.

It was fun because alliances changed constantly, and more than once a betrayal occurred.

It was fun.

 

I like doing tag-team skirmishes, when your character is incapped they leave play and when they would stand up a new hero enters the field from a designated area.  You can rotate 3 heroes or draft a big team and work through the roster, but it works well, because you get incapped you lose that hero and have to re-enter the battle.
