Scenario #2 is a tough one, your best course of action is trying to block his line of sight with AZ, using Hazard attacks to stop drones from landing in advantageous areas and using abilities that lower drone attack rolls.

It is almost imposible for heroes to win the second one it they lost the first. (assuming the omnitron player plays well)

AZ, Unity and anyone that can clear out multiple drones with one attack is really strong in that scenario.  I like visionary - Unity quite a lot for that one.
