The rules do not say that Aim tokens go away, or even that they affect the next attack, those rules from playtesting appear to be gone.

You can save Aim tokens and use an aim token before an attack to gain its effect on one target you hit with that attack, so you could not spend 4 before an attack to use its effect on four different target.

You can save them up and use them on an overwhelm with a bunch of proletariats using hammer of the people on the next round.

 

Proletariat is almost unkillable with Defensive sacrifice and overwhelm (once you get a few copies out) you restock with overwhelm/duplicate, and if you don't need to do that you can overwhelm move/sprint, or overwhelm hammer the crap out of someone.  When they turn to kill you you sacrifice your clones and restock them the next turn.
