Fueled Freeze adds additional radius to his Area Attacks, but not his Hazard Attacks. (Area attacks affect all targets within the radius; hazard attacks trigger against specific targets within the radius when those targets move or end turn.) Thermal Shockwave is Absolute Zero's only current Area Attack, but forward-compatibility is important; Absolute Zero may have another Area Attack someday.

 

Christopher has said that (hopefully Septemberish) www.sentineltactics.com will have a Card database, with FAQs and such. In the meantime, feel free to ask any questions here; there's invariably a response before too long.
