You can stack them in one action.

each one must be adjacent to another one so you could go:

 

AZ - IS - IS

Then go back towards AZ

AZ - IS2 - IS

Then go back again if you have Fueled Freeze.

AZ - IS2 - IS2

You can also end up with

AZ - IS - IS2 -IS

Or several other ways.

 

This works insanely well to block LoS lines for Baron Blade and Omnitron's drone launching.  When you raise a street level to height of 3 it messes with people.
