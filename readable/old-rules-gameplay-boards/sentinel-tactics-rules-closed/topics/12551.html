1 Prole=all Proles and they all share a health pool.  So Defensive Sacrifice is a great card if you want to run up into the thick of things and not risk getting incapped.  Essentially, as long as you still have 1 clone safe somewhere, you can get a clone to the front lines and if it takes a big hit, it goes away instead of the character taking damage.  That Proletariat is the one being targeted with the attack that does damage
