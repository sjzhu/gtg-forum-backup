1a. No, Pylons do not have a turn, so are not able to end their turn on a hazard space, thus immune.

1b. Player created hazards last until the card that created is removed.

2. Just the 1 movement, the character goes back to normal after that.

3.Yes they need LoS, unless otherwise stated.

4. In the setup, the last sentence. It has you make a pile of 5 Scenario Markers, at the end of every round you remove 1. After the last 1 is removed, the match ends.

All reach does is allow you to check as if the target is closer. If a target is at range 3, then AZ's then dice rolling 1+ will not be removed for not making range. If the target is at range 4, then rolls of 2+ will not be removed for not making range.

It does not increase the radius of Thermal Shockwave.
