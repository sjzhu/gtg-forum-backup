2.  Yes they would, at the end of the Villain turn, all targets brought into play by powers of the villain, that do not get their own turn also trigger hazards.

3.  When Omniron moves it is not three hexes moving, you select one Hex omnitron occupied and move with that hex, then set him back down on the final Hex.  So when moving Omnitron has a profile of one hex, so you aren't continuing through it unless you choose to, and Omnitron could move through a one hex opening between two hazard spaces wihtout triggering them, but he will trigger things for setting his other two hexes when you set him back down.

That said, he is one target, and multiple hexes of Omnitron will not trigger the same hazard
