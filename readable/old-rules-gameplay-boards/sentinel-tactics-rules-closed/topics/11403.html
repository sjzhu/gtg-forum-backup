Your "Movement" is different from your "move value". Your movement is the number and arrow printed on your sheet that tells you how many dice to roll and which to keep. The one you keep tells you your "move value".

 

Adhesive Foam Pellets and the other power reduce move value, but they don't change your movement -- at the end of your turn, you'll reroll movement and generate a fresh, untainted move value.
