Devastating Aurora's incap does not count as a Incap for winning.

Sentinel Tactics is more of a team based game. While all characters are balanced, some of ways of countering other characters. Take 2 versus 2 and the match will be much more balanced.

Dawn is one of the strongest characters in the game, and 1v1 she is really tough.  Ra isn't a great 1v1 character, because while he can outdamage anyone, he is far too easy to incap.  Legacy + Ra is a crazy team, since Legacy can protect Ra.

There are characters that are really well suited to defeating specific other characters, and it balances out a lot more in team fights.

Also while Skirmishes are cool, the straight up fight to incaps style is great for learning the game, but not much more.  Which is why we've been working on other skirmish types.

The game really comes into its own when you get to teams of 3.  That is my favorite team size, not so big that rounds take forever, but there is so much more flexibility in team comp.
