#2

The attack is resolved after dice are blocked and any damage that gets through is dealt.

It does not have to deal damage.

Like Stun Bolt and Kusarigama, they don't have to deal damage either.  As ranged attacks they recquire at least one die to make range (so you can't Stun Bolt or Kusarigama someone way out of range) but with the melee attack it doesn't matter.  As soon as the dice are removed for auto-missing, blocked or damage dealt the effect takes place.

Light Speed Barrage can work with Sucker Punch, take the wound, and Sucker Punch rolls dice equal to your current movement.

I don't believe that health cost abilities can be used to self-incap, that would be game-breaking in any game where you record incaps for the offense.  If that is allowed you would definitely incap before gaining any benefit.  The health is removed first, then the effect occurs.

I've been thinking about this more and I'm thinking maybe Sucker Punch does need to hit to have its effect. Firstly, it doesn't really make sense for a punch that doesn't connect with the target to take them down. But secondly, and more compellingly, Baron Blade has a similar power, Maniacal Gloating, but it's just an action that automatically incapacitates the target if they have 1 health left. (And gives them penalty tokens, of course.) There's only two reasons I can think of to structure Sucker Punch as an attack instead of an automatically effective action.

1) If it has the potential to deal damage before the automatic incapacitation is checked. Possible, but it would be unlikely to happen given the 1 die it rolls, and if you made that attack on a target that already had one HP and did damage, then the special effect would be moot. Also, to me it seems like an attack would "resolve" at the end of the attack step, before defense and thus before damage is actually dealt. I could be wrong about that, though.

2) If it has the potential to miss, and thus not take effect. In this interpretation, you would not succeed in triggering the special effect if all the dice were removed, by automisses, failing to make range, or by a minus attack token. But again, since I assume that an attack would "resolve" before defense dice were rolled, you could still KO your target regardless of their defenses as long as you had one or more successful dice. This makes more sense to me.

Either way, it's speculation and an official read would be nice.

#10

How Sucker Punch works.

Declare an adjacent target that you will by attacking.

Roll attack dice.

Enemy rolls defense dice.

Enemy takes damage from any unblocked dice.

If enemy has 1 HP it is incapped.

Sucker Punch itself does not need to make range, however it is a Melee attack, so it has to be someone adjacent to the target when selecting. Unless the defender has a way to counter attack before the attack is resolved, the defender will be incapped if they have 1 health left.

You move before dice are ever rolled or anything.  After moving if you aren't eligible the attack never starts, it is declared and then aborted.  You wouldn't use an Aim token you were intending to use or lose an Attack-1 token, the attack fails.
