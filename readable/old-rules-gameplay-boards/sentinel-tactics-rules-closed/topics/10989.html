On Synaptic Interruption, after blocking the attack and sending those blocked attacks back to your attacker, does the attacker then get a chance to roll defenses against what is essentially their own attack? Yeah, you always defend against an attack. You reassign the dice that you blocked as an attack from tachyon, so you pretend like Tachyon was attacking them and happened to roll the same numbers on the dice.

One important thing to note on "re-assigned" attacks is that you cannot add dice to it with attack+1 tokens, nor will it trigger using an attack-1 token.  You also can't use aim to affect it.  Those exact dice will be reassigned, you can't affect them otherwise.
