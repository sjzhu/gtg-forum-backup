This is the kind of thing that would probably be cleaned up towards the end of playtesting, but I'll mention it here just in case -- when you refer to a ship, you put a "the" at the beginning.  The Vigilance, the Grey Hammer, the Tharendim.  The "the" doesn't need to be in the ship's name because it's added when you refer to them.  However, some of the ships do have the "the" put in their names on their panels: The Bjarlspire, The Ranak Warbeast, The Conclave of Niphon.  For consistency's sake, I would think the "the" should be removed from all the ships' names.

Also, "Bjarlspire" has a capitalized "S" on the ship panel, but it's not capitalized in the descriptive text on Kickstarter.  For consistency's sake, I would think the name on the panel wouldn't have the capital "S" either.
