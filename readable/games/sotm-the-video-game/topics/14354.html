I purchased the game and the Season 1 pass and have been enjoying playing immensely. My problem occurred when the Shattered Timelines expansion was released. Was told I didn't have enough available space to download and install even with 2 gigs free. Here is the issue as I understand it. 
 
 
(1) Season Pass is its own DLC rather than unlocking the individual DLC so you have to redownload all the previous DLC just to get what's new. 
 
 
(2) Even though you can move apps to a SD card on the Kindle the DLC does not move and takes up space on the internal memory. 
 
 
These two situations combined mean that Sentinels takes up over half the available space on a brand new Fire (I've not installed anything else) and so prevents you from being able to update and the only fix I could find to get Shattered Timelines was to completely uninstall the game and do a fresh install. I'd rather not do that for every update so was hoping to get some advice from other Kindle owners.
