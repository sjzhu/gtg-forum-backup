Now that the Void Guard is out, I wanted to see all the different nemesis dialogue variations for them and La Capitan. So, here they are! (As the Void Guard variants are revealed, I'll add them here)

 

Normal:

La Capitan: "Ahoy, Niños! Have over all of your valuables, or be thrown overboard!"

Sentinels: "We stand as one! We fight together! And we oppose your evil ways!"

Adamant Sentinels: "Everyone, into Alpha Formation! Just like we practiced! We're ready for this."

Dr. Medico: "Heads up, heroes! Dr. Medico is on call!"

Mainstay: "We got ourselves a little a [sic] learnin' opportunity here. Welcome to the School of Hard Knocks!"

Idealist: "If we beat up the villain quick, will we have time to get ice cream?"

Writhe: "Hush. The shadows have some silence they want you to hear."

 

Nemesis:

Vs Sentinels:

LC: "And so we meet again! Or is this the first time? It is hard to know. But it shall be the last time!"

S: "Once again, the time-travelling ne'er-do-well! You're going to jail this time!"

 

Vs. Adamant Sentinels:

LC: "You can withstand the seas of time, landlubbers! The time-turbulence has you looking rather green!"

AS: "Ha, ha, very funny. Is there not a timeline in which you learn better jokes?"

 

Vs. Dr. Medico:

LC: "Si, my crew could always use a Medico, glowing one. First, your defeat. Then, you shall join my crew!"

DM: "Great, now I have to sterilize the area. We don't want any bystanders picking up 17th Century diseases!"

 

Vs. Idealist:

LC: "La Pequeña Centinela! Have we met before? Do you remember who I am?" (Note, it should be Centinela Pequeña, because Spanish, but whatever)

I: "Look out, it's Maria Helena Teresa Fafila Blah Blah Blah de Falcon, and she's being condescending!"

 

Vs. Mainstay:

LC: "Quieres Bailar, Mainstay? I know how much you enjoy my unexpected visits!"

M: "We've done this dance before, darlin'. Can't say it'll work out any better for you this time."

 

Vs. Writhe:

LC: "To ride the shadows themselves... Think of the potential bounty to be had with such power..."

W: "You want my cloak? Take it. All you have to do is reach inside..."
