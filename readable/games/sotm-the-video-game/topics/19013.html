 I was going to say most of my YouTube channel is recordings of sentinels from iPad directly, if you’re on iOS 11 follow the instructions below.

 

1. Go to settings, control center, customise controls and make sure Screen Recording is included.

2. Swipe down from the upper right of the screen (where the power indicator is)

3. Press the record button.

4. When you finish repeat steps two and three to stop recording.

5. Your new video should shortly be under photos and can be transferred to a pc or Mac like any other photo.
