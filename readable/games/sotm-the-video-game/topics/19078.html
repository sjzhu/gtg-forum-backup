Here's an unofficial fireside chat preview! We'll be getting those all together once more bugs are fixed and we have time to collect them up.

If OblivAeon deals damage to the Representative of Earth via Disrupt Spacetime (“move any heroes damaged this way to the other Battle Zone”) then, that hero and the Representative of Earth card go on a trip! They live in that other environment until something sends them back. If/when Representative of Earth gets destroyed, it returns to its proper trash pile. If the Game Over effect occurs, it takes place in that Battle Zone, destroying the other environment rather than the Celestial Tribunal.

Hmm, you're making me want to buck the whole system and say that since the Representative of Earth and the hero target associated are now in two different Battle Zones, the target can't be tracked by that card (Like Stun Bolts. It can't keep tracking the trigger "would deal damage"), and therefore can't actually detect when that hero is incapacitated and thus, will never trigger the Game Over text, unless that hero comes back to the CT battle zone. Consequently, with my new stance, if the RoE card leaves play while the other hero is not in the same battle zone, then that hero doesn't go back in the box.

I understand that Christopher and Handelabra have reached an accord for ease of use (can't have cards floating without a play area/associated card), but this might be the first time that I explicitly houserule against the digital version, as I think it's funnier to view the rules as explicitly written. The Celestial Tribunal has put someone on trial, are going through their proceedings, and then Oblivaeon comes out of nowhere, punches that hero through reality, and then leaves. The CT is then left to scratch their heads and declare recess while they wait for the summons they put out to pull that hero back so they can resume trial.

With the digital acommodation, the ROE card is not in the CT environment when it pops. Period. It is, for all intents and purposes, part of the other battle zone's Environment. It is in the environment zone of the other environment, so it is part of the other environment. The game over effect takes place in an Environment, not in a deck. It's the difference between saying
"[...]Instead, all cards from that Environment's Deck are removed [...]"
and
"[...]Instead, all cards from that Environment Card's Deck are removed [...]"
