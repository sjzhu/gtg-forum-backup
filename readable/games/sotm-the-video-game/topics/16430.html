I'm doing the "play all villains with the new team" thing, too (albeit with guest stars).

The one thing about Termi-Nation is they're all pretty slow and need a lot of setup. Absolute Zero is going to spend a lot of time skipping his power phase until he's good and ready to unleash frosty heck. Unity's card-draw is fantastic for her, but also means you'll have a lot of turns without any bots in play (not too different from how I usually play her, at least; and you can always Flash Forge something into the trash to put in play!) TN Bunker sets up a lot faster than his counterparts, but that's still pretty slow compared to many other heroes. (He's still one of the best Bunkers.)

So it takes a little patience, and if you're playing a villain who punishes patience, you might find yourself in a heap of trouble if you don't have anyone else to rely on. Still, Unity with a lot of setup is a monster, no matter who you're fighting (and TN AZ doubly so), so they can be rewarding!

The only real 'synergy' I see in them, because they don't really have much in the way of interaction built into their decks, is having Unity give AZ an extra power usage to boost his damage the net turn.

One neat trick I did once, though -- using them (with Legacy and TLT) against Apostate, and Unity was up, with AZ playing clean-up -- but AZ had used Violent Shivers last turn, had Cold Snap out, and there was a Relic Spirit on the board that he would've killed with it before getting a chance to finish off Apostate. Easily solved, however, by nuking Bee-Bot, taking out Cold Snap, smacking Apostate and putting a Platform Bot back into play.

Outside of that, though, there's very little in the way of interaction between them.
