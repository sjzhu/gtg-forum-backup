Powerhound_2000:

In those cases the unlocks are only looking for targets that entered the trash and doesn't care who did it.  In this situation Fanatic has to specifically destroy the targets and if they are under Savage Mana she isn't destroying them.  

Note, though, that End of Days is attributed to her.  When End of Days triggers, it destroys all cards on the field in the order of your choosing.  The cards below Savage Mana count as targets (as they do for all effects which can target Environment cards).  Thus, provided you choose to destroy the cards under Savage Mana before you destroy Savage Mana, then that destruction will be attributed to Fanatic, as the player of End of Days.
