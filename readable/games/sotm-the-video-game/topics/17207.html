He's a bad choice for just about any unlock, because unless you have reliable, early ongoing destruction (which is impossible – only one character has Ongoing destruction as a base power, but he still requires certain cards in hand to activate it), you're playing with a time limit.

And even if you have tons of ready ongoing destruction, he has a card that pulls out several Ongoings at once, so you can't really avoid the countdown.

Ambuscade, however, you can stall indefinitely. He can only win through doing damage (which is easily manageable), and he never destroys any of your stuff. So if there's a particular set-up you need for an unlock, you can do it with him no problem.
