Soooooo…

Not sure if I did this one “correctly” as it was my longest game ever.

BUT!

I decided to take the game’s name to heart and I had fun doing it.

Ended with all 4 heroes having their entire decks in their hands except for what they had in play and full HP. Haka Savage Mana’d every single target in both Voss’s and the Wasteland’s decks. Ended up splatting Voss with 34 Savage Mana and 33 Haka of Battle for 104 toxic damage.

Things I learned:

  1. If you eat all of the environment, Ancient Library is free card draw and Con’s Bunker is free health

  2. If Legacy has his Ring, Fortitude, Motivational Charge, Superhuman Durability, Surge of Strength, and Inspiring Presence all out, with 1 other damage reduction (such as Energy Bracer), he can punch himself in the face for 2+1 Galvanize +1 Ins Pres +1 SoS =5 -3 SD -1 Fort -1 EB = 0 damage and heal everyone!

  3. If Haka has Dynamic Siphon and it is the lowest hero target, Voss’s Flipped side will hit it before his minon goes, and Haka can eat it

  4. You actually have to try really hard to NOT kill the boss if you want to cycle through his entire deck. No Unity Bots allowed (see below)

  5. I realized after the game that I could have had Haka eat the bots for even more damage. I may have to try this again sometime and feed him the bots as well

  6. Haka will need a Cosmic Weapon to eat the ships. The above is a good way to get Cosmitron if you haven’t already.
