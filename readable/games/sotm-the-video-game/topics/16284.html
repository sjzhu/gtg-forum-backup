SPOILER ALERT:  It seems to me that the first round is pretty important, because Dreamer spits out damage so fast.  Here's what I've been doing -- unsuccessfully.  Is anybody trying different tactics?

 

Dreamer:  2 Grotesque Arachnoids, Tooth Fairy, Toy Master, Illusory Demon. All things being equal, this is not a terrible draw--in some of my practice rounds I had double Tooth Fairies, or (ugh) double Dark Heroes.). When Tooth Fairy comes out, I force-discard:

 

-Positive Energy (yeah, losing the healing sucks, but you can’t scrap Sentinel Tactics, I want to use Fling into Darkness to instantly get rid of one projection, and TK Wallop hits harder and more selectively.)

-Bounty Board (geez, what a terrible draw for Chrono--three cards that bring back (at this point nonexistent) bounties from the trash?)

-Scorched Earth.  It's Indiscriminate AOE damage, and much as I'd like to burn her to a crisp, we can't harm dear little Vanessa, can we?

-Critical Multiplier. The way this game has been going, Parse hasn't been discarding that many cards, so this just isn't as useful as Buffer Overflow or 2 irreducible-damage one-shots.

 

1st Hero Turn:

 

SENTINELS:

Play: Fling into Darkness on Illusory Demon--kills him instantly, and no Ongoings in play for him to destroy.

Power: Haymaker on one of the Arachnoids (8-->7HP)

Draw: Restorative Burst

 

CHRONO RANGER:

Play:  Displaced Armory -- here’s where a real choice comes in:  Usually when playing Chrono, I choose the Hat first, every time.   Here, though, Jim’s initial hand is so crappy (two of the cards have no use and can’t be played), and his opportunities for extra card draw so limited, that you would need to wait several rounds just in hopes of getting the extra cards to make use of the Hat’s power. In the meantime, the heroes are getting killed, and Jim is stuck doing 1 damage as his power.  So--get the Hat, or grab a weapon?  If the latter, which?  Danny Boy or Masadah (hoping to get some bounties later to power them up)?  Dart-Thrower, and let Jim DR every round?  Bow? (hoping that Ra will draw Imbued Flame or that Parse can provide some buffs)?  The Grenade seems the least useful, because Dreamer has no Ongoings, and getting rid of environment cards isn’t nearly as important as stopping the Projections from killing you.  I went with the Dart-Thrower, but arguments can be made for any of them, and used my 1 damage on the Arachnoid that Mainstay hit. (7HP -> 6 HP)

Power:  Dart-Thrower on the as-yet undamaged *other* Arachnoid--no damage, but reduce its damage by 1.  

Draw:  No Executions.

 

RA:

Play:  Staff of Ra.  (He has no damaging powers, so let’s at least heal him/set him up).

Power:  Draw 3 (Staff of Ra, Fire Blast, Solar Flare), discard 2 (Solar Flare and Wrathful Gaze - I agonized over this one; I wanted to keep Wrathful Gaze in case of a Whipacorn attack, but given that Parse is going to get a Reveal the Flaws, I wanted to keep Solar Flare as a potential damage boost.)

Draw:  another Staff of Ra.

 

PARSE:

Play:  Impossible Shot -- kill Tooth Fairy

Draw:  Reveal the Flaws

 

My idea from here on out is to have Chrono keep DRing stuff, have RA FireBlast/sacrifice his Staff to kill stuff, and bring out Parse’s Buffer and Sentinel Tactics next round (doing it first round would have eventually sacrificed one of them to the Illusory Demon, most likely).  Some round, Parse will play the Reveal the Flaws, and Ra will use Solar Flare as a one-time damage boost that round--the way Dreamer deals out damage, Ra just can't afford the *4* (ouch!) damage to keep it in play. Seeing as I'm 0-10 (or more), I'm not sure if I should offer "tips," but...

-One thing I've discovered is that if you don't blow up the environment, you can all but ignore the Granite Onis when they come out, since they target the lowest HP *target*, not just the lowest HP hero.  This means that they usually attack the environment for you.  When you're ready to flip Dreamer, have Parse play a Reveal the Flaws and take them out.

-This is subjective, but I think you need to flip Dreamer while your high-HP heroes (Parse, Ra, Chrono) are at least in the mid-teens, HP-wise.  Any lower than that, and you only have a round or two before Advanced Dreamer's ridiculous damage (+1 damage + play three cards) kills you all.  

-Putting Gauge on Parse while you're still trying to flip Dreamer (a) gives Parse more cards, and (b) allowed me to bury a couple of Dark Heroes while I was still trying to get Dreamer to flip.  I'm not sure how much it helps *after* the flip, since Dreamer plays 3 cards per turn; might be a good candidate to blow up when Dreamer kills 4 of your Ongoings.

 

 

SPOILER ALERT:

 

 

-On Dreamer's second card play (i.e., after the heroes' first turn), she's going to play the card that says "The hero with the most cards in play cannot play cards."  This will ding The Sentinels, since each of their character cards counts as a "card in play."  If you want to get out Sentinel Tactics early (I haven't been doing this, because I want to Fling into Darkness the Illusory Demon), make it your very first card play, or you won't get the chance until Hero Turn #3.

I had a long chat with a coworker today about this match, and we concluded that I should try it like Skinwalker Gloomweaver; get as set up as possible, and go nuts after the flip. So, that's what I did.  Parse was easily the MVP of the match.  Here's roughly how it went down, keeping in mind that this was a really long match and I can't remember all the details.

Dreamer's Front Side: I got out as many ongoings as possible, some worthwhile (Aura of Vision, Hippocratic Oath, Imbued Fire, Extrasensory Awareness, 3x Crit Multiplier, 2x Exploit Vulnerability, all the Bounties) and some destruction fodder (Flesh of the Sun God, Living Conflagration, Gauge, Drawn to the Flame). Once I found it in Parse's deck, I used Extrasensory Awareness every round to sift through Dreamer's deck and discard the really nasty stuff. I intentionally flipped her when she was down to 7 cards in her deck, all of which were Projections, all of which were Not Dark Heroes.

During this ridiculously long setup, The Sentinels ended up drawing basically their entire deck and by the end of it, they were playing Restorative Burst, using Dr. Medico's power, and using Hippocratic Oath for a total of 2 AOE healing per round. Chrono-Ranger was also healing via his healing bounty. Ra unfortunately blew up three of his staves early on to help burn through Projections, so he was relying on the Sentinels for healing. Probably could have done that better.  Chrono-Ranger was also pinging environment targets with the NeuroToxin dart thrower in order to nullify their AOE damage, and he had The Ultimate Target on a Battalion Brute so he could get multiple Dart Thrower uses each round to help nerf the environment.

When I finally flipped the Dreamer (with all my heroes in the mid-teens for HP) by taking out that last Granite Oni, plowing through her Projections was trivial. Parse was playing Recompile every round for 9 stored damage boosts, Chrono-Ranger could do 5-6 irreducible with Masada, and 2x Exploit Vulnerability meant that any damage reduction on the board was nullified. For two rounds, Dreamer played 3 projections, the Sentinels and Chrono-Ranger absolutely obliterated them, Ra twiddled his thumbs, and Parse played another Recompile to get everyone charged up for the next round. 

On the last round, Dreamer shuffled trash into deck and did get off a lightly boosted AOE attack, but Writhe was the only character who ended up getting incapped. Final HP 3/0/5/3, 3, 3, 9. I probably could have done a little better - charging up Hippocratic Oath instead of Mainstay / Idealist could have been healing me for 8-10 per round, and that way Ra could have actually contributed some damage. But hey, it worked out.
