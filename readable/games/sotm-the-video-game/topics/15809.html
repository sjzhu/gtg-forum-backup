I had a surprisingly fast game, winning in only three rounds. The start is setup for Tachyon to get Chaos Lord by blinking, and Chaos Lord loves cards like Pushing the Limits, Oblivion, and Proportionist (to increase Sky-Scraper's huge power). By her third turn she had 10 bursts in her trash and two Lightspeed Barrages in her hand, and that and the 3-4 Oblivion (which gave Nightmist The Indiscriminate) and a Synaptic Interruption caused Kaargra to flip in the same turn that she entered play, as well as add an amusingly high number of tokens to the hero favor pool, winning us the game.

Meanwhile, Visionary had fun using Turmoil on the Title Deck, and Wraith ended up being a non-damage dealer, due to using Infrared Eyepieces her first turn and not being able to deal damage due to Ashclaw R'velos on her second.

Missed my first shot, due to something I hadn't realized before....

 

When calculating sources of damage for Mindbreaker, each of Sky-Scraper's sizes counts as a different character card. I was at 19 favor, and was counting on winning by Sky-Scraper taking damage from other heroes, but I changed sizes on Sky-Scraper's turn, and the game tracked two different streams for Sky-Scraper's damage sources. She ended up with 2 sources for one size (huge), and then 3 sources for another size (normal). I was surprised and saddened when she didn't pick up Mindbreaker for the win, and then I promptly got stomped on Kaargra's turn.

Someday I will beat Kaargra without her flipping.  Today was not that day.  Got to 18 favor and had put both Get Back In There's on the bottom, so I thought I had it for sure.  The only gladiator left had only 4 HP, but also Stonejaw, so I thought I was safe.  Unfortunately, Tachyon had Chaos Lord, with Supersonic Response on the bottom, so she hit him for 2 when Sustain the Portal came out.  I still thought I was OK (should have been 3 damage), so I was tapping through all those instances of damage pretty quickly, so I was surprised to see him die and Kaargra flip, at 19 favor (like she always does...).
