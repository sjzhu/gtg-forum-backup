Version 2.6 with Mini-Pack 4 is now available on Amazon Appstore. I know it can take some time for the Appstore to update automatically, but it's there! https://www.amazon.com/Handelabra-Studio-LLC-Sentinels-Multiverse/dp/B00…

It was actually up Saturday morning.  Had to clear my Amazon app data to force the download, but I've played a mess of games so far.

Something I wasn't expecting: the word I'd most use to define both new heroes is "inconsistent."

Benchmark has so many unique cards, he can never be sure of getting anything in particular.  Every game with him will be very different; it's hard to use a strategy in advance.  Sometimes you get nice combos, sometimes you don't.  Thematically it makes sense–he's sort of an Inspector Gadget-type, with a lot of useful tools but not a lot of experience–but it can be frustrating at times.  It does add replay value, though.

Stuntman is heavily dependent on getting either Lance-Flammes or Pistolet-Mitralleur.  His base power has its uses, but is usually vastly inferior to those.  Sky-Scraper and her Micro-Assembler can be his best friends.  He also has a lot of cool things to do that may or may not be what you really want.  I've only drawn In Medias Res three times out of a dozen or so games, and in none of them was I able to use it to particularly good effect.  So he's also very swingy–can be a lot of fun but is often just going through the motions.
