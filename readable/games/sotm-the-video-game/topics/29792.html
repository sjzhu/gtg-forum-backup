My first round wasn’t that unusual, at least in my head. I just went all in on Lifeline. And it wasn’t Hyperactive Senses that got me going, it was:

Spoiler

Repair Ley-Line. It brought out Defensive Displacement which then ended up being destroyed at the start of the environment turn. Technically, it was using Argent Adept’s base power on Lifeline to play Calculated Action to play Haunted Memories to pull Unleash Energy out of the discard and then play Unleash Energy with Calculated Action’s second card play and then play Repair Ley-Line.
