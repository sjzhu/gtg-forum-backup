The Lowsley brothers team up with many of Hugh's friends in Dok'Thorath to confront the spawn of Nigel's mind.

Advanced Heroic Infinitor vs Requital Captain Cosmic, Haka, Sky-Skraper and Tempest in Dok'Thorath.

Sloggy but fairly standard mint.

As is usual with Heroic Infinitor your goal here is to prevent Nigel from being hit. Spamming constructs to protect him from the Reapers, redirections (be careful of Enduring Intercession until you get Ta Moko, Energy Bracer and/or Aggression Modulator out. There are a lot of constructs to get hit), and healing. Just make sure Nigel doesn't get hit as that +2 burns through his HP fast.

Also note that the 0 damage from Portia's Concussive Clap is increased by the +2 so be judicious when you use it. Tempest gets an early Hailstorm so it doesn't hurt too much to give up the area damage.

Mint.

Easier than I thought it would be. Infinitor took a lot of hard knocks early, which made me think I’d have to deal with the flip, but after that I had little trouble keeping him alive. This, despite stupid things like forgetting again that Into The Stratosphere doesn’t work on indestructible manifestations.

Early on the main threat to Infinitor was the Lambent Reapers, but once Captain Cosmic put a few constructs in play, they started chasing those.

Double Aggression Modulators on the Thorian Military meant I had no real need to do damage, since the Thorians were blasting away at them for 6 damage per turn. Mostly my heroes sat idle while waiting for Infinitor to play one of his “1 damage to everything” cards, which took a while.

Otherworldly Resilience and Energy Bracer meant Tempest could tank any damage redirected away from the Abject Refugees. I played Thorian Monolith a couple of times to reduce damage from manifestations, but eventually Captain Cosmic got the Energy Crest out, which neutered most of the manifestations.

The environment was the main threat to Infinitor. It helped that a couple of Syphons on Tempest meant he could retaliate immediately with Grevious Hailstorm, which sometimes killed off the environment card before it could do anything else.
