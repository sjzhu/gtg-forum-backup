A propos of little, I've been enjoying recently taking a hand-picked team against the Advanced Ennead:

 

Legacy

Ra

Tempest

Haka

Fanatic

 

My thinking was that I wanted the boost from Legacy, the AoE from Tempest, and then as much consistent damage as I can muster. Ra's nemesis bonus, Imbued Fire boost, and AoE from Scorched Earth are all also greatly desired. 

 

I've won less then half of the matches so far. One unexpected star card has been Heroic Interception. A couple of my wins had everyone at high health except for Legacy, who was low or incapacitated. Another great card has been Ground Pound.

 

I am not claiming that these five are the best configuration for beating the Advanced Ennead. They just happen to be what I was playing. I imagine getting Argent Adept to give Tempest more power uses would be pretty cool, as would getting out Mr. Fixer's Jackhandle or Dual Crowbars. Expatriette's Submachine gun with an attached Hollow Points would be pretty sweet, too.

One really disgusting combo you could try is Legacy + Visionary + TL Tachyon.  You use TL + Visionary to give Legacy the draw to find 2 Take Downs, and then you just use Mental Divergence to cycle them.  To be fair, that works against MOST villains, but especially so against Ennead, because plays are everything to them.

 

Edit: Add in Argent Adept, and you could do it with just 1 Take Down.  AA Sarabande's Take Down away, Mental Divergence puts it back on top, TL gives him the draw, he plays it on his turn.  With Rhapsody of Vigor and Motivational Charge, you could keep that up forever.
