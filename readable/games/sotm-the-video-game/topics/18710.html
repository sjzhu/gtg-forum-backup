Not-easy Mint. I think the final totals were 8 5 4 6 4.

He starts off with an indestructible Pouch of Bones! Zombies become really hard to kill! So use gratuitous No Executions, or just Brain Burn and get them all back into the deck so he doesn't heal off all the damage he takes! Turning Gloomweaver's home turf against him was just immensely satisfying.

And don't forget, Ultimate also means Gloomy's immune to melee and projectile damage. Idealist does a lot of the former for a psychic (though large Karate Robots are still worth it), and Chrono-Ranger won't even be able to hit him until you summon some of his equipment!
