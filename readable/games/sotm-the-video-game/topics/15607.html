McBehrer:

The only ones I haven't completed yet are Advanced Wrath of the Thorathian, Rescue Under the Sea, Frontier Justice, When It Rains, True Heroes of Destiny, and Ridiculous Challenge Time.  Any advice on those?

Oh cool, some of those go back quite a ways.  I'd love to help with these, it's been months since I've tried several of them.

Ridiculous Challenge Time's thread is still alive on Steam, you might want to check those forums for thoughts.  In general, The Contract is the worst, Hairtrigger Reflexes and Driving Mantis can be your best friends, and don't feel bad if you have a lot of trouble with it.  Not a whole lot of people were able to complete that one.  A couple people did manage to mint it, but most who finished were like me and took 20+ tries to get it done.  On the Sentinels Live stream, I think they said the win rate on that game was under 1%.

When It Rains starts rough, but it gets easier if you leave the Gene-Bound Firesworn alive and try to keep Absolute Zero at the highest HP.  Eventually, Voss's deck slows down, Adhesive Foam Grenade becomes your best friend, you get Cleansing Downpour out, and you get time to set up to nuke Voss.  If you can keep everyone alive after they get down into single-digits, it's easily possible to build up powerful attacks to one-round KO Voss and have everyone back up into the 20s.  

I remember that I abused the crap out of the environment in "Rescue" Under the Sea, to the point where I think the Krakens did as much damage to villains as I did, but I'd have to play it again to remember how I did it.  I'll be back with more thoughts on these later, or you can drop me a message if you like.
