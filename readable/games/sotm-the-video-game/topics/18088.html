Really easy mint. I guess they heard that I screwed up the easy Voss in Egypt challenge, so they lowered the difficulty another notch.

Didn’t do anything clever, didn’t use Into the Stratosphere even once.

Visionary put Twist the Aether on Ra since he was consistently doing damage, though in retrospect it would have been smarter to put it on Visionary herself, since this variant does damage pretty much every turn. Normally I treat Twist the Aether as a defensive card, but doing damage to Blade on his front side is a much higher priority than blunting his occasional attacks.

I left the Obsidian Field up for a long time just because of the damage race on Blade’s front side.

I used Wrest the Mind in the canonical fashion - on the second Blade Battalion to come out, so it was doing 6 points of damage a turn to whoever I wanted (+1 damage for Obsidian Field).

I made a point of leaving the Blade Battalions alive while Blade was on his front side, just to avoid the chance of Blade’s discard pile growing, but I flipped him so fast it turned out to be unimportant.

Haka was the only hero to get seriously hurt because he kept stepping in front of damage. At one point he was down to 1 HP. Unusually for Haka, he never did any damage himself, since I drew no damaging cards.
