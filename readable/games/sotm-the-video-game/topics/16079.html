This actually may be working as intended.

Jackhandle actually replaces what would have been the original attack with the new attack jackhandle creates. There is a rider clause on Catch-a-Ride that requires the first attack to have happened and dealt damage for the 2nd attack to activate. Since the first attack from CaR never happened (since it was replaced by the jackhandle effect for a different attack) it seems to me that the condidtion for the 2nd effect was never actually met.

Strange edge case here! It certainly would seem intuitive that CaR's 2nd attack would occur, and thats very likely how I would play it live on the table. But it doesn't surprise me thats how it works in the game due to how Jackhandle is coded.
