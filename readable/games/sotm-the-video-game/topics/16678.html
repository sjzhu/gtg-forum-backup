Wow, am I the first to post? Well, so it goes.

The Eternal Haka, OG Bunker, and OG Ra against OG Baron Blade in Wagner Mars Base: Challenge Mode.

(Which means that even after you flip the Baron, if he gets 15 cards in his trash, game over.)

Minted this one by the skin of my teeth. Haka had 3 HP, Bunker had 1, and Ra was (ahem) toast. Tanking speed was the name of the game for me: I didn't wait to set up or get damage reduction or Ongoing destruction out, so at the end of the game Blade had both Basklash Fields, the Living Force Field, and even a Remote Turret out. Between Punish the Weak, Imbued Fire, the Staff of Ra, and Flame Barrier, I think Bunker actually ended up being the least damage-dealing party member. Though he also did lay the final smackdown when the numbers were getting close and I started worrying about getting double-tapped by the Backlash Fields. (Hid behind a Meteor Shower for a round or two to let the Maintenance Bot push Bunker into the safe zone, then dropped it for a multi-power Flak/Grenade combo turn that dropped Baron from 3 HP to 0. Yes, I needed both--Living Force Field.)

You don't choose the order.   It's based on the order they came into play.   If you played Ground Pound then Self-Destruct Sequence comes out during the environment turn at the start of Haka's turn Ground Pound is destroyed then you get the option about skipping your turn for Self-Destruct Sequence.  If Self-Destruct Sequence comes out then on Haka's turn you play Ground Pound on the following start of turn for Haka you have the option to skip the rest of your turn for Self-Destruct Sequence which if you chose yes makes the check on Ground Pound skipped.
