Ultimate Kismet vs. Hunted Naturalist, Requital Captain Cosmic, Chrono Ranger, Setback, and Guise in the the Carnival with the Overly Long Name.

When I saw "ultimate" I thought this was going to be tough. Particularly since the Carnival can be really brutal. Not Rook City brutal, but still bad.

It was a surprisingly easy mint for me. Mainly because I was taking out what jinxes Kismet was playing to keep her damage down, and of course I didn't touch the talisman since it's in hot potato mode. Naturalist has Crafty Assault, Chrono ranger has his grenades, Setback has "Whoops Sorry" to take out ongoings.

I pulled a lot of stupid moves, like screwing up discards with Hyperactive Senses. I often find cards which say "put multiple cards on the top / bottom of a deck" confusing because it's not clear if I'm choosing the top card or the bottom card. In this case, I reversed it, and put the cards I meant to keep on top, and the cards I meant to discard next.

Even so, it was fairly low pressure. Kismet doesn't do a lot of damage until she gets several Jinx cards out, and I was keeping her damage down anyway with Neurotoxin Dart Thrower. Guise got Guise the Barbarian / Batant Reference early, which meant a cool 15 points of damage, and Naturalist fed him extra cards to discard for that.

I actually got some use out of Uncharmed Life. At one point You Won't Believe Your Eyes was out, and Setback used that to tank a hit so only 4 targets did themselves damage, and the card self destructed. It would have been 7 targets except that Kismet was toxined and Captain Cosmic had a bracer out, and there was only 1 attraction out.

Things did get a little dicey toward the end because Kismet drew Karmic Disjunction and played 4 Jinxes at once, but she was low enough that I could take her out before it became a problem.
