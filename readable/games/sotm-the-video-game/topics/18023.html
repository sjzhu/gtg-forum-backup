Near mint.

First game I underestimated how much burning Catastrophes was going to hurt my team. My take is that you have to let him have his disasters unless you’ve got Hypersonic Assault out, because the infernal retaliation damage he does is just too much otherwise.

Second game I just gave up on doing anything defensive and had KNYFE punch face. I thought I’d lost when armor auto-caster came out, but I managed it anyway. Critical Multiplier is fairly nullified by the Armor Auto Caster, except I managed to wiggle around it. Snap Decision let me play targeting arrow at the start of her turn, which turned off the auto-caster for the rest of her turn, and then she used Syntax to let KNYFE play twice, which meant she got the damage bonus from Critical Mulitiplier.

At the end everyone but Nightmist was down to single digit HP, and KNYFE was down to 1 HP. Which was one reason I couldn’t play cards like Incidental Contact, she’d kill herself doing it.

I’m not a big fan of damage-race games like this setup.
