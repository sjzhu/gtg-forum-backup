Welp, my copy was definitely ruined.
Fine after 2 owie games, with the last one being 0/13/0/0

Toxic vats are NOT your friends, considering the amount of plink damage Teams Villains do, AND the fact that it makes Plague Rat immune to damage. Everyones game is going to be different based off Completionist Guise and the Displaced Armory you start with, but here is what I did to get where I did.

Firstly, starting with GI Bunker means Biomancer is actually a viable first target. Combined with the lack of self damage this team has, his flip side isn't terrible. I used Guise to get out Regular CR as I find BoT to be very niche and not great, targeting the Impulsion Beam because Implusion Beam.

Second, GI Bunker targets Biomancer and I focus fire down the Impulsion and start working on him. Leave any Plague Rat Handlers in play, as they tend to die anyway and the +2 damage without them hurts....

Third, I pulled the Neruo-Toxin for CR to help mitigate what damage I could. PR is a great target, even though it won't do damage, to just reduce his output.

LifeLine just did Lifeline stuff with his axe.

Next round, I swapped GI Bunker with FV Bunker, and used the GI Power via Guise. FV Bunker is more utility imo and he started tanking. I brought down Biomancer that round via focus fire.

After that, my next target was Citizen Anvil then Hammer, with a side priority of getting rid of Blade's ongoing so he starts killing himself. Also, as above, I tried to take out any Toxic Vats asap. By this point, however, everyone was pretty hurt, though Bunker was doing alright with his Heavy Plating/Recharge Mode. I swapped Guise for his base version as he was getting low and his incap abilities are better long term on his base. After managing to bring down the Citizens, it was "Keep Bunker Alive" mode and Guise, CR, and Lifeline quickly dropped. Plague Rat was full, and Blade was high teens.

However, Heavy Plating and Recharge Mode's 2 DR is hard for villains to overcome, especially with incaps that heal and an Omni-Cannon charging. It took 5 rounds to get the 15 cards to one-shot Plague Rat, but in that time Blade and PR both beat on Blade enough to drop him. So a fully charged Bunker blasted the Rat and we won.

~Komori

Never got a Gattling Gun either until after Recharge Mode was in play, and by that point the Omnicannon took priority.

That being said, getting through the first two turns is key and with the right plays, you can easily weaken Biomancer and get rid of troublesome devices in time to hurt all the villains consistently. With access to more variants I could have worked through the chellenge more easily.  The first two Environment turns net you a Vat Explosion and a Supercooled Trisolvant Vat, respectively, unless you modified the decks with something like Guise's Gimmicky Character.

That card is not your friend here. I had thought, with the Environment and three of the four team Villain decks stuffed with targets, Guise would majesticaly leap over the industrial complex on some sort of acid-breathing shark with a frickin' laser beam attached to its head, but sadly this was not the case. Using that card made the first two games worse for what came out of the environment after, even on the turn I chained two of them together I did not top more than 1 HP regained.

Ideally, it would be great to keep Lifeline alive longer because he and Bunker are good friends in this match if you have Ammo Drop out. If it wasn't for all the damage he has to eat through as the 4th Hero turn, Lifeline would be great using the splash from his Nordidian Sulp-Axe and Vitality Battery to keep his HP up. Another thing to watch for is that with Baron Blade having frequent Old Trick - New Allies plays, you might end up Citizen Hammer's Scorching Snap doubling up on your highest HP target, which is likely Bunker shielding. Still, when I had that happen it was always fun to prevent the blowback from External Combustion.

Also had the glitch occur with Incapacitated Heroes swapped out by Completionist Guise. I'd gladly take the glitch since this week's on-shot stunk. You are a mad genius if you unlock Completionist Guise from this monster.
