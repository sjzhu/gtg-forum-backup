So I was fighting the Ennead, Ra was one of the heroes with Flame Barrier.

The Deserts Wrath gets played (lowest HP villain deals everyone damage).  Nuit has the lowest HP. I choose Ra to take damage first.  Flame Barrier reacts and incapacitates/flips Nuit.

Desert Wrath contines, but then shows flipped Nuit as the source attempting to damage the other heroes.  Since she's flipped, she does 0 damage to other heroes.

Thematically this can make sense (Ra incapacitates Nuit before she can damage anyone else).  But is this correct behaviour according to the rules?

I'm thinking about Gene Bound Shackles - the villain target with the highest HP can change dynamically as you're dealing damage to multiple targets.  So with the right order, you can get multiple benefits.  Same with Smoke Bombs - hero target with lowest HP can change dynamically and you can get multiple benefits.

So my question is, after Nuit gets flipped, should Desert Wrath dynamically change to use the next villain target with lowest HP to continue to damage the rest of the heroes?
