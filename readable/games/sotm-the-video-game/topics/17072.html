Ambuscade, Baron Balde, La Capitan, and Greazer, Advanced, against Setback, the Scholar of the Infinite, the Argent Adept, and Nightmist in the Tomb of Anubis.

Got this Fine on my third attempt. Some thoughts (cross posted from Steam):

Impulsion Beam is probably my least favorite card in the game, especially with Advanced Vengeance Baron Blade. Cards that let cards come out but that don't count as being played or that play cards out of turn sequence are key. (You'll get some of those up front, thankfully.)

There's going to be a fair bit of redirection. Make some of it work for you.

If you can keep Flesh to Iron alive, the Scholar is an absolute tank.

The Argent Adept is not going to last long. Get whatever use out of him you can and then let him flip. His incap will at least let you get more powers going and maybe even more cards out, with Setback's base power.

Trials hit Villain cards too. Let them.

Mint with Scholar at 4 and Nightmist at 6. Misplayed a couple things thanks to Chiquito, who at one point ended up with 5 cards underneath him, but it turned out OK. Too difficult to remember some specifics because of the nature of LC Villains games, but here are some things that I noted:

- The extra card plays from Chronological Hotspot did more good than harm. I left it out until LC blew it up herself with Quando? Ahora! CH helped me get Nightmist set up with all of her Relics except the Ring, and Setback to bring Wrong Time and Place back from its grave.

- Speaking of Wrong Time and Place, it was super useful. Each round it was out, I was able to redirect a hit from Baron Blade back to himself (net 5 damage), which served three purposes: getting him dead ASAP, allowing Flesh to Iron to survive, and keeping Argent Adept alive for one last use of Vernal Sonata / Alacritous Subdominant.

- I put Living Paycheck on Setback. My plan was to put it on Scholar if he started with Flesh to Iron... and he didn't, so. Bye, Setback ol' buddy.

- Don't worry about the Swarm of Scarabs, LC should blow it up before it affects too many people, and Scholar can play around it temporarily with Bring What You Need.

- I got to play Oblivion three times (1/3, 1/2, and 3/3) thanks in part to LC making Nightmist reshuffle, and immediately following that, I used the Tome to grab one final copy. 

- This is pretty much the Nightmist and Environment show for damage. Anubis was super helpful - as soon as Scholar became the highest HP target (which didn't take long), he hit a villain character every time he went off. 

- I focused as much damage as I could on Blade. By the time he went down, Ambuscade was down to 10 and LC was in the teens. I focused on LC from there and Ambuscade went down to AOE in the meantime.
