Prevention is at the same timing as Redirection. Thus it gives the choice. Prevention is in no way the same as damage Immunity or being unable to deal damage. How do we know this? Take Null Point Callibration Unit, if Meteor Storm is in play and he tries to deal hismelf Cold damage, it would not work. Same if Absolute Zero is suffering from the effects of Ensnaring Brambles. Null-Point Callibration Unit also takes place after damage type, say Imbued Fire is in play you will be unable to deal Absolute Zero Cold damage and thus not trigger it. Null-Point Callibration also factors in Damage Modifiers, so it can be reduced to 0 and actually be unable to trigger or it can be increased so it makes it a higher amount of healing. Effects like Meteor Storm and Ensnaring Brambles will not trigger any of these things because they are before them and stop them from working. This is exactly why Undaunted, Null-Point Callibration Unit, Flak Jacket, and any other cards that can prevent damage can occur at the same time as redirection cards.

Aside from "order of operations," there's another way to look at it.  Redirected damage is always considered to come from the source to the end target and not be affected by effects that apply only to the target(s) not taking damagebecause of the redirection.  This is most easily visible with Nightmist's amulet or Fixer's Mantis style. 

For examplre, Baron Blade's Backlash Field.  The Baron strikes Nightmist for 5 damage; she uses her amulet to redirect to the Baron.  Legacy's +damage bonuses do not apply to the damage jujsyt because it "bounced off" Nightmist; the game considers Baron Blade to be attacker AND target.  The Backlash field will attack Blade in retaliation.

The "middleman/woman/robot/alien" is not considered to be part of the damage sequence.  It's always worked trhis way with "normal" damage reduction, too, like Haka's Ta Moko.  If damage going to Haka is redirected to Legacy, it doesn't get to "keep" the -1 reduction from Ta Moko and apply it to the damage hitting Legacy.

If that principle is applied, when Lead from the Front redirects the damage from Fanatic, cards that affect damage to Fanatic (such as Undaunted) would not apply because Plague Rat is now directly damaging Legacy.
