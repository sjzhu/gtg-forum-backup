AKA the Fall of the Prime Wardens, part 2.  Challenge Kismet vs Tempist, Captain Cosmic, Haka, Fanatic, and Argent Adept, all Prime Wardens Varients, at the Myrinian Refuge.  Compared to last week's oneshot, this is a lot easier since all Kismit's doing is 5 damage to whoever holds her Talisman at the start of their turn.  Mint at 13/14/19/10/10.

The gimmick with this match is that Kismit gets out Lady Luck and Incociveable Obstruction out very early, making it tricky to hurt her and tricky to get rid of her stuff, and only AA draws the tools needed to deal with ongoings, and this is only after two sessions of Conduct to get the drum from intrumental conjuration.

Aside from that, make sure everyone has at least one card in play at the start of the match and make doubly sure that Fanatic's is Undaunted.  There's a lot of 1 damage going around, including an early Two Left Feet which has no effect on someone immune to instances of 1 damage.  I alternated giving the power use to Haka and AA for either more damage or just more attempts at breaking ongoings.  I only got rid of Inconcivable Obstruction, but since the only jinxes I saw were Shakey Arm that was put onto AA and Two Left Feet all Lady Luck did was mill Kisnmet's deck.  Aside from that I just lay as much smack down as I could and it worked out.

A public announcement in the Maerynian Refuge:

"Let's hear it for the Squall-Guard, those fearless defenders of the Maerynian Refuge!  A villainous human tried to invade our new homeland, but the Squall-Guard bravely battled her, and even in the face of her strange human powers were able to defeat her.  Good work, Squall-Guard!  Let's give a mighty 'Hurrah' to the brave boys... um... girls.. um... members of the Squall-Guard!"

 

Yeah, this week's one-shot goes a long way for making up for that <word-I-won't-use-in-public> that was last week's one-shot.  When I saw last week that it was listed as "part 1", I was very nervous, but this week's one-shot was a nice change of pace.

The heroes started out well-stocked and quickly started knocking down Kismet's hit points.  Some of the heroes took a lot of possibly-unneeded damage from the Talisman being in the wrong place at the wrong time.  I should have paid more attention to that.  But just blindly pushing through and punching things, the heroes did pretty well.  They won in 4 rounds with the heroes at 13/26/26/21/21.

And as I opened with, the Squall-Guard ended up doing a fair bit of damage to Kismet.  And Kismet, with her "Inconceivable Obstruction" card in play all game, really accelerated getting the entire Squall-Guard in play.

Fun match.  Don't worry too much about Kismet's annoying ongoging cards.  They are hard to get rid of, and ultimately you can just brute-force your way past them.  Luckily this team of heroes have lots of ways to do damage multiple times a turn, so while "Inconceivable Obstruction" was annoying, it wasn't a show-stopper.

5/5.  Would fight again.
