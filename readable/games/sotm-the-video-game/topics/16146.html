Played a 3-player multiplayer online: Wraith, Naturalist (me), and …uh…maybe KYFE?..against Spite in…I think it was Pike Industrial [edit: Tomb of Anubis, now I remember!].  I forgot to get a screenie.

Because Spite is Waith's nemesis, he did extra damage to her.  Really fast.  Wraith didn't get any Suture Selfs into play, and was too busy using Infrared Eyepieces to fish for Spite's drugs to use Stealth much, so her hit points were in free fall.  Surprisingly soon, she was down to 1 hit point.  Seeing the end coming, Wraith heroically played Inventory Barrage and did a lot of damage…but far from finishing Spite off.

The Wraith player calmly admitted she was doomed.  "Wait a moment," I typed, "I can tank a bit."  The Naturalist had been drawing a lot of cards with my second power use each turn (since we were trying to let Spite get all his drugs into play and flip, I'd been sort of biding my time) and had 18 cards in hand.  Amng them was Indomitable Force, his "redirect all damage to me" card.  So I could draw everything (even envoro damage) down onto my own head to save Wraith for a turn.

But I also had Shifter's Strength – lets Naturalist discard (unlimited) to draw an equal number of cards.  AND then play one – this is key.

So I played it and discarded everything but Indomitable Force and a second Shifter's Strength.  Then I played Indomitable Force.

By cycling so many cards each turn, and ignoring everything else, I was able to keep ending up with at least one Indomitable Force and one Shifter's Strength every turn, and all the damage directed at heroes bounced off Naturalist's awesome defenses.  Wraith spent the rest of the game fighting her nemesis with a single hit point showing, and we beat him down.  I didn't do any damage until the last turn, when I threw a Threatening Stomp as part of finishing Spite off.

At the very end, Wraith got a Smoke Bombs into play.  I loved the mental image of an exhausted and confused Spite searching for his archenemy, while an enraged rhinoceros kept appearing and disappearing in the dense smoke.

With Scholar, remember you can have teammates damage you if you need to lower your health a bit to do damage. This is a great use for "leftover" attacks that would go unused (say, the second hit of Haka's Taiaha, or if Fixer has Dual Crowbars and only one villain target).  If your damage reduction is too high, you can always let an Iron Skin go.

Speaking of Legacy, let's not forget the game's original tank!  While he can't redirect environment damage, "only" villain, he can do it at will, or skip it if it would be better.  Once he has the right cards out, and with the ability to become immune to one or two types of damage, he can be spectacularly tough.
