OG Haka, Dark Visionary, OG Scholar, Freedom Six Absolute Zero, and Omnitron-X against Progeny in the Omnitron-IV Environment: Challenge Edition. (Which means 4 Scions can share the table before they start fratriciding.)

Mint. Surprisingly easy, or maybe not surprising given there was a Twist the Ether in DV's opening hand. But Ground Pound held off the damage long enough for us to finish putting the hurt down.
