Well, it's "because Christopher said so" really, but I can give a rationalization.

You decide to play Uh Yeah, I'm That Guy!

The card begins to enter play.

"When this card enters play, give another player a high-five. If you do, ..." - the card is entering play, so that happens.

Now Uh Yeah, I'm That Guy! has the text of, for instance, Inspiring Presence, including:

"When this card enters play, each hero target regains 1 HP." - the card is entering play, so that happens.

The card finishes entering play.

You continue on your merry way!
