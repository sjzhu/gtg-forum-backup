After the one-shot was de-bugged (and to tell the truth, part of me was wishing that the "bug" was the start of a new Oblivaeon-preview ARG), we get Challenge Infintor in the Enclave of the Endlings vs. Rogue Agent KNYFE, Omnitron-X, Fugue State Parse and XPW Captain Cosmic.

 

Mint.

Challenge Infinitor brings extra damage reduction to the Manifestations, but I hardly noticed, because Parse had Reveal the Flaws active for what seemed like the whole game.

 

There's quite a bit of random deck peeking and reshuffling with KNYFE and Parse and Omnitron-X, so everyone's game will play out differently. The heroes scraped out a win thanks to Parse's flaw-revealing ways, making hero damage irreducible. Honorable mention to Omnitron-X's Reactive Plating Subroutine. Combined with Elemental Exochassis and Reveal the Flaws, I was very pleased everytime the Recalescent Hellion tartgeted the robot (which was many, many times). Take that, Infinitor!

 

Ended with KNYFE at 3HP, Omnitron-X at 6HP, Parse at 4HP and Captain Cosmic at 5HP.

Minty with everyone ~5 hp
For those having trouble, set up with the first round (plinking away at infinitor directly if you deal any damage), and play Focusing Blade on KNYFE and Reveal the Flaws on Parse. Second round everyone should be mid-low teens from all the manifestations. Put any crushing cages on anyone but KNYFE then play Incidental Contact to deal 2+2 irreducible energy damage to every manifestation and wipe the board. After that focus fire everything possible on Infinitor and ignore any manifestations that come out. Play Cosmic Crest for tanking and everything should be alright in another 2-3 rounds.

Finally got to this after missing out on the week when it was up. With all the damage reduction out, I figured the best bet was to use Captain Cosmic and let everyone focus on Infinitor. A big race to the finish to see who could do the most damage the fastest.

Infinitor won that race.

Next game I decided to dump all the free card plays on to Parse instead. I used Reveal the Flaws, Focusing Conduit-Blade and Incidental Contact to wipe all the manifestations off the board. But my hit points were all in the low 10s at this point. Omnitron-X didn't get much out of his components, but Disruptive Flechettes helped a few times to clear out Manifestations. Exploit Vulnerability helped mitigate the damage reduction from Infinitor and Parse also kept trying to removing damage reduction or add damage as much as possible.

All of the heroes were down to their last hit points, at 3 hit points or less. Slamara came out and I would say it was more help to me than it was to Infinitor. The manifestations all targeted the constructs since they came out at 4 hp. Cosmic Crest helped absorb more damage and Destructive Response helped ping Infinitor a little more. Just as the heroes were about to start getting KO'd, Bloggo came out and saved KNYFE. That gave just enough time to deal out the 20 or so damage I needed to barely squeak out a win.
