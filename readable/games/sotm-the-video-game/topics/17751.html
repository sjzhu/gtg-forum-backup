Ultimate Citizen Dawn versus the Freedom Five. Yikes.

Miracle Mint for me, all thanks to AZ. Once he’s set up fully, the sheer amount of damage he can dish out and heal up is staggering. Especially with extra plays or powers thanks to incapacitated teammates. Two Impales on Dawn, Thermal Shockwave, and keeping his HP high are critical.

When I won, Bunker was at 2, AZ was at 16, the rest of the crew was down. Dawn never flipped.

Reconcile yourself to losing your entire opening setup, though. Bunker’s Tactical Uplink is critical to having a setup at all.

And get an Eyepiece out. Control the deck–i know I buried an Aurora.

Fairly straightforward mint. At the start, I used pretty much everyone to setup Absolute Zero and a little bit of extra card draws for Tachyon. I grabbed the Eyepiece just in time to miss the Devestating Aurora and then controlled the deck pretty easily after that.

Bunker used his initial power all game to help set everyone up. Tachyon did some damage at the start to kill off citizens and was fairly quiet afterward. Legacy used Lead from the Front and along with Smoke Bombs from Wraith, all villain damage, most of it fire and ice, went back to Absolute Zero.

I let Dawn flip and then sat tight while Return with the Dawn and Channel the Eclipse brought out the citizens in quick order. It only took a few turns to get 5 extra citizens out. Citizen Winter, Citizen Hammer and Citizen Dare were among the returned recruits. I got rid of the ongoings and all the citizens except those three. And then Absolute Zero went on a rampage. Coolant Blast hit Citizen Dawn for 38 points and then when Citizen Hammer took his next turn, all the damage went flying back to Citizen Dawn. Hit points for everyone were in the 11-18 range.

I feel like Tachyon could have contributed more. She did damage when needed and took out the ongoing cards when needed. She had more than one Fleet of Foot come out which really makes sure everyone else is set up. But I didn't realize how close I was at the end to finishing off Citizen Dawn and she could have bursted for a lot of damage and ended the game a lot earlier, especially once Inspiring Presence came out.

Absolute Zero seems to be extra deadly with the Freedom Five variants. Except for Tachyon, the whole Freedom Five is about speeding up your setup and once Absolute Zero gets setup with Smoke Bombs nearby and the enemies are doing any fire or ice damage, it's pretty much game over.
