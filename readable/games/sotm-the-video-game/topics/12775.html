Matchstickman:

Spoilers to me are any information about the game/show/book which lessen the enjoyment.

That's a poor definition I'd say

Though I agree with it in it's own way

The problem with it

from where I sit

is that there's two definitions, okay?

 

There's two parts to the definition of 'spoiler'.  The first part, is exactly what you said - any information which spoils your enjoyment.  But not everyone gets spoiled by the same things.  So the more frequently used definition is something closer to "any information provided by someone who isn't the content creater before the content creater intended it to be revealed in the course of consuming the content".  This is a more useful definition, because it provides a more measurable and less opinion-based definition of spoilers.  It's almost certainly more broad than it needs to be, but that's intentional, overshooting won't spoil anyone, while undershooting might.

 

By this definition, the triggers to get the promos would count as spoilers, thus allowing the consumers the choice of whether or not it will spoil them.
