With Mad Bomber Blade lacking any environment destruction and missing his alternate loss condition, you can sit behind a Meteor Storm forever to get set up (using Flesh of the Sun God to protect you until you get there...).  If you feel like it, you can get to the point where all of your cards are either in hand or in play.  Once each of the heroes has a piece of equipment handy, the dust becomes helpful, as repeatedly re-playing the Staff of Ra and Absolution will heal them up.  (I guess Fanatic could use Holy Nova repeatedly to get AZ healed up if you wanted to).

Then with Ra’s ability to stack the environment deck, you can take Blade down at your leisure.  Get a Meteor Storm where you want it in the deck, then use a Fueled Freeze (and a Consecrated Ground, if necessary) to remove any ongoings Blade has out.  Ra skips his turn to end one meteor storm, Fanatic and AZ pound him a bit (especially if he has Cold Snap and both Impales out), and the new Meteor Storm arrives to protect the heroes.  Repeat as needed.

I wonder how this plays out thematically.  The combatants scramble around endlessly in futile attempts at battle, until Ra finally has enough, and blasts just enough meteors to give the other heroes a shot to pound Blade before the next meteors arrive.  Blade somehow manages to scatter bombs around but they don’t work.  After he flips (and the heroes have so much stuff out that they don’t care that he trashes a bunch of it), his death ray keeps getting stronger but is still useless, until finally it resets (Blade’s trash gets shuffled), rendering it weak again for a while.

I also made the mistake of accidentally skipping a turn on the Meteor Storm instead of the Self-Destruct Sequence (Consecrated Ground is much better…), and Fanatic and AZ nearly died.  But it turned out to be quite helpful, because it gave Fanatic big damage from Wrathful Retribution, and made it much faster to pound Blade.

The drawback, of course, is that it takes FOREVER to do this, and it isn’t particularly fun (it would be a very boring comic).  This is one of those one-shots that I don’t plan to re-play.
