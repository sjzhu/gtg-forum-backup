Can you really speedrun the video game version of a tabletop card game? I say, of course you can! :P

 

With the idea of a 'campaign' of sorts from the recent survey, it's sort of pushed this idea a bit further for me. First thing needed is some ground rules for single games:

 

  • Categories will be split by villain and number of heroes on your team (Ex. Fighting Baron Blade with 4 heroes, or Omnitron with 5)
  • Timing starts on clicking 'Start Game' in the actual fight itself, and stops on the fade to the victory screen.

What is allowed:

  • Any combination of heroes in any turn order.
  • Any environment.
  • Seeing your heroes cards before starting the game itself (and restarting to get an acceptable first draw)

What is NOT allowed:

  • Rewinding (gotta keep going forward)
  • Pausing the timer mid run (You can pause your game, but the timer must keep running)

 

Just some preliminary thoughts on rules for runs. Anyone have any additions or questions, let me know. :)
