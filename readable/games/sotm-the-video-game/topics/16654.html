I feel like maybe there should be such a thing, as we're collecting more and more shows and showrunners who want feedback on them.

Current Lineup:

Sentinels Live on Tuesdays at 7PM ET. The usual weekly Let's Play with Jeremy and John and occasional guests just playing the game, talking about beardcare and geeky things, and sometimes showing off previews of upcoming content.

Dolphin's Dive on Thursdays at 7PM ET. Dealing with strategies and how to best play the game. Pattern right now is that first game is LewdDolphin's choice, second game is viewer's choice, third is totally random.

Tales from the Archive on Saturdays at 7PM ET. Taking Sentinels card games and spinning them into a way to tell stories with the cards. Very RPG campaign-esque.

Future Lineup:

There was a Dev Stream this past Friday at 1PM ET. MigrantP wants to make it a regular thing and bring in other Handelabra folks to show off their workflow, but don't know what the eventual standard day and time will be. This one's not always Sentinels; MigrantP worked on Bottom of the 9th development this time around.
