Dreamer is brutal - I got buried in the first game but was able to rebound fairly comfortably.  Some tips for people wanting to mint this (this is from memory so I'm admittedly hazy on some details):

- First turn:  Have Chrono take the first hit from the Toy Master(?) so that Tachyon gets Whipacorned.  Tachy's job the first 2-3 turns is to feed people cards and soak Whipacorn damage.  Everyone else's job is to kill the Dark Hero ASAP.  On the first turn, Omnitron's power will flip Reactive Plating Subroutine, so play Ablative Plating on your main phase so you get a few free hits on Dark Hero.  

- General tips:  At some point it may become more useful to soak up Psychic and Infernal Damage so count up the damage Omni will take each turn.  You'll probably lose components at some point so don't over-extend.  For Chrono, Masadah, Whole Gang and By Any Means are your friends.  Absolute Zero's job is finish off stragglers, and play cards so he or Omni can take the hit for the one-shot that prevents the hero with the most cards in play from playing cards.

- This is the critical point in the game:  on turn 4(?) a Prison Riot will flip Char and some inmates.  Tachyon starts with a Hypersonic Assault:  *This is what you save it for*  There will likely be a Dark Hero in play to prevent HSA from hitting the projections but that's fine.  If you let Char and his friends go off you're gonna have a bad time.  Fortunately the environment will take care of the inmates the very next turn.  This is literally the only thing you need to worry about from The Block - use all your other resources to manage the projections; you'll need it

- The Dreamer has 6 hit points:  use them!  On turn 3-4 a second Dark Hero appears; if you let two of them stick around for more than a turn, you probably can't recover.  At this point I'd used every trick in the book to get rid of the first Dark Hero, what I did for the second was:  Chrono replayed "By Any Means" on the second Dark Hero and shot him down to 3.  Omni-X used Defensive Plating to finish off Dark Hero and also hit everything else for 3.  It's OK if you dink the Dreamer, you lose when she has zero HP, not one, nor two, nor three.

- If you get past Char and the inmates and the second Dark Hero the game gets a lot easier from that point.  Without Whipacorn and Dark Hero, the Sonic Vortices and Lightning Barrages that Tach's been hoarding make the rest of the projections very manageable.

The currrent operation seems consistent with a similar issue I had a while ago (posted on the video game's forum) about Nightmist's Amulet of the Elder Gods with relation to the Citizen Tears+Citizen Blood combo.  In that case, Citizen Tears must do damage equal to the number of cards discarded by that hero's player during that turn, but Nightmist's Amulet allows her to discard cards to redirect that damage (or to discard 1 card for no effect).  Doing so does not change the amount of damage, even though more cards have been discarded during that turn.

I've confirmed that this hasn't changed, and there are certainly one or two differeneces between the effects (e.g., the effect that alters the damage being done occurs during the attack instead of between multiple attacks), but I thought it might be worth mentioning it for comparison.

Okay, that didn't go so well. I got a Fine on my third try - the first one was an unmitigated disaster and the second wasn't much better.

First round was to set up Chrono-Ranger and AZ to take out the Dark Hero, done with a "By Any Means" on him, and getting the Psychic Reduction plating out for Omni-X (or any 4-damage plating). With that done, make sure the Whipacorn doesn't hit Omni on the second turn - he's going to Defensive Blast to wipe out all enemy targets *without* hitting the Dreamer. If you take out Illusory Demon last after all other targets *except* Dreamer, its death effect will destroy Defensive Blast so Dreamer doesn't take damage. You have to throw By Any Means on the Grotesque Arachnoid first so that it'll take 8 damage instead of 4, and make sure everything else is at or as close to 4 as possible - I had a Toy Master still up but AZ finished him right after.

Getting her to flip that early with everyone still at 20+ HP made the game a lot easier. Temporal Grenade removed Char easily; after that it was just killing her projections as quickly as possible to stack them up underneath her. Sucker Punch removed the Treacherous Ape before he could do damage to anyone other than Tachyon (I think I actually got it from playing Nimble Strike while she couldn't deal damage just to get the card draw). The rest was a couple of Sonic Vortexes, lots of little shots and bounties from Chrono-Ranger, and another Defensive Blast (this one actually hit Dreamer) although I think that one wasn't 100% necessary, I just wanted to kill as many of the projections as possible. AZ finished two off with a Frostbound Drain and his power to put her at 9 and that was the game.

Very tough fight but finished it without losing anyone that time.
