Mint with a fairly easy slog.

I turtled behind Scholar using 2xFlesh to Iron and Bring What You Need in the first few turns to tank everything. This soaked up all of Apostate's per turn damage and pretty much all of the environment damage, too.

Sentinels used Unique Capabilities and Team Communication so in the first two turns, they had all their Signature Cards and Sentinel Tactics out. Eventually Mainstay threw a Human Shield up as well for good measure. Writhe discarded a lot of pesky cards from Apostate's deck and otherwise, the Sentinels threw a lot of healing around. Runes of Malediction stopped a lot of the 2 damage cards from doing much to the board.

Nightmist drew lots of cards, redirected like crazy and used Planar Banishment on any Apocalypse cards that came out. I sat with Unhallowed Halls out for a while to get the bonus on Infernal damage, but there was lots of unused healing on my side of the field and Fanatic sitting with a dead hand, so I nuked those, too.

Except for Imp Pilferer, I left the demons and the environment targets alone. Scholar's Grace Under Fire was doing big damage (10+), so I could take out Orb of Delirium or Runes of Malediction without any issue. Using Don't Dismiss Anything kept a steady supply of them in my hand. (I should have done the same trick with Wrathful Retribution, too) Fling Into Darkness and Final Dive were used to get rid of low hit point relics that were otherwise a pain to deal with due to damage reduction or the orb.

I kept slogging through, dealing with relics as they came up and trying to keep the demons in play. At the end, I was going to wait for Apostate to reshuffle his deck before killing off Runes of Malediction, but then I had Nightmist use Mistbound to stop Apostate's deck. The Sentinels took out Runes of Malediction and the demons that came back did nothing to help Apostate. Know When to Let Loose destroyed Condemnation in one hit, followed by a Channel to Apostate to take him down to 1. After the Sentinels took out the rest of the relics, Fanatic used Absolution to deal the final blow. All my heroes were at max, except for Fanatic, who I had let drop on purpose for Wrathful Retribution.
