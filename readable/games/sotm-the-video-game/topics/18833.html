Finally got it on my 4th try, using the Bolster -> Onboard Module -> Fuel Freeze start. Feels like cheesing it, knowing that a particular sequence changes the card draw to get something important, but this has been so over the top brutal I don’t care.

Legacy really must play Next Evolution / Lead from the Front ASAP after the round 1 (and the first Flechette card). In game 3 I lost because I delayed that a round unintentionally. Other than that, this is all Tachyon’s show.

Wraith really needs to use her base power constantly to feed Tachyon card draws, even though she’s got stuff that’s normally more useful. Tachyon needs to draw Hypersonic Assault again, and that requires a lot of draws. Putting burst cards into Tachyon’s trash is the 2nd most important priority, higher than anything Absolute Zero, Wraith, or Bunker can do.

It’s also worth playing Research Grant and using it instead of Blitz, just because every burst card in her trash is 4-5 damage when it comes time to take down Omnitron. Once her trash burst count is Omnitron’s HP / 4, it’s time to unload with Lightspeed Barrages.

Tachyon’s Synapic Interruption is your only defense against Electropulse Explosives if she doesn’t have a Hypersonic Assault to play.

Part of the reason Wraith needn’t bother setting up is that 2x Technological Singularity is going to wipe equipment. Playing equipment is thus a low-priority task for her, something she should do only if there’s nothing else. She can freely discard equipment to feed Tachyon card draws because she’s not going to play most of it.

Bunker needs one weapon out, otherwise he shouldn’t bother with equipment either.

Absolute Zero can’t do much without the Transducer, so he has to play that. Beyond that it’s Cold Snap and Impales. The Null Point Calibration module only comes out if he’s doesn’t have one shots to play. I did do some self-healing with his cold damage instead of hitting Omnitron, just because I have a hard time letting heroes die. He did manage to make it to the end.

I don't think there's any problem with using AZ's power round 1. You're trading 2 of AZ's health for 2 damage to Omnitron. Which is pretty much standard for AZ, he frequently trades health 1:1 with villain cards, mainly because he can usually get his health back later. If you're aiming to incap AZ to use his incap power, the lost health is beneficial. I didn't play it that way, but you could.

Freedom 5 Tachyon gets more out of Research Grant than basic Tachyon IMHO.

It’s true that F5 Tachyon will sometimes forgo Research Grant for Blitz because she needs damage now, or wants to finish off a target and is willing to burn Burst cards to do it. Basic Tachyon never uses Rapid Recon if she can use Research Grant.

Synaptic Interruption counters the electro-bombs. Choose Tachyon as the first target, and then redirect the blast back to the bomb, destroying it. If you’re making a point of feeding all of Wraith’s cards to Tachyon via her base power, you’ll draw the interruptions before the bombs hit.

While normally I’m a huge fan of the Infrared Eyepiece, I think it’s nigh useless against a flipped Cosmic Omnitron and actually harmful for this particular match. The problem is those chain drone plays. The strength of the eyepiece is delay, and putting a drone on top doesn’t delay anything. So often the eyepiece has you putting a drone on the bottom and accepting a strong villain card as the top card.

In this particular match the 2nd Flechettes card pretty much ends the game, and it’s buried nearly at the bottom of the deck. Every time you use the eyepiece you reduce the number of cards Omnitron has to play until it reaches it. I found this out the hard way.

What you really need is a card that lets you discard from the villain’s deck, like Parse’s Extrasensory Awareness or Buffer Overflow.

Round 1 Throat Jab.

Round 2 Inspiring Presence, Hypersonic Assault.
