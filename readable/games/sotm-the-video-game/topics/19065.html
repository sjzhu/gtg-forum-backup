Villan: La Capitan (advanced)

Environment: Nexus of the Void

Team: La Comodora, Knyfe (Rogue Agent), Parse (Fuge State), Benchmark, Akash'thriya

 

Still Needs a suitable story to go with it, feel free to submit one.

 

The idea here is that Maria is chasing herself through time, trying to stabilize the timeline enough for the other heroes to fight Oblivaeon, and possibly to redeem herself and her original crew in the process.

Knyfe is a natural here, given her status as a rogue F.I.L.T.E.R. agent.  And Parse is there to study the timeline and try to figure out how to get a step ahead of The Falcon while she constantly bombards the team, and The Comodora does her best to weather the hits.

The cross synergy on this team is complex, interesting, and powerful; and it all centers around breaking the normal rules (constantly playing stuff out of turn, and from places other than your hand, filling the environment deck, constantly manipulating all 7 decks, and of course Buffer Overload to occasionally retcon the timeline).  You really do start to feel like you're manipulating time
