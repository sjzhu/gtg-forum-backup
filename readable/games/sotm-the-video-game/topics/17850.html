Minted this when it came out, pretty much the way most people did. Everyone fell down except Legacy, but Legacy was indestructible because of Next Evolution. When the Cyclohexane Vat came out, Absolute Zero was down to 2 HP, so I didn't destroy it. I figured that he'd get wiped no matter what I did, so it was better to keep the +1 damage and ensure that Legacy only needed to be immune to Toxic damage to ignore everything.

At the time, Legacy had no way of doing damage on his own, but Ex-Patriette was letting him do 1 damage on using a power, which meant he was still doing damage with Next Evolution. Soon enough I drew Motivating Charge, which became my main source of damage. I did have the Legacy Ring out so I could do that without giving up Toxic immunity.

Legacy's very good against the Organization even without the Vat, since almost all the damage the Organization does is melee. Get Next Evolution and Lead From the Front out soon enough and he'll tank most of the damage. My problem was that by the time I got Lead from the Front, Legacy was alone.
