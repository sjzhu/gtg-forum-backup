So, her character arc runs something like this:

  • Young Kismet has a stream of minor encounters, which she inevitably loses, including against The Sentinels (lands her in jail) and Setback (lands her in The Block).  Her first major event is the prison break in The Block.
  • From The Block, she ends up in Atlantis.  This is the the first real Kismet event with multiple heroes involved, so this is probably the canonical "original card" Kismet battle.  We know Ra, Visionary, and Haka (at least) were part of this battle, and Guise was also there.
  • She gets paroled from The Block and ends up in Mittermeier's, so this is Trickster Kismet.  She and Glamour capture the Naturalist, which attracts other heroes.  This is presumably the canonical Trickster battle.

So once the event goes live, I'd probably start fiddling around with the Atlantis battle setup.  Since Kismet never actually fares well in a battle against heroes, not sure if she has to achieve anything herself, like getting lots of Jinxes out.  I suppose it's possible she might have to win a battle in The Block, but story-wise she never battled any heroes there.
