I believe I got the redirects by taking Legacy, Wraith, the Scholar, the Naturalist, and Fanatic out to play, Legacy, Naturalist, and Scholar got their respective "redirect to me"s out, Wraith played Smoke Bombs, and Fanatic shot herself in the face a bunch and then hit someone to redirect all their damage to her.
