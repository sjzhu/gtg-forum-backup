I just unlocked Hunted Naturalist, and had a question about his base power.  The card reads in pertinent part:

 

"Select [Croc], [Gazelle], or [Rhino].  Until the end of your next turn, you may activate that icon's effects."  

 

So if The Naturalist is in (ex:) Croc form, and uses his power to "activate" (ex:) Gazelle Form, that means until the end of his next turn, he can activate *both* Croc and Gazelle's effects, or either/or?   For example, if I played Primal Charge, would he both draw 4 cards (Gazelle) and deal a target 3 DMG (Croc), or would he have to choose from one of those effects?  

 

As I read the text of Hunted Naturalist's base power, I tend to think he can do both:  The text of his base power does not say "...you may ONLY activate that icon's effects...", and none of the cards with animal-form effects have any language saying "only one of the effects below," or anything similar.  And this would seem to be in keeping with the "story" behind the variant, which is that Naturalist is losing control of his transformations, and is close to becoming some sort of strange hybrid Croco-Gazelle-Rhino.

 

Are there any official rulings to the contrary?

Platypus or Turducken, how ever you want to spell it, it's outstanding.   I played him in a couple of matches yesterday, and I think I prefer him to Base Naturalist.  In base Naturalist, it seems that sometimes you get "stuck" in a Form -- tanking in Rhino, for example -- and want to occasionally want to get in a Crocodile attack, or use Gazelle to draw cards/get rid of Ongoings/deck control, but don't want to lose Rhino's benefits.  Hunted Naturalist totally solves that problem.  It takes a few card draws to get him going, but between Shifter's Strength, Primal Charge (Gazelle) and Natural Form Power (Gazelle), he has massive card draw, and can hunt up (no pun intended) the cards he needs in no time.  

 

The results when I first "platypused" him were mind-boggling.  How many other heroes can in *one* turn (here via Bestial Shift/Natural Form's Power/Natural Born Vigor):  (a) heal themselves 5 HP; (b) draw two extra cards (or let another hero do so); (c) get an extra card play; (d) play three powers; (c) give themselves -1 DR; and (d) deal 3 toxic damage, 3 melee damage, and (x2) 1 toxic damage?   If you combine him with AA, PW Fanatic or Parse for even more extra power uses, it's ridiculous.   I played a game against Dawn in Insula Primalis and just tore her down, particularly when the Obsidian Fields started popping up.  THN may be my new favorite hero.   If he only had some enviroment destruction, he'd be perfect (...but of course, he then wouldn't be "The Naturalist," would he?)
