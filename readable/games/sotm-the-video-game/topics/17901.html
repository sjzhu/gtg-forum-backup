Mint.

Relatively easy, because it’s a strong team. Target order was Freight Train (no choice there) -> Friction -> Ermine -> Proletariat -> Blade. Friction because she has some scary damage if she has enough cards in her trash, Ermine because she tends to screw things up with ongoing destruction, Proletariat because he does more damage than Blade, and Blade is not so scary on his own anyway.

I went looking for Ongoing destruction first because there were some nasty ones out. I was looking in Parse’s deck for Segment Fault, but Naturalist got Craft Assault first. That’s not repeatable like Segment Fault, but gets the job done anyway. Next priority was Blend Into The Pack so Naturalist could tank. After that is was pretty straightforward “do as much damage as possible.”

No one fell down, though Setback did use Silver Lining to not die and reset to 9 HP. Toward the end it was as stable as Starkenburg said, but I didn’t think to milk it for the Flawless achievement. I’m not entirely sure I could have managed it, anyway, since Parse was down to 3 HP. Blade kept hitting her and I kept healing her, but I wasn’t really making forward progress on taking her out of the Lowest HP spot.

No issues here. Easy mint. The only two heroes who went down were Idealist and Dr. Medico, but I saw two copies of Second Chance, so they popped right back up.

The first time I got Parse's power to play a free card, I used it on the Sentinels and was happy. Setback also had some good times, getting out High Risk Behavior and spending two rounds tanking behind Naturalist while punching very effectively with Looking Up.

Fright Train went down in one round; Friction went down the next round; Ermine went down very quickly afterward. That left a very sad combo of Proletariat and Blade against a very well set-up Naturalist and, by that time, Parse, with the Sentinels bearing down for tons of damage as well. 

The only character who didn't do a whole awful lot was KNYFE, who dropped For the Greater Good as a 4 damage one-shot in turn 1 and mostly sat there looking for things to do otherwise. But I didn't need her to do much, this team was really, really good, and as Takewalker noted above, Challenge vengeance 5 is probably easier than Advanced.
