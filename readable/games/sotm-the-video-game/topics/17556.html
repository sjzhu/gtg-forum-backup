Declining their offer to join forces agaist OblivAeon, Ultimate Baron Blade faces off against the Freedom Five in their F5 variants on Insula Primalis.

And let me say, Ultimate Baron Blade with this setup is some word removed by mod. I am convinced anyone who goes in blind and gets a mint here must be psychic.
However, once you know what to expect, he's fairly manageable. I got the win on my second try, securing a Near Mint. At the end I had Absolute Zero with 5 HP and everyone else in the low teens - I could have taken one or two more turns to defeat him, depending on what he would have played next, so technically I suppose it was a fairly comfortable victory. His countdown is so frantic though, that any win against him feels like it's snatched from the jaws of defeat at the last second.
