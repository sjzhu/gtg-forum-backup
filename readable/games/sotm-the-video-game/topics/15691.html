Here's how I did it:

Fixer last. 

Restart game until the first villain card play is prison break. 

If expat starts with no guns, restart game.

if fixer starts with no tools, restart game.

Setback provides ongoings for fixer. Generally provides damage, healing, redirection  

If possible, put nightmist into mist form ASAP. Otherwise use Oblivions to clear minions. 

Expat shoots stuff. 

Fixer uses Jack handle (if he has one) or dual crowbars (if he has them). If he has neither, restart game. 

Abuse the rewind feature with nightmist. If you reveal the wrong thing, rewind. 

It still requires luck, but setting up the initial conditions makes it a lot easier. You just need to be willing to restart the fight a lot.

Maybe that’s not as hard as it sounds at first. Neither condition (Perfect Human Specimens last played, Tony and Dr. Tremada are the only cards in the environment deck) really relies on chance.

Assume both are are incapacitated. Any time Tony or Dr. Tremada is in the trash, Mr. Fixer puts them back. Eventually the environment will be only those 2 cards. Any time the Chairman plays Perfect Human Specimens, Nightmist puts that back. Unless the environment forces another Chairman play, or an Informant plays a Chairman card, the Chairman is locked into playing that card forever. The Operative will shuffle the Chairman’s deck every turn, but that happens after they play Perfect Human Specimens.

The main issue is that the Operative will still play all the underbosses, so you need to deal with each of them in turn. You can assume that Jailbreak is put on hold forever, so they won’t come back once you get rid of them.

Informants and Scum and Villany are both a problem because they can break the Perfect Human Specimens lock. Twisting Back Alleys can also break it, but it’s destroyable before it takes effect.
