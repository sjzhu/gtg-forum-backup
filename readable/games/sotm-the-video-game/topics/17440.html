Near Mint.

Like others, I gave it a good try on the first attempt. Nightmist went down first and I ended up with Setback left. Good damage reduction and some healing ability kept him alive for a while, but not enough.

For the second game, it was obvious I would have to rely on incaps, so I chose Nightmist because she can do everything, especially heal and tank damage, and everyone else's incaps were way better than hers. With Mistform out, she relied on Mr. Fixer, Setback, Tony Taurus and Dr. Tremata to stack the decks very favorably. (while soaking up all damage to Tony and the good doctor so they stayed out for a long time) Eventually the Toxic Sludges came out and hurt Progeny badly. With all the deck stacking, Progeny went from 75 down to 3 while Nightmist hid under Mistform the entire time. She had her healing amulet out near the start, so even if something bad happened somehow, she was at full health.

At the end, Nightmist came out of Mistform and played Amulet of the Elder Gods. (no sense playing an attack spell- there were no cards in her deck) Progeny then promptly blasted himself in the face.

I almost wonder if the Dark Watch is built to be beat down like that and then rely on incaps to win the game. It does seem like Nightmist is their Superman and everyone else is there to set her up. It's fairly easy to control the villain deck with Mr. Fixer and Nightmist can only be hurt by ongoing destruction once she's in Mistform. This is generally a bad match up for them anyway- Dark Watch deals better with multiple targets and they have decent ongonig destruction. Neither of which is great against Progeny since they need the ongoings to stay our and destroy Scions.
