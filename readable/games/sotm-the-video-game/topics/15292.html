So I’ve had this experience a few times now.

I learned that I was playing something incorrectly in the tabletop version of the game because of what happens in the video game. Has this happened to anyone else?

Here’s what I discovered today.

  1. Wager Master’s “Breaking the Rules” causes each player to draw TWO cards at the End of Turn, presumably because you haven’t played a card or used a power that turn. Even though you probably will.
  2. Also dealing with Breaking the Rules: If a card effect, such as Scholar’s “Know When to Hold Fast” or Nightmist’s “Mist-Fueled Recovery,” causes you to immediately end your turn, you get to immediately have a second turn!

As a side-note Darkwatch Nightmist + Breaking the Rules = stack your own deck.
