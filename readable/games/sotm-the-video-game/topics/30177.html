Kaargra takes a lot of practice, she’s an unusual villain and there’s a decent chunk of unusual information you need to track. You have to keep track of what’s going to trigger titles and make sure not to let her get the nasty ones. (And on Advanced she is a nightmare and a half precisely because you can’t.)

For example, in this fight, if you lose track of how many people have hit Kaargra and let her get Mindbreaker, yeah, it’s going to get hairy. On the other hand, if you know Mindbreaker is out, you can manipulate Friendly Fire to make sure that Setback gets it, and keep it away from her.
