You are mis-remembering the ruling.

Visionary in her Cocoon (and the others with this restriction) cannot play cards, which is what the Atlantean Font of Power does. However things like Timeshift which say put a card into play are different and are not prevented.

Put into play and Play are different flavours of similar actions.
