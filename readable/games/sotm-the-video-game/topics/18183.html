Managed it the second time. Near mint.

Key differences:

Assault must die on turn 1 if Cosmic Crest is to live. Cosmic Crest ends up negating a ridiculous amount of damage if it sticks around, probably enough to turn the tide of the game by itself, considering that several heroes were near death at the end.

The RPG, when it shows up, must take out Channel the Eclipse. It’s much worse than Return with the Dawn.

Citizen Dawn will play Auroras back to back, since there’s nothing that reshuffles her draw deck. Which isn’t too bad, though the first one hurts.

Constructs are immune to Blazing Aurora, so KNYFE’s plays should all go to Captain Cosmic, helping him get stuff out. The constructs are, however, vulnerable to the area-effect minions (Winter, Hammer, Assault), so they’re high priority targets.

Wounding Buffer is a good way to zap AoE minions before they can hurt more than 1 target. Particularly if the Wounding Buffer is augmented by Imbued with Fire.

Dynamic Syphon on Ra is good once he has an AoE power, particularly Drawn to the Flame.

SMG and assault rifle are ways of pinging off Dynamic Syphon on Ra to get better AoE damage.

Citizen Hammer can one-shot constructs if Imbued Fire is out. Fortunately, Wounding Buffer took him out before he could smash all the constructs.
