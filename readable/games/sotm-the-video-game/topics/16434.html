Blackout in general is great on heroes that have a lot of general (not damage-type specific) damage bonuses. You trade one a single hit to that hero for hits to all the non-hero targets - its like a general-purpose version of Bunker's External Combustion.  Setback (High Risk Behaviour), Chrono-Ranger (Hunter and Hunted), Mr. Fixer (Bloody Knuckles, Styles and Tools) and Termi-Nation Absolute Zero (Shivers) can have big boosts to damage dealt (and received, so be careful - they have to survive the hit!).  Argent Adept can also boost damage,  and more globally Legacy and Ra.

Mr. Fixer also has bonuses from style (Grease Monkey Fist)/tool (Wrench or Dual for +1 damage, or Hoist to reduce enemy damage)/harmony (+1)

Note that if you have Twist the Ether on the hero you can manipulate the outgoing damage type and increase damage as well.

The only caveat is that Writhe has to actually deal damage,  so while you could mitigate some of the damage, you also need to be sure you do at least 1 point of damage otherwise the area hit won't happen. Also,  you must attack all the non-hero targets,  so if you're trying to not explode an explosives wagon or keep around a non-hero target you need to be careful.

Because of Fireside chats

When Dual Crowbars and Jack Handle are in play at the same time and Mr. Fixer deals damage, the following occurs. Mr. Fixer deals the damage to all non-hero targets (due to Jack Handle). Then once that is fully resolved, Dual Crowbars takes effect based on the damage dealt to the first target. That damage is dealt to all non-hero targets (due to Jack Handle).

Suppose Guise has Dual Crowbars and then uses I Can Do That Too on Sky-Scraper’s Huge card. After the first “round” of damage (to non-hero targets), Dual Crowbars causes Guise to deal as much damage as was dealt to the first non-hero target to one more target. Then, after the second “round” of damage (to hero targets), Dual Crowbars causes Guise to deal as much damage as was dealt to the first hero to one more target. (This is how Dual Crowbars interacts with Jack Handle)
