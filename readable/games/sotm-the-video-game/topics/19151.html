Ultimate Akash vs. Argent Adept, America's Greatest Legacy, Freedom Six Tachyon, La Comodora, and the Scholar.

Super easy mint, with everyone within 1-2 hp of full, except for Tachyon because I kept out Pushing the Limits longer than I really needed to. Even she was around 20 though.

Soooooooooo much card draw. Everyone had pretty much their entire deck in hand by the end, which suited me fine. Always had exactly what I needed, although I didn't really need all that much. Played Don't Dismiss Anything 5-6 times through La Comodora's power, could have done the same with Takedown if I wanted to lock it down even more. Decided to go with Tachyon as my damage dealer, and she worked pretty well.

One "Cheeky Bastard!" play I found in this one shot: If Tachyon has an empty deck but full of Burst card and Lightspeed Barrage trash, and La Comodora has 3 copies of Harnessed Anomaly in hand, Tachyon can play another 3 Lightspeed Barrages on the same turn :) Comodora puts Barrage in Tachyon's empty deck, "shuffles", then plays the top card, then Comodora chooses to play another Anomaly to repeat.
