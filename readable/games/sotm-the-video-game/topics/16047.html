I'm sure this is working as intended, I just hadn't realized it before:

 

Wraith and some other heroes vs. Chokepoint in Rook City.

 

Wraith has a few equipment out, plays Impromptu Invention, draws, puts out another equipment, then plays Grappling Hook to get rid of Blighted Streets (my least favorite card ever.)

Upon destruction, Blighted Streets plays Chokepoint's Harvest the Mighty. Wraith has most cards in play. Interesting, because I thought Wraith was tied with another hero for that…but maybe I miscounted. 

I figure that Wraith will have to give up one of her shiny equipment cards to be harvested…but no! When given the options, I learn to my surprise that Impromptu Invention and Grappling Hook are technically still "cards in play" at this point! That's also why Wraith had the most cards in play, because it was counting the one-shots that hadn't hit the trash yet! I had assumed that Harvest the Mighty (and other cards like it) could only destroy Ongoings and Equipments…but I was wrong!

I choose to put the Grappling Hook facedown in front of Chokepoint and move on.

 

Playing the physical game, this woudn't have occurred to me, because I usually (and incorrectly, I guess) play my one-shots sort of off to the side of my play area, so as to not mess up my pretty display of ongoings and equipments. It never occurred to me that until they hit the trash these one-shots are "in play" and "in the play area." Granted, most of the time, it doesn't matter–they hit the trash right after they are played. But sometimes it matters! And can make a huge difference!

 

Thank you for reading about what I learned today.
