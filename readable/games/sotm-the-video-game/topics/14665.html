Provided you're on Steam (as you can't access the files on iOS, etc., I'm guessing), check the following location (subbing in your username):

C:\Users\<username>\AppData\LocalLow\Handelabra Games Inc_\Sentinels

There should be a log.txt, which is your most recent game, and usually a log-copy.txt, which will be the one before the most recent.
