Mint. Barely.

I was setup & in good shape when Don't Dismiss Anything pulled End of Days & wiped my ongoings and equipment. The Ennead took advantage of that & started cleaning up my heroes. NightMist fell first, followed quickly by Ra and Fanatic. Haka held out for a while but could not stand versus the onslaught from the Ennead. Finally, the Scholar stood alone versus 5 of the Ennead, gravely wounded, and bolstered only by the memory of his fallen comrades.

And he kept standing there, getting healing & card draws from his fallen allies. The Ennead's incap effects kept his deck cycling so that he had Flesh to Iron always up, 1 to 3 Mortal Flesh to Energy most turns, and a Solid to Liquid up about half the time. The various incap heal effects kept damage going on the Ennead, barely outstripping their combined heals for a while, but eventually gaining enough momentum to take them out one by one. And in accordance with the intro for this one-shot, a Kraken got the final blow in, finishing Gen and the Ennead.

It was a long, slow war of attrition, and took about an hour to complete. It's the first time in a long while I've had all 9 of the Ennead come out without playing on advanced. Rough one, but a fun game nonetheless.
