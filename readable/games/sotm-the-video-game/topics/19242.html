Most of the time when I play, it's using the "random" button in the app, and going with whatever it gives me (sometimes changing e.g. the villain if I don't feel like fighting the Chairman that day).  And sometimes while playing, I find myself thinking, "I wish I'd rearranged the player order."  Some specific examples:

  • Stuntman's card that makes the game skip straight to his turn is awesome if he's first, and you use it right before the environment's turn.  Skip the environment and the villain, hooray!  But it's not so good when he's last.  Hmmm, should I use this during the first player's turn, and skip everybody but me?  No thanks.
  • Unity benefits from extra card draws or free plays before her turn, so they activate during her turn.  If she gets a free bot in play after her turn, it might get killed by the environment or villain before it does anything.
  • It's nice to give Legacy, Haka, Guise, etc extra plays after their turn, since cards like Heroic Interception, Take Down, and Ground Pound disappear at the start of their turn.  (But Haka can be nice to have later in player order, so other heroes can weaken targets, letting him finish them off to stash under Savage Mana).
  • Most of La Comodora's cards have upkeep at the start of her turn, so you don't win either way -- except for Concordant Helm, which is nice to play any time out of turn.

I don't recall seeing any discussion of player order in the character guides or elsewhere (maybe there's something buried in the forum somewhere).  So I'll ask the community: which characters are better in certain player order spots than others?

From what I've seen and heard, it basically goes:

Stuntman->support heroes->everyone else->Guise and La Comodora

I hear Guise likes being near the environment, anyway, and I find I prefer at least base La Comodora to go last so she has trash cards to pick through for her power.

But other than having your non-Comodora support heroes go before all your damage dealers so they can get the full use of their buffs over the course of the round, I haven't found too many other positioning benefits. Though having Dark Watch Mr. Fixer be first can be nice, to get a free use of his power with no downsides.
