Super easy Mint, even getting a Flawless Victory with everyone at max health. This was very much because of a MVP Visionary with Brain Burn and just keeping troublesome cards off the top of the deck. 

After the brain burn, I did go overboard on turtling up. I was worried with Visionary down to 8 hp, so I focused on Cleansing Downpour and Healing Pollen cycling with Akash. This meant the game went on longer than really needed, but I was in a super safe position by the time I started working on the Chairman and Operative. Once I did though, it only took a few rounds. Akash ended it by having 7-8 Seeds out and having them pollinate the Chairman to death. 

Funny note, I used the Enforcers to farm Seeds. Since the seeds were always the lowest hp target, I never discarded a card and had the Enforcers destroy the seeds I wanted. 

Environment was more a nuisance than helpful or harmful.
