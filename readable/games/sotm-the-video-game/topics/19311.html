Eventually, Dreamer had to shuffle her deck while still on her front side(!!!), but eventually we got all the manifestations. She flipped, and things were going alright again but Mordengrade was getting out of control. I was getting worried until Luminary drew the Terraluna Translocator with ~30 cards in her trash and 5 Manifestations in play. Bip Bop Boop, dropping the moon on the manifestations took us to 7 of 8 under Dreamer (I was VERY careful to not target the ones already under her) and cleared Mordengrade. From there, both Setback and Luminary went down to some combination of plays I don't remember, but Scholar was fine behind Iron and had enough damage to destroy Mr. Bones for the win.

Weird notes: No AoE as has been pointed out, due to challenge. ALSO, no Backlash Generators for the same reason. Setback barely survived the flip due to putting out extra ongoings to keep Silver Lining in play. The Toymaster is actually useless in this match as no one uses Equipment. The Tanks actually helped during the Plan: Drop the Moon on Them!(TM) as they played an additional manifestation that I could destroy.

Was overall fun because I minted, but whoo boy I don't know that I want to try again on the PC
