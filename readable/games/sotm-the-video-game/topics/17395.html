Villain style game with new hero variants.

La Capitan, Citizens Hammer and Anvil, Greazer Clutch and Miss Information vs. Best of Times Chorno-Ranger, Visionary Unleashed, Extremist Sky-Scraper and Requital Captain Cosmic in the FInal Wasteland.

 

It's a wild and unpredictable one, with lots of discarding and deck shuffling. I suspect everyone's game will play out pretty differently.

 

Mint.

Getting Chiquito under the deck via No Executions was pretty key for me, but again, it is wild and varied. I learned that Judge Mental is very dangerous for Requtial CC...the extra damage incapped him.  BUT Visionary Unleashed LOVES Judge Mental. They were best friends. Extremist Sky-Scraper was also incapped, but that was okay, because it meant that Greazer left the field.

 

At the end, the Villains went down. Once the Citizens and Greazer were out of commission, it wasn't too tough. Only BoT Chrono and Visionary Unleashed were standing at the end, Chrono with 16 HP, and Visionary with 2HP.
