The first preview of Mini-Pack 1 was 90 days after Sentinels Digital was first released.

Rook City was released 49 days after the first preview.

The first preview of Infernal Relics was 21 days after Rook City was released.

Infernal Relilcs was released 62 days after the first preview.

The first preview of Mini-Pack 2 was 102 days after Infernal Relics was released.

Mini-Pack 2 was released 35 days after the first preview.

The first preview of Shattered Timelines was 25 days after Mini-Pack 2 was released.

Shattered Timelines was released 43 days after the first preview (8 days after the Listening Party).

The first preview of Wrath of the Cosmos was 41 days after Shattered Timelines was released.

The listening party for Wrath of the Cosmos is 28 days after the first preview.
