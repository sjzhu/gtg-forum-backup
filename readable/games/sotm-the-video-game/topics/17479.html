As those who have been following the variant unlocks have probably guessed, this week's one-shot is the Freedom Five against Progeny in Rook City. Progeny's Advanced rules are in play, and the team order is Wraith / Absolute Zero / Bunker / Tachyon / Legacy. Randomized start.

I had a super easy Mint. Wraith started with the Eyepiece and Throat Jab; AZ started with Module Installation, Null-Point, and Thermal Shockwave; Bunker started with 2x Adhesive Foam Grenade; Tachyon started with Pushing the Limits and Hypersonic Assault; and Legacy started with Inspiring Presence and Take Down. Pretty much everything you need to nuke Progeny from orbit with this team.

I made a math mistake with Blighted Streets and lost Absolute Zero in the final round, but he still went down swinging with Frost-Bound Drain and Thermal Shockwave after two good rounds of piling on the damage with Shockwave. When the environment finally got to play a card, it was Toxic Sludge, which we left out for a second round to help chip Progeny down.

Near-Mint after an owie inducing loss with Progeny at 88.

First game I had all the damage control. Throat Jabs, Hypersonic Assaults, Heroic Interceptions... all the goodies. Unfortunately, that meant that Progeny could slowly build up Scions and kept destroying enough of my equipment and ongoings that I couldn't really beat him down. Once he played Hour of Reckoning and I couldnt stop his damage, he wrecked me the next turn with 6 Scions out, Time of Tribulations as his first play, Form of the Harbinger as his second, (playing another Scion), The Inevitable as his third to play Obvious Futility, dealing stupid amounts of plink damage repeatedly.

Second game I decided on a different tact. Legacy started with Next Evolution and Heroic Interception and I got the idea to let Legacy go sub-10 hp, then make himself immune to Radiant while everyone stays high hp. I had to shoot him with the Flak Cannon (Sorry Legs!) to get it to work when I wanted to, but it worked great. Progeny flipped, started destroying Scions, and was beating futily at Legacy. Smoke Bombs kept any other type of damage from bringing him down and the rest of the team could handle the 1-2 AoE damage he'd throw out occasionally. Lightspeed Barrages and Omni-cannon took Progeny from 65 hp to dead. Hp was mid-teens for Wraith and Bunker, near max for AZ (cuz hes just so cool), and Legacy and Tachyon near 10.

Finally got around to getting this done. Only Good since it's so far past the deadline, but I did do it on my second try.

I took Wraith down to 9 figuring that of everyone, she could handle it the most even though I couldn't get Smoke Bombs or Lead from the Front out, or at least not for very long. With enough Hypersonic Assaults and Throat Jabs, she stayed safe while the Scions could finally be destroyed. Suture helped keep her health up when she dipped and when she finally did get damage done to her, (thank you Hour of Reckoning) Stun Bolts and building up her base power damage reductions kept her alive, if sometimes only barely.

I kept the Fire Scions out compared to the other ones since I was negating damage so much, but when Progeny did hit, it hurt a lot. AZ started pouring on the pain and when it looked like everyone was getting close to knocked out, Tachyon let loose with a pair of Lightning Barrages for 32 total damage. Then everyone but AZ and Bunker went down, but Progeny was at 10 hp. From there, it was trivial for AZ to finish him off.
