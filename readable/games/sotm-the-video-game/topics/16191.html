I minted it, but it was a very challenging fight. It's very hard to keep Equipment or Ongoing cards in play with Ermine, especially with the cards she plays. Here are a few tips that might help.

1. First round, legacy will be unable to do damage, but can make himself immune to melee. This will spare you some pain with Fright Train, friction, and proletariat.

2. Take out Baron Blade's ongoing ASAP. Constant prattle is annoying, but Ermine is target #1, so she won't be around long. 1st round, you can use Bunker's Locomotion to get rid of it.

3. Let Crushing Hallway stay out the entire game. 

4. Wraith's base power works great against Ermine and Fright Train. Until Ermine goes down, use it and sacrifice equipment from AZ and Wraith in order to keep Bunker's flak cannon out. 

5. Make ABSOLUTELY sure that you have a sacrificial ongoing in play before Ermine's turn if you want to keep things like Heroic interception in play during the Hallway... two sacrificial ongoings if Calypso is out. 

6. Once Ermine goes down (don't be afraid to use an early LSB for 5 damage or so to do it) drop Fright Train. He cranks out a lot of consistent damage, and if you've been using Last Stand on Legacy and Wraith's turn, he'll be close to dead. Don't be afraid to use things like Throat Jabs purely for the extra damage. 

7. Once the Negation Bands come out, chip away at them with Bunker's cannon, but let the Hallway finish them off. Focus on powering down Friction. 

8. I know it's tempting to use the 2nd Heroic interception, but consider waiting a turn. Legacy can do damage to finish off the bands, put down fortitude to tank a bit... it all depends on what you need at that moment. If Everyman's Strength or Fixed Point is out, go for it.

9. Get rid of Everyman's strength ASAP  and defensive measures, if you haven't already.  I prefer stripping him of defenses early on, so that the Hallway can do it's work unhindered.

10. Once Baron Blade is gone, finish off Friction. The constant damage from the Hallway will have everyone low, but hopefully you have more heroes up and running, and the hallways should have crushed her stolen gear by now...

11. Get AZ's equipment set up ASAP once Ermine dies  I'd reccomend getting the focused apertures out first, then modular realigning a null point so that you can tank a bit, then isothermic.  At that point...

12 Proletariat is the last one standing. Gang up on him.
