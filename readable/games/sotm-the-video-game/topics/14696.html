So, I was trying to unlock DW Nightmist, and I had things perfectly set up.  Nightmist had Master of Magic in play, Oblivion* in hand, and Argent Adept has two Vernal Sonatas ready to put two 4s on top of my deck.  But there is a slight problem.  Actually, two slight problems.  The first is that I only have one 4 in the trash (having just used a Mist Fueled Recovery on AA's turn in order to come back up from 9 life), and there's an Ethereal Bonds in play ready to paralyze Nightmist.  So, what do I do?  I could just discard a 4 to Mistbound, but that wouldn't deal with the Bonds.  I have a Planar Banishment, but that isn't a 4.

But!  I actually have two Oblivions, and one of them is a 4.  So my plan is this:  Play Oblivion, kill the Bonds, and then on the next turn Vernal Sonata twice (using Alacritous Subdominant) to put the two 4s on top of the deck, and then Oblivion.  Sounds like a plan!  So I play Oblivion, and... flip two 4s accidentally, and unlock the variant a turn earlier than I had planned.  Whoops.

I then proceeded to crush Voss as he so rightly deserved.

 

 

*Suddenly, spelling "Oblivion" normally is really difficult.  I'm really looking forward to defeating OblivAeon using Oblivion.
