Did this last night and got a mint. All I can say is don't give up. It seemed hopeless many times throughout the fight, especially when Prison Break gets played, but you can still manage it. I played this as a standard Chairman game- beat down the bosses and thugs as they come out and hit the Operative when I could.

- Chrono-Ranger used Sudden Contract to grab the bounty that lets him use a power whenever the target does damage, and it was played on the Operative. That way, as the Operative did retaliation damage, Chrono-Ranger could get extra attacks in. Even with his base power, those small attacks let him do enough damage to help clear out lots of thugs. He's very good for helping to knock down Crooked Cops so Sky-Scraper could finish them off. This is one of the few times it's worth it to use No Executions on thugs. It will avoid the Operative counterattack and put those thugs where the bosses can't get them. Plus a single thug is generally less painful than any other card the Chairman will pull up.

- Sky Scraper rampages across Megalopolis. Skyscraper stayed in her large form for nearly the whole game. Besides keeping the environment in check, it let her do damage to everyone on the field and there were a lot of targets to damage. Even when her damage was indiscriminate and hitting the other heroes, it was worth it. And Thorathian Monolith is great no matter what.

- Mr. Fixer does the beatdown. Lots of Bloody Knuckles off the start, so it's easy to do some good damage and not destroy anyone else's cards. This big damage lets you take out the bosses early on. Later on, Dual Crowbars will let you take the Operative and the Chairman from full to nothing in a few turns- save up your Overdrives for those turns.

- Tempest the tank. With Elemental Subwave Inducer and Otherworldly Resilience, Tempest can soak up the Operatives retaliation damage. Later on, Cleansing Downpour helped keep everyone alive, but I didn't play or use it until everyone was at death's door.

At one point, the Operative was at 23 and then a Prison Break saw the Fence and his thieves heal her back up to full. But after a few painful turns of clearing the board again with Chrono-Ranger at 3 and Mr. Fixer and Sky Scraper at 1, I managed to claw back and win. I never saw the second Prison Break and I was waiting for it to end me, but I managed to finish it off with 3 or 4 cards left in the villain deck.
