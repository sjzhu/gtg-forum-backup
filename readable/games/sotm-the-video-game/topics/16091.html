The only saving grace to this one is that you'll know the outcome almost instantly.  

If you get any crushing cages in the opening Villain turn, you'll likely lose. The damage comes in so fast that losing a turn means that you'll be in trouble, especially since no one has a power that can do anything without cards (or, in Tempest's case, until he flips). 

If you get any Ocular swarms in the opening villain turn, you are likely to be in trouble, though a loss is not certain.  

If Bunker has no guns in the opening hand, he's likely out of the fight.  A single round of damage from infinitor + Manifestations can easily be in the 10-15 HP range, so not being able to remove any ASAP means that by the time you are set up... you're at 3-4 HP.  

If the environment helps you, if you kill Tempest ASAP, If you start with all the right stuff, if the fates align... this one is completely doable.  But it is a nasty, nasty fight.
