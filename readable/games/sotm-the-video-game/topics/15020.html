The play order for Dawn is Blood, Sweat, Tears, Blinding Blast, Summer, Anvil, Return, Battery, Leadership, Leadership, Assault, IIRC.

Silver Gulch goes Sustain, Stout Barrel, Water Trough, Explosives Wagon, Matthew Hayes.

You can clear two citizens on the first turn by having Visionary Enlighten Ra to draw a Staff, have Fixer Bitter Strike on Citizen to 2, and Ra play Staff and Pyre the other Citizen.  Sustain will then clear the citizens on the Environment turn.

You want to flip Dawn right away so she'll flip back after 1-2 turns, then you can just wail on her.
