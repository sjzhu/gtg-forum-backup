Another funny infinite loop I ran into has to do with (V) La Capitan's "A Good Time Span". 

All you need to implement this one is to have La Capitan at +1 damage (from an Obsidian field, for instance), and get her to hit herself for 3+ (with that card out).

A Good Time Span prevents that damage, then causes La Capitan to deal the source of that damage (herself) 2 damage... but since she is boosted by 1, A Good Time Span triggers, prevents that damage, and causes La Capitan to deal the source of that damage (herself) 2 (+1 from the boost).

This continues until the heat death of the universe, or until you close down the game.

The trick is that A Good Time Span only destroys itself AFTER the retaliation damage is successfully dealt... so if she is hitting herself for enough to trigger the prevention, it never triggers the destruction. Or so I think.
