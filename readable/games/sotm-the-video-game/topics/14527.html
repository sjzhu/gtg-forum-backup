Winning with no cards is easy:

Mad Bomber Blade vs Legacy (boost), DW Fixer (4dam), Fanatic (2dam+2dam), Haka (3dam), Ra or F6 AZ (3dam)

14 points of damage per turn, and MBB doesn't get his platforms or other minions.  He's only got 70 points, so if all goes well it'll only take you 5 turns, not really enough time for him to kill you (with lucky draws from the Environment, I've done it in four turns).

I did it in the Final Wasteland as you can often get the critters there to throw some extra damage his way as well.

You could also do:

Mad Bomber Blade vs Young Legacy (4dam), DW Fixer (3dam), Haka (2dam), Ra (2dam), F6 AZ (2dam)

But that's one point less damage per round and can be harder if MBB draws a force field.

Winning w/o using powers was harder, but basically the same strategy - Heroes with lots of damage dealing one-shots against MBB

My trick for winning with 1 hero standing and 1hp left was to play against The Chairman in the Block with Grandpa Legacy, Haka, Visionary, (and I don't rmember who else.  I milled for a couple of games until I got a good start in which I could get the Chariman to flip early and then start using Savage Mana to trap every Underboss, Thug, Agent, and Inmate in the game.  Grandpa Legacy kept giving Haka heals and extra plays (to pull more targets into savage mana) and Visionary used Twist to boost Haka and keep Villain Damage controllable.  Once I got everything but The Chairman under Savage Mana, that left the environment powerless and the Villain desk was just 3 healing cards, 2 useless prison breaks, 2 useless play more environment cards, and 3 damage cards (which only targeted Haka as he had the most HP).    

Once I was set, I got the Chairman down to a couple of HP, carefully got Legacy down to 1hp, knocked out Visionary and then on Grandpa Leg's turn he use his 1st power to have Haka use Savage Mana to knock himself out then Legacy used his second power to Motivational Charge the Chairman.

I suspect there were easier ways to go about this, but once you've got the basics of this going, there's almost nothing the Villain deck can do about it.
