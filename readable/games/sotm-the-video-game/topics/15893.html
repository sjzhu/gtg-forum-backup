I'm going to confidently say the answer is A. The card reads "At the end of the environment turn," which states the time in which the condition must be met.

At the end of the Environment turn, each player may discard any number of cards. If (H) plus 2 cards are discarded this way, flip an incapacitated Hero character card, restore it to 8 HP, and that Hero's player shuffles their cards into their deck and draws 2 cards.

The "discarded this way" phrase also leads me to believe that the revival happens when that condition is met during the end of the environment turn. This can be easily tested in the video game.
