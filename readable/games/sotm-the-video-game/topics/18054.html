Voss is generally considered “not hard” because he doesn’t do ongoing or equipment destruction, and people here are saying it’s easy... but it’s completely wiping me every time.

”Deck control” sounds good until Parse dies, and then there’s no deck control.

Usual Voss strategy is to keep 1 minion out so he doesn’t flip, but the environment regularly slaughters minions. The Trial of Blades does 3 damage and kills all the minions.

Tachyon’s pretty useless against Voss on either side unless she plays Lightspeed Barrage, due to his damage reduction. She’s extremely problematic against the minions because she tends to hit all of them, setting up Voss to flip.

There are lots of things about Setback that I really still don’t get. Like Uncharmed Life, which is part of your opening hand here. It’s a way to step in front of damage, I guess, particularly if someone else is liable to die otherwise, but he doesn’t do damage reduction or damage immunity like Legacy so it’s not like Lead From The Front. Sure, he’s got Silver Linings, which you can do great things with in this setup because Argent Adept can give it back to him with Vernal Sonata, but the 2 tokens he has to spend to use Uncharmed Life come out of his HP if you’re counting on Silver Linings.

While it doesn’t come into play in this game, there’s also Friendly Fire. Take 2 damage to gain 2 tokens. Yeah, I know it combos with things like Fixer’s Crowbars, but I have trouble seeing it as being a good idea absent that.

Of course, is someone else can give him some DR, like a couple of Captain Cosmic’s Energy Bracers, or Argent Adept’s Bulwark, these are a bit more attractive.

He does have some opportunities to do lots of damage if he has the tokens. In one of the games I lost, Voss had a boatload of minions out from a Forced Deployment, and Setback took out 10 of them by himself with Exceeds Expectations and High Risk Behavior. It’s not hard for him to get do 4 or 5 damage to as many targets as you want with those cards.
