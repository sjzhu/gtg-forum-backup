Challenge Kismet in Magmaria vs. Rook City Wraith, Fugue State Parse, Hunted Naturalist, Setback and K.N.Y.F.E.

 

There is so much deck manipulation available to the heroes and so much shuffling of the villain deck that I'll bet everyone's experience is going to be different with this one.

 

Mint for me.  Deck manipulation, well-timed Crafty Assaults and huge gobs of damage from KNYFE kept Kismet from being too pesky.

 

Hunted Naturalist finished off Kismet with the power of Cornered Beast, so that was fun. Nobody puts croc-y in a corner!

 

Wraith 9HP, Parse 14HP, Naturalist 8HP, Setback 14HP and KNYFE at 11HP.
