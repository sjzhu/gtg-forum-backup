I've always speculated that it would be Final Wasteland or Magmaria.  I could also see Silver Gulch being it, bizarrely.

 

The environments have been extremes related to the core factors that drive the hero.  Argent Adept had the extremes of nature in Insula Primalis.  Captain Cosmic went to the extreme ends of the universe ending up in the Enclave of the Endlings.  Tempest's extreme is a representation of his refugee status, with Dok'thorath being the homeworld of the tyrant that destroyed Tempest's home planet.  Fanatic in the Court of Blood is, of course, an extreme of religion.

 

Thus we need to look into the extremes for Haka, the eternal warrior who wanders the Earth.  The Final Wasteland would be representing the extreme of eternity, but we've already had that environment used for another Haka unlock, so I kind of doubt it.  Magmaria on the other hand, certainly counts as an extreme environment, and this would be representing Haka's exploration of Earth; you don't get much more extreme than the core of the planet.  Silver Gulch would be an extreme of time, but I feel it is certainly the weakest concept of the three.

 

So yeah, I'm rooting for Magmaria.  My guess for the actual effect is something regarding Savage Mana, but I guess we'll see...
