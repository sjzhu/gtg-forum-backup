Ok, just had to share this.

Going for Naturalist unlock. Already defeated Baron Blade in crocodile form so I wanna try gazelle.

I play PW AA, Legacy, F6 Wraith and Naturalist vs Deadline in DokThorath. Should be easy, right?

So Round 1 he has two catastrophies.

Round 2 he plays Pandemonium Key and a third catastrophe.  Wraith has no weapons out and I'm keeping Naturalist in Gazelle just to see how he is (only my second game with Naturalist).  So can't kill the key yet.

So that environment turn, a target is played and Deadline plays Unnatural Disaster playing 3 more Catastrophies.

Start of Villain turn, Calculated Orogenesis plays an Environment target which makes Deadline play a second Unnatural Disaster. Deadline's play phase - that is correct, the third Unnatural disaster. That's twelve catastrophies that Deadline destroys the environment with.

Good news? Legacy had played Heroic Interception. So no one took damage other than Legacy who gets incapped.  But I'm not gonna win right? Deadline still has 73 HP. I've never heard/seen the Deadline destroys the environment lose screen so at least I'll get that.

Wraith has Ordinance but that's it so still can't kill the key. Environment plays a target playing a catastrope.

Round 4 Deadline stays flipped, plays a catastrophe, destroys two leaving just one environment card left (Rebels).  Wraith got Microtargetting and Belt so can do a bit more. AA only has Supertonic, Bulwark and Lyre. I'm still keeping Naturalist in gazelle - I just wanna see the defeat screen.

Next round, Deadline unflips, plays two catastrophies has 50 HP. I'm dead right? Wraith got some Throwing Knives but no Sarabande or Grappling hook at all.

Next round, Deadline plays Auto-Armor Castor so still alive! But that's 10 more HP to kill and the third catastrophy is played. I'm dead right? But Naturalist got Craft Assault - hooray for Gazelle! Castor gets destroyed, Deadline at around 24 HP.

Next round Deadline plays last two cards in his deck so three catastrophies on the board. AA and then incapped Legacy each let Wraith Ordinance for 10 damage. Wraith Inventory Barrages for 10 more and Last Stand brings Deadline to 3 HP.   Naturalist, still in Gazelle plays Threatening Stomp for the victory!

So that was my very long winded story of Naturalist dealing the finishing blow to Deadline in Gazelle form. One mor game for the unlock!
