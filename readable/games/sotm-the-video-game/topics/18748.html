Omnitron-U, Benchmark, Supply and Demand, Cosmic Inventor Writhe, Dark Watch Setback, and Hunted Naturalist against The Chairman in Omnitron IV.

 

I recomend a practice game to get used to the team dynamic, as this one is pretty mean against The Chairman.  You can use Writhe to negate the counter attacks he brings when you hit him as well as the ones The Operative.  Also be careful, there's a prison break about 4 underbosses in.  Try to save your AoEs for that.

 

Either way this was a mint for me at 17/19/11/6/15.  Like the last oneshot I had, Setback did a lot of damage here, in fact I think he was doing the most damage since he had Looking Up early and eventually got High Risk Behavior.  That said everyone else put in work.  Omni and Benchy were doing plenty of damage, Writhe kept everyone from getting too beaten up by the gang, and Naturalist kept the enviorment from doing anything at all with enviormentalallies.

Pretty easy mint. As Bolnerap said, one of the easier Chairman games I’ve ever played.

It was 90% that Cosmic Inventor Writhe eliminates all that nasty Operative retaliation, so eliminating the minions wasn’t painful. He pretty much did that and little else.

Benchmark had a runaway setup so he was doing ridiculous things by the time the first Prison Break came out. He was doing things like “next damage to The Contract is irreducible and increased by 2, now the Contract takes 4 damage and dies.” Ultimately he did the majority of the damage.

The Chairman’s one of the few decks where Countermeasures is really effective, and I forgot about it. Mainly because it so rarely gets to trigger. There was a turn where it would have killed off Hired Gunman before he did any damage, and I selected “Choose For Me” because I thought order didn’t matter, and Setback took 9 damage due to High Risk Behavior. D’oh!

The Naturalist’s main contribution was Environmental Allies, but that of course is excellent in Omnitron IV. Sure, the Chairman’s immune, but the various drones and devices chew through minions really, really fast. It also made it relatively safe to leave Recylers in play so heroes often got equipment back. Particularly the Shadow Cloak.
