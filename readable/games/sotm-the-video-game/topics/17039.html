A few notes from my Mint (and then a replay):

Unless you get the Next Evolution trick, give the Talisman to Expatriette and let her tank the damage. Yes, this means she might get incapp'd early. She has an Ongoing destroyer in her incap powerset. Worth it.

I have never found FSix Unity's power to be a fair trade, though I guess if she gets a couple of "reduce damage dealt" Jinxes, that might push it into worth while. But on the other hand, Inspiring Presence is probably going to show up too.

Ra is reliable blow-it-up, but if he does get incapp'd, he also has an Ongoing killer.

There's nothing much to FSix Bunker here. Nothing special, anyway. It's probably not worth using his power to kill Jinxes, but give him his usual time and a suite.

America's Greatest Legacy lives up to his name as always. My favorite trick was Gung-Ho into Motivational Charge, especially with Surge of Strength and Inspiring Presence in play. Even without the Legacy Ring, that's pretty powerful.
