A few days ago, in the "Arena Champions" thread (https://greaterthangames.com/forum/topic/challenge-arena-champions-8877), Nepycros posted the following strategy that he (she?) used to accumulate ridiculous amounts of Favor against Kaargra Warfang, with a team of (in order) Grandpa Legacy, Dark Visionary, Argent Adept, and Wraith:  

 

Nepycros:

Perfect lockdown. Take Down happens to be an Ongoing, which Mental Divergence can infinitely recycle. Not only that, but Argent Adept helps Legacy to draw AND play Take Down out of turn. Legacy becomes immune to Melee damage, Wraith reduces all the damage the environment deals to 0, and then picks them off with some heavy damage powers. So the Villain cannot play cards or deal damage, and the environment cannot deal damage. 

 

I experimented with the technique against Kaargra, and without even trying very hard, won 54-7.  It then struck me that this technique could be nigh-unbeatable (absent really bad card draws) against just about any villain.  I tried it against Advanced Progeny (one of my bete-noirs from another thread), and neutered him in two rounds.  Granted, I had some really good draws, but once you get the loop going in which the villain can't draw or deal damage (thanks to a profusion of Stun Bolts), the environment damage is neutered, and Dark Visionary is getting rid of any problematic environment cards that could kill your strategy, I'm not sure any villain can get out of it.  I'm thinking about trying it next against Advanced Chairman in Rook City.  If I can kill him there with no trouble, this might be the unbeatable team/loop.   Thoughts?

Omnitron-X can use his base power to just put the top card of Legacy's deck into play. That gets around environments that prevent you from playing/drawing cards. There is no way I can think of to stop Paparazzi on the Scene, so that will break the loop for a turn. Any environment that does more than 3 damage (Krackens) will still deal some kind of damage. If I recall right, Nepycros did this in Final Wasteland for this exact reason. And as always, add Team Leader Tachyon to get the setup faster.  

Apostate and Voss deal multiple damage types at the end of their turn, so that has to be managed until Visionary draws Twist The Ether. Omnitron can still put cards from his trash into play, if he's played cards before the lock started. Visionary can just Suggest those cards back into the deck. 
The Chairman, The Dreamer, Kaargra Warfang, and Miss Information technically can't be beaten immediately with this combo. The Chairman can if O-X is given extra powers to Timeshift Chairman's cards into the trash, thus flipping him eventually. Similar idea with Kaargra- Timeshift gladiators into play and have Wraith hit them for favor. The Dreamer and Miss Information can be beaten by using this method to floodgate them- for Dreamer, let her flip and then don't use this combo for a turn to allow her to play some projections. For Miss Information, let her play clue cards to flip, then lock her down.
