It's a difference in how Dual Crowbars and Jack Handle work.  Catch a Ride with Jack Handle lets you pick any target since Jack Handle took the original damage and applied it to all non-hero targets.   Dual Crowbars is only using that first instance of damage with the intial hit and that hit causes Dual Crowbars to allow Fixer to hit another target.   Since the second hit was a reaction to the first hit the second part of Catch a Ride will only do damage if the intial target is still standing.   Again I'll go back to the case with Friendly Fire.   The targets you hit with Jack Handle doesn't allow you to trigger Friendly Fire again but with Dual Crowbars the first attack on Setback causes a second reactionary hit which allows you to trigger Friendly Fire again as it is seen as a new hit not associated with original hit on Setback.
