Challenge Trickster Kismet vs Dark Watch Setback, Completionist Guise, Luminary, Mainstay: Road Warrior and Akash'Thyria at The Temple of Zhu Long. With randomized start! How much candy can you get before you're knocked out? Spoilers below.

 

Mint at 1/18/12/8/8 with swaps to regular Setback, regular Guise and Spirit of the Void Akash.

Practice showed this would require a methodical approach. Rites of Revival will be around in case a Hero is incapacitated, although Challenge Trickster Kismet means you want Hero targets or the Environment to remove your Devices & Seeds, because if Kismet does she will play out more of her deck in a hard hitting loop. Getting reduction out can also help mitigate damage and mind what side Kismet is flipped to when the Jinxes stack up.
