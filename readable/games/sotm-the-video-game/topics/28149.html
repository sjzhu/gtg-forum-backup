A couple more items for my wishlist:

  • If you’ve used any card effect to stack the top or bottom cards of any decks, clicking on the deck to show the cards “not necessarily in order” ought to denote any cards whose fixed positions are known. Maybe a colored border would work. Top card on the left, bottom on the right, as with trash. This would be an immense benefit to Visionary in long games where the villain and/or environment deck never shuffles herself, as multiple Precognitions can stack the bottom of the deck until you know exactly what’s coming. You can already do this by writing that info down on scratch paper, so having the app do it instead wouldn’t alter outcomes, just make the player experience more convenient.

  • Specific to Deadline, the cards he removes from the environment deck are super important to know about (your decision of whether to keep Mysterious Ceremonies out changes significantly if The True Form is gone, versus if he still exists and the deck contains fewer other cards to delay his arrival). Yet the program only briefly shows the names of the removed cards, and you won’t generally have time to read them. You can look at the environment deck and see what cards are still there, but I do think it would be more ideal if the player could see all of the cards which were just removed, and then had to click through before the program would move on.
