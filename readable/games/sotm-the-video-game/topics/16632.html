This one is a marathon, not a sprint. Here are some tips to help!

1. Gloomy typically does 3 damage. A construct can take a single hit, so prioritize which constructs get that hit. 

2. Unless you have a Cosmic Crest in play, you can use destructive reflex to ping your own constructs to activate them. 

3. The sable pins are just awful. I found that getting rid of them ASAP was extremely useful. 

4. Cursed Acolytes are a major threat, BUT you can prioritize their damage. I found that stacking wounding buffers on the Argent Adept was extremely useful as a counter to them, as was having them hit Dynamic Siphons to let Legacy charge motivationally. 

5. You want the Argent Adept to get an Inspiring Supertonic soon. Having Legacy be able to charge twice in a round is very useful, especially once you have some autonomous blades in play. 

6. Relics can be destroyed by the Adept. Since Gloomy can potentially use the book to destroy every construct in play (4 damage to all hero targets if no one discards), you need to consider VERY carefully if it is worth sacrificing an instrument. 

7. Once Gloomy flips, have a plan. He does 3 damage to all targets... which isn't enough to kill any constructs if you have them at full HP, but will activate all of them. So it can definitely be turned to your advantage if you stack siphons on Legacy. 

8. The Cosmic Crest makes Captain C. And all constructs immune to energy damage. Battalion gunners do Energy damage. Maybe you prefer being immune, maybe you prefer letting the gunners ping your siphons and vitality conduits.  Either way you choose it, think about how their damage will impact the health of your constructs on Gloomy's turn, since he does 3 damage. 

9. Remember that you can choose which target Gloomy hits if their are multiple ones that meet the criteria!  It can be REALLY useful to let a gunner or mechanic get punched, particularly if they are hiding behind a shield. 

Good luck!
