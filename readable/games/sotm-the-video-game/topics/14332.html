The easiest way I found requires Baron Blade, Visionary, and Tachyon. Tachyon must be the lowest hero target, and Baron Blade must have Twist the Ether played on him. Get out all three Synaptic Interruptions, both Elemental Redistributors, and then get Blade to play Slash and Burn.

Baron Blade attacks -> Twist the Ether (fire; not a redirect, but necessary)-> Synaptic Interruption -> Elemental Redistributor-> Synaptic Interruption -> Elemental Redistributor-> Synaptic Interruption

When I did this, I also used Lead From the Front to add an extra sixth layer on.
