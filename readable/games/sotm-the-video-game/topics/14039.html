Only a small number of things in the game are determined using the random number generator, and shuffling is the main one. Ambuscade tends to shuffle his deck a bunch. Having a different number of cards in his trash or deck before he shuffles will result in a completely different outcome of the shuffle, and subsequent shuffles. Another example, shuffling a hero deck first will result in different shuffle for the next villain deck shuffle.
