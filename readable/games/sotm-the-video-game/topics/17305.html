a.k.a. I lose so you don't have to! :P

A few weeks back there was a slight mishap on Tales of the Archive where Gloomweaver got the better of the heroes, and everyone in the chat had the same general reaction of "Wow, that's awesome artwork, I've never seen that before!" to the loss screen afterwards.

So I thought it might be interesting to go ahead and cobble together an album of every villain and environment loss screen I could think of. I went through my collection of screenshots from over the time I've been playing and pulled out my unique losses, then went and lost a few games on purpose to fill in the stuff I didn't already have. (FWIW the ones I had to lose on purpose were Deadline, Gloomweaver, Heroic Infinitor, both Kismets, Agent of Gloom Spite, Wager Master, and the Wagner Mars Base Self-Destruct Sequence.)

I put them in my Dropbox: https://www.dropbox.com/sh/3tznv927wtgq45u/AADnDhBaaao6OyhEpxSyLYi9a?dl=0 It can be a little slow to load, but the links to the full pics should work OK. (If they don't I can try to upload everything to Imgur instead, but I wanted to use something I can update if need be.)
