Hi boys and girls. Just in case you didn‘t know it, if playing Akas’Thryia you should always have a look at the top card of the environment deck. You won‘t know which card will be played  (okay, for those smartypants of you, if there is just one card you will know it) but at least you will know from which deck that card will be. This was just a friendly reminder. I assume that a lot people already knew it but from time to time if playing online I notice that some rules/features are missed like this one, or the repeat decision feature which was added in one of the latest updates, or if using the Concordant Helm you should always draw first because if it‘s your first action during your turn you will get 2 cards. That‘s it, have a good time saving the multiverse.
