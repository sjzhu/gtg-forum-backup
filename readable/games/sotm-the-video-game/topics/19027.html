Ah. This happened to me, too. It does thematically make sense that stunning him wouldn't prevent him from exploding, but it sure was an unwelcome surprise at the time. Now I know  : )

 

You can prevent Borr from dealing damage with Ground Pound or Grease Gun. One neat trick is to get Scholar's Alchemical Redirection and Expect the Worst both out. This protects all of your heroes from Borr while leaving villain and environment cards exposed. You need an extra card play to do it, but those aren't too uncommon in Obivaeon mode. Making your heroes immune to damage does the same, but because he deals both fire and energy damage you still need a combo to do it. Examples include Immunization and XPW Haka, Heroic Interception and Leader of the Pack, or Flesh of the Sun God  with Lead From the Front and Next Evolution.
