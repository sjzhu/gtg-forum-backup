When Iron Legacy was released, it was neat seeing that all the playable Parsons had unique dialog with him, even though he's not any of their Nemesis. So it got me thinking what other neat interactions there could/ought to be in the video game and I figured I'd start a thread on the subject. Without further ado, this is what I'd want from the game in a perfect world:

1. Team dialog: If all the Prime Wardens are on your team, it'd be cool to have the leader say something to note it. Same with Freedom Five or Dark Watch.

2. Let the others talk: Things like having Setback or Expatriette throw out some banter if they're in the line-up. Or Unity and OX. Maybe Tyler has a problem with Maia's darker variant. Have the other heroes inching away uncomfortably from Dark Visionary. Lots of possibilities here.

3. Environmental dialogs: Akash'Bhuta being on Mars is straight up weird -- maybe the heroes should notice. Or have Tempest comment on Voss returning to Dok'Thorath to end the civil war. Have DW Fixer call out Zhu Long when you go to his place.

4. Story battles: If you're taking all the Prime Wardens up against Akash'Bhuta, they could throw in some references to that particular storyline in the dialog. Ditto Dark Watch vs. The Chairman or Freedom Four vs. Baron Blade.

That's my short list. What do you guys want to see?

Note here that I'm not talking about technical stuff or game mechanics so much, but what you'd like to see to have the video game flesh out more of the game's lore and world.
