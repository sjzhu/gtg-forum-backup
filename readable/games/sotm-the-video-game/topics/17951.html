To my AAR I have to add:

I don’t much like randomized Wager Master challenges. Given how ridiculously random Wager Master games are, with turn-2 wins from “Losing to the Odds” being fairly common vs. games which are nigh impossible because of Who Are You Fighting, a randomized start means that comparing war stories  after the challenge is pointless. Some people are going to have trivial games which teach nothing, and some are going to Kobayashi Maru games which no amount of clever play can salvage. With most villain match-ups we can talk strategy and clever tricks, but with Wager Master strategy doesn’t rise much above plays to take advantage of Losing To The Odds.
