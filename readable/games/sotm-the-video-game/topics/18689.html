Was messing around against Citizen Dawn. She got an Aurora on the turn while she was invincible with five minions, nuking my Naturalist tank cards. Guise, Tachyon and Ra get killed instantly by her full field of minions with no guard. Writhe (variant) and Naturalist (variant) survive both with literally 1 hp. Let the game run for a few turns, and turns out Dawn continues to be unable to play cards until Shadow-cloak leaves play, even though it's facedown.

 

Bug? Intended interaction? I assume not, as the text on the Shadow-Cloak should not apply while facedown.
