To all my fellow newbies to this fantastic game, if you are going for any of the story mode Freedom Five unlocks (and I recommend going for all 5 at once for obvious reasons) please don’t repeat my mistake! (Though let’s be honest, if you found this thread you probably already have.) If you only unlocked costumes for the Prime Wardens or Darkwatch using the “You can unlock this variant here without doing the story challenge” thing (cheating as Guise calls it) you can lose as many of the prerequisite games as you want and it won’t unlock for you. You have to unlock them via story mode. I had only unlocked Darkwatch Harpy and Darkwatch Setback by cheating so I had to play through their unlocks and redo the last 2 Prerequisite games. Harpy was easy, Setback took more games than I’d care to count.

 

If you don’t know how to do the unlock at all and you stumbled across this thread: https://sentinelsdigital.com/variants#freedomfive

And if like me it took you far longer than it should to notice the explanation for what the “Prerequisite Games” are: (mouseover the Freedom Five logo to see prerequisite games)
