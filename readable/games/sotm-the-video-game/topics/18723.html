This is just an exercise in silliness, but it occurred to me that the trouble we’re having with some of the Void Guard unlocks could just be because the conditions are absurd.  Now, I don’t think that the unlock for Super Sentai Idealist is “Play a multiplayer game with the Void Guard.  Each player must type “Void Guard!” into chat within 30 seconds of the game being started.”  But then the question occurred to me- what sort of weird, meta unlock conditions could be done in the video game, and what hypothetical variant could they work with story-wise?  Here are some I came up with:

Renegade Chrono-Ranger: Play a game in Rook City where rewinding and taking a different action prevents at least 1 hero from being incapacitated.

Blade-Bot Luminary: Lose a game against basic Baron Blade.  At the end of the game, have at least 50 decisions set to repeat all game.

Time Management Guise: Spend 10 minutes with Guise’s hands on your screen in one game.

Trivia Host Wager Master: Play a multiplayer game titled with either “Freedom Five,” “Prime Wardens,” “Void Guard,” or “Dark Watch.”  Use those variants for that team during that game.
