Ridiculously easy mint. Though it seems I played slightly differently than anyone else.

My primary concern was preventing any further faux Egyptian gods from showing up. From the title I expected all 9 to arrive. First two turns I did what TakeWalker mentioned - Fortitude + Take Down to delay while I looked for deck control cards.

Parse is probably my favorite hero by far, and she has a wealth of deck control. Wraith’s IR Eyepiece is nice, but Parse has Guage, Extrasensory Perception, and Buffer Overflow, all of which could block additional gods, equipment or ongoing destruction. Thus I used Wraith’s Clandestine Funding to give Parse card draws until she got Extrasensory Perception, and tended to alternate between Parse and Legacy for card draws afterward. Legacy mainly for Lead from the Front and Next Evolution.

Parse, predictably, was MVP. She rapidly dug through her deck until she had 3 Critical Multipliers in play, along with Snap Decision. Wraith helped her look for cards and Legacy helped her play them. With 3x Critical Multiplier, Recomplie reads “one hero does +9 damage.” It wasn’t too difficult to get at least one of those a turn, and often two. On the last turn, Wraith did 15 points to Geb, and Legacy did 25 points to whatshername.

With so much deck control between Extrasensory Perception, IR Eyepiece (which shows up early) and eventually Guage, the Ennead was limited to 1 and 2 point plinks. No additional gods showed up, no equipment destroyed, no ongoings destroyed. The one AoE attack got Throat Jabbed.

It’s true that Extrasensory Perception gives the Ennead plays - but it felt worth it, initially, to get rid of a really threatening card. Guage and IR Eyepiece only delay bad cards, they don’t eliminate them. In truth, though, it was over before that could have been a problem.
