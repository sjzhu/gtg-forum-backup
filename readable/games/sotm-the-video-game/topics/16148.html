You absolutely need someone to manipulate his deck in order to make it happen. (You can also chain Good Samaritans at the start of the environment turn if you're lucky.)

The good news is, once you're able to flip a drug face-down, you have him. Unless he plays Forced Entry, you'll keep the victims in the Safe House, and you can search through his Drugs until you find the ones that aren't as bad, and keep him stuck on those. I had a game recently where he played nothing but Victims all game and it was just a question of "do I make him flip"? To which the answer was "no". :) Easy game!
