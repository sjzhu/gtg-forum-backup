Crowbars+Friendly Fire explanation.

  1. Mr Fixer hits The Operative with his power.
  2. Because has dealt damage to a non-hero target, Friendly Fire triggers and asks if Mr Fixer wants to deal damage to Setback.
  3. Mr Fixer deals damage to Setback.
  4. Mr Fixer has dealt damage, Dual Crowbars triggers and Mr Fixer hits The Operative with this "Dual" hit.
  5. Go to 2.

So everytime you deal Setback 3 damage, you can deal The Operative 5 damage!

Setback gets absolutely wrecked but gains a ton of tokens and The Operative is probably dead.

Huzzahs!
