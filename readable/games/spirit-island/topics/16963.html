I wanted to take a moment to admire the fact that even though none of the cards have flavor text (which is a real shame, but there's just no room*) pretty much every card portays it's flavor so perfectly that they don't need any text to accompany them.  The art, the evocative titles, and the rules text fill in all the gaps.  When Lightning is Shattering Homesteads, it's clear what's going on.  When Fangs Preys on the Builders, it's perfectly clear why it's preventing builds.  When the Invaders start looking for Scapegoats, or the Island suffers a Year of Little Rain we don't even need art to tie the effects to the flavor.  That is really cool.

 

* Also, I'm pretty sure that the flavor text on at least half the cards would be variations of "Ach, Hans, run!  It's the ____" and "Aiieee!"
