Morning everyone!

I see no reason to hide the setup- you have the cards and the spirit mats.  Be aware that these are still being tested, so they might change!

Grinning Trickster: Put 2 presence on your starting board: 1 in the highest-numbered land with Dahan, and 1 in land #4.

Burning Plague: Put 3 on your starting board: 1 in the Wetland without Dahan, 1 in the Mountain without Dahan, and 1 in a land with towns/cities. In that final land, also put 1 blight (from the box); this does not destroy or cascade.  1 of your presence starts the game already destroyed.
