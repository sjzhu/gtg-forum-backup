The easier way to think about it is that powers do not have a target until you use them, even if you have plans for how you might use them.  That way you can play powers without worrying about if they have a target yet but know they are likely to help and give you needed elements.  Also lets you coordinate with other Spirits after you've already played powers, but before they're used.
