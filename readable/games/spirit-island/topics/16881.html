It is not really correct to say that the Invaders attack the land and then attack the Dahan.  The invaders deal damage once, but that damage affects both the land and the Dahan simultaneously.

It's mostly a semantic difference, but considering ravages in this manner makes things like defense a lot clearer.  Defend protects both the land and the Dahan because there is only one instance of damage to be reduced.  And Vital Strength's sacred sites will protect the Dahan even if the ravage causes blight to destroy your sacred site, because the damage to the Dahan was dealt (and defended against) is the same damage that blighted the island.
