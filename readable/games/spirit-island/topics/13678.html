As we know, there are many artists working on Spirit Island, and many of them have released some of their artwork for the game. Since they all prefer different platforms, it's a little hard to track it all down. I'm going to compile a list of the pieces I find here.

The artists, as listed at the end of the Kickstarter video, are

  • Jason Behnke
  • Loic Belliau
  • Rocky Hammer
  • Sydni Kruger
  • Nolan Nasser
  • Jorge Ramos
  • Moro Rogers
  • Graham Sternberg (misspelled in the credits)
  • Shane Tyree
  • Joshua Wright

Adam Rebottaro is also credited for Art Design, but I don't know that he did any of the principal illustrations himself. In addition, I found one artist so far who appears to have done some work but isn't (yet) credited:

  • Beth Zyglowicz, SpaceTurtleStudios

Here's a list I will update with released illustrations by each artist:
