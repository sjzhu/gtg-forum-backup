Agreed on (1), you can place presence in lands with Blight.

(2) has never come up with me, and the situation you describe seems like a reasonable time it might. Good question! In the absence of any guidance, I would say that you do have to do all the options in the growth box.

In terms of order, the growth choices are ordered the way you would want to order them, anyway; eg, gaining a power card before you decide where to put your presence, or adding a presence at range 2 before adding one at range 0.

You are definitely required to do everything in the Growth box, but grysqrl is correct - you can move Presence already on the board. (I think there's a "Moving Presence" sidebar next to the section on Presence on the page with the spirit panel? If not, there should have been.) This mostly crops up when a Spirit has all the Presence on the board that they're legally allowed to place, but might in theory be relevant at other times.

At some point the rule was that you do everything in the box in order from left to right. I think we may have dropped the "in order" part at some point, but the question really hasn't come up in my games

That was dropped for the sake of simplicity - especially since the options were nearly always in the order you'd want to do them anyhow.
