The invader board is great when you're starting, it has more than enough information to help you while you learn the game.

I don't need that anymore, and the structure of the board comes with a lot of issues and visual noise baked in, so this is my first draft at solving some of the problems.

- Clarify fear-state. It's way too easy with large fear pools to have some fear nebulously between the pool and earned fear.
- Increase visual access to blight. For something so critical it is hard to see the grey tokens on the beige card, especially from far away.
- Improve flow. The structure of blighted island > event > fear > invaders is all over the place on initial board.
- Most importantly, reclaim some table space.

https://imgur.com/a/qu8BQRF

I currently hide the back of the standing island card with a second island card, and plan to eventually make a victory card for the bottom of the fear deck.
