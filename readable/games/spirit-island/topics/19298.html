First question: Yes, you trigger the effects in order. So you can push the Explorer, then add the Wilds.

 

Second question: A Sacred Site is 2 or more Presence, and you only trigger the rule when creating a Sacred Site. So adding a third Presence in a land does not trigger the rule, as you say. And it has to be 2 of your Presence, you can't combine with another Spirit's Presence.
