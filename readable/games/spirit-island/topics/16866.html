I was so inspired by lynkfox that I made my own! The game arrived Wednesday night, so of course I spent all of Thursday making this thing. Before I even played the game! Where are my priorities?! This is my first board game insert and it's a little rough (I don't have the right tools for foamcore), but I'm happy with how it came out.

Of couse, pictures:

The box has a lid. It closes. Everyone knows this; I don't know why I bothered saying it.

![](upload://1yavf2OfCKGA5FKTIJrF8dSeUCo.jpeg)

The manuals sit on top of everything:

![](upload://w4DGvnJ9i66vgmnKMzNLbmuGSmO.jpeg)

And the island boards underneath:

![](upload://zC7OOfPUOaFe1lrPUy0LDKe8Uo1.jpeg)

Under the island boards, the box is split in half. The insert on the far side and two Plano boxes on the near side. The Plano boxes hold all of the bits that you need frequent access to during the game (invaders, Dahan, energy, spirit tokens, scenario tokens). I split it evenly into two boxes because, in 3-4 player games, I like to put one box near each end of the table so everyone has easy access without having to reach across the island. Presence, reminder tokens, fear, blight, fear dividers, and adversary reminders are all in baggies along the right side. You generally only need these things during setup.

![](upload://nSWdDiZOw8ksOuABjJytSPEREA8.jpeg)

The top section of the insert holds the spirit panels. They fit snugly and keep everything in place during transit. The cutouts on the near side of the insert are for clearance for the hinges of the Plano boxes.

![](upload://cakGH34HSqNlxhJMwOQJpxwYUMN.jpeg)

The folded base game board sits in a cavity under the spirit panels. I stash the fear and blight tokens on the right because there's lots of space for them.

![](upload://tQTLa2gy7vHb6ZHvrZur7uueaQP.jpeg)

There's a little cutout for the adversary and scenario panels under the board:

![](upload://5GwIpRGp9bz7vUsEo9eReg2q4AZ.jpeg)

And all the cards go at the bottom. The power progressions and reference cards fit under the event deck and the blight cards fit under the fear deck. There are little finger wells in the middle to make removing the cards easy.

![](upload://atKDoFnSj2MXxd8cXBZU68Gknbh.jpeg)

And that's the thing. I didn't make a space for the Branch and Claw board extension because I don't use it and don't really want to carry it around. The canonical map mat, sadly, won't fit in here; I might try to make some kind of carrying tube for it.

Be careful! The world of creating custom inserts for your game boxes is addictive! You will soon find yourself making random boxes for random objects in random containers! xD

 

In all seriousness however, it is very easy. You need:

 

A good, sharp exacto knife with extra blades

A -METAL- ruler (plastic or wood will shave and cause uneven lines)

White glue (elmers is great)

Foamcore. (I like black, white is fine. Personal preference)
 

Not necessary, but very nice: A self healing cutting mat. If you don't want to drop the cash for that, some big sheets of cardboard work just as well.

 

All of the above is available at your local craft store most likely. Jo-ann, Michaels, Hobby Lobby if you are in the States all work great. If not, I am certain you know what your local variety of bigbox craft store is and with a proper list the people there can help you find it. The Plano boxes are almost always found at those stores too (people use them to store beads and jewelery parts) but if that doesn't work you can also find them often at fishing supply stores.

 

For making:

First, watch this video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3hfKaDX2P2k&amp;index=30&amp;list=UUzm-X8tDtEyrOeYiC9SdcJg

(edit, if the edit is working, here is the playlist for all 3 foamcore videos: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL3qhmvdVjd2uRvjS_6G3tcH1bGK6vHz2R )

(and others on their channel) - they do an excellent job of explaining what to do.

Next:  be patient! It takes a lot of patience, otherwise you'll rip the foamcore and cause uneven edges, or you'll cut things the wrong size. Measure multiple times and think about it, them measure again. 

But it is a lot of fun and feels really good.
