No.1

1. Invaders deal Damage to Dahan and to the land, reduced by Defend. Add Blight if necessary.
2. Dahan fight back (if any survivors)
2. Let Them Break Themselves Against the Stone (if Invaders dealt Damage to the land)

No.2
1. Invaders deal Damage to Dahan and to stone.
2. Dahan fight back (if any survivors)
2. Let Them Break Themselves Against the Stone (if Invaders dealt Damage to the land)
3. Invaders deal Damage to the land. reduced by Defend. Add Blight if necessary.

Which way is right?
