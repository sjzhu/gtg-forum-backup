Rymdkejsaren:

So for the first example, that must mean that you have to push 3, whether you like it or not?

Yes, unless it says “You may Push 3 ,” in which case you can push 0 or 3.

Rymdkejsaren:

Second, I assume based on the do as much as you can principle that I can still target a land that has fewer than 3 Dahan with that power?

Yes, unless the effect said, “If there are at least 3 in target land, Push them.”
