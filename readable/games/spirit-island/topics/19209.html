This is a thing that's been around since the early days of Sentinels - basically fan-made content is okay with >G but you're just not allowed to post any of it here because their lawyers suggested such, on the basis that if >G come up with an idea that resembles something someone already posted, that person might start accusing them of stealing their idea and stuff. It's just to cover themselves against that kind of thing, really. But go nuts on posting your own ideas anywhere else - I understand the Board Game Geek forum is a popular place for such things, and is where the bulk of the fan-made Sentinels content is posted. I expect there are at least a few fan-made Spirit Island spirits and stuff up there too, by now, but I never visit so I have no idea personally.

BGG or your own website is the place to post fan-created content to an existing game.  This avoids any sort of legal issues with us "stealing" ideas if we use something vaguely similar down the line in (in this case) a spirit island expansion, or if you eventually decide to turn your creations into their own thing in a different game.  Lore Forge Games (who are designing Prime War) started as a bunch of Sentinel Tactics players who wanted to make Tactics better and ended up taking it in an amazing new direction.  Heck, I started going down the path that ended with joining GtG as someone who liked Sentinels stuff so much I designed a bunch of custom heroes, dove deep into the lore, and just kept going down the rabbit hole!

There's no issue with you posting links to said content once it's done, and Lore/Fan Fiction is always welcome here. We have a specific section for Sentinels Lore and Sentinels Fan Fiction, but thus far there hasn't been any need for Spirit Island specific versions.  Have fun designing!
