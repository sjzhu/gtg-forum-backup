So, here's a conundrum.

Currently, I own every deck that's been sold for the SotM game--all the expansions and mini-expansions, as well as quite a few of the promos.

As it sits right now, I have these cards in two boxes:  the SotM EE box (which holds all of the Heroes and the Environments) and the Vengeance box (which holds all of the Villains and oversized villain cards).  I use one of the smaller expansion boxes to hold tokens and promo cards.

Right now, I can probably fit 2 more Hero decks and 2 more Environment decks in my SotM-EE box.  Concurrently, I could probably fit 4 full Villain decks in the Vengeance box.  Or, perhaps, a set of 5 Vengeance-style villains.

I submit to you, ladies and gentlemen:  the next expansion for Sentinels of the Multiverse will be the final one, as nothing more will fit in my boxes!

Well, heck--since I've already given my guesses above, I might as well throw out some completely too-early italicised (not bold) predictions for the next/final expansion:

  1. Our theme this time will be "unlikely alliances at the End of the World".  I picture something like the final two episodes of Justice League Unlimited, where Lex Luthor joins with Superman to fight Darkseid.
  2. Our heroes will be hero-fied versions of Baron Blade and The Matriarch.  It'd make a nice completion to the Baron Blade arc, and there's already been a ton of foreshadowing regarding The Matriarch as a hero.
  3. Our major villain will be the Cosmic Entity--a Galactus-style world-ending creature, who we'll fight through his Heralds:  something like a SotM version of the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse.  We've already seen him in Nightmist's deck, but he'll be a Vengeance-style villain, but one that you can't directly attack until his Heralds are destroyed.
  4. One of the environments will be The Center of the Multiverse (or something similar), which will play somewhat like the Realm of Discord or the Time Cataclysm--lots of constantly shifting terrain, a handful of targets, but lots of weirdness and unpredictability.
