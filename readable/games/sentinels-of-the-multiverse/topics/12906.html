Visionary has always beenn a favorite, but she just absolutely owns the Rook City villains and enviornments.

Mass Levitation takes a lot of sting out of Pike Industrial and Rook City itself. They go from brutal to heroes to fairly neutral. I almost never bothered with that card before, but here it’s nuts.

Chairman with Brain Burn is, of course, just huge, but add in Suggestion and it gets silly. And Suggestion on Matriarch can just stunt her whole ability and put a hurting on the Cohorts. Twist wrecks both of their damage output too.

For Spite, she digs for his drugs fast while also being able to give him less damage with Twist.

She’s less useful against the Rat, but even there her constant ongoing destruction or going into a coccoon to soak an infection is at worst solid.

So, yeah, if you didnt love Vis with the original villains (she isnt great against Blade or Ambuscade), time to look again.

She is useful against Plague Rat. If you've got AZ on the team, TtE AZ and now the Infected damage is Cold or Fire. She can also Telekinetic Cocoon, and become immune to the Infected and Irreducible damage. Sitting there soaking Highest HP damage and for some Heroes after PR flips, letting them target her to heal 3 when PR is flipped. That often does not work out well, but it is an option. TtE on PR can also make all his damage splash off Legacy's LFtF and NE.

But I do not like Visionary in general, because Wrest the Mind is horribly useless and too common in the deck. In a few rare instances, she can redirect 5 to take 3 and deal the mook 3, but after Silver Gulch, that much damage from one environment card is extremely rare. They're dealing 3 max, which makes WtM a highly questionable play. If you can get her cocooned, WtM works better. But in most games, WtM goes unplayed because she cannot handle that feedback damage. She is good support for Legacy, putting his Ongoings back, and good against single target villains who tend to have Ongoings. But her damage is rare. I would like to see her environment deck manipulation removed and something more substantial added.

Her non-damage stuff is fun, and combos interestingly, but a lot of that is rare in the deck and can never hit her hand. Since you do not play her for damage reasons, her damage cards are clogs in the deck, stalling her more interesting cards.

Wrest the Mind is more situational than most cards.  But I'd hardly call it 'horribly useless'.  If nothing else, it allows you to deal up to 45 damage to Omnitron when dropped on an Electropulse Explosive, which is usually worth the 15 damage Vis takes.  More generally, if the wrested target dies from the 3 damage to it, then Vis won't take her damage, so it's easy to come out ahead.  Take, for example, Baron Blade's Powered Remote Turret (7 Hp, 2 damage to all hero targets @ EoT).  Wrest the Mind the damage at Blade, and instead of each hero taking 2 (6-10 damage total) Blade will take 6, Vis will take 6, and the turret will be destroyed.  That's 13 damage to the villain team, at the cost of 6 damage that the heros were going to take anyways.  On Citizen Summer, there's a 13 point swing (6 redirected to the villains, 4 to kill Summer, and 6-3=3 damage saved from the heroes)  Taking out Summer by convential damage is only a 6 point swing (because that damage is headed towards the villains anyways, so the only benefit is the prevented damage to the heroes).

Belle Sorciere:

If you let the Mine hit Visionary first, she won't be able to deal damage, then she can redirect everything else for free.

Besides the obvious uses when you have a big target, I use Wrest the Mind in many other ways. It is like a Mind Spike that will penalize you for not killing the target, but you get to through other damage somewhere. 

If the target has more than 3 health, you can always redirect the attack back at them for a bigger attack. If the target has a 2 damage attack, suddenly Wrest the Mind is dealing 5 damage, that's Lightning Slash and Fire Blast territory. also, If the target deals damage based on it's health, it will instantly kill them.

I also really like to for killing/hurting things that just come out on the villain's turn. Let's say you are against Voss, and one of the three damage villain are there. Play Wrest the Mind on that target, instead of dealing straight damage. Now, at the end-of-the-villain's turn, a new minion will probably come out. Redirect the damagefrom the first minion with Wrest the Mind, killing the new minion before it deals damage, then Visionary kills the first minion. Wrest the Mind just dealt 6 damage, and saved a lot of damage.
