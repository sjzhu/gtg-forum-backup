Incapacitated Heroes stay in the same play area, and there is a special list Pre-Start of Turn actions every player can choose to do each turn. Most of them involve Mission cards, but one of them is ‘Use a single Incapacitated Ability in your play area”. So you have to decide if you want to use the Ability, or do Mission things.

If you Ressurect any hero that was incapped before Redacted takes over it goes to the box and is available for later.   If it’s after Redacted takes over then you can only resurrect one hero in your play area through Rites of Revival and not mulitple ones from one play area.  So if you still have an active hero you are unable to use Rites of Revival on any incapped heroes you control.
