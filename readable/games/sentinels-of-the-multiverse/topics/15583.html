#1

FF Tachyon - She was amazing, nearly broken.  Even against Deadline who kept trying to shuffle her deck, she easily did the most damage.  Any damage boost at all takes her from good to absolutely insane.

FF Legacy - I didn't use his environment control much with Argent on board, but he got set up awesomely fast.  He has so many great cards that only see play late game since his deck is so strong, extra plays getting them early left him a powerhouse!

PW Argent - His new power was nice for a turn or two... but he's the Argent so once he got some instruments going, it was less amazing.The strength boosts elsewhere on the team hurt it a bit too.  I can see him being amazing with someone like A/Z, but I think I still like the PW version best.  I'm going to end up playing Kvothe an awful lot because I LOVE his new art.

Cosminfinitor - well, I smashed myself for 8 damage turn 1, but managed to survive the game at 3 hp.  He was really fun, but also really, really suicidal.  He'll be amazing against villains who wreck his toys or if you see a better power early.

FF Bunker - I tried him twice because he was so fun.  This allows me to really explore his control options, as I'm not just digging for a gun.  He's... really fun and strong in a bigger party, great for cards like Don't Dismiss Anything, PW Fanatic, and Gimmicky Character.  This is my favorite promo so far.

Extremist Sky Scraper - Another I tried twice. Game was in the Enclave, so I made SS big and just tossed endangered species at the villain all game.  The second game, I managed to switch a lot more between sizes which was super fun. I really like how just staying in normal is more viable and how many cool tricks flipping between sizes opened up.  This is a seriously complex hero though.

New Parse - She was okay... got set up really fast, but was constantly short on cards. That may be variance, but without more cards being fed, the discard is a bit tough.

 

Who have you tried and what do you think?

I'm telling you guys, quite possible the best team I have ever played with is Legacy, Tachyon, Guise, and Captain Cosmic.  Start with Completionist Guise for it to work, and also start with Team Leader Tachyon and Green CC to start for best effect. Those two are absolutely two of teh best heroes in the game.  For Legacy, start with whatever floats your boat/is best for the matchup.

First, after CC gets some constructs up, you can sub in XPW CC and start hammering away.  Who says CC doesn't deal damage?  Also, a note; even without Completionist Guise, green CC is good.  Like, really good.  CC can destroy his own constructs with most of his one shots, making the negative part happen less often than you might think.

Play the game as you would, jumping around with Legacy so Guise can use the powers, and also for green CC is the constructs get culled, up until Tachyon has managed to get a lot of burst in and hopefully played a lightspeed or two.  Then, sub out for regular legacy, galvanize, blitz.  A friend and I did this combo, and we have a house rule that the game doesn't end until the end of the last hero turn once the villain dies, so we can deal overkill damage just for fun.  With this combo, we ended up dealing 137 damage to Infinitor.  That's double his life, so as you may notice, more than half of that came in the final round.  We kind of cheated, in that we specifically left him with 4 life the round before, but hey, fun's fun!

The Naturalist promo is wicked, when you get a form.  You only cross 50% odds at 9 cards into your deck, or your starting hand plus 5 card draws.

The FV together are insane.

Here's how I run them to "game" the team

Legacy, Bunker and AZ are all about the team being set up fast.

Legacy should start focusing card play on whoever is best able to use it, usually himself.

Wraith I go very basic, Eyepiece, Belt, Bombs, then a second Eyepiece.  That's all you need, then just start feeding cards to Bunker and whoever else needs them.

AZ you want to dig for Apertures, as Snap and Impale are part of the gig.  Not too hard to pull off with this team.

Bunker is another basic setup, you don't want to go turret early, just keep using your base and be willing to blow up modes to keep using your power.  Get Omni-Cannon though, because Wraith is feeding you cards.

Tachyon gets a lot of extra cards from Wraith as well, so play as many as you can.

You should hit late game builds really early with this team.

You should get late game set ups early and with Blitz, omnicannon, inventory barrage and all those ongoings for AZ the game won't last long.

Got a bunch more games in-

FF Legacy - if it weren't for just how good all the other versions were, this would be nearly broken. That free card play from the start is a godsend for everyone's set up, helps outpace destruction, and lets you set up the fastest uber Legacy in the game.  Add in TL or SS Tachyon or new Bunker and Wraith and it's nutty.

FF Bunker - In my first game with him, it was a three person. I just played him in two fives... and wow.  Get one gun out and Turret, then choose the best play for everyone the rest of the game. As long as that top card isn't immediately a mode, you'll never draw it.  It makes his hand the top card of everyone else's deck as he does huge damage. Awesome fun!

Xtreme Cosmic - Played with OG Legacy, new Parse, and Ra at various points... just wow.  This damage dealing is insanity.  Any boost takes him from good damage to an easy 9 or more a turn.  

Extremist Sky-Scraper - changing sizes a couple of times a turn is amazing (and with Proportionist, valuable).  This is not the best of the promos since so many are nearly broken, but it may be the most fun.

FF Tachyon - Remains silly with any strength boost, but is weirdly the most strategic Tachyon to play otherwise.  She goes long periods of time with no power to speak of and figuring out when to let loose is amazing.

#49

Since all three of Extremist's powers have the possibility of switching her size (either explicitly to Normal or from the chance of playing a Tiny/Huge card from the bottom of the deck), nearly every power usage will trigger one of Proportionist's when-you-change-size effects. Depending on the direction of the size change, this either means a free card draw or pinging all non-hero targets for 1 melee damage every time you use a power. More power uses means more size changes, giving Extremist with Proportionist a draw/damage engine at least on par with her standard powers.

The size-changing effects of her powers also let her make better use of effects that give her extra power uses during her turn -- to use Fanatic's Embolden, base SS needs to have Micro-Assembler or another deck's power-granting card; similarly, Captain Cosmic's Augmented Ally requires either Micro-Assembler or size-changing cards. Extremist's size changes let her use either one fairly reliably without needing any other specific cards.

And if the stars align and you have Proportionist, Embolden, AND Augmented Ally against a villain with little/no Environment destruction? Get ready for some insanity:

  • Start your turn at Huge size.
  • Discard a Link (via AugAlly) to use the Huge power. Switch to Normal if possible.
  • Play a Tiny card.
  • Use the Tiny power and choose to switch to Normal.
  • Use the Normal power (via Embolden) -- either play a Link or return the discarded one to your hand. Bottom-of-the-deck play potentially switches you back to Huge or Tiny.
  • Draw and end turn.

2-4 cards drawn, 1 Tiny card played, 1 optional Link played, 1 free bottom-of-the-deck play, 1-2 rounds of 1 melee damage each to all non-hero targets, X irreducible toxic to one target, 1 Environment card destroyed, and either 3 projectile to 1 target or 3 sonic to everything. Also works with Tiny > Normal > Huge > Normal > Tiny/Huge, and can be repeated almost indefinitely until either SS is dead from Embolden and Huge power self-damage, your whole deck is in your hand, or the villain destroys the cards needed for the combo.
