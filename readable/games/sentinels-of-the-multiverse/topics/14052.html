This is a sequel to the Cleansing Downpour Challenge that I had last year. To be brief, I took the team that was pictured on the CD card and went through all the villains and all the environments (though not all combinations thereof) with that team in the computer program.

Matthew Gray has updated his handy difficulty chart and prominent at the top (meaning most difficult) are the Dark Watch heroes. Since I now have access to three of them, I asked myself why not make a team with those guys? Well, why not indeed? So I will. Here are the nuts and bolts:

DW Expatriette and DW Nightmist are scored at +36 each, with DW Fixer at +32. The 3-person team scores out at a hefty +148.
With some opponents I have decided to add Dark Visionary to the team. Not strictly thematic I know, but my game and my rules. DV scores out at -31, and that team is overall +41.
I refuse to play against Spite. Personal preference, sorry.

This is the schedule of opponents, subject to change without notice:

Matchup #2 - Advanced Gloomweaver (-86) at Atlantis (+50). +112 overall.
Matchup #3 - The Ennead (-81) at Tomb of Anubis (+4). +71 overall.
Matchup #4 - Baron Blade (-72) at Wagner Mars Base (-14). +62 overall.
Matchup #5 - Miss Information (-55) at Megalopolis (-10). +83 overall.
Matchup #6 - Mad Bomber Blade (-50) at Silver Gulch (-8). +90 overall.
Matchup #7 - Apostate (-49) at Realm of Discord (-17). +82 overall.
Matchup #8 - Akash’Bhuta (-43) at Insula Primalis (+4). +109 overall.
Matchup #9 - Grand Warlord Voss (-23) at Final Wasteland (-58). +67 overall.
Matchup #10 - Plague Rat (-14) at Pike Industrial (0). +134 overall.
Matchup #11 - Omnitron (-10) at Silver Gulch (-8). +130 overall.
Matchup #12 - Citizen Dawn (-1) at Insula Primalis (+4). +151 overall.
Matchup #13 - Skinwalker Gloomweaver (+3) at Realm of Discord (-17). +134 overall.
