#5

It used to be that his Traps were face up in the deck to start.  When revealed they did their thing, got shuffled into the deck face up again, and then played the top card of the deck.   It got changed to this:

  • At the start of the game, turn all Trap cards in Ambuscade’s deck face up. 
  • Whenever a Trap card would be discarded, first the top card of the villain deck is played, then the Trap card is discarded. 
  • Whenever the villain trash is shuffled into the villain deck, all Trap cards are turned face up.

 

The reason was that if you prevented Ambuscade from dealing damage you could end up in an almost never ending loop of Traps being played.
