The Operative is mostly dealing damage and has no way to protect herself from damage. Of the three targets in her deck, only one of them actually does damage. This means if you stop The Operative from dealing damage then she will not be doing much. 

Miss Information is hard to say, as I don't have much experience with it. Though she does have several cards that not only hurt heroes, but can also help. Such as Judge Mental increases all Psychic damage by 2, so if you have a hero that is dealing Psychic damage then it will be pretty handy. Unless The Sentinels are in play, which Judge Mental then also prevents Hero damage from being increased. Another makes it so Heroes cannot use powers, but at the end of their turn that may play a card. Also, I think most (if not all) her Ongoings have an alternate means of destruction.
