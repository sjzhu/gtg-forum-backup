Killing ongoings is a must, as is deck control.  For these, either Visionary or Nightmist make for great additions.

One of the easier ways to kill Iron Legacy is through a single "Nova" turn.  Fanatic, Tachyon, Haka, and Bunker all have solid "nova" options, with Tachyon's being the most likely to appear.

Damage mitigation is also quite important, as IL hits like a mack-truck.  The Scholar and The Naturalist both have great tanking options that can help turn the tide.

A solid 4 man team?  Try The Scholar, Fanatic, Chrono Ranger, and Nightmist.  The Scholar can spend the majority of the combat in Flesh to Iron, Chrono-Ranger can up your damage output while slowly chipping away at IL's health.  Nightmist can focus on deck control and damage redirection using Mistbound and Amulet of the Elder Gods, while Fanatic can be your "wild card", taking out ongoings with Consecrated Ground (and End of Days, perhaps) and dealing damage where possible.

Things you want:

1.  Ongoing Destruction

2.  Damage that is good but not reliant on shots of 5 or higher.

3.  Damage Prevention and redirection:  these are huge, it's a low total damage race, a swing of 4-6 points of damage can be all you need to win.

4.  Ways to get around reduction if you can't clear the field.

5.  Card draw/searching.

Heroes that work well:

1.  Nightmist.  Nightmist is awesome vs. IL, her card draw, ongoing destruction and damage are good, and Amulet is huge if you get it.  Mistbound is a great card play as well.

2.  Tachyon.  The destruction is big and LSB is great if you can get enough cards in your trash.  Synaptic is awesome, if you can get it out of the trash and reuse it, the best way to hurt IL is with his own fist.  Hypersonic Assault rules if you can get it to deal damage to him.  TLT was the pinnacle in this fight, because card draw is so big in a short fight, but SST puts cards in the trash so fast she trumps TLT.

3.  Fanatic.  So much awesome in her deck, just let it out.

4.  Fixer.  Yep.  I love Fixer vs. IL.  Mantis/Tiger/Crane all great vs. IL.  he does enough shots of 2 damage that I take Fixer against him on advanced.  Also Grease Gun.  Because Grease Gun is the bomb.  Mantis is actually a great offensive card in this match if you have team mates that have extra pings of damage that need a second target, because you can turn it back on IL to double up damage, and it is awesome with Nightmist and her Oblivions.  Bloody Knuckles plus riveting crane is often a great way to set up your team to end the match, usually at the cost of your own life, but hey, it happens.

5.  Legacy.  Lead from the Front-Next Evolution is amazing in this game.  Just stop the melee damage and all he can do is play cards, which you can delay with takedown.

6.  Tempest.  Ball Lightning loves Iron Legacy.  It sits in the box constantly dreaming of the day when it gets to crush Iron Legacy.  Shackles, Cyclone, Shielding Winds actually being useful, it is just too much to pass on.  Tempest is awesome.  He dies fast though, which just lets him make heroes immune to melee damage, which in this fight is Crushing to the Legacy.

7.  Wraith:  Stun Bolts, Smoke Bombs, Throat Punch, Grappling Hook, combat stance.  All great cards.

8.  Parse:  Destruction and the irreducible damage.  Yeah!  A good starting hand for Parse crushes IL.

All of the other ones can work too, I've had games where Expat and Adept are the reoason we beat IL, even have beat IL with Unity.  Try some of the suggested ones then branch out, just make sure you have the desired abilities on your team.
