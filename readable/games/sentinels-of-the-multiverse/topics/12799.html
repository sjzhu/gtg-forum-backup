I have had a poke around a few times but never managed to actually join in a game because usually it's not at a time when I'm actually around (or it's at about 1am so a bit late for me).

The Sentinels are cool - they each have their own base power and have the unique ability of being able to bring themselves back from being incapped (if your healer guy isn't incapped himself, that is)…but once all four of them go down then their setup card flips and they have four available incap abilities there, rather than the usual three that everyone else has. Also, they count as four hero targets, but only one hero, mainly for the purposes of stuff like Infection (otherwise Plague Rat wouldn't be able to flip). Their deck is mostly one-shots and they can dish out a lot of damage. Despite having low hp individually (14, 13, 11) and thus susceptible to anything hitting the lowest-hp hero target, they can be really effective. As well as dealing plenty of damage they have a healer, as mentioned, and a tanky guy who can provide soak. They also have the means to provide healing and extra draw to their fellow players. Oh yeah, and it's quite possible to chain about eight cards or so in a row if the right ones come out :D.

Printer's Studio is basically telling you that it thinks the images are too simple, since it's expecting photographs. You can ignore it, assuming the wiki images are a reasonable size. It complained at a lot of my images but they printed fine.

With PS you do need to make sure there's enough black border to the images that they don't go into the bleed area beyond the dotted line - there are appropriate images out there that fit perfectly (someone posted a link to a Google drive folder with them in, but I forget who it belongs to, hopefully they'll chime in with a link for you).
