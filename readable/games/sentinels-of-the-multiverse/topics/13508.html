Let's see.  Wraith has a mansion, and would favor dark colors.  Tachyon would have at least one, but probably two or more playrooms filled with scientific toys to mess with.  I'm also kind of imagining that her wife would have a large hand in decorating the rest of the house.  I'd expect Legacy's house to feel very 'ordinary middle class family'.  Also, we've got a picture of what their kitchen looks like on Next Evolution, although considering what that evolution was, it wouldn't surprise me if their kitchen needed a bit of a remodel after that.  I imagine that Legacy would have converted his garage or basement into a gym.  Bunker would also have some sort of exercise apparatus, and probably some tools and things in the garage.  As far as I know he's a bachelor, so it would be pretty small.  Absolute Zero's house would be very austere and empty, and probably also fairly small.
