I had to step away from playtesting a few months because of real life. Had the pleasure to see that some of the issues that had me railing for the longest time were finally addressed. Playtesting also shifted in gears and is going very fast at the moment. Taking a step back also means realising that the scope of this expansion is bigger than the original core set.

 

Comparable number of heroes 11 instead of 10, but their interaction need to be checked not only amongst themselves but against all previous deck with an attention to the risk of power creeping. To complicate things more these decks had to be tuned to two character cards at once as some ofthose were rushed and imbalanced in vacuum and finalised before the deck.

Greater number of environments than core set 5 against 4.

A whole new villain mode that needs to be epic AND accessible. There is only one try at this as no expansions will patch it's holes. This is the scariest one and has come a very long way. Truth is it probably wont be universaly acclaimed, but it is fun and epic and still takes too much setup and play time.
