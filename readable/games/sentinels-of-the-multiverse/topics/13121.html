So last night I'm wanting to brush up on Fanatic so I'm playing the video game and using random to find an interesting match. I get Chairman in Atlantis vs Legacy, Fanatic, AZ, SS Tachyon, and Tempest. I'm thinking this will do, Legacy is a great buff and I love Tempest, I'll get to pratice Fanatic with what looks to be a good team backing her up.

 

I start the game and all is going well. I loose AZ to a bad Thermal Shockwave play because I forgot about a few +1's no big deal, just a setback. I get the Chairman to flip and start to wail on him. I've got the Chairman out and about 20 hp left on the Operative when the environment goes crazy and takes out my team minus Legacy. Now I can't remember the exact order they were played, but these cards were out: Pillars of Hercules, 2x Phosphorescent Chamber, and Toxic Seaweed. Legacy had a great setup out: 2x Next Evolution, Danger Sense and thank god Motivational Charge. 

 

This was looking hard, but win-able when I encountered what I presume to be a glitch, as I have no idea how it happened: Chairman flipped back to 1:Alive with full HP, and 2: Master of the Underworld side. I've gone back and looked at all the cards in all the in play decks and can't see what triggered this, but there it was. So with incap plays and environment gone mad, Next Evo kept Legacy immune to Melee, Danger Sense nulled the Environment, and Legacy slugged it out. Chairman kept drawing Perfect Specimens so this too longer than I wanted, and I had to kill thief on sight as a lost ongoing would be fatal, but fun was had and almost the entire environment deck was in play by the end. It was almost Legacy vs Chairman and Atlantis vs the Mob. I ended up winning with near perfect health for Legacy at 30. 

 

All in all crazy fun, but I'm a little concerned by the Chariman respawn. Was that supposed to happen? Has it happened to anyone else?

It is consistent with everything else. When a card is destroyed, doesn't matter which card, it goes to the trash. If it doesn't, there will be a rule that specifically says what to do. When hero character cards are destroyed, they flip. When Omnitron is detroyed, he is removed from the game. Now, all villains should go to the trash, except that the game ends as soon as they are destroyed. The only corner case is the Chairman. Since it doesn't say what to do when he is destroyed, and since the game doesn't end, he follows the same rules as all other cards, and he goes to the trash.
