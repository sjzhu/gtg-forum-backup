After already seeing Omnitron and Fright Train change their ways, it appears with the box cover for Battle for Broken City that the Matriarch eventually switches sides to join the heroes. If they ever made a Matriarch hero deck, that would be really cool.

  • Baron Blade

This seems doubtful. Considering his past deeds and his apparent madness, I'm not sure he would ever choose to work alongside Legacy.

  • Citizen Dawn

Pending reconciliation between her and Expatriette and her mysterious father, this one might be possible.

  • Voss

It's unlikely that that the galactic dictator would have a change of heart but he might however be persuaded to work together against a common threat.

  • Ambuscade

As more of a hunter/mercenary type, Ambuscade seems like a leading candidate for a hero conversion. It would be fun to be able to play traps and use his fancy equipment.
