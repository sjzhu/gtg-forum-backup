I was super excited today cause i got to play SOTM with my wife and a few friends and achieved a feat that has eluded me since I started playing this game a few years back. We managed to win the game with all four of us at full health. We stalled a little towards the end to make sure we healed our minimal damage, but I was still very impressed with our victory.

We were playing against Omnitron on Omnitron IV. I was playing Omnitron X and we had Wraith, Parse, and Prime Warded Argent Adept.

Wraith got out the infrared goggles to control which cards came out mostly trying to avoid the destroy all ongoing/equipment cards cause that would have sucked for all of us. It tooks several turns of Technological Advancement and Reset, but I managed to get 2 of most of my components out even after having to destroy them several times due to the environment. At that point I was healing enough and dealing a steady stream of damage that we just overwhelmed Omnitron and any drones he put out. Parse set up as a debuff increasing all damage to new nonhero targets and played mostly cards that let us recycle or play extra cards helping my Omnitron X and my wife's Argent Adept set ourselves up quickly. Argent Adept set up for heavy buffing for Omnitron X and Wraith.
