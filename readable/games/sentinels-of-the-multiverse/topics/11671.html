Tachyon first - she's the fastest. If she's chugging at speed, she's going to overwhelm her system pretty quickly. She'll recover first though. Heck, she'll probably recover and get back in, only to pass out again before the rest. But she's still going down first.

Haka never falls. He's not much of a drinker, but he happily puts away pint after pint, with shots when they are demanded by his compatriots. As soon as Expatriette passes out, he finishes his current drink, then carries his allies to the nearby couches, wraps them each up neatly in a blanket, puts cool washcloths on their foreheads, and then slips out to the store to buy eggs, sausage, and other supplies for making a hearty "hangover-cure" breakfast.
