So I have a question about Captain Cosmic:Requital’s incapacitated side. 

It says before getting rid of your cards shuffle all constructs into a deck and place your character card on top of that deck. Then on his turn he can play a construct or the top card under his character card in a different field. Can I do this to the villain deck? If I can does that mean the villain can’t play cards? Or is this mechanic just suppose to be used on Captain Cosmic’s deck? Or possibly on another player’s incapacitated deck?

No.

When you are incapacitted you put all the constructs in CC's deck under his card.

The first  incap ability sys you can move 1 construct or the top card under this card to a new play area. So you may move a construct already in play to a new area or put a new construct in play from the top of the construct deck you made when he was incapacitated. You can't mesddle with other decks.
