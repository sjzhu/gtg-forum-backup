So, I normally don't post about mechanical things and such, but I just had a match with Xtreme Fanatic for the first time.

 

I randomized in the Video Game and got Plague Rat, and then I added in Legacy because I got an evil idea.

 

First turn, Infection was played on Fanatic. Every turn thereafter, I used "Kill the Spirit" and all my infection damage (with legacy boosting and the last turn with a Explosives Wagon from Silver Gulch) was redirected to Plague Rat. It was sooooo much fun.

 

Also, for the first time in... I think ever? I played Chastise. I've pretty much never played that card, because taking the damage isn't generally worth it and there are very few targets I want to just delay instead of trying to destroy, but with this version? It was just a delayed damaging one shot, and also kept the locus around longer (cause why not have more +1's when trying to burn down the rat)

 

I really, really, like this version of Fanatic
