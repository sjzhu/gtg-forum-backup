El Legado Mejor! (The Best Legacy)

It should really be "El Mejor Legado!"

Mejor is a limiting adjective and as such it goes before the noun in Spanish. Other examples: mi mejor amigo (my best friend), último lugar (last place), primera base (first base). 

Hopefully a playtester can comment on this before this goes to production.

See, this is exactly why I think this will need a clarification.  The "munchkin" interpretation (add, multiply, subtract) would have him deal ((1+1)x2-1) = 3 fire damage and healing himself for ((3+1+1+1)x2) = 14.  The method you describe is (add/subtract), then multiply.  Mathematical order of operations would make it (1x2+1-1) = 2 damage but (2x2+3) = 7 healing.  "If you would deal damage, double it" would make it 2 damage, but only 5 healing.  I think those are all valid interpretations and the card should try to answer it before print.
