I'm surprised no one listed one of the quickest wins that can occur. Lets see if I can do this right:

We'll be fighting Heroic Infinitor with Omnitron-X, Mr. Fixer, Expatriette and Bunker at the Enclave of the Endlings.

Heroic Infinitor starts out with two Twisted Miscreations and Two Occular Swarms. He plays a third Occular Swarm and punches a Miscreation for 1. The Miscreations choose to hit Expatriette and Mister Fixer.

Omnitron-X is up, and he knows what is about to happen due to time travelling. He plays Temporal Shielding, then uses Time-Shift on Infinitor, revealing Insanity.

We put Insanity into play, and it plays a Twisted Miscreation, then all three Recalescent Hellions, then all three Lambent Reapers. So all now all Manifestations are in play.

Insanity plays one last card, which is Whispers of Oblivion, so all Manifestations are down to 3, with one Miscreation at 2.

Whispers of Oblivion plays another card, which is another Whispers of Oblivion, so all Manifestations are down to 2, and with one Miscreation at 1.

The second Whispers of Oblivion plays a card, and guess what, its a third Whispers of Oblivion, so one Manifestation is under Infinitor, and all the others are at 1.

The third Whispers of Oblivion still has its card play left, so it plays Machinations of a Madman, which reveals a Crazed Artifice, so 1 last wave of damage comes through to wipe out all the Manifestations, winning the game.

Oh, and Omni-X finishes with full health since Machinations of a Madman chooses to hit all the Manifestations first, so the only damage that could get through to him never actually arrives.
