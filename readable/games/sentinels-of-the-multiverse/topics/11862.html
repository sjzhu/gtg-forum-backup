One of my local gaming/card/comic shops saved the simple white rectangular boxes that they received large shipments of cards in, and sold them for pretty cheap ($.75 to $1.00 for a foot long box).  You could try that route provided you have a similar shop in your area.

Also, a simple Google search of "Game Card storage" will give you plenty of options, including this 3000 card count storage box for a few bucks.

 

Or, if you are a purest and justify your spending that way, you could purchase the Rook City & Infernal Relics box (same size/layout as the Enhanced Edition box) and use that as your spill over box.  I was a late comer to the game and missed those two expansions anyway, so purchasing the box made sense.  Admittedly, if I was in your position, I would probably save the money and just use a generic white card storage box.

I’ve been very happy with my current storage solution, although now that I got so many expansions for Christmas, I’m just about out of space.

I bought a heavy cardboard decorative photo storage box from a craft store and modified it. It’s got metal corners and a nice leather handle, and it latches shut. I superglued in small strips of balsa to hold plexiglass dividers in place, and that’s made enough space to hold vertically placed cardboxes (Spiff’s boxes) for the base set, Rook City + Infernal Relics, Shattered Timelines, all but the newest mini-expansions, and most of Vengeance. I haven’t yet added the 5 vengeance villains to the box, and I’m pretty much out of space. If I removed the tokens and the oversized hero cards with the spinners that I use to track HP, I’d likely have enough space left for Vengeance and Wrath of the Cosmos. I’m not sure what I’ll do though.
