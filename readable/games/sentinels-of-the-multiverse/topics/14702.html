Belle Sorciere:

I love seeing it. Previously I'd primarily seen this sort of naming in the X-Men and among City of Heroes players.

It's more or less become the standard for new characters across the past few decades - look at the Teen Titans OCs (Raven, Cyborg, and Starfire), or the members of Youngbloods (a pretty awful comic for gender equality in general, but I'll admit that I couldn't tell you based on names alone which of Chapel, Masada, Riptide and Photon are women*), or a bunch of other characters of more recent vintage.  The Legion of Superheroes even gave up their absurd "boy" "girl" and "lass/lad" style names in favor of classier stuff like "Umbra" and "Polestar" for a while.

But, certainly, it was X-Men that set the tone and led to that becoming the norm - and it's chiefly in X-Men where characters in that style are the majority of famous and respected icons, rather than C-listers.  That's part of what makes it so sweet to see that convention picked up in Sentinels.
