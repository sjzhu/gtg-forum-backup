What we Know

 

Contents
  • 5 heroes - Harpy, Akash, La Comodora, Lifeline, Luminary
  • 4 Villain decks - Oblivean, Scions, Aeonmen, and missions decks. Shield cards too
  • Comic Book
  • Two Environments
Extras
  • 27 hero variant cards
  • 6 additional heroes - Stunman, Benchmark, The Void Guard

 

Other Misc Info
  • The Void Guard heroes character cards are not intended to be able to be used when using the Sentinels deck. - Art Feed
  • The Void Guard will have special instructions included with it explaining how it interacts with the Sentinels deck. - Art Feed
  • One or more of the new (ex villain) heroes will have cards with previously villain exclusive keywords i.e. fowl, primieval limb, device - reddit ama
  • It's possible that Citizen Dawn is represented somehow within Oblivean - reddit ama
  • Luminary Info from Twitter Teaser - Lumniary - 27HP

    Innate Power: "Devise" - Power: Discard the top two cards of your deck. Move any Devices discarded this way into play. [Interesting that it says devices, not equipment]

    The nemesis icon on Luminary's card seems to be OblivAeon (The icon matches Obv's chestplate and the fuzzy filename text corroborates this)

    From the text entry box on one the other screen in the picture, you can also see:

    Lumniary's Incap Abilities: One player may play a card / One player may discard 2 cards. If they do, they may draw 2 cards. / Destroy 1 hero ongoing card. If you so, destroy up to 2 ongoing cards. [From text entry screen]

    Variant Luminary's Innate Power: "Empowered Attack" - Power: Luminary deals 1 target X lighning damage, where X = the number of Devices in play plus 1. [From text entry screen]

  • The Harpy seems to involve some sort of flippable tokens. Dark Watch Harpy has 27 HP.

  • It appears Harpy and Luminary have the same nemesis icon, but they could be placeholder icons to avoid revealing secrets.

 

 

This topic is intended to help keep known info from the Reddit Ama, art feeds and such into one place. I probably missed plenty of things, so feel free to comment and I'll update it.
