Current behavior in the video game is that Hasty Augmentation boosts all instances of Volatile Wiring damage, but only while Hasty Augmentation is in play.  I shot a message to Handelabra, but no word yet on whether this is a bug or deliberate behavior.

 

Anyhow, NinjaMonkey, you mustn't be afraid to dream a little bigger.  Also, any 1-round damage puzzle that doesn't include Scholar is probably not the maximum you could reach.

 

[note:  I'm optimizing for O-X's damage here, and ignoring any potential damage other heroes could deal]

Team:  O-X, Scholar, AA, GL, Unity

 

O-X's turn:
Play Technological Advancement for an equipment.
Use Volitile Wiring, play Technological Advancement for an equipment (2 equipment in play).
Draw a card (5 cards in hand)

Scholar:
[quote=Don't Dismiss Anything]
O-X plays Technological Advancement for an equipment (3 equip, 6 cards)
AA plays Alacritous Subdominant

Scholar plays DDA:


O-X plays Tech Advance for an equipment (4 equip, 7 cards)
AA plays Instrumental Conjuration for Telamon's Lyra (3 cards)
GL plays Bolster Allies (O-X 8 cards, AA 4 cards)
[quote=Scholar plays Proverbs and Axioms]
everyone draws a card ( O-X 9  cards) 
O-X uses Volitile Wiring, plays an equipment (5 equip, 8 cards)
AA uses Lyra, uses Subdominant to play Inventive Preparation (3 cards), uses Inventive Preparation to play an O-X equip (6 equip, 7 cards)
GL uses Gung-Ho to let AA Vocalize and use Subdominant to play Instrumental Conjuration for Musarangi's Harp. (3 cards)
Unity heals
Scholar heals


Unity plays Hasty Augmentation on AA.  AA uses the Harp to play Inspiring Supertonic, and heal.
[/quote]
Unity puts Hasty Augmentation back on top of her library
GL plays Bolster Allies.  Unity redraws Hasty Aug (AA 4 cards, O-X 8 cards)  [note:  O-X has 14 equipment total.  Currently, 6 are in play, and 7 are in your hand, and 1 is in the deck where you can't get to it.  O-X's last card in hand is Singularity]
[/quote]
Skip Power Use
Draw a card

AA:
Play Silver Shadow into Instrumental Conjuration for Xu's Bell.  Inventive Preparation on O-X (AA 3 cards, O-X 7 equip)
Use Harp for Subdominant to play Syncopated Onslaught, then Subdominant to use Bell to activate Onslaught, then use Subdominant to use Lyra, play Silver Shadow into Subdominant, use original Subdominant to use Vocalize to use Supertonic on O-X, then Preparation on O-X (9 equip)
Draw a card (1 card in hand)

Legacy:
Play Inspiring Presence
Use Gung-Ho on AA.  AA uses Harp for Supertonic, O-X using Wiring (10 equip), Subdominant to use Bell, Onslaught then Subdominant to use Lyra to use Subdominant on O-X (11 equip) and Preparation on Unity (Scrap Metal)
Draw a card

Unity:
Start of Turn play Hasty Augmentation on AA.  AA uses Lyra to use Supertonic to use Bell to use Onslaught, then Preparation twice on O-X (13 equip in play)
Play Hasty Augmentation.  O-X uses Volatile Wiring, and plays Singularity, destroying 13 equipment (and AA's 3 instruments).  There are 5 Wiring triggers per equipment, and each trigger is 2 damage + 2 (Hasty Augmentation) + 3 (Syncopated Onslaught) + 1 Inspiring Presence = 8 damage.  That's 1358 damage, or a total of 520 damage.  Then Singularity deals 16+4 = 20 damage, for a grand total of 540 fire damage.

If in fact the current behavior in the video game is a bug, then this isn't quite as high, only 1 trigger will get the Hastey Augmentation boost, and you'll only deal 1346 + 13*8 + 20 = 436 damage.  Still pretty good.

It's possible that this could be tweaked to optimize it further, but I'm done now.  If you want to take it further, I forgot about Scholar's second Proverbs and Axioms, with which you could almost certainly get O-X's last equipment out, and possibly do even more schenanigans.
