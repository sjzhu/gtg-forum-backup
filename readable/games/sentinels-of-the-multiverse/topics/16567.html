As I'm sure you guys have seen, a new KS update came out! Here's where you can give it a read: https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/gtgames/sentinels-of-the-multiverse-oblivaeon/posts/1862354

And with this we've had a glimpse at each new OblivAeon hero! I like the idea of Benchmark and of how 'technological' his powerset is shown to be. Without looking at any of his other cards I'm going to say he's a 1-2 difficulty hero, depending on what cards of his you decide to focus on.

It looks like we really are gonna see Stuntman's personality through his deck, him being all about out-of-turn actions and such to 'show other heroes up'. I find that kind of funny/annoying, but that's the whole point, isn't it?

And of course we get a shout out for Sentinels of Earth-Prime(which I totally backed already, and you should check out if you haven't), which happily funded on its first day.

And it looks like we're gonna get a look at our five new environments next time, along with an update about the collector's box! Can't wait for May!
