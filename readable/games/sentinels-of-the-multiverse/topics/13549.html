There are no rules on how to handle a villain character card in the main rulebook beyond the standard "When the villain is defeated the game is over with a hero victory", as such unlike Hero Character cards (which have the flip function in the rules, replacing normal destruction rules), if a villain character card is destroyed and a card is preventing a hero win, and no card in play specifies what to do with the character card (or the game like in the case of The Dreamer) the card winds up going into the villains trash like every other villain card.

It's impossible to remove The Chairman from the game, even in The Final Wasteland, as the only card that would allow him to be removed from the game is an environment card and specifies that he'd have to be destroyed by an Environment Target, since the Chairman is immune to Environment Damage he can't be removed from the game.

End result, official ruling is that the Chairman can get reshuffled back into the deck, if you're using the oversized villains I recomend keeping his small character card around for this eventuality, or just use a card from a deck not in play to represent him.

Of course, as with all the official rulings you're free to play as you please and completely ignore everything written above.  But I thought I'd clarify why The Chairman is an exception to the normal villain character card rules.
