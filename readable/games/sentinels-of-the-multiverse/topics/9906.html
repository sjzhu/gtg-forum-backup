Hey Everyone,

We've developed an Android app to let you easily keep track of HP for all Sentinels of the Multiverse targets, and we're ready to make it known to the Sentinels community now that it's stable and fully featured. We intend to keep it updated with each new expansion to Sentinels of the Multiverse, and it currently contains cards for every deck up to Vengeance. It may not be the prettiest app in the world, but it works great, and best of all it's FREE! :)

If you have any comments or questions, we'd love to hear them. You can reply to this topic, or send us an email directly at sotm@nispesoft.com

You can find the app here: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nispesoft.sotmcommander or search for SotM Commander on the Google Play Store.

Up until recently, we weren't aware that Kindle Fire owners couldn't access the Google Play store. We've uploaded it to the Amazon Web Store for all our Kindle owning friends. :) http://www.amazon.com/Nispesoft-LLC-SotM-Commander/dp/B00J6O8L7A/

Hope you enjoy it!

Of course! We didn't think of that when we were entering all the new card data, but we will have an update that corrects that in the near future. Keep your eye on the Play and Amazon Store updates over the next week or so. We'll also post another forum update when it's ready to go. Thanks for all the errata, we try to get as much right as we can, but nobody's perfect. :)

 

As for finding the highest and lowest HP, if you are looking at the main battle screen and flick to the left, you'll see a list of all the cards played, sorted by HP (from lowest to highest). You can use the checkboxes at the top to disable the different types of cards to easily find the highest villain card, or the lowest hero card, or the second-lowest non-environment card, etc. If you uncheck the green H, you'll hide hero cards, if you uncheck the red V, you'll hide the villain cards, and if you uncheck the blue E, you'll hide the environment cards.

You can also edit the HP of targets right on the sorted HP screen. Just press Reorder at the top when you're done changing the values and it'll sort with the new HP values.

 

Thanks again for all your support!
