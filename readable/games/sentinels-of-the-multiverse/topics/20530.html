I'm excited about it, personally, since the changes so far seem to address long-standing issues I've seen people have about the game.

It addresses Adam's long-standing unhappiness with his art, which I've also seen be a sticking point for people not already invested in Sentinels.

It also addresses many issues people have had with balancing in the game, ranging from making characters and cards often perceived as weak stronger, to making overly punishing earlier villains more in line with later villains. I'll be interested to see how they address villains like Miss Information and Spite who are currently heavily disliked by the fanbase or heroes like Expat that are currently often viewed as universally weak.

There's also meta things like repackaging the game into six expansions from the current situation of a multitude of individual products that often have people confusedly asking on Reddit what they need to buy to actually have everything.

Then there's the addition of new content like the new First Appearance variants and the scenarios.

My reasons for being unsure are purely personal logistics (mostly lack of closet space and lack of offline people to play with even before the pandemic), not because I'm not excited by the content, because I am excited.

Something I was thinking about: I know the original intention behind the card game was to be “story Legos,” and that there was an overall story tying things together, but until playing OblivAeon games I never really felt the game lived up to that idea. Sure, The Letters Page was around for some time before the OblivAeon release and gave us many of the major story beats, but that never really shows up on the game table unless you set up a particular Villain-Environment-Heroes game. And even then, it’s still just…a normal game of Sentinels, and it doesn’t tie in to anything else happening in the game. Nor does that game proceed in any special way; it’s still random from the various decks.

So, I think it’s going to be great to have the Events actually tied in to the game, and to have Rewards for the events to keep players invested in them. That was one of the best additions to the game of OblivAeon mode, so it’ll be extremely cool to have it expanded!
