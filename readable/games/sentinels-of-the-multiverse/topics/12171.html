We've reached an interesting point in Sentinels of the Multiverse where nearly every hero in the game has a nemesis they can be paired with. After Villains of the Multiverse (which will give us vengance style nemesis for almost everyone) you have to imagine they're going to go for another small set expansion. Another comfy "Two Heroes/Four Villains/Two Enviornments" deal. But where will they look? Which heroes are left?

Well I'm guessing there will be some Mini Expansion hero(s) alongside the VotM expansion so that will give them something. And they'll have to Heroes in that expansion to pair up. Who else?

The Scholor is (to my knowledge) the last Hero to lack a nemesis deck of anykind. He has a "Nemesis", but it's a minor one in Proletariat's deck. He's probably high on the list.

Bunker and Absolute Zero probably need one too. They have nemeses, but only in the Vengence style. Will Freight Train and Proletariat get individual decks? Or Will we see new Nemesis for our heroes to battle? Time will tell but I'm leaning on them to get new hero decks, maybe in mini Expansions!

Finally Unity is probably due for one. She has a Nemesis in Iron Legacy, but that's sort of alternate Timeline biz and he's also Nemesis to 5 other heroes, I think Unity deserves her own Villain.

Those are my thoughts so far. Is there any Hero I forgot about who is lonely with no name to curse at the heavans? Let me know in the comments. Also, who do you think deserves a Nemesis next? My Vote is on Unity and The Scholar for sure with Bunker and AZ as runner up's or Mini Expansion candidates.
