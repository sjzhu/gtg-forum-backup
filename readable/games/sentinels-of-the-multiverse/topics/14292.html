Simple question: Is our friendly neighborhood puzzle maker/ hero aide actually the hero Parse in the Net?

In the art livestream, Christopher made an interesting comment about Parse along the lines of: "People who are interested in Parse should look at her backstory, then look at her incap art, then look at her back story and incap art again

So in the interest of more story I did just that and here's what I found:

Parse worked on the Omnitron Core Project with RevoCorp after its original iteration had been defeated. With the "cosmic entity" we now know as Oblivaeon affected Omnitron to give us Cosmic Omnitron, Parse was in the same room, and saw the "overflow of information." This overflow also gave Parse enhanced mental capabilities. It also seems that Kim was "updated" in order to accept some sort of code into her system.

The implication (and the explicit statement =P) being that Parse's natural powers were amplified by OblivAeon to get her superpowers.

That's cool enough on its own.

On her incap?  Well it shows Kim's Glowing eyes and glasses, with a blank expression. There sees to be code running across her eyes. Both her eyes and her glasses (which are on top of her head) emit some pale blueish light. The code in Kim's eyes also looks like it is flowing through/from her rather than something that Kim/Parse is staring at. 

So, is the implication that Kim has gone even more digital? Enough that she is now able to become data?

Going one step further. Does this mean that she can now communicate to others through code and data?  If she can, that brings me to my final thought: 

Was Parse Tim Cosing/the Scion?

Parse had a noticeable absence in the ARG comics, being one of 3 heroes not mentioned (the others being the naturalist and guise).

In the second-to-last comic in the ARG, the Scion pretty much owned up to giving the Heroes messages and clues to lead them to the Scion.  

The heroes's describe the voice of the Scion as "not-unfamiliar but unrecognizable" Could this be a way of point us to look at some one we already know as the Scion?

Depending on how the Power-Granting of OblivAeon works, does that mean that OblivAeon took control over Parse at some point becuase of her power? The scion says that it met OblivAeon "outside space and time"; Could this be Parse's subtle nod to being data? Outside normal space and time in the net? In the art that Adam drew of her recently, she also has a distictive circlet on her head, and the scion mentioned being granted a collar in exchange for the power given by Oblivaeon; this could also help tie the two together.

So can our friendly neighborhood Parse be a unwitting villain in OblivAeon? Is this just a way to give her a fancy promo? Who knows? Christopher knows. I guess we all will Feb. 9th
