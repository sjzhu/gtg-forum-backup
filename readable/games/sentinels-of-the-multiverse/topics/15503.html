I noticed during the Foil Hero Collection unboxing video that nearly all the new OblivAeon heroes and variant cards unlocked during the most recent Kickstarter seem to have the exact same art as was revealed during the campaign. However, it was stated a number of times during the campaign that all the new characters and variants would have new art in the Complete Hero Variant Collection and 5th Anniversary Foil Hero Collection. Here are two examples (emphasis mine):

The 5th Anniversary Foil Hero Collection is a box full of over one hundred hero character cards, all in foil and with new art! It will have not just the hero variants, but all of the hero character cards, including those from the core game and each expansion! [KS main page]

Just to clarify: The Complete Hero Foil Collection and the non-foil Variant Collection will have the same, new card art for the variant hero cards, and not the old variant art. The Complete Hero Foil Collection will *also* have foil hero character cards for all of the non-variant heroes with new art. It will not have foil version of the original art for any of those cards. [comment from GtG on update #17]

So is all the Kickstarter art being used as the "new art" for the Variant and Foil Collections? Otherwise, if the art is the same, I believe there's been a bit of a misrepresentation regarding what we would be getting in those collections. Thanks in advance to the GtG crew for any clarification they can provide.

I had issues with GTG explaining all this via language which can be rather ambiguous in its interpretation (unintended, I’m sure) instead of proper graphics. I, too, initially interpreted it the way you did. But now see that there’s an alternate interpretation that is consistent with all the GTG has said as well as what has transpired.

“Old art” refers to the card art during its original publishing. Say, Legacy in the base game. Or the original Newest Legacy.

“New art” refers to card recently done just for the 5th Anniversary Collection (art done for Oblivaeon counts as “old art”). So, the Legacy shown in the recent unboxing video.

“Same, new art” refers to “New art”. So cards in Variant collection and 5th Anniversary collection have the Same art, but these are New art compared to the Old (Original) art.

In the case of Harpy, there will be these arts:
‘Old’ Harpy in Oblivaeon box.
‘New’ Harpy in 5th Anniversary box.

In the case of Dark Watch Harpy:
‘Old’ DW Harpy in Oblivaeon box.
‘New’ DW Harpy in 5th anniversary box.
‘New’ DW Harpy in variant collection box.
