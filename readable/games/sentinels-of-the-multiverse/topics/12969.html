Killswitch:

On paper, it looks fantastic. Visionary brings to the table her TtE on AZ to grant any desired effect at a given time, and protects against the environment with her Mass Levitation. Ra is the heavy hitter, and Haka shuts down the grave, deals massave rounds of damage and nulls it with Ground Pound.I'll definitely have to try this when I get home.

The problem is that Mass Levitation rarely helps against Rook City since Rook City itself rarely damages the players (except for the falling statues and gunmen).  Most of the time, Rook City is just beefing up the villain damage (blighted streets) or making you play more Villain deck cards.  Even for Scum and Villainy, the damage source is the Villain, so Mass Levitation does nothing.  

Twist the Ether on Absolute Zero though is quite nasty.  Or better yet, put it on The Operative and kill her minions with zero guilt as all the damage goes to Absolute Zero and heals him, keeping him as the highest HP hero.

And all the other pieces add up just fine.  AZ, Ra, and Haka have enough damage to control the villain targets.  And once the Chairman flips, Ground Pound can buy you a full turn of free damage against retaliations.
