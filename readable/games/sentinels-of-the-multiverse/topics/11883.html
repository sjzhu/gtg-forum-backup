Deck control is your friend. Being able to control Spite's plays is huge; if you can stop him from playing Forced Entry then any victims you saved are completely safe, and the sooner you get his drugs into play the sooner you can start piling on damage. Beyond that, accelerated card plays are slightly more useful than normal, since fodder for PL531 is very useful. (On a related note, PL531 can make certain one-round cards last turn after turn; Omnitron-X can bounce and play Slip Through Time turn after turn; Bloody Knuckles or Hunter and Hunted can be returned to hand to negate some of the self-harm potential; damage prevention cards like Heroic Interception, Grease Gun, Expect the Worst, etc. can block damage many times, especially if PL531 comes out later than most of Spite's other drugs.)

As for Bunker, I am not particularly great at Bunker, but I can point you towards Pydro's 8000-word opus on playing him: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1_BC9zCpZ0dA2lwMY3yoaOJWR5n2KhNd79y82TeI7mYQ/edit

Spite is many people's (including mine) least favorite villain to play against, not because he's super hard, but because you have to get fairly "gamey" with him.  Essentially, you are just trying to get your stuff out ahead of his card bouncing and accelerate his flip, and just pound on him on his flip side.  This also include ignoring most victims, which feels wrong thematically.  This a very simplified version and there are certainly other ways to beat him, but that is the most consistent.

 

The key with Bunker (and most heroes) is not try to go for an optimal setup, but recognizing he has a lot of cards that can do damage in different ways but often will need cards to fuel them.  Taking advantage of powers like Aux Power Source and Initialize and cards like Ammo Drop is certainly one way to go.  There are a couple of really good guides about Bunker and his damage output potential on the forum, check the Strategy forum

I tend to find the Wraith pretty useful against Spite - Utility Belt and two Stun Bolts basically nerfs him completely (at least when H=3) and if you can get Smoke Bombs out and have the lowest hp then even the Nemesis bonus isn't a problem. Even if you can only nerf his damage output a bit (or get everyone to soak some of it) it helps on his front side, since he only heals as much hp as heroes lose when he attacks them. We have killed him on his front side a few times but it can be tricky. At least once he flips you know what he's gonna do every turn for the rest of the game. As Meerkat has mentioned, the drug that returns cards to players' hands can be very useful - we once let Legacy be the one to return a card to his hand because he had Inspiring Presence out. He then just kept replaying it so we kept getting 1hp back every time it was his turn, which was nice :). But stuff like Bloody Knuckles is good too - take advantage of the damage boost but then return it to your hand before Spite has a chance to hit you for increased damage :). For me, I find the most annoying drug is the "take damage and discard your top five whenever you use a power", though if Spite's been nerfed enough then even this isn't too much of a problem.

As for Bunker, I don't really play him very often but my partner uses him sometimes. He tends to do the "Turret Mode and at least one gun" set-up quite often. I can recall playing him as a tank with the Heavy Plating and Recharge Mode whilst charging up the Omnicannon (with the extra draws from Recharge Mode). But as has been mentioned, it's probably best to just look at what you've got and see how to achieve the best outcome you can with that, rather than spending the whole game waiting on a card (or card combo) which ends up not appearing. Mind you, that's probably true of any hero.

Bunker, like most other heroes, needs to be "played from the hand, not from the ideal", as has already been said.  The problem for Bunker in particular is that his deck looks like it should be played Recharge Mode for a few turns, then Upgrade Mode for a few turns, and then Turret Mode for the rest of the game.  Which is, in fact, super slow, especially because you're never going to draw the cards in the correct order and ongoing and equiptment destruction can set you way back.  It doesn't help that his base power, which draws him a card, will almost certainly be displaced as your default power almost immediately by Flack Cannon or Grenade Launcher.  So a full setup is going to be difficult to achieve.  But you don't need the "full setup" for Bunker to be extremely effective as a damage dealer or a tank.
