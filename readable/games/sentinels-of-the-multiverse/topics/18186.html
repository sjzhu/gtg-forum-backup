I will often times let the environment eat Matriach's birdies if there is an AoE damage card ala Fire in the Biosphere. Just let it sit out and focus fire on her.

Final Wasteland is a good place for the Chairman as a lot of the stuff hits everything or second lowest hp. Combined with Unforgiving Wasteland, you can whittle down minions pretty quickly.

Several times I have been saved from defeat by Rook City, of all places. Either Toxic Sludge plinks away at the boss while leaving my low hp heroes alone, or the occasional Falling Statuary will KO something important.

Damage boosts from the environment (Explosives Wagon, Obsidian Field) can also be really useful depending on the villain.

There have been several times where the villains target the highest non-villain and the environment targets the highest non-environment and I just sit there with my heroes at 3 hp and let them beat on each other :D
