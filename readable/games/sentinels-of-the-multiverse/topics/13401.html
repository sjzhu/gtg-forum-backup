The promos have been giving out as part of their Kickstarter and pre-orders (with a couple being given out in different ways). Right now, the only way to get "official ones" is to get them at a convention, and even then, you won't be able to get them all.

The cards themselves simply replace the hero card used in each deck. Since the cards are not actually in the deck, you don't need them to be actual cards. GtG has made the content of the cards available. So you can either just write down the info, or print out your own cards. 

You can find the abilities under each hero in the SotM wiki and on BGG.

Once the last expansion comes out, GtG will sell promo packs that will include all the promos.
