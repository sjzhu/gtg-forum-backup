That works, just the opposite way, and obviously requires an empty deck and a second card play.

Play quick insight, draw three cards, discard 2.  Play 3 fleet of foot's, drawing the 2 cards you discarded and one more.  With 4 cards in the trash you draw a card, leaving you with no trash and a deck of three cards.

Play three fleet of foot and 1 more card.  Play quick insight.  Play three fleet of foot and nimble strike.  you now have four in your deck/trash.  Draw 2, one of them is guaranteed to be fleet of foot.
