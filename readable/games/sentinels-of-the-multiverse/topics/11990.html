It's tricky.  Destruction effects happen again.  BUT - cards under Savage Mana have no text (except for which deck they came from).  So destruction effects on the cards themselves don't exist, and many other destruction effects (such as CC's construct destruction effects) won't recognize that they are the proper card type.  Only cards that trigger when "a card is destroyed" or "a ___ card is destroyed" where that blank is Hero, Villain, or Environment will trigger.

 

Check the Savage Mana section.
