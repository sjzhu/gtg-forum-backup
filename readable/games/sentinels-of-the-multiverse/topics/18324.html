At first glance, I was really disappointed with the foil version of Completionist Guise, which did not appear to have alternate art. However, if you look closer, there are two major differences: He's bagging a different comic book on the front (Justice Comics vs. Prime Wardens), and threre's a different quote on the back ("These are from an extremely limited print run." vs. "I'm going to need like a million dollars for these."). Clever.

HOWEVER, if you really want to be a competionist, there's actually a THIRD version of this card in the Complete Hero Variant Collection. This version has him bagging a copy of Freedom Five, and it has probably my favorite quote of the three ("Some of these have minor variations in the covers that make them really valuable!"). As much as I like this third version, I'm not sure if it's worth opening up that otherwise duplicate set just to pull out a single card.

If anyone from GTG is lurking, any insight into the decision to put an exclusive version of this card in the non-foil variant collection only?
