Glamour:

The first time a Nemesis card would be dealt damage by a Hero target each turn, redirect that damage to the Hero target with the highest HP.

 

Backlash Generator:

The first time any Device is dealt damage by a non-Hero target each turn, this card deals the source of that damage 1 Lightning Damage.

 

So, I guess the difference is “any” instead of “a”.

The word “any” was used to try to make it clearer to players how this was supposed to work because the word “a” has been used very inconsistently in the past in Sentinels. Provockitor Tarnis in Kaagra’s deck and wrong time and place in Setback’s have virtually identical wording but work differently based on different definitions of the word “a”.
