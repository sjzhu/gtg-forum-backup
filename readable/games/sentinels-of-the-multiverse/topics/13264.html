Just got done with this one (took a while), was unlocking Mad Bomber Blade, so I set up a team I could control the game with, Greatest Legacy, Tempest, Bunker, Wraith, and Visionary in Wagner. A few turns worth of set up and Blood Sweat and Tears hit play, So I decide to knock them out with Grenade Launcher, effectively getting the Hero Achievement: Sundown, Dawn flips, fourth Citizen is Anvil, who I decide to knock out with Motivational Charge, effectively getting the Lead the Charge hero achievement, then, just gor giggles, I control Dawn's deck so Hammer comes out next while a Meteor storm is in play, which I use that time to spam Downpour and get two Twist the Ethers on him, fetching Controlled Burn hero achievement. The last round, Hammer goes down, I could easily end it (dawn's at 4 with Bunker, Wraith, and Visionary looking at her) Instead, I realize that Visionary is only down 1 HP (everyone else is max) so I adhesive foam grenade, Throat Jab, and Infrared Eyepiece to let Dare come out next. 

 

Long story short, three hero achievements, an unlock, full health. (and really, if I hadn't already unlocked Rook City Wraith and Bunker, it'd been three unlocks)

 

And no, I'm not going to try that again on Rook City in Advanced. It was a long enough game as it was.
