The rules for a character being incapacitated already state that an all of an incapacitated characters cards except their character card/s are removed from the game ((IIRC the sole exception to this is Captain Cosmic Requital who places all his constructs under his character card), and IIRC (can't actually check since the rulebook has been deleted from dropbox), the Oblivaeon rules state that a player who's hero is incapacitated can bring in another hero from outside the game who hasn't been played and doesn't have a variant already in play.

Yeah, when a hero is incapped, their cards go to the same “removed from the game” pile as any other cards from their deck that were removed from the game. At that point there’s no distinction about how they were removed from the game.
