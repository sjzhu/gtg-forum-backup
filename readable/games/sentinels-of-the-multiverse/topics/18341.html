What are the best choices you can make when a "Representative of Earth"  is called out?  Obviously, you'd want to pick a card with a power that one of the other heroes might be able to use. The choice is obviously situational, but the ones I've been focusing on are:

Top Choices:

Captain Cosmic (Requital): Play top 3 cards of a hero's deck, no worry about a construct penalty

Sky-Scraper (Base, Huge): 2 sonic damage to each non-hero (If hero damage due to boosting, then Base Tempest instead)

Tier 2:

Legacy (Base): +1 damge for a round

Grandpa Legacy: Heal 1 hp + use a power of choice

Team Leader Tachyon: Everybody gets a free card (sometimes super helpful, other times unimportant)

 

Are there other good options I've missed? I haven't gone through all of the Oblivaeon stuff yet, so I'm not sure if there are other useful choices in there.

I use F5 Bunker and F5 AZ frequently.  Reveal the top card of each deck and pick one to play, or deal a little bit of self-damage to dig through your deck and pull out any Ongoing you want.  Sometimes XPW AA--a bit of damage for another hero's power use and a play is quite nice.

A lot depends on whether you have heroes that can trigger another hero's power, though.  If you have Grandpa Legacy, using him on Requital CC is pointless (he has no deck), but using him on F5 Bunker is even better.
