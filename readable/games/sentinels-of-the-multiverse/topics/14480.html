I've used them before. Honestly, you get what you pay for. They aren't high quality, but they are dirt cheap and if your goal is to sleeve every Sentinels card then I would recommend them. My biggest problem with them is it can be hard to shuffle large decks, as they are quite slippery. But with the 40 card hero decks this isn't too big of an issue.
