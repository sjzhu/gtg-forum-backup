For any high-end traditional villain (not the Vengeful Five-style villains), my first two picks are Team Leader Tachyon and Dark Visionary. After that, it depends on the villain, but Legacy or Greatest Legacy are usually my third pick, followed by Tempest, Wraith, or Haka as the situation calls for it. Try and get him down to that 21 HP and keep him there until you're ready to finish him- between Fanatic and Team Leader Tachyon, you should be able to take him pretty easily if you managed to get any Light Speed Barrages in the turns building up to the final punch.

For Kaargra, you really want to bring along along a good tank, like Legacy or the Naturalist. Scoring 20 Favour and beating her up isn't the hard part. Preventing her from getting 20 Favour is.

Chrono Ranger is ridiculous against The Chairman (well, specifically against The Operative). Against Iron Legacy, it's hard to go wrong with Fanatic and Absolute Zero. I imagine Skyscraper can do pretty well too, though I haven't tried it.

seesome:

So, what are your recommendations for the best hero teams to use against Iron Legacy, The Matriarch, The Chairman, or Kaargra Warfang?

Iron Legacy…ugh. Your first priority is Ongoing destruction, and your second priority is damage mitigation. The Naturalist, Tachyon, and the Wraith have good options for both. Chrono-Ranger does too, but his ping can make him a liability while you wait to get out enough Bounty cards to matter. Try to pick an easy-going environment, like the Mobile Defense Platform.

The Matriarch, I’ve discovered, is absolute easy mode with H=3 and Unity. Do whatever it takes to get Stealth-Bot into play and just sit there laughing. Legacy is a good back-up, but it’s hard to get his combination of “Lead from the Front” + “Fortitude” and/or “Next Evolution” into play, so don’t rely on it. Anyone else who can get armor is great.

The Chairman doesn’t tend to give me much trouble. Despite their low HP and how much damage can be put out quite quickly, I’ve found the Sentinels, with their basically unparalleled damage output, can completely ruin the Chairman’s day. Major damage output is your primary concern, so consider Haka, KNYFE, Ra, and Tempest; Haka in particular is a rotten cheater with his “Savage Mana.” I generally try to have either Parse or the Visionary in my team to control when those bloody “Prison Break” cards come out, preferring Parse because she has more options to just discard them. I also recommend ignoring the Operative as much as possible until the Chairman himself is dead. You don’t need her flipping and giving him a damage boost.

If you’re still having trouble with the Chairman, consider replacing one of your damage-dealers with Legacy. “Lead from the Front” + “Next Evolution” makes me feel kind of embarrassed to win, like I turned on a godmode cheat.

Kaargra Bloodfang…ugh. She gives me crap and I haven’t figured out a reliable strategy. You need lots of damage, so Haka and KNYFE are good to have. Never hurts to have Tachyon around to turn off enemy damage for for a round or two. I’ve had good luck with Mister Fixer, though it should be noted I play with the unofficial rule that his damage gets bumped by one in the same round he plays a Style or Tool card; but even without that rule, “Jack Handle” + “Grease Monkey Style” or “Driving Mantis Style” can be quite useful.

Ideally you build a team to handle the specific villian.  But that's more specific than OP originally asked.

If you just want an all around best team I feel like there are two schools of thought:

 

1.  The ramp up and win team

Combines a lot of card draw, play cards effect and using additional powers to just build up an overwhelming advantage.  

The best for these are   Team Leader or super Science Tach, Omni X, Kvothe or prime warden Argent,  Greatest legacy, and Prime warden Tempest for enablers

The best heroes to target with ramp I feel are Legacy, Tempest, Sentinels, and Wraith.

 

2.  The silver bullet team

A flexible team that can shut down a lot of decks using specific cards/powers for example  visionary can brain burn to shut down voss or chairman or levitate to shut down all the environment damage.

strong cards for this are Dark or reg Visionary,  All the legacies, Wraith, Scholar, Parse, Engine of War bunker, Haka's savage mana

 

The key is coming up with the best version of each strategy and then deciding which one would have better wining percentages in the long run.  I don't think I've played enough to really say myself.

1) Iron Legacy

This fight is over in 4 rounds, win or lose, so no slow-build characters. That means Argent Adept and Absolute Zero are poor selections. Ongoing destruction is absolutely necessary, and ideally all 5 decks have some. IL will do lots of damage, so Omnitron-X can't maintain Components and is a poor choice, unless you pray for a melee damage reduction to open with. Damage Redirection is a great choice. You need to overcome damage reduction, so Legacy is a good choice to help everyone else. Characters with big One-shots (Ra, Tempest) do well. Damage Reduction and Healing work, which spells Nightmist, but I have not tried her on him.

2) The Matriarch

Damage reduction works nicely. This deck does a lot of 1 and 2 hits. Bunker, Legacy, Tempest are great. Wraith is great for manipulating the villain deck and damage reduction on Matriarch. Visionary is great for TTE on Matriarch. Nightmist is a survivor in this fight, but doesn't do much damage. Sudden Mana (Haka) Huginn and Muninn, Domains, and the Mask, but no more than that. Too many fowl under SM means Matriarch plays Ongoing destruction too fast to deal with, then SM is gone. Legacy for NE and LFTF are a definite plus. Tachyon is actually a very good choice, despite Nemesis penalty.

3) The Chairman

Haka to Sudden Mana all of the Thugs, then the Underbosses after Chairman flips. Legacy for LFTF and NE. Again, lots of small damage from this deck, so damage reduction is highly desired. Ongoing destruction doe snot come from one-shots, so SM is safe.

4) Kaargra Warfang

I have no standard for beating this deck, yet, but my current philosophy is to concentrate on generating favor fast on damage instead of destruction of targets or grabbing Titles. Wraith is self-supporting and can pop out 2 favor per round by Turn 2 often, or can churn away Fickle fans to protect favor. Haka is self-supporting, and can rampage a lot of favor. Legacy boosts a lot of 3 damage single target powers to 4 (Bunker, Ra, Naturalist, Fanatic), and 1 damage multi-target powers to 2 (Tempest), to help others produce favor. With good card draw, you can win Turn 3, but you want to go for reliable favor. If you take Haka, then cherry pick the villains that generate a lot of favor for Sudden Mana, but don't take all gladiators or you'll see Fickle fans spoiling the game.
