When I join a forum, I try to generate conversation, so here we are.  I'll go through deck by deck with some random thoughts on each.

1. Captain Cosmic  - I love him.  He's a phenomenal swiss army knife character, keeping your guys standing and dropping opponents.  He MUST be paired with ongoing/event destruction, but with that given, his extra card plays and passive abilities turn an average team into something dominant.  Augmented Ally is absurd with Team Leader Tachyon.  Cosmic Cannon isn't my favorite unless someone has extra power uses.  Most cards have powers for specific reasons.  I do like it with Setback and a few others, though.  Dynamic Syphon is just nuts.  Even at only an extra power or two before it's destroyed, this can win a game.  Energy Bracer may be my favorite of all these cards.  Adding it to Scholar, Stealth Bot, etc... sheez.  I don't love the cards he uses to damage, but, well, he supports enough for it to not matter.

2. Deadline - I adore his flavor, but I don't love his deck itself. He seems like a big damage dealer with healing who will take forever to beat. It feels like enough ongoing hate will make him a bit easy.  The enviornment manipulation seems fun, but in a long game, the wrong enviornment will make this torture. This needs testing to see how to max challenge and fun with enviornments.  I hope I have that much patience.

3. Infiltrator - He feels a bit like Voss with more consistent oomph.  He isn't terribly difficult, unless you get particularly bad luck with Crushing Cage.  Still, LOVE the Beta Ray Bill thing.

4. Enclave of the Englings - love the Kirby theme. Immutus (the Celestial lookalike) is deck defining.  This should never be played with the Dreamer.  Still, it will be wildly fun, especially with villains that have a few targets to give the idea of a wild battle royal.  Tempest should ROCK this deck.

5. Sky Skraper - I want her Small at like all times besides Thorantian Monolith.  Those links are crazy control. Rebounding Debalitator is phenomenal, especially against Iron Legacy. Micro-Assembler really helps characters like Unity, Nightmist, Wraith, Chrono Ranger and Legacy, but almost everyone has at least one equipment he or she really wants... and it puts the card into play! Cortex Hyperstimulator in multiples is ridiculous, as is aggression modulator with any enviornment heavy in targets.  Finally, grab them all with FOUR Linking Incursion?  This is a wildly dynamic play experience and one of my favorite characters.

6. Dok'Thorath Capital - This is just cool.  It can help or hurt you and the idea of rebels and civilians on a foreign planet?  This is going to see a lot of play in my house.  It isn't new with most of the text, but the flavor works.

7. Bloodsworn Colosseum - So, who is each person, here?  Champion is Tempest, Chaos Lord is Tachyon, Death Caller is Kvothe (shut up, I love that card), Reckless is Knyfe, Living Weapn is Chrono Ranger, Seeker is Little Dawn, Unbreakable is Haka, the Mindbreaker is Scholar... but who is the Indiscriminate? Stonejaw?

8. Progeny - T-2000 Amazo with cosmic power?  Yes please.  Scoin cards are going to be a bastard.  This is going to require big damage once a few are in play, so alpha strikes and set up is a must.  Of course, he can destroy your stuff, so it's a balance. Ongoing hate with Alpha Strikes.. and even then it may not be enough.  Seems like Tachyon and a damage sponge would go a long way... probably Super Scientific, which works for me as it's one of my favorite cards, but Scholar goes an awful lot better with Team Leader.

9. Kaagara Warfarng - perhaps my new favorite villain.  I love the alternate playstyle and how challenging it is to find creative ways to mess with her deck, her gladiators, and the titles.  The win condition is fairly brilliant and can be accomplished numerous ways.  I cannot say enough about how cool I find this character and how much fun I'm going to have trying to get the right titles on the right people and find different ways to attain victory points/avoid flipping her.  A+ design.

That's all for me, for now. Any thoughts and replies appreciated.
