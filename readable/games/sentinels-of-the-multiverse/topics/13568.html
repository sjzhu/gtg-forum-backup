livingintruefreedom:

But issues is the same regardless of hero team size. Those 3 cards in that order prevents winning because no winning condition can be meet other then losing.

But it can be. It's different if you have 3/5 Heroes than 4, but basically your goal is to modify the damage dealt by The New Deal so that at the end you all have even HP instead of having it modified to odd HP (basically change the 3 damage to an even number damage). It also depends on which one is checked first, if Losing to the Odds or The New Deal. Let's say that The New Deal is checked first (which is harder than the other way around):

Wraith: Stun Bolt on yourself and/or other Heroes can make them deal themselves 2 psychic damage instead of 3. Combat stance with increased/reduced damage.
Legacy: increase all damage dealt by heroes so they all deal themselves 4 psychic damage.
Tempest: Otherwordly Resilence, Localized Hurricane, Elemental Subwarve Inducer (same reasoning)
Visionary: Twist the Ether, Cocoon
Ra: Imbued Fire+Flesh of the Sun God
Fanatic: Divine Sacrifice

Fixer: Crowbars, Jack handle, Monkey Fist, Driving Mantis+Pipe Wrench, Hoist Chain, etc.
Expat: Flak Jacket

Argent Adept: give +1 or -1 damage dealt/taken

Setback: Uncharmed Life, Wrong Time and Place, Silver Lining (a bit of an extreme case)
Naturalist: Croc or Rhino form do the trick.

Captain Cosmic: Energy bracer, some weird combo with Autonomous blade+Siphon+a Power Use
SkyScrapper: Thortharian Monolith for 4 Heroes, Cortex Hyperstimulator,

Scholar: For him: Double Flesh to Iron/Expect the Worst. For the whole team: Alchemical Redirection, Offensive Transmutation.

 

I'm sure there are others. Basically there are ways around each even HP guy dealing themselves 3 damage and end again with even HP and winning. The goal there is just to play stuff on the heroes instead of dealing with the Villain. And getting lucky with the Environment!
