I decided to see if I could test something similar, so I looked through powers for one that mentions "that target" after doing damage (sort of similar to Xtreme CC) and found Adamant Mainstay's Haymaker was suitable (The next damage dealt to that target is irreducible). So, when Guise borrows Jack Handle and uses that power, the temporary Irreducibility gets applied to... none of the targets. Either this sort of thing hasn't been thought of, or jack handle removes these riders intentionally. I'll put an ask in through the handelabra contact page.

Further thought and flicking through the fireside chats has brought me to the ruling for Look What I Found with Jack Handle. Specifically the "if that target is still in play" clause is ruled something more like "if any of those targets are still in play" (so it only applies once, but doesn't care which targets survive the jack handling so long as any of them survive). So I'd guess that Captain Cosmics power (used by Guise with Mr.Fixers Jack Handle) would change to something like "Guise deals each target 1 energy damage. Each Construct deals any of those targets 1 energy damage." That doesn't sit right with me, as the grammer doesn't quite work and it's unclear if it's conditional for each (which it shouldn't be as it wasn't before the change). However, if you add in "one" as in "if any one of those targets" then it reads, "Each construct deals any one of those targets..." which grammatically makes sense, and fits with the previous ruling for Look What I Found and provides the rule of thumb to replace "that target" with "any one of those targets" when dealing with Jack Handle.

Deeper thinking has led me to "Kill the Spirit" which you'd expect to redirect all the 1 radiants to the first thing you (try to) hit. Though I'd have to ask, when does the redirect effect happen? As you do the damage (like hypersonic assault)? Or after you've finished dealing damage (like Jack Handle and Look What I Found)?
