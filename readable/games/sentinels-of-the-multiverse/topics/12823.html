I believe it still does. I know I needed a prop for a tabletop RPG and decided to build it in Lego bricks, and this let me create and order exactly what I needed. My mind was blown...

Yep. I used that service to make model satellites for my desk at work. They make great references!

It used to be that the LDD was connected right up to a "buy" button, and you could just order whatever you designed and have it shipped right to you. That feature was discontinued, but you can use LDD to make the design and parts list, and then go to the Lego store Pick-a-Brick area to order all the parts. The downside is that Pick-a-Brick has a much smaller selection of pieces than the LDD allows you to use. So if you are serious about putting something today that you can buy, you need to have both LDD and the Pick-a-Brick page open, and be careful to cross-reference the two.  For my most recent model, I did exactly that.
