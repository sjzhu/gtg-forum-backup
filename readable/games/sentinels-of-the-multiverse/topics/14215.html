The story so far… This is my attempt at a summary. Rather than following the chronological order of discovery, I want to follow what the intent was, with the aim of discovering what the principles behind the puzzles are.

Comics found so far
(These appear to be the goals of this hunt.)

  1. http://greaterthangames.com/genocidal
  2. http://greaterthangames.com/982354617 (image name: 716453289, reversed)
  3. http://greaterthangames.com/harbinger
  4. http://greaterthangames.com/421234220
  5. http://greaterthangames.com/oddallies
  6. http://greaterthangames.com/100263350
  7. http://greaterthangames.com/omnitronu ; straight version of cover at https://pbs.twimg.com/media/CZv_LdZWAAAu35F.jpg:large

They appear to alternate between comics where the clues are a word, offering a cover and a summary of the story, and comics where the clue is a number, giving a snippet of the action.

Other key pages

  • http://greaterthangames.com/replaceme : Apparently a replacement cards page, but not the standard URL.
  • http://greaterthangames.com/itscoming : first clue page
  • http://i.imgur.com/G2seRZ7.jpg : the “intervene” image obtained by playing three Mega Computers in Megalopolis
  • http://greaterthangames.com/xbtufmboe : gif sequence leading to Omnitron-U. Exploded GIF at Dropbox - xbtufmboe - Simplify your life (wyken09)

Getting to Genocidal

  • Tim Cosing appears many times in the playtester list (CornCakes, presumably GtG staff)
  • Tim Cosing has a twitter account with a QR code leading to https://sentinelsofthemultiverse.greaterthangames.com/replaceme (ComradeBubbles, Powerhound 2000)
  • Same QR code appears in the Villains manual (bessyrocks12)
  • Tim Cosing is an anagram of “It’s Coming” (Matchstickman)
  • https://sentinelsofthemultiverse.greaterthangames.com/itscoming or https://greaterthangames.com/itscoming leads to a page with some strange text, repeated nine times (dpt)
  • https://greaterthangames.com/begining leads to the same place (Reverend Pete)
  • The strange text comes from the flavor text of the cards Gruum, Endless Possibilities, Negative Energy Field, Oblivion, Chronological Hotspot, Indomitable Force, Demoralization, Apocalypse, Lambent Reaper (arenson9, Tindiyen, RySmith6, VisforYoshi, grysqrl)
  • The initial letters of these cards spell out “genocidal” (Chogb001)

Genocidal to 982354617

  • The barcode on the “genocidal” comic reads “982354617” in text. (The barcode itself appears to be deformed to me.) (DocBrown)

982354617 to harbinger

  • The 982354617 comic clues us to play three Mega Computers in Megalopolis, which we had actually already done by accident.
  • Playing three Mega Computers in Megalopolis gives us an “intervene” page, also linking to the “replaceme” page: http://i.imgur.com/G2seRZ7.jpg (Kratos13)
  • The screens shown on the intervene page come from specific cards: Scion of Blight, Diplomatic Envoy, Beginning of the End, Eye on the Prize, Retcon, Between the Lines, Don’t Dismiss Anything, Fixed Point, Reveal the Flaws (DocBrown, Donner, VisforYoshi, Chogb001, Julia, mackcrockett)
  • Bunker is eating Flavor Chips in the 982354617 comic. Flavor text of the nine cards is relevant. (dpt, Julia)
  • The pages in the Villains rulebook have different colors, giving a color → number code: yellow=1, blue=2, pink=3, orange=4, red=5, brown=6, grey=7, white=8, green=9 (arenson9)
  • The screens are colored on the “intervene” page, (bluedarky)
  • Reading off these number positions from the flavor text gives “formofthe”, which completes to “harbinger” (mackcrockett)

Harbinger to 421234220

  • The initial letters in the first paragraph of the Harbinger comic spell out “base eight” (VisforYoshi)
  • The printed number on the Harbinger comic read 716453289, but the bar code is 71645328, missing the 9 (DerpTheBugbear)
  • Converting 71645328 to octal gives 421234220 (brettwo, DerpTheBugbear, kitmehsu)

421234220 to oddallies

  • The 421234220 comic references the previously posted Freedom Four Annual #1, which appears to now have 9 different modified versions at http://greaterthangames.com/downloads . Apparent original can be read page-by-page at http://sentinelsofthemultiverse.com/comic . (DerpTheBugbear, BlueHairedMeerkat, brettwo)
  • The differences are Tachyon’s bedside clock and a letter appearing behind Legacy (brettwo, dpt)
  • The bedside clock reads OLSDALEDI (brettwo, others)
  • The letters behind legacy read RAILFENCE (dpt)
  • There is a railfence cypher (BlueHairedMeerkat)
  • Decoding OLSDALEDI by a 3-row railfence cypher yields “ODDALLIES” (brettwo)

Oddallies to 100263350

  • The first letters of the first paragraph of the Oddallies comic read “Leet Speak” (jagarciao)
  • The actual barcode on the comic reads 804RD64M3, leet speak for Boardgame (DerpTheBugbear)
  • The last letters of the first paragraph of Oddallies read “Health pts”. (eric.reithel)
  • In the HTML comments for the “replaceme” page, the string “One Two Six Nine Four Nine” appears. 126949 is the BGG ID of Omnitron II. (kitmehsu)
  • In the HTML comments for the “itscoming” page, the number 124185 appears. This is the BGG ID of Unity. (arenson9, …)
  • In the HTML comments for the “421234220”, the number 134393 appears. This is the BGG ID for Eternal Haka.
  • The text below reads 633000/126951. 633000 is leet speak for Geek, and Ambuscade has ID number 126951 on BoardGameGeek.com (tyches_coin, BlueHairedMeerkat)
  • Omnitron II, Unity, Eternal Haka, and Ambuscade have 100, 26, 33, and 50 health points respectively. Concatenating them yields 100263350. (BlueHairedMeerkat, kitmehsu)

100263350 to omnitronu

  • Hovering over the 100263350 image gives “uif” (ComradeBubbles)
  • Augustus’ code replaces each letter by the next. Decoding “uif” this way gives “the” (Chromium Man)
  • Omnitron X is speaking in code of Unity’s bots: Turret, Cryo, Champion, Raptor, Champion, Bee / Turret, Turret, Platform, Bee / Bee, Cryo, Bee (BlueHairdMeerkat, kitmehsu)
  • Hit points on these bots are 978281/9931/171 (MrLeRobot)
  • 9782819931171 is the ISBN-13 for The Waste Land, by T.S. Eliot, also seen being held by the Eternal Haka promo (Chromium Man)
  • Encoding “wasteland” by the August Code" gives xbtufmboe. http://greaterthangames.com/xbtufmboe has an animated GIF (Chromium Man)
  • The gif has 25 images, all card art, most (all?) related to Omnitron in alphabetical order. The missing letter is “u”. Images split up at Dropbox - xbtufmboe - Simplify your life (RySmith6, wyken09, grysqrl)

Omnitron-U to ??

  • There is a bar code, which after some clarification decodes to “bitly1QupVMD”. http://bit.ly/1QupVMD goes to the Wikipedia page on Hexadecimal. (Julia, Comrade Bubbles, plus other attempts)
  • Below the bar code is written =232D2D2D.
  • 232D2D2D in decimal is 590163245. (hynju)
  • 232D2D2D in ASCII gives #— . (Trajector, Foote)
  • There is also a grey image that looks like it might be Morse code there. (grysqrl)

Outstanding mysteries

  • Does the location of “Tim Cosing” in the playtester list mean anything? “Oddallies” is not represented, but he does appear where “genocidal” and “harbinger” would appear.
  • CornCakes said that Tim Cosing appears 20 times in the rule book. We have identified 9 occurences.
  • In the villains rulebook on p. 2, letters spelling out “nines” have shadows. Nine items have appeared many times, but is there some other meaning? (Tindiyen)
  • When some people post the hunted for URLs, they get mysterious strings of numbers afterwards. Do these mean anything? (My guess is no.)
  • The number on the Self-Destruct Sequence in Wagner Mars Base is weird. Does it mean anything?
  • The colors of the Villains page numbers match the backgrounds of 9 out of the 10 villain bios in Villains. (Yak Guardian)
  • The colors in the text “intervene” give the number 679142171 by the color → number code. (Donner, MrLeRobot)
  • The Villains rulebook has a GtG die with a 4-pip face rather than a 5-pip face (RySmith6)
  • If you rearrange “Tim Cosing” according to the “716453289” code, you get “it’s coming”. Was this some alternate entry point? If so, I don’t quite see how. (jorcbrow)
  • Tim Cosing posted a video with multiple-level encoded Morse code, leading to the number 918273645 (Estelindis)
  • We very quickly went from the 25 images at http://greaterthangames.com/xbtufmboe to Omnitron-U. Do the specific cards chosen there have significance for later?

Apparent red herrings

  • The QR codes go to a strange URL, sentinelsofthemultiverse.greaterthangames.com, but you can drop the “sentinelsofthemultiverse” part and get to the same places.
  • The other playtesters in the Villains rulebook are on the official list posted in the playtesting forums (bessyrocks12)
  • The “lorem ipsum” text at the end of the modified Freedom Four Annual #1 appears to be meaningless, like most Lorem Ipsum text. But why is it there? (Estelindis)
  • The Ambuscade bio on the web site and the bio as posted at BGG differ in various ways. (bullfroggy95)
  • Sending the TimCosing user on the GtG forums does not yield anything useful. (BlueHairedMeerkat)
  • The TimCosing account on the GtG formus has the e-mail address “TimCosing@secrets.shh” (?)
  • On the 100263350 page, “Argh” and “no” are bolded. Encrypting them with Augustus’ code gives “BSHIOP”, an anagram of “BISHOP”. (cmschex)

Not a summary of the clues, but rather a summary of the story... At least my interpretation of how things went (there are a few gray areas due to story pages not exactly matching up with certain card art).  And who knows, maybe there will be some significance in knowing how things went. If a puzzle ever somehow requires knowing the plot points, this may be useful anyways:

 

THE PROGENITOR'S SCION AWAITS

Progeny Burrows out from deep within the earth in Algeria. He is found standing motionless, waiting for something.

Prime Wardens approach Progeny, and he attacks them.


A HORROR AWAKENS

[Prime Wardens Vs. Progeny]

Progeny eludes the Wardens, or simply gets bored with them, and burrows into the earth.  He resurfaces near Rook City.

Dark Watch and The Scholar, and possibly a reformed Matriarch go out to Progeny to stop him from entering the city.



THE DESTRUCTION OF ROOK CITY

[Dark Watch + The Scholar (+ The Harpy?) Vs. Progeny]

Progeny defeats and/or pushes back the Dark Watch, and begins destroying Rook City. Nightmist expends her powers and goes into mistform.

The Freedom Five arrive with the aid of The Sentinels and Dark Visionary.

[Freedom Five + The Sentinels + Dark Visionary (+ any Dark Watch members who aren't incapacitated?) Vs. Progeny]

Progeny defeats Legacy. Dark Visionary retreats.  Progeny continues destroying the city while battling the remaining heroes.

Chrono-Ranger arrives via a spacetime portal with Parse and KNYFE.

[Chrono-Ranger + Parse + KNYFE (+ any previous heroes still capable of fighting?) Vs. Progeny]

Progeny, seemingly satisfied with the amount of destruction caused, moves on from Rook City.  He starts making his way towards Megalopolis.



AN UNYIELDING MENACE

Progeny continues making his way towards Megalopolis, Fighting many different heroes along the way.
(card art shows at least a few battles with various combinations of F5, PW, DW, and unaffiliated heroes working together against Progeny)
[mix of PW heroes + Matriarch/Harpy (possibly other DW members?) + Parse Vs. Progeny] (Reveal the Flaws)
[mix of PW heroes including Tempest + mix of F5 heroes including Tachyon Vs. Progeny] (Time of Tribulations)


Progeny defeats most of the heroes, and eventually arrives at Megalopolis.
