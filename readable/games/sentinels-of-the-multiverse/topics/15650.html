Playing in the Bloodsworn Colosseum the game started with "Stonejaw" as the first title in an (H)=3 game.

By the end of the second round Stealth Bot entered play, taking Stoneskin (did some work to set that one up).

Then things got interesting.

Taking the first hit from target Kaargra, one from Dymkharn and a hit from the Kraken gave Stealth Bot "The Mindbreaker".

Mind Breaker quickly earned Stealth Bot "The Indiscriminate", which at 2 damage earned her "Champion".

A tie for Fickle Fans (2+2=4) earned Stealth Bot "Reckless", and some timely healing form that, and other things would earn it "The Unbreakable".

This was all aided by [Redacted] (a not released hero that can do some healing) focusing on keeping our Golem friend alive.

So we ended up with Stealth Bot earning 6 titles, leading the team in damage and nearly taking out Unity on more than one occasion.  Fortunately we were able to keep "The Seeker" off of Stealth Bot or we would have lost horribly to our berserk little friend.
