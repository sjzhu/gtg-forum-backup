In the 1000 games thread I started playing through every villain on advanced with the Freedom Five.  I decided to use whichever environment I felt was most thematic (and use each environment at least once) and play each battle as many times as it took until I won.  When I finished I decided to go back and repeat this process with the Prime Wardens and Dark Watch teams and make it a competition of sorts. Here are the "rules" I decided on:

1. Each hero team will battle every villain (on advanced) currently in the video game version until they either win or lose 10 times.

2. Environments will be selected randomly but once selected the environment will not change for a particular villain. To keep it fresh if I randomize the same environment for 2 consecutive villains I will re-randomize.

3. No variants for heroes but the variant villains must be fought as well.

4. Turn order of the heroes will be fixed for all battles:

a. Freedom Five: Legacy, Bunker, Wraith, Tachyon, Absolute Zero

b. Prime Wardens: Argent Adept, Captain Cosmic, Tempest, Haka, Fanatic

c. Dark Watch: Expatriette, NightMist, Setback, Mr. Fixer

5. When I finish I'll tabulate total losses for each team fewest losses wins. I'll also tabulate how many wins each villain  and environment had.

 

(Sidenote: Season 2 preview pack came out when I was midway through the Prime Warden battles, so I went back and battled Chokepoint with the Freedom Five when I got up to facing her with the Prime Wardens.)

 

I'm going to post the results of the rest of the games I play for this purpose here.  If anyone else wants to take on this challenge feel free to post your games here as well and I'll add your results to the tally.  And now... back to saving the multiverse!
