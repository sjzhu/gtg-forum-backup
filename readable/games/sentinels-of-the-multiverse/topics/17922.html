I’ve played this game, Sentenals of the Multivers, and I must say that I am NOT impressed.  Here are some changes that could be made:

- The Wraith should have a cat.

- I get the Legacies confused.  Which one is Paulina, again?  This isn’t an accessible game for people who aren’t super geniuses.

- Why doesn’t Bunker have a mustache?

- Absolute Zero’s modules should be relics as well!  Seriously, step up your consistency, LESS THAN Games!

- I feel like Argent Adept doesn’t do enough.  Like we’ll be playing with Ra, Wraith, AA, and Mr. Fixer and everyone else’s turn takes like ten minutes and the guy playing AA feels left out because his turn is too short.  Please give him some more damage, it would be an improvement to the character.  And also explian those green lasers?  (And if you mention the lettuce page I don’t care because I didn’t buy the salad edition.)

- The Sentinels are two squishy and should have 25 health each.

- Bring back the 3.14 difficulty for Santa Guise!  I don’t like political games, and the Tauist message you’re shoving in my face is too much.

- The Wraith should have two cats while Utility Belt is out.

- How many times have you printed this game?  And still never fixed the misprint in Absolut Zero’s power?  He should deal a villain target damage, not himself.

- Also, the difficulty curve is insane.  I’ve played against Baron Blade 5 times in 5 different environments and still haven’t flipped him?  How am I supposed to get ahead if I don’t draw the perfect thing on turn one?  Maybe each player should start with cards in hand?

- Your weird logo circles aren’t consistent between heroes.  Like I get you think it’s cute to match them to the heroes but it’s not realistic, really pulls me out of the story.

- I haven’t played the Matriarch yet but she looks too easy?  Like Baron Blade has 10-health minions so of course he’s hard.  Matriarch has a bunch of 1-HP birds, what are they gonna do?

- Like if you agree!
