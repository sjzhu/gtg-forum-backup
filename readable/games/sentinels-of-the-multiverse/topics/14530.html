Lohgrim:

As other have pointed out, this game does seem to be the bread and butter for GTG, so I've just been wondering if they are really planning to leave it 100% behind.

The problem, from a "story" point of view, is that, once the OblivAeon event happen, either the whole universe disappears, or alternate universes and timelines are no more accessible. And one of the prinicples of the game is that each fight we have is an "alternate timeline" one - not the fight in the story. Baron Blade only tried to pull the moon out once - and was stopped by the F5.

In other words : Sentinels of the Multiverse gameplay is based on the idea of multiple timelines/parallel universe.

Of course, another company would not hesiate to keep expanding the line - but it seems important for GtG. They prefer to expand the universe in other directions through other games. Tactics, the RPG, other projects they haven't talked about yet ( cooperative FILTER strategy game, anyone?).

However, it is always possible… I think for instance a "Golden Age" expansion for SotM, with Heroes and Villains from the past like Black Fist, old Legacy and Baron Blade's father could be compatible with the SotM line and would work well with us fans…

Anyway, they WON'T leave it "100% behind". They'll keep promoting it, will make events and challenges for it, maybe publish a few comics and books, will keep expanding through other games. And in a few years, if there is enough demand, especially from new players, work on a new edition with new art and revised gameplay. But, as a fan, I am content with the fact that they stop right where they wanted to. It's like TV shows - shows who try to outlive their story for money tend to slowly wither and lose in quality It's better to end big than slowly fade away. Time for a new adventure - that may or may not work, we will see !

If you have one person doing a lot of the work, so long as they are nice about it, the impact on prep is minimal for the rest of the group. The in-game complication is also a factor.
