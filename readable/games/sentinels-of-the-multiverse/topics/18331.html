So I was playing the video game weekly last night and discovered a rule I never knew existed. Cosmic Parse was incapped and let OmniX take his turn actions in any order. Evidently, if you enter your draw phase first, it allows you to draw 2 cards, as you didn’t use a play or power action yet and that clause checks in the draw phase specifically. In my head it was always closer to an end of turn thing. It never occurred to me before seeing it in game that this is how it would work but it makes sense.

this is crucial to understand exactly how the boat wheel works for La Comandora. I believe this will be a game changer for you folks at home wondering how you’re gonna sustain more cards in hand. honestly, the 2 rating is deceiving, but her villain deck was originally rated easier than it actually is too. If anyone knows things about history repeating itself, it’s her I guess.

I'm amused that this comes on the heels of the revelation that the game's original design was Draw, Play, Power, but they decided to change it so that players would have a whole round to look at their new card and consider strategy.  Concorda not only flips the rule back, but gives you an extra card for doing so, slowing your turn down even more.  Not good game design, there.

This card was heavily discussed during playtesting and was polarizing.
