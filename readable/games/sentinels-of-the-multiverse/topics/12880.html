We like to set up games that "make sense." Then when we inevitably lose, we reroll the game with our "heavy hitters", a small group of characters that we seem to do really well with.

I think that while some characters are more clearly geared toward support than others, you can still choose to play them in other ways. For example, you could play a "punch everything!" Legacy, or get the Adept to use his buffs and damage-dealing on himself to actually kill stuff. It might even be fun to mess around with playing a hero against the way it seems they "should" be played, because then you might find a new way to play them. If the Scholar has enough card draw (hey there, TLT :D) he can start demolishing things with a couple of copies of his Liquid and Energy forms - make sure he takes some damage, use his base power to heal, the healing is increased by howver many Liquid forms you have out and then you get to deal that much damage (plus more if buffed by, say, Legacy) to something for each copy of Energy Form you have out :D. Also works well if another healing hero is around (eg Tempest, Adept). When I played him this way once, I actually had problems with being on full hp because I'd been getting too much healing, therefore couldn't heal in order to hit anything else.

It's just generally very cool to find out all the little nuances and tricks you can do with some heroes. In some cases it's a very specific set-up you need to get in order to pull something off (Visionary in her Cocoon with Twist and Wrest on a Psy-Weaver means death to all Minions at no extra cost :D), but when you do manage it, even if doing so is a complete accident and you didn't plan for it or even see it coming, it's brilliant :D.
