Got a quick question about ruling from a solo play I'm trying

So I have Cosmic Crest, Dynamic Syphon, and Sustained Influence out on the field. I understand the ruling that I can only use a power of a card once a turn, so in this scenario I have already used Dyanamic Syphon's ability to trigger Cosmic Crest to heal other constructs from an environment's AoE damage. A second AoE environment damage would destroy Cosmic Crest, and I can use Sustained Influence to "put it back in play." In this case, can I use Dynamic Syphon to trigger Cosmic Crest again?

My main reason for thinking you can is along the line that the rule allows you to play two of the same power IF you have multiple copies of a card. In this case, the Cosmic Crest was destroyed and came back "as a new copy," per se since it entered your trash, and should therefore NOT be the same copy that was already in play. Is that correct, or is the Cosmic Crest still not usable in this instance?
