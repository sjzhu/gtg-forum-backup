Yeah, it's a 1-5 player game, but a 3-5 hero game. If there are two players, suggestion is for each to play 2 heroes (which is what we do at home) or have one person play 2 heroes (which might be better for you so your dad can just focus on one?). Playing just 2 heroes will be nearly impossible, and will result is issues with the (H) mechanic that's used to balance the game. 

No environment will likely be a little easier, but it's not something I've ever tried and will remove a lot of the fun of the game. You might want to try a more friendly environment to help things, but I don't know what you have available to you... :-(
