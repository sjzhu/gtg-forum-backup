Just finished a game against Voss with the heroes near full strength.

 

GI Bunker started with Ammo Drop

Young Legacy started with Inspiring Prescence

Mr. Fixer started with Dual Crowbars

Ra played Imbued Fire

Absolute Zero neutered a Forced Deployment with Fueled Freeze

 

Neither ship came out.

 

Realm of Discord had little negative effect. Positive Energy didn't help the minions enough to keep them alive. Time Crawls only slowed me down a little. Ethereal Bubbles got one-shot by the second hit from Dual Crowbars. Time Flies was fun.

 

Bunker had a hand full of cards but never got to take advantage of Gatling Gun or Omnicannon because of Time Crawls. Both of them got played when Time Flies came out, but I won the game a couple hero turns later. Bunker's removal of Voss' DR meant I could leave one minion out and just pound Voss on his front side. I actually had to leave two minions out so that Mr. Fixer would have a first target with his Dual Crowbars so he could hit Voss as the second target.

 

Imbued Fire meant Absolute Zero couldn't heal himself after the first round, making Thermal Shockwave less useful, but two Impaling Structures were quite nice.
