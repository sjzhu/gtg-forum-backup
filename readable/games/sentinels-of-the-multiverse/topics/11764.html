PW Argent Adept: Adept in the graps of some Shuma-Gorath style tentacled horrors with a central eye, his pipes held in one of his hands and the other glowing.

PW Captain Cosmic: Cosmic apparently transmuted to gold, a green-skinned woman is in the background with a white pony tail, armour and red lines crossing her cheeks.

PW Faantic: Fanatic being backed up against something while grey-skinned ghoulrs or vampires swarm around her, blood dripping from their mouths, bald with pointed ears. A shadowy cloaked figure is in the far background but no details.

PW Haka: Haka restrained with suitably high-tech and glowing metal bars, glowering down on a taunting Ambuscade.

PW Tempest: Tempest on the ground, a gene-bound maerynian (I think) crackling with red lightning standing over hir, a trident pressed into the ground around Tempest's throat, the tines on either side of hir neck.
