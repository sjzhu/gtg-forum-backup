I'm looking for feedback from people who use my oversized villain cards (if you don't use them, this question isn't for you).  The setup and then the question:

Currently, the oversized villain cards have a section on the top-right with a comic book-style title and a chunk of text setting up the villain encounter.  I originally put that section there way back when the game first came out and I felt like not enough had been done to explain the villains' evil plans, so I added that blurb to give it more of a comic story feel.  Since then, the lore of the game has deepened considerably, and I'm not sure that section is useful or interesting anymore.

Further, since I never play against villains on Advanced, in order to save space on the card I moved the Advanced rules off to a separate card which people could use when they're playing Advanced but could ignore when they're not (like I was not).

So the question is, would current users of the oversized villain cards prefer than I eliminated the story blurb and use that space to put the Advanced rules back onto the card, eliminating the need for the separate Advanced cards, or do you prefer it how it is?
