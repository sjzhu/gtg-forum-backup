I think the problem really stems from trying to make a competitive scenario out of a decidedly non-competitive game. That's not to discourage any effort to try, but it's going to be difficult to form rules and a system to this tournament that have "fair" parameters, as I can't imagine a system that won't have some problems or large disadvantages in it.

But for the sake of discussion and to be more helpful than pessamistic, I think MWC146 is probably on the trail of something workable. You could have a point system for games that assign points based parameters like:

  • win
  • loss
  • villain difficulty
  • environment difficulty
  • heroes survived
  • advanced mode
  • number of heroes (though you might want to consider making this standard throughout the tournament)

I'd stay away from assigning points based on HP or hero difficulty since that will unnessesarily promote/discourage certain heroes from being considered.

I think if you were to design a point system like this, the tournament points would revolve around cost/benefit and risk assesment of the games. A team participating would need to evaluate how much risk they want to take in overall difficulty of the game vs how much points they stand to win in relation to how many points they could gain by doing an easy game. This risk assesment in points should be weighed correctly to make this choice interesting. For example, if a win vs Chairman in Rook City only awarded 3 points total, a loss in that scenario awards 0, but a win vs Baron in Freedom Tower is 2 points, then there would be no realistic insentive to take the higher risk, especially if there is any sort of time constraint attached where teams will feel pressured to not only consider quality of games but also quantity of games within the alloted timeframe. With timeframe and the above scenario, Baron in Freedom Tower is clearly the better choice as you could easily get in two games of that with easy wins for 4 total points in the same time you might finish a game vs Chairman where the win is much harder to get and the payoff is only 3 points in the same timeframe. Again, no inscentive to take the risk. This should be avoided.
