I use premium sleeves.

First card-width drawer: Heroes, w/ their character cards and variants

Second card-width drawer: The rest of the heroes, except void guard, also w/ their character cards and variants

Third card-width drawer: Villains, w/ their character cards and variants

Fourth card-width drawer: Environments, Void Guard, and OblivAeon missions/rewards

Larger drawer, front well: Tokens and dice

Larger drawer, second well: Other OblivAeon decks besides the missions/rewards, plus the five Vengeance decks

Larger drawer, third well: The ten Villains decks

Larger drawer, fourth well: OblivAeon-specific tokens, plus the OblivAeon oversize cards

Wide, top drawer: Rule books and a menagerie of oddities: Sentinel points, pins, old tracker cards, signed cards, and others.

I'm a no-sleevin' heathen, so my box is as follows:

Wide drawer, section 1:  all rectangular tokens - damage types, all the original ones for +1 / -1 DR, damge dealt, etc

Section 2: all other tokens, including bagged 1s, 5s, 10s, 25s, the 100s and 1000s loose (may bag those with the 25s next time for organization's sake), devestations, Harpy's (might find somewhere else for these?...) etc

Section 3: Obliv's long/wide stuff, the booklet, the scions, etc.

Secftion 4: Vengeance wideboys, and I've got the large villain card pack on order so they'll go in there too.

Top right drawer - Villains.  For all following drawers, dividers are alphabetical, alternating left-right tabs, starting left.  All decks are card back facing front, 90 degrees counter clockwise oriented from upright.  Character cards are starting side facing front, before deck cards.

Bottom left drawer - Environments, then foam, then Vengeance/VotM villain decks

Bottom middle drawer - Heroes, A-L

Bottom right drawer - Heroes, M-W

Thin drawer up top - booklets, Obliv's standee, comic, etc  No idea what to do with the small section on the side, nor the other section not holding booklets.  Might move some tokens upstairs, or do some print-outs of Spiff's stuff and put it there.

I don't sleeve either, and have my trays laid out as follows...

Top tray - Oblivaeon rulebook, comic-book-thingy, and hero comparison booklet

Large drawer in second row - Front section has Vengeance/Villains character cards, section behind that holds Oblivaeon rulebook and all his large cards (Scions, etc), plus the Oblivaeon standy-uppy-mini thing and the numbered "Hero" tokens for battle zones

Small drawer, bottom left - Heroes arranged alphabetically by expansion, up as far as "La Comodora" (who comes under "C", not "L")

Small drawer, bottom middle - The rest of the heroes plus all Cauldron heroes (the latter in the little baggies I was given them in as I don't have dividers for them, obviously ;))

Small drawer, bottom right - All environments, alphabtically by expansion, then the Oblivaeon decks (minus oversized cards of course), then Cauldron villains and environments (again, in baggies)

Small drawer, middle right - "Normal" villains, alphabtically by expansion, then "group" villains, alphabetically by expansion

Note - Decks released as a promo and not part of an actual expansion (eg Unity, Ambuscade) are grouped alphebetically at the end of whatever deck type they are (hero/villain/environment).

Let's do this!

 

Top tray - All rulebooks, hero comparison, comic, the art prints, and a few Cauldron sets I've printed off.

Large drawer -

  • Front section has 1000/100 health tokens, Harpy tokens, Devastation tokens, and also "decks" of the old-style dividers for the heros, standard villains, and environments; when I have people playing, I can hand them those as a nice visual way to choose what we want in the game, rather than flicking through the drawers themselves.
  • Next has all the over-sized villain cards
  • Third has the old-style dividers for Vengeance-style villains, and all the misc token (damage types, effects etc.)
  • Last section has the small villain cards, and some spare old-style dividers.

Small bottom left - Standard villain decks, and all the Oblivaeon decks

Small bottom middle - Environments at the front, Vengeance-style villain decks at the back

Small top right - Heroes from Absolute Zero to Nightmist

Small bottom right - Heroes from Omnitron X to Writhe

For the heroes, all the different character cards are at the front, so people can pick the one they want, and put the rest away. I'm kinda tempted to split out the character cards into their own section, as there are the dividers for it, but it just seems to make more sense to have them together with the deck; if you're going to grab the deck anyway, why go elsewhere for the rest?
