I love to play with editing and layout in Microsoft Publisher and Photoshop. This is a supplement I made for one of my favourite games, Sentinels of the Multiverse!

At my house, we each read the backstory of the hero we’re playing as in that game (plus the villain). I’ve been working on this printable book that mirrors the standard rulebooks and has the bios of any promotional alts for heroes and villains that aren’t in print elsewhere. It also has the flavour text for the environments!

It’s still a work in progress, but I’d love some extra eyes on it before it’s printed.

I'll be putting the final version up on BGG, and the best way I've found to print it is to use Staples's online print submission service.

Right! So, if you were to submit the file to a document printing service (I use Staples), the sheets will nest perfectly, as-is, into a booklet just like the official ones. If you took the staples out of a comic (or a Sentinels booklet) and separated the leaves, you would find the exact same thing.

You will also notice that the paper size is 11 X 17 ". That's so once you trim it, it is the exact size as the official books! (8.5 X 11 " is too small).
