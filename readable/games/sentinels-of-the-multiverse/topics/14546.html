Okay, played a couple of games.  The first one was, as planned, against Ambuscade.  I chose Freedom Tower, which seemed like a good place to hold a press conference.  Ultimately, the game was a bit of a farce - the Tower ended up putting a bunch of Hero Ongoings/Equipment into  play, and Ambuscade has no stuff destruction, so we wore him down in a slow game which never really felt in question.

Clearly, I needed a tougher villain with a woder range of threats - but anyone too fast would probably just kill everyone before their stunted setup could work.  Also, an environment which did damage could be more interesting.  So, Tachyon opened Cretaceus World, and called the Sentinels, Legacy, and the Scholar (as well as those pesky members of the press) to tour the facilities.  Inevitably, they got attacked by Egyptian gods.

Okay, so the story's a little odd there, but the Ennead (non-Advanced) play relatively slowly, but can still be a threat and dish out some Ongoing/Equipment disruption.  It was a brutal match - and I got lucky, never seeing the extra card play Ennead members.  Over twelve turns, Legacy and the Scholar locked them down whilst Tachyon and the Sentinels took them out.  I'd recommend this game for anyone looking to try a unique challenge - though Insula Primalis for the Environment may be a bit much.
