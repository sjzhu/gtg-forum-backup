I like the more complex decks, and AZ was my early favorite. The feedback of all his cards and flow of damage suits my need for numbers.

AZ without support is a B- deck. His effectiveness comes from + Damage Dealt, which makes Legacy all that much more important. Adept, TTE, and the other rare + Damage Dealt abilities Are far more effective on him, because it helps him damage and heal himself. With support, he is one of the few decks I call A+. That amplification may be why AZ has no normal nemesis deck, just a Vengeance minor leaguer in Proletariate. A full on Nemesis for AZ may amp him up, and might wind up with problems for balancing that deck.

AZ is vulnerable to large-scale destruction, but so is everyone else. He can take longer to recover, but he has good deck search for his mandatory modules and trash search for what was wrecked.

I tend to use AZ to soak Highest Hero Damage (HHD) before worrying about him dealing damage with Impale, etc. When he is soaking well, games end with 20 HP remaining on all heroes, regardless of how long the game lasts.

#18

Not all Heroes are great against all Villains. They all have strengths and weaknesses.

For Miss Information you want Ongoing destruction and deck manipulation. AZ can do some good Ongoing destruction, but he needs to hold onto it until all the clues you need to flip MI are in play. AZ has no deck manipulation. Since he is reliant on Modules to deal damage or heal, if you can't keep Equipment in play he is nigh useless. Other characters have reasonably useful Innates and decent one-shots, but his one-shots are also suicidal without Modules.

Wraith and Visionary for MI. Both can put Clues on the top of the Villain deck and get you to where Ongoing destruction can start. You may want to destroy some Ongoings that are harsh on particular characters.

There are multiple ways to deal with Equipment and Hero Ongoing destruction (or at least the ones that don't destroy it all). Choose heroes that all put out lots of it to overwhelm the destruction, or heroes that rely on One-shots and don't need Equipment or Ongoings. When you go with random heroes, you sacrifice this and lose because your strategy was poor. If AZ is the only Equipment heavy character, then put him away and take someone else. Unlike Wraith, who can flood out Equipment, or Bunker, who can switch modes to flood out cards at times, AZ rarely puts more than 1 Equipment or Ongoing in play per turn, so even 1 per Turn destruction stops him... cold. But if 4 heroes are putting out 2.5 per turn average, then they build slowly, but they do build.
