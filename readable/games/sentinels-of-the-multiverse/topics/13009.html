We did F5 vs. V5 twice this weekend, once in the Dok'Thorath Capital and once in the Realm of Discord.

The first game, the Dok'Thorath civil war basically won the game. We and the V5 took so much damage, between Orbital Bombardments and just Civilians and Minions shooting everyone, that even though we came close to winning, it felt like we were mostly dodging civil war battles. It was a ton of fun, though, and we locked the Villains down for three rounds in a row (Heroic Interception and then two rounds of Accelerated Assault). We wound up with just us and Proletariat alive, and the environment killed all the heroes, leaving Prole with about 7 HP.

The second game was a grindfest, but in a good way. Friction was a beast, but we took out her Shock Dampeners and Blade's Physique rather quickly, so they handled themselves. Bunker got early damage resistance and a grenade launcher and just blasted Fright Train each turn for the extra Nemesis + Engine of Destruction damage. Absolute Zero took a long time to set up, but once he did, he lit up the board. Tachyon never saw a Lightspeed Barrage (ever, in the whole game), but Sonic Vortex is great against the V5 when you've gotg Inspiring Presense, Galvanize, and ~10 Burst cards in your trash. Wraith got her Razor Ordnance and Micro Targeting Computer out really quickly, and with Legs' support she was doing crap-tons of damage… especially to Ermine.

In the end of the second game, we had lost Legacy and AZ, and no one else had more than 5HP. It came down to Proletariat again, and one more round would have killed us, but we brought him down.

At the end, I triumphantly cried, "We have save the Realm of Discord!" and we both kinda went, "Hm." All things being equal, the V5 could probably have the Realm of Discord, since neither I nor my wife have any real estate there.
