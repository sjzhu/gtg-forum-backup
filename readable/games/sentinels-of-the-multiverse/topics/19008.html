In Oblivaeon games, where do effects live?

I’m talking about things like Legacy’s Galvanize buff, Wraith’s Stun Bolt reducing the damage a target can do, or Void Guard Dr. Medico’s Immunization.

The way it was explained to me, they “live” in the play area of the player, character, or target that created the effect, even if that thing moves Battle Zones. That doesn’t seem like a big deal, but it can do weird things when you’re switching Battle Zones 

Case in point, if VG Dr. Medico uses Immunization in Battle Zone 1, and all other players take damage, but then Dr. Medico is incapacitated and the new hero moves to Battle Zone 2, the Immunized heroes won’t be immune to damage until they also move to Battle Zone 2 at some point before that player’s next turn.

Or, if Wraith uses Stun Bolt on Oblivaeon, but then moves to the other Battle Zone, Oblivaeon won’t do less damage unless Oblivaeon and Wraith are in the same Battle Zone again. So, if Wraith is going to Stun Bolt the big guy, she also has to stay with him or her stunning effect goes away with her… unless they come back in contact, then it restarts.

The fact that the effects always travel with the origin and never with the recipient is what makes this weird. I don’t imagine that Wraith is carefully removing her Stun Bolts when she leaves the area, then asking for a time out if they come within reach before the beginning of her next turn. The Immunization shots Dr. M distributes don’t seem like they would only work when you’re around him.

For now, I’ll play it this way, but it’s a rule that doesn’t seem to make sense as it currently stands.

I understand you may feel there's a thematic disconnect, but the mechanics are fairly straightforward and come from that single rule in the rulebook.

The only other logical place for the effect to live would be with the target, which would mean it should move with the target between Battle Zones and always be in the same zone as the target. Your question is based on an incorrect assumption (and believe me, most of us held it for a long time before official clarification came through).  Effects do not have locations.  They do not "live" in a place.

If someone uses an untargeted effect with a duration, like Galvanize, it stays with them.  If someone uses a targeted effect with a duration, it functions only as long as the source and the target are in the same zone.  If one of the two moves zones, the effect is suppressed.  I assume, though have not seen clarified, that if the second target then moves to the same zone as the first, the effect resumes.

The weird thing, and the one I'm hung up on, is that a card cannot affect another card if the first card moves to the other battle zone, but it can affect another card if it is removed from the game entirely.  In fact, I believe, and I'm happy to be corrected, if The Wraith uses Stun Bolt on a target and then is removed from the game somehow, and then the other target changes zones, the effect will persist!  And if my assumption above is correct, if The Wraith uses Stun Bolt, then moves to another zone, the effect will stop working, but if The Wraith is then removed from the game, it will start working again.

Thematically nonsensical?  Yes.  Unintuitive?  Yes.  But a logical inference from the way the rules are phrased.
