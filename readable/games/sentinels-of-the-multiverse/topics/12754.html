It works with Lost in the Past (thanks grysqrl). If that card is out, and there are no cards in the trash, you lose. Since it is actually really hard for the trash to run out, there isn’t much of a point to discard a card.

Also, Haka only draws a card when they are destroyed not discarded.

Lost in the Past causes you to lose if there are no cards in the environment trash. This gives you another way to get a card into that trash. It is also useful if you know something nasty is on top of the environment deck and want to get rid of it.

Edit: I guess you could use also use it to accelerate an Akash'Bhuta flip. So, there are a few very specific cases where this is useful, but most of the time people probably don't discard for it.
