I realized something. We've talked alot about the meta of Sentinel Comics. Things like movies, cartoons, spin-off tv series, and even the company itself. Yet there's one thing we haven't touched. I don't know about you guys, but one of my favorite aspects of comicbook culture are the toys and figures that flood the isles of Walmart and Toys'R'Us(RIP). So with that, I want to talk about what kinds of action figures and toys kid you(and maybe even adult you) would love to see and play with. :3

I already know I'd really want a Bunker deluxe action figure. You know, the kind that's way bigger than your standard action figure and costs at least twice as much. They'd have some snap-on missile launchers that fire 'real' missiles, or maybe a gattling gun that can spin and makes sounds. Maybe there's even some buttons on the back that makes the suit talk or make the suit's eyes light up. The best feature by far would be that the suit's chest could open up to reveal a small Tyler Vance figure that you can take out and play with! :D

What about you guys? Have any ideas what kind of action figures or toys you'd want of the SC characters?

I always preferred my toys to be relatively small (palm sized at most), pretty resistant to damage (I want to throw it around without much risk of losing my toy), and water resistant (so I bring them to the pool and play their adventures in Atlantis or similar).    

Some specific ideas:

-Vehicles that you could put all the other toys inside: The Freedom Five Jet, Expatriette's motorcycle, any kind of cool car like... The Wraithmobile?

I don't if there's any iconic vehicle in Sentinels, but I'm sure toy companies would make up lots of new ones for some extra bucks: Legacy's desert buggy! Absolute Zero's snowmobile!  Tachyon's jet-skateboard! Naturalist's monster truck (with accessories for each animal form, and a smoke-blowing gimmick)!

-Cool bases and enviroments that you can play with the toys inside: The mobile defense platform (with a retractable semi-transparente plastic dome)! The Freedom Tower (or atleast rooms of it)! Wargner Mars Base! The tome of Anubis (with little buttons to activate the traps)!

-Cloth (or something similarly flexible)! I was so used to toys having stiff plastic capes and similar that any cloth accesory was the coolest thing ever. Give me those flowy capes and those extra uniforms that you can dress your guys with (like Baron Blade's laboratory coat o Chrono Rangers Poncho). 

-Transformable toys were some of my favorite. I'd spend hours just transforming them again and again. Naturalist would be a bit hard to do without looking weird, but I could see something like a Unity Golem that you can transform into another, or even combine the small ones to make a Turret Bot or similar.

 

>  The best feature by far would be that the suit's chest could open up to reveal a small Tyler Vance figure that you can take out and play with! :D

Exactly what I always wanted as a kid, although Kid-Clerk would probably get rid of the "boring military dude" and put other toys inside it to mix things up. 

Something similar for Baron Blade's armor would also be neat.
