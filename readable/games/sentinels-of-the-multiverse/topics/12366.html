Try this team: Legacy, Visionary, Wraith, Tachyon (Super Scientific, if you have her), and Tempest.

 

Use Visionary to give cards to Wraith until she has out Infrared Eyepiece and the Utility Belt, then use her to boost Tachyon. If you get a Twist the Ether put it on either Wraith or, more likely Tempest.

 

Tachyon should mostly focus on getting as many bursts in her trash as she can, but save your hypersonic assaults for when you need to keep Omnitron from dealing damage.

 

Tempest should focus mainly on dealing damage, boosted by Legacy. Later in the game, Wraith can help out with the damage. You want to keep the Infrared Eyepiece going, though, so you can bury Omnitron's card descrutction and EPEs.

Compare Tachyon to Legacy, Team leader is as good a power as Galvanize, situationally worse or better, but roughly equal.

Original Tachyon is more Young Legacy (though not as powerful a power) her base power is all about her, and feeds her trash enough until you get Research Grant, which is when she takes off.

SST is Grandpa, can use that wicked power on herself or others.  To me she is far stronger than original Tachyon used selfishly, because the odds of putting a burst card in your trash with Experiment are better than Research Grant, while feeding cards in play unreliably instead of feeding cards to hand.

The offset is the risk of discarding cards you want, but since it is from the bottom it takes a while before that can be a negative, at which point you are shuffling your deck anyway.

There will be games where it underperforms Rapid Recon->Research Grant, but those are rare animals.
