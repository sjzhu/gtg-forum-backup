Ever since I first played against Kaargra, I've wanted to use the title deck in games against other villains.

If you have opaque sleexes you can create an arena environment deck pretty easily.

Titles + Fickle Fans + Bloodsworn Judgement + arena card.

Favor works as such:

End of the Round (Between Env. and Villain turn)

1.  If Heroes have more favor than villains you can destroy one title card in play.

2.  If Villains have more favor than heroes take the oldest title card in play and put it under the Villain target with the fewest titles (high->low HP).

3.  If Favor is even, nothing happens.

 

Then I add a rule I house rule into arena games anyway:

Anytime there are more than (H) titles in the title area take the titles that have been there the longest and put them under the deck until there are only (H) Titles in play.

 

If you don't sleeve you can run a variant where you play the deck face up so you don't see the backs, you play a card by moving the top card off the top of the deck, and the top card of the deck is always in play.

(This is because Fickle Fans and Bloodsworn Judgement have different backs and will be easy picking for deck control)
