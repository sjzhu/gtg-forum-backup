skoticus:

How do you use these? Just curious what your method is with them, though they look great.



The token faces are glued to wooden discs and I use them on a tracker sheet to more easily see the relative HP of the various heroes and villains:

1204×677 264 KB

 

It can be as simple as just cutting them out of paper and using them as-is, but this is how I do mine after each new expansion, step-by-step:

  1. Print actual size on high-quality photo paper (8.5"x11" in the format provided).
  2. Laminate on image side only.
  3. Use 3/4" punch to remove individual tokens from sheet (I'm sure you can do it with scissors just fine, but it would require extra work that I'm too lazy for).
  4. Color 3/4' wooden discs black (I use permanent marker because I like the finish, but paint would work just as well). 
  5. Glue token faces to discs with gloss Mod Podge, allow to dry.
  6. Write character names and starting HP values on back of token with metallic sharpie. I also color-code the edges - black (unaltered) are regular heroes, silver for alt/promo/fan-made, gold for villains, and bronze for the generic villain tokens that are used most frequently. It's hard to see in the pictures, but easy to distinguish when they're all stacked together.
  7. Seal remaining surfaces with Mod Podge to keep the ink from fading.


I realize it sounds arduous, but it's really not too bad once you get in a little bit of a workflow. I built myself a set of cardboard tubes to hold them all together in the lid of my box, but the clear tubes that coin collectors use to store nickels in also fit them perfectly.

I'm only set up to monitor character targets on the tracker sheet. All the minions just get tracked with HP tokens. Therefore there is generally only one villain token on the playmat at a time, and when there are more (Ennead/Vengeance/Organization) it's not difficult to separate them based on portraits.

The reason for the wide black border is so you don't have to be exactly perfect when cutting them out and to help it blend with the colored disc behind it. Rabit's suggestion is a good one and I can make make some with red villain borders if you guys want them, but I'm kinda picky about aesthetics and would personally be frustrated by the portrait border not matching the color of the disc below it (they're never exactly the same size). I have the edges of the discs color-coded to make them easier to sort when taking out/putting away and that has always been sufficient for me.
