I had one awesome fight, with Haka, ExPat, Ra, and someone else (I don't remember exactly, but their incap let them destroy environment cards, I think; this was before Infernal Relics, I think) VS. Plague Rat in Insula Primalis. At a certain point in the game, Plague Rat was still at 65 HP, Haka was down to 1, and everyone else was incapped. I still won.

As it turns out, Mere + 3 dominions + 2 extra powers per round + a free environment destruction each round is a pretty good way to build up your hand size. It turned into what I imagined to be a down-to-the-wire, no-holds-barred beatdown between Haka and Plague Rat. No one else there (conscious, anyway), nothing getting in the way; just an extremely pissed off Maori in Barbarian Rage mode and a giant rat man beating the crap out of each other. Each round it went something like:

Plage rat plays a card, deals damage. Deals a little more damage at end of turn.

Ra's turn: Haka punches Plague Rat. Draw a card.

Expat's turn: Haka punches Plague Rat. Draw a card.

???'s turn: destroy an environment card. Draw 3 cards.

Haka's turn: play elbow smash, rampage, or Haka of Restoration; punch Plague Rat; draw a card. draw a card.

Environment happens. Not very relevant.

Repeat.

 

It was an awesome turnaround, and still one of my favorite victories.
