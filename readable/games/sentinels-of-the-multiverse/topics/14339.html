One of my FLGS (Brave New World in Cologne) happens to be the lone German distributor of GTG games. I was told one time that every shop that stocks GTG games in Germany has gotten their copy of Sentinels from there, and because they are the distributors, the Brave New World booth is also the only booth at Essen Spiel that ever sells the game- and they do tend to run out.

I don't know exactly how much Robert (the owner) orders each time he orders from GTG, but I can say he mainly does it each time a new expansion becomes available, at which point he apparently orders a couple of whatever else happens to be in print at the time. Then some of those copies make it to other German stores, but I think it's never more than two or three per store, max, and not everybody does it, obviously. Even the Brave never has more than one copy on the shelf, and usually not the whole range of expansions.

So basically, your game stores would have to ring up the Brave to see if they can reserve some copies for the next time that there is a shipment of GTG games and then work out whether that's financially viable for them. Unless one store wants to become the Dutch GTG distributor, in which case they should probably talk to Paul about that.
