So I decited to make up my own rules for him:

 

Omnitron-X has suffered a Logic/Emotion chip Failure. (Omniton-X loses 1 HP)

Omniton-X rolls a 6 sided dice and decides what to do.

 

Roll 1 Or 3: Omniton-X Feels the “Meat Sacks” need to die and will do something sinister.

 

Roll 2 or 5: Omnitron-X May Choose to help or Hinder the “Meat Sacks”.

 

Roll 4 or 6: Omnitron-X Must either Help the “Meat Sacks” or help himself.
