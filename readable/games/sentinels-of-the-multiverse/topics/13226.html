The Argent Adept Kvothe Six-String (henceforth refered to as Kvothe) starts with Rhapsody of Vigor, Syncopated Onslaught, Instrumental Conjuration, and Silver Shadow.

Mister Fixer starts with Jack Handle, Toolbox, Overdrive, Alternating Tiger Claw.

Expatriette starts with Speed Loading, "Prejudice", RPG Launcher, and RPG Launcher.

Absolute Zero starts with Thermal Shockwave, Glacial Structure, Sub-Zero Atmosphere, and Onboard Modular Installation.

Dark Watch NightMist (henceforth refered to as NightMist) starts with Mist-Fueled Recovery (4), Call Forth (4), Astral Premonition (2), and Oblivion (2).

Omnitron-IV plays Overcharged Systems, Techno-Pursuer, and Resource Recycler. Techno-Pursuer deals 1 damage to a Twisted Miscreation (1).

Round 3

Infinitor plays Whispers of Oblivion, dealing 1 damage to the highest Twisted Miscreation (1) and lowest (X), also 2 damage to Infinitor (41), Kvothe (7), Mister Fixer (6), Expatriette (7), Absolute Zero (4), and Techno-Pursuerer (3). Mechani-Accumulator destroys Null-Point Callibration Unit and Convueyor Panels. Techno-Pursuerer deals 1 damage to Twisted Miscreation (1).

Round 4

Absolute Zero plays Fueled Freeze, destroying Sub-Zero Atmosphere, Cold Snap, and Impale, dealing 6 damage to Infinitor (10), Internal Defense Grid (X), Mechani-Accumulator (X), and Techno-Pursuer (X). Then deals 2 damage to Infinitor (1). Attunenment, plutting Planar Banishment (1) ontop, followed by Mist of Time (4), and finally Mist of Time (3). Draw Planar Banishment (1).

Episode 2

Advanced The Ennead versus The Argent Adept Kvothe Six-String, Mister Fixer, Expatriette, Absolute Zero, and Dark Watch NightMist in The Block

Heroes win!!! Kvothe at 11 HP,  Mister Fixer at 1 HP, Expatriette at 11 HP, Absolute Zero at 5 HP, and NightMist at 9 HP.

 

The game was fairly difficult at times, but thanks to Grease Gun and Mistbound, they had 4 turns where they did squat. Even with Nuit out, Mister Fixer used Riveting Crane to allow the heroes to keep on wailing on Set and Atum. Expatriette got a round 3 Tactical Shotgun, which was very nice. An early Cold Snap and Focused Appertures for Absolute Zero lead to him slowing widdling every non-hero target's HP. NightMist let off a couple of well timed Oblivions to weaking the villains significantly. All while Kvothe was giving the highest and lowest hero damage reduction and throwing out card plays, also using Vernal Sonata to rig the hero plays from Set. Kvothe even allowed Mister Fixer to get two Bloody Knuckles and an overdrive into play, with Dual Crowbars also around, it was a nasty sight. Also Char was his usual helpful self, dealing the killing blow to Set, allowing the heroes to focus on Atum and Nuit.
