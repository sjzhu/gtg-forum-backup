Just wanted to share a super-satisfying game my partner and I had last night, fighting Skinwalker Gloomy in the Final Wasteland. First environment card out was a New Jersey Devil, second was Unforgiving Wasteland, and the team consisted of Lgacy and two hard-hitting heroes. I think you can see where this is going ;). We got the Devil to eat the Pouch and about three Zombies before we whittled Gloomy down low enough and I double-checked his card to see that his flip condition is "when he would be destroyed", not "when he's reduced to 0hp or fewer". So then we made sure that we didn't accidentally flip him and spent our last round basically doing nothing with him on 5hp so that he got finished off by an Abominable Snowman and that same New Jersey Devil we'd deliberately left out. Boom, game over as an unflipped Gloomy got nommed :D.

Yeah, I'm sure people have done this already, with this version of Gloomy and with other villains who have similar flip conditions, but that doesn't make it any less cool for me when I managed to get it to happen in a game I was playing >:D.
