A solution I've found that works fantastically for moving the case is one of the IKEA big blue bags.  The box fits almost perfectly (getting the box in or out of the bag is a tight fit), the straps are reinforced, and the weight is distributed evenly enough that I can carry the box one handed.  I'm not sure if you have an IKEA in your area, but I highly recommend the bag for Sentinels transportation.
