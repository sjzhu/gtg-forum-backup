I know there's already a powers list, but I found a few that they missed, and I already did this on YouTube anyways so I decided to go ahead and copy and paste what I was able to get; anything without parantheses is verbatim, anything inside of parantheses is my own words:

Freedom 5 Tachyon:
Blitz
Tachyon deals 1 target 1 melee damage. You may move a burst card from your trash to the bottom of your deck. If you do, use this power again now.

Freedom 5 Wraith:
Clandestine Funding
Draw a card. Until the end of your next turn, whenever you draw a card, you may discard a card. If you do, another player draws a card.

Visionary Unleashed:
Crushing Lucidity
The Visionary deals 1 target X psychic damage where X = the number of your ongoing cards in play plus 1.

Parse Fugue State:
Neural Processing
Play the bottom card of your deck. If you discarded a card this turn, one other player may play the bottom card of their deck.

Xtreme Prime WardensArgent Adept:
Rebel Yell
The Argent Adept deals 1 target other than himself 2 sonic damage. If a hero is dealt damage this way, they play a card and use a power.

Captain Cosmic (with Infinitor's power):
Requital
Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck and put them into play in any order. Then, if there are 4 or more constructs in play, each construct deals Captain Cosmic 2 psychic damage.

Xtreme Prime Wardens Captain Cosmic:
Play With Madness
Captain Cosmic deals 1 target 1 energy damage. Each construct deals that target 1 energy damage.

Xtreme Prime Wardens Haka:
Die Hard
(Couldn't actually make out most of this one, the best screen I saw was pretty blurry, but I'm pretty sure I see something about choosing a hero character, and either reducing damage or redirecting it to Haka).

Xtreme Prime Wardens Tempest:
Thunderstruck
Tempest deals 1 target 1 lightning damage. A non-character target dealt damage loses any "End of Turn" (hard to make out the rest of the text exactly, but seems like it's removes the end of turn effects until Tempest's turn).

Luminary:
Devise
Discard the top 2 cards of your deck. Move any devices discarded this way into play.

Luminary (AU female hero):
Empowered
Attack Luminary deals 1 target X lightning damage where X = the number of devices in play.

The Harpy:
Arcane Blast
The Harpy deals 1 target 2 infernal damage.

Dark Watch Harpy:
Refocus
Flip 1 control token. You may play a card.

Akash'Thriya:
Dormant Essence
Put a primordial seed from your hand or trash into the environment trash. Draw a card.

Akash'Thriya Spirit of the Void:
Essence Erruption
Either draw a card or play a card. Each primordial seed in play deals 1 target 1 toxic damage.

La Comodora:
Time's Lessons
Shuffle a hero deck. (Can't make out the rest. Seems like it might be a deck stacking power?)

La Comodora Curse of the Black Spot:
Backtrack Shot
La Comodora deals 1 target 2 energy damage. Discard the top card of a deck.

Lifeline:
Extract Power
Draw a card. One player may draw a card.

Lifeline Blood Mage:
Vitae Strike
Lifeline deals himself and each villain target 2 infernal damage.

Stuntman:
Tireur Incroyable
Stuntman deals 1 target 1 irreducible projectile damage. Increase that damage by 3 when this power is used outside of your turn.

Stuntman (Rambo-like):
All My Own Stunts
Increase damage dealt by Stuntman by 1 until the end of your next turn. You may play a card.

Benchmark:
The New Standard
Draw a card. Benchmark deals 1 target 1 energy damage.

Benchmark Supply and Demand:
Desperate Measures
Benchmark regains 1 HP. Play the top card of your deck.

Void Guard Doctor Medico:
Clinical Consult
Select a target. The next time that target regains HP, increase the amount by 2.

Void Guard Doctor Medico Malpractice:
Red Right Hand
Until the end of your next turn, whenever a hero target would regain HP, Dr. Medico deals 1 target that amount of energy damage instead.

Void Guard Mainstay:
Beatdown
Mainstay deals 1 target 1 melee damage, then deals 1 target 1 melee damage.
