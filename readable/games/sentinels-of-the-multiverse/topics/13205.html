Here's a question; are there any truly impossible-to-win situations that can come up in Sentinels, dependant on the combination of heroes, villain, and environment? To be clear, I guess the criteria would be;

  1. It's possible for a card or simple combinations of cards to come out such that, as long as it/they are present, prevents the heroes from winning (or will quickly/instantly result in them losing),
  2. There is no power, card effect or other mechanic etc amongst the decks included that would allow or cause those card/s to be removed, countered, prevented from being played etc (or the nature of those cards means those removals/counters would not then make the game winnable/prevent the game from being lost), and
  3. This situation is possible regardless of the heroes health levels (because a basic "hit everyone for x" card coming up, after the heroes are all already low on health, isn't really what we're looking for).

For example, imagine a (theoretical) team put against Omnitron that all used the same damage type and had no redirecting other damage or ongoing removal; Adaptive Armour Plating would be gg.

For obvious reasons I'm guessing most such setups would not have heroes with villain/environment deck manipulation like Visionary or Wraith, so bonus points for setups that include them!

Not as bad as the Ennead since there is an alernate win condition but advanced Gloomweaver against Legacy, The Wraith, and Parse in Megalopolis leaves you with a long game.  As it leaves you only with the option of taking out his relics or letting him flip.

Careful managing could in theory cause every target besides the Horrid Skunk-Ape, the heroes, and Gloomy to be lost in the Final Wasteland.  This would leave Gloomweaver playing nothing but Vast Followings and Strenght of the Grave, and the environment having nothing but Con's Bunker, Unforgiving Wasteland, Ancient Library, and the last Horrid Skunk-Ape, leaving the game in an infinite unending limbo as the heroes fight on forever, unable to harm Gloomweaver but unwilling to surrender what remains of our once beautiful planet into his hands.
