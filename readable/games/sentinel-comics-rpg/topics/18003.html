I try to break up the scene into locations if it’s big enough and then basically use the rule of “could the character cover this distance in one panel.

For example, in the second scene of the first issue we have the FF facing the spider bots in the park around Akash’Flora, as well as sniper bots on the rooftops surrounding the park.

This gives the locations as follows

 

Rooftops <> Park <> Akash’Flora

 

Adding NPCs into this we have 

 

Rooftops - Armoured Spider Snipers

Park - Spider-bots, Spider Mothership

Akash’Flora - Harvester drones, hostages, Argent Adept.

 

Now for character movement

 

Legacy has a movement of two in the current scene, we know he can fly fast enough to cover the ground between Akash and the rooftops in one panel.

Absolute Zero and Bunker have a movement of one, Bunker is basically an armoured tank and AZ still needs to dodge around things on the ground, so it would take them both 2 rounds to move between the rooftops and akash.

Unity and Wraith have a base movement of 1 but can easily increase it with creative uses of their powers.

And Tachyon has speed ∞ within the scene due to her speedster archetype.

 

as for range, I just declare everything in the same area as close by.
