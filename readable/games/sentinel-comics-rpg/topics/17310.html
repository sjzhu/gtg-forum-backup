Just received my latest issue of GTM, and was at first disappointed that it didn’t have the Cthulhu Wars promo mini included.

Then I got to the middle of the mag and found a full-size pull-out of the starter kit’s Tachyon booklet and my heart soared.

I’m hoping the four-page fold-out style is in the final release for all the hero booklets, because it is way better organized than the stapled booklet that was at Gen Con. Instead of flipping pages back and forth to figure out what is being referenced, the info flows and makes sense because it is right next to the page to which the info is referring. Love when design form follows function.

If you haven’t gotten yourself to a demo at a convention, check to see if your FLGS has any copies of GTM in stock. There’s also a four page article with FF artwork!
