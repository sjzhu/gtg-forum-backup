I’m not a backer either, but I’ll do my best:

1) Looks to me like that’s intended to be one book: the Diamond Book Of Monsters, which I would guess to be a supernatural-focused monster manual, plus some adventures.

2) I don’t recall if Time-Slinger is a member of any particular team, but yes the Sentinels of Freedom is a successor team to the Freedom Five. Heritage is still involved as something of a mentor. His character sheet would certainly be different, as he has changed his role (semi-retiring, as you said) and passed over the torch of Legacy.

3) The way they’re presented here, Fright Train and Ermine might be intended to be used together. That said, you can change things, retool characters and adventures to your heart’s delight. If you don’t like Fright Train, you can always find Ermine someone else to hang out with - maybe some villain left behind by the mist-portals, maybe just The Hippo...whatever you prefer.
