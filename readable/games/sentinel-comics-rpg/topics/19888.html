I've been running some games via Roll20 using your character sheet and we are really loving it! Thanks so much for creating this! I don't know if you are continuing to work on it, but I thought I would add some comments here:

- It would be really cool if you could link your abilities into the chat so that everybody could see them. So when you use "Flying Smash" the full text of the ability goes straight into the chat. That would be a cool setup, both for players than use minions as well as for the GM's minions. That way, "Khopesh Slash: This minion gets +1 to attack rolls" feels a lot more like a Khopesh Slash than just a +1 bonus. 

All of these things can be managed through roll20 already, but they would be great improvements to the sheet.

Hey everyone who’s using my Roll20 implementation of the character sheet. I wanted to let you know that I just updated the sheet with a couple new features. I had noticed that I never made the sheet compatible with Archetype #19: Divided, so I decided to remedy that.

At the bottom of the Forms/Modes tab, there is now a “Divided Options” section, and it has 3 parts:

  • Selecting either of the Forms adds a Toggle to the top of the Abilities Tab to switch between them. This toggle is an arrow that points to the Form you are in, as well as controlling the other 2 features.
  • There is space to fill in dice for 2 separate Personalities. (If you decide to stick with one, just fill them identically)
  • There is a checkbox for Divided characters that roll 2 Powers in one form and 2 Qualities in the other.

That last part doesn’t combine well with the other Forms/Modes options. If someone decides to play a Divided Form-Changer (Why?), or finds another way to use both options, contact me. I’m sure we’ll be able to find a way to make the sheet work for you.
