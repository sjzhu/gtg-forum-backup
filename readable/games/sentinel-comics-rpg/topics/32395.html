First actual play session is up already.

Like I suspected, having an entire team where everyone could take the very strong ally-defense/minion Redirect reaction presents some issues, and similarly group access to Unflagging makes penalties quite a bit weaker than the norm. At least not everyone took either ability, but there’s at least two copies of each. Universal Experimentation as a power source is thematic as it gets for this campaign, but there just aren’t enough options to avoid significant overlap in ability choices - at least not by the book. Adding another 2-3 Yellow and 1-2 Green abilities would probably alleviate a lot of that.
