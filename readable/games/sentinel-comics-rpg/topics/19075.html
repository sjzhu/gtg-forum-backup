Sorry, noob question, what’s the difference between super sentai and kamen rider?

Pit bluntly as possible.

Super Sentai/Power Rangers: Focuses on team dynamics and always always always has a huge monster fight at end of almost every episode. The plots tend to range from serious to just plain silly.

Kamen Rider (English Translation Masked Rider) Technically KR was about a year before Super Sentai(Sentai meaning Team) and Sentai is based off Kamen Rider to appeal to kids better. Rider focuses on one hero they may get friends that they band together with or villains(who may or may not join heroes after so n so amount of time being evil) and those friends may be important but they tend to die near climax or lose their powers. 

 

I feel that Super Sentai/Power Rangers would easily devolve into everyone having the same power set but one or two differences. Whilst Kamen Rider could have each hero be similar in looks and power origins but could have their own unique ability list.

VGBM:

Sorry, noob question, what’s the difference between super sentai and kamen rider?

 

So, at their heart, they are both “Tokusatsu” shows, which roughly translated means ‘special effects,’ but it’s more of a genre in Japan.  Dudes in silly costumes doing faux martial arts while fighting a villian of the week whereas a bigger villian behind the scenes is trying to accomplish something bad (almost always involving taking over/destroying the earth.)  Each season focuses on a new set of characters.

The U.S. translated a slew of them in the 90’s using the special FX footage but filming with American actors and modifying (or sometimes fabricating) the scripts.  Power rangers was the first (loosly based on Kyōryū Sentai Zyuranger.)  but after the success of that, there were others.  Masked Rider was based on Kamin Rider, Big Bad Beatle Borgs (Juukkou B-Fighter), the list goes on.

I’m not a huge Kamen Rider fan, but the biggest difference is that a super Sentai series focuses on a team (usually three or five people, usually expanding through the series to five to six) and they all have similar costumes.  Kamen Rider is usually about a single superhero who maybe sometimes gets one or two allies.  Sometimes Kamen Rider tends to be a bit more mature and darker in its themes . . . but not always.

I actually Disagree with Anubis that a Super Sentai themed game would be too same-ey, as Christopher and Adam have gone on record saying you can build similar characters in so many different ways that you could really have fun with it . . . choosing different abilities and different weapons and such.  Sure their costumes would be the same, but, like, you’re in a theater-of-the-mind RPG so that doesn’t really matter?  And with the introduction of Civilian powers that crop up in some seasons ( https://powerrangers.fandom.com/wiki/Category:PR_Rangers_with_Special_Abilities ), you could even have different powers.  Mind you , I also think a Kamen Rider based RPG could work, the two are not mutually exclusive.
