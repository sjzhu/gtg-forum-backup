It really depends on how you decide to do things.

Given the structure of the dice pools one makes (Power + Quality + Status), if one has the right powers, getting up close and personal with a flyer isn't that hard.  Leaping is a great way to reach a flying target (it works for the Hulk at least), but Agility and even Speed have been used by various heroes and villains in comics to launch themselves into the air.  Using the Acrobatics quality with a more offensive power could also work.

And that isn't the end of the possibilities.  Have a super strong character?  Have them Boost your close combat character by throwing them at an airborne opponent... "Fastball Special" style!.

In short, the flexibility of the game design makes it easy to justify how almost any character can attack any opponent.  All one really needs is a little creativity.
