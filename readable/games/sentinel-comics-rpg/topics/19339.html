The one area of Hero Character Creation I find myself stumbling around is the decision that certain Principles are only available if you pick a certain Background or Archetype. For example, if you have a Military background, you should have an Ideals Principle. Why not an Expertise or Responsibilities Principle? The idea that choosing Option A also locks you into Option B seems to run counter to everything else about this game, with its free form Build It How You Want it mentality.

Any issues of game balance that I am overlooking here?
