Hi, everyone!

I’ve been working on a fan supplement for a while about playing as supervillains in Sentinel Comics RPG, and in honor of the longest night of the year I’ve finally finished putting it together!

This book contains a set of entirely new Backgrounds, Power Sources, Archetypes, Personalities, and Principles for use in creating supervillains, especially those appropriate to the Silver and Bronze ages of comics. It can be mixed and matched with character creation steps in the core Sentinel Comics rulebook, or used to create thorough villains ready for trouble. The book also contains some advice on what sort of a supervillain game to run, and a slightly tweaked version of Sentinel Comics’ Hero Points to help your villains slip away from danger.
