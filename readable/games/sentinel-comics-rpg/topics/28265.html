I guess it depends. Flavor wise the ones the only one you haven’t mentioned that could work is Deep Space Knowledge (for those flying spaceships), Creativity (daredevil type pilots) , and Self-Discipline (remaining cool under pressure of a dogfight)

As for a background for pilot, I don’t think you can wrong with any number of them. In addition to the two ou mention I could see reasons for a pilot to have a background of Upper Class (you were born in a family that had money to let you do this for fun), Struggling (Maybe you were a pilot before things got really bad), tragic (This can be made to fit anything before tragedy struck) , Performer (you’re a stunt pilot) , Retired (this was a skill you had before retiring), criminal and former villain (Being bad doesn’t exclude you from flying a plane), Exile ( This could’ve been your thing before being forced out), Interstellar(you’re an alien flying spaceships), dynasty (flying planes was a thing your family did), Otherwordly (your more human side knows how to fly a plane), and Created (you were made to be chauffeur and flying a plane was part of that)
