Better yet, buy a few binder sleeves from an office supply store (or a big-box store).  Slide those character sheets into the binder sleeve, and you now have a surface usable with dry/wet erase markers, with no damage to the sheets themselves.

It's what I do with all my demo characters and pretty much every convention game I run.
