Animal Control: Your summoned minion is one from the environment that you are controlling for the scene duration

Cold/Electricity/Fire/Weather: You summon an elemental from the environment to aid you for the scene duration

Materials: You temporarily bind a nature spirit into a form of some material, enabling it to assist you for the scene duration.

Edit: I thought you were referring specifically to the Nature Power Source, but it gives you a couple of options to work with. There's also the Minion-Maker Archetype, or the Summoned Allies Red Zone ability. Summoned Allies comes from the Elemental/Energy, Materials, Psychic, and Self Control Powers.
