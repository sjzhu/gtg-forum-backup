So I don’t know what abilities they’ll have till I read Char Creation chapter but this is a WIP of what I have so far for my heroes. 

 

Jack of Aces

 

Background: Performer

Power Source: Sorcery

Synopsis: Alex Marquis was a famous poker player winning millions and living rather comfortably in Las Vegas. However, he entered a two-player game with a mysterious woman and lost. He was unaware of the bet he had made when he entered the game. Now cursed with the powers of magic Alex uses his magic (even creating his outfit from magic) to defend Las Vegas from the forces of supernatural evil. 

Fighting Style: Alex as the mage Jack of Aces uses his magic to attack rapid fire at enemies with various magical poker cards he creates from thin air, he also imbues his cards (often aces hence his hero name) with debuffs that damage the enemy and weakens them at the same time. Alex is a fairly decent mage and can fight without cards he just likes to out of aesthetics. 

 

Masked Sorcerer

Background: Performer

Power Sorce: Sorcery

Synopsis: Nathan is a popular actor for the Saturday morning tv show Masked Sorcerer. As an action hero, he does a lot of stunts and fight scenes thus the call to being a real superhero wasn’t something he had trouble to adjusting. By some strange coincidence his real powers are identical to his character on the show and thus he knows already how to utilize them to beat real monsters. 

Fighting Style: Nathan has access to several transformations called Styles and uses them to combat his enemies. Each Style has it’s own unique elemental magic that he utilizes to overcome any situation. His favorite is Wind but he also uses Water often as well he also has a 3rd form Earth but only uses it when the going gets really rough.
