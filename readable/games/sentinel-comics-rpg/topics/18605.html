#20

They broadcast 2 mini-campaigns where folks played original characters. There's a step by step process, and you have the option to roll randomly or pick items at each step. It's very simple. You pick/roll what you were before becoming a superhero (nerdy high school kid, billionaire genius philanthropist, etc) which determines some of your Qualities. Then you pick/roll an origin (bitten by radioactive spider, struck by lightning during a particle accelerator accident) and archetype (speedster, blaster, shapechanger) which determine what sort of powers (and some more Qualities) you have. If you check GTG's YouTube page, you can see both campaigns character creation episodes.
