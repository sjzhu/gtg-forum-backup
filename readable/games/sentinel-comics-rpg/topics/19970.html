It's not too bad using the Guided method.  Just have people roll and tell them what their options are.  Copying and pasting just those bits should be considered fair use over a private medium.  Just don't post it to a public Discord or anything.  Constructed would be a bit harder, they'd have to tell you what they want and you'd have to help choose what seems to fit best.
