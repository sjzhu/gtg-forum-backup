If it helps, keep in mind the difference between boosts/hinders and bonuses/penalties. A boost or hinder is an action that places a bonus or penalty on one or more targets. So while a villain may be able hinder multiple heroes at once, each hero gets their own individual penalty applied to them, and Turn the Tables explicitely states that it can turn penalties into bonuses, not hinders into boosts.

The other thing to consider is that Turn the Tables is an action- you have to burn an entire turn to change a hinder into a boost or vice versa.  It does work with persistant/exclusive boosts (in that it makes them persistant/exlusive hinders), which is one of the reasons why it is terrifying in the hands of a villain- it can be used to completely negate something that a hero might be relying on.  The same could be said in the hands of a hero- there are villains that specialize in loading players down with debuffs, and Turn the Tables can provide a much needed advantage to a friend... provided you are willing to (and I can't stress this enough) burn your ENTIRE turn to change it. It also (as has been eloquently said by Powerhound), only works on one target at a time. 

It's not broken in any way, shape, or form.  GM's know that the players have this ability- its not like it is a secret- and there are a LOT of easy counters to it.  The most obvious one is to disrupt their turn order- Turn the Tables is only really effective if the players can maximize the advantage that they are getting out of it, and since the player who Turns the Tables has to burn their entire turn to do so, they need their teammates to take advantage of it.  I'm not a particularly adversarial GM, so I always applaud my players doing this kind of thing.  It rewards teamwork and lateral thinking, and that tends to lead to a better story.  In the hands of a villain, its the same principle- they have to burn their entire turn to Turn the Tables, so work around it. If they keep doing it, then you'll need to work around it, but they can't attack you if all they are doing is changing around buffs/debuffs.
