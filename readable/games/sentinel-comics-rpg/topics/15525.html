eviltoon asked Christopher about the RPG at the meet and greet, and Christopher said that post OblivAeon is when he will be working on the RPG.  The demo was (I believe) pretty much the exact same demo they've been running.  The system, a few characters and a mission.  There's a whole lot more needed before they have it ready for kickstarter.

 

We got some tidbits on the story:

When OblivAeon is defeated the timelines stabilize into 2 lines.  One turns into the Tactics timeline (after 5 years) and the other becomes the RPG, after only a short while.  Apparently time travel and hopping between the lines is no longer possible.

I'm really interested to see what caused the split and what made them initially different, but that's plot and Adam seems more inclined to talk about the characters than the evnts.

SRD is "System Reference Document". Sorry, that's super RPG geek terminology to just assume and type out-- it's definitely a bit deeper a geek dive than many have done in that realm. System means, broadly, 'how the game works', so an SRD is a 'document for the rules of the game'. So the D&D core book would be the SRD for D&D, in a greater sense, but it usually means stripped down to the bare bones. No flavor text or anything-- just the rules and nothing but the rules.
