Heyo!
I've been trying to port all of my characters for a world building project into SCRPG. The tone and scope of the SC verse fits my world really well and going through the full character creation process helps me flush them out.

However, I have one villain whose power set doesn't translate as well.

Chelsea Glasgow (Rictus) amplifies the powers of others, causing them to spike out of control. Affected heros may burn themselves out or hurt their teammates. If this were D&D, she would temporarily turn normal casters into wild magic casters. My first thought was to craft custom abilities like, "Boost multiple targets using your Max die. If the targets roll doubles while this bonus is active, they must remove the bonus and take a minor twist. Their actions succeed or fail as normal.", but I don't know if that level of chaos would break the game.

What I have so far:

  • Approach: Disruptive (Enraging Touch and Heedless Explosion)
  • Archetype: Guerrilla (Even Odds and Fighting Rhythm)
  • Powers: Psychic Feedback (custom), Illusions, Suggestion and Telepathy.
  • Qualities: Magical Lore, Conviction, Alterness and Means To The End (roleplaying).
  • Upgrade: Power Dampening Field (roleplayed as the opposite)
  • Mastery: Total Chaos

I like where the Qualities are, but most of the powers feel useless and tacked on. The abilities are nice, but don't fit the character 100%.

I'm also having issues building a medic hero, but that's for later.

Please for advice?

Hm... Well, there's nothing saying that a minor villain can't impact the Environment around them. I'd suggest building an environment for her encounters, reflecting the ambient power spikes that she generates. The twists from the environment could easily reflect that aspect of her powers, maybe something like:

Forced Feedback: Boost one hero using the Max Die, then that hero loses health equal to the size of that bonus plus the Min die.

Or, if there's a particular powered individual around, you could use the effects of her powers on that individual to guide environment twists.

 

Ultimately though it's up to you.
