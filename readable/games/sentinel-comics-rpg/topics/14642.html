Outstanding - curiosity about this had been killing me!

 

So, here's what I caught as new information beyond what we saw in the old thread:

  • They have embraced the rule that you can use Kickers of a different color for a Minor/Major Cost.

  • Science is now a Power, not a Quality.  Tachyon added "Physics" to take its place as her d12 Quality, but Unity got some stranger changes - she has no Science, but did add Banter and Creativity.  Guys... I think Unity might be neglecting the more formal duties of her internship.

  • On the plus side, "Technopath" gets called out as a Power in addition to Ferrokinesis.

  • It may no longer be possible to take Defense as a reaction by taking a Minor Cost.  The possibility is never mentioned.

  • The Megalopolis Stress Track was 12 boxes, 4 of each color.  What we had before on Environment Stress was 3G-XY-3R, so it seems like more Green and Red boxes are on the table.

  • The GM allowed Create A Bonus to be split among multiple recipients.  This did not appear to be official.

Good times.  The new information here is a little more subtle than last time, but there's some good stuff to find:

  • Using a back issue to max out a die is a choice you make after rolling.  It would appear that it also means you use your Max die for the action, even if you would otherwise use your Min die - that's a BIG buff.
  • Also, I don't recall hearing previously what a big deal it is to roll triples - you get a Spotlight Moment and an automatic success.
  • It's the PCs, not the GM, who decides whether the Minor/Major Costs are paid as Hero Costs or Environment Costs, subject to things making narrative sense.
