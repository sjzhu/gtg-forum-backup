Given the evidence here and here, we are dire need of a Bad Ideas thread.  As such, I have created one.

 

The purpose of the Bad Ideas Thread is to share your ridiculous, stupid, and/or horrifying character ideas.  Anything from bad puns and obvious ripoffs to the downright horrifying.  That said, no idea is too good for the thread!

 

And since one person's trash is another one's treasure, let's make the default assumption that all characters posted here are free for adoption unless specified otherwise.

 

 

Try to keep the ideas PG13 and less than Biomancer levels of creepy.

 

Some ideas to get us started off:

Lima
An Omega whose appearance is similar to the later stages of the condition shown in A Bad Case of Stripes.

 

 Half-blood
Half-blood is an Flying Brick (a la Legacy) with invisibility - but only down to the waist. Below the waist they're the same baseline human that they were before they manifested.
 
The original inspiration calls for the them to also be capable of shrinking  (down to the waist) . But I couldn't think of a way for that not to be... horrible... so I removed it.

A long while back my RP group had once planned to play a game set in the Fallout universe which would use its radiation-drenched status as a flimsy excuse to have people who had Mystery Men-level lame superpowers.

I came up with the idea of a PC who was sort of the opposite of Absolute Zero: He was an engineer who at one point had accidentally absorbed a massive amount of radiation that left him physically unharmed but now basically a walking source of radiation emission. So he wore a suit (and slept in a lead lined tent) not to keep the rest of the world out, but to keep himself in for the sake of everyone else.

The other was a lady player who came up with the idea of a PC who had chameleon skin which could mimic everything around her and rendered her skin effectively invisible... so long as she wasn't wearing any clothes over it, was the kicker.

(There also was a PC who had very uncanny both bad and good luck and mistakenly thought he had psychic powers because of it, but that seems rather too similar to Setback and Kismet.)

I also once had a D&D campaign where I played a fighter who had been specifically engineered to be a tank drawing aggro/danger for an army, so while she had the upside of rapid healing and being revived any time she was mortally wounded, she also had the downside of being a magnet for any sort of ranged projectile or otherwise free-swinging object in the area. And I do mean anything; as in stuff like if you sneezed in her direction, the resulting goo would end up flying at her. If you were flailing your hands gesturing while talking your hands would be drawn to accidentally hit her if she was close enough. Etc.

***

In more Sentinels-specific ideas, I submit for the cause this thread that derailed into people listing ideas for ridiculous or weird Virtuoso instruments. (I do remember coming up with weird Bard ideas was a cherished pasttime in my old RP group.)

Of my favorite 3 character concepts one of them deffinately belongs here. He's also the only one who is actually finished (but for rules stuff) because i'm still trying to figure out Jasmine. The second one i'm still waiting on the Tempest episode for more info on how Maerynian's powers work & how consistant they are. Any way, on to my concept

 

Robert 'Robbie' Brooker Jr. is from the backwoods foothills area south of Rook City, he lives in a rural community where most people know everyone. He gained powers just before graduation when a freind jokingly pushed him into a rainwater tub at his grandfathers old still. He got stuck & nearly drowned but there we no hard feelings. They didn't know it also contained Isoflux Alpha which after his time in the tub had thoroughly suffused his entire body.

Now Robbie is an Omega who is able to absorb and then redirect light energy to fire energy blasts, heat or burn things, manipulate the way light moves to make himself or other things invisible or disorient his enemies, Fly, and speed his healing. His first problem is that he is light powered so he can't opperate for long in complete darkness.

"But if that's his only weakness he would be near Citizen Dawn's level!" I hear you protest and you would be correct, except for his second weakness

 

The light also has an effect on him very similar to Alchohol.

 

Yep, if he goes out into light of any kind, the source of his power, he starts getting drunk and doesn't stop until he expends all the energy he's absorbed, no storing energy for him. The more light on him the quicker he gets powered up for impressive effects and the quicker he gets drunk. In daylight he wears a long brown coat(cause everyone wants a billowy coat or cape or something) long gloves, collared shirt, hiking boots, dark pants and his grandfather's wide brim stetson. All this is not just his style but is required if he needs to be coherent in daylight. A half hour in direct sunlight in a normal T Shirt with no hat leaves him in a state similar to someone at the legal limit.  His powers so overwhelming that he doesn't usually pull out his Sleveless hero costume(similar to Setback's) until after dark to do hero work because he can only opperate effectively at night. Well, Mostly.

 

If he has to pull out the stops to deal with anything bigger than a mugging or some "idjit" trying to rob one of the small banks in town, he will probably miss at least once, leaving creaters in walls or setting fire to a tree or something. Being drunk is not conducive to accurate energy blasts. It is also not conductive to flight, if you've tried those drunk driving simulators you know how difficult that is! Now try flying at up to barely sub-sonic speeds while blitzed out of your skull. He has spent a couple days sitting in the sun to repair broken bones after a relatively high speed collision with walls around town. There are a couple places with loony toon style imprints of him in alleyway walls.

 

Whenever he deals with local law enforcement he tends to seem either Still somewhat drunk, or hung over after expending all the stored up power he can. Luckily he is a cheerful and kind drunk, constantly throwing out "I love you"s and "Your great"s as well as a gentle happy glow from any left over energy he has but can't quite contain because he's drunk. Sherrif Dougie from his hometown has said "I'd love havin' 'im round, ifn he didn' seem like a Mountain Dew runner who couldn' stay out o his own stock." Between that and the drunkenness and the opporating at night, and the folk heroes from the area he lives in (to which he is unknowingly related) his superhero identity could only have been one thing, even if he was too drunk to come up with it on his own.

 

Moonshine!

After remembering that the RPG suggests that one can actually deal damage by taunting your enemies and hurting them psychologically, the following sprang to mind.

Rachel Ho was raised by a Jewish mother and a Chinese grandmother.  Between the two of them, she learned every parenting trick traditional to both cultures and was more than prepared when she became a mother herself.  Unfortunately, her husband and children were killed in the OblivAeon attack.  Rachel herself was critically injured, but she almost would rather have died.  Her loss, combined with exposure to the massive cosmic energies unleashed in that battle, caused her pent-up passive-aggressive mothering style to grow to arms-grade power.  As The Ultimom, she wields guilt, shame, and adherence to tradition to lethal effect, subduing all who oppose her tiniest whim.

During a defeat in a sinister genetic modification laboratory, The Ultimom fell into a vat of volatile DNA and became augmented with the genes of a great cat.  Adding feline strength, speed, and claws to her psychological weapons, she is now known as Tiger Mom.
