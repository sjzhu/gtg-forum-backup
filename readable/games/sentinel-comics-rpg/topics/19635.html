The Minion Maker archetype, as part of character generation selects a number of minion forms which they give to minions they make at time of creation.

My question is simple: If a Minion Maker takes the Red Zone ability Summoned Allies, can they apply Minion Forms to the minions they make using the Summoned Allies Red Zone ability?

 

Summoned Allies: Use [power] and create a number of minions equal to your Mid die. Choose whether they all can Attack, Defend, Boost, Hinder, or Overcome. They all act at the start of your turn.

 

I've been running it at my table that they can, provided that they can pay the normal cost to do so for each minion they apply a form to.
