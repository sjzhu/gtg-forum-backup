In October Megalopolis is setting up for a grand Halloween festival that'll last from the 10th till October 31st however a slew of horrible undead arrive to wreak havoc. At first, people assume it's just vandals wreaking havoc (not the ones from Stolen Legacy) however as the police try to arrest these party crashers it appears they are not thugs in costumes! Thus a new supervillain has arrived. The Sinister Lord of Death. With his diabolical powers to raise the dead, the heroes must band together to stop him before he turns the Halloween Festival into a true Monster Mash. 

The Lord of Death: Grimmalkin: Archetype: Minon Maker. Powers: Necromancy, Animation, Infernal. Qualities: Magical Lore, Anatomy, Dark Magic.  

Looks: A dark red headed boy about 5 feet tall and wears a dark purple cloak with a flame motif and a large skull on the back. Wears fingerless gloves with a skeleton motif and wears a mask only covering nose and eyes with a skull theme.  

Motivations: Mayhem for sake of mayhem, Admiring his summons. 

 

The six issues would have this rough concept once rules are out might make Google docs to list all this.

Dense Macabre (Collection name) The heroes are tasked with battling Grimmalkin an evil necromancer hellbent on causing as much chaos as possible. A true villain who has no motive other than watching the mass destruction his army causes. The heroes must battle undead from skeletons and zombies to evil spirits as they attempt to stop Grimmalkin from opening the gates to The River Styx and ending the world.
