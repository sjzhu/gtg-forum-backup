SIDEKICKS - [Season 2] Ep. 11

The story of the sidekick's journey to becoming a true hero!

I will not claim that this webcomic is a must-read by any means (although it did show steady improvement over time, especially in the fight “choreography”) but I will say this for it: The linked issue has one of the most over the top examples of an “Attack multiple targets…” ability in use you’re ever going to see. It benefits from the scrolling webcomic format that you couldn’t do as smoothly in conventional print book and would probably be an overlong CGI fest in video, but even with that cheat it’s worth a peep.

To get to the good stuff, scroll down a bit over halfway to where you hit the marching ranks of ranked-up “Alex” minions (which goes on for a bit itself, it’s hard to miss by accident) and just keep going, the action starts soon.

Deserves to be a meme, really. “This is what your minion sweeper ability looks like when your effect die after mods is a thirteen.”
