Welcome to the forums, cynlover01!

Yeah, as Omegatron points out, it’s about the application used to open/edit it. When you open it “without downloading it”, it’s loaded into your browser (e.g., Edge or Chrome). Then once you have it locally and you open it, it’s probably in something like Adobe Acrobat Reader, the full version of Adobe Acrobat, or (as Omegatron has) Foxit PDF Reader. The issue is with the application’s interpretation of the text fields.

I believe, though, that if you edit it appropriately there then open the local file with your browser, it should show up correctly?
