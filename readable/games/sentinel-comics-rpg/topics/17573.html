I was quite surprised to see that all of the Freedom Five (along with some of the other heroes and villains) have new costumes in the RPG. Epsecially since with the Freedom Five variant cards being released recently (ish), I expected the F5 to be dressed in those.

What're everyone's thoughts on the new costumes? Favorites/least?

I personally love Absolute Zero's new costume the best just on pure style compared to his old ones, and Bunker is also pretty high up on my list. Maybe I just like power armor too much. Bunker's look is REALLY divergent from how he was before, but his suits all look pretty different from each other, and it's got a very heroic "shining armor" look to it.

My least favorite is probably the new version of Anubis. He's got somewhat of a mummy/death vibe to him which I dig, but I honestly couldn't tell who he was supposed to be when I saw the art without a label. The lack of any sort of jackal motif is kind of weird. I thought Anubis had a real jackal head and not even a costume in the original card from the Tomb, similar to how Tefnut, Geb, Isis, and Shu all have extreme physical mutations.
