Thanks for watching (or listening), everyone.  I certainly had an amazing time playing Mishap, and I hope that I can bring her back someday to break even more things.  

Here are some notable things I've learned over the course of season 2*

  • Offer peace.  If rejected, offer crowbar. 
  • If someone offers you incredible weapons, you should definitely break them.  It's probably a trap.
  • Getting major twists to succeed at more or less irrelevant overcomes works out everytime.  
  • Don't accidentally kill a bunch of people. 
  • No matter what, just power on through. 
  • Figuring out what is going on is overrated.   

*None of these things apply, and will almost certainly get you killed, if Adam or Christopher is the GM.

In all seriousness, playing this game taught me a HUGE amount about team synergy and thinking about complementary powers during creation, instead of designing everything in a vaccuum.  We were all very punchy heroes, with very little that could help us defend, sustain, or deal with ranged stuff... and none of us had any sort of investigative or stealth capabilities.  While I don't really regret any of the choices we made (even breaking the weapons!), there are DEFINITELY things I'd do differently if I was creating Mishap knowing all of this!  If I ever get to play her again, she'll definitely have a few new tricks. 

From all of the Escalators, thank you, and remember the wisdom passed down from the greatest rock muse of all time... "Somebody once told me..."   

Mishap, Team Leader of the Escalators
