Just as I am playing around with character creation, I thought I would share some here. This is John Bull, Britain's cyborg Super Soldier! Haven't written up his bio yet, but Bradley, a popular British soldier, was severely injured in a bomb attack in Afghanistan, leaving him with both legs and an arm amputated. The British government used an experimental nanobot cyborg surgery to restore his limbs, granting him incredible physical prowess. However, when his medical facility  was attacked by enemy forces, the secrets to his incredible technology have now been lost.
