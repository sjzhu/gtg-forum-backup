Imagine a Singular Discord where Players and GMs could come together to play Sentinel Comics RPG in a Play-By-Post setting! Now imagine that all of the games take place in the same instance of the Sentinel Comics world! 

Essentially I wanted to create a Play By Post Hub where GMs could run 1-3 Scene Adventures called "missions". Players could view available missions and apply for one and join-up! Each Player would partake in the same Mission until it's conclusion before setting off to the next adventure, however GMs could run multiple missions. At the conclusion of Missions the GMs would report to a Moderator or Admin- Who Played and if the Players succeeded. Hero Players would receive 2 Fame for Success and 1 Fame for Failure. Villain Players would receive 2 Infamy for Success and 1 Infamy for Failure. Fame and Infamy would unlock certain Perks as Players accrued more and more. 

The next fun Idea for the server is that Players could form Teams. People they like to play with, friends, etc. Every Hero and Villain team would have it's own Fame/Infamy, Role in Discord, and Text Channel in the Server. Teams would be like mini-guilds! There would be perks for players on highly Famous or Infamous teams if they were playing with other members of the team. 

Just a few ideas. I would like to get some GMs and Players together to see if we could make it happen. I have seen som PBP posts here and maybe if we just moved all our games to the same community we could run a sort of Living RPG Setting for SCRPG.
