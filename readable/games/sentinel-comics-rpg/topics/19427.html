I think many people are holding off on writing adventures for the time being, due to the lack of rules for villain creation (including lieutenant and minion creation).  Also, I'm sure that a number of other GMs are waiting for additional information on how to use the scene tracker effectively, as well as other system-specific nuances that would make adventure writing much easier.

While I'd love to see a broad licensing, I think the D&D Community Content model is not a good one. Excessive percentages, lots of low quality content, no route to the FLGS, forfeiture of copyright rights. It's one thing to get a decent paycheck for a work for hire as a freelancer, it's another entirely to forfeit the IP for potentially trivial cash. Futher, the way the license is set up, it's very possible that they could pull a GW and go after fans' personal websites.

I do think non-onerous licensing is a good thing, even with a fee... but most of the Community Content licenses have onerous provisions.
