OK, since I was invited to go first, here we go - even though I only promise mediocrity.  This was an Environment used to represent one of the hero's powers going out of control, causing small black holes to open.  The Action took place iin a vacant lot behind some tenement buildings.  This is one of the few Environments I designed completely from scratch.

AURA's POWERS ARE OUT OF CONTROL!

Black Holes Appearing d12

Things Flying Everywhere d8

Aura is stressed out d6

GREEN

Minor Twists

Gravitational Forces: Hinder all with the Min die.

Flying Street Debris: Attack 1 target with Mid die.

News Crew Arrives on Scene: Challenge (1 success needed).

Major Twist

"Where'd the flying van come from?": Add 1 success needed to "Free First Responders" (a Challenge already in the scene)

YELLOW

Minor Twists

Flying Car!: Attack 2 targets with Mid die,

A Hurricane of Flying Debris: Hinder all with the Min die.  (I never realized that this is the exact same as a Green Twist)

More Innocent People Caught in Black Holes: Challenge (2 successes; 2 Round timer or they die).

Major Twist

More Holes Opening!: Advance Scene Tracker.

RED

Minor Twists

Torn Apart by a Black Hole: Attack 1 target with Max+Min

Torn Between Two Black Holes: Attack 1 target with Max die and Hinder with Mid die.  This penalty is Persistent.

Gas Main Exposed Near a Flame: Challenge (2 successes, 2 Round timer) + Hinder 1 target with Max die.  (Failure: lots of collateral damage.)

Major Twist

Buildings Start to Fall Down: Attack all with Max die.

The villain that the heroes are fighting is a sentient computer virus from SPACE and it's been driven more than a little insane. To the point where it propogates and communicates using internet memes, logical fallacies, and reaction images, as opposed to any actual language.

The environment is the inside of the network that it calls home, as the heroes experience it once {technobabble} puts them inside of it so that they can actually have an effect on it. Unfortunately, since the network is home to the Villain, it's basically an echo chamber for their insanity. This takes a few different forms, but generally it's not pretty for the heroes.
