Those words are just suggestion to get you thinking about how to fill out power and Qualities. The game is more describing your actions, finding a Power Quality that fit what you say and then doing it (rolling dice). I recommend you have an idea of what kind of Hero you want to make and can picture it in your mind. Thinking about what actions it would perform and what choices it will do, that is what is important. If you can not I am sure the core book would help with that. If the Core book is written as nicely as the starter kit it will be easy for new people to pick it up.

Being able to come up with Ideas and rationalize it with comic book logic is more important. If anyone gets into the RPG I suggest using risky actions. They can make for some interesting situations.

 

I hope it comes out soon, not sure how long the RPG can stay without a core book. How many people will just make up their own character creation and just run it (That is me BTW). But a core book would be nice to have. Having some templet villains and environments would be every so nice.
