It's actually on page 248 Alternate Rewards. Basically using your hero points for a Minion of size based on points spent - 2 points = d4, 3 = d6, 4 = d8, 5 = d10. Minion acts at the start of your turn as normal, and if it survives a scene, it can recover one die size automatically. If it gets knocked out, it's out for the session.
