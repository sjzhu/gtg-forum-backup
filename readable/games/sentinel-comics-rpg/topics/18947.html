From what I know (because My wife is still fleshing her history out) Zara inherited her grandmothers cloak, which has given her the magic abilities to influence and dazzle people towards the greater good, Somewhat like charming them.  Inside of her magic cloak she carries a extensive collection of gadgets, in which she has solutions for all kinds of conundrums. (Very much like the wrath in that regard)

Floodgates ability is that he constantly charges kinetic energy. Then he is able to create energy “force fields” with it. He can also turn this energy into orbs in which he can throw or utilize for who knows what. They don’t last long when removed from his being, but are sustainable when he keeps them close. When he first started trying to save people he learned the hard way that his body itself is not super strong or tough, and crushed himself trying to save someone from an oncoming vehicle. After years of recovery, he was approached by a company willing to sponsor technology in support of the initiative to create more of the next generation of super heroes.  The company gifted him an exoskeleton that can keep his forcefields at bay when under pressure from crushing him, as well as providing some sweet body armor.

Sigh, mis-clicked and posted without my last pics.

Edit - may as well describe them a bit.

Walking Target was a security guard caught in some kind of lab explosion. He got some measure of super-strength out of the deal, but more importantly, is now extremely resistant to injury and so primarily focuses on tying up whatever big brawler types are around while the other heroes focus on bringing them down.

Candle-light is an inventor/chemist. She produced a chemical that, when aerosolized and burned, uses up more than the expected amount of oxygen while burning at a lower than expected temperature. That is, it can cause her foes to pass out without burning them too badly (when I came up with her character I was trying to find a way for a fire-blasting hero to also be non-lethal). She's got a variety of other devices she uses too - mostly the cloaking device (leaving her looking like a heat haze) and proximity mines full of her fire chemical.

Defibrillatrix was an automatic debribrillator that got some weird firmware upgrade that gave it sentience. Further upgrades gave her mobility and a larger range of first-aid techniques to make her an ambulatory ambulance. She still has the shock hands, though, which she isn't afraid of using offensively if necessary.

Which also gets me thinking of that friend’s main, the Gallopin’ Glacier - an Ice/Ice tanker who, along with the Walking Target were our super group’s “giga-tanks” as we both maxed out our respective defensive powers. WT’s was Invulnerability, which was based around getting hit, but not caring because everything was highly reduced (except for psychic attacks), but was also limited in that the power-set’s “taunt aura” basically only affected things right next to you. Ice tanks, by comparison have a very large taunt aura, but their primary defense centered on being hard to land hits on in the first place (being vulnerable to enemies who had high-accuracy/high-damage). As such, he was mostly on crowd-control duty while I tied up the boss. It was a good partnership.

Phantom5613:

A number of people in the KS campaign comments seem to want to bring their ‘City of’ characters back to life with SC.

Yes! I’m pretty sure Blasters, Scrappers, Masterminds, Stalkers, Brutes, and all the Epic Archetypes will be possible to convert completely. I’m not sure how much you can focus on support Abilities in SCRPG, so Defenders and Corruptors might be tougher to adapt. CC abilities don’t seem to be as common in the released pregen character sheets, so I feel like Controllers and Dominators might require a bit of homebrew mechanics to work. And Tankers seem to be thematically possible, though probably not nearly as durable as they were in CoH. I’m working on writing a guide for adapting CoH characters and NPCs for use with SCRPG rules, but that’s a ways out. I’m also working on adapting some of the story arcs from CoH for my gaming group, and maybe for others if there is interest. I can’t wait to be back in Paragon City!
