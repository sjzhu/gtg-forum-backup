We have all the publicly available information.  Back when this first started we got an extremely early alpha look, which was mostly Christopher putting the characters into a skeleton of the system that I assume was to give the RPG writers (who are not GtG) a sense of how the Multiverse should work and power sets for key characters.

That is out of date, as is a lot of the really early stuff that we gathered from the publicly available information.

 

It is key to remember that GtG is not the primary writer of the game, and the game system is not Christopher's.  This is a system already in existence that is re-skinning and being reworked to better fit Sentinel Comics.

The people in charge of it are good people, and from publicly available information there is collaboration going back and forth and testing from not us.

I assume we are being left out of the loop because OblivAeon is front and center job #1 Ultra right now.  We are all really excited to get to try the RPG out, but we aren't as important to that process as we are to OblivAeon, so I assume we won't see anything until it is ready to go, OblivAeon is finished and they say "here, try this and see what you think."

At that point we'll test the heck out of it from a Sentinels comics fan perspective.

 

Again this is all my opinion, and not official.  We know work is going into the RPG, we know we are busting our butts to get OblivAeon to the printers, we know that another company and other testers are heavily involved in the RPG process, and Christopher is involved as well, but not to the level he is on OblivAeon right now.

My thoughts are that the RPG guys know what they are doing, and Christopher gave them a lot of Sentinels comics story and an alpha build to work from, and communication since then.  The product that comes to us when OblivAeon is wrapped up shouldn't need a lot of balancing, it will benefit from our fan-first perspective.

I'm surprised that no one has said anything about the recent GTG newsletter that revealed the release of the Sentinels Comics RPG Starter Kit.

They talk about how the kit will be comprised of a sum total of thirteen booklets, the first being a 'how to play/how to run an adventure' guide for both players and GMs.

The next six booklets are pre-made character folios for the Freedom Five and Unity, and will give you all the info you need to learn how to play that character and how the character sheet info is relevant to the game.

The last six books will be episodic game adventures with an over-arching plot, picking up right in the middle of the aftermath of the SotM card game. Each will act as a complete issue of a comic book, each one introducing useful game mechanics.

The Sentinels Comics RPG Starter Kit will be for sale August 2017 for $19.95.
