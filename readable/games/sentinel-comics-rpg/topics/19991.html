I think it's exactly the other way around.  Had they done RPG books 6 years ago, it would have been at the expense of SotM, which was massively successful.  That would have sunk them.

Now, if they'd launched the RPG 3 years ago, instead of OblivAeon?  Maybe.  OblivAeon took way too long and my understanding is that it was primarily Spirit Island that kept them afloat while the core GtG team worked on OblivAeon.  But also 3 years ago wasn't the right time for the product.  Not only did we need the story of OblivAeon for the setting to make sense, I don't think the market for RPG's was in a good place then.  It was saturated with 5th Edition D&D and indies trying to compete with it.  IMHO there's more breathing room now.

I also don't think we'll see more than one book a year... but that's a good thing!  You don't want to flood your base with too many purchases in too short a time.  You do need to allow for proper proofreading, playtesting, and art development and direction.  Companies that push out too many cheap books too fast (looking at you, Green Ronin) publish a lot that's trash.
