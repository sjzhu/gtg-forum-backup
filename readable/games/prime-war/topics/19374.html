What am I, chopped liver? Surely I wrote something about this. Let's go see ...

https://greaterthangames.com/forum/topic/gencon-2019-19436

"I got a chance to play the co-op version of Prime War and enjoyed it. I have no idea if it will end up being a hit or not, but it seemed like a nice combination of easy enough to play and lots of interesting choices about how to play. Our scenario involved being sent to the Block to shut down the fighting between agents and inmates. A character has one card in play at a time, and it specifies which dice are used for defense as well as what action the character can take -- usually some combination of moving and attacking. After being used, a card is put into a recovery track (I'm getting the name wrong) and is put in different slots depending on the card, so some cards are returned faster than others. Cards have speeds on them, so can occur earlier or later in a turn."

My son and I played the Block co-op scenario at GenCon 2019.

I played Fanatic. He played Parse. Other folks played Writhe and Haka.

It's been many months ago now, so I don't remember the mechanics details well enough to describe them. However, I was really impressed by how much the characters really "felt like" the characters we know and love from SotM.

Fanatic's powers, many with the same names and similar effects, led her to dive into the fray and bust heads. Parse boosted everyone. Writhe seemed almost impossible to hit.

I left very excited about getting the finished version of the game. I had the impression they expected to have a near-finished product to play test at GenCon 2020, which now certainly won't happen. :(

Seems like my impression was overly optimistic, given the other posts I've been reading here, which makes me sad.

I've been impressed with the RPG, but honestly I don't have space in my life for an RPG. Too much effort required.

I'm hoping that Prime War will be a fresh way to play with my favorite characters and meet a few new ones.

They keep bringing it up on The Letters Page, so I'll keep hoping.
