Beacon is definitely more Progress-y than Legacy, IMO, which is part of why she changes her name.  If they split up, I definitely see Paul going with Preservation, especially in light of his becoming Heritage in the RPG.

Tachyon is certainly the Chaos Lord, all right.

I still see young Ra as Glory more than anything else.  Maybe Progress (he's forward-thinking and hero-worshipped Legacy), but probably not now that he's Ra.

AZ is probably Preservation.  Bunker, too, though I can see a character arc that sends him toward Conquest.

Not sure about Wraith.  She's just kind of desparately treading water.  She's fighting The Chairman and his Spites but she doesn't seem to have much of a plan beyond that.  She doesn't want to "preserve" Rook City--it's never been a good place--but she doesn't have any ideas how to "progress" it, either.  She's purely reactive.  I can actually see Conquest being an option--she's moving openly and wants to "win" by brute force, really.

Several of them would fight over Baron Blade.  Clearly Conquest (he wants to rule), but also Glory (he wants recognition) and Progress (he wants to move toward a technocratic future).  The idea of him being on the same team as Legacy is delicious.

Citizen Dawn is similar--Conquest and Glory both suit her well, as does Chaos to a degree.

Ambuscade has to be Chaos at this point.  Another time, maybe Glory, but not now.

Operative is Conquest here, for sure.

Unity could fit on a few teams.  Progress is a natural fit, but so is Glory.  She is finally being recognized, after all.

Visionary seems solidly Preservation to me.

Proletariat doesn't seem to have any driving force in Mist Storm.  Heck, in every story he's basically just obeying orders.  Chaos would work better than anything else for him (and we don't have many other good Chaos candidates).

Omnitron... doesn't really have a personality.  It wants to progress itself but doesn't have any overarching ideals.  It could be Conquest.

 

Now, all that said--even if there is a conversion kit, nothing says the heroes have to be on predetermined teams.  They have to work mechanically, they don't necessarily have to fit into the story.  And they could be alternate reality versions, not from Mist Storm.
