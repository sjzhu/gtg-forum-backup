I don't have the game yet, obviously, but many of the reviews complain about how much time is spent checking cards for effects which trigger during each of the phases of the game.  I plan on using these tokens to mark cards with phase-triggered effects, similar to the ones I use while playing SotM.  Having these types of tokens standing up off the cards in my SotM games really helps me keep track of what each card is doing, and I think they'll help in GSF as well.  If you think they'd help you, feel free to grab the file.
