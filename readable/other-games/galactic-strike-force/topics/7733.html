Techs which have weapon or defense values are installed as a stack, visually by installing then on the left side underneath the ship panel for weapon techs, and on the right side for defense techs, such that the last tech installed is the outermost. When removing energy, defense is removed first, and, when depleted, weapon is removed. When removing from a ship with techs, tokens are removed from the outer most (most recently installed) tech. When the tech is depleted, it is scrapped and the next outermost tech removes any remaining tokens (continue until all techs and ship is scrapped, or until all removal has been satisfied).
Also, any tech which has a destruction reaction will have a symbol identifying it so you know to do something when the tech is scrapped without having to have the text visible.

Ok give me a min to write this up.

When removing tokens (either from taking damage in battle or through various card effects) you follow the following 3 rules:

1) Remove ALL defense tokens before removing any weapon tokens.

2) Tokens must be removed from techs before taking them from the ship itself (your in a fierce dogfight and they shoot out your brand new [redacted])

3) Remove tokens from the newest tech first.

Using these 3 rules we can come up with a definitive list of how to remove token:

1) Remove defensive token them from the newest defensive tech (if there is more damage proceed to the next newewst, etc).

2) Remove defensive tokens from the ship.

3) Remove weapon token them from the newest weapon tech (if there is more damage proceed to the next newewst, etc).

4) Removie weapon tokens from the ship.

 

Knowing these rules are very importantn for 2 reasons:

1) It allows you to protect and cover a tech that you want by installing another tech over it.

2) Many techs and effects upon destruction. Planning ahead so that you know what will be destroyed if I do X can have some fantastic effects.
