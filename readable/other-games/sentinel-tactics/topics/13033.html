Why do you think that these things not happening is a problem? Genuine question, not me being snarky.

I'm not a big fan of random characters, or having your character(s) changed by the other team. Selecting who you'll play is a big part of the strategy and I wouldn't like that to be removed. Which ones you talking about? The random environment or random character?

if you are talking random environment, my reason is if the players are the best then they should be able to know all the characters in the game and take one look at the environment and as a team come up with a couple strategies of who they want to pick and run with it.

if you are talking about the random character then In my head if both teams are completeLy random they should have roughly the same luck issue. What I am worried most about is these tournaments start becoming the same issue that Magic the gathering tournaments have and made me fall out of love with the game. What has happen is 3 or 4 decks are the ones that win, sometimes it's to the point that only one deck will always win. So therefore if you go to play and don't play those decks you are going to be done early and everyone knows it so everyone has the same deck. No knowledge of the game required. 

Ultimately that's what I want to see from the first sentinel tactics champions. A knowledge of the game well enough that it doesn't matter what team they have, they can figure a way to win.
