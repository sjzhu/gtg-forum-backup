Prologue - October, 1943

The Orionid meteor storm was unusually bright that year, unexpected not the least of which was because it was nearly the midpoint of Halley's Comet's orbit around the sun. Observers in extreme southern latitudes claimed that the meteors were interacting with the aurora australis in unusual ways that could not be explained with known science. Allied and neutral military generals in the UK and Scandanavia were concerned at first that Hitler was using the cover of the meteor shower as a way to test some new weapon that would turn the tide back in the African front.

Following reports of extreme devastation on the island of Uttian off the coast of mainland Norway, the allies decided it was the perfect opportunity to test some new equipment of their own. Corporal Vernon Carter & Captain Paul Parsons VII were dispatched to investigate the source of the devastation with all due discretion and report back. As they were stowed their parachutes, they discovered a strangely dressed civilian with a prosthetic limb who appeared to share their interest. His fluent English minimized the possiblity of a German spy, so favoring the maxim of "keep you where I can see you," they dragged him into the crumbling ruins with them.

Villain: Advanced Plague Rat

Heroes (decide order yourself): GI Bunker, America's Greatest Legacy (aka Grandpa Legacy), Chrono-Ranger

Environment: Pike Industrial Complex
