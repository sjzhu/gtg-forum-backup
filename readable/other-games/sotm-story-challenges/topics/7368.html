I've thrown together a kind of basic update - it feels similar to the original Story Challenges in that there are 2 per environment, nemesis challenges, some special challenges, etc…

 

I removed two that bothered me… namely, the ones for cumulative damage and healing - those generally end up with people saying "meh, I've done 500 damage over all my games by now…"  Concepts were fine, but they were just hard to track.

I was working on an update for the Story Challenges with the expansions as well, but I decided to really do it right I'd have to incorporate the game statistics into the point values. In other word defeating Iron Legacy should be worth more than defeating Baron Blade.

 

Also, most of the Challenges don't specify how many Heroes are in use. Three Heroes in most cases will be a lot more challenging to win with than five. Given the (H) mechanic, there are probably also some scenarios where having three might be easier than having four or five.

 

So I decided that that was probably more work than I had time for at the moment, and changed the Challenges doc to a simple checklist.
