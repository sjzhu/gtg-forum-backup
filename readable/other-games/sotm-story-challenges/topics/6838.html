I introduced the game to 3 other people last night and had quite an entertaining game.  It was Bunker, Unity, Tempest and Fanatic vs Omnitron at the Pike Industrial Complex.

It was a very entertaining game.  At one point we had 4 escaped lab rats and an experimental mutagen in play.  We were a little worried until Omnitron terraformed them, and then we were a lot worried!

We kept at it and the game was pretty close at the end.  Bunker, Unity and Tempest were all incapacitated.  Fanatic was down to 6 HP's when her turn finished and Omnitron was at 1 HP.

We flipped over the supercooled trisolvent vat which ended up finishing off Omnitron.  It felt like the end of Terminator 2 when he got melted down.   It seemed like it would be a good Story Challenge, when the Rook City ones are written. 

 

Very fun session and 3 more fans!
