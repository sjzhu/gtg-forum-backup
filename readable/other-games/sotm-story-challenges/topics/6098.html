So. One night I decided to get 3 challenges at once.

  1. two-Heroes
  2. Legacy vs. Baron Blade
  3. Win a game using all the Original 10 heroes (I had Fanatic left)

I decided to play it flavoresque (That’s a perfect name for a porno). I was in Megaopolis. My thinking was thus:

“Legacy is showing Fanatic about the value of mortals, because she seems to be a but grumpy and away from the little people. Baron Blade then revisits his plan and attacks the humble city. With the other heroes on other missions, or too far away to get there in time it’s up to Legacy and Fanatic.”

I played 6 games. Each one I lost. One game I was this almost puts thumb and index finger together close .Legacy was kaput. I just needed one use of my power from Fanatic. She was was boosted by Heroic Resolve. Then the paparazzi came and made sure Baron Blade wins. I couldn’t use powers, I had no cards to play, and I was on the bring of death. In the end, the paparazzi didn’t get their story, they were “unfortunately” casualties in the wake of a vengeful mad scientist. Legacy and Fanatic were no more, and Megalopolis was doomed.
