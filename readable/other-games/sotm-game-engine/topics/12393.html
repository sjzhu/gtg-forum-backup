Note that you can completely evade Apostate's flip mechanic if you have any kind of insta-kill effect (Sucker Punch, Wrathful Gaze, Cornered Beast, etc) as his flip mechanic only engages if his hp is reduced to zero or fewer and these aren't reducing his hp but just destroying him outright.
