Cards that let you play an extra card (or use an extra power) on your turn allow you to do so during the play (or power) portion of your turn, starting the turn they are played. So, for example, if Tachyon play’s HUD Goggles, she can then immediately play another card (since HUD Goggles allows her to play an additional card on her turn).
