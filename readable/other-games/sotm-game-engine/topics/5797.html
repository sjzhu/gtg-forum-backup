Wraith’s Stealth power stacks with itself. If you don’t take any damage, the damage prevention number keeps increasing until she takes damage, at which point it resets to 0.

To clarify, her power is worded wrong. It says “prevent the next 2 damage she would take,” when it SHOULD say “The next time wraith takes damage, prevent 2 of it.”

This way it’s clear that it only happens the next time she takes damage.

It’s also important to note that since it says prevent – not reduce – it DOES prevent irreducible damage. When Plague Rat, for instance, does his attack and is buffed to 3 damage, it only deals Wraith 1 damage, even though it’s 3 irreducible.
