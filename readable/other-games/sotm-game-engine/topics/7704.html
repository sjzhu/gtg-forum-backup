It's my personal opinion that Spite should be one of the last villains "new" players face.  ("New", because after facing 17 villains, you're probably not all that new anymore.)  Not because he's hard or complicated or boring, though these are all criticisms that have been leveled against him.  He should be faced last because he subverts everything people know about the game.  You have to have experience with the game to appreciate how he turns it around.  A completely new player facing Spite is going to be like, "What's the point of this game?" and maybe never play it again.
