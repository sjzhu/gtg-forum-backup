You only get one use out of synaptic per time you play it, unless the destruction is prevented, the card cannot act again once it is being destroyed.

Ambuscade's retaliation is not part of the redirection, it takes place after the redirected damage is dealt.  Synaptic redirects and destroys itself.  it doesn't wait for all the events to play out, it does both.

Alacritous works because the destruction is "if you do, destroy this card."  It won't trigger until the effect finishes, because until then you haven't "used a power." Since the effect of Alacritous' Accompany effect is to grant you another power, it isn't finished until the power it grants is finished, and if that power grants a card play that grants a power. . .  You get the idea.  The whole chain has to finish before the destruction is triggered.
