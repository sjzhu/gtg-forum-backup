Since Bio-Engineering Beam doesn't have an "If you do..." attached to it, you can deal damage when there are no Environment cards in play.  However, if there is an Environment card in play, you must destroy one of them to use the Beam.

Ultimate Target (for thematic reasons and because a destruction effect wouldn't fit on the card) stays in play even after the target leaves the field.  The idea is that since it is "The Ultimate Target", you won't really need to attach the Bounty to anything else.  Fortunately, it's pretty easy to destroy it since it's an Ongoing and Jim's Hat can pop it as a SoT effect.

There doesn't have to be an environment card in play to use it. You can attempt to destroy anything even if that thing is not in play. So you could play Disruptive Flechettes if you desired and only do the damage portion of it. Though if there is an Environment card in play you will have to destroy it if you use that power as there isn't a "may" in it.

 

"The Ultimate Target" is intended to stay in play. It is like this for two reasons, the first being that there wasn't enough room on the card. The second being that it actually fits as it is the Ultimate Target, you can't just easily change that. So either ongoing destruction or the start of turn effect on Jim's Hat can get it back into your trash.
