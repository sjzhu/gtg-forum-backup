I'm playing a game currently with Expatiette and it's got me thinking, again, about her.  Specifically her Incendiary Rounds.  They change her damage to fire and add 1, and I'm trying to think why this is a particularly desirable.  The damage buff is a duplicate trick to Hollow Points, which  give a much better damage buff.  One possibility is if a villain should be immune to projectile damage, which frankly doesn't happen often.  And if it does, Cryo Rounds also changes the damage type and the damage debuff is both not duplicated by another ammo type and a quite useful status attack.  The other might be if paired with Ra or having some other method of increasing specifically fire damage.  Which I feel is even less likely to happen than the first case.   Functionally, Incendiary Rounds just don't seem to have much point beyond the +1, and HPs do it better.

 

(As a potential alternate I'm considering house ruling them into "Armor Piercing Rounds" - the effect of which is to make the attack irreducible.  It makes great sense thematically and expands Expat's bag of tricks while getting rid of a redundant ammo type.)
