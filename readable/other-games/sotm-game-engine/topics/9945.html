We've covered this already.  You can argue that hero character cards replace the effect of destruction, or that the card is destroyed then returned to play and flipped, but there is absolutely zero evidence that the destroy effect is replaced.

 

There are two templates for this sort of effect. Skydeck reads " Whenever a target is destroyed, put it under its associated deck." This is clearly distinct from Unforgiving Wasteland, which reads " Whenever a Target would be destroyed by Damage dealt by an Environment card, remove that Target from the game instead. "  UW clearly replaces destruction with the word "instead", the fact that Skydeck does not include the same wording indicates that the act of destruction is not replaced.  Since the wording of the Hero Character Card destruction rules follows the Skydeck template, not the UW template, the destruction is not replaced.
