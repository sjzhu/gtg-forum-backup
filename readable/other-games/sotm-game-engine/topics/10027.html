Last night, we were playing against Ambuscade in... Time Cataclysm? I don't remember, but there was some environment effect that played the top card of the villain deck.

 

Ambuscade was flipped into cloaked mode, with the Personal Cloaking Device in play. Across the players' turns, we were able to destroy the cloaking device, but Ambuscade doesn't flip back until the villain turn. 

 

On the environment turn, a card was played that caused the top of the villain deck to be played, which was a card that said to search the villain deck and trash and put the Personal Cloaking Device in play. When it comes into play, the Personal Cloaking Device says to flip Ambuscade, which resulted in the cloaking device being in play and Ambuscade being on his "A side" (where his damage isn't prevented.)

 

Is there an official ruling/errata on what to do when this happens? We let Ambuscade stay in cloaked mode, but I'm pretty sure there are multiple environments that have effects that play the top card of the villain deck, so this could come up in other situations as well.
