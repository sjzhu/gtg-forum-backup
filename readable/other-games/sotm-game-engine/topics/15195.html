Sure.

Breaking the Rules:

The phases of hero turns now go in this order:

End of turn

Draw a card

Use a power

Play a card

Start of turn

 

Usually if you reach the end of your turn draw phase and have not played a card or used a power you can draw an extra card, the question was what happens now the end is the beginning? There were 2 schools of thought.

  1. You have not played a card or used a power so you can draw an extra card. Subsequently you can still both use a power and play a card because the "check" for that condition at the end of turn has already passed.
  2. You have not played a card or used a power so you can draw an extra card. If you do you cannot use a power or play a card because by drawing the extra card you have committed yourself to not using a power or playing a card.
We now know the videogame states that 1 is the correct interpretation.
