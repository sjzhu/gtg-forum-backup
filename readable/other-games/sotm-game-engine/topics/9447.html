Played a solo game to test out some of the new Vengeance heroes.  K.N.Y.F.E. felt like a major disappointment.  She's got some cool cards in her deck that can make for some pretty powerful combos, but a Overdo It felt completely out of place.  Overdo It sucks for two reasons:

First, in most cases you will play the card during your own play phase, which means you have to take damage at the end of that turn before you can benefit from that card whatsoever (assuming the card is not destroyed before it gets back to your turn).  Rarely another hero will allow you to play a card out of turn, but this seems like quite a hassle just to get decent value out of the card.  Both Tachyon's Pushing the Limits and Ra's Solar Flare at least allow you to benefit from the card before choosing whether or not to destroy it.  I am considering house ruling a swap on Overdo It allowing you to play a card at the end of your turn and take damage/destroy at the start of your turn.

Second, even if I house rule that swap, the effect is still not very good because K.N.Y.F.E. has virtually no mechanisms for filling her hand.  Almost every other card in her deck allowing her to draw also allows her to play the same number of cards.  The only exception is her "Overcharged Null Shield" (2 copies in deck) which allows you to draw two cards but only as a secondary effect of destroying the shield.  The only way she'll ever have more than 3 or 4 cards in her hand is if another hero gives her card draw.  Without having a big hand I see no reason to play "Overdo It" because I need to have it out for at least two turns to even recoup the play action used to play it.  The chance of having four cards (my entire hand) to play that I can get maximum value out of is pretty small.  In the rare case that I do pull it off (perhaps with the help of another hero), I certainly wouldn't expect to do it again after I've emptied my hand, so what I'm going to do with the other two copies of Overdo It in the deck I do not know.  Having two of them in my hand of 3-4 cards made for boring play.  I think K.N.Y.F.E. would be a stronger hero if I simply removed these cards from her deck because they don't work with the rest of it.

Heres my general take on it, and how I use it for greatest effectiveness.

When playing overdo it, you need to have a plan for your next two turns.  Past that becomes difficult to plan for, and the self-harm gets prohibitively nasty, considering the other ways KNYFE has of hurting herself.  when you play it, you are essentially delaying your card play for a round.  Every round after that is a bonus- thats why I like to keep it around for two rounds- the first gets your delayed action, the second is a bonus.

 

Here's a few examples of how to use it to the greatest effectiveness:

Lets start with "Primed Punch"-

this is fantastic as a power if you have any sort of buffs, and as a delayed-action one shot.  You can play it on one turn, then detonate it at the start of the next turn for a large burst of damage.

How it works with "Overdo it": Because you can choose to detonate Primed punch at the Start of the turn, you can play it first, detonate, then play another card.  this is great when you really need to take out a specific minion that is providing damage resistance (like a Gene-bound guard), so that you can maximize your play-phase card (like Incidental contact).

 

Lets continue with "Flawless Execution"

this ends up being way more useful than you'd think as a bonus card.  Combined with Amplified combatant or incidental contact, it can mow down hordes of Low-HP creatures (including Unity's bots and heroes if you use Incidental contact carelessly...).  It also works very nicely with the Servo guantlet or battlefield XP.

Why it is useful to "Overdo it" with this card: Flawless Execution is one of those cards that will only become useful when played in conjunction with other things.  as such, it is rarely going to be top choice for your play phase- but as a bonus card, you can start to see the appeal.  You play it, then the next card which will take advantage of it.  And after that, its now out so all of your stuff is nastier.  this is one of the reasons I like to keep Overdo it around for 2 rounds- this is an ideal second-round combination.  

 

Taking it to ridiculous extremes...

the biggest appeal of Overdo it is that it allows you to engage in ridiculous start-of-turn shenanigans. Here is my favorite usage of it

Start of turn :

Greater good- play battelfield XP.  Use battlefield XP.  Play Greater good, play Battlefied XP.  Use Battlefield XP.  Play primed punch, detonate primed punch.

Play phase: Battlefield XP.  Use battlfield XP.  Play wrecking strike or amplified combatant.

I tend to mix this up, depending on what cards I have.  sometimes Overdo it is fantastic for putting out the Servo-gauntlet or a focusing conduit blade so that during the play phase you can get crazy with the battlefield XP.  Sometimes its great for taking out a specific person who is fouling up my chains (like V5 Baron Blade's impulsion beams), or who is protecting everyone else.

 

Overall:

I only play it when I have a goal, when I have a potential chain in hand, when I have lots of cards, or when healing is available.  I keep it out for 2-3 rounds (longer if healing is available), because it really pays for itself in the second (and third) round. It is a superb card to get timeshifted or from the Scholar.  

Potential pitfalls:

KNYFE can run out of cards if you don't have battlefield XP/greater good chains (or other players helping with cards).  It can get prohibitively nasty when Legacy is around helping with damage.  

 

I can totally understand why it appears to be underpowered or not useful.  There will be times when you absolutely don't want to play it. But there will be times when you wipe out everything on the board, or pummel a single target into oblivion in a single turn.  Especially when you are fighting the V5, who have lots of targets who you'll want to get rid of!
