phantaskippy:

If it was Deal the 2 highest HP targets then it would not recalculate

?!?  That seems inconsistent.  Everything I've learned about timing in this game suggests that each instance of damage is unique, and calculates everything afresh.  Normally, the recalculation wouldn't matter for that sort of text, but sometimes it would.  Here's a (slightly vague) hypothetical, broken down:

  • Some effect says "Deal 3 damage to the 2 hero targets with the highest HP."

  • Determine who the two highest HP targets are.  Call them A and B.

  • Players choose which one gets damaged first.  Let's say they choose A.

  • A gets damaged for 3.  Some weird situation reacts to A taking damage and does a lot of side-efffect stuff, part of which causes B to take 4 damage.  Eventually we resolve all the side effects and come back to this calculation.

  • Have two targets been damaged?  No.  So we need to damage another.

  • Determine who the two highest HP targets are.  They are now C and D.

  • Players choose which target to damage.  They choose D.  (If A had still been one of the highest 2, he would have been ineligible as a target due to already having been picked, so the "choice" would be forced.)

  • D takes 3 damage.

  • Have two targets been damaged?  Yes.  We're done.

Hm...

 

When you designate a target for a multi-hit attack like that, you check first to see if it's been designated as a target before.  What if you also check the 'ranking' of the target so that the ranking hasn't been designated before?

 

For example:  Three targets - A [20] B [19] C [18], are being hit with an attack which specifies "Deal 2 sonic damage to the two targets with the highest health".  For the first hit, you designate B, which is the 2nd highest (and therefore valid).  Now you have A [20] C [18] B [17].  You can't designate C because it's the 2nd highest health, and you've already designated the 2nd highest health, so you choose A instead.  A and B have taken damage, which is what you would expect, but a full target recalculation was allowed like usual.

 

The only caveat is that sometimes you could end up with a situation where there are no valid targets.  For example: A [20] B [19], same attack.  This time, you hit A first, leaving you with B [19] A [18].  You can't choose A because it's already been chosen, and you can't choose B because the 1st target has already been chosen.  In this case, the "can't choose a target twice" rule would trump the "can't choose a ranking twice" rule, and you'd choose B as your second target.

 

You could still end up with some weird situations when response damage gets involved, though.  If you have A B C and D (in order), and A getting hit triggers response damage which hits B so that after the first attack the order is now C D A B, this solution would dictate that you now have to hit D (and that you can't hit C), which is a little bit weird.  But other than that, it still works.

 

EDIT:  On second thought, it seems like the 2nd target should be the highest (or lowest, or whatever ranking you're using) health target that hasn't already been chosen, regardless of whether the first target was the 1st or 2nd highest health.  But I'm not sure how you would apply that to 3 or more targets while still preserving the ability to hit the targets in any order.
