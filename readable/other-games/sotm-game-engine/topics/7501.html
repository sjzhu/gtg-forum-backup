Anything with HP is a possible target for attacks, so you can reduce it to zero.

Environment cards with HP are environment targets (which is a separate glossary entry but is also, sort of, mentioned in the glossary entry for environment cards in general. but yeah, it might take a moment to realize what it's saying depending on how you read it)

Yeah, an environment card with hp is also a target (any card with hp is a target). Since it's in the einvronment deck, it's also an environment card and therefore can also be removed by anything that destroys environment cards (eg Grappling Hook, Bio-Engineering Beam, RPG Launcher, etc).
