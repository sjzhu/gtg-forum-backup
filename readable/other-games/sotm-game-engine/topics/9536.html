I did a 7 player game recently because we just got Vengenace and everyone wanted to try it out, so we decided against splitting into multiple games.

We used 4 Vengeance villains and one normal villain (with H = 3). You could probably do the same for a 6 player game with just dropping one of the Vengenace villains.

It was a long game, but turned out to be okay. Since the villains were doing stuff more often, player boredom didn't come up. The down side is that once the Vengeance villains were down, finishing off the normal villain was really easy. A possible fix to that might be to increase H by one when a Vengeance villain dies. That plus the Vengeneance villain incap effects might keep it challenging though the whole game.

I've done multiple games with 8 heroes.  Villan hits that use the H-mechanic are very, very powerful.  We've even lost one or two games even with all the extra hero-damage.  The main downside to 5+ games are how long each round goes, so players can get board.  

You do run into some issues with some villans.  Like Iron Legacy needing to put out ongoings equal to the number of heroes.  I don't remember exactly how many ongoing he has so he may not have enough.  You do run out of infections with Plague Rat.  We've done him twice with 7+.  Once we did that when one person a second person got a copy as well, even though we didn't have a second physical copy.  So this way eventually everyone had one.  The other time we just based his flip on if all infections were played on a player, even though we had some players not infected.

But other than that, they're actually very do-able.  The H-mechanic really does a good job of balancing for both 5+ games and 2 player games.
