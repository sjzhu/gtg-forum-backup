For most villains, more heroes make it easier. Regarding villains dealing more damage, I will copy a post I wrote a while ago here:

Pydro:

I think this is a good example of the theoretical vs the practical. True, in lower H games you can nulify the damage, but does it really matter?

Consider 3 heroes with 25 hp each, and an attack of H-2. Let's see how many instances of damage are needed to wipe out the team:

3p game - No DR: 75 / 1 = 75 instances

3p game - each with DR 1: Nullified

4p game - No DR: 100 / 2 = 50

4p game - each with DR 1: 100 / 1 = 100 Instances

5p game - No DR: 125 / 3 = 42 instances

5p game - each with DR 1: 125 / 2 = 53 instances

In each case, DR does help. Now, its true a DR of 1 can nulify the damage in a 3p game, but look how many times the heroes need to be hit before they are killed. Even in a 5p game with no DR, it would still take 42 times of being hit before being incapped. That's huge. You can often kill the villain before then, so it doesn't really matter, and villains don't play more cards based on the number of people. To compensate, you get an extra play, power and a variety of effects to help you kill the villain even faster. While it's nice to end the game at full hp, in many circumstances it simply doesn't matter, and more players make the game much easier.

True, if you have a villain that can put out that many instances (Matty), then a 3p game will make a difference, but if they can't, a 5p game will almost always be easier. The only other instance this matter is if there is a lot of AoE damage.

I was a little bored, so I went through the numbers with an H-2, H-1, and H-0 attack.

And certain villains increase their difficulty in other ways based on H. The dreamer is much more difficult on H=4 than on H=3, and H=5 is near impossible, but that's just because you have to survive much longer, getting closer to Pydro's number. Miss Information is the same way, she requires you to take more bad cards, and she deals more instances before you're able to hit her. 

 

That said, almost every other villain is easier on H=5. Voss is a great example of this. He scales with H by having more starting minions and his damage on his back side. The minions deal more instances of damage. This is why if the heroes can get past his DR, it's almost always better to leave him on his front side and beat on him there, where after you're down to 1 his damage is minimal.
