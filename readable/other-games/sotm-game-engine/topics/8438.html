Workbench says if you discard a card you put a golem into play, so if someone else did you wouldn't get to play the golem.

I believe that discarding is personal, if it isn't it says so, like Chrono-Ranger's card.  Equipment and ongoing are generally treated the opposite way, with it specifying if it is your card, otherwise it is any.
