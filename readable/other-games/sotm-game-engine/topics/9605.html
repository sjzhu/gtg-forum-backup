As others have said: no, Pride won't let you fire Prejudice twice. You can use your guns in any order, and not all the powers you use have to be guns. This does mean that if you have, say, Shotgun, Pride and Prejudice, you can use Load to play Unload, and then use the two powers you get from that on the Shotgun and then Pride... no power left for Prejudice, but Pride lets you fire it anyway, so that's fine :D (It wouldn't work if you fired Pride before the Shotgun though; then Load, Pride and Prejudice would be your three powers used, even if you only fired Prejudice because of Pride.)

You can absolutely use Prejudice twice in a single turn. You guys are dropping the ball on the OP here.

Example:

Pride and Prejudice are in play. You play Unload to gain (2) power uses. Use Prejudice first. Then use Pride. Since it specifically and explicitly tells you to use Predudice, you are allowed to do so. 

This is similar to two cases:

1) Overdrive. You can use the same power twice when given explicit instructions to do so

2) Effects that break other base rules, like effects that allow you to play multiple cards during your play phase when the normal rule is play 1 card.

Silverleaf, your plan involves a few problems:

  • No matter how many powers you get, you can only use Load once per turn.
  • The second Unload play doesn't do anything - the first one allows you to use as many powers this turn as you have guns in play. The second one doesn't add more powers (the same way a second Overdrive doesn't let Fixer use Strike 2 more times).

 

If X can change, then an abbreviated form of your plan could work:

Step 1: 1 gun in play. Play Unload - 1 power.

Step 2: Use Load to put a gun into play. Gain another power since you have another gun.

Step 3: Use final power to shoot one of your guns (or both, if they are Pride and Prejudice).

However, this is really just an overly complex way of playing the second gun and then using a power. You might as well save Unload for a future turn.
