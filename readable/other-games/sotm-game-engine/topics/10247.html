Friction is tough, especially in a two player game, but it's perfectly feasible to build up against her. Naturalist has Natural Born Vigor, that lets him use two powers in a turn. With that out, it's perfectly viable to burn Naturalist's current form to satisfy Friction on her turn, play a new ongoing on your turn, use Naturalist's inherent power to recover the Form card, and then use whatever additional power you got from the Form or the new ongoing card. It's not ideal, and it's annoying, but it's not impossible.

I don't really recommend taking out Friction's dampeners with targeted damage. That's 9 damage that could be spent reducing her HP by 9, and there's no guarantee that she'll do 9 or more damage to herself from her own cards. (Obviously, if you have someone like Tempest doing a lot of lightning damage, that can change the calculus.) Her Stolen Gear is nasty, since it plays more cards and puts more Surges in her trash, making the cards she plays worse, so that's a reasonable target.

Locking down her deck can be worthwhile. Wraith and Naturalist can't lock her down completely, but Wraith can use IR Eyepiece to get rid of the cards that destroy ongoings. She's only got two Blinding Surges (which only destroy ongoings, so Wraith should be have nearly uneffected) so if you can control her ability to play multiple cards, you shouldn't have to worry about them until the end of the game.

I agree with the focus fire method.  The incap sides are much less dangerous than an active villain, especially if they have minions out.  It went against every fiber of my being but my group adopted a strategy of just focus fire each villain, not anything from their deck, just the actual villain one at a time.  After the first one or two (depending on H) go down the game gets much more managable.
