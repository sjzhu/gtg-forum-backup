That's right, it applies to the final target, whether the final target takes damage or not.

So if Fanatic tries to use Kill the spirit on Glamour, and it redirects back to Fanatic who has Undaunted in play, the damage will be prevented, but all the damage she deals until the end of her next turn will be redirected to fanatic, because she was the final target.
