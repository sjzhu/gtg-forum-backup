Sorry if this has already been discussed - I looked and didn't see it.

When Guise guiseshifts into The Naturalist using "Uh, Yeah, I'm That Guy" while The Naturalist is in a Form, "Uh, Yeah, I'm That Guy" gains the Form's text. The Form's text includes the phrase, "Guise's Player may activate (color/animal) effects." It's now The Naturalist's turn. He is in Crocodile Form, and he plays Bestial Shift. What happens?

Guise has a card that says he can activate Green Crocodile effects. The Naturalist just played a card that has a green crocodile on it. Obviously, The Naturalist can activate the Green Crocodile effect to deal 1 target 3 toxic damage and then use a power. Can Guise also activate that with the text on "Uh, Yeah, I'm That Guy"? If so, what does the Green Crocodile effect do? Does it result in:

(1) The Naturalist deals 1 target 3 toxic damage and then The Naturalist uses a power. Even though Guise activated the text, the text still refers to The Naturalist and doesn't get changed by "Uh, Yeah, I'm That Guy" because it's a One-Shot and not an Ongoing.

(2) The Naturalist deals 1 target 3 toxic damage and then Guise uses a power. Guise is activating the effect, and the text of the card says "The Naturalist" deals the damage, but "you" activate a power. Since Guise is the one activating the effect, the "you" refers to Guise.

(3) Guise deals 1 target 3 toxic damage and then Guise uses a power. I have no idea how one could argue that this is the case, but I guess it's a possibility.

(4) Guise cannot activate Green Crocodile effects on cards The Naturalist plays, so none of the above occurs.

 

What about when, during Guise's turn, Guise uses Natural Form's Power. What happens?

(I) Guise activates the Green Crocodile effect and Guise deals up to 2 targets 1 toxic damage each, and that's it.

(II) Guise activates the Green Crocodile effect and Guise deals up to 2 targets 1 toxic damage each. Then, The Naturalist activates the Green Crocodile effect and Guise deals up to 2 targets 1 toxic damage each.

(III) Guise activates the Green Crocodile effect and Guise deals up to 2 targets 1 toxic damage each. Then, The Naturalist activates the Green Crocodile effect and The Naturalist deals up to 2 targets 1 toxic damage each. 

 

As a followup question, what about The Naturalist's form-based Ongoings, like Resilient Hide. Resilient Hide states: (Purple Rhino) At the start of your turn, The Naturalist regains 1 HP. Which set of the following happen:

(a) At the start of The Naturalist's turn, The Naturalist regains 1 HP.

(b) At the start of The Naturalist's turn, The Naturalist regains 2 HP.

(c) At the start of The Naturalist's turn, Guise regains 1 HP.

(d) At the start of Guise's turn, Guise regains 1 HP.

(e) At the start of Guise's turn, Guise regains 2 HP.

(f) At the start of Guise's turn, The Naturalist regains 1 HP.

There are two cards in play. One has the Purple Rhino effect "At the start of The Naturalist's turn, The Naturalist regains 1 HP." The other has the Purple Rhino effect "At the start of Guise's turn, Guise regains 1 HP." Both of them can activate Purple Rhino effects. So, at the start of The Naturalist's turn, one Purple Rhino effect triggers. Can they both activate it? If so, (b) happens since both trigger since both players are triggering the text on The Naturalist's version. If not, then (a) happens. Then, can Guise activate his version of the purple rhino effect at the start of The Naturalist's turn? I'd expect no, but if so, then (c) would trigger. And then, the converse. If (a) happens, I'd expect (d) to happen, same with (b) and (e), and (c) and (f).

EDIT: To reword this last one, I can only see three valid outcomes here  (all assuming Resilient Hide was played prior to "Uh, Yeah, I'm That Guy"):

(A) At the start of The Naturalist's turn, The Naturalist regains 1 HP. At the start of Guise's turn, Guise regains 1 HP.

(B) At the start of The Naturalist's turn, The Naturalist regains 1 HP, then The Naturalist regains 1 HP. At the start of Guise's turn, Guise regains 1 HP, then Guise regains 1 HP.

(C) At the start of The Naturalist's turn, The Naturalist regains 1 HP, then Guise regains 1 HP. At the start of Guise's turn, The Naturalist regains 1 HP, then Guise regains 1 HP.
