Yes, as Pydro said, if a thing is dealing damage and gets killed halfway through dealing said damage, it's no longer around in order to continue dealing damage. Another example for you is a game we had once which was epic fun - Ra, Expatriette, and someone (possibly Legacy - I think Expatriette was buffed from somewhere but not sure) versus Gloomy. Expatriette has Hairtrigger Reflexes. Ra has Flame Barrier. A Cultist (let's say, the Cursed Acolyte, because he hits everyone) comes into play and gets plinked by Expatriette. Then he attacks everyone...but we'll start with Ra. Ra's Flame Barrier goes off, finishing off the Cultist, who dies and comes back as a Zombie...which promptly gets insta-killed by Hairtrigger Reflexes (so Expatriette was definitely buffed, then, for it to have been at least two damage there...). Fun game :D.

Also, it works for hero stuff too - Visionary using Wrest the Mind on something is a good example - the Wrested target hits something, she redirects its damage elsewhere, then has to hit it and herself...but she hits it first, dealing enough damage to kill it, at which point Wrest is destroyed too so she doesn't have to hit herself because Wrest got destroyed before she could get that far :).

My opinion: Once a source is determined, it doesn't change. That specific target deals this specific instance(s) of damage. If you open the door for the interpretation that the source can change, then would the new source have to deal each hero target damage? In other words, you could argue the new source has not dealt each hero target damage, and re-damage targets already hit by the first source. So for simplicitys sake, I'm on the side of sources not changing.

But Ronway is right. This has been discussed before and its currently up to players choice how they would like to handle this without a ruling from up high.
