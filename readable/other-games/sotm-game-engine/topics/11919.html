So I started a thread earlier, whose initial question was quickly resolved, but which brought up another issue that seems thorny.

How, precisely, should we evaluate "<do some effect> to the 2* targets with <some condition>."

* Could be more than 2, as long as it is some specific number more than 1 and less than "all".

This seems obvious, but isn't, because of some well-established meta-rules.  There are two very different ways of interpreting this:

A) This is how the App currently works, and is the simple english interpretation.

  • Determine the 2 targets who fit the condition.  If there are ties, players choose.  Call these targets X and Y.
  • Players choose whether X or Y gets affected first.  Let's say it's X.
  • X gets affected, and any appropriate side effects happen.
  • Have 2 targets been effected?  No.  So we need to process another.
  • Y gets affected, and any appropriate side effects happen.
  • Have two targets been damaged?  Yes.  We're done.

B) Based on rulings involving End of Days and Forced Deployment, and the like.

  • Determine the 2 targets who fit the condition.  If there are ties, players choose.  Call these targets X and Y.
  • Players choose whether X or Y gets affected first.  Let's say it's X.
  • X gets affected, and any appropriate side effects happen.
  • Have 2 targets been effected?  No.  So we need to process another.
  • Determine the 2 targets who fit the condition.  If there are ties, players choose.  One (or sometimes both!) of these targets will be different from the original pair, so let's call them Y and Z.
  • Players choose which target to damage.  Let's say they choose Z.  (If X had still been one of the 2, he would have been ineligible as a target due to already having been picked, so the "choice" would be forced.)
  • Z gets affected, and any appropriate side effects happen.
  • Have two targets been damaged?  Yes.  We're done.

(So, interpetation B would routinely allow players to react to situations such as "Damage the 2 targets with the highest HP", by damaging the (originally) 1st and 3rd highest!)

 

The argument for B is that each instance of damage is completely unique, and any variables involved are recalculated each time.  The question is, does that recalculation include target selection in the general case of "<do effect E> to the 2* targets with <some condition>."

Interpreatation B *is* a bit counter-intuitive, but it seems to me to be most correct, in the absence of an official ruling.    I hereby request a ruling along the lines of:

"When an card effects multiple (but not all) targets, those targets {ARE/ARE NOT} all determined before the actual effects start.  The targets {ARE NOT/ARE} recalculated after each intance of the effect."

A is incomplete - it doesn't cover what you do if reaction damage from the first attack kills target Y.

 

Also, I've come up with an option C.  It is most similar to option B, but I think it creates results closer to option A.

 

In order to explain it, I shall use the attack "Deal N damage to the 3 targets with the highest HP"

  • Determine the 3 highest HP targets
  • Choose one target (X) amongst these 3 to take the damage.
  • Calculate damage for the attack on X
  • <3 attacks have been made, so continue
  • Determine the 2 highest HP eligible (i.e. not X) targets
  • Choose one target (Y) amongst these 2 to take the damage
  • Calculate the damage for the attack on Y
  • <3 attacks have been made, so continue
  • Determine the 1 highest HP eligible (not X or Y) target
  • Calculate the attack on this target
  • 3 attacks have been made - you're done
