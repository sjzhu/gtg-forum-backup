What is the consensus on when the game actually ends upon the destruction of the villain? For example, will hit-backs like Backlash Field or the Chairman's ability activate when the villain is reduced to 0 or fewer HP (assuming all other conditions for victory have been fulfilled)?

MigrantP:

At what point do you check for victory? And why do only villains seem to get a pass on striking back from the grave?
