The text on Annubis states, "The first time a non-environment card is destroyed each turn, this card deals the non-environment target with the second highest hp 2 infernal damage." Does "turn" mean environment turn, villain turn, and hero turn? Or does it mean the overall turn--i.e., the "tunr" that starts with the villain and ends with the environment?

What you refer to as "the overall turn" is actually defined as a round.  A round consists of a villain turn, all hero turns in turn order, and an environment turn.  Also, if something refers to a turn without qualifying it, it refers to any turn.  In this case, the first time every villain, hero, and environment turn where a non-environment card is destroyed, Anubis deals damage.
