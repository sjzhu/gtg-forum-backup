Unlike Embolden Overdrive does not state you do it during the power phase.

 

There is no inherent restriction on when you are allowed to do certain activities.

When Reset says to draw a card we don't wait until the draw phase, and no one questions Team leader letting you draw during the Power Phase or Adept letting you play a card during his power phase, but when powers get involved we start inventing restrictions.

The power phase is the point during your turn when you get a free power use.  Just like the play phase and the draw phase.

Overdrive lets you use Strike twice during the current turn.  That would fit anywhere between the start and end of turn.
