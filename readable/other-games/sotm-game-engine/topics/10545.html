First, it says "destroy" not "discard." minor point, but destroy is from inplay, and discard is from your hand.

Second, yes, the entire point is to be out for only a single turn. Why isn't it a One-Shot? Because the text only works if it is inplay, if something destroys the card, the villain can still play cards.

Without going into too much detail, the game tries to avoid having effects inplay without an actual card representing it. By making it an Ongoing, you always have that text there reminding you about it. Now, there are cards that will have a longer effect and go to the trash, but they are worded a little differently (e.g. Mistbound), and have slightly different mechanics.
