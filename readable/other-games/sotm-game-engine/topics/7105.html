I don't own Unity but I can let you know the answer to whether you can destroy a card in your hand. Basically, you can't - losing a card from your hand is discarding it, not destroying it. Destroying a card can only be done once that card is in play.

Also, if all it says is "Destroy an equipment card", it isn't specifying a deck, so you could destroy an equipment card belonging to another hero if you wanted.
