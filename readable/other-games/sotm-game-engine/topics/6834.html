Yeah. You only shuffle a deck when you need to draw from it. If you need to reveal from it, you don't shuffle. It's why getting Nightmist's entire deck into your hand is a bad idea (I found out, once I did it and realised I couldn't play half my cards). I've always also played it that you don't shuffle if you only need to discard (likely to come up against someone like Akash'Bhuta).

With the Thug thing in the Chairman's deck, when you start the game you take all the Thug cards from the deck and put them into the trash pile. Any other villain starts with nothing in their trash - this is unique to the Chairman's deck. If you have any cards that let you move stuff out of their trash (eg the Visionary can put trash cards back into the villain deck), this can be helpful since the Underboss cards require you to move cards from the trash into play. If a certain card isn't in the trash, you can't move it from there, so it doesn't come into play :). On the other hand, having Haka play Savage Mana and then eat all the Thugs can also be fun. But don't let him eat more than two of the Underbosses or the Chairman won't be able to flip! Once he does, though, you can smish them however you like :).
