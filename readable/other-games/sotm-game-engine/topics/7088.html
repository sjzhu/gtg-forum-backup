In the example you have given it does in fact have to take damage after any reduction to trigger the effect. There are some cards that don't have to deal damage, such as Wraith's Stun Bolt "The Wraith deals 1 target 1 Projectile damage. Until the start of your next turn, reduce all damage dealt by 1." as it doesn't have the "cards dealt damage" clause in it. Now Throat Jab on the other hand reads 'The target dealt damage this way cannot deal damage until the start of your next turn." Will have to deal damage.
