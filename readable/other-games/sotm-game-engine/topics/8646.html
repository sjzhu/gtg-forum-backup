Ronway:
BlueHairedMeerkat wrote:
...I now kind of want to compile a list of cards which are strictly better than other cards, across every deck, just because...  
Here, i'll help get started.

  • Ball Lightning is better than Back Fist Strike (EDIT by me: except for a different damage type), same damage but destroys 2 ongoings.

  • Thokk is better than Elbow Smash (EDIT by me: or Psychic Spike, except for a different damage type), same damage but allows a cards draw.

  • Absolution is better than Blazing Tornado/Flak Cannon/Razor Ordinace, same damage but heals when enters play and can choose out of 3 types of damage.  (EDIT by me:  One of which virtually nothing is immune to, so the comparison is not close.)

  • Inspiring Presence is better than Imbued Fire, heals when enters play and doesn't risk increasing non-hero damage.

  • Divine Focus is better than Gatling Gun, as Divine Focus can do damage every turn. (EDIT by me:  This one is disputed on the original forum, as Gatling Gun isn't restricted to damaging only the highest-HP target.  It also is capable of continuing indefinitely as long as you're able to draw a card every turn, while Divine Focus is much faster, but therefore does not give you much chance to replenish your hand.)

  • Psychic Maelstorm is better than Accelerated Assualt, 1 extra point of damage.

  • Know When to Hold Fast is better than Prayer of Desperation, allows 5 cards to be drawn no matter what where PoD only equals that if 1 cards is in hand and is only better when having an empty hand.

  • Grevious Hailstorm is better than Submachine Gun, does an extra point of damage.  (EDIT by me:  This ignores the possibility of giving the Gun an Ammo card, as this comparison is about evaluating each card in a vacuum, not its potential for combinations.)

  • Impromtu Invention is better than every other search card in the game.

  • Trust Fund is better than Quick Insight, has 1 extra card to be drawn but still discards the same amount.

*Assualt Rifle is better than Throwing Knives/Grenade Laucher, deals more damage to the same amount of targets.

That's a good start, right?

Indeed it is Ronway.  I've come up with a couple more I want to add.

  • Tachyon's Fleet of Foot is strictly better than Legacy's Bolster Allies.  Both let everyone draw a card, but Fleet doesn't cost you your play, plus it's a Burst and thus contributes to the rest of your deck (I'm drawing a fine line on the "in a vacuum rule" here, but I'm not assuming any specific other card being necessary to make this one work, as with the Ammo in the Submachine Gun).

  • Bunker's Auxilliary Power Source is better than AZ's Glacial Structure; both let you destroy them and draw three cards as a Power, but AuxPS can instead be destroyed to use two powers in one turn.

  • Bunker's Heavy Plating is better than any of the Ongoing cards which likewise reduce damage by 1 (Legacy's Fortitude, Tempest's Otherworldly Resistance, and Haka's Ta Moko; I'm told the Scholar has a version which is not Limited, and thus would be better than all of these as well as mostly-better than HP), because villains are less likely to destroy Equipment than Ongoings, and because (as far as I'm aware) there are more effects on various Environment and friendly-hero powers which can help you get Equipment into play from your hand or trash, compared to doing so for Ongoings.

  • Haka's Taiaha can damage two targets, and so is better than the various cards mentioned under Absolution, and arguably better than Absolution itself, except in terms of damage types (more things are immune to Melee than to anything else, it seems).

  • Legacy's "THOKK!" is strictly better than Fanatic's Brutal Censure except in terms of damage type, dealing more damage while both let you draw a card.

  • Ra's Flame Blast and Tempest's Lightning Smash are both better than Legacy's Back Fist Strike (which in turn is better than Haka's Elbow Smash and Visionary's Psychic Spike, as mentioned above), dealing more damage and (except in the case of Psychic Spike) being less likely to get ignored, because more things are immune to Melee than any other damage type.  (I'm pretty sure this is true; certainly it's true in the subset of the game I'm familiar with, which consists of the Enhanced Edition and about half of the first two expansions, but excludes Timelines and the mini-expansions.)

  • Legacy's Danger Sense is better than Haka's Enduring Intercession…well, better for Haka at least.

I think I had another one in mind when I started typing this, but it now escapes me.  Actually, I remembered it.  This will do for now.  Thanks to BlueHairedMeerkat for prompting the original question and to Ronway for doing most of the work of answering it.

One thing to keep in mind is that you can't just compare cards the same way you would in a game where you build the decks.  Absolute Zero's self damaging would be really bad in other decks, but they become benefits with his modules.  Similarly, something like Fortitude becomes better than Otherworldly Resistance when combined with Lead From the Front redirecting damage to Legacy.

I'm curious what makes you say Villains are more likely to destroy ongoings than equipment.  The vast majority of villains destroy "Ongoing and/or Equipment cards" to the point where it seems like they should have had a common keyword.  When they do make a distinction like Dreamer or Omnitron, I think they always have equal amounts of destruction for each.  And then there's the environments.  There is no forced Ongoing destruction (short of Imbued Vitality + damage), but there are a few mandatory equipment destruction cards.  Equipment is probably easier to pull back out of the trash though.

Card balance is definitely dependent on the hero's other cards and powers, and I'm glad it is.

Although I hate playing Wraith's self heal card (Suture Wounds or Self Suture I think) because it's kind of a waste, it works well in her deck because she is very dependent on powers via equipment.  So by making it an one-shot, it fits her synergy and flow much better.

Fleet of Foot is better than Bolster Allies in all ways indeed, but I think it was for both balance and theme reasons.  Tachyon is fast.  Legacy is fast, but not nearly as fast as Tachyon., it makes sense.  As powerful as Fleet of Foot is, I think if Legacy got a free play, it would be far more powerful relative to Tachyon.  Legacy has so many powerful cards that for him to get free plays would be making him far more powerful.  I'm actually surprised that Legacy has an extra power card (Legacy's Ring), since once he can use 2 powers, it's completely devestating to the villains (be immune to 2 damages, +1 to damage and immune to 1 damage, +1 to damage then deal 3 damage and everybody heals 1, etc).
