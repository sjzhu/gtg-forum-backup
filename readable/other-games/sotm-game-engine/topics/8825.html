You stay with no hand. If something tells you to discard a card (like Consider the Price), you just cant discard since you have no cards to discard. I think Consider the Price does more damage if less cards are discarded that way (and this is why)

Also, keep in mind that if you do not play a card and use a power during one turn, you may draw an extra card in your draw phase. So if your Wraith has no hand, an option is to skip playing a card and skip using a power to draw 2 cards.

If you have no cards in-hand and something tells you to discard, then you can't discard. So if "Consider the Price of Victory!" comes out there's a player with no cards, that's one less card that's going to be discarded. The only way to draw new cards is during your Draw phase (note - this is optional, as you never have to play/power/draw during those phases of your turn) or if some other effect says you can. At no point do you ever automatically draw a card for no reason. There is also no maximum hand - you could have your entire deck in-hand if the game went on long enough and you never played or discarded anything. I've certainly played at least one game (possibly two, both as the Adept) where I had my entire deck out (not all cards were in-hand, though - some were in play).

Note also that if something tells you you can "discard your hand" to do something (like Toxic Seaweed), a hand of zero cards is still a hand, so you can still "discard" it :).
