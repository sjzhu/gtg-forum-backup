Powerhound_2000:

Viz takes the damage unfortunately.   That loop can happen in the app currently and you end up with Viz taking the damage. 

This is because each redirection can really only occur once.  So, first the damage is redirected to the decoy projection, then it is redirected to Visionary (but reduced by 1).  If it was damage to the Decoy Projection instead, it would go to Visionary (from Smoke Bombs) and then to the Decoy Projection, but reduced by 1.
