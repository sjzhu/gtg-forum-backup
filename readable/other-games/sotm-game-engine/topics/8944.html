Hate to undercut phantaskippy's awesomeness, but I believe you are correct.  By the same token that Overdrive can give you two strikes during an incapacitated hero's "one player may play a card now" turn, or on his own turn even after he fires a Rod of Anubis or uses The Talisman...all of these work because they set "available powers for Strike" equal to 2, and setting it equal to 2 a second time wouldn't give you two more.  Fixer is from the same set as Expatriette, and I believe it's been confirmed that if Expat uses Load to play Unload and get extra powers, her earlier use of Load counts toward the total; this supports the same view that you get a running "available powers" total, and that Overdirve redefines that number rather than incrementing it.

There are numerous effects which allow you to do things (i.e. play a card, use a power, draw a card) outside of the relevant phase. I'm not sure what having access to a power phase means.

My interpretation of Overdrive, as it is written, is that whatever phase of whatever turn you happen to be in, you can use the Strike power twice that turn. Repercurssions of this would be as follows:

  • If it happens to be your (Mr. Fixer's) turn and you get a power use anyway, you can use the Strike power two total times on your turn.
  • If it is your turn and you have a relic that grants you a power other than Strike, you can use the relic power (this is the one power granted by your power phase) and then still use Strike two times (two Strikes in this turn, granted by Overdrive).
  • If you are prevented from using Powers you cannot use Strike, as it is a power.
  • If you manage to play Overdrive twice in the same turn (yours or someone else's), the second Overdrive has no effect as it doesn't change the benefit conferred by playing the first Overdrive (you can use Strike twice this turn - not twice more).

The only thing about this card that confuses me is that it doesn't say -when- you can use the power. For example, if you are Mr. Fixer and you play Overdrive during your play phase, can you skip your Power phase, draw a card during your Draw phase, and then use Strike twice during your End phase?

I'm going to weigh in against my action that kicked this thread off and say I was not allowed the 3d or 4th use of strike that turn.

Playing Overdrive again would of course grant me the right to use Strike twice during that turn, which I had already done.

The only thing about this card that confuses me is that it doesn't say -when- you can use the power. For example, if you are Mr. Fixer and you play Overdrive during your play phase, can you skip your Power phase, draw a card during your Draw phase, and then use Strike twice during your End phase?

Yes.  You get to use strike twice during that turn, at any point in that turn you can use them.

In fact if Adept lets you play overdrive, you can wait until he finishes his power chains (possibly boosting your damage, or even play a second card, like Bloody Knuckles or a tool or style) and then before his turn ends use strike twice.

It may be good to get an official ruling on that, but there is no limitation as to when you get to use it.  I generally resolve it as soon as it is played, just for bookkeeping sake, but there is no reason to believe that it must be done that way.

 

I didn't think about it at the time because I was rolling late at night and had honestly never faced that situation, and without analyzing thought "Whoa, I could play Salvage yard and that would let me play overdrive again bfore knuckles wears off, wee!!"

Oh well, doing it again I would have had Expat play unload then, and the difference would have evened out pretty close.

 

Lastly, while we talk about a "Power Phase" or "Draw Phase" those terms simply mean the built in time of your turn where you get to do that activity without anything else telling you to.

The turn goes:

Start of turn

Play a card

Use a power

Draw a card

End of turn

 

Those aren't the only time you are able to do those activities, it is just the only hardwired in time to do them.  Outside of that you have to have instructions to do so.  Some cards even let you end your turn on the part of you turn where you start your turn.  All kinds of interactions can happen.

The idea of those cards granting you a free phase is troublesome, because if Adept can grant Bunker an out of turn power phase, and Bunker has turret mode out he would get to use an additional power.  That isn't how it works.
