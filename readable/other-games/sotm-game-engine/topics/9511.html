I still think Fixer is one of the weaker heroes (not that the gulf is large).  Sure, he can dish out a lot of damage with a good setup, and has somewhat better odds of a good setup than other heroes who can do better.  But he's still fairly limited in the range of effects he can produce.  No Ongoing destruction, no villain deck control, little ability to contribute if heroes can't use Powers, even less if they can't play cards in the early turns, and virtually none if he can't deal damage, unless he has one of a very small number of powerful utility cards in his hand (Grease Gun, Salvage Yard, am I forgetting anything?).  That combined with the fact that many of his card plays sabotage each other make him fairly narrow and situational.  He can be an all-star in certain situations that are not exactly rare, but he can't always be counted on to help, and he only rarely proves the best contributor.

Compare all this to, say, Tempest or The Argent Adept.  It's clear that, while the heap is pretty evenly-distributed, there are heroes which are at the top, and Fixer is at the bottom.  (Although given that I can name more very-strong heroes than very-weak ones, perhaps "heap" is the wrong metaphor..."trench" perhaps?)

Things Mister Fixer can do other than specifically dealing damage:

  • Penetrating DR. 
  • Allowing others to penetrate DR.
  • Returning destroyed equipment to players' hands (while dealing extra instances of damage in the same play!)
  • Redirecting particularly common damage instances (2-3 damage).
  • Preventing all non-hero targets from dealing damage.
  • Changing damage type to preferred types.

Things Ra can do other than specifically dealing damage:

  • Putting cards from the Environment trash under the Environment deck.
  • Healing himself 3 hp.
  • Making the party immune to fire damage (at the expense of dealing significant damage).
  • Providing a flat +1 damage buff.

 

I've said it before and I'll say it again, Mister Fixer is not not not not not a "damage-oriented" hero.  Ra, Haka, Expatriette, and even Bunker will consistently out-damage Fixer.  That's fine, because it's not his primary role.  He's there to "fix" the situations that come up as they arise.  Need an "off-tank"?  He can do that.  Need some global damage?  Pull out the Crowbars or the Jack Handle.  Need to punch through DR?  He's got that handled.
