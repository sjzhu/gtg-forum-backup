I generally wouldn't bother unless I had knowledge of the top card(s) of the deck and it mattered. So if you're playing Nightmist or Omnitron-X is on the table, and I'd seen my top card, then I would shuffle. But if I don't know what the top card is or I know it but the only thing that is going to happen is I'll draw it on my next turn, I likely wouldn't go through the hassle of shuffling.

Really, what matters I think is whether or not you've seen any of the deck. If the deck is randomized, then shuffling it won't make it any more random. If you've set cards in certain spots, like with Foresight or whatever, or seen it because of looking through the deck, then you have to shuffle.
