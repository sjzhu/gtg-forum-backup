I'm no expert but I believe you did it right.  The moment Blade is reduced to 0 HP, before anything else happens, he would normally be destroyed (there might be exceptions on cards which do something when destroyed, such as Ambuscade's Sonic Mine or Unity's Bee Bot; it's unclear exactly how long those stay in play and whether they can resolve their effects from the trash, but other cards definitely stop instantly when destroyed, we have a long-standing ruling about Visionary's Wrest the Mind which makes this definite).  Flipping him to his second side replaces his destruction, and you would then resolve both Backlash Field and Flame Barrier.  Note however that if Ra reduced the Baron below 0 HP with the original attack, the extra damage does not carry over to the Baron; he always heals up to 30 HP after flipping.  This Flame Barrier interaction might be one of the promptest ways to start cutting him down again, though.
