The game text of uh, yeah is treated as the text of all ongoing cards in the selected hero's play area.  If the ongoing cards in that area change, the card is immediately updated.  When Expect the worst leaves play nothing happens to Guise, since uh, yeah was copying that text, and scholar's card had no effect for Guise.  It was scholar's card acting, not Guise's.

However, if guise was before scholar in turn order, at the start of Guide's turn the copied text would trigger, healing Guise and destroying uh, yeah I'm that guy.  Because in this instance it is the copied text on Guise's card acting.
