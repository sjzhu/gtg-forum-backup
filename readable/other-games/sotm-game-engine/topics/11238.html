It is reset for each target. For example, if Visionary puts Wrest the Mind of the Electro Pusle Explosive it will look like this:

Target first hero target with 15 damage, redirect to another target. EPE and Visionary take 3 damage.

Target second hero target with 12 damage, redirect to another target. EPE and Visionary take 3 damage.

Target third hero target with 9 damage, redirect to another target. EPE and Visionary take 3 damage.

Since EPE's hp, and therefore damage, changes between targets, a different amount of damage will be dealt.

Another example of AE damage changing would be Ra using Scorched Earth when there are multiple environment targets out - say there are four of them currently in play. Scorched Earth will do four points of damage (assuming Ra has no buffs of any kind) to all non-hero targets. When this is applied to the first of the environment targets (the order is player choice), if that's enough to destroy it then there will now be three environment targets reamining, so Scorched Earth's damage will now be three. If that's enough to kill the next target, then the damage will reduce again, and so on.

So in a case like this, it's a good idea to attack all the villain targets first (assuming you do want them all destroyed at this point - sometimes you might want to leave someting in play for various reasons) so that they take maximum damage, then attack the environment targets in the order that seems most useful if the damage is enough to destroy any of them.
