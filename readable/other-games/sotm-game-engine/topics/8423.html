If a card has damage increased (not specific type, just all damage) and they use a power that hits for multiple kinds (say Bob does one target 2 melee damage and 2 fire damage), is the total damage increased by 1 or because they are two different kinds of damage they are both increased by one?

They are both increased by 1.

Such as if Fanatic uses Exorcism for 1 melee and 1 radiant, and Legacy as Galavanized this turn, she would instead do 2 melee and 2 radiant.

#4

Even if they were the same type, but dealt in separate instances, each would be increased by 1. For example, if a card said (for whatever reason) "Deal 1 melee damage and deal 1 melee damage."  and Galvanized had been played by Legacy, you would deal a total of 4 melee damage (2 melee damage and 2 melee damage).

#5

The one exception is Dual Crowbars, which specifies that it does the same amount of damage on the second swing.  Thus, it benefits from buffs only once, but will suffer from DR twice (Fixer w/Dual Crowbars against a Gene-Bound Guard would deal 2-1 Melee, which equals 1.  He would then deal it 1-1 Melee damage, making it zero.)

Ronway confirmed this at Kantcon with Christopher:
 

https://greaterthangames.com/forum/topic/answers-to-debated-questions-3871
