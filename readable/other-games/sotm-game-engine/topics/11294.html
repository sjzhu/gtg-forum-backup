Whenever a card affects a hero's hand or trash, the card refers to "a hero." Since The Sentinels isn't the first deck to deal with multiple targets (Unity's droids, Expatriette's flak jacket, Visionary's projection), I believe there has been no problem with affecting an entire hero's board as far as taking damage. It's always been referred to as hero targets.
