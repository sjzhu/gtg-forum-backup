Not quite. Cards under Savage Mana have no text, so they aren’t targets. Ammo Drop triggers on villain cards, which they still are, even though they have no text. A villain card has a villain back, which it still has under Savage Mana. A target needs hp on the front, which it doesn’t have while under Savage Mana.
