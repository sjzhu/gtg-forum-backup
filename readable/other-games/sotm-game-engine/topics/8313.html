Your character card is indestructable. So you can't choose that.

The card gives you an Either/Or choice. If you do not have 2 cards to discard, you must destroy one of your cards in play (not character card obviously). If you do not have a card in play to destroy, you must discard 2 cards. If you have no cards in play and less than 2 cards in your hand, you would discard that one card. If you have no cards in hand and none in play, well, you were in a bad spot to begin with now wernt you?
