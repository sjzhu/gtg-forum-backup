Spmething we know about whenever a card is destroyed, any destruction effect may still happen. Take Savage Mana versus Omnitron, if Haka does the killing blow to Omnitron, then you have the option to place him under Savage Mana, but Omnitron will still leave the game afterwards. If you choose to have Omnitron removed from the game first, he is no longer in the game to put under Savage Mana.

This is similar to Prime Warden Captain Cosmic's power, thanks to the "instead" on it. If it is first put under Savage Mana, you can totally shuffle it into PW Captain Cosmic's deck. However, if you choose to shuffle it first, then the "instead" would mean it wasn't actually destroyed, thus Savage Mana cannot trigger.

According to the text of Prime Wardens Captain Cosmic, the timing worksheet is wrong.  Step 17 applies to cards that say "would be destroyed" (like Unforgiving Wasteland), indicating that the effect replaces the destruction.  Cards that say "is destroyed" (like PWCC) respond to the destruction rather than replace it, and are handled by step 18.  It could be that PWCC is intended to be a replacement effect but was worded as a response effect, in which case the text should be changed from "is" to "would be" (this has happened before).
