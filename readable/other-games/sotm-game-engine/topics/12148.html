I just noticed a huge bug in how Omnitron is written.  His text reads, in part: "When Omnitron is destroyed, remove Omnitron's Villain Character card from play. The Heroes cannot win until there are no Devices in play."

Clearly, the final sentence is meant to remain in force, even though the card it is written on has been removed from play.  This sets a weird precedent.  On the other hand, I can't think of a clearer way to word it, since most players (including me) do what seems to be the correct thing with this card.  It took me ages to even notice there was a problem here.

Doing some searches on the spoilers, it appears that nothing else in the game *definitely* removes a card from "in play".  Unforgiving Wasteland is an arguable edge case; with our current timing understanding, it would remove cards while they were stll in play. Anyone feel like arguing that when UF removes a card from the game, the text on that card stays in effect?  I don't really think this is *right*, but I think it's a technically arguable position, and could lead to some even stranger edge cases than UF already produces.

The "When Destroyed" keeps Omnitron in play until the last effect is activated.

 

That effect activates When Omnitron is destroyed.

It lasts "until there are no devices left in play."

The Effect is "The heroes cannot win."

The card leaves play, but the effect is a triggered effect that has a clear cut start and end time.  The effect is not static, so it doesn't end when the card leaves play.

 

The Operative's "Heroes cannot win" effect is a static effect, that effect enters play with her and leaves play with her.  Omnitron's is different because it triggers with a set cutoff time.
