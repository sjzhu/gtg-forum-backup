PY

2015-07-22 20:12 UTC
#34

It's immediate. The ruling of Order of Operations for Start- and End-of-turn effects is based on when the card as a whole enters play, not the particular text. Since the villain card as a whole (both sides) was in play first, it acts first.

So, after a villain flips, you do not have to remember how many cards came out before it flipped and after it flipped.

Another example is where one of the Ennead that came out later dies first. Even though it flipped first, the incapped ability of an Ennead that entered the battle earlier but died later would trigger first.
