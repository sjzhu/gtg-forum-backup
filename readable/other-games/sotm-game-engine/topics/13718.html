No, because he isn't destroyed. When a hero character card would be destroyed, you flip that card instead. Savage Mana specificaly acts on being destroyed. If a hero were to be killed by by the environment with Savage Wasteland out, they would be removed from the game as that has the text "would be destroyed" rather than "When Haka destroys a target".
