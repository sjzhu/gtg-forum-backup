I think for the Sentinels, anything that affects "A Hero" or "A Hero Target" becomes player's choice on which Sentinel is affected.

Anything that affects multiple targets can affect more than 1 of them.  For instance; "All Hero Targets" would affect all Active members of the Sentinels.  "__ damage to Hero with the highest HP and ___ damage to the Hero with the lowest HP."  If Dr. Medico had the most out of everyone and Writhe had the least HP out of everyone, they would both take damage.

Basically, they are always treated as separate Hero targets, except for certain conditions such as Infection in Plague Rat's deck.  The player chooses which Sentinel is actually Infected, but for the purposes of flipping Plague rat, your entire "Hero Card" is considered Infected (but, only the Sentinel that is actually Infected suffers the affects)
