I seem to remember seeing specific rules on when to reshuffle the trash upon emptying a deck, but I can't seem to find them anymore.

1) Do I reshuffle the trash when I need to "reveal" cards from an empty deck?

2) Do I reshuffle when I'm asked to "discard" from an empty deck?

3) If my deck becomes empty, do I reshuffle immediately, or do I wait until I need to draw/reveal/discard from that deck?

I know this sounds nitpicky, but I've been in situations where it matters. Like, "Reveal cards until X equipment is found," when there's only 1 Equipment card left in the deck and I know I'd have to reshuffle the trash to get enough to fulfill the card.

Do I take the most recently discarded/destroyed target? Choose randomly? Or put in whichever one the players agree to put in?

Also, if asked to reveal cards from a deck and do different things with each of them when there aren't enough cards, you only do as much as you can, in order. As an example, if you're playing the Adept and use Arcane Cadence (which reveals five cards) when you only have three cards left in your deck, you'd reveal those three and do the first three things listed on Arcane Cadence (but culd choose which card each thing applied to - the reveal order doesn't matter, only what you need to do with them when your actions are limited by not having enough cards).
