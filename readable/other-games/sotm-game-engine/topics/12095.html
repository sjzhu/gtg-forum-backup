They remain in play. Their text most likely has no effect, since omnitron is not in the game, but they may have some effect based on other cards in play (Sub Zero ongoing destruction, Ra's card that does damage based on ongoing, and so on). In addition, you continue to play one card each villain turn from Omnitron's deck until the heroes win (by destroy all devices) or are defeated. Since Omnitron's character card is destroyed, you don't do either of omnitron's bonus end of turn plays.
