It's interesting you brought up Bunker. An ability that says you can take a card into hand is probably a legit way to gain cards while in turret mode. Draw is a pretty core term, so I could see them being different since they didnt use that word. 

But mechanically speaking, taking the top card of your deck into hand is really the same thing as drawing a card. And in that case, should there even be a difference?

although, in a situation where you reveal the top card and decide whether to put it into your hand or replace it then take it, I think I would be hesitant to call that drawing as those were both very different effects mechanically.

With all that said, I think the safe answer Is to assume they are different (else why not just say draw for consistency sake).

It is different, you cannot draw cards from your trash, and it selects a card.
