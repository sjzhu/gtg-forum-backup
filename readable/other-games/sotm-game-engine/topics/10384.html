Hello,

Is there a difference between "Destroyed" versus "Discarded"?  My thought was that "Destroy" means to put out-of-play (i.e. no longer able to be used for that match) and "Discard" means put it in the appropriate discard pile for potential re-circulation and/or selection based on card ability.  The issue came up with regards to Expatriette.  When her "ammo" cards are used, the are "Destroyed" (which would make sense because it's ammo).  This would mean that she *never* gets them back for that session (limited amount of ammo, for example).

Is this correct?  Or, do "Destroy" and "Discard" *both* go back to the appropriate discard piel for potential re-circulation and/or selection based on card ability?

Thanks in advance!

-Ronicus

Destroy means go to the trash from play.

Discard means go to the trash from your hand.

Both get recirculated.

 

There are very few times something will be permanently removed from play, and not recirculated.. For those situations, the cards will explicitly say so.

Discard is from cards that are not in play. If an effect says to discard cards without clarification, it means from your hand; if it specifies that you must discard from the top of your deck, from revealed cards, et cetera, then of course you discard from there. Only cards in play can be destroyed.

That seems to cover everything.
