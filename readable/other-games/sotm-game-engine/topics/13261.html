Hey all,

My friends and I encountered an issue or two while playing and we would like some feedback. Someone was playing Expatriette couldn’t play any cards from her hand because they were all limited ongoings/equipments already in play. We know there’s no way to actually play them, but we wanted to know if she could activate her power, even if it meant nothing happened. So:

A.) Can this Expatriette still activate her innate power if there are cards in his hand, but they cannot be played? (E.g. If for some reason you wanted to activate the effect of one of Spite’s “PL626 Compound XI” drug card.
B.) Can her “Play a card” power still be activated if there are 0 cards in the player’s hand?

Similarly, The Wraith’s card “Grappling Hook” reads as follows:
"Destroy 1 ongoing or environment card.

You may draw a card."

So:

C.) Could The Wraith play “Grappling Hook” from the hand if there were no ongoing or environment cards to destroy?

Thanks,
The Hootenanny Crew.

PS: Spite’s drug card reads as follows: “Whenever a hero used a power, Spice deals them 2 Toxic damage and that hero’s player discards the top 5 cards of their deck.”

Let's see…I think the text for "Load" just says "Play a card", doesn't it? So you would have to play something, and if you ever try to play a Limited card that's already in play, the new one goes to the trash and never manages to enter play. So I suppose using that power when you have no applicable cards would basically amount to "discard a card".

I would also expect that you can activate the power when there are no cards in hand - it just wouldn't do anything.

And Grappling Hook can be played, I'm sure, if there are no ongoing or environment cards to destroy. Note that there is no "may" about this card - you must destroy something if there is something to destroy, even if that means taking out a fellow hero's ongoing card. If there are absolutely no environment or ongoing cards in play then you would just skip to "You may draw a card", at which point you can choose whether or not to do that (as it says "may").

The only way a Limited card can attempt to come into play is from cards that put cards into play from top or bottom of your deck.   In those cases the card goes into the trash.  If you only have Limited cards in your hand and they are already in play then you have no valid cards to play.
