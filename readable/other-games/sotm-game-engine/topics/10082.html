It just goes to show that sometimes my first impressions are utterly, completely wrong.

My first game was with Reckless, Nielzabub, and Rapscalion back in original edition. They had played a couple games already so I asked what hero they didn't like because I was used to playing games with people who alway played the same characters or same group position.  I figured that way I wouldn't have to compete with people for heroes in future games.  They told me Visionary wasn't very good.  I took her, and shuffled her deck, as we prepared to square off with Omnitron.  I quickly came to like her deck control and then an "Electro Pulse Explosive" happened.  Everyone dropped what they had planned for their turns and started discussing what they could do to get DR or damage it.  I just looked down at my hand and see this card called "Wrest the Mind" that I had been reading for half the game trying to figure out what a target was and why I would ever want to take that much damage of my own for anything.  (When we first started we were really conservative with hit points and almost revered DR as the holy grail of effects.)  I just said, "Guys, I got this."  I throw it on the "Electro Pulse."  Reckless pulls out his Kindle and jumps on the forums to check that it was a legal move.  Then we go through the round and when the villian turn comes up he asks, "Alright so you want to redirect it the explosive and blow it up."  To which I respond with, "No, I want to hit Omnitron."  I hit Omnitron. Then he explains to me how the damage on "Wrest the Mind" works like an interupt and asks me if I want to redirect the next hit to the "Expolsive" and he tells me I will take some total amount of damage that I can't actually remember off the top of my head (I can't remember how many heroes we were using.)  I respond with, "It's a three to one damage exchange rate!  I will gladly take that."  And from then on she has always been one of or depending on the day my favorite character.
