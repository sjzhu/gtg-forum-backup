Yeah, two heroes is SUPER rough. We suggest in the Advanced Play segment near the end of the rulebook that, when playing two with two players, that at least one of the players control 2 heroes. Sentinels of the Multiverse is technically a 1 to 5 player game, but does best with 3-5 heroes.

Paul and I often play (and do much of the playtesting) by each controlling two heroes, and since hero turns are just play a card, use a power, draw a card, it goes very smoothly.
