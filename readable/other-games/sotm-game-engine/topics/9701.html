The Scenario:

I'm playing in Freedom Tower. Entry Point comes out and is placed atop a Room. The Room card beneath Entry Point is then destroyed.

What happens?

Occam's Razor and the standard way of reading SOTM cards say that what happens is, the Room is put into the Environment trash, and Entry Point remains in play; Entry Point has nothing saying that something happens if the Room card ceases to exist, so it's still there, the same as if it didn't require an attachment.

It is possible this is not the intent, however. It could be intended to attach to a different room instead, or get shuffled in per what you do if there is no Room card in play when it is revealed. These seem like more arcane readings of the card.

I can make both fit thematically (you successfully disabled the effects of that Room, but did not literally destroy the Room, so that point of entry is still there for the villains to take advantage of until you specifically address it; or, the Entry Point *is* addressed, and they have to go looking for another point of access), but I want to be sure Occam's Razor is working properly here.
