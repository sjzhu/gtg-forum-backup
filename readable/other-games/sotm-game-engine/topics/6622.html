Well, in the case of a trash, there is no defined order (it can be rearranged) and can be searched at any time (and, in some cases, such as Tachyon, Citizen Dawn, and a few others, must be searched at times).  This makes "revealing" from the trash until the first available to not really work at all.  Thus, any time you are to take a card from the trash, you just take a card from the trash (if there are restrictions on which card you can take, make sure the card fits with the restrictions).
