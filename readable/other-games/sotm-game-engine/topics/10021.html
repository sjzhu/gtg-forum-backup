Yep, same as Infection - one hero is Infected, but as the Sentinels are collectively one hero, you then choose which of the four individuals recieves the actual Infection. Same goes for anything else which targets the Sentinels as a "hero".

On Spite flipping - the condition for him hitting himself for Victim damage is that he has flipped - once he's flipped, he no longer lifetaps, and the Safehouse specifically triggers on his "flipped to Drug-Wracked Monstrosity side". So he'll then hit himself for "Victims x 5" damage and soak H-2 points of the damage (from that drug that lets him soak once per turn) but also deal +1 damage to himself (from the damage-buffing drug). If H=3 then the two cancel each other out.
