Random picks pitted Legacy, the Sentinels, Captain Cosmic, and Ra against Plague Rat (in the Mobile Defense Platform, for the record, though that never amounted to anything). It’s not the best possible setup, but far from the worst.

Now, I’m sure I’m not the first person to realize that Imbued Flame + Flesh of the Sun God doesn’t just de-fang Plague Rat, it grinds his fangs into powder, mixes the dust with kitty litter, and flushes the mixture down the toilet, because all heroes become immune to the damage they’d inflict on themselves while infected or that another card would make them inflict on one another. It was still a delight to discover, though, because it freed Legacy up to Galvanize people without me having to worry about suicide pacts.

What really made it fun, though, was Captain Cosmic deciding to buff Ra with Augmented Ally to make sure he could still deal damage while more or less immunizing the party from harm, Autonomous Weapon so that power would hurt even more, and Dynamic Siphon to punish Plague Rat for damage he inflicted directly.

I almost wish I’d left Plague Locus in play to put the screws to Plague Rat just a little harder.

I’m not totally sure this one is legal, but…

Setback, Tempest, and Omnitron-X took on Kismet in the Block. Setback had Wrong Place and Time in play, allowing him to spend tokens to redirect damage to targets of his choice. While the Talisman was in the hero play area (Omnitron’s, if you were wondering), a Block Guard came into play. It wanted to shoot the Talisman for 1 HP and render it incapable of inflicting damage; because of Wrong Place and Time, it was redirected to Setback; because he always had at least one token in his pool, Setback redirected it to Kismet and she was left unable to inflict any damage to the heroes for the final 44 HP of her criminal career.

I’m still chuckling.

When speaking of fun interactions, there's always the obvious two: Mister Fixer in Time Cataclysm's Fixed Point being able to use multiple tools/stances at once, and The Naturalist in a Fixed Point being able to morph into all three forms at the same time.

Another obvious one that has led to many trivial games is Ra's Flesh of the Sun God + Insular Primalis's Volcanic Eruption. Legacy's Take Down + Atlantis's The Pillars of Hercules is a wonderful one, especially if you have multiple Take Downs or you have Visionary use Mental Divergence to recycle them.

Absolute Zero in Atlantis's Phospherescent Chamber is pretty fun! It buffs his self-healing via cold damage by +2.

In addition to the 'Vernal Sonatas + Reclaim from the Depths + Take Down' combo above, I had a pretty good run with Argent Adept on a different game by comboing off of Scholar. Specifically, using Inventive Preparation to let the scholar play extra cards.... repeatedly.

Fun cards to play from Scholar:
Expect the Worst when Alchemical Redirection is in play (all damage goes to the Scholar, reduce all damage the scholar takes to zero)
Know When to Hold Fast - lets him draw 5 cards without ending his turn (amy of the cards that end your turn are good targets, Mr. Fixers Grease gun also comes to mind)
Don't Dismiss Anything - turns one card play into one per deck, albeit unpredictably. In a 5 player game this can be epic.

The king of the  combos, however, is Proverbs and Axioms. Everyone draws one card, then either heals for two or uses a power and hits themselves for 3.... and then Argent Adept takes the hit and starts the play over again.
By chaining together cards like that, and using the Prime Warden Agent Adept,  and/or using Silver Shadow, I've been able to use every instrument in Argent Adepts deck in one turn. Works exceptionally well when Scholar has enough card draw to cycle through cards relatively fast.

In the simplest case, all N non-Hero targets would take N points of damage (in single-point packets).  If multiple targets have <=N health, then timing matters, and you may not be able to do that much total damage.

Since the damage is delivered in 1-HP packets, the effect of buffs and damage resistance are also (approximately) multiplied by N.
