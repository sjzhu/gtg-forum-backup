If your character reaches 0 HP, you flip them over. Then, every time it's your turn, rather than taking a normal turn, you chose one of those three actions as your turn.

When your hero is reduced to 0 HP, you remove all cards, except the character card, for that hero from play and flip the character card. Then each turn, that hero can use one of those three bullet pointed abilities but that's it as they no longer have a hand or deck to play with.
