Sounds like you had a magnificent game.  Yes, both cards played by DDA would have all of their normal effects even if it's not the right turn.  (One way another poster on this forum explained it to me, which I really liked, is that Unload's "you may use X powers this turn" works because your power phase is normally the game as a whole saying "you may use a power now".  If you play Load on your own turn, you could effectively resolve its effect immediately, using a power for each of your Guns, and then you couldn't use your Power normally because you've already used one or more powers.  It's a little abstruse, especially when you look at interactions like Embolden or Load, but in general the meta-rule of SOTM seems to be "don't overthink it; do what seems obviously right".  After all, it's co-op, so not much is at stake if you accidentally do it "wrong"…at most it means your bragging rights might be unjustified, but big deal, it's not like there's money or nationwide prestige on the line.)
