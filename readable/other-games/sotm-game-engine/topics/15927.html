I agree the tricky thing with the normal Fanatic is that she has lots of different useful cards without quite enough big damage or card draw to ensure you can keep out zealous offense. The alternate versions have a way easier time. If you are going to make a specific effort to use it under sub optimal settings, that means you need to accept that Fanatic thrives on getting damaged. I think she is pretty easy to play ok but difficult to play optimally. In deed you should often be aiming for Fanatic to be the lowest hp. It seems risky but one way or another you should get the Aegis in the nick-of-time or if not you often get an early play of her big blast card for massive damage right before incap. Most villians are basically toast if they take either a 25+ damage shot from fanatic or if they waste damage on Fanatic just to have her bounce back again (and maybe again) at 10.

My favorite is also to start with chastise since it is fun to have chastise go away to instantly be replaced with zealous offense. I most recently used this for an easy victory against Chairman keeping those nasty gunmen where I could see them until Haka could put them in his sack.
