Rod of Anubis and Overdrive!

 

Did you know it works this way?

 

With Rod of Anubis in Mr. Fixer's play area, Mr. Fixer plays Overdrive, which allows Mr. Fixer to use his 'Strike' power twice this turn. Then, in the power phase, Mr. Fixer used the Rod of Anubis. Afterwards, he can still use 'Strike' twice!

 

Combined with Dual Crowbars and Grease Monkey Fist, Mr. Fixer wiped out the remaining members of the Ennead, doing six shots of damage for a total of 29 HP.
