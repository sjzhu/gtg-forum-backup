If a hero has the Vitality Conduit in front of them and a villain plays a card that deals them 1 X damage and 1 Y damage, does the Conduit activate once or twice?

The answer is zero, if it's the hero being hit.  If it's the Conduit being hit, then it will be twice, assuming the conduit survives both hits.  If it breaks, it won't trigger for the damage that broke it (or any damage after that)
