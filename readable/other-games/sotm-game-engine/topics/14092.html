Remember it is end of next turn, so you can have a form in play, activate another form, then for your next turn power activate the third form and play a one-shot.  Then if you have the second power use throw down Natural Form's Power for all three effects.

With 2 of Croc, Powe and Vigor in play and the other one and Primal Charge in hand and a banked form active from the last turn you can deal 2 targets 2 damage, 1 target 3 damage one target 4 damage, draw 6 cards and regain 2hp.

It is a good time.

I find Bestial Shift less good, and Natural Form's Power much better with this version of Naturalist.

This promo also turned Naturalist from a Hero I viewed as really strong, but not as fun to a really fun hero who goes crazy if he has a form in play.

Just to make sure I understand how it goes (in case I want to make a tracker), is this correct:

Turn 1:

  • There are no forms in effect.
  • The player chooses a form (croc) on their Power phase.  Croc is in effect during their turn and until the end of their next turn.

Turn 2:

  • Croc is still in effect from last round.
  • The player chooses a form (rhino) on their Power phase.  During the player's turn, both croc and rhino are in effect.
  • At the end of their turn, croc goes away but rhino stays in effect until the end of their next turn.

This means that Naturalist will only ever have two forms in effect from the Desperate Prey power (not counting anything they'd get from an actual form card in play) while it's their turn, and when it's not their turn they can only ever have one form in effect.

Do I have it surrounded?

Here's what I'm going to use to track this:

It has two dials to show which form you chose this turn (top) and what form you chose last turn (bottom).  When your turn's over, you clear the "chosen last turn" dial (one of the choices on the dial is blank) and rotate the tracker so that your current choice (the top dial) becomes your previous choice (the bottom dial).  Rinse and repeat.

It's card sized for easy storage and will look good sitting next to your other cards on the table.  It lamentably uses placeholder art because... you know why, but I'll update that to the good stuff if/when I ever get it.
