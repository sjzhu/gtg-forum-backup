Yeah, it works just like Haka of Battle and Taiaha.  Only the very first actual strike gets the damage.  Also note that Critical Multiplier's bonus is not optional, so if Parse is last in the turn order and your villain is going to cause heroes to damage themselves, you should avoid playing it (or at least avoid discarding cards as much as possible).
