I think I've technically missed the video game discussion, but I don't care. I never get to talk about video games anymore.

On the Final Fantasy debate, it is safe to say that 6 and 4 are the best the series has to offer (in that order) and the third place game is 9. All three had a certain charm to their characters that kept the player intrested enough to continue playing, just to see what would happen to them. Now, it should be noted that I began playing video games as a way to experience more interactive books, which is why I generally play RPGs and didn't enter the world of other genres (except fighting) for years and years after I started. Anywho, I put characters above pretty much everything else when it comes to my gaming.

And, because of that, I shall now plug my favourite game series: Tales of... This is a series that rarely has a fantastic story, is always fun to play, and usually gives their cast several shining moments, thanks in no small part to the skits that litter the later games. You see the characters as heroes, but also as individual people, dealing with standard things. Legendia is (and with how RPGs are heading straight to the crapper of late, probably always will be) my favourite in the series, and possibly off all the games I've played. Tales of Legendia is the only game I've played where the credits roll and I don't harbor any ill will toward any main cast member. It's great that the cast had reason for their behaviour.

This is coming from the guy that writes character analyses for fun.
