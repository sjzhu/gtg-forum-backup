Not necessarily - Mr Fixer with Driving Mantis and a Pipe Wrench can redirect Iron Legacy's end-of-turn damage back to himself, as long as Iron Legacy isn't buffed. Random teams certainly aren't taboo against Iron Legacy.  I have a decent win record against the advanced version of him playing random teams.  There is probably a way for any group of heroes to win, even though it might take very specific draws, or incapacitated shenanigans, or no small amount of beating your head against brick walls.  It's certainly not your average recreational activity.

The teams that would do worst are the teams with zero ongoing destruction or the teams with miniscule damage.  It also seems like he doesn't scale quite as well as some villains - that is, a 3-hero team will have a much tougher time than a 5-hero team.  This is especially if the normal strategy against him is debuff him for a couple rounds, then one-turn him from >20 to zero on turn 3 or 4, right before he kills you.  Three characters just won't be as efficient at that.

So… who is good at him?  Anyone with a good amount of damage and a few ways to remove ongoings are probably the best.

I think the members of the Freedom Six are generally good at him, except Unity, who'll just have an infernally tough time keeping bots out.   I would avoid Omnitron-X (I'm talking advanced here - Plating is worthless and he can't keep components, unless you choose to flip him).  I'd avoid the Scholar in this fight against irreducible damage, since he also lacks ongoing removal and doesn't typically bring a large amount of damage.  Chrono-Ranger and Expatriette have good options.  Nightmist is always good, but she has to be on the ball to not die right away.  Fanatic has some good options in the fight as well.  Argent Adept and Visionary can field the ongoing destruction, but not so much the damage.  Ra, Haka, and Legacy can field plenty of damage, but not the ongoing destruction.   I would probably avoid Fixer - his damage isn't especially high very quickly, and he lacks ongoing destruction.
