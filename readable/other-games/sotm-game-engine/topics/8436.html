My brother and I were playing a game the other night against Citizen Dawn, and Citizen Autumn was in play.  Citizen Autumn, of course, destroys all ongoing cards of the hero with the lowest hp.  At one point Bunker and Fanatic were tied with the lowest hp.  Bunker had one ongoing card in play, and Fanatic didn't have any in play.  We chose to have Citizen Autumn target Fanatic since she didn't have any cards in play thus protecting Bunker's card.  Did we play this right?  

 

I think the main confusion comes from the wording on Citizen Autumn's card.  The wording makes it seem as if only one hero is targeted.  However, I'm not sure this is the case if two or more heroes have the lowest hp.  So, a second question, If two or more heroes have the lowest hp does Citizen Dawn target all of them?

 

Thanks for the help.

Whenever there's a tie like this, the players decide. So you can pick Fanatic and have her effect do nothing. You played it right.

The rulebook states the following on page 8:

>

Addiotnally, there are time when playrs may encounter ambiguous situations, such as tied HP. in any ambiguous sistuation, the players choose the outcome.

example: Baron Blade plays "slash and burn", dealing the hero with teh lowest HP 4 melee damage and the hero with the highest 6 fire damage. Absolute Zero and the Visionary are currently tied for the highest HP at 23 HP each. fter brief consideration, the players decide that the 6 fire damage should be dealt to Absolute Zero.

 

So yes, you played it right.
