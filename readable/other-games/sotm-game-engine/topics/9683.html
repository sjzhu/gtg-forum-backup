We have a houserule that targets that have some sort of retaliation won't hit themselves with it.  Gauntlets of Perdition will still activate, but Backlash Field, Flame Barrier, Combat Stance, the Chairman, etc. don't.

Not strictly official, but it makes a lot more sense than Wraith punching herself in the gut every round because she's got the Sewer Flu.

You can come up with thematic explanations for it.  Combat Stance is about Wraith having trained herself to be extraordinarily alert---but when she's infected, she's weakened, so maybe staying that vigilant actually puts an extra strain on her body.

 

For Flame Barrier, maybe when Ra's infected he starts having trouble controlling the fire around him.  He's keeping that barrier up all the time, but when the infection kicks in he gets dizzy, loses his focus, and burns himself.

 

You've gotta go a little bit afield from what the effects are normally signifying, but I think you can still keep things interesting and flavorful.
