I had a question about this rule and one of AZ's cards Thermal Shockwave.

Thermal Shockwave: Power: Absolute Zero deals up to 3 targets 1 cold damage each. Absolute Zero deals himself X fire damage where X = the total amount of cold damage dealt by Absolute Zero this turn.

 

So if HP Reduced = Dmg Dealt, does that mean that if he deals two targets damage and heals himself, he only takes 2/3 of that number in fire damage?

If AZ had Focused Aperatures out and did 2 cold to two villains each and himself for 6 damage -2 heal = 4 cold damage, does he only take 4 fire damage with no other damage bonuses active? 

That's kind of cool if the case, because that means he really only takes 2 damage to deal 4 damage fire damage if he also happens to have Isothermic Transducer out.

Any damage healed by Null Point Calibration Unit doesn't count toward the fire damage he deals himself as a result of Thermal Shockwave.  Even if a target has 1 HP and you deal 2 cold damage it still counts as 2 cold damage towards the fire damage Thermal Shockwave would deal.
