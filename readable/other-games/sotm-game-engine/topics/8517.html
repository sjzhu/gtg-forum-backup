OK, I've played this game a lot and i camt believe this never came up before in any of our game sessions. But im confident the forum will have an answer :)

If a card says that a target deals X irreducible damage, is the end result of the damage irreducible, or only X amount?

For example, let's say there is a card that's says "Mister Fixer deals a villain target 1 irreducible damage and currently there are two active actions that increase damage dealt by Mister Fixer by 1 so his total damage output is 3, but the villain has a damage reduction of 1. Does the villain:

a) take 3 damage because the entire damage instance is irreducible?

b take 2 damage because the reduction does apply to amounts higher than 1?

 

Follow up question:

If the original cards says "Mister Fixer deals 1 target 1 irreducible melee damage, but there is an active card that increases damage dealt by Mister Fixer by 1 AND changes that damage type to fire, does it then lose its irreducibility since it is no longer melee damage (and therefore no longer matching what the original card said)?

#2

Irreducible can be increased, but not decreased. No matter how much you increase it, it will never go back down, so Fixer would be dealing 3 damage.

 

Alternating Tiger Claw says something along the lines that all his damage is Irreducible. So no matter what type of damage it is, it will always be irreducible if he is the one dealing the damage. And if I card ever comes into existance that changes the type of damage, I would still say it would be irreducible, as it it more of a subtype.

a) and no, I believe. Irreducible damage can be increased but not reduced, so even if it had been pushed up you couldn't reduce it, and to the second point, irreductible is a seperate quality to the damage type; the latter can be changed without affecting the former.

To me, choosing to destroy a card or discard a card also includes choosing the card to destroy/discard.  So if there is no card, that is not an option.
