It’s been a while since I did this fun little puzzle. How to play all six instruments in a single turn? For ease of reference, I’m going to give the involved cards shorter names as follows:

  • “Super” is Inspiring Supertonic.
  • “Sub” is Alacritous Subdominant.
  • All Instruments are referred to only by the instrument name (Harp, Lyra, Drum, Bell, Horn and Pipes).

Step 1: During your play phase, play Polyphoric Flare to gain your first Power of the turn by taking 2 damage. Activate one Instrument that isn’t relevant to the rest of this sequence (the Pipes are a good choice, since they don’t do an Accompany).
Step 2: With your normal power, use the Lyra. Choose to Perform Super, regaining your power.
Step 3: With the Lyra still waiting to Accompany, use your third power of the turn to activate the Bell. Perform any Rhythm you like, then activate the Accompany of Sub. You now gain your fourth power, at the expense of Sub being destroyed as soon as the Bell finishes resolving. Choose to Perform Super again, regaining your power yet again.
Step 4: The Bell and Lyra are still pending; with this activate your fifth Power and play the Harp. Have the Harp use the Perform of Super again, and regain your power yet again.
Step 5: The Lyra, then Bell, and then Harp all have Accompanies pending, but first you must fully resolve one more Power; use this to play the Drum or Pipes. For ease of reference we’ll say it’s the Pipes; you Perform two Melodies and that power fully resolves.
Step 6: With the pending Harp accompany, activate a Harmony other than Sub. The Harp effect now fully resolves.
Step 7: The Bell activates the Accompany of Sub, which will be destroyed when it fully resolves. Use the resulting Power to activate the Horn, Perform one of your Melodies a second time, and then re-activate the Accompany of Sub, giving you one more Power. With this, you activate the Drum.
Step 8: The Drum activates a Rhythm Accompany and then Performs a Melody, possibly for the third time this turn.

Okay I think I did something wrong. I need to test this in the program to see how it resolves the timing of Sub’s Accompany - is it destroyed the first time one of the pending Accompanies resolve, or only the last time? If the latter, this is a much easier formula and doesn’t require Polyphoric Flare, unless you want to Vocalize on top of all six Instruments.

The bell and the horn can probably be switched, and the Lyra could move to virtually any position in the sequence, but it’s absolutely crucial that you start with bell and horn BEFORE doing harp, and end with pipes and drum in either order after harp. All this is of course assuming you have no additional powers from outside your deck, and no more than the bare minimum 8 cards in play (which would mean every Rhythm and Melody activation would go to waste, you need one each of those and preferably a second Melody to get the most out of this achievement, all the Instruments and 1 each of the two relevant Harmony cards is the minimum for the achievement itself). Throwing in a Polyphoric Flare or a Silver Shadow somewhere in this process helps, but not as much as you’d think; the need to get the exact sequence of “horn/bell before harp before pipes/drum” is the crucial part.
