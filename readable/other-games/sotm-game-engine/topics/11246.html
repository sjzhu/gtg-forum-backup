"Indestructible" might not be the perfect word, but it is the best word. 99.9% of the time when a card leaves play it is because it is destroyed. If you want to have a word that prevents that, having the perfect antonym would be the most intutive decision. Is it the clearest for all situations? Maybe not, but for 99.9% of the time it is clearer than any other word. Let's say it did say "Can't leave play," there would be many questions asking "How does a card leave play without being destroyed?" Which is better, to have a question each time the word came up, or have a question the rare times that an exception comes up.

Regardless, you have yet to give a good alternative to "indestructible." Everything you gave will lead to many more questions. For example, the card doesn't leave play, but was it destroyed to trigger "on destruction" effects? The way SotM is setup, this question is wide open, but by having the word indestructible, the question is completely eliminated.

However, there will be other specific cases. For example, if it said cannot leave play, how does it interact with Savage Mana? The card is still in play, so would Savage Mana work or not?

Essentially, it comes down to the following: if you can't have a perfect word, you want the most intuitive word, which would be the perfect antonym to the situation that comes up 99.9% of the time.

I also want to add that the argument of "I shouldn't have to read the rulebook" is ridiculous.

Any target can be reduced to 0hp or fewer - note on several villain cards it says "When X is reduced to 0hp or fewer, flip this card" or whatever (I think Apostate's flip condition says that, which is how you can get away with insta-killing him via Suckerpunch or other such cards since then you're not reducing his hp, you're just destroying him). It's just that normally you don't continue counting once you get to 0hp because any overkill damage in the hit isn't really needed.

With Fixed Point, you track the hp into the negatives and, as Pydro says, if a target goes into the negatives but then heals back up again to at least 1hp by the time Fixed Point goes away, that target isn't destroyed. Any targets that are at 0hp or fewer when Fixed Point goes away suddenly go "Oh crap, I have no hitpoints left, urgghhhh" :D.
