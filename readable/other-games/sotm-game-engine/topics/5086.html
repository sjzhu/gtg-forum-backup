There’s technically a table-uniqueness rule, so while you could have both Legacy decks in play, their abilities and equipment will have to be highly focused. As discussed on another thread, you could do a house-rules thing and use both of them - you’d just have to watch out for multiple copies of things like Lead from the Front. It’s not considered balanced to use both of them, and you couldn’t do so in a tournament, but for the flavor of it in your own private games, feel free.
