It goes by instances of damage, not points.  This matters for those effects that hit everything twice, such as Char, Explosives Wagon, Cursed Acolyte, and "Diplomatic Envoy".  In all cases, if you have a Decoy Projection or Stealth Bot out and one of these effects hits all hero targets, you can first choose to resolve the damage against one of the heroes.  You redirect the first instance to Decoybot, and if they're still alive, you then redirect the second instance.  You then move on to the next hero.  If enough damage reduction is in play, you might manage to have the Decoybot still be around after each hero character card has been damaged - in which case Decoybot itself is also directly targeted by the blast because it's a hero target.

For example, Char is in play and there are no bonuses to damage; Stealth Bot has 5 HP and it innately reduces all damage it takes by 1.  Char hits Unity for 2 fire and 2 toxic, which becomes 1 and 1 to Stealthy.  Then another hero takes 2 and 2, which becomes 1 and 1 to the bot.  Then a third hero takes 2 fire, which becomes 1 fire to Stealth Bot, killing it, and then that hero must take the 2 toxic.

Example 2:  All other heroes besides Unity are incapped.  Char hits Unity for 2 and 2, which becomes 1 and 1 to Stealth Bot.  Char then hits Stealth Bot for 2 and 2 which becomes 1 and 1.  Assuming Stealth Bot was at full, she survives, but if even one hero was alive and redirected damage to her, she wouldn't.
