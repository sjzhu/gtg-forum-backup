Like many people, I was very impressed with The Dreamer when I first played her.  The variety of Projections was reminiscent of Dawn, but with enough changes (most notably, a villain you don't want to hurt) to keep things fresh.  And when she flipped and started raining cards and damage at an absurd rate, it was just the kind of shock that good flip should be the first time you see it.

Unfortunately, for me at least, it just didn't hold up.  Once you know how much harder she is on her flip side, a clear tactic emerges: just kill all but one Projection (something like Whipacorn, or especially Stone Oni), then get to work playing out some massive, monolithic setup, so that you can stand to lose a few Ongoings and Equipments and still breeze through her flip in 2-3 turns effortlessly.  In a recent game I played involving The Naturalist, Setback, and Omnitron-X, this waiting game even managed to be HP-positive for most turns.  Clearly, this is a pretty boring tactic for the game to be rewarding.

But there has to be some fairly simple means of breaking it, to restore The Dreamer's greatness.  If you fight her "as intended," without artificially delaying the flip, she runs great!  So, what works best?  My first instinct is either to give her a Blade-style mechanic that ends the game once there are enough cards in the Trash, or a variant on that where the game ends outright after, say, 10-H rounds, and you add a couple to the clock when you flip her.

Even that feels a bit Bladey to me, though, so... what other ideas for ways to put the heroes on the clock do people have?
