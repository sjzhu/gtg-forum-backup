While Imbued Vitality is in play though, they have HP and can be destroyed by taking damage that drops them to 0 or below (or by Sucker Punch/Wrathful Gaze or similar).

When Imbued Vitality leaves play it doesn't drop HP to zero, it's just like a character card without a printed value (i.e. the incap sides of Hero cards or Miss Information), they don't have 0 HP they have no HP, they are not a target and are not destroyed because only targets with 0 (or less) HP are subject to destruction due to that.
