There was one card that was bothering us. Maintenance Level from Wagner Mars Base (Environment Card)

The card states:

"At the end of the envionment turn, play the next environment card"

"At the start of the environent turn, if each player shuffles thier trash into their hero deck, destroy this card."

"When this card is destroyed if "self-destruct sequence" is in play, put this card on top of the countdown pile"

We had Tachyon in play and this is quiet annoying for her, as her power is from burn cards. This essentially takes her out of the game, so we had were able to destroy the card eventually. However, we had to play the next environment card. So if we do not destroy this card by everyone shuffling their trash into their deck or using a destroy enviroment card, would this card stay in play and we would have to draw the normal environment card plus another one for the maintenance level? Dealing with 2 Environmental cards per round is tough.
