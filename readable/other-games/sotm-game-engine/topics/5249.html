One thing I’d like to note about Embued Fire, even thought it was officially stated that both effects (hero’s damage changed to fire and fire +1) were both static and didn’t rely on order, examples have shown that at least changing hero’s damage type does in fact rely on order (playing Embued Fire then Cramped Quarters results in melee damage, playing Cramped Quarters then Embued Fire results in fire damage). Basically a simple way to interpret the ruling is that any damage count modifiers simple at the time damage is being applied, and order doesn’t matter because damage count modifiers can’t cause confict (due to both the commutative and associative properties of addition).
