Someone posting on the Handelabra forums had an interesting situation come up:

I noticed an interesting behavior, perhaps not a bug, but definitely not what I was expecting. I was playing against Citizen Dawn with Visionary on the team, and Visionary had a Decoy Projection in play. The decoy had the lowest number of hit points on the team. Citizen Autumn came into play and targeted the decoy as the lowest hitpoint hero. Fair enough. Autumn then destroyed all of the ongoing cards belonging to Visionary. That surprised me. I would have guessed that Autumn would try to strip the ongoing cards from the decoy, and since the decoy had no ongoing cards that Autumn's attack would have had no effect. Is it the correct behavior that Autumn's attack targets the decoy but actually hits Visionary?

The wording on Autumn does seem a bit ambiguous.  She goes after "the hero with the lowest HP".  The existence of The Sentinels, in particular, complicates things.

Possible interpretations that I see:

1) read the ambiguous text as "the hero with the lowest HP Hero Character Card".

2) read the ambiguous text as "the hero with the lowest HP Hero Target Card".

 

1 feels right to me, but it's a wacky enough circumstance that I'd like to hear other opinions.  Or is there an interpretation I'm missing?

The important bit is the wording on Brain Burn. She deals herself X psychic damage, where X is the number of cards. That's why it's one "packet of damage." 

There are other cards that have different wording, and work the way you expected. Take a look at Omnitron's Technological Singularity. It says Omnitron deals 2 lightning damage for each card destroyed. If 3 cards are destroyed, then it deals 2 lightning damage 3 times (and each one is modified separately).

If the Visionary has Decoy Projection out and a card like Technological Singularity attacks her, it would work like B).
