I want to know more about every one of them.

I for one don't especially give a damn about, say, Hermetic or Major Flay.  We already know who Vyktor is, and there's probably not much to know about Citizen Slash or The Hippo (we already know Slash isn't Expat's father, which pretty much answers the only question I really had about him).  I'd say I only want to know more about maybe 2/3 or 3/4 of them (assuming you don't count the Crackjaws as individuals; that would make it closer to 4/5).

I had made a previous post where I said I thought it would be cool if all those other villains in the background of the Vengeance pic each got their own deck so we could weave them in with the V5 and not have to only fight the same V5 villains over and over again.  I guess the fact that all of those villains are actually already in the V5 decks lowers the chance that they'd get their own V5-style deck, which is a shame.

I still think it'd be worth houseruling.  Frankly, given what I've heard about Vengeance, it seems like a MASSIVE act of over-compression; I wanted the V5 to be fully-playable solo villains that are just capable of being slotted together into a team, but instead they're 5 cards short and have another 4 (5 for Blade) cards per deck which are random other villains working for them, which are always the same per deck…I'm rather disappointed.  Stripping those targets out to create new decks around them, then adding 9 (10 for Blade) cards to each deck, roughly quadrupling their HP (probably more for Fright Train and less for the two females, for flavor reasons - not that women can't be tough, of course, it just doesn't seem to fit either of their styles, as they seem more likely to dodge blows rather than enduring them), and patching whatever rules no longer work without the teamwork mechanic (about which I know nothing, I'm just presuming there is one) seems like an eminently worthwhile project.  And once that's done, we can start expanding these mini-nems into 20-card mini-decks for a Vengeance-style game, if not into full decks (creating whole new character cards for them).  Many would presumably be diff-1 villains, which the game badly needs more of.
