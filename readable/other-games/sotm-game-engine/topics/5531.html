Respectfully, I disagree. If the card listed a number of cards (such as: "1 player must discard 1 card to destroy this card"), then yes, you would have to discard that number of cards. However, if a card says to discard your hand, well, you can discard a hand of zero cards in order to fulfill that requirement. As a player, you always have a hand, even if you happen to have zero cards in hand.

The only time that this is not true is when your hero is Incapacitated. At that point, you've removed all of your cards from the game and no longer have a hand, unfortunately.
