>

15. If a hero does not take damage from an effect, such as Pushing the Limits, Solar Flare, etc. does the card remain in play?

No, they have to take the damage listed on the card. If Tachyon does not recieve at least 2 damage from Pushing the Limits, than it is destroyed. So if she is immune, redirected it, or reduced it to 1 damage, the card card has not fulfilled the condition and thus is destroyed. This applies to Solar Flare as well, so no Imbued Fire + Flesh of the Sun God tricks. However the damage type can be anything, just as long as the amount of damage listed is taken, the card will remain.

https://greaterthangames.com/comment/33371#comment-33371 A new ruling has been proposed by Christopher.

grim88:

Can someone give a quick recap of what Rule 15 is? Searching for it is not very effective.

Hahaha sure! (Ruling 15 is the colloquial term on the forum. It came from a post Ronway made detailing a few rules questions he asked Christopher)

The ruling focused on Ra's Solar Flare and Flesh of the Sun God combo. Initially, Christopher ruled that if Ra did not deal himself all 4 damage from Solar Flare that it would be destroyed. This extended to other similar effects like Fanatics Embolden, Divine Focus, Chastise and Tachyons Pushing the Limits. After some dialogue through the forums, Christopher altered his initial stance to allow for some of the self damage to be mitigated, but your hero would still need to take at least some of the damage to keep those cards in play.

Ruling 15 essentially broke Ra's super combo
