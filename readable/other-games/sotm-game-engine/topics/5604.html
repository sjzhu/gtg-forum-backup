But revealing is not the same as drawing, as drawing a card from the villian deck is then playing the card from the villain deck. In all current cases, only the specific cards mentioned are played, any unmentioned cards are shuffled back into the deck (at least I can’t recall anything else done with the unmentioned revealed cards). Thus, if you are to reveal cards but the mentioned cards to not appear, then the revealed cards are shuffled and replaced as the deck, no other cards are shuffled in with it.
