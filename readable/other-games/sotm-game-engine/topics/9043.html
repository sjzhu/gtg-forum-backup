Greywind is correct.

A card must be in play to be destroyed, which includes cards under other cards, unless those cards are specifically stated as not counting as in play (I think promo Blade says that) most cards under other cards are treated as not having text though, so they would not be equipment.  (the designation is important for a few rare occasions though)

Cards in hand or in the deck may be discarded, but not destroyed.  I only think the deck is involved if specifically mentioned, the one spite drug does that.
