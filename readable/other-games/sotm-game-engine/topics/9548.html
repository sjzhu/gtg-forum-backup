Incidentally, you're aware that Stealth can stack, right? You can use it over several turns and all those +2s add up…so say you use it for three turns in a row without getting hit, you now have a +6 reduction. But if you get hit by anything, even if it's for a single point of damage, all the Stealth goes away, even the points that aren't blocked. And if the damage was irreducible, you lose both the hp and the Stealth. So even worse if you're Infected and have some Stealth up while Combat Stance is out (not sure why you'd do this…unless it was your first time fighting Rat, I suppose ;)), because then you hit yourself for Infection damage, removing your Stealth, and then have to punch yourself in the face with your own Combat Stance. For three points of damage, if the Locus is still out!
