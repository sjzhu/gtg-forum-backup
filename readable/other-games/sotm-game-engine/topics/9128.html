This is wrong now.

The reason Effects that triggered on cards entering play happened before the text on the card was because the order the cards entered play is the order they were resolved in.

So any reaction to a card entering play was already in play before the card just entering, therefore it went first, followed by the card that just entered play.

That rule no longer applies, and the new rule is the order is resolved in the order the players choose.

 

So card enters play:

Its text and any triggers that activate on a card being played (or entering play) all try to happen at the same time.

The players then choose what order they happen in.

For Example (from our recent Forum game vs. IL Vigilence vs. accelerated assault:  Tachyone played Accelerated assault when she was at 1hp, we chose to have the damage go through to IL before vigilance incapacitated her.  If the card had been Hypersonic Assault or Wraith with Throat Jab there would not have been any retaliatory damage.

 

As for the Eye on the Prize double up it would go as such:

1.  Play Eye on the Prize:  choose to activate the card effect or the response (simultaneou)  Let's say you choose to do Eye first:

2.  Damage Informant 1, draw a card play Eye on the Prize:

3.  Now your choice is to resolve the second Eye on the Prize or the Informant response to the second Eye (the first has to wait for the first Eye to fully resolved, due to your previous choice.)  Let's say you choose Eye again.

4.  Deal Informant 1 damage, destroying her, then Operative deals you damage (since there is no competition for this timing there is no choice), then you draw and play another card.

5.  The two reactions waiting to occur do not occur, as the Informant left the field and cannot do anything anymore.

Now let's say in a fit of raging insanity you decide in step 4 to damage something other than Informant:

4.  Deal #wrongtarget 1 damage, draw a card, play a card, now you get a third choice, the card play for informant's latest reaction or the card itself, lets say you go with the card and it is your Danny Boy.

5.  Danny Boy is played, having resolved its effect: all at once Informant would process three card play reactions as the two Eye on the Prize cards resolve as well as Danny Boy, all at the same time.  (You could worry about the order they resolve in, but the result remains that three cards are played from the top of the villain deck, and your two Eye on the Prize go to the trash, while Danny Boy remains)

Okay, I agree with Pydro's last post, I don't think I was very clear in my explanation though.

What I believe the proper rules are:

1.  Text is always there, but it is not active or in effect until it enters play.

2.  When a card is playedenters play its text is now "In the Game" or in glossary terms, it takes effect.  This means reaction triggers are live (like Super Human) and static effects are affecting the game.

3.  The effects above do this because they have no timing when the card is played (they do not compete for timing at this time, powers and response triggers don't trigger by their card entering play, and static effects don't have timing this way)

4.  If the card is a One-Shot its effects are triggered by the card entering play.  This is a triggered reaction to the One-Shot card entering play and therefore has timing that can compete with response triggers.

 

Here's a Hypothetical example:

Lets say there is an ongoing card that has a power on it, the first line of the card text is "When this card enters play you may play a card now"  the second line is "POWER: do the Hokey Pokey"

When this card enters play (no response triggers in this example) the text takes effect and the first line triggers.  You could then play a card that said "Use a power now!" and that power could indeed be the power from the first card played, because that text is in the game, it is "live" you may indeed do the Hokey Pokey.

You do not need to wait for the first line to fully resolve for the Power to be usable, that text is in the game the instant the card enters play, the effect is not activated until it is selected as a power to be used.

Similary Super Human Durability's text would be "live" as soon as the card enters play, there would be no timing conflicts, it would just enter play.  Vigilance would trigger, but if boosted to 5 damage Super-Human would reduce it, or Synaptic interruption, it's text would be active and you could choose to redirect the damage from vigilence.  It stinks for Expat and Flak Jacket, since playing that when Legacy is doing three damage with Vililance has the same effect as not playing a card.

One-Shots will always compete with any effect that triggers when a card enters play, since One-Shots have an inherent trigger of activating their text effects as soon as they enter play.  The current ruling of player's choice causes that conflict to always be resolved by heroes choice.
