Not the infinite loop combo alluded to in other questions but a pretty devastating one all the same.

As I understand it (and I’m perfectly happy to be proved very wrong) the Demoralization/Darken the Sky combo doesn’t work because Demoralization isn’t a triggered effect, it is evaluated at the end of each action.
Hairtrigger Reflexes has a trigger and is thus constantly evaluated, twigging off as soon as the trigger condition is met (a villain card entering play).
Darken the Sky doesn’t pull all fowl cards out of the trash first and then play then one at a time from the villains (temporary, entirely fictional) ‘hand’, it pulls and plays the fowls cards one at a time until the trash is empty of fowl cards.

So if Darken the Sky is played with Hairtrigger Reflexes in play does this kill all the heroes? (or, more accurately, everyone who has less HP than Expatriette, and herself)
