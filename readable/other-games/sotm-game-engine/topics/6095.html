My guide is forthcoming. He will blow you away if he has assistance from the other heroes; specifically, if there are damage buffs out.

In general, once he’s set up, he’s got limitless healing, given time. This allows him to spend his hit points to tear the villain apart, using Thermal Shockwave. To be the most effective, he needs (of course) Isothermic Transducer and Null-Point Calibration Unit, as well as Focused Apertures. His one shots Frost-Bound Drain and especially Hoarfire are surprisingly flexible. They also boost the power of Coolant Blast, especially if the villain or environment or another hero has dealt Absolute Zero fire damage. The appeal of this is that fire damage dealt to Zero can be re-dealt twice - once when he shoots it out of the Isothermic Transducer, and again when he uses Coolant Blast.

Impale, Fueled Freeze, and Cold Snap are all generally useful cards that can amp the cold damage he deals for Thermal Shockwave if needed. Subzero Atmosphere can make villain cards much less dangerous. (See the subject with that title.)

On the whole, I don’t see the grand payoff for the setup he takes. But he’s fun sometimes.
