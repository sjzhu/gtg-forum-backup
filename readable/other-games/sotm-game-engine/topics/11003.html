The Prime Wardens version of Haka has a power which states: "Play a card.  If that card has "Haka" in the title, you may apply its effects to a target other than Haka."

What exactly does "its effects" refer to?  Does it refer to the entire text of the card, or only the part of the card which happens after the drawing/discarding?

In other words, if Haka wanted to use his power to play Haka of Restoration to heal up Fanatic, does Haka draw 2 cards and then discard 1 or more cards so that Fanatic regains health, or does Fanatic draw 2 cards and discard 1 or more cards to regain health?
