In all of these cases, you only put the cards into play that you are specifically told to put into play by the card in question. For example, Citizen Dawn’s character card says:

[quote=““Citizen Dawn””]
Shuffle the Villain Deck and draw cards until 3 Citizen cards are revealed. Put them into play. Shuffle the Villain Deck.
[/quote]

In this case, you draw cards until you reveal 3 citizens. Those 3 citizens are put into play, and the rest of the Villain deck (including all of the other cards that you just drew) is shuffled. Only 3 citizens come into play.

The same sort of thing happens on Omnitron’s card. Only the card it explicitly tells you to put into play is put into play.
