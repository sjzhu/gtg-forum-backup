Pydro is correct.

She is a card from a villain deck, so she is a villain card.

She has a different back than the rest of the deck, so she is a Character Card.

She does not have the Keyword Villain, so she is not a Villain Character Card.

The number one reason for Operative to be like she is is the win condition and cards like Cedistic Dissonant and End of days.

"Unless otherwise stated when a villain character card is destroyed the heroes win the game."

Having Operative not a Villain Character saves having to state otherwise.

Having her a character makes her immune to End of Days and Cedistic Dissonant, both of which cannot destroy character cards.
