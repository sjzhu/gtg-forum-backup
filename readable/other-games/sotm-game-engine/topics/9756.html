A few things here, just to clarify. 

1) the damage dealt must share a damage type with your current plating

2) you must actually take some damage for subroutine to trigger. 

Example: you have elemental chassis out and Set deals you 2 lightning damage. Now, the condition for 1 was met (matching types), but since omniX would take no damage (2-2=0), subroutine would not trigger.
