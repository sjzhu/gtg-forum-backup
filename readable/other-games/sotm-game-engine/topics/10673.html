No - each hit is a separate instance of damage, even if it's in the same power (eg Fanatic's Exorcism or Jim's Compounded Bow). As such, if someone's damage is boosted, the boost applies to both plinks of damage. The only time this wouldn't be the case is if a certain damage type is boosted and the attack consists of both that type and another - say there was something boosting all infernal damage (there isn't, but just as an example) and Jim fired his Bow - the first hit is projectile so wouldn't get a boost. But then the second hit can be any type he likes, so he decides to make it infernal, so it gets the +1 and becomes two points of damage.
