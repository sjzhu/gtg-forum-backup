Hero vs hero is never more than a fun and unbalanced side game, but it can work.

The games that woek best are 3v3, or 3 hero free for all.

How we do it:

Villain and non-hero refer to any hero not on your team, hero refers only to heroes on your team.

Ongoing destruction can also destroy equipment.

The environment deck treats all targets as hero and villain targets, if there are seperate effects (pillars of hercules) for villain and hero only do the hero one.

There's a lot of on the spot rules we make to help balance, and certain cards are not allowed, like takedown, if you draw it you put it in the trash and draw the next.

It only works as a friendly diversion, if people get competetive it goes bad really quickly.
