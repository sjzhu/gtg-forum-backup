You're not the only one who has wished for a better search function... Google is your friend (at least sometimes).

On this issue, I'm with the liberals. On every card other than this, the text is careful not to make the order of cards in a trash pile matter. For instance, Return with the Dawn (and similar cards) shuffle the trash before pulling out cards from it. You are told to shuffle your deck but not your trash after searching it. Etc. For the order of the cards in the trash to suddenly start mattering for Brain Burn would seem odd; without this one card, it wouldn't matter if you sorted the trash.
