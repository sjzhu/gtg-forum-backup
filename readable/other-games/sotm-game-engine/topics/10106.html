How did Fixed Point kill you in that instance? If it was still out when Devious Disruption entered play (which I gather from context is what happened), it wouldn't matter what you did - no-one would be dying right there and then because of Fixed Point. If you were still there when Fixed Point went away, then you'd be incapped.
