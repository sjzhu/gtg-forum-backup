phantaskippy:

What I wonder is if Smoke Bombs works like that, redirecting the damage causes a new reduction to trigger, whether that happens immediately or if it enters the stable of reductions and increases is the question.

In the link I made above, the claim is made that "If a redirect does occur, then modifiers before the redirect are re-evaluated from the start of that sequence until damage is actually dealt."  So the -1 from Smoke Bombs gets added to the (newly re-evaluated) list of Damage Amount Changes.

This sort of thing can be easily observed in the App, though seeing it with Smoke Bombs in particular can be tricky, since SB's reduction doesn't itself involve any choice.  But whenever damage gets redirected from a source that has Twist The Ether on it, the player ends up making both of the TTE choices (type and amount) twice, once for the original target, then again once the new target is chosen.
