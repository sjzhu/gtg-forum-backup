We've been considering Vengeance to be a mega-expansion, in that we're getting five new heroes and five new villains, plus a new way to play the game.  However, it occurs to me that since the villains can't be played separately, we're really only getting one new villain, the Vengeful Five.  Which villains make up the Vengeful Five will change from game to game, but if your usual gaming group is four people, you're going to see four of the five villains every game.  That's sort of less replayability than we get with a usual expansion.

Discuss.

One additional thought - when we first saw the pic of Baron Blade with his cronies arrayed behind him, we all oooohed about all the villains in the background.  I specifically remember people pointing out the asian villain who we figure might be the guy who turns into the golden dragon on Mr. Fixer's "Overdrive"card.  For Vengeance, none of those background villains are in evidence.  HOWEVER, if more villain mini-decks were made available (Overdrive Guy, Granite Oni, Toy Master guy from Dreamer's deck, etc.), perhaps as one-off purchases from the site, I'd be a seriously happy camper.  At that point, the possible number of Vengeful Five combinations explodes, and Vengeance becomes in my mind a seriously Mega expansion.

I still think that it ought to be possible to play all of the Vengeance villains (except possibly Six-Pack Blade) as a single, normal villain.  It makes sense for the Ennead to always travel in a group, since they share an origin, but the VF includes a Soviet idealist, a still semi-patriotic American mercenary, a jewel thief and a frustrated scientist; aside from each being pissed at a particular FV member (and that's weak in Proletariat's case, plus he's the one I'd most expect to be able to succeed without any help, for obvious reasons), they don't really have terribly strong reasons to want to work together.  And we've never previously seen them together except on Scum and Villainy, where Friction and Proletariat don't seem especially more chummy than Char and "Burn-Victim".  Coolant Blast and Lead From the Front just show Proletariat, and more significantly, Fright Train not only shows up alone on Flak Cannon, but is very observably present on Mars in Maintenance Level, while none of the rest of the FV appear there (Vengeance Blade has been seen in the Realm of Discord and we have the other two in Rook City, but that's all we know location-wise for now).  So if Bunker and Fright Train were duking it out on Mars, were Vengeance Blade and Ermine also there, but we never saw them?  If not, why not?  I think it makes more sense to imagine that it was just FT rampaging his way through the base, driving Bunker into hiding while (for example) Visionary and Absolute Zero monitored the situation from afar and tried to help.

tldr - I think you should be able to fight anywhere from 1 to 5 vengeance villains and have it be a workable game.

On the rest of the subject in general, I do think that there will be more than adequate replayability provided by the extra Environments and Heroes; more Villains are the least needed overall.  We're going from 216 villain/environment pairs (not counting the distinction between regular and advanced games, or promo villains) to 264, while the number of possible 3-hero teams goes from a bit under 5000 to well over 10,000.  The change is somewhat disproportionate, but those extra 48 MDP, Freedom Tower, and VF games are nothing to sneeze at.  I'd say "mega-expansion" certainly remains valid.

Well... I don't think this is TOO spoilery, but since the AMOUNT of villains is modular around H, the cards themselves don't actually use the H modifier. They mostly have static numbers.
