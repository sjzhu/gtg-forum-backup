I hate to inform you, but you can't mix cards between Original Edition and Enhanced Edtion, they switched companies inbetween the two printings. If you really want to upgrade, you'll have to buy the Enhanced Edition as a whole. I guess you could also just buy the decks outright, but I think you'll end spending roughly the same amount anyhow.

If you go here http://sentinelsofthemultiverse.com/downloads you'll can get the PDFs with the changes.
