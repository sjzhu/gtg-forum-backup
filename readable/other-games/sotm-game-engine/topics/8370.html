If the card specifies that Apostate is doing the damage then the nemesis bonus would apply. This only matters for Condemnation since it specifies on the card that Apostate deals the damage. The Gauntlets of Perdition specify that it does the damage, and so extra damage dealt from it won't get the nemesis bonus.
