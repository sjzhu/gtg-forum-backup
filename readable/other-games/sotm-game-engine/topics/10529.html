Things beneath things are still Environment, Hero or Vilain cards (and come from the same deck, e.g. it's Tempest's hero card), they just don't have any text or 'sub-types' (e.g. Ongoing or Target) on them. Anything that triggers off of 'card type' destructions triggers, anything that triggers off of 'card sub-type' destruction does not.
