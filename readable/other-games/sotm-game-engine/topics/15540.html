I have a question that’s come up since I finally got the video game.

We know that, generally, (1) when an effect triggers, it interrupts other effects to take its own actions, and (2) cards remain in play while resolving their own destruction. Therefore, the way I’ve been playing the table top game, if one of Captain Cosmic’s Constructs (with an effect that happens when it takes damage) takes enough damage to destroy it, this is the order of operations I apply:

  1. Deal the damage to the Construct, removing all of its HP tokens.
  2. The Construct is in play and was dealt damage, so I trigger its effect and resolve whatever ensues.
  3. The Construct has 0 or fewer HP, so I destroy it.

The video game skips any Construct “on damage” effects. What rationale am I missing?

Yep, if the card says on destruction, then it stays in play whilst it's effect resolves.  If it says on damage, then it leaves before it's effect resolves.

So in order - 

1. Card takes damage that destroys it.
2. Effects from the cards destruction resolve.
3. Card is destroyed.
4. Other effects on the card fail to resolve as it is no longer in play.
