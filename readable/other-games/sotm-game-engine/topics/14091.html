Let me relate a hypothetical example.

Setup: Termi-Nation Absolute Zero has Isothermic a Transducer, Null-Point Calibration Unit, Focused Apertures, and Coolant Blast in play. Last turn, he used Violent Shivers in preparation for a big damage turn. Assume he has at least 20 hp remaining so that he doesn't fry himself this turn.

Play: AZ plays Frost Bound Drain, dealing Unfortunate Villain Target 6 cold damage (3 base + 1 FA + 2 VS), then takes 7 fire damage (3 base + 2 VS + 2 VS). Isothermic Transducer activates, sending 10 cold damage at UVT (7 + 1 FA + 2 VS). Alternatively, he could target himself with the cold damage and regain 12 hp (7 + 1 + 2 + 2), but I'll assume for the purposes of this scenario that AZ is trying to drop the UVT as quickly as possible.

Power: AZ activates Coolant Blast, dealing UVT another 10 cold damage (7 fire damage taken + 1 FA + 2 VS).

Draw: as per usual

Final Tally: Absolute Zero has taken 7 fire damage, and the Unfortunate Villain Target has taken 26 cold damage.

Abbreviations used: 

AZ = Absolute Zero

FA = Focused Apertures

UVT = Unfortunate Villain Target (choose your favorite punching bag)

VS = Violent Shivers
