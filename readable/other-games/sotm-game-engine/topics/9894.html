I was playing advanced Ennead at the Tomb of Anubis vs Legacy, Tempest, Unity, and Omnitron-X. Set was incapacitated, and at the start of the villain turn, everyone had to play the top card of their deck.

Legacy drew a Takedown. Villain cards could no longer be played. At the start of his turn, the card would be destroyed, and at the end of his turn, the member of the Ennead with the highest HP would deal him 2 energy damage. But since the card would be destroyed at the start of his turn, it wouldn't be around at the end of his turn for him to take damage, right?

If so, there's a perfect lockdown set up with Legacy, Visionary, and Omnitron-X. Legacy gets a copy of Takedown into his trash. Visionary then uses Mental Divergence to move it to the top of Legacy's deck. Omnitron-X uses Timeshift to put the top card of Legacy's deck into play. The villain can't play cards, and then Takedown is destroyed at the start of Legacy's turn without Legacy taking any damage. Cycle repeats.

It's not perfect, and villains like Skinwalker Gloomweaver who play cards when it isn't the villain turn would still have some opportunity to play, but it would shut down a lot of the nastier villains like the Matriarch, the Ennead, or Iron Legacy.
