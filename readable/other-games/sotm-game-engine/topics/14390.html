I really hope I’m doing something wrong here.

Setup: Alternate Infinitor, unflipped, and an environment that can reduce damage by 2 (The Block, Pike Industrial Complex). Infinitor has at least 1 manifestation in play with at least 2 HP, and all other manifestations are either in play in front of Infinitor or already taken out of play. Additionally, no hero effects currently in play make damage irreducible.

With this setup, all remaining cards in Infinitor’s deck will have the effect of playing the top card of the villain deck. Normally, Whispers of Oblivion or Machinations of a Madman would break the loop (by either slowly running the heroes or the manifestations in play out of HP), but with the damage reduction that wouldn’t happen.

So does this result in an infinite loop, or am I missing something?
