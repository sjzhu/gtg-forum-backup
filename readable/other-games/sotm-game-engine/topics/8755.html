#7

If something tells you can "play a card this turn" it doesn't matter if it's your turn or not, you can play a card this turn!

Similarly if something tells you "You can use an additional power this turn" then 0 powers + an additional power is 1 power, you can use it now or you can choose not to use it but you "lose" the additional power if you don't use it immeadiately.

grysqrl:

It all happens when the card is played. Playing Overdrive out of turn is very nice for Mr. Fixer.

It also works well if he has another Power to use, due to the Rod of Anubis or a stolen Talisman or something.  You play Overdrive on your turn, then during your Power Phase, you have two things going on - the game rules say "you can use a power", and Overdrive says "you may use your Strike power twice".  Thusly, if you use Strike once, the first condition has been fulfilled, then the second lets you use Strike one more time, and you're done.  But if you use the other Power first, fulfilling the first condition, the second condition still lets you Strike twice.

(I find myself hoping that at least one hero in Vengeance might have the ability to grant extra Powers for the use of the player you play the card on, and that there will also be an Overdrive-like card, so that this interaction can be specifically called out in the rulebook for the set.  This is pure speculation, but I feel as if it would fit the Idealist well to be able to grant such shenanigans, either to other Sentinels members or to a completely different hero.)
