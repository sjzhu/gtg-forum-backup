Yeah, if something reacts in response to a card being played, it does so before that card's effect - for example, the Informant from the Chairman's deck causes the villain to play a card whenever a hero plays a card. This will happen even if the hero card played is a nuke that will be enough to destroy the Informant.

If something acts in response to a hero using a power (for example, that toxic drug of Spite's), the power's effect gets to finish before the reaction kicks off.

I skimmed this topic and didn't notice anyone putting a reason why you can use a power and not trigger power effects, but playing cards trigger.

The reason being, is a Hero has not used a power until the effects of the power has actually activated. Just saying "I'm going to use Squall" doesn't mean you have used the power, however once you heat all the non-hero targets for 1 projectile, you have used a power.

As for playing cards, the moment you put the card into a play area, the card has been played. Thus causing the interruption before the effects of the card just played can take effect.

 

Edit: Toxic Seaweed:

Whenever a hero uses a power, deal that hero (H) minus 1 toxic damage.

At the end of the environment turn, 1 player may discard their hand to destroy this card.

With the Matriarch and fowl cards, they should move the "when a fowl card enters play play the top card of the villain deck" to The Matriarch's character card, that way it would trigger before Hairtrigger reflexes.  I play it this way, and just pretend that it is the Matriarch's card causing the effect, and not the fowl card.

The problem comparing card play to powers is the card being played is what triggers its effect (the text), while a power is triggered by the hero choosing to use it, for the two to have the same timing it would have to say something like "when a card effect occurs" instead of "when a card is played."

A card entering play is what causes a card's effect to take place.  Therefore any other effect that also triggers on a card entering play would happen simultaneously with the effect of the card, and since that would be resolved in the order the cards came into play, the new card is at the back of the line.

A power being used is not a trigger that causes the power to be used, it is the power.  Like Bunker's ammo drop, it triggers to the actual destruction of the card, not the start of the process of destruction, so cards that delay their own destruction delay Ammo Drop's effect from triggering.
