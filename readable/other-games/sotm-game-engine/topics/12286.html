Then would you still get the power use from Transgressor?  Embolden designates power phase, but Smite just says an extra power this turn.

I don't think we've ever had it really spelled out how phases work, but my thoughts have always been that phases aren't periods of time that begin or end, they are points where things trigger.

Your play phase would just be a point of question:  Play a card or skip.  Same with power and draw.

End phase is just the point where all end of turn effects trigger, the end of turn phase wouldn't have a start of the end of turn phase and an end of the end of turn phase, it is a single point that triggers all end of turn effects.  To me it would have no duration at all.

Cards can add a second trigger to end of turn, just like it can add a second trigger to the play phase or power phase.

The way the rules are for the game there really is no reason any phase should have a duration.  Everything we have seems to point to things occuring in instances, and simultaneous resolution for Start of turn and End of turn effects seems to work that same way.

 

This is of course just how I've always seen the game, I don't think anything official has ever gone either way.

MigrantP:

This came up during development and we could not find any official ruling. It is working as we discussed with Christopher. Here is the logic, which also generalizes to “play an additional card” and “draw an additional card” text:If the card granting an additional power use is played on a turn other than the card’s owner’s turn, then they immediately get to use a power. If they do not do it immediately, it’s gone.If the card granting an additional power use is played on the card’s owner’s turn, after the use power phase, then they immediately to use a power. If they do not do it immediately, it’s gone.If the card granting an additional power use is played on the card’s owner’s turn, before the use power phase, then must wait until their use power phase to use the additional power. (this is the typical use case, BTW)
