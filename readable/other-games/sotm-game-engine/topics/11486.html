Nope, it's a turn, not a round.

The villain, each hero and the environment are separate turns. So anything that references a turn is referring not to one complete go around, but to the individual 'portion' where a single hero, villain or environment act.

If Omnitron-X takes 5 damage in just the Villain Turn then it loses it's components. If Omnitron-X takes 5 damage in just the Environment Turn then it loses it's components. If Omnitron-X takes 5 damage in just Hero 1's Turn then it loses it's components. If Omnitron-X takes 5 damage in just Hero 2's Turn then it loses it's components. If Omnitron-X takes 5 damage in just Hero 3's Turn then it loses it's components. etc etc.

 

The healing has no impact on 'the count' of how much damage Omnitron-X takes in a turn.
