How do you win this one?

Plague Rat's damage can't be reduced, so Stun Bolts and such are no good. Immunity can help, but only a few people have it and it takes a while to come out. Redirection also helps, but he tends to hit like a hammer (2 irreducible per hero on the opening turn, in addition to whatever he plays) and most people can only redirect limited amounts of damage. Mr. Fixer is helpful here, I guess, but he has to balance adding damaging tools or whatever the environment throws in.

On top of everything else, the environment varies between lethal (Exploding Chemical Vats) and obnoxious (those Healing Vats requiring Mr. Fixer to up his damage to get hits in, followed by a Vat explosion preventing him from contributing).

I've tried with heavy hitters and I've tried with utility characters and everyone just damn well blows up.

Only Plague Rat's end of turn damage is Irreducible, all his one-shots is regular damage. Wraith also has Throat Jab, which will shut his damage down for a round. Smoke Bombs helps out quite a bit, as he does slightly more damage to the lowest with one-shots. Grappling Hook is also great for destroying the Supercooled Vat and any of Plague Rat's ongoings.

With Mr. fixer, Jack Handle and Riveting Crane is fantastic. Let Fixer get infected, he will now deal all non-targets for 1 irreducible toxic damage and all damage dealt to those targets will now be Irreducible. Driving Mantis or Dual Crowbars is a good place holder for Jack handle. Infact, they are great together while he is infected, as he will try to deal himself 2 damage at the start, which he can redirect into Plague Rat, then he can hit a second target for another 2, since Fixer was supposed to be the first, you can hit Plague Rat again. Driving Mantis with Hoist Chain works as well, redirected Infection damage for the 1 point to Plague Rat, reducing his damage by 1. Then of course Strike will reduce it by another 1. Weakening his one-shots quite a bit. Dual Crowbars works will with Riveting Crane as well, sadly you will have to hit yourself for 2 and will no long benefit from DR, but the same applies to Plague Rat, since Dual Crowbars allows for a second target to be hit. Finally there is Grease Gun, allowing Plague Rat to do no damage for a round.

For a third hero, I would recommend Tachyon or Tempest. Tachyon will allow for extra card draws, meaning Wraith and Fixer can get through their deck faster and get wanted cards. Blinding Speed to get rid of nasty Ongoings/ENvironment cards. Hypersonic Assualt to Shut down Plague Rat's damage. She will also be building up a ton of damage with Light-Speed Barrage.

Reason for Tempest, he is also able to destroy Ongoing/Environment cards. Reclaim from the Deep will allow the heroes to reclaim some powerful one-shots. And Tempest is quite capable of dealing heavy damage throughout the game.

Edit: Also Wraith has 3 copies of Mega Computer, which if she can get all 3 in play will pratically shut down Environment damage. Pair that with a Infected Fixer with Riveting Crane and Jack Handle, Plague Rat will be the only one taking damage from the Environment, as long as Sewer Fiends can are not in play.

Yeah, Jim versus Rat is epic fun :D. You can get a whole load of extra damage out without any input from other heroes - Nemesis bonus, Infection bonus (if Infected while the Locus is out), By Any Means, and Ultimate Target give you +4 to Rat, meaning the Compounded Bow will deal five and five damage by itself. And five damage for every plink from your many one-shots. Jim versus Rat doesn't tend to be a very long game, but in a good way - I've done it several times now and I don't recall losing (and all my games at home are with three heroes) :).

As mentioned, Mr Fixer is good against Rat if you can get out Jack Handle or something. Environment destruction is useful in Pike Industrial, since you can get rid of the more annoying cards (like the Mutagen or the vats). Damage nerfing is good, as long as it's damage prevention rather than reduction (since Rat deals a lot of irreducible damage). Also, ongoing destruction if he gets out the annoying Sewer Fiend or the pain-in-the-arse Bestial Vitality.

One good combo I've done once or twice is Omni-10 with his Ablative Coating and RPS - if he's Infected, he hits himself for irreducible toxic damage, which ignore his plating, therefore the RPS goes off as he's taken damage of a type covered by his current plating and he gets to hit Rat. Even better if you have either or both Innervation Rays out to regain the lost hp from hitting yourself :).

Omnitronx and Ablative Coating + RPS have made quick work of Plague Rat, for sure.  Omnitronx has an incredibly quick set up (likely to offset the fact that 5 damage will make him lose his stuff).  Also, since most of his activities are done at the beginning of his turn, he isn't hindered as much by Plague Rat's infected side, which forces heroes to use different Powers.  Add his Innervation Ray (or two, like Ameena said) for some great consistent healing and he makes a great choice against this villain.

The Visionary also shuts down Plague Rat every time I let her tag along; being able to control the villain/environment decks on top of Twist the Ether and her excellent Ongoing destruction for those pesky Infections makes her a must-have as well if you're really looking to stomp him.

For a good tank, Legacy is iffy on this one; he can provide constant healing and completely shut down the villain damage.  However, his damage boosts can begin to hurt as heroes deal themselves damage.  If you're looking for a tanky bruiser, I'd suggest Haka.  He can still shut down the damage with Ground Pound and Enduring Intercession can divert all the environment damage to him if you're desperate to keep OmniX and others away from thost Vat explosions.  And from what I've found, the best way to beat Plague Rat is to just smoke him into the ground with damage, and Haka is much better at dishing it out consistantly than Legacy is.

If you have the DW Promos, DW Ex-Pat makes for a fun choice, especially with Hair Trigger Reflexes in play.  That'll shut PR down fast.  Ex-Pat is also good for supplying constant, good damage and her Flak Jackets are perfect for the backlash that comes from destroying Infections.

Nightmist is also a beast (what else is new) with her ability to redirect damage every turn, allowing her to redirect the backlash from destroying Infections during other players turns.  

Overall, you can't go wrong againt Plague Rat; every hero will have their uses; just make sure you're prioritizing your defenses first, since as you've found out, PR can hit very hard, very quickly (especially in Pike Industrial Complex) and you can be down in HP before you even get set up.
