I had a question regarding what happens to incapacitated heroes that die during their own turn. Several baddies do “revenge” damage. If I play a card, and the revenge damage kills me, can I still use an Incapacitated ability that turn? What if I die from “revenge” damage from using a power? As an especially weird exception, we were playing the Chairman the other day, and Tempest died due “revenge” damage from the Chairman from his Lightning Storm, which takes place during his “start of turn” phase. My GUESS to the answers are as follows:

The action you take while incapacitated takes the place of the Play a Card, User a Power, AND Draw a Card phases, thus if you have already performed one of those this turn, you can’t also use an incapacitated ability. However, if you die during your “start of turn”, you could still use an incapacitated ability, because you haven’t performed any of the phases that are replaced by the incapacitated abilities.

As a side question, would Pillars of Hercules have any effect on an Incapacitated Hero? I know they still have a “start of turn” phase, so a villain card would be played, but would they be able to use 2 incapacitated abilities, since all of the phases they replaced are being doubled?
