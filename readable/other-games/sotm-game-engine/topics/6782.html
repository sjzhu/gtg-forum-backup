Some would say that, if Shielding Wind were played before Ta Moko, it would activate first (as it was played first), thus giving the most protection.  Others would say that Ta Moko is a static, always in effect modifier, while Shielding Wind is a triggered effect modifier, and the damage would have already been reduced to 4 before Shielding Wind has a chance to protect.  I'll leave it alone after this.

Technically, what Ameena does is not the `correct' way to play, but personally I find it a much more enjoyable way. Officially it's about the order the cards were played, which is a pain to keep track of between decks, and, as TheJayMann says, even then the answer isn't entirely clear.

 

My advice: play as Ameena does, it plays smoother. Sure, it'll give you the advantage at times, but it won't happen often, and when it happens, the alternative feels a bit artificial. Perhaps try to keep track of the situations where it would be relevant, and see if you prefer one style or the other.
