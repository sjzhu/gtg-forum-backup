Prayer of Desperation moves you to the end of turn phase. It is still part of the same turn, you will not be able to use the same power.

 

Edit: While the above example cannot be used in the video game, there are similar things that an be achieved.

The method I used requires:

  1. The Scholar
  2. The Argent Adept
  3. Musarangi's Harp
  4. Inspiring Supertonic
  5. Alacritous Subdominant
  6. Proverbs and Axioms
  7. Know When to Hold Fast

 

First off, you want to get every one of The Argent Adept's cards that are listed above into play. Next you want The Scholar to play Proverbs and Axioms on The Scholar's turn. The Scholar must choose to take the 3 damage and use a power before The Argent Adept. After The Scholar has used his power, then The Argent Adept must take the 3 damage and use a power. The Argent Adept will want to use Musarangi's Harp to activate the Perform text of Alacritous Subdominat. Once Alacritous Subdominant is used, have The Scholar play Know When to Hold Fast. The Accompany of the Musarangi's Harp is used to activate Alacritous Sundominant. Now Vocalize to do the Perform of Inspiring Supertonic, if the only power that The Scholar had access to was the power he used with Proverbs and Axioms, his name will not be visible. If he has another power, then you can click his name and see the power he had previously used is no longer selectable.
