Ok, I guess I am a little behind the times. I see that there is a promo card that came out for KNYFE. When did that come out and how can I get one? 

Since preorders greatly exceeded their expectations for Wrath of the Cosmos, they added that as an extra preorder bonus, in addition to the preorder bonuses they already offered (Prime Wardens promo pack, Guise mini-expansion, Wager Master mini-expansion, and Omnitron IV mini-expansion).
