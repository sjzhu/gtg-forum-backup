Your interpretation is correct for both cards.

 

Kaargra is dealing the two instances of damage seperately, so the HP order is recalculated both times.  If I had three heroes: AA [24], AZ [23], and OX [22], then she would hit AA for 4 (AZ [23] OX [22] AA [20]) and then hit OX for 3 (AZ [23], AA [20], OX [19]).

 

If the text was something along the lines of "Kaargra hits the two highest for 4", then it would not be recalculated and you would hit AA and AZ.  But as it is, the two attacks are entirely seperate.

"Kaargra deals the highest hp hero 4 damage.  Kaargra deals a different hero with the second. . ."

I'm not sure that wording would work. 

Characters A, B and C have 20, 17, 14 health. Kaargra deals the highest HP target (A) 4 damage. This leaves us with B on 17, A on 16, C on 14.

Kaargra then hits "the different hero with the second highest health" for 3. Who is that? A has the second highest health currently, but is not a different hero. B was the second highest health, but has the highest at the point the second attack happens. C has the *third* highest health overall, and has since the start... but has the second highest of all the "different" heros (and would even if the first attack hadn't changed the order of A and B).

And you're right, the app is official, I'll be glad when WotC is released and we can see how the card as-worded works for certain!
