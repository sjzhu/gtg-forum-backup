Technically, the trap isn’t revealed. After a card above it is revealed, the trap is on the top of the deck and explodes. After it triggers, you keep revealing cards, since the trap wasn’t revealed, it doesn’t count as one of the revealed cards.

EDIT: Just realized that if the trap kills Visionary, we have another case of revealed cards in limbo.
