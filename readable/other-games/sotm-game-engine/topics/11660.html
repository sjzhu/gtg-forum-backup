While the usefuless of any individual promo hero would always depend on the specific situation, which promo versions are ones that you generally pick over the base hero? Or are there known situations when you'd always pick a promo over the base?

SS Tachyon and TL Tachyon seem obviously better than base Tachyon most of the time

F6 Absolute Zero's Elemental Wrath helps get past the slow starts he can often have

F6 Tempest's Sacrifice is pretty good as his base power isn't generally relevant once he gets the hailstorm

I find Young Legacy works better than Legacy in fights with only three heroes

Most of the other promos I either haven't played with enough to get a feel for when or why they might be better or I find that their variant power isn't notably more useful than their base power (e.g. the GI Bunker & RC Wraith powers can be useful in some situations, but I generally find I'm using other powers for those heroes anyway)

Off the top of my head, I've grown a liking to DW Nightmist, TL/SS Tachyon, and occasionally Young Legacy.

DW Nightmist lets you plan her spell plays out better, and saves you from burning yourself. So you onlt take self-damage when you're using spells, and not her base power.

Team Leader Tachyon for obvious ridiculous draw power reasons. SS Tachyon because I feel it's a good way to mill Bursts while occasionally getting lucky enough to play them. A bit of a luck factor, but it suits my play style more than base Tachyon.

I usually prefer base Legacy because bolstering all instances of damage helps tremendously against DR. Young Legacy switches your playstyle a lot, thus making cards like Surge of Strength more useful, since you're doing more damage than you are buffing. Plus, 3 damage for a base power is nothing to sneeze at. Unless you're allergic to being awesome.

Regular Nightmist's most effective card is Amulet.

Her base power gves you the exact cost of amulet for 2 damage.  So you get a basic 2 damage now to redirect 3 or more damage later is a win, otherwise you redirect the 2 damage Nightmist deals herself.

Running Amulet Nightmist is all about knowing what amounts of damage are going to come your way in the fight, and saving enough cards to cover big hits, and using the rest to take care of the biggest hits you can, accounting for her own cards as well.

I love DW Nightmist because is removes the repeatable stable cost of cards and makes her a much more interesting character.
