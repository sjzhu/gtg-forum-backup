Yes, you reshuffle a deck when you need to either draw, discard, or play a card from the top of it and there are no cards left. You don't reshuffle just because the deck has run out - you only do so when you need to do something with a card. You do not reshuffle if you are told to "reveal" a card, and if an effect tells you to reveal multiple cards and there aren't enough left (eg using Arcane Cadence when there are only two cards left), you reveal what you can and follow the instructions for as many cards as you have.

You reshuffle when you would need to draw or play a card from that deck.  "Draw" and "Play" effects force a reshuffle, but "reveal" and "search" do not.  You also don't reshuffle just because it's empty, only when you need to "draw" or "play" and there isn't anything there.

 

Edit: Right, like Ameena said, "discard" effects as well.
