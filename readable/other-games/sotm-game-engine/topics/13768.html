The only reason Omnitron can work that way is he has an On Destruction effect that removes him and keeps the game going.  Kaargra has no such effect.

This is actually a very interesting test of the "all on destruct effects happen" rule.

Both Kaargra Warfang's card and Unforgiving Wasteland say "When would be destroyed _____ instead."  One flips the Character Card, one removes it from play.  The trouble is if you remove it from play the game is over and the heroes win, by rule.  This is because, "Unless otherwise stated, when a Villain Character card is destroyed the heroes win".  Kaargra Warfang's target side of her character card does say otherwise, but the effect "The Heroes cannot win the game" is a static effect.

Static effects are only active while the card is in play.  If Kaargra is removed from play, or flipped then removed, then that effect is no longer active and the heroes can win the game, and they will, immediately upon her card leaving play.

So monsters drag the Arena Master off into the dark night, and everyone decides they have had enough and go home.
