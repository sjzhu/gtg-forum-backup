1)The Kraken will target villains

2)That depends. 2 Krakens would be 2 seperate 5 point damaging cards, not a single 10 point attack but 2 Cramped Quarters Combat (Megalopolis environment) would both add 1 damage for a total of +2 for any damage! The reason being that you process the cards one at a time but if they have 'global' effects then the effects stick around until the card disappears.

3) No. If a single line is on a card says something like "Deal up to 3 targets 3 melee damage" (a single effect) or " Deal 1 target 2 melee damage and a second target 3 projectile damage" (specifies a second target) then it needs to be separate targets. If a card has multiple lines that say something like "Invincible Jones deals a target 2 lightning damage. Invincible Jones deals a target 2 infernal damage" then it can be the same target as a) it doesn't specify a second/different target, and b) it is two separate effects.

Yes, the kraken can target villains as they are not Environment targets. The Kraken finds minions very tasty, apparently.

Dpends what you mean by stack. If you have two Phosphorescent Chambers, then that will be +2 damage dealt, +2 HP recovery. If you have two The Krakens, then each would attack the lowest HP target at the time, which may be different if the first The Kraken kills one, for 5 melee damage each rather than one target for 10 melee damage. Cards that don't have the same name also stack. The exceptiobn is Limited cards as you can only have one of those in play at a time.

That depends on the card. If it targets multiple targets at once (Greivous Hail Storm, Holy Nova) or specifically says another target (Grenade Launcher, Hoarfire) then they can't be the same thing. If you have a card that says "target 1 rtarget, then target 1 target" (such as Schirezo of Frost and Flame) they can be the same target. The last one is really unusual though, so most of the time the answer will be "No".
