Villain card: any card from the villain deck. Even unusual cards, like "Safe House", "Bloodsworn Arena", or "Shrine of the Ennead", which don't actually spend any time in the deck, count. The Title Deck... doesn't? 

Character card: a double-sided card. Notable oddities include Kismet's Talisman and The Operative, both "character" cards and "villain" cards, but not "Villain Character" cards. 

Villain Character card: a double-sided card with the "Villain" keyword. The nine members of the Ennead are each Villain Character cards, as are all the Vengeance-style villains.
