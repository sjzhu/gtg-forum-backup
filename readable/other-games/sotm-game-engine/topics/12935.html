Now, *most* hero turns, no one will be damaging Nightmist.  But she can definitely redirect the first damage to her during the environment turn *and* the villain turn.

Of course, if she has lots of cards, and there are few enemy targets in play, she might just say, "Hey Haka, why don't you use Taiaha to hit the bad guy and ME; then I'll redirect my damage to the bad guy."  Or similar tricks with other heroes who do multi-target damage.

EDIT: And of course, if anything damages her doing her own turn, she can redirect that.  Including using her own base power: net effect, trade two (presumably non-awesome) cards for two new cards *and* do two points of damage!
