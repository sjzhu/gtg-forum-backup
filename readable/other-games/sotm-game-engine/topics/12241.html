The damage to earn a point is by target, so in your example as soon as Hero A deals the second target the 2 damage the heroes earn a point.

As soon as Hero B deals the second target the 2 damage the heroes earn a point.  The damage dealt by the power is irrelevant, that hero already earned their point for dealing 2 damage to 2 targets in a round, and cannot earn another point for achieving that until the next round.

If Expat uses her assault rifle to deal 3 targets 2 damage on her turn she gets a point, is that same round Adept lets her use a power and she does it again, she gets no more points, because it isn't a new round yet.

Hero C deals no damage, is awarded no points, and may God have mercy on their soul.
