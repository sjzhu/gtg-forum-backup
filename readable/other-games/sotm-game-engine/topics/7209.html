Yes, the Operative spawns an underboss who, when it's it's turn in the end phase, spawns a thug who then acts as well. There's a reason the Chairman is a level 4 villain.

As long as you're in the Start or end phase, you go through all the cards, even if they only appeared in that phase.

The key is that card effects happen in the order they entered play. The Underboss is the last card to enter play, so it's the last card to check in the end of turn phase for actions.
