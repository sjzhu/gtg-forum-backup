Powerhound_2000:

Thematically I can see how Advanced Gloomweaver makes sense but you limit character options with those rules.  

You limit character options when you pick a deck, an environment, play a card, any time anything happened other options were eliminated.

Any time you raise difficulty you either increase the effectiveness of the villain or decrease the effectiveness of heroes.

If every advanced mode and every difficulty increase was solely based on increasing villain power the game would be far more limited in scope.

You have to look at how it limits choice, and for how long, for how many players, and how important the options you took away were.

For Gloomweaver you don't even need to damage him to win the game, so it pushes heroes to focus on a different path to victory if they take those characters.  He is such a target rich deck that I've never found Wraith, Setback, Haka or Tachyon to be minimalized by the immunity.
