[quote=““Horrid Cacophony””]
One-shot
Destroy (H) hero ongoing cards.
Deal each non-villain target (H) minus 2 sonic damage.
[/quote]

Usually, a source is always associated with a damage being dealt, even for one-shots. However, Horrid Cacophony did not state the source of its damage dealt. Even Falling Statuary says “This card deals the target…”.

So, back to the question, what/who is the source of the damage written on Horrid Cacophony?

This may have some impact on the game.
