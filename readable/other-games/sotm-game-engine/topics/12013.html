The word heal is not a game mechanic in Sentinels.

 

There is recovery, which is the only thing that gets increases and decreases.

There is regaining, which is the in game turn for the act of raising HP.  Regaining is Recovery.

Then there is restoring, which is to a certain amount, regardless of the amount before restoring.  Restoring is now clarified to not be Recovery.

 

Thanks for doing that Pydro, I had completely forgotten about that.
