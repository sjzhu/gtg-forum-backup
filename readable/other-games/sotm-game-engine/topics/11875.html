For all these, assume that Prime Warden Capain Cosmic's base power is in effect: "Power: Until the start of your next turn, whenever a Construct card is destroyed, you may either draw a card or play a card.", that a Construct "C" is destroyed, and that PWCC uses the effect of his base power to play one of the following cards.

1) Conservation of Energy reads, in part, "Destroy any number of Construct cards."  Do you count C as being destroyed by CoE?

2) Contruct Cataclysm reads, in part, "Destroy all Construct cards."  Do you count C as being destroyed by Construct Cataclysm?

3) Destructive Response.  If C was the first Construct destroyed this turn, can Captain Cosmic immediately trigger its ability?

4) Potent Disruption.  Can Captain Cosmic count C as the Construct that this card destroys?

5) Sustained Influence.  Can Captain Cosmic immediately destroy this card to put C back in play?

I suspect that the answer to these is "no", but I could argue either side, and there are many slightly different cases here.  So, thoughts?

Instead is the key word in that power.  You can let the card be destroyed, or you can shuffle it back into the deck for the effect of PW's power.

If you shuffle it back into the deck the card is not destroyed and no other effects can respond to its destruction, it also would not count as the first construct destroyed that turn, because it was not destroyed.

The other thing is the power's effect is optional.

so given that:

 

1-3) Yes, unless you choose to shuffle it into your deck, then it is not destroyed and would not count for any "When a construct is destroyed" or "number of constructs destroyed" effect.

4) You could choose C as the target of Potent, but if you choose to shuffle it into the deck you would not get to deal any damage, because there was no construct destroyed by its effect.

5) Not if it is shuffled into the deck.

Would that change mean that constructs shuffled into the deck with PW CC's power could trigger destruction effects?

 

The argument that they can is that since all the destruction effects trigger before the card enters the trash, you can choose the order in which they are applied.  If you apply the shuffle trigger first then all other destruction effects would get cancelled, but if you apply the shuffle trigger last, then you could trigger any number of other destruction effects.

This wouldn't break PW CC's power, as any shuffle trigger would still cancel any other shuffle triggers, so multiple uses of the power still won't stack.

It would have the effect that effects that replace destruction would no longer cancel any desirable destruction triggers, but undesirable destruction triggers would all get cancelled.

 

Also, if all destructions triggers trigger before the card hits the trash, then do we need a seperate step (in the google doc) for the triggers on the card itself?  I would combine that all into one step.

The power does not say: "When a construct card enters the trash you may shuffle it into your deck instead."

It says: "When a Construct is destroyed you may shuffle it into the trash instead."

I would resolve it like this:

1.  Construct starts destruction process

2.  Option to shuffle it back in or not occurs.

3.  Is shuffled in it is not destroyed.

4.  If not shuffled in the card is destroyed and other effects happen.

 

The rational is Unforgiving Wasteland.  On destruct effects don't happen.
