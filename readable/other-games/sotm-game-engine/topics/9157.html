Yes, you can do the 3 damage without destroying anything as long as there are no hero cards in play. The Sentinels rules say that if you cannot do the full effect of a card just do as much as you can.

If the wording had been "Destroy a hero ongoing or equipment. If you do then Mr. Fixer deals 1 target 3 damage." then you'd have to destroy to get the damage.

Note that if there is an equipment in play but no ongoings, you can't say "I'm gonna destroy an ongoing, oops there aren't any!" in order to get the damage for free (or vice versa).  If there is a valid option in play, you have to choose it.

What Jimmy said. The other statements are not wrong, but that one seems to answer your question more precisely. When they say "destroy a hero ongoing or equipment card", it's not really a statement of "destroy a hero ongoing card or destroy an equipment card" (which I believe would let you avoid destroying equipment if you had no ongoings, and vice versa); it's more "destroy a card (which may either be a hero ongoing card or an equipment card)".
