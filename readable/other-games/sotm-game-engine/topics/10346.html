I'm unsure on two things.

 

The first relates to damage type boosts. If a card gives a certain damage type increased damage, and then another card changes the damage to another type, is the damage still increased by the first card?

 

For example, Wraith's Micro Targetting Computer and Razor Ordinance are in play, and Irradiated Cyclohexane Vat comes into play on the environment turn. If Wraith used her power from the Razor Ordinance would it "start out" as Projectile damage, be boosted by 2, and then changed into Toxic and boosted by another 1? Or would it "always" be Toxic damage in this case, and only get boosted by 1 from the vat?

 

 

My second question regards cases where there is only a single appropriate target in play, and something affects the second highest (or lowest, etc) target of that type. I am unsure if it would default the only target of that type, or if it would have no effect at all.

 

For example, all but one Hero target is incapacitated and Citizen Slash is in play. At end of Baron Blade's turn, does Citizen Slash attack the only remaining Hero, or does he do nothing? A second example would be K.N.Y.F.E.'s Infiltrate and Obfuscate when there is only a single Villain target in play. Would the first instance of damage be redirected to the only Villain, or would Infiltrate do nothing?

 

Thanks!

#2

1. Wraith's damage would not still be increased by Micro Targeting Computer, since she is no longer dealing Projectile.

If Ra is using Imbued Fire, which changes damage dealt be heroes into fire, while also increasing Fire damage by 1, then the Cyclohexane Vat comes into play. Players may choose to turn it to toxic, then fire, thus getting the +1 for being fire and the +1 from the Cyclohexane Vat, since it isn't reliant on damage type.

2. If a card refers to the second highest target, and there is only one target in play, then the card would have no effect. So Slash would deal no damage and K.N.Y.F.E. would still be hit by the environment target.
