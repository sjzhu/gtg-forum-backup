The short answer is yes.  Start of turn effects that are played during start of turn trigger the same way that end of turn effects trigger when played at end of turn.

 

The longer answer is that start of turn effects and end of turn effects always trigger in the order that the cards came into play.  So the Villain character card is always first, and then whatever was played next, then the one after that, etc.  Since a just played card by definition came into play after the card that caused it to be played, it will still be in line to be triggered.

Since the order for start and end of turn effects are the order they enter play, it works.

Basically you do the following:

Start of turn:  Check for start of turn effects that haven't already been activated this turn, if there are any resolve the first one that entered play, then repeat.  Continue until all start of turn effects have been activated.

 

Do the same for the end of turn effects.

Also, if a villain flips during their Start phase and has Start phase actions on their flipped side, those will immediately trigger. For example, Dawn will flip and then flip straight back if enough Citizens are already out (which is why it's super-useful to make sure that's the case before you put the fifth Citizen in the trash ;)).

 

Edit - Also also, if a hero gets incapped during their Start phase (eg kills themselves via Infection) they will still get to use their Incap ability, as an incapped hero's turn now has the phases Start, Use Incap Ability, and End :).
