Yep - when the villain or environment plays a card like an ongoing or a target which stays in play (ie it's not a one-shot which activates immediately), it goes at the end of the line of cards currently already in play. These then activate in order during the appropriate phases (start/end of turn). So to take your Citizen Dawn example, at the end of her turn Return with the Dawn brings back a Citizen from the trash, who goes at the end of the line of cards Dawn currently had in play. If this returned Citizen is one who has an End phase action (all of them except for Anvil, Truth, and Dare, I think) then they'll perform that action after any other cards have done so. That's why you need to make sure all villain and environment cards stay in order when you put them out.

Doesn't matter so much for hero cards since not many of them activate in specific phases - Omni-10 is the main one who needs to worry about it since all his components go off in his Start phase. Some other heroes have cards which are destroyed at the start of their turn, and Unity's bots all (except for Bee Bot) act at either the start of end of her turn.

Note also that there are a few villains who have Start phase actions on both sides of their character card, so if they flip during their Start phase (because their flip conditions have been met - examples are Dawn and Rat) and have a Start action on their flipped side, it's still their Start phase so they'll perform those actions. So you can get Dawn to flip and then immediately flip back provided there are enough Citizens in play (she counts as one herself, don't forget :)) when she does so :).
