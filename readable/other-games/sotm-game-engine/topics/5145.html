If the deck were to not be removed, then it would continue playing one card during the play phase. Any card that says “Omnitron does this” would not be valid as there is no Omnitron (even though it’s stated in the lore that Omnitron exists in all the devices he makes, this is not stated anywhere in the rules or the cards, so there is no Omnitron to do something). The play a card at the end of turn, as well the draw a card from the trash at end of turn, are part of the Omnitron game text. When Omnitron is removed, the gametext no longer exists, and thus no longer continue to play. As well, the advance mode stuff goes away as it is no longer in play. Given how complicated the results are, either “remove all villian cards except devices from play” has a slightly different meaning (not very good, as this will cause confusion), or an errata is needed, saying that all villain cards except device cards in play are removed from game (much clearer). Unless, of course, all this extra complexity is desired.

Alrighty, folks. Here’s the answer.

When Omnitron is reduced to 0 or fewer HP, the Villain Character Cards are gone, but the Villain Deck is still in play. The rules say that on the Villain Turn, regardless of which Villain you’re fighting or anything like that, you play the top card of the Villain Deck. Now, Omnitron can play a second card each turn, or pull a Drone or Component from its Trash into play, depending on what side of the Villain Character Cards is up. However, if there IS no Villain Character Card, those effects do not happen. But there is nothing to stop the top card of the Villain Deck from being played each Villain Turn.

So, in short, when Omnitron itself leaves play, the Villain Deck continues to produce a card each turn, until there are no Devices in play. Omnitron’s programming is in all of its robotic creations.

Does that help?
