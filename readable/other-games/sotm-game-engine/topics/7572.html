Yeah, anything that counts as "destroyed" at any point either has text on it to say "destroy this card" under whatever conditions are required to do so (eg Take Down at the start of Legacy's turn), or is a target and therefore is automatically destroyed when its hp are reduced to zero (unless otherwise specified, eg it flips instead or is currently indestructible). Or it's destroyed via text on another card (eg Sedative Flechettes versus hero Ongoings).
