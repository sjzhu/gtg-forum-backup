This card is one reason I really like the Naturalist. Note also that having damage "prevented" means it doesn't even matter whether it's irreducible, since you're not reducing it but stopping it altogether. Try that against an unbuffed Plague Rat :D.

But yeah, combine this card with Rhino Form and stuff has to be dishing out at least three damage in order to hit you. There's a one-round Ongoing card (Indomitable Force, I think) which gives you another point of sock (till the start of your next turn, at which point the card is destroyed). And a one-shot, Threatening Stomp, which deals one target four damage and, if you're in Rhino Form, forcibly redirects all damage from that target onto you for a round. Tank power! :D
