Omnitron's end of turn ability happens first, because he was in play first. Then each other effect happens in order they came out. If the highest HP hero gets hit by the first drone and is no longer has the highest HP, the second would attack the new hero with the highest HP.

Yeah, with end of turn or start of turn effects they go by the order the cards came into play.

The best guide we have right now is:

If a single event would cause multiple card effects to activate, they activate in the order they entered play, if multiple effects on one card activate, do them in the order on the card.

If a single card effect causes multiple things to happen (Destroy a bunch of cards, everyone draw a card or deal multiple targets damage) the players decide the order it happens in.

Simultaneous effects (like multiple end of turn effects) are always handled by order of play. It's in the rulebook. Omnitrons character card was in play first so he would go first. 

Consecutive effects happen with newest resolving first. I think that's what skippy was getting at. I just kinda parroted it with less words.
