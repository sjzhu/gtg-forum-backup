Freedom Tower: Rooms that are covered by Entry Points have no text so do nothing, but otherwise, if it's in play, it has the effects listed. It also doesn't count as a room card so you have to cover up a different card next entry point. Unless you meant domething else by flipped.

Argent Adept: Yes, but the Pipes must select a different Melody card, even if it's a copy. The songs are not limited so multiple copies can ge in play, but only the pipes cares.

Start/End: Yes. Cards played in a phase will go off. at their time in that phase if apropriate.

Disallowed actions: Disallowed actions are completely disallowed, unless another card specifically overrides it, such as Bunker's Ammo Drop. Overrides are rare. Also, keep in mind that however similar they look, put into play and play are different things.

All rooms stay in play unless they are destroyed or something on the card triggers it's destruction.  

Yes, you can use multiple instruments to activate the same melodies, rhythms, and harmonies multiple times.  They are not powers which is why they can work that way but you still need a power to activate a melody, rhythm, or harmony. 

If you have an ability to put a card into play during start of your turn and that card has a start of turn ability it will trigger during the same start of turn.  Keep in mind the order they were played because that is the order they activate.  This is especially with Omnitron-X who has a lot of components which activate start of turn.  

Depends upon the wording but in most cases the card isn't played.
