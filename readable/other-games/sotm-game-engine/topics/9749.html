lynkfox:

Or, err ont he side of the players.

See, I would say err against the side of the players; victory in a coop game means nothing if the game-loss condition (the villain in SOTM) doesn't pose a substantial threat.  Arkham Horror is just about the ideal coop game because you almost always lose, so finally beating Cthulhu proves that you've really accomplished something.  SOTM is much more enjoyable in the process than Arkham (not to mention quicker), but the outcome can be a bit of a foregone conclusion except against the very toughest villains (about a third or so).  More than half the time, not even one hero goes down, which makes me think perhaps SOTM as a whole (again, with exceptions for the likes of Chairman and Capitan) is excessively easy, so its bragging rights aren't worth all that much.  Ever being generous to players in rules calls would exacerbate that effect, though of course it might be worth it, if the stakes are whether a player is having fun.  So if someone feels like Ra is useless, and then they get out Solar Flare and Flesh of the Sun God and now they think he's awesome, maybe it's acceptible not to point out Ruling 15 to them and have them go back to feeling lame.  But it means that your victory in that game doesn't entirely "count" either, and likewise if you legitimately don't know what to do, but you rule in your own favor and that ends up giving you the win.
