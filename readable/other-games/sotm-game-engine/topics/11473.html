When a hero becomes incapacitated, do all of their cards and all cards that were on them leave play?

Some specifics: 

Chrono-Ranger's Bounties, AZ's Impale, or Vis' Twist the Ether. This seems like an obvious discards according to the core rules. They may not be in front of that hero, but they are still one of their cards.

However, what about hero cards that are under other cards (e.g. La Capitan or Savage Mana)? Are those removed from play as well?

And here's the one that confused us today: if Isolated Hero was on the hero that becomes incapacitated, is it discarded?  The general consensus in the forum seems to be that Isolated Hero can affect Incapcitated abilities, but as an incapactiated hero isn't a valid target anymore, would a villain card like that be discarded when the Hero character card flips?

#2

All of a hero’s cards, regardless of where they are, leave play when the hero is incapped. Cards played on a hero only leave play if the card specifically says “when the target leaves play, destroy this card.”

As an example, Infections do not have that phrase, therefore they are not destroyed when a hero is incapped.

Another useful general ruling is "Cards underneath other cards do not have any txt or HP, but they *DO* have their backs.  So, cards belonging to a Hero still belong to that Hero. 

One interesting wrinkle to this is that anything that destroys Environment cards (especially ones that destroy "all" Env cards) can/must target Environment cards that have ended up under Haka's Savage Mana.  Similarly, Tempest's Into The Stratosphere can pick any "underneath" cards and put them on top of the correct deck.
