#2

1.  Since Ra is immune to Fire damage he does not take any when dealt Fire damage.  This does not activate Flame Barrier.

 

2.  If no damage is dealt to The Wraith, I believe that she deals no retribution damage.  I'm not for sure on this one, but I would be surprised if she dealt retribution damage after taking 0 damage.

 

3.  Since Baron Blade is immune to damage when a Mobile Defense Platform is out, he takes no damage and Hypersonic Assault doesn't prevent him from dealing damage.  This is because (the cards are not in front of me, mind you, but I believe) Tachyon's Hypersonic Assault reads something like "Targets dealt damage this way cannot deal damage until the start of your next turn."
