I'm sorry, but Guise cannot use the Gun's power in the first place.

  • Power: "This Hero's Player may choose to use one power from one of their cards in play which has a power."

Lemme See That edits an equipment card in play so that the name on the card affects Guise instead of the owning Hero; however, it does not make that card part of Guise's deck or move the card to the play area of Guise's player. That means Wraith's MTC would increase Guise's Projectile damage by 2, but Guise could not use Razor Ordnance, if Lemme See That was played on Razor Ordnance. "Razor Ordnanace" is still the Wraith's player's card in play, not Guise's despite it now having Guise's name on it, so Guise cannot use its power. (Just look at the back of the card. Does Razor Ordnanace say Guise or Wraith? That's how we determine the card's owner, not the text on the front.) If Wraith uses Razor Ordnance on Wraith's Turn, then Guise deals the damage. So the real answer is far simpler: Guise cannot use the Power on Expatriate's guns even with Lemme See that, so the Ammo destruction is still Expatriate's player's choice, not Guise's player's choice. Since Lemme See That does not state that the card now belongs to Guise, it still belongs to Wraith. All it does is edit text, not change ownership.

Uh, Yeah, I'm That Guy is completely different. It can copy Powers from (for instance) Legacy, and so Motivational Charge text is copied to UYITG's text, placing a that Power on one of Guise's cards. Guise can activate that power because it is on his card, and since Legacy is edited to Guise on that card, so Guise deals the damage. The original Motivational Charge remains in play and can still be used by Legacy with Legacy dealing the damage, of course.
