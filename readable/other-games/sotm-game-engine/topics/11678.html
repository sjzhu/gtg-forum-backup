Once Forced Deployment is destroyed, it triggers it's text before completing Fueled Freeze, so the minions will be in play. Here's how it works:

Fueled Freeze destroys Forced Deployment.

Forced Deployment puts all the minions into play..

Fueled Freeze damages them all. Most probably die.

You cackle maniacally.

In general if a card triggers another card you stop processing the first card at that point and resolve the second. Once that's done you go back to the first card and finish resolving it (then move to the trash if it's a one-shot). It's possible to nest a whole series of cards.

Similarly a one-shot doesn't go into the trash until it's completely resolved, so if you've played Fleet of Foot for example, it remains in play until the card that you played as a result of it has resolved itself.

The other related ruling (which hasn't been explicitly stated in this thread yet) is that nothing happens simultaneously, even things like "deal 3 damage to each non-hero target" or "destroy all cards in play other than this card, character cards, and relic cards".  Even though lines like that seem like one action, it's really several mini-actions.  This means that you can do tricky things like killing the minion that provides damage reduction before the other minions get a chance to use the damage reduction, or possibly most famously, use End of Days to kill all the villian cards and the environment cards you want killed, then kill Unity's Bee Bot, which kills EoD in response, ending the destruction before any other hero cards are destroyed.
