Yeah, each hero gets hit for damage equal to the number of cards that hero discarded - so if Tears is buffed by something, or if Sweat is out as well (in which case Tears forces everyone to discard two instead of one), then you'll get hit for even more.

Same as Omnitron's Tech Singularity nukes each hero for damage equal to how many equipment cards that hero just had destroyed, rather than everyone getting hit for the combined total from the entire group's destroyed equipment.
