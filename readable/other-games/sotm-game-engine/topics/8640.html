Is irreducible damage considered a "type" of damage?  Forgive me for not remembering all the card names, but I know that Legacy has a card that allows him to become immue to a choosen type of damage and Visionary has a card that allows her to change the type of damage to that of her choice.  Is irreducible damage a valid type to choose for these (and other similar) cards?  Or is irreducible a modifier, like +1 or -1, rather than a type?

#4

Damage types are Melée, Fire, Sonic, etc. Irreducible is an attribute which can be applied to damage (regardless of type). It mean cards like Fortitude and Ta Moko are of no use, because they reduce damage, so if that hero gets hit with irreducible damage, it ignores their protection. This does make Omni-10 good against Plague Rat, though, if you can get out the Ablative Coating and Reactive Plating Subroutine ASAP :).
