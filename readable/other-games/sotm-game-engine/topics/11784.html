3-2 = 1, not -1

You may draw up to 2 cards, but you do not have to draw any cards if you wish. So X can be as high as 3 and as low as 1 depending on how many you wish to draw.

You can draw up to two cards. Then, you will deal 3 damage, but subtract 1 for each card drawn:

0 cards drawn-3 damage

1 card drawn-2 damage

2 cards drawn-1 damage
