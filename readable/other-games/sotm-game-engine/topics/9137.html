This is all correct it seems so far.

Allow me just pose a quick senario to demonstrate that simultaneous triggers and consecutive triggers can be different, just to cover all the bases here.

Villain plays imaginary card [V1] which reads: "Play the top card of the Villain deck. Then, destroy all hero ongoing cards". The next Villain card in the deck is the imaginary card [V2] which reads: "Destroy all Villain cards in play". 

In this senario, all three of these effects are consecutive triggers. Consecutive triggers, unlike simultaneous triggers, resolve in order of newest to oldest. You would have to resolve the card [V2] before you moved on to the second effect clause of [V1]. End result would be that the heros would not have their ongoings destroyed.

Edit: Also, going to be nitpicky, its "Cryo" bot, not Gyro. Cryo = greek for "icey cold".
