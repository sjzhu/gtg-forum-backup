I like this idea, it's a cool way to look at how we use Heroes.  

 

I'll modify it a bit, I'll split it into what I want a hero to get from their own deck, or a general impact on the game, and what I like for help for them.  (both apply to when I play them or someone else plays them)

I'm not doing everyone, just ones that are clearer in my head.

AZ:  I want Thermal with Modules, or Impale/Cold Snap.  lots of damage instances.

Help:  Damage boosting, if you have you modules it's a win win, without it's a trade off that is still pretty good.  Twist the Ether is a dream card, just insane, and extra powers when you have Coolant Blast is awesome.  A good starting hand can benefit a ton from some extra card play.

Argent:  I like bonus card play early, to help everyone get set up, then extra powers or damage bonuses/reduction is awesome.

Help:  Just keep him alive.

Expat:  Shotgun, love me some Shotgun.  Assault rifle is good too, but Shotgun is my favorite.  I expect Expat to also be a big factor in games with lots of card destruction that isn't universal, playing equip cards that can eat up destruction and help other heroes get ahead.

Help:  Card draw if she gets a slow start, then extra power uses are great.  out of turn play for Unload is awesome.

Mr. Fixer:  I'm big on Hoist Chain right now, and Salvage yard is amazing.

Help:  Out of turn card play for Overdrive, Salvage Yard and Grease Gun is awesome.

Nightmist:  Damage and shenanigans.  Just be awesome.

Help:  Twist the Ether, it's insanely good on NM.  Card draw for DWNM, deck manipulation for regular.

Setback:  Something awesome.  If you don't pull of something awesome as Setback I don't see the point.

Help:  Anything.  Really.  Extra card draw or play, more powers, healing, reduction, almost anything helps.  Setback does more with active help than most heroes.

Tachyon:  Synaptic, Fleet of Foot and Hypersonic.  I know you can kill the boss with Lightspeed Barrage, but Tachyon is best when she's using those cards well, also if you can Sucker Punch something it is a huge win.

Help:  Damage boosts, stuff like Enlighten that let's you draw and discard.

Visionary:  Twist the Ether, I want that.  Then using her damage well, and not enlightening yourself all game, it's dumb.  Help your team.

Help:  don't let her die? 

 

That's the ones I'll add.

She can avoid self damage without even needing outside input if she can enter a TK Cocoon…though of course, probably best to have someone let you play that after you've just played Wrest on an EPE (or similar target) so you don't have to go a round without it.

One ieda I had once is one that requires a very specific setup - I tried it once but didn't manage it. Anyway, play Visionary versus Voss and wait for a Psi-Weaver to come out. Stick a Wrest on it, and if you have one, Twist as a bonus. Then go into a Cocoon. Every round, the Psi-Weaver will try to hit everyone and so you can redirect its damage to another minion and kill that instead (Twist helps if there is a Guard out, to get through soak, or if there is another Psi-Weaver out, to hit with something not-Psychic). Then Visionary has to deal it and herself some Psychic damage for having redirected, but the Psi-Weaver, being a Psi-Weaver, takes no damage due to its natural immunity, and Visionary takes none due to being immune to everything. Then you can all just pound on Voss without worrying about any more minions (though I suppose you have to deal with him having a permanent point or two of soak, depending on whether or not he's flipped) :D.

Very silly situation but imagine the fun if it actually works - have the other heroes all be ones who can deal irreducible damage (to get through the aforementioned perma-soak on Voss) and it'd be even better :D.
