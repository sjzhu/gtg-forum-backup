A quick question I had, one with probably a simple ruling, but I'd be interested to hear what you guys have to say/think:

 

"Indestructible: If a card is indestructible, it cannot be destroyed or removed from play."

 

"Sky-Scraper Gameplay: [...] start the game with her normal character card in play and the other two character cards off to the side - neither in play nor in hand. Whenever you play a card with a size keyword, switch her character card to the size named on the played card"

 

So lets say Fixed point is in play with Skyscraper. Her character card are now indestructible meaning it cannot be removed from play. So, if a new size keyword is played what happens? Could she simultaniously tiny and huge? Or would she stay in her size are you are unable to complete the switch? Or are her character cards and exception, the same way one-shots are (to my understanding)?

They aren't being destroyed or removed from play. They are simply being shifted from one to another by "switching." This is different from, say the Naturalist, who can effectively be a freaky beast hybrid, because his previous forms are later destroyed. But for all intents and purposes, Sky-scraper is one entity. All forms share the same HP, and she will always have only one character card in play.
