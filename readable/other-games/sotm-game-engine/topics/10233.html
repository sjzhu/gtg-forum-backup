I'm afraid not - you have to perform the action for each copy of the card in play. Examples are the Scholar discarding to keep his Elemental Form cards in play (one discard per Form card) and environment cards such as Hostage Situation and Papparazzi on the Scene (both in Megalopolis) which require each hero to discard in order to get rid of it (every hero would have to discard a total of two cards each to get rid of both the aforementioned cards).

A sort of exception of note which can be easy to miss is the Toxic Sludge, a card in Rook City - this specifies that at the start of the environment turn, "each hero may discard a card to destroy one environment card" or words to that effect. In this case, each hero may individually choose to discard a card. For every card discarded, one environment card is destroyed.

Be sure not to confuse payments with triggers.

A payment is a cost to do something. a trigger is something that happens when you perform a certain action.

e.g. Critical Multiplier is a trigger so having more than one will trigger each of them if only one card is discarded. Snap Decision is a payment, discarding one card would allow one additional card play and not one card play for each copy in play (if you were able to get two or more into play somehow)

I would say Toxic sludge is a bad example as an exception, it is a single payment that each player can make use of it.

No, they're pretty much the same. Anything that says "do X; if you do, then do Y" is a payment. Triggers usually say "whenever X happens, Y also happens." Critical multiplier is a trigger. Friction's card is Speedy Sidestep and reads  (Don't forget we have all card text 'spoiled' now, no excuses anymore!)

"If Friction would be dealt 5 or more damage from a single source, prevent that damage and destroy this card."

So what happens here is

  1. Someone tries to deal Friction 5 or more damage
  2. The first of Friction's Speedy Sidesteps sees 5 or more damage coming in and triggers, it prevents the damage and destroys itself
  3. The second of Friction's Speedy Sidesteps checks to see if 5 or more damage is coming in, as it's already been prevented there is no damage so it does not trigger
  4. Frictions taunts the heroes
  5. (Optional) You may taunt your friends for being wrong.
Nothing in Sentinels is simultaneous, so there is no point when multiple copies of cards check the same damage at the same point in time, they would always check one after the other (your choice as to which order), which can stop one card from triggering if something else happens to the 'trigger' (like in the Speedy Sidestep example).
