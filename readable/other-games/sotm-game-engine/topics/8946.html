Galvanize increases all damage dealt by a Hero.  That includes multiple instances of damage from one Hero (such as Haka's Taiaha, increasing both hits from 3 to 4 damage) and even damage a Hero does to themselves (such as through Fanatic's Divine Focus or Tachyon's Pushing the Limits).
