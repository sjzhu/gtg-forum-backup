The only Oversized cards that are incorrect are The Dreamer, La Capitan, The Matriarch, and Spite. The Operative's oversized doesn't match the small one either, but the small one targets a "hero", while the larger targets a "hero target", with the change to hero targets for a lot of cards, they may have just missed it on her small card.
