Hey all, got the game recently and stumbled across a couple uncertainties while playing our last couple of games.

1. Some cards are destroyed when something happens in a round. Does this happen IMMEDIATELY when the event occurs or at the end of the round.

An example are the different devices that Omnitron plays that are discarded when Omnitron takes at least 7 damage in a round. Does this happen the moment he is dealt that 7 damage total or at the end of the environment turn in the round he is dealt at least 7 damage?

 

2. 'Damage would be dealt' vs 'Damage Dealt'.

  I'll use legacy to explain this one. 'Superhuman Durability' states that "when legacy would be dealt 5 or more damage from a single source, reduce that damage by 3". When Legacy has 'fortitude' in play ("Reduce damage dealt to Legacy by 1") and a source (would) deal him 5 damage, would 'Superhuman Durability' still trigger since he's only actually dealt 4 damage with fortitude.

 

3. Damage Reduction + Absolute Zero

This is a follow-up to my previous question with a different hero.

Cryo Chamber (reduce fire damage dealt to absolute zero by 1) + Isothermic Transducer (whenever AZ is dealt fire damage, AZ deals 1 target that much cold damage) = When a source sends 5 fire damage to AZ, does he deal the full 5 cold damage to a target or only the 4 damage after the damage reduction by cryo chamber?

The same question for his inherent power (thermodynamics) which deals AZ 1 fire damage, which would be reduced to 0 with cryo chamber. Or is this situation different because he takes NO damage vs reduced damage?

 

Thanks in advance!

Triggered effects are conveyed through if/then statements. If trigger, then effect.

SHD fits that sentence structure perfectly. When he could be dealt damage of 5 or more is the explicit trigger condition for the effect of reducing it by 3

Fortitude is only laid out as a single effect clause. When do you reduce damage dealt to legacy by 1? It doesn't say. No explicit trigger clause. Thats what makes this a static effect since the implicit trigger is when the card is played. 

The trigger clause specifically makes SHD a triggered effect. 

But I agree with skippy, that there's a lot of valid interpretations and you are free to chose whichever works best for you.

If we treat whenever statements as if-then statements then both of undaunted's effects would be a triggered effect.  Which would mean that if Fanatic had undaunted and was lowest hp, and was dealt 1 irreducible damage from a villain and smoke bombs was out before undaunted you would redirect even though you would be redirecting damage that could not be taken.  Since we already have a ruling that that does not occur we either have to assume that Immunity breaks game mechanics or that undaunted's prevention is a static effect.

I side with the second option, that Fanatic's prevention of exactly one damage is a static effect, and is written using the word whenever simply because the conditions of applying the effect are complicated.

Another whenever statement that creates difficulty if treated as a triggered effect is flesh to liquid.  It uses the word whenever in its statement that increases his healing.  Which means you would have to play it before mortal to energy or you would deal the damage before increasing the healing.

Again it makes more sense that Mortal to Energy's increase in healing is static, even though it uses the word whenever.
