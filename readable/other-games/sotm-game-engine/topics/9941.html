I like these overall, but I would say a few might be pretty punishing. Which, depending on the group, could be a good thing or a bad thing

In particular, I would definitely not call Citizen Dawn in Rook City a novice level challenge. I think she gets underestimated a lot since she's from the base set, but she's pretty nasty. And Rook City makes everything worse.

Also, I don't know if there's a consensus on the Tomb of Anubis, but I find it to be annoyingly distracting - it's basically an extra villain deck. Could just be me. Combined with the Ennead, it's going to be a lot to keep track of.

As for the hero teams, they all look like they would be fun, with a good spread of complexity levels so new and experienced players can both enjoy it. The Mystic Masters would probably be the most difficult to work with new players (Visionary is the only of those I would even considering handing to a newbie), but I imagine that's why you marked it as difficult
