Crono-Ranger, Team leader Tachyon, Legacy and either Fanatic or Wraith I'd go for.  

You still need a good start, you really need inspiring presence/by any means first round, and the other one puts it in the bag.  Then it is all about focusing lots of damage instances on Dawn, which Tach Fanatic and Crono can do well.

+2 martyr is 7 to yourself, 9 to the villain, 10 if you add By any Means.  Add Embolden and her base power doing 6 or 8 and it's a quick game vs. almost any villain you can focus down, if you get retribution you can take down the boss yourself.
