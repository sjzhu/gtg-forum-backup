#2

As long as Fixed Point is in play, nothing is destructable, so everything stays in play and plays as normal.

As soon as Fixed Point leaves play, anything with 0 or fewer gets destroyed and player cards would flip to incapped (you should track negative HP with Fixed Point to account for healing).
