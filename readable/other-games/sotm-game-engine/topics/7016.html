1st question is currently causing a philosophical discussion in the "Can't deal damage thread". Personally, I rule that even if you're immune to the damage, you can still take it. Otherwise, Ra would have to destroy ongouings to deal with a Fire in the Biosphere, which really doesn't match my conception of how Ra should handle fire.

2nd, it's per hero. The "belonging to THAT hero" is the part that specifies that this is a per hero effect, versus Baron Blade's Devious Disruption which affects everyone.
