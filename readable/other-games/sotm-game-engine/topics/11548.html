I was playing a game today in Time Cataclysm where Parse had her Buffer Overflow up, and Fixed Point was out. So, does she get to discard villian cards for free? Fixed point states:

 When a villain card would enter play, you may discard it instead and destroy this card.

When this card is destroyed, draw a card and play the top card of the villain deck.

I realize discarding is not destroying, and the first part would play as normal. The only issue I'm seeing is a simultaneous action of discarding and destroying, and if one can't be fulfilled, the entire first sentence is prevented from happening.
