I only asked two rules questions of Christopher over the weekend and I know this was hotly debated somewhere but I can't remember which thread (and searching for "play" would be useless). It was debated in this thread.

 

If (card A) says "Play a card" on it, it does not go to the discard pile until after the card played by that card is resolved.

e.g. 3 Burst cards in trash. Play Fleet of Foot, draw Light Speed Barrage, play  Light Speed Barrage, the damage will be 3. Put  Light Speed Barrage in discard pile, put  Fleet of Foot  in discard pile.

 

EDIT:  If (card A) says "Play a card" on it, it does not go to the discard pile until (card B) is resolved, if (card B) also  says "Play a card" (card A) will only go to the discard pile after both (card C) and (card B) are resolved and (card B) will only go to the discard pile after (card C) is resolved.

Easiest way to do it is just to make a stack of the cards in front of you. For example, Blade plays a Hasten Doom. You put it on the table in front of you and read its text. He hits everyone and plays his top card, which is another Hasten Doom. You put that on top of the first Hasten Doom as it's "interrupted" that card's actions. You carry out this second Hasten Doom, and put the resultant third played card on top of it. Say it's a Blade Battallion. Okay, they just enter play and act in a minute, so you move them off the stack and put them wherever on the table you put your villain ongoing cards/targets. Now look at the top card of that stack you had (the second Hasten Doom). You've finished the "play the top card of the villain deck" part. Are there any more instructions? No. Okay, so it's done and goes into the trash. The card beneath it is the first Hasten Doom. Again, you check it to make sure it's all resolved. It is, so that, too, goes into the trash.

Probably doesn't matter so much with Blade unless you've gone all the way through his deck and a new play will require it to reshuffle, but with someone like Ambuscade, who repeatedly chains cards and reshuffles his trash into his deck, you can end up with a rather lengthy stack of still-in-progress cards piles up in front of you.

I find that this sort of tracking is easiest if I put a die or pawn on the card, covering all the text I've executed up to the moment that I stopped and did something else.  It's a lifesaver when running Argent Adept.  Die 1 = Musaragni's Harp over the word "perform".  Die 2:  Inspiring Supertonic on self.  Die 3:  Drake's Pipes over the "perform".  Die 4 - Sarabande of Destruction.  Die 5 - Explosives Wagon.  Die 6 - Bee Bot.  Resolve dice 4-6.  Then move die 3 to the word "two", place die 4 on Rhapsody of Vigor, move die 5 from one hero to the next as you give them their HP, remove dice 2-5, move die 1 to the word "accompany", put die 2 on Alacritous Subdominant, die 3 on Xu's Bell, die 4 on Syncopated Onslaught, die 5 on Inspiring Supertonic's accompany, and then resolve all that in reverse die order before finally being done.  (I have had it get almost that ridiculous in actual play, and would utterly melt my brain trying to do it all by memory.)
