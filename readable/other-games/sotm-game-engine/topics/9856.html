Like all villains, there are some heroes that are just terrible against her. An entire party that relies heavily on a small number of ongoings or equipment, for instance, really doesn't stand a chance against her. Having some heroes who can increase card play really helps, because you can drop some useless ongoings/equipment for her to trash.

 

So, ignoring hero specific strategies, you want to drop "bait" ongoings/equipment for her to steal. If you can stack her deck, it really helps. If you can move cards from the trash into her deck, that really helps. If you can tank, tank as much as you can. Leave environment targets alive if they attack the lowest. Don't bring pure melee heroes if you can help it because the acrobat kills. Area of Effect damage is your friend - save it for when there are six crew out.

 

I just played La Capitan for the first time two days ago, and we won, but it was really close for a while. I was running two tanks (Haka and the Scholar), and my wife was running constant damage (Chrono Ranger) and support (Tachyon). Her two died pretty fast, because she kept targetting the lowest HP units with her crew. Scholar and Haka went into super tanking mode and then we focused her down partially with Scholar's Grace Under Fire. Haka used his 3 damage to 2 targets power to hit her and either the Viking or the Samurai, and then used Rampage to finish them off. The other ones couldn't hurt Scholar faster than he could heal.

What I learned from that was, kill order is important. Kill the Acrobat first if she's out, then the guy who hits at start of turn based on ongoings, then the Ship, then the Viking, then the Last Breath, then Siegebreaker, then focus on her and let AoE kill Chip and any others.

Cappy likes to come out of the gate swinging; in order to beat her, you need to weather the initial assault and then get her stabilized long enough that you can build up for a killshot.  Avoid the likes of Mr. Fixer and Absolute Zero unless you have other heroes who can compensate for them, like Argent and Omnitron-X.  Tachyon is great since she can shut off damage for the entire crew (except Siege-Breaker) for a round without having to keep a card in play.  Unity might be fun, since nothing ever blows up Golems except with damage (beware of Battle Forged, though), and Bot-Fix can prevent equipment destruction from finding a target on the first or second turn (discuss this plan with your teammates first!).  Otherwise, in general, just figure that your job is to kill the ship, let her flip (so she stops shooting you every turn and instead does piddly healing), plink her down to 1 stolen card, then sit and build up.  If possible, control the villain deck to keep her from getting the ship back.  Once you're ready, unload on her fast; if you're Advanced, you need to plink her once at the start of a round to get rid of her last stolen card, then again for the redirection, and then let your heavy hitters take her apart in a hurry.

She's the exact opposite of Omnitron or Akash'bhuta in a lot of ways.  Both of those are slow, buildup-heavy villains with a ton of HP, whose victory is inexorable in the long run if you can't manage to do a better job of building up than they do.  La Capitan starts built up, and prevents you from building up in the short term, so she's definitely fierce, but the battle will eventually reach a turning point, after which just a handful of cards will pose any real threat.

I was about to ragequit after she stole every single frigging one of the Naturalist’s forms before I could even use their powers oh my god go screw yourself lady, though.

Anyway, I’ve rarely had a particular problem with La Capitan. The general strategy is multiple instances of damage per hero turn to ping down her passive immunity when she’s flipped and to punish her while she’s active. If there are multiple targets on the board, then chances are you want something to inflict damage to multiple targets (I’ve never used Scorched Earth as much as I did last night); if you’ve finally got her alone, and you can damage her, then pick whatever will do the most damage; if she’s alone and invulnerable, pick whatever will hit her the most times, but maybe save your big guns (say, if Tachyon has a lot of burst cards in her trash, you might not want to waste Lightspeed Barrage just for the sake of getting a card out from under her).

She has a lot of effects to steal your equipment and ongoing, so put out whatever piece of crap equipment or ongoing to you can to provide a buffer for when she steals, so you’ll have something to sacrifice in order to keep the good stuff out. I take particular pleasure in doing this with cards like Pushing the Limits, which will be destroyed on my next turn anyway. Unless the team is on board with sacrificing their stuff for you, though, I wouldn’t count on being able to build up big combos; I wasn’t even able to get the Staff of Ra and Flame Barrier out at the same time last night because she kept stealing my crap. Hope that you can set up a long-term strategy, but don’t depend on it. Your plays should be about what you can do in the moment.

La Paradoja Magnifica needs to be destroyed as quickly as possible, even to the exclusion of La Capitan herself, but the card I hate most and want to see destroyed above all others is Mable. Nothing wrecks your setup like her, and she needs to go down. Similarly, don’t even bother inflicting damage on Trueshot unless you’re going to focus on her until she’s dead, because her regeneration makes it a waste to do anything to her unless you’re committed to taking her out. It’s probably worth ignoring La Capitan herself for a while to take out Siege-Breaker too, since he’s going to kill someone faster than she is.
