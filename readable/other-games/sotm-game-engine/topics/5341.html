Dealing damage is only optional if the word “may” is present. Dealing damae to multiple targets is optional only if the word “may” or “up to” is included. Not having the cards in front of me I can not tell you if said cards contain “may” or “up to,” but if they do, then the damage is optional. If they do not, then the damage is not optional.
