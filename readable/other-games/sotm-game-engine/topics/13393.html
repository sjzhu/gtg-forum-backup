So I've been playing against AOG Spite in the video game, and it just seems impossible to get enough Victim cards A. in play and B. under the Safe House to make his drug cards flip back without making Spite flip. Because of the order of operations, he always starts with one Drug card flipped and one Victim card out. Then, at best, he plays another victim card. Even if you put both of them under the Safe House that turn, he's going to flip another Drug card at the start of the turn, and since it's now equal to what's under the safe house, neither of them will flip back. Even if he plays another victim card, there's still no way to get ahead of the curve.

And once he's got three drug cards flipped face up, there's just plain no way to get any of them flipped back before he flips to his other side.

So am I missing something? Outside of forcing him to play cards, which only applies to a tiny handful of heroes and one or two environments, there doesn't seem to be any way to get ahead of the drug curve.

He starts with a victim and plays another. One of which has to be a Good Samaritan and the other has to be any of the other Victims. Over the course of the hero's turns, save the other victim, at the start of the Environment turn you save the Good Samaritan and if it plays a Good Samaritan you can save that one aswell. If another victim is played, then you now have 3 Victims under the Safehouse. At the start of his turn he'll either flip his Character Cards if (H) = 3 or he'll Flip a drug at the start face-up and then flip a face-up one back face-down at the end.
