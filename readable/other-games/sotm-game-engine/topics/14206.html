Some of Ambuscade's Snare cards have wording along the lines of "Put this card next to the Hero Character Card with the fewest HP".  A player in our group (whose Hero was incapacitated) argued that his Hero Character Card had "the fewest HP".  I (thinking like a programmer) argued that there was difference between "0" and "NULL".  Opinions?

Powerhound is correct.

An incap side of a Character card has no HP number on it, is not a target and does not have HP.  Zero is a number you can have, but your incap side lacks the ability to have HP, even 0 hp.
