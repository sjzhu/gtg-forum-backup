Ok so I played the app and here's what I discovered. It's a little wierd.

If Omnitron has been dealt 7 or more damage already in the round and then one of his components is played then the component is not auto destroyed. HOWEVER, it does track the damage dealt so far that round. The card will only get detroyed when a damage event happens, though. Dealing damage is the trigger for the destruction check. So if Omnitron takes 1 more damage the component will be destroyed.

I've tested it several times now.

So, that being said, here are the implications for the Bloodsworn Colosseum titles:

1. If a Reckless comes into play and a target has already dealt itself 3 damage in the turn the target may not claim Reckless. However, if it deals itself any more damage at all then the condition check will trigger and then it is claimed.

2. If a the third target in a turn being destroyed causes Champion to be played then all 3 targets do count toward the 3 targets in a turn. However, the title cannot be claimed until a another target is destroyed while the title is face-up.

3. Similar timing and triggering applies for Mindbreaker, Chaos Lord, Indiscriminant, and other titles.

All things track backwards except Omnitron's components and Rooftop Combat (which are treated as if they had "since this card entered play" text, but those cards are already overly full of text).
