"Nemesis cards" and "Nemesis" are those with Nemesis as a keyword. "Cards with a nemesis icons" are any cards with a nemesis icon on the card.

 

Edit: Enticing Target would redirect damage from any Villain Character Card, as they all have nemesis icons, along with cards with the nemesis keyword, as they also have a nemesis icon.

Cards with Nemesis Icons are any card that includes a Nemesis Icon, including Hero and Villain Character Cards.

Nemesis cards are cards with the word Nemesis in the "descriptive" box (where Ongoing would be).

Ray Manta is referring to the same thing, again as if the instructions was "All Ongoings".
