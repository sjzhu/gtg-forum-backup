Hi, folks--

Probably a dumb question here, but I was wondering about Parse's "Critical Multiplier" card. Card says the effect goes off "whenever you discard a card from hand."

So for single-card discards (like "Segmentation Fault") this obviously means you get to pop a +1 bonus on someone, which is nice.

But what about cards that allow multiple discards (or force them) as a single effect? Examples would be Parse's own "Recompile" or the various environment cards that are destroyed by discarding your whole hand. In these cases, do you get:

A) A single +1 bonus for the entire discard action, or

B) One +1 bonus for EACH card discarded?

Under A, I discard a theoretical 10 cards when discarding my hand and grant only +1. Under B, I instead grant +10 overall using the same discard.

Any help would be appreciated. Thanks!
