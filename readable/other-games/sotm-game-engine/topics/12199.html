I don't recall if we addressed this particular issue in playtesting, but figured it would be good to make the answer public either way.

If a target would be destroyed by the damage that would earn it The Unbreakable, what happens?

Here's an example scenario:

Orrim Hiveminded has taken 3 damage this round and has 2 HP left. The Unbreakable is an available title. Expatriette pulls out her shotgun and deals 4 projectile damage to Orrim. Here are some possibilities for what happens:

  1. Orrim is destroyed before he can claim the title.
  2. Orrim claims the title immediately upon taking the shotgun damage and the Bloodsworn gain 1 favor. The title's effect kicks in, undoes the 4 projectile damage, heals Orrim for 6 (leaving him at 8HP), and then the title is destroyed (shuffled back into the title deck).
  3. Orrim claims tht title immediately upon taking the shotgun damage and the Bloodsworn gain 1 favor. Since he takes no damage after gaining the title, it does not trigger and Orrim is destroyed. The title is returned to the available title pool.

I believe that item 3 best represents what would happen. This is also potentially relevant for Mindbreaker, Reckless, and Champion titles. Thoughts?

Here is Unbreakable

Title: The Unbreakable - After a target is dealt 6 or more damage in one turn, put this card under that target
You don't gain the title until after the damage is dealt and resolved.

Also, immediatly upon a target being reduced to 0 HP or fewer, it is destroyed and leaves play.
