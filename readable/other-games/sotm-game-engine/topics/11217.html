"Your heroes" means only The Sentinels.  (Confusingly, this is true even if you're playing two heroes at once, as is suggested for when you have only one other player.)  Another player's hero is not "yours", and if you're playing two heroes, you pretend to be two different players, one controlling The Sentinels and the other playing whoever else (I recommend Legacy).  Thusly, Legacy is not "yours" when you take your Sentinels turn.
