If more than 1 hero are tied for something, most cards, most/least hp, etc. Players choose who will be affected from the heroes that fit that description. However, only the number of heroes afected doesn;t change from what the card says. So if the card says the hero with the most cards, you choose 1 hero among the ones with the most cards.
