The real reason to play Recharge Mode is NOT the extra cards. It's the -1 damage. With Armor Plating and Recharge Mode Bunker has a -2. Very few heroes can set up a -2 DR. Against some villains (Matirarch, Vengance Team) that is the difference between winning and losing.

True, no one likes sitting around drawing cards. That's probably the bigger reason why Recharge Mode is not played more.

Playing Gattling Gun or the Omni Cannon before Recharge Mode lets you still contribute while you super tank.
