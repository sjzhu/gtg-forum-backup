If it's in play and then moves to the trash it's destroyed, if it's in your hand and it moves to the trash that's discarded.

So Omnitron's "destroy all equipment in play" is destroy, and the police live on to fight another day.

The discard effect has to trigger for police backup's "would discard" to take effect.

For example if Fixer was highest HP hero vs. Matriarch and a fowl was destroyed, and Fixer chose to destroy a card he would not get to redirect the damage that he would have taken if he hadn't destroyed a card.

In Police Backup's case you would have to choose the discard effect if it was optional for Backup to be destroyed.

If you get a "discard your hand" and a hero has 0 cards in hand and they choose to discard their hand of zero cards police backup would be destroyed because they would have discarded.

This is how I interpret Police Backup.
