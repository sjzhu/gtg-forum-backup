It was not "destroyed by a villain card", but by itself, so it would go to your trash (and be a Burst therein).

  1. Omnitron-X's Rocket Punch reads "Deals Omnitron-X deals 1 target 2 projectile damage. Omnitron-X May deal 1 target 1 projectile Damage" Can this be used as 2 instances of damage on 1 target? It's not worded like Tempest's Chain Lightning or Bunker's Grenade Launcher ("may" and "second/third/fourth").

I'm semi-certain that's a "no", but I could be mistaken.  The same logic in either case would also apply to the Terrible Tech-Strike of Chrono-Ranger.

  1. If I used Tempest's Ball Lightning to destroy a target and 2 of Maria Helena's Revenge ongoing cards were in play, could I destroy both of them before they deal each hero H minus 1 psychic damage? "You may destroy up to 2 ongoing cards" is written after and below (like separate paragraphs) the deal 1 target 4 damage…so I was wondering if that makes a difference at all.

You always execute all of a card's instructions in the order written, so first you deal 4 damage to a target, and completely resolve all effects thereof, including death triggers from a target killed by the damage.  Only then, when all aftereffects of the damage are cleared, do you move on to destroy Ongoings.  (And only then, after all aftereffects of that are cleared, do you put Ball Lightning in the trash; this is effectively an invisible last line of rules text on all One-Shot cards.)
