I'm actually not sure what happens to a Shinobi that is on top of a hero deck.

For starters the Rulebook's glossary has this to say about incapacitation

A Hero is incapacitated when reduced to 0 or fewer HP. When this occurs, immediately remove all of that hero's cards from the game and flip the Hero Character Card.

Since the Shinobi is not one of their cards, the card would not be removed from the game. So my best guess is that it would remain where there. I guess cards like Fumbling Fool could discard it, thus putting it back into play. That would then allow the arguement of allowing other Shinobi to be stacked ontop of it.

If I were playing and this occured, I would just have it go back into play in the Environment play area.
