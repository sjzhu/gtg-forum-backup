Ive updated the wiki with the Sentinel Tactics characters found in the artwork. (http://sotm.wikidot.com/character-references#toc59 link to jump right to it)

 

Some interesting things! 

 

The White cloak guy from Sucker Punch shows up again!

 

As does the Brown and Gold armor from Nible Strike, Hyperactive Senses, High Risk Behavior, and Plucky Break

 

The Melty Guy on Volitile Parts is back as well, though it's still uncertain if that is The Radioactivist or not

 

And a new face! A Wolverine/Sabertooth looking fellow on Beacon's Head on Collison!!

 

Someone check over my work, make sure I didnt miss anyone or make any glaring mistakes on guessing who some of the harder to see figures were. There are a couple other people to note, It's great to see come Sentinel Tactics, Tempest and Setback are still around.

 

Quick question for those with better eyes  - Devestating Aurora - do you think that is Expatriette in the lowercorner? Could she have survived the Dark Watch Incap?? Is that why Setback is back in Uniform?

 

Also, interesting to note, Tactics breaks the 'Villain fighting Villain, hero Fighting hero' no no 'rules' we've come to think exist. Legacy is fighting what might be Champion Bot (or might not be) The Operative is against Citizen Hack, and Proletariat is taking on Omnitron.

 

 

 

And one last thing.... Heroic Interception in Legacy's cards.... is that Aminia Twain? Does she not become Miss Information with the Timelines all wonky now????
