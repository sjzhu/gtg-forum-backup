Siege-Breaker is an interesting member of the crew since we don't know what was his deal. Final Breath is a ronin, Chip is a pilot that got lost (or that's what I remember from Letters Page) and Mable is a circus member. But SB? Is he a modern man using weapons, or a medieval warrior that got some new glasses and a razor after being recruited? 

Maybe he is a larper that Capitan confused with a real warrior, and who couldn't refuse a life of time-travelling adventure and a cool armor. 

 

So, favorite minions: 

-The entire Paradoja's crew. I hate them, I want to see them destroyed as soon as possible, but I can't deny they have style. Same thing for Seargent Steel's agents.

-The informant: She's the one member of the organization I take most care in neutralizing. She can screw my game with some telephone calls. 

-The Chosen Disciple: Of Gloomweaver's minions I get the sense she's the most competent. Maybe because her quote is the most composed, as opposed to the others melodramatic threats (being surrounded by an army of the undead also helps). 

-S'Sdari The Bloody: Because of her backstory, a fashionista turned scientific-hero turned gladiator. 

-Orrim The Hiveinded: I was trying to keep a single favorite for deck, but I love the alien-parasite-hivemind with goofy eyes trying to give "hugs". 

 

Favorite Nemesis: 

-Glamour: She's the Nemesis that messes up games the most, the one I'll have the hardest time getting rid of (and one of the reasons I like to play vengeance games on target-heavy enviroments).  

-Ammit: I already liked the mythological monster, but the way she outgambited Ra and eventualy turned into the new lord (lady?) of the underworld is impressive. 

-Equity: He's ominous, creepy, has an interesting visual and powers that, while quite low in the super-power scale, can bring lot's of problems to any of  his victims of people fighting him (unless you're Mr. Fixer).    

-Heartbreaker: Like others said, his backstory is quite tragic, specially since it happened to one of the few cards people liked seeing on Rook City. The way he holds that coin and pencil... Weirdly threatening. 

 

Favorite Enviroment Characters: 

-Blood Countess Barthory: I like vampires, the countess has style, and after the Letters Page on Blood Magic I kept wishing she was a main villain. 

-I will have to add the entirety of the Endlings. There's no way I can choose a favorite. 

-Dr. Tremata: The other person in Rook City I like to see. Shame what happened to her. 

-Anubis: Because it's Anubis. 

-The Ninjas: They can be incredibly annoying, but in any target-heavy game I'll try to play them from my deck as soon as possible.
