Something I caught during this latest listen, which I missed back when this first went live (Chaosmancer did notice, and presumably others did, I freely admit to being dumb here). When Guise says he had “47 things I wanted to do with bananas!” it’s clear that, as much as he’s still being a goofball, he did notice that something tragic just happened, and this is his way of dealing with it. It’s essentially a sort of trauma response; he’s not going to consciously acknowledge that 47 innocent people just died, but he is going to avenge them and do the right thing, even if he’s refusing to acknowledge that.
