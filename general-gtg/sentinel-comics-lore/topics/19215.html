The loosely Christian upbringing of C&A is extremely apparent in their definition of magic. I have an extremely different attitude on the subject; I don’t think magic is this inherently dark and dangerous thing, it’s just the capacity to rewrite reality, and is no more light or dark than the person doing it.

So Cantrip is definitively (ha!) the reason why the Fey Court is a deck in Definitive Edition

Earth has at least twice been resettled by a group of superpowered refugees - first the Egyptian gods, and now the Maerinyans.

“Clearing steer” lol.

I would define essence, spirit and soul very specifically. The Egyptian mythology concept of spirit or essence was basically the Ka, while the Ba was effectively the mind; they also had a bunch of other parts of the self, possibly as many as nine or ten of them, and “soul” is what you call the whole collection. I get a lot of this conceptualization from the White Wolf RPGs Mummy the Resurrection and Mummy the Curse, which are slightly different takes on that myth-cycle. I figure that no other culture in history has been quite as invested in death and the soul than ancient Egypt, so I like using their names for this model.
