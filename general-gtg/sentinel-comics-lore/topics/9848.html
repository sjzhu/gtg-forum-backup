My apologies if this theory is old news. While hardly new to the game, i've only recently discovered this very fun board to discuss game universe theories. I did a couple of searches and didn't find this particular theory anywhere.

 

My idea is that Con from the Final Wasteland is the Time-Crazed Prisoner from the Block.

 

I have read that Con is someone who definitely has appeared elsewhere in the game. The markings on Con's bunker bear (in my opinion) similarities to the diagram on the prisoner's wall. We are are fairly certain the prisoner has done some sort of time travel or at the very least has knowledge of the future (or possible futures). His bleak nature could certainly fit with someone who has seen the future of the Final Wasteland. The name "Con" works with him having been a convict in the past.

 

If the TCP has seen the future he may very well have built the bunker and stored his mind inside of it's computers as a way to survive the apocalypse (my preferred theory).

 

Alternately, he could be a conciousness from that future that has somehow come back into the past to inhabit a body in the present (or wherever in time he was arrested from). Perhaps he is "unstuck in time" also Slaughterhouse Five and doesn't move through time in the same fashion as others. 

 

At any rate, I hope this is a new discussion. If not, I apologize for re-treading well-worn ground, it seems like someone else must have picked up on this before me. Just something that popped into my head.
