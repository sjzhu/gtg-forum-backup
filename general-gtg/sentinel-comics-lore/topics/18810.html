The Vertex episode… I can understand nominating it for some hypothetical Saddest Episode, but worst episode?

But IIRC, most of the protesting furor in the Discord was over the Book of Monsters nomination, FWIW. I know that while it wasn’t a favorite ep I nonetheless liked it; I particularly liked the bit with the pixie and Charles for giving us some interesting ethical dilemmas outside the usual superhero/villain dynamics. I still am wondering why it was nominated.

I will sheepishly admit to having a dark and morbid enough sense of humor to find That One Cult of Gloom Question amusing in a dark way. That said, I did not nominate it. I was a dummy and mixed up the deadline with Jean-Marc’s Mordengrad project and so thought I had a weekend to work on it when I didn’t X( but I would have nominated The Director’s love letter to Parse in the Shipping Editor’s Note, FWIW. I think everything else I would have nominated was so anyway, so small loss.

Those two shipping episodes were a gold mine of humor. I ended up voting for TakeWalker’s question as funniest because “How much of a Space Dad IS Hugh Lowsley?” got a big snicker from me back in the day.

My votes:

Funniest Episode: Shipping, of course.

Worst Episode: The Fake Legal Advice

Best Audio Production: Something Wicked. While I absolutely loved the Radio Play and thought it was perfect, the Something Wicked episode struck me as something where Trevor didn’t have to put in the work of jazzing it up but he went and put in a ton of extra flourishes anyway so I had to appreciate that.

Best Storytelling: The Xtremeiverse episode is still my favorite with the lovingly detailed stories that were also ridiculously over the top, so I had to go for that one.

Funniest Question: As per above.

Saddest Question: The Cult of Gloom has this one locked, IMHO.

Best Fan Theory: I loved the one with La Comodora going back in time to become OblivAeon just for the fact of being both over the top ridiculous and yet still eminently plausible (if of course still wrong).

Most Revealing Question: This was the one that kept me hung up on not submitting earlier, bah, and I still don’t really have a personal answer. I voted for the two Wager Masters question out of the ones nominated, as I thought the tidbit of “what happens when there’s ever TWO of a Singular Entity?” is an interesting cliffhanger to worry about for the future.

This episode is mostly not very fun listening since it’s largely a clip show (and commits the severe sin of replaying the same clip twice), but I really love the three fan theory questions that were nominated. One is a super cool theory that’s definitely wrong, one is the fans (one in particular, and a bunch of others getting an HM for glimpsing part of the same truth) successfully sussing out a secret that C&A had carefully concealed for more than half a decade after deciding it was the outcome they wanted…and one, I strongly suspect, is a fan masterfully laying out and explanation better than anything they’d had in mind, which thereby became the canonical vision of how the Vertex timeline happens. I can’t be sure on that last part , but it seems likely, and I love the symmetry of three fan theories getting responses that can be summarized as “thanks, but you’re wrong”, “thanks, and you’re right”, and “thanks; we were wrong, you were right instead.”

I like the idea that Bourgeoisie has the power to literally turn money into super-constructs, more Captain Cosmic than Unity (since part of being “boozhie” is disposing of things which are no longer shiny and new). The more of her billions she spends in a battle, the more potent her constructs during that battle, but then she has to spend months doing various wheels and details to make back the profit she wasted that day.

From a perspective of legality, Kismet has never committed a murder, but is definitely guilty of reckless endangerment, and might well be possible to charge with manslaughter and make it stick.

Earl Christopher and Viscount Adam. Trevor’s a baronet, but doesn’t go by the title, because it sounds awkward with either Trevor or Casterline.
