McBehrer:

 Maybe a promo Akash'Bhuta would be neat. No idea what kind of mechanics would be involved, but maybe one less dependent on environment targets – she's absolutely DECLAWED in the Freedom Tower – but plays extra cards consistently.

 

I actually made one that did this! She interacted with the environment a lot more, and had two sides, one of which corresponded to high-target environments, and one of which corresponded to nontarget-heavy environments. The way it works is that on the "target" side, she played an extra env card the first time each turn a nontarget environment card came into play, and it was another nontarget, she flipped (Because two in a row suggests a nontarget heavy environment) and on the other side, she did the same thing for environment target cards. It's a mechanic I feel works really well, and interestingly makes her the most dangerous in very mixed environments (because she'll end up playing many extra environment cards, and flipping often which forces the heroes to be capable of handling both sides rather than just one.)

 

Actually, since it's apparently cool if we share such things…

 

(starting side) MOTHER NATURE:

 

The first time each turn a nontarget environment card is put into play, play the top card of the environment deck. If it is another nontarget environment card, flip Akash'Bhuta.

 

Redirect all damage dealt to villain targets by the environment to the hero target with the highest hp.

 

At the end of each villain turn, put a random environment target from the environment trash into play.

 

Advanced: Decrease damage dealt to environment targets by 1.

 

NATURE'S WRATH

The first time each turn a environment target is put into play, play the top card of the environment deck. If it is another environment target environment card, flip Akash'Bhuta.

 

Redirect all damage dealt to villain targets by the environment to the hero target with the highest hp.

 

If an environment card is destroyed during a hero turn, Akash'Bhuta deals that hero H toxic damage.

 

Advanced: Increase damage dealt by the environment by 1.

 

(200 hp)

 

The redirection can be brutal if she's got a lot of limbs in play and an environment card comes into play that deals damage to all targets. So, y'know, be careful if you're fighting the living spirit of nature in a place that has frequent volcanic eruptions and such. Also she's sorely nerfed by Danger Sense, although she still has all her limbs and normal cards, so not COMPLETELY nerfed. But it's kind of an issue that means if you want a serious challenge, you might want to avoid Legacy.

I'm guessing it's because Akash-Bhuta is usually abbreviated A-B…Abby…Envisioner has a tendency to give nicknames to the female characters for some reason:  Fanatic becomes Fanny, the aforementioned A-B becomes Abby…

I wasn't consciously doing it to the females in particular, the names just tend to work out that way.  Haka is short enough not to want for any abbreviation, Chrono-Ranger can easily be CR or just one of the two halves of the name, but Akash always looks weird to me, so I go either with Abby or Akasha, and the latter has connotations that don't really fit.  Besides having three syllables, Fanatic brings out the irreverent in me because she's so utterly committed to her dogmatic beliefs.  Unity is faster to type, though not to say, so I only occasionally shorter her to Uni.  Legacy is usually Legs, whether it's the two male versions or Young Pauline.  Bunker, Ra, Scholar, Argent…pretty much all the male characters have short, convenient names, or can be abbreviated to two letters, like AZ or AA.  And Omnitron-X can be abbreviated many ways, but is genderless.  This changes a bit in Vengenace, with Naturalist becoming Natch.  And among the villains, I pretty much always shorten Gloomweaver to Gloomy, drop all of Voss's titles, refer to members of the Ennead as "Ennies", etc.  "Chair" just sounds weird though, so even though the Chairman's name is two-point-five syllables, I tend to say the whole thing (not counting "the").
