The government cracks down, the Dark Watch is formed, the Freedom V bide their time setting up their big move.  During this time Pauline starts taking a bigger role, leading to Beacon, Expat and Setback become a team, an item and then a heartbreaking end (possibly).  Then we move to Tactics.
