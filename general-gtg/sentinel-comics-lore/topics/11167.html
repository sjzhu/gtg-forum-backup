Do you think we'll eventually see every hero belonging to a canonical team?  With Freedom Four/Five/Six, the Dark Watch, and the Prime Wardens, somewhere over half the existing heroes are on a team.  And the Sentinels are a teams.

 

The teams that already exist seem to hit tropes from existing team comics.  The Freedom Five hit a lot of JLA tropes (and also some Fantastic Four tropes, with Tachyon and Freedom Tower reminding me of Reed Richards and the Baxter Building).  The Dark Watch evokes a lot of the Spiderman Team Up comics, in which he frequently worked with heroes like Daredevil, Punisher, and Doctor Strange.  The Prime Wardens reminds me of an early Defenders line up (Dr. Strange, Hulk, Namor, Silver Surfer, Valkrie -> Agent Adept, Haka, Tempest, Captain Cosmic, Fanatic)

 

Sentinels Tactics Uprising sort of makes an (unnamed) team out of Visionary, Beacon, and Unity, and because of their age they seem kind of like the Teen Titans or Young Avengers.

While it would be fun to see lots of teams in SotM, I don't think it is necessary.  If you look at most comic book heroes, unless they have their own mainstream team-up comic, heroes tend to show up from time to time.  That and there will be times where teams end due to conflict.  I think the great thing about SotM is that really specific teams don't need to exist.  The promos associated with those teams are nice, but I love mixing up a member of the FF, DW, and PW with another random hero in different environments against diffrent villains.  At this point at least if there were going to be another "promo team," they would probably have to be a team that travels through time correcting timelines.
