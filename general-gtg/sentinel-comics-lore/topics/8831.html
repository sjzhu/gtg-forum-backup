So, Dr. Medico and Mainstay, the first and most "core" members of The Sentinels (the other two being latecomers and not long-time college friends).  I was looking at the picture of Mainstay on Iron-Fist Strike, and I remembered reading in the Sentinels' backstory how Mainstay "just kept getting bigger and stronger", and realized that this sounds not entirely unlike Haka.  And then, when I was re-reading their bio to confirm this, I saw the reference to the compassionate Medico as a "being of living energy", and it clicked to me - The Scholar also turns into glowing yellow light, powered directly by his lifeforce (hence he deals damage whenever he heals).  So very possibly, these two members of the Sentinels, and possibly others besides, are tapping into the same power sources as our other heroes!  Equating the Idealist with the Visionary is tenuous since they glow in different colors, but they are both child psychics who were exploited by mad scientists, so it might be a match...that leaves only Writhe as not having an analogue among existing heroes (though, if I absolutley had to pick one, I'd go with Nightmist, who looks completely different in her amorphous form, but both are dabblers in shadowy forces with a thing for tentacles).
