Theta_Sigma:

Doesn't Chrono-Ranger just hate rat monsters, though? And The Matriarch is Tachyon's cousin

I think the point is that they have a direct connection to big aspects of Rook City:  The villains.  Chrono-Ranger is trying to claim a bounty on the Ur-Rat Beast that stole his arm and overran the world, turning it into the Final Wasteland.  Tachyon has a younger cousin obsessed with undermining her because her parents compared their emotionally disturbed teenage daughter to a very successful superhero superscientist in her late thirties.  Bad move.

I think what we can take from this is that there are layers to the "most thematic" five person team to take to Rook City.  A Plague Rat party just wouldn't feel complete without Chrono, and The Matriarch would be screeching about her cousin joining the brawl while her birds stole every shiny thing that wasn't nailed down.  But The Organization may not require Chrono or Tachyon's involvement at all.
I'd say that the appropriate Rook City group would be bigger than five, with certain subsets and side characters that could be included in certain situations (like the rap supergroup Hieroglyphics, except RC Wraith/Fixer/Expat are Souls of Mischief and Chrono-Ranger is Del Tha Funkee Homosapien).

I think the better question is who would feel out of place in an exclusively Rook City themed team?
