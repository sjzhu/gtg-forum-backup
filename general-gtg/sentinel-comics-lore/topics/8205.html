It would be interesting if we had some kind of opportunity to give you our assessment of the characters. That would be really hard, though, and >G would have to put some specific limitations on what could and couldn't be shared, which would make it even more challenging...

The playtesters don't get the character background info - we just get the card names and rules text. We don't even have art or flavor text to give us insight into the hero (other than a sketch of the hero on their character card. We might be able to talk about how each hero plays, but even that is hard to do without getting into the mechanics.

(This is best demonstrated by our playtesting of Unity, for those who haven't heard the story. A (not-insignificant) number of playtesters just didn't like her, didn't understand her, and didn't think she was a hero they would enjoy playing. We had these weird cards like Stealth Bot that took damage for others - shouldn't a stealthy bot actually just prevent its own damage? This didn't make a lot of sense. But we were missing that Unity is a Freedom Five fan, that her five key bots represented the members of the F5 (at the time). We were missing her voice from the flavor text. We were missing a lot. When we saw the proofs of those cards, many playtesters reversed their opinion of her - all those details we were missing completely changed our views.)
