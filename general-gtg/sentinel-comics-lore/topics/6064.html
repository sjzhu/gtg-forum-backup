Hey, all, sorry I’m not around here much. Busy times! To answer specifically regarding the Argent Adept, the red/brown “fret board” on his chest would go along the opening in the chest of the tunic, so it wouldn’t be on the back. He does have the cape though, so whatever’s on the back would be hidden. The idea with his costume is for it to be a solid black silhouette with the cape closed, then bright, shiny silver when he opens it to do magicks. If you have any other time-sensitive questions regarding art or costumes or anything really, feel free to email me at adam@sentinelsofthemultiverse.com, as I’m much more likely to see it there.
