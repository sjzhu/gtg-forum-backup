With the Cosmic Contest contest upcoming, I thought it would be a fun thing to do to work through the brackets with my friends. However, I am the only one of my friends who listens to the Letters Page. So to get my friends up to speed I made a quick primer on every character in the Cosmic Contest. Then I thought, maybe someone else would get a kick out of this?  have no idea if this is actually the place to post this, or if anyone is actually interested in this, but I couldn’t see any harm in just posting it. 

A few clarifications. First, none of this was created by me. The story is from the Letters Page and the Sentinels wiki, then art is mostly from the card game, with one or two other pieces pulled off of google image search. Second, I definitely got some things wrong. A lot of this was working by memory or by my understanding which can totally be flawed. Third, a large percentage of the content is essentally speculation. It is mostly based on their story/mechanics but, especially in the Strengths and Weaknesses sections, I absolutely could have misread or misunderstood a ton of things. So feel free to ignore any part I got wrong. Finally, This is based on the Information I had as of episode 41 - Wager Master. Many characters have incomplete information because they haven't had an episode yet. On those characters I used a lot more guess work to fill in the gaps, so their information is even less reliable than the rest.

If anyone out there finds this useful or interesting despite all that, great!
