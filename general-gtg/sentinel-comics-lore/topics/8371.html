Short intro; I work at a board game store and most of our customer base focuses on Euro games. While SotM has been doing very well I get frequent questions about how to set the game up and where to start. Apparently the amount of choice available is overwhelming when you are used to just sitting down and following a rulebook to start your game. To help with this I have been working on a starter guide for SotM that I can hand out for people just purchasing SotM as well as keep a copy in our demo game.

Having successfully typed the guide up I am looking for more input on it; preferably constructive although if you think something is not good tell me please. As time goes on I am hoping to expand what I have written as well as incorporate other forum goers work with their permission. Guide here
