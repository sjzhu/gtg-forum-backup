With my necromantic powers, I bid this thread... LIVE!

Nobody ever thought much of Jonathan Donovan as a singer. He could carry a tune well enough, and he could give you a note if you asked for it, but there was never any passion behind the music.

Baron Blade once composed a classical opera, just to see if he could. It tells the story of the founding of Mordengrad, back in the mists of history. It's entirely fictional, of course, but the Baron believes that it illustrates a higher truth.

While Maria Helena Teresa Fafila Servanda Jimena Mansuara Paterna Domenga Gelvira Placia Sendina Belita Eufemia Columba Gontina Aldonza Mafalda Cristina Tegrida de Falcon's singing voice never improved beyond passable, she did make time to improve her skills at the Flameas she got older.

One of Grand Warlord Voss' military innovations was to formally allow and encourage the use of music in Thorathian military manoeuvres. Soldiers had a long tradition of playing loud and aggressive music to get in the mood for battle. Voss not only allowed this, but permitted armoured vehicles to have enormous speakers mouned on them. Not only did it improve morale, but the din proved an effective tool of psychological warfare.

Sergeant Steel only listens to classic 80s heavy metal. And he will NOT shut up about it.
