In reference to Ermine, I think that there’s a definite continuum. On the hero side, you have “will save the life of his worst enemy” through “eighties Parse”, while for villains you’ve got “willing to be an accessory to Baron Blade murdering” on up to “Spite”. The big important thing that made Jack Donovan into a monster was that he got blood on his hands and got a taste for it. I don’t think Ermine would ever get that sick thrill out of knowing she caused a death. In fact I believe she would probably not care if someone died “off panel”, but if she was holding a sharp object and someone flung themself onto it, punctured an aorta and bled out all over Ermine, I think she’d probably throw up, even though it was arguably not her fault and definitely not a first degree murder; the viscerality of it would probably trigger the aversion of most normal non-psychotic humans to causing death.

I think Legacy keeps the pairs together, and BB is most hesitant to kill Tachyon the fellow scientist, plus killing the Wraith would be slightly a favor to the Chairman, who BB is in business with to a degree.
