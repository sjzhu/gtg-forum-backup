If you don't have Tactics, I can provide some footenotes of the important story information we know from Flame of Freedom and Uprising:

  • Tactics takes place about 5-10 years after SotM
  • Following the cataclysmic events that ended SotM, access to the various timestreams has been cut off for good.
  • There is a general distrust and backlash against superheros among the general population. Specific reason is yet unkown. But it is related to how the events at the end of SotM played out.
  • Due to the backlash, the US Governenment exerted more control on the Freedom 5 requiring a court order before they could legally act. In response, Mia Montgomery revealed her true identity as the Wraith publicly and leveraged her personal wealth in order to buy the Freedom Tower, build Tachyon a new lab, pay off the outstanding dept on Absolute Zero's cryo suit, and build a new Bunker suit. The Freedom 5 was now an independent entity.
  • Even with his dept paid off, Ryan Frost commited to stay with "his family" and continue to fight with the F5
  • Baron Blade returned with a new master plan to destroy the Freedom 5 Tower.
  • During Barons newest plan, a new Ra had returned to help after the original Ra (Blake Washington) vanished during the final events of SotM
  • Some time after, a broken Omnitron IV, after assimilating a vast amount of data gathered from the destroyed parts of Omnitron-X, made another move to regain all of his former glory. Now in his 5th form, Omnitron V plans to finish what Omnitron IV couldn't...the ultimate creation of the Omni-Reaper!
  • Dawn and her citizens have been waiting and biding time. Now that a new power source, the Oblivion Shards, has found its way to our world, Dawn and her citizens have begun striking out and launching offensives to secure these powerful artifacts.
  • There is another mysterious figure interested in collecting these Oblivion Shards as well. Enlisting the help of Ambuscade, Proletariat, and the Operative (who is now in the proccess of setting up her own version of the Organization in Megaopolis), this Mysterious figure is also in the hunt for Oblivion Shards.

 

Thats about it until we get the next part of the story and find out whats happening over in Broken City!
