For my own edification, I've been making a list of variant cards and their status as to existence in the main Sentinels universe or another Sentinels universe. I thought I'd share.

The terms I'm using: Sentinels-Prime (the main universe), Sentinels-Iron (the Iron Legacy universe), Sentinels-XTREME! (the universe of the XTREME Sentinels), Sentinels-Wasteland (the universe represented by the Wasteland), Sentinels-Two (the universe where Baron Blade kills Paul Parsons and Pauline becomes Legacy), Kingkiller (the universe of Kvothe the Six String), Sentinels-Mirror (the universe with a heroic Baron Blade/Luminary and an evil Legacy), Sentinels-Revo (universe where Benchmark is a real hero and Revo-Corp are good guys), and Sentinels-Puzzler (the universe America's Cleverest Legacy comes from).

Not listed, because no promo comes from them yet, are Sentinels-Cocoon (the universe Visionary comes from), Sentinels-Soviet (the universe ColdWar comes from), or the universes represented on the cover of Darkwatch #18.

The main universe (from our viewpoint). The home of the Freedom Five, Dark Watch, Prime Wardens, Southwest Sentinels, and many others. The primary setting of Sentinels of the Multiverse.

  • Cosmic Omnitron
  • Agent of Gloom Spite
  • Skinwalker Gloomweaver
  • Unstable Kismet (?)
  • Tormented Ally Infinitor

SENTINELS-TWO

A universe where Baron Blade kills Legacy (aka Paul Parsons) and Pauline Parsons takes up his mantle much earlier than expected.

Associated variants:

  • Young Legacy

SENTINELS-IRON

A universe where Baron Blade kills Pauline Parsons and her father becomes a global dictator after killing the villain.

Associated variants:

  • Freedom Six Absolute Zero
  • Freedom Six Bunker
  • Freedom Six Tachyon
  • Freedom Six Tempest
  • Freedom Six Unity
  • Freedom Six Wraith

SENTINELS-XTREME!

A universe where a more "extreme" variant of the Prime Wardens exists.

Associated variants:

  • XTREME Fanatic
  • XTREME Haka
  • XTREME Tempest
  • XTREME Argent Adept
  • XTREME Captain Cosmic

SENTINELS-WASTELAND

A universe where, in the future, earth has become a world overrun by monsters.

Associated variants:

  • Eternal Haka
  • Best of Times Chrono-Ranger (also Sentinels-Prime)

SENTINELS-PUZZLER

A universe where Paul Parsons' chief abilities seem to be puzzle solving and not physical strength.

Associated variants:

  • America's Cleverest Legacy

SENTINELS-MIRROR

A universe where Baron Blade (and perhaps other villains?) are heroes while Legacy (and perhaps other heroes?) are villains.

Associated variants:

  • Ivana Ramonat Luminary

SENTINELS-REVO

A universe where RevoCorp is a group of angel investors and most heroes have fallen - except for Benchmark.

Associated variants:

  • Supply and Demand Benchmark

KINGKILLER

Based on the Kingkiller Chronicles by Patrick Rothfuss. It is unknown if this is the actual universe created by Mr. Rothfuss or an alternate version that mixes super heroics with high fantasy.

Associated variants:
  • Kvothe the Six String: Kingkiller.

SENTINELS-COCOON

A universe where the US Government's Project Cocoon led to the creation of Visionary.

Associated variants: NONE

SENTINELS-SOVIET

A universe that is home to ColdWar and possibly other Soviet versions of familiar heroes.

Associated variants: NONE

UNKNOWN

Guise variants, given their whimsical and metatextual nature, may exist in universes other than Sentinels-Prime.

Associated variants

  • Santa Guise: Who the heck knows?
  • Completionist Guise: Who the heck knows?
  • Action Hero Stuntman: Possibly from Sentinels-Mirror.
