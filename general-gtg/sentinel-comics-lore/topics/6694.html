Now then. I was watching Mulan last night, and this song came into my head just now. I got this image of Tachyon running to the line of "You must be swift as a coursing river", and I had this amazing idea: a music video of Ra becoming Horus in a slightly differently-worded version of this song. I know he goes into the desert alone and comes back as Horus, but... this is something I'd love to see.

Picture Tachyon doing running exercises with Ra...

Tempest blasting him with typhoons...

Absolute Zero creating hoarfires while Ra creates a flaming tornado and blasting Legacy, who shrugs it off...

The Wraith and Mister Fixer showing him some of their techniques on a moonlit night...

Picture The Argent Adept playing his instruments in the background...

Picture "the huns" being the Ennead...

Picture Legacy asking for "suns", and Young Legacy and Expatriette showing up instead...

Picture Ra slowly growing out his beard...

I think this should be a thing, and if I was an animator, I would totally do it! If anyone on this forum is an animator, it would be amazing if they could give it a shot... With >G's permission, of course! What does everyone here think?
