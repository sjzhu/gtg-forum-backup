Is anybody else getting more and more put off by the Argent Adept as we learn more about his abilities? He seems to be WAY overpowered as a single combatant with abilities that do WAY too much at once, especially given how supporty, plinky, and self-comboy his deck is.

To me it makes sense since I've always seen the Virtuosos as having strong parallels to being the magical equivalent of the Legacies.  They're both long lines of people who pass down a heritage of extremely powerful abilities used to protect people, just with the Virtuosos passing down knowledge instead of genetics.

So both Legacy and Argent hold back their abilities to leading and supporting their allies, because we've seen they both have the sort of personality who prefers to handle things in less-lethal ways where they can. And much like Iron Legacy was absolutely terrifying when he felt like he had his back put against the wall, so is Argent.

Makes sense to me in a RL comic analogy, too. I feel like the Void reminds me in many ways of the Superflow, and the Starbrand connected to the Superflow is also more or less a "do whatever your mind can dream up" kind of power.

P.S. Also bear in mind that Visionary is connected to the Void and she's also pretty stupidly powerful.

I dunno, this is right up the alley of the Adept. He gets Combo-absurd:

1. Silver Shadow lets you activate an accompany; use Alacritous Subdominant to trigger Xu's Bell for +1 Damage, then get another power use off your second Subdominant. 

2. Your second power use goes to the Lyre, which blows up Xu's Bell to kill a Gladiator and then hits Gladiator #2 for 2 damage.

3. You still have a card play. Play Silver Shadow. Hit Gladiator #2 for 2 damage. Then play Polyphoric Flare. Deal yourself 3 damage, and then use that power use to trigger the Harp, killing Gladiation #3 and hitting Gladiator #2 for another 2 damage (total 6, possibly killing them).

4. You still have a power use. Use it on the Pipes to trigger both your Melodies of Frost and Flame, hitting one gladiator for 8 damage and killing them. 

That's four gladiators out in one go.
