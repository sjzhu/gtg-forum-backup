TakeWalker:

I have to assume the relic being asked about is the Atlantean Conduit?

I think that what’s happened there is that the letter-writer (I have already forgotten who it was, sorry) has confused two things as being related that aren’t.

First, there are the Atlantean Inheritors, whoever they may be and whatever they may represent. These are the people who have inherited something from the original twelve Atlantean masters, which apparently you can do without knowing what you are.

Secondly, there are the Atlantean relics that were used to contain xxtz’Hulissh during the fall of Atlantis. During the xxtz’Hulissh episode, it was said that the masters created twelve such relics, enough of which have been broken, damaged, or misused to weaken the barriers and bring xxtz’Hulissh back.

I believe that the letter-writer thought that because twelve masters created those twelve relics, there was a specific link between them, and the owners of those relics were the current twelve Atlantean Masters. This confused C&A, because there are more than twelve total Atlantean relics, not every relic of Atlantis is one of the keys holding xxtz’Hulissh, and xxtz’Hulissh wasn’t mentioned in the letter.
