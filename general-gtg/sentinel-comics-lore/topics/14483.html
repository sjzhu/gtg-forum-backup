Otherwise known as my theory that Expartiette is, in fact, a member of the Parsons clan.

Here is what I believe we know:

1. Citizen Dawn, Expat's mom, has an almost religious belief that superhumans are superior to non-powered humans.

2. The first child born to the Parsons family in each generation has the powers of the previous generation plus one new power. Additional children born into the family do not seem to have powers, though I'm not certain if any Legacy has actually has a known sibling.

3. Expatriette was born without powers. This did not please her mom.

4. It has been stated that we have already "met" Expat's dad.

My theory: Citizen Dawn decided to procreate at some point. Considering her beliefs, it isn't difficult to imagine her subscribing to a eugenics viewpoint of creating children. What better father for her child than a Legacy, the second most powerful (from her point of view) superhuman on the planet? Legacy's genetics plus Dawn's genetics should create the perfect superhuman child.

I'm not suggesting that any Legacy cheated on his spouse to father a child. Instead, I believe Dawn got hold of genetic material and used that, plus a bit of comic book super science, to impregnate herself. Given that Expat was born powerless, it stands to reason that she would be the second child of her generation and not the first.

Given that Pauline, the future Legacy, is only 18 around the start of Sentinels I think it is reasonable to assume that Expatriette was created using Greatest Generation Legacy's DNA and not the current Legacy's. In other words, I believe Expatriette is Paul Parsons' little sister and Pauline's aunt.
