What an awesome card!

I know you have been speculating about it (Chaosmancer linked to Omnitron new bio); but I still wanted to analyze carefully this beatiful, mysterious card. So if you allow me, I will highlight the writings on his wall (writings in red! Like the Joker in Batman: Arkham Asylum) and then we can play a little bit with its significance...

This is what appears clearly in the wall:

Top, from left to right: NO WAY OUT-- IT SENDS ITS SERVANT-- THE GUARDS KNOW

Left, top to botton: ALL WILL END AS ONE -- NO WAY OUT -- THE FUTURE IS GONE -- NO WAY OUT -- a strange symbol of 4 lines crossing with each other-- NO WAY OUT -- a bunch of marks, a circle, some lines forming a king of triangle.

Right, top to botton: CONCENTRIC WORLDS -- marks of the days passing -- and the figure of the Time crazed prisoner, in the bed, hiding in the shadows and looking at us.

Center of the card, top-right: THE CHILD IS THE CENTER -- and a a concentric line linked to the sentence before by a second line, as its origin; this concentric line does not close but  gets bigger and bigger, with more lines crossing it at different points  -- and at the end, the eyes/infinity symbol, with the sentence CAN'T FEEL MY EYES.

Center of the card, top left and botton of the card: the window, pointed at by an arrow (night, stars, and a pink light crossing the galaxy) an below the arrow, THIS WAY -- more NO WAY OUT -- a hand of blood in the wall -- a drawing with a planet in the center and more concentric lines , with more planets that have been crossed out, all but the one in the center; on the left of this, EARTH WILL FALL -- below the planet and the concentric lines, we can see two things: another arrow pointing at Earth, and a figure (cut in half) of a human in a circle fro which more arrows emerge;  and in the left, a line stright from Earth to the NO NO NO NO NO NO NO.

Also, the text of the card says this: "NO WAY OUT.. THE LINES POINT TO IT! IT'S ALL THE SAME! IT ALL COUNTS! NONE OF IT COUNTS! IT WILL BE HERE SOON!"
