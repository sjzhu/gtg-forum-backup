The Train Man is in Matrix Revolutions.

Military Industrial Complex guy is very ripped from the headlines of my life, and I can’t say much of what I would like to about him, because this forum isn’t the place for controversial political takes. It took a while for me to figure that out.

But, without saying literally anything about the real world, I strongly feel that comic books are better served by just having villains who do bad things because they’re evil and therefore the heroes should punch them and shoot them and send them to jail. That’s the tone that I think is most likely to make an entertaining story. Don’t get preachy, dont try to reflect reality, don’t teach lessons to the next generation…just be clearly labeled as escapist fiction, and give the reader a fun story to enjoy in exchange for their several dollars, so they can feel good for a few minutes out of their difficult working and family lives.
