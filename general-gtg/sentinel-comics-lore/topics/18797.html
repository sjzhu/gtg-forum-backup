I just developed a headcanon for why no one ever saw Haka in the Inversiverse.

Instead of being inverted to be "super-edgy-evil", that universe's Haka is just really apathetic about the goings-on in the world. Everything flits by so fast! People live like mayflies. What even is the point of saving them, or of conquering them? Either way, you're just going to have to do it all again in fifty years. 

In my mind, Haka in this universe just lives in a cabin in the middle of nowhere, and occasionally he turns on the TV and grunts and says, "Same old thing" and turns it off again, and then wanders outside to fight a bear.
