With the impending doom that is Oblivaeon nearing our doorstep, with Prime War in the works, and the RPG a little (?) ways a way, I wanted to create a thread, and also a pretty flowchart for the wiki, about the timelines in the Sentinels universe.

This thread does exist, but I'm hoping to keep it less detailed. https://greaterthangames.com/forum/topic/chronology-2819. 

I just wanted to keep this simple, I personally don't need to know if "CHARACTER" got coffee first, then ate her bagel in the Annual #2, or did she get a bagel only, because the coffee machine was possessed at the time?". I'd also like to keep it to major events, such as character deaths (and if they led to a new future), character appearance, teams formed. But I'd like to stick this to timelines, universes, and the ilk. 

NOTES

- This will contain NO spoilers for playtesting notes, cards, or playtesting forum discussions. Unless it is public information, it can't be here.

- The goal of this is to have one nice place to clarify it up, discuss theories, and to help newcomers who were only introduced into the game recently.

- If we can get this on the WIKi then AWESOME. I will be willing to create the picture/flowchart/document, and pass it on to the wiki's gatekeepers.

The following points were added after Foote's comments

- Applicable to playable Environments/Heroes/Villains (variants included if applicable)

- Non-dystopian futures as well

- Must have enough background information to warrant attention. So, a timeline briefly mentioned in the flavour text may not count. 

- The point is to help ease new and current players into getting a basic understanding.

OVERVIEW

Based on Post #3 from this discussion https://greaterthangames.com/forum/topic/gen-con-and-the-rpg-9225 I understood the following

- When/If (?) OblivAeon is defeated the timelines stabilize into 2 distinct universes. One turns into the Tactics timeline (after 5 years) and the other becomes the RPG, after only a short while. Apparently time travel and hopping between the lines is no longer possible.

- The Sentinels Card game has two other alternate futures. The first is "The Final Wasteland" and the second is "Iron Legacy's Rule". 

- After Oblivaeon, two timelines appeared

1) Tactics

2) RPG

FINAL THOUGHTS

Is this correct? Am I missing anything?

To be clear, the SotM card games has way more futures than Final Wasteland and Iron Legacy paths. First consider that, in an abstract sense, the multiverse contains infinity timelines where every possibility that can happen will happen, though I expect that this is not within the scope of what you would like to discuss.

For more tangable "dystopian" futures, if we are casting a wide net on the term, consider the timeline Benchmark's Supply and Demand varient is from. A future where he is the only hero left alive would be on par with Wasteland or IL timelines in terms of "oh crap-ness". And if we are counting Iron Legacy as a dystopian future, might we also consider the timeline that the alternate Luminary is from where Legacy is the bad guy and the female version of Baron is the hero? Something to consider. What about Vanessa Long's original timeline where she is from? Would that count since it was so bad there she had to resort to timetravel?

As a side note, do you want to log all of the possible alternate timelines that we could possibly know about? Or just the ones that turn out really really bad?

Christopher clarified that Visionary's future timeline is not the same as the Iron Legacy line (and presumably also not the Final Wasteland line). My understanding was that there are many possible branching futures, and every time you travel back in time you end up going down a different path.

 

There are ultimately a LOT of different worlds that showed up in the OblivAeon puzzles and Kickstarter. For instance, the alternate Benchmark (Supply & Demand) comes from a completely different world than any we know, where he is the only hero. His foil incap art is worth studying.
