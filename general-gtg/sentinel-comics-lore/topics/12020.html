Because I love speculation so much, I figured I'd start a thread for everyone to post ideas about the unreleased details of the new >G products. PTing hasn't started yet, but I gotta get my thoughts and ideas down before I am not allowed to anymore. So, lets get started.

VotM:

Sgt. Steel's name got dropped early. This is possibly the FILTER agent. He seems the most likely candidate to me for using a military title.

We have 1 villain mini expansion. And I think that will be The Host (or a character closely related to it)

We have 7 promos coming, but it doesntly say they are all hero promos. I think there will be at least one villain promo. I think we will see Apostate as one of our promos. An Akash or Dawn promo are high on my list.

We will see hero promos as well. Naturalist, Scholar, Chrono, Sentinels, Parse, and Omnitron-X are the most likely to see promos here.

Edit: I forgot about Environments! Ok...I think we might see the Citidel of the Sun as an Environment. It fits with the theme as a villain centered hideaway. The other one? It might be the place that Prime Warden Fanatic is standing in front of which I speculated as the Hosts HQ. But these possibilities are so open. They literally could be anything and we have few hints to go on.

Broken City:

Exemplar is the former Chairman Pike who has been in hiding since the Organization was taken down by the Dark Watch. 

Pike is up to his old tricks using geneticly modified goons as his lackies. Pike had a hand in another famous villain who was experimented on with his Chemicals. Spite was subject to many test batches of formulas that altered him. And theres more than a few people who think Spite is on the cover of Broken City. I think they are close. I think Pike has perfected some of his older formulas! Exemplar has 5 new lackies, Demon Fist, Grudge, MindPhyre, Karnal, and Cyst. 

Mind Phyre drew the first flag. Thats the exact codename of one of Spite's drugs. 

Demon Fist sounds awfully close to "Demon's Kiss", another one of Spites drugs. Demons Kiss shows a picture of Spite's first engulfed in a red flame. To me, that is very similar to the purple flame around the guys hands on the box cover. 

The other 3 are a bit more of a stretch, but follow me here.

Karnal. Now the word "carnal" means "relating to physical, especially sexual, needs and activities". You could say Spite had a carnal need to kill. It's a little interesting that PL602 compound drug shows a picture of Spite's chest, notably the side where his heart is. Coincidence?

Grudge. PL531 Compound not only increases Spite's damge, but depicts his flexing arm. If a Grudge is a persistant feeling of Ill will, what better way to portray that than with such an intimidating pose? It kinda works.

Cyst. Now, a cyst is a closed sac, having a distinct membrane and division from the rest of nearby tissue. On PL626 Compound XI, I think we clearly see what could be very much considered "cysts", those bulging pockets of red tissue on his monsterous left arm.

Maybe seperatly these are not convincing, but together they form a pretty interesting picture. Pike has perfected and isolated the different chemicals that Spite was injected with and given one to each of his new test subjects. 

 

As for other characters, I think we might see Naturalist and the Sentinels fill out the hero roster as I could see them being good fits around the theme (and I contend the Prime Wardens will be introduced in their own core set)

For Profit:

We have a clear look at our Villains, but the 2 heros to be included have yet to be revealed. I have a strong feeling Chrono-Ranger might fit well here being a bounty hunter. I think the Scholar will round out the 2nd spot. Why? Because For Profit stole a luxuray Yacht, and I think Scholar wants his boat back!

 

 

Ok guys, those are my guesses. Lets hear yours!
