For anyone who doesn't know, this character is obviously meant as a reference to the Mirror Master, one of the Rogues who persistently oppose The Flash in DC comics.  Since Tachyon is a Flash homage, "Mirror Mistress" was clearly included in the art of one of her cards as a reference...I didn't expect to see her again.  But perhaps her inclusion in the SOTM storyline will be something cheaty, like how Treacherous Ape (a clear reference to Gorilla Grodd) is pictured on one of Tacky's cards, but appears as a Projection in Dreamer's deck...where Tacky recognizes him, so presumably this is not his first appearance and she knows that he's out of place in the dreamscape.  Thusly, while there was presumably a "real" Treacherous Ape, his only representation in the card game (aside from art) is actually an illusionary duplicate of him, not the real him.  And Mirror Master is also big on creating illusionary duplicates....
