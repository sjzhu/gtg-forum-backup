LonelyMan:

Killing Unlimited Deplorable Zeroes Underground

There was an official acronym in the second episode that I didn’t catch, but I did think of an improvement on this wrong version even if it’s still wrong. I was thinking KUDZU was an Underground interested in Killing Unlimited people, and referred to those people as Deplorable Zeroes, meaning they’re so worthless that you can kill and kill and kill them forever, but you still haven’t done anything. That’s not bad, but I thought of a better one. It’s Killers United Defeating Zoological Undermenschen. You’ve got your German in there because obviously, but you’ve also got the idea that because they splice themselves with plant DNA, they think they’re not animals anymore, and thus they refer to everyone else as Zoologicals or “Zo-ons” for short.

You could further build this out in the Metaverse by saying that post-OblivAeon, when we know Shear Force is in the Xtremeverse, several people who are Citizens of the Sun but didn’t really seem Xtreme enough for Xtreme Citizen Dawn, they’re now retconned as the same equivalent people instead ending up as KUDZU members, because the two groups have a similar “people who aren’t us are garbage” attitude. KUDZU doesn’t reject everyone who lacks powers, if they’re willing to be given powers by KUDZU, but anyone who refuses an offer of membership dies, and not everyone gets an offer. It’s kind of like CotS mixed with RevoCorp, if RevoCorp didn’t care about money and was just openly building an army (of Plague Rats with Desert Eagles and Revolting Revenant Rays). The Citizens meanwhile are more willing to accept people who approach them and already have powers, but less likely to seek out powerless people and give them The Process.
