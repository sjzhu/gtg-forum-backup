The contents of that one early container cover every Kickstarter backer, regardless of where they live in the world. As soon as it arrives at our warehouse, Jodie begins stacking the boxes into pallets so she can forklift them around. Paul and I grab armfuls of boxes right away and begin packing them (we're already creating empty boxes for shipping, organizing invoices, putting art prints into easily boxable envelopes, and arranging the shipping area for maximum efficiency). While Jodie finishes unloading the cargo container, Paul and I pack up all of the domestic shipments, piling them on pallets for FedEx to come pick up once everything is done. Then, we pack up pallets of games to be shipped overseas, most via the "ship an entire pallet to another country, where they break it down and ship it out to individuals" method.

This entire process will most likely take place over several days and nights, and probably over a weekend. Once EVERYTHING listed above is packed, it will all get shipped out simultaneously. So, yes, people in the US will get their boxes first, only because their boxes have a shorter distance to travel. 

Then, we pass out.
