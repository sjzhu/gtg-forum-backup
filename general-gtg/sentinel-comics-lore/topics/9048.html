I suspect that it is difficult to find an official quote about there being "exactly two expansions after Vengance" because that is just a paraphrasing of some things we have implied and not an absolute truth (because it depends on what you mean by "expansion"). Things that are true:

1) There is a "regular" expansion after Vengeance.

2) There is a "final" expansion to the card game story arc.

3) There are a few mini-expansions, accessories, and special expansion-like things that we have planned out and are super excited to publish, market willing.
