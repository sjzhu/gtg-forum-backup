The kickstarter revealed that, in the end, if Big O wins, there will be no more timelines at all - but if the heroes vanquish him, there will be only ONE timeline.

The consequences for the Sentinels (our heroes - and the game) are enormous.

The Iron Legacy timeline disappears. No Freedom 6, No Iron Legacy (so, once the story finishes - we can't play them anymore ! )

The Final Wasteland Timeline may well disappear too. It explains the Renegade… And, by the way : Eternal Haka is no more.

The Tactics bio for Visionary tells us she can't access the timelines anymore. Does it also mean, above the fact that her own timeline is "lost", that time travel is now impossible ? It could contribute to explain Renegade, again…

What about La Capitan ? She is all about time travelling and exploring timelines - does the "joining" of the multiverse destroy her ? Change her ? Force her to find a new job ?

Miss Information's origins are in another timeline - and her new reality altering powers are probaly linked to Timelines manipulations. What may happen to her now ?

And, yes, no more WWII heroes either.

What do you think ? What other aspects of the sentinels comics world, as we know it, depended on alternate timelines - and will now be impossible ?

About the game itself, I understand now why, after OblivAeon, many of the game content we have is "impossible" - after all, most of our fights are "alternate" versions, easily explained by alternate timelines, and they don't exist anymore. "Sentinels of the Multiverse" had to be a "closed" game.

So, we're talking about thematically appropriate gameplay.

Well, we know Ra is dead, so he's out.

Other decks would be unavailable as well. The Sentinels are out, as they've been upgraded to Void Guard. The Matriarch wouldn't be available as she's now The Harpy (a pit stop on her journey to become Pinion). Also a bunch of Environments (Final Wasteland, Silver Gulch, Time Cataclysm, Tomb of Anubis, ...)

Baron Blade would still be around as a villain, but his existing card and promo are all older plots, so thematically he'd need a new promo (or even deck) to reflect his post-OblivAeon, post-Luminary status as a new-and-improved bad guy.

The time-travelling characters can still exist. The Multiverse which contained their alternate universes or alternate timelines still exists, it's just that each universe is cut off from all the rest. So Omnitron is fine (but only with his Omnitron-U promo card). La Cap as a villain is probably out, but La Comodora has tech to protect her so the severing of the multiverse could easily have left her alive, but stranded in the Prime Universe. Chrono-Ranger is fine, he's just not in the universe where Con and the future he saw exists. It's possible he's also not in the unniverse where his past exists. But those universes are still out there so he owuld still exist. Miss Information probably just lost most if not all of her power.

A bunch of Promo cards would be defunct/not available in this just-after-OblivAeon universe:
Freedom 6
Eternal Haka
WWII Legacy & Bunker
Xtreme Prime Wardens (unless they got trapped in the Prime Universe)

Other promos are fine. Sky-Scraper could probably use her normal cards or her new promo (the tech that allows the new promo is from Luminary (BB version) but that doen't mean she gets rid of it when the threat is gone). Prime Warden and Dark Watch promos are fine except...

Captain Cosmic would (like Omnitron) be playable ONLY with his new promo. Likewise, Luminary would probably only be available with the Ivana promo (again assuming she got trapped in the Prime Universe) as Baron Blade would almost certainly revert to his evil ways as soon as someone else stopped treatening to destroy the world that he considers his to destroy.

and probably a hundred or so other changes that I haven't even begun to think about yet...
