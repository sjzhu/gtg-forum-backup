“We’re in rare form today” is what they should have said at the beginning of the episode.

Also, Adam is a gas, not because he’s a hilarious card of a person who’s fun at parties, but in the sense that he expands to fill all space available. So he feels very big in relation to the cosmic universe, since he’s ever spreading and growing more rarefied, until his extremely diffuse cloud is spread across every observable galaxy to infinity and beyond.

By contrast, the various Christophers are very dense, all simultaneously occupying the same single coordinate point.

The Thorathians are being referred to throughout this episode as Nazi allegories, but earlier episodes said that the early comics mostly used Thories and other aliens as Communist allegories. I’m not sure how all this lines up with GWV, but I’d be amused if there were two distinct administrations running the Thory military, and one was more Nazi-ish, another more Soviet-y, and a third that was GWV’s forcible unification of the two previous factions.
