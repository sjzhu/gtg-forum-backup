So, I was browsing through the bios and other such things from the video game. I was hoping to find new information in the Xtreme Prime Wardens bios, and I found a little bit I don't remember but not the type of things I wanted to learn.

 

So, first off, it seems from the Xtreme bios, which we may have heard before, the Prime Wardens of the Multiverse Timeline make there base in Atlantis. We know that the Ennead end up in Atlantis during Tactics, and I don't think we know who ends up there during the RPG but it is fair to consider it unoccupied at the moment I think.

 

The Temple of Zhu Long has an interesting line I don't know if we've ever discussed before. " The Ancient Purveyor of Life and Death holds the keys to certain doors which shoudl remain closed." I wonder if this is in some way related to similiar phenomenon like Anubis and Ammit. We know they hold on "Entrace to the Underworld" but also that they are not the sole Underworld. Could Zhu Long be another guardian(?) type figure like Anubis who helps keep the world balanced and free from horrific things which would destroy civilization and all that? He's clearly a dangerous and selfish individual, but that doesn't mean he wants to see the world burn, and it could help explain who he discovered the secrets to reanimanting corpses and binding souls.

 

A quick glance through other stuff hasn't revealed anything else that we haven't discussed, but it is the "Fall of the Variants" in the video game, so it is entirely possible that we'll get more information in the comng weeks for me to add to this thread.

 

Or I can post reminder things, like Tempest's sword, found in Atlantis, most likely being a Artifact. I'd forgotten that tidbit.

Getting back to bios a little, to go with the height comparisons in another thread, seeing where all of the Americans are from in the US is kind of fun, too.

 AZ is from Texas, for one, so there's a Texan accent coming out of that suit. Blake Washington is from Joisey. Setback and Miss Information are both from Missouri. Chokepoint, Friction, and Citizen Hammer are Californians along with Argent. Sergeant Steel is from NYC. (Which makes it kind of interesting that both Megalopolis and NYC are a thing.)

Hair and eye color is sometimes entertaining, too. Unity is the sole person with grey eyes. Hazel seems to be the second least common "normal" eye color: Argent Adept, Citizen Dawn, Miss Information, and Proletariat.

Or occupations, like how Captain Cosmic was a barrister and Infinitor was a constable.
