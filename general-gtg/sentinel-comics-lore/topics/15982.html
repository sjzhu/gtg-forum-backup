Tier 1: the big brains
  • Wager Master (immortal master of games)

  • Baron Blade (mad genius, probably right at Tachyon's level or above)

  • Unity (natural inventor)
  • Writhe (inventor)

  • Ra (a wonder in his field)
Tier 3: gifted and talented
  • Operative (intelligent enough to get the Chairman's attention)
  • Friction (one of Tachyon's interns, but whose inventions don't actually work)
  • Haka (undying mystic, teacher; very well read) [edit: adjusted as per Foote's comment]

  • Set (Ennead mystic)
  • Dr. Medico (med school)
  • Captain Cosmic (law school)
  • Visionary (psychic training, high academic test scores)
  • Dreamer (same intelligence as Visionary, really)
  • Ambuscade (gadget based villain, but not an inventor)
  • Scholar (lots of folk wisdom and mystic knowledge)
  • Chokepoint (talks to machine, seems to have some sort of mind-expanding experience)
Tier 4: bright
  • Blood Countess Bathory (old and probably well-read)
  • Sky-Scraper (freedom fighter and saboteur)
  • Legacy (natural leader and tactician)
  • Iron Legacy (same as Legacy, really)
  • Nuit (guardian spirit of the Ennead)
  • La Capitan (smart enough to navigate a time ship)
  • Citizen Dawn (natural leader; careful planning)
  • Miss Information (administrative assistant with in depth knowledge of the heroes)
  • Absolute Zero (college graduate turned janitor)
  • Benchmark (no idea yet, but if his head is partly computer, he probably has brain enhancements)
  • Young Legacy (expert tutelage)
  • Kaargra Warfang (no real idea how smart she is, but she does run a colloseum, so probably not dumb)
  • Nephthys (healer and destoyer)
Tier 5: maybe not book smart, but good at what they do
  • K.N.Y.F.E. (highly trained agent)
  • Mr. Fixer (skilled mechanic)
  • Chrono-Ranger (old west sherrif)
  • Greazer Clutch ("above average" alien bounty hunter)
  • Expatriette (cunning tactition)
  • Sergeant Steel (dangerous F.I.L.T.E.R. agent)
  • Proletariat (exemplary soldier)
  • Fright Train (skilled soldier)
  • Tefnut (warrior of the Ennead)
  • Kismet (thief; likes playing mind games)
  • Infinitor (was a police officer before going mad from cosmic energy)
  • Apostate (Fanatic's dark mirrior; seems slightly more devious)
  • Fanatic (stubborn and pious)
  • Citizen Hammer (seems cocky and brash, but is based on a wizard archetype)
  • Citizen Anvil (careful and elusive, but based on a paladin archetype)
  • Argent Adept (musician and bartender)
  • Matriarch (goth poet)
  • Idealist (middle schooler with psychic powers and a wild imagination)
Tier 6: average or low intelligence
  • Mainstay ("meat-head football player," but smart enough to finish college)
  • Guise (Guise)
  • Setback (lovable loser)
  • Geb (known for raw destructive power)
  • Bugbear (hired muscle turned primitive monster)
  • Spite (drug addled serial killer)
  • Progeny (alien force of nature that never speaks)
  • Akash'Bhuta (raw force of nature, none of her cards quote her actually saying anything)
  • Plague Rat (somewhat above animal intelligence)
     
    What do you guys think?

    I get using the Wraith as a basis for comparison, and she is wicked smart, but I'm not sure how smart we should be treating her.

     

    We've little real information to go on, she graduates from everything ealry, getting a doctorate in Mechanical Egineering by 22. This is incredibly impressive, but not something I would be shocked to hear on the Evening News. She comes across to me (to not use the Batman or other comic book people as an example) as a mix between a decently skilled hacker and Macgyver. Very smart, capable of thinking on her feet, but is she always the smartest person in the room?

     

    As individuals the Wraith would combat, and being signifigantly older and villainous, I feel like the Chairman and Zhu Long have to both be smarter than the Wraith. She can still figure out their schemes, but they would be able to pull the wool over her occassionally. They aren't threatening as master-mind type villains if they aren't capable of getting one over on her.

     

    Edit: And also, The Chairman has to go to Zhu Long and give his right hand lady to him for a period of time. I'd say Zhu Long is easily a bigger brain than Pike, as Pike is perfectly willing to play ball with the old dragon instead of trying to double cross him. I wouldn't be shocked if the Chairman had a bit of a fear of Zhu Long, not being able to figure out the wizards plans or motivations, but knowing that he is not someone he wants to tangle with unless he's got no other choice.

     

    End Edit

     

    As for Nightmist, different areas of expertise obviously apply here. The Wraith uses Science and Technology, Nightmist uses magic. However, we've got a massive plus that shoots Nightmist up the charts in my mind, and that is "The Gate". While as an excerise of pure power it is utterly insane to open a gate to every dimension simultaneously, the mental effort of crafting such a spell and executing it had to be immense as well. I'm going to go out on a limb and say that Nightmist wasn't just casting a spell she found, she was actively altering the magic to create something no one had ever done before. While I have no idea how Sentinels magic works, one general rule of thumb  for teleportation/portal magic is the ability to picture where you are going, otherwise you play roullette with the universe. She was able to open a portal to everywhere at the same time, it is probably her most impressive feat ever, and was likely enhanced with magical drugs beyond the cupid potion we see in the ARG story bit, but still would have required someone truly impressive on the mental scale to actually pull off.

     

    So, maybe not top tier for either of them, but definetly above Writhe (who while an inventor was a failed inventor until the shadow tech actually worked... and he decided robbing a bank was the best possible test for it. Void Guard Writhe is probably way smarter, but base Writhe I wouldn't put so high up in the rankings, smart but clueless)
