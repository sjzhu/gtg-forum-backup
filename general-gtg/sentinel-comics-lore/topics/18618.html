I enjoyed the story part, but there was one bit of language usage that grated on me the entire time. "Prolate" is an adjective, and so the portal fiends' eyes can't be described as simply "prolates."  the missing word for what they wanted was "spheroid" - that is, the phrase "prolate spheroid."   For those who care, the variety that's squished in the other direction (flattened at the poles and bulging at the equator, like the Earth) is called an "oblate spheroid."

Although deciding to call himself a luminary is certainly very consistent with his character.  Sure, he's always been Baron Blade, the hero of Mordengrad, but now he also gets to be Luminary, the guiding light who helps lead the heroes to victory against Oblivion.  He understands the nature of the threat, he understands what must be done, and he's the one best able to make sure that happens, if only the heroes would be wise enough to listen to his advice.  As far as I can tell, Blade has always considered himself to be the smartest person in the room, and choosing the name Luminary is definitely consistent with that.
