Okay, so over on this thread, I was inspired for the idea for this thread:

We've got a bunch of people here on the forums who like making stuff and we do all love Sentinels, right? Right! 

Also, who doesn't love getting stuff? No one, that's who!

So here is the proposal: We do a Sentinels craft swap. I'd even volunteer to do the organisationals. 

Crafty forumites get partnered up and make each other a Sentinels themed thing. Then they send that thing to their partner, and once they've got it, they put a picture of it on here and we all get to admire that thing (and be crazy jealous, because that always happens). 

Anything is a thing: scarf, pencilcase, doll, ornament, notebook, somthing useful for the game, jewlery, cookies (okay, that might be a mess), a funny hat, a fancy token box, a t-shirt, fridge magnets, artwork, woodwork, metalwork, yarnwork, fimowork, 'turning-one-thing-into-another-thing' work... 

What I want to know now is:

Who might want to join this swap?

So, to recap (before this thread goes off the tracks and derails into another train pun one), the following people are up for this swap, although they may not yet know what to make (which is totally okay!):

Julia,  BlueHairedMeerkat, jagarciao, Silverleaf, Under3, Reckless, Imprimi5

So it looks like we have enough interested people to do this, which is really cool!

So, further suggestions:

Schedule: I am thinking of starting a proper swap thread for this first ever sentinels craft swap on the 25th of May. Everybody who is willing to go through with this can register on there and I’ll do a blank questionnaire thingy (Favourite hero/Villain/environment/card, what you can produce craft wise, what item you might want to have, any crafts you are allergic to and maybe what continent you are on). After a week we partner people up and get crafting. Everybody sends their stuff of when they are ready (but check in on the forum first, in case someone’s partner flakes- man, I do not want that to happen) or on the 30th of June at the latest. Does that sound like a plan (a good one, I mean?)

Late swappers: If there is anybody who really wants to join in on the swap but can't craft in June due to life getting in the way, I think we can accommodate those people as long we can find them a partner who is up for a later swap (say, over the month of July). Also, if this does not wind up to be a frustrating mess for everybody, we can totally do another swap later in the year (in case someone needs a Fanatic Christmas Tree topper or something equally odd shudder).

Size: Are we going to put some constraints on the dimensions or complexity that a crafted item can have? For instance, we could say  each item has to fit into a particular parcel size, or we could say that no item should take more of two days of active crafting time. I am flexible on that, I just know that this is often done in swaps so nobody feels like they got the short end of the stick.

Opinions? Suggestions? More puns?
