The classic of course is Enid Blyton. Her children are continually hungry despite gorging themselves constantly and the food consumed is often listed despite it having nothing to do with the story.

It's one of my pet hates in writing, to be honest - those utterly unimportant but lovingly described details. I think that if a description of anything doesn't help define a character, or strengthen a world/setting, or progress the plot, it should be ruthlessly edited out.

But yeah, I reckon the majority of Sentinels heroes would end up in Gryffindor because that's where the people who aren't evil, pathetic, or clever-to-the-exclusion-of-everything-else live. Few of the characters from non-Gryffindor houses are allowed to be anything other than stereotypes of their houses.
