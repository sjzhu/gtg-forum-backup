I just skimmed through this one, and I'm sure this has been said, but I want to type it anyway.

 

Wraith was practically reluctant to join the Freedom Four, preferring to work on her own. However, as the team grew into the Five & Friends we love today, she too grew to love and respect the team as her family. Having such a...well, romantic change of heart puts the Freedom Five on a pretty pedestal for Wraith. If that is shattered, then she would be left with her necessary but false life (Maia Montgomery) and patrolling the streets of her crime-ridden city, just like old times, without the togetherness and all that. Thus, after the whole Death of Legacy run (Young, leading to Iron Legacy), Iron Legacy, the father figure in this super family, is breaking apart the very thing Wraith has learned to hold very dear and very sacred. So, the Price of Freedom isn't for freedom in the sense of no limits, but Freedom as in the Freedom Five -- the family. The price she has to pay to keep that together is brutality, and potentially murdering her 'father' to keep the family going, which also happens to be the exact opposite of what that Freedom meant before. But, all is necessary, just for the glimmer of that same Freedom.

 

And here is where we'd have some triumphant national anthem, or something.
