Hello! Medium-term enthusiast, first-time poster. Good community here!

We were fighting Iron Legacy for the first time- Team Leader Tachyon, Argent Adept, Omnitron X, and... Young Legacy. A father-daughter showdown for the ages!

We could tell he was tough -- Tachyon got pounded for 15 or so in the first round -- but we got lucky. OX and AA took care of his ongoing, and the young Parsons got in Takedown after Takedown (with some help from that AA card that heals everybody and brings back cards from the graveyard with the goofy name).

Finally, with her (alternate dimension? just plain bonkers?) dad down to his last hitpoints, Young Legacy turned her atomic gaze on Iron Legacy and declared,

"Pauline is the one who does the killing around here, and I have killed YOU! I killed you, DAD!"
