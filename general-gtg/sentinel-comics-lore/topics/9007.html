The more observant amongst you will have noticed Ronway's recent shiny (and let's be honest, crappy ;)) new avatars recently. I may have followed suit with my own avatar but of course I can't hope to compete with his artistic talents.

As the title suggests, these masterpieces of rubbish digital fan art were created in MS Paint in less than 300 seconds, with very little care and attention or technique or drawing ability. I love them!

And we also have Bluehairedmeerkat's excellent Ennead effort - 9 characters in 75x75px. I don't know how long that one took so it may not be strictly eligible for Paint>5mins status, but it certainly captures the flavour of this medium. [EDIT: I take it back, after a better look on a screen that's a decent size. That picture is far too good to be a P>5min. Far. Too. Good.]

So it's my birthday on Wednesday. And what I want as a present is... more pictures! And this is my challenge to you, oh gracious forum dwellers. Your prize is the knowledge that you made me and your fellow forumites smile. :)

The Rules

  • Draw something from the Sentiverse - any character, object, building, whatever. Doesn't have to be a copy of official art, be imaginative if you want.
  • Use MS Paint/similar to do the drawing. Alternative programs are acceptable as long as they are equally as basic, with no fancy stuff. What the hell, if you can do a Paint-style drawing in Illustrator then go right ahead.
  • No filters, special brushes, layers, or other poncy clever tools. We're talking the standard pen/brush, and maybe the spraycan if you're doing glowy light or something.
  • 5 minutes ONLY. This is very important. Less is probably better. My original drawing of Citizen Dawn took considerably longer than that and entirely lacked the charm of Ronway's Dawn (which I regrettably saved over in my idiocy).
  • I can't stress this enough: don't try to make it good. Recognisible, yes. Good, no. Crappy is what we're going for here because crappy is amusing.
  • Make it Safe For Work please. Personally I'd be totally happy to see a hot Tempest/Legacy/Mongolian Death Worm session in all its lurid glory, but let's not get anyone sacked or arrested or in trouble with their significant other or parents or whatever.

 

Do me proud, guys.
