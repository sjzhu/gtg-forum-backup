Ah, so he does.  The "give or take" in his age made me chuckle, but nothing else enlightening.

Looking at the villains, though, sheds a little light.  Looks like Capitan and Kismet are both in their 20s - for Kismet that's about what I guessed, but Capitan surprises me, since I'd thought she would be cast as having spent decades plundering the timestream.  I suppose that age is a relative term for such a person, but still, it at least implies she has less experience that one might think.  Also, we now know the name of La Capitan's ship - La Paradoja Magnifica, "The Paradox Magnificent".  That is stylish, Maria, I'll give you that.

Perhaps more meaningfully, the power sources of some characters aren't what you'd expect: Kismet has "Genetic" as well as Luck and Magic Talisman, implying that her family's ongoing fortune isn't just because of the item they carry, but also due to some innate trait they posess - the dark mirror to Legacy continues.  Likewise, La Capitan doesn't have "Time Travel," like some other characters, but "Time Cataclysm."  To me, this implies that she (like Astro City's Samaritan) has some actual superhuman physical traits due to her trip through the rift, while someone like Chrono-Ranger has only gained technology and experience.

On the hero side of the same coin, listing "Empathy" as one of Omnitron-X's powers is a really nice touch, saying quite succinctly that the new Omnitron is stronger because of what it has gained.  That, or it has Raven-style magical healing, but I tend to assume the former.

Fun stuff, even after most of the bios themselves were spoiled - I like it!

Ironic

P.S. Of course, really, characters bios and even text take a back seat here.  The new bios have shown me my new Favorite Thing In Sentinels - it's the rainbow-haired punk shooting energy blasts in the upper right-hand corner of The Block's splash.  Look at him!  Such a totally absurd yet iconically simple design is a thing of beauty.  What might his name be?  Raver?  Psypunk?  Rainbow Clash?  Whatever his name, he is clearly the most fantastic aspect of Sentinels continuity - I would not complain if he was the enemy in literally every piece of Sentinels art, forever.  He is so totally awesome (nay, totally radical), I instantly recovered from my initial sadness that the blues armored guy getting effortlessly flung aside by Freight Train is not, in fact, Siege-Breaker, better knwon as Evil Gordon Freeman Who Gets Blasted On Polyphoric Flare.  I really wanted it to be him, just in the hopes that all his appearances would be him grabbing someone, and getting pwned - the Bad Hugs supervillain.
