Since it's been well noted so far that Setback's deck is brimming with Spiderman references, It just occured to me that High Risk Behavior is no different. I think it is a nod to the original Sinister Six.

Mirror Girl/Man = Mysterio

Bird Guy = Vulture

Lava Guy = Sandman

Static Shock = Electro

Ambuscade = Kraven the Hunter

Raichu = Doc Oct. (Yeah, this one is by far the biggest stretch. I wanted to say he seems more of a nod to the Lizard, but I dont think he was ever a part of the Sinister Six which would ruin this fun little thought)

It seems to me that Ambuscade put the team together, but toward what ends I'm not sure. But with only two more expansions, I'm having slight difficulty trying to imagine where they all would fit in individually. Could there be any chance that this Sinister Six nod could end up being a Vengence style senario? They could be a part of an environment. I could imagine them being in a RevoCorp deck for instance as henchmen for Revenant. They could be targets in another villains deck as well, but in both cases the idea of Ambuscade ever being a minor target in a deck just doesn't sit well with me on a thematic issue. Maybe Ambuscade will be separated from them wherever they show up in the game, but connected through the overall narrative.

In either case, I want a Venom-Writhe deck.
