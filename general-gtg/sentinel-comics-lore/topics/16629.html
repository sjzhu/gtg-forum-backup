I'm in the process of redesigning the Character Appearances page, and am done with the heroes sections. I thought I'd let everyone know because what I did was each hero tab now has the appearances of characters in their deck and power cards, plus where they appear in every other deck.

 

This will make it easier for all you people with enterprising questions to figure out where, say, Argent Adept appears in every deck, so you can go look at the art and ask questions based on it as you wish.

 

the two pages are linked from the Character Appearances page for now, but here are the direct links

 

http://sentinelswiki.com/index.php?title=Character/A-M

http://sentinelswiki.com/index.php?title=Character/N-Z

 

Just for example, because Unity is coming up soon, here are all the cards and decks that Unity appears on.:

 

  • The Freedom Tower - Caspit's "Playground"
  • Magmaria - Magmarian Throng
  • Ambuscade Team Style Villain - Shrewd Ambush
  • Chokepoint - Empowered Self Repair and Stripped Resources
  • The Ennead - Blast of Flame
  • Friction - Supersonic Streak
  • Fright Train - Off the Rails
  • Greazer Clutch - Groovy Moves
  • La Capitan - Walk the Plank
  • The Operative Team Style Villain- Shinken Strike
  • Baron Blade Tactics - Regression Burst (Promo)
  • Sergeant Steel - Mission Objective
  • Wager Master - A Burning Sense of Failure

 

I haven't got to any of the villain pages  yet, so there is still work to be done, and I did it quick and dirty for now so there are some inconsistencies (ie, the order in which things are organized, just using 'Team Style Villain' and 'Tactics' to deliniate different decks with similiar names, something I want to do in a better way later) but its a good resource for getting more pointed story questions in. Good luck in asking great questions everyone!
