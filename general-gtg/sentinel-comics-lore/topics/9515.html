It's soon shipping day, so let's have some fun.  I don't know if it was ever already mentioned (I tried to search and found nothing).

I recently re-read the flavor texts of the Time Cataclysm cards, and something hit me. In the flavor text for Tendrils of Madness, AZ calls Visionary "Vanessa".

It's nothing special, really - but, when reading it, I thought it was strange for these two to use their first names, especially in a strange situation as this. Could it suggest that they are closer than it appears ? Probably not "lovers", but at least very close friends ? There are other cards in the game where we see both of them together - suggesting that they sometimes "work" as a duo. I should browse them all again and make a list ( first to come to mind is the red dust card in Wagner where they both are, hum, dusting their costume).

They both are "outcasts", in their own way : she is not of this time and reality, he is ridden with guilt.  He can't survive without his suit, she is  slowly dying. He can't control his power without his modules - she can control the powers of others ( reminds me of Jean Grey/Cyclops ). He feels guilty about his past - she risked everything to change hers…

So, at least they seem to be a good "pair" of heroes to team together - if not a secret couple My personal theory is that they feel comfortable near each other, and have a strong friendship, with shades of something more. They are not the romantic kind, so rather than dating, they do heroic things together.

The fact is that I'd like to see comics about this two people team !

Anyway - this little "Vanessa" thing obssessed me for days, I had to let it out
