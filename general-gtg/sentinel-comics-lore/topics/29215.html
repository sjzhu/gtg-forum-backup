Revenant was a downgraded full villain who deserved more story than he got, but most of the other mini nemeses were described as well as they deserved imo. The big exception is Argentium, just because he’s such a cipher.

The discussion of Vanessa Long and/or Visionary fixed points really weirds me out, because there could be several completely unrelated Long families (eg one in England and one in China), and whatever force causes psionic awakening is destined to grant powers to either Vanessa the daughter of Charles Long in London, or Vanessa the daughter of Nezha Vu Long in Hong Kong. If one of them dies, the power granting automatically has to happen to the other. It’s baffling to try and find a sensible explanation for how exactly the fixed pointing works.

I would like to believe that shipping is not as out of control in the metaverse as it is in our meta-meta reality. Without getting too controversial, I think the social forces at play in our reality drive the fanfiction slashfic concept in a way that I think wouldn’t happen in any world that was even slightly different.
