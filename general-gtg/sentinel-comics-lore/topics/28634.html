So apparently they’re not completely done with Vertex, or so they claim at least (me, I’ll believe it when I see it). Which begs the question of what exactly Vertex is from a metaverse perspective. To analogize this to non-metaverse comic book companies (aka “real” comic book companies, although C&A are so close to being actual comics publishers that I would consider disputing that term), is the Vertex-style reboot a reshaping of the Sentinel Comics Universe along the lines of DC’s Flashpoint and New 52 rebranding (or, to go much much earlier, the Crisis on Infinite Earths)? Or is it more of a Marvel Ultimates situation, where they leave the original Sentinel Comics Universe alone for a while, but launch a totally new line in its place? (The difference in this analogy being that Ultimates didn’t replace the standard Marvel continuity, it existed alongside it. But I don’t think that entirely kills the analogy, since we don’t know 100% of everything Sentinel Comics is doing in the metaverse, we just know that they publish a bunch of Vertex titles after several years of the Sentinel Comics Universe. Even if they aren’t actively publishing their own SCU comics anymore, they could sort of put the continuity on pause while going and doing Vertex, and I would argue that the existence of the RPG suggests that the metaverse has kind of a D&D-Pathfinder thing going on, where third party companies are still publishing characters in the original continuity even while Vertex premieres.)
