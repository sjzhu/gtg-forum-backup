I personally like to think of Haka as a soft spoken and pleasant fellow to be around most of the time.  He teaches children, and has lifetimes of experience with them.  You gotta be careful raising your voice around the kiddos if you're as imposing as Haka.

 

And I totally get the focusing on his chants.  If Ambuscade busts into Haka's classroom filled with children, he can't afford to lose control and hurt them.  Haka's a teacher, and he has to do his best to provide an example for his students.  He's a bruiser and can tear things limb from limb, but his chants focus his power rather than unleash it.  I get the feeling that he wouldn't ever Rampage unless his teammates fully understood and accepted that cutting lose is the best option (which is often what happens at the table, as well).

The biggest reason why I think Haka wouldn't go completely wild is his Eternal Haka ACC.  Haka doesn't fight for himself.  He doesn't look at enemies and think of how he'll be able to smash and bash them.  He thinks about what he needs to do to protect innocent people from getting hurt.  Note that when there isn't anyone left to protect, he stays inside and reads T.S. Eliot.  He'll scare off any cryptids that come his way, but he just wants to be left alone with his books.  I think that illustrates how gentle Haka's soul really is.  He fights to protect rather than to hurt.
