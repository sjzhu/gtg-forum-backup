TakeWalker:

But it does mean "magic eater" in Welsh!

Well… at least, it combines the word for magic and the word for eat.  Putting them together that way is probably totally ungrammatical if you asked an actual welsh speaker. I think hud-bwytawr would be more accurate (magic-eater rather than magic-eat), but I'm not sure.  And the B might turn into an F in that context… or maybe an M… I don't know the rules for consonant mutation in Welsh.

For those who don't know Welsh, W and Y are vowels. W sounds like "oo", so cwm ('valley') is pronounced like 'koom'.  Y is… complicated.  Their pronunciation 'hud-BOY-ta' was pretty much correct.
