I'm not really competent, but I answered it here: https://greaterthangames.com/comment/36658#comment-36658. The top does read "Owari", The End, as you gussed. (I'm not clear how much this implies he is actually dead.)I missed the other block. That is a signature block, and is written in katakana, one of the Japanese phonetic syllabries, the one typically used for foreign words. It reads "Adamu Rebotaro", i.e., Adam Rebottaro.In addition to his Nemesis symbol (to fix/mend), the other Kanji I noticed on his cards is on the style cards; I believe they are names of the relevant animal in Japanese, although I haven't checked carefully.
