Got bored waiting for Christmas and compiled a spreadsheet of which cards reference which issues in the Sentinels comics verse.

This pulled out the following facts:

Hero with fewest different appearances in their deck: Bunker with 10

Hero with most different appearances in their deck: The Argent Adept with 17

Villain with fewest different appearances in their deck: The Matriarch with a mere 5

Villain with most different appearances in their deck: The Chairman with 13

Issue with the most appearances in a single deck: La Capitan's La Paradoja Magnifica one-shot.

Issue with the appearances in the most decks: Science and Progress One Shot appearing in 8 different decks. After Vengeance decks are included, Vengeance #4 dethrones this crossover juggernaut with appearances in 9 decks (but only 1 pre-Vengeance).
