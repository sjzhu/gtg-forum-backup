So I started thinking about the Dreamer's projections.

It occured to me that the "Dark Hero" was a bit strange.  After all,  Vanessa (as the dreamer) was only 6 years old!  Legacy was a shining figure of goodness, and so it seems like it would be extremely strange for the Dreamer to have such a sinister version of him lurking about. So I started thinking about the projections in general, and why she has such specific nightmare creatures in her head.  

 

See, the Dreamer is unique.  Her brain actually exists in two places at the same time  in the head of the 6 year old girl, and the head of the adult Visionary. And they are both powerful psychics.  So what I think is happening is that the Dreamer's mind is taking the Visionairy's knowledge of the future, filtering the terrifying bits through the mind of a 6 year old, and distilling them into these projections. I actually think that each projection represents something or someone VERY specific.

 

Take the Dark Hero.  I think that this is Iron Legacy, as understood by someone who had NEVER met or seen him, but HAD seen (or knew about) Legacy. The future as described by a child who didn't really understand all of the context.

 

So, with that in mind, I started looking at the other projections.  

Dark Hero: Like I said, I think that this represents Iron Legacy.

Grotesque Arachnoid: Given the Argent Adept's involvement, and the eyes, I think that this might represent Akash'Bhuta.  "Earth spirit, sort of, kind of a primordial force really that can animate and create limbs out of the environment", boiled down to a kiddie version of "A scary big thing, with lots of arms and eyes!"

Illusionary Demon: I think that this represents Apostate.  A demonic thing, but its not quite real.  Maybe just the tip of something bigger that is dangerous in and of itself, but which is concealing the truth.  Its an illusion, or a lie, covering up what it really represents.  Foote has had some great ideas about what could actually be going on with Apostate, and I think that to a child, "Illusion demon" might be a good way of distilling it.

Granite Oni: this one is a bit more complex, but I think that it could represent either the operative, or the force that Fixer and the Operative are both contending with.  something immeasurably strong and incredibly tough.  this could be the operative herself, as this is a good description of her (and she can be pretty demonic), or it could potentially involve Zhu Long as the Dragon.  Something that you need to entangle and ensnare (like with a hoist chain), since confronting it directly is dangerous.  

The Whipacorn: Taking a look at Mainstay's incap, it seems like he is being confined and imprisoned.  Though  I don't have the card in front of me, I recall it looking like he was being held on a spaceship.  I'm wondering if he is being taken as a slave, or as a potential gladiator (possibly ot fight in the Arena seen on one of Parse's cards...)  Certainly, if you wanted a gladiator cantidate based on looks alone, Mainstay would be a good pick.  

The whipacorn could represent one of the alien slavers.  

Treacherous Ape: the Ape's laser pistol makes me think of the guy on Blackout, and I wonder if the circus could be another representation of a gladiator pit/arena.  A circus of the Roman kind, with the ape representing a guard who kept people in line?

Tooth Fairy: I've suspected for a while that we would see the Old Gods come back, possibly related to Atlantis.  Tempest (in silver shadow) certainly seems to be wearing druidic garb and carrying a blade.  I've speculated about possible Matriarch links to gods, and links in Atlantis to dragons.  I think that the tooth fairy is a representiation of myths and legends coming to back, but not like the good, cute, disney-fied versions.  and ot a kid, I could see that coming out as a Tooth fairy....that eats your teeth.  Basically, think fairies done by Guillermo del Toro.

Toy Master: I think that this represents FILTER.  someone who sits back and collects tech, pulling strings and moving puppets around.  It could be a specific person Iinside FILTER.  It might also represent the V5, since one of his toys is a train...
