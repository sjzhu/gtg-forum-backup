So, I've been working up an idea that I think is pretty neat, but I wanted to see what the SotM community thought of it. I've noted over the years that gamers keep hoping for a campaign-driven experience for SotM, but the guys from GtG have made it pretty clear that the story is within each game we play (they even went so far as to say that every game of Sentinels is canon since it all takes place within a multiverse). The upcoming RPG also somewhat scratches the same itch, though in a different way.

However, I know there are plenty of folks still looking for that campaign experience. With that in mind, I wrote up a full-on campaign that I've converted into a radio play for podcasting that I think would be pretty cool. A central aspect of this is to have community involvement within the campaign. I have a branching storyline that could veer out along various paths depending on the decisions of the community and the results of various battles that the community collectively plays through.

So, how would this work? We'll release the first episode of the radio play setting up the scenario and ask players to either make a decision or play a scenario of Sentinels and report the results via online poll. We would take the collective inputed of the community and use that to shape the direction of the story. We would then record the next episode. Wash, rinse, repeat until the final conclusion of the campaign.

Quick disclaimer: I have no intention of making any momey off of this. It would be released as a podcast free-of-charge. The podcast radio play will not feature any ads whatsoever. It is purely for fun.

Back to my original purpose this forum post, I want to gauge the community's opinion. Would any of you enjoy something like this? Enough to participate?

I'm open to any questions and/or suggestions.

Thanks.

Blossercubbles
An ongoing story that changes based on listener input is pretty intriguing a concept, though weak to the whims of the many. By it's very nature a success!

Hopefully, we won't have too many problems with the whims of the community. Having been more in corporate meetings than I ever care to admit, I tend to dislike the "story-by-committee" approach. They have a tendency to derail from their original vision and leave everyone involved with a bad taste in their mouth.

The story is already laid out until near its conclusion. I'd call it linear with branching elements. There are still some kinks to work out, but basically the variations in the story will come in two ways: 1) community results when playing the episode's scenario and 2) choices presented. Both will be gathered via an online poll. By having the branching story structure established ahead of time, it's less susceptible to those whims.

The biggest weakness I see is that we're on the honor system concerning the results of gameplays. We have some pretty difficult scenarios planned, but people can still lie and report a win where one didn't happen. But, I'd like to give the community the benefit of the doubt on this one.

For the polls, we may or may not use a weighting scale. As in, where an option to play the advanced version exists, wins and losses on the advanced mode carry more weight. That may get more complicated though than I can to make it.
