So this random Publishers Note happened to reveal just a bit of OblivAeon info I hadn’t caught before, and suddenly I have an answer for one of the two biggest logical disconnects between lore and game fluff (the other one being Voss’s sandwich bag and the resulting fact that there should only ever be one universe for all SCRPG games ever, despite them all having different GMs). If OblivAeon is destroying all reality every time anyone anywhere loses a game to him, then how is there ever more than one such game? Because, as a combination entity of destruction and creation, he constantly recreates reality every time he destroys it, because the destruction wasn’t perfect. And then the reconstruction isn’t perfect, so he has to destroy it again. And thus he’s in an infinitely repeating cycle where it doesn’t matter whether he wins or loses, it’ll all happen again forever. He’s Owlman!

Ps: “Extreme” spelled with an X…more properly, spelled without an E. Casa Nova is lame, so an Xtremiverse version of him would need to lean 90s rather than 70s in order to not be lame.

PPS: “Death of Nightmist”…that’s not accurate!

PPPS: I wonder if John England works with GLOBAL.
