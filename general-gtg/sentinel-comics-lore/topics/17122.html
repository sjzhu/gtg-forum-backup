The way I understand it, it's kind of like a funnel with two spouts.

Up until OblivAeon, crossing into realities was easy, as each swirled together.  Try to go back 'up' the funnel by time-traveling, and you really just ended up in a different stream, still heading down the funnel towards OblivAeon.

OblivAeon, naturally, is the apex of the funnel:  all of time and reality pours towards that single point.

After OblivAeon, all of time and reality goes in one of two directions: RPG-ward or Tactics-ward.  And, just as you can't 'pour up' the funnel, you can't time-travel past OblivAeon (or, really, even at all).

Haka spoilers:  In my mind, the "core card game reality" Haka heads through the Mistgate in search of other heroes, ending up marooned in the Tactics timeline.  Meanwhile, his alternate-reality female counterpart takes on the role of Haka in the RPG-reality.  Together, the two make up the "final pair" of Hakas which make each other effectively immortal.

Just my 2 cents.
