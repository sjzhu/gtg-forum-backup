Because this would be the demon prince version of finding a gnarly feathered mask in an antique shop.

Ps, Wrath is understood by a lot of Bible scholars to include despair, which is thought of as anger against God for failing to provide hope, and so I like the idea that Ashdia is like Abbados’s cousin or something.

By the way, I’m gonna cape for Acedia a bit, because I see sloth as flat refusal to move, a determination to stay put and waste no energy, and listlessness as movement in various aimless directions with no plan, never getting anywhere and squandering one’s energy in the process. I was disappointed that Christoher’s dissertation didn’t make any mention of the fact that Lust is a translation error and the original sin was Luxury (again, somehow distinct from Greed and Sloth).
