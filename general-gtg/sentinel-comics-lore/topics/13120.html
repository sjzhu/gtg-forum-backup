I'll agree with you that the most common use of mind control is horribly outdated in modern storytelling and does exactly what you described in robbing characters of agency until a convenient plot contrivance allows them to win.

 

I think the potential of Wrest the Mind is mind control done right. That three psychic damage backlash can be heinous, and it turns Wrest the Mind from a potentially broken card to something mechanically balanced that you only use when the situation is dire. The way I view it is that kind of psychic power takes a huge toll on Visionary, and so she only uses it in emergencies.

 

That would be how to do mind control correctly in a modern story. It has to have limits, and it has to have consequences for its use.
