So in a recent episode, a letter writer wrote in asking about a world in which the powers of the heroes and villains were inverted, and that actually gave me some pretty cool ideas

 

Bunker, instead of being a man piloting a suit, was part of an experimental program to save the brain of a combat veteran onto a chip. That chip can now be implanted into an adaptable biological construct that can change to best fit its surroundings and is now the amazing Adaptor

 

Benchmarch, instead of being a corporate backed hero with a cutting edge super suit, is a crowd funded online hero fan calling back to the golden age when the heroes were just better then all these crappy Johnny come lately and uses the money to remake old gadgets and gimmicks as the hero Throwback

 

Absolute zero now wears a suit to limit the burning inferno inside from making his environment deadly to everyone else as Melting Point.

Legacy is the latest in a line of demi-gods stretching back into the annuals of history who has arisen time and again to save the world, but each generation sacrifices a bit of power, losing one ability the pervious one had while needing to struggle against ever worsening odds

 

The Matriarch finds a pair of magical boots that allows her to control all animals that burrow under the ground to do her evil bidding

 

Expatriate is a master swordswoman, while the Operative is a master markswoman

Proletariat can absorb people into himself, gaining their strength, abilities and memories

The green grocer can throw exploding bacon and other meat products as the boisterous butcher

Feel free to suggest others!
