So, one of the best things about SotM for me is the way it evokes drama in gameplay. I thought it would be interesting for us to share some of those stories, if folks are interested in doing so. It's not just that games are close, but that the activity of trying to take down the villain before they eliminate the heroes (or otherwise win) give us an interesting and dramatic narative.

A recent game really brought this home. It was against a home-brewed villain, unfortunately, so this won't be as clear-cut of a situation, but basically once the heroes reveal his fiendish nature to the world, he became a rampaging beast, killing and eating everything - and everyone - in his way. Unfortunately, we got our evidence of his infernal nature earlier than we would have prefered, so we hadn't weakened him enough to make the game manageable. (In other words, we flipped him before we were ready and paid the price!) It was really scary and down to the wire, with a lot of damage being thrown around. We didn't know if we could pull it off - and honestly we were sure we were going to lose just two turns earlier, making for some exiting and energetic game play. The last active hero, Naturalist, was down to 1 HP, and successfully took out the villain before being taken down on the villain's next turn. (Not the closest game we've had, but it was impressively dramatic nonetheless!)

What kinds of dramatic moments can you share? Looking forward to hearing them!

The nice thing about Raiding Party is that ALL the revealed cards looking for crew members get put in the trash instead of back into the deck.

In high [H] games, usually this means she has a large chance of discarding a ton of her really nasty stuff stealing oneshots. If you can time that with destroying the ship, her deck can become very managable. 

La Captain, the more you play her, you start to see why she is rated at lvl 2. When she combos, she combos very hard, and that kinda gives her an elevated sense of danger. If you can manage to destroy the ship early and keep it in the trash, even her nasty combos will not give you much trouble. I have had so many games where she never flips at all, so she can be very swingy.

Her advanced mode takes the swingy-ness out of her and gives her a decidedly mid 3 difficulty
