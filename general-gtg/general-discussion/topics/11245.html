First off, the wiki is a fantastic resourse for SotM information.  I noticed the other day that they have a complete list of promo cards for both heroes and villains, however for most of the heroes there is only an image of the front side of the card and no image for the back.  There has been lots of talk about people printing their own copies of the Promo cards and that GtG is fine with people doing that to have access to the promos.  My suggestion would be, as long as GtG consents to it,  to post high quality, unmodified images of BOTH the front and incappacitated sides of all the promo cards so that people can have a central place for all of their promo printing needs.  I know that Chrisopher posted both sides of Super Scientific Tachyon lately and that was really great.

I know I would love something like this if I was a newer player because my experience has been that finding high quality images of the fronts are not too hard to find, but it has been pretty difficult for me to track down unmodified backs. The best I have been able to do for some of them is to use the images from Spiffs oversized cards which are slightly modified from the originals.  For some other promos I have found the incap sides, but only very low quality images that don't print well.
