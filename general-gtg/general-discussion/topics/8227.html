The token pack contains the following:

  • 1HP, 5HP, and 10HP tokens (same as the ones included in the Enhanced Edition box)
  • +1 damage and -1 damage tokens (same as EE)
  • Start of turn/end of turn tokens (NOT included in EE)
So I guess the only reason to buy it is for the start/end tokens, unless you find you desperately need more of the others. I made my own start/end tokens and they are really helpful to stop you forgetting effects that are easy to miss when you skim the cards.

I also have Spiff's Universal Effects Tracker which can cut down the number of tokens you need in play quite a lot, and makes everything neater.

Hope that helps!
