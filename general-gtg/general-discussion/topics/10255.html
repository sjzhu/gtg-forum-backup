Spiff:

Quite a few people mention GSF's difficulty level.  Maybe we'll need to come up with an anti-Advanced rules houserule to make it fun for the casuals.

The game has a bunch of easy to turn nobs to adjust the difficulty. You can:

  1. Start the game with more/less ships deployed

  2. Add/Remove shields or weaponry to taste

  3. Start the game with +1W/+1D on your ship panel

In the PAX East demo (which, if anything, was critiqued for being almost too easy), we stacked the mission deck and did a combination of #1 and #2
