I had a thought earlier today and wanted to share: I’m really glad Sentinels of the Multiverse is not a collectable card game. I think allowing us to enjoy the universe, creating interesting and impactful flavor while providing enough mechanics and capabilities to ensure many unique and enjoyable games was a much better way to go.

It would have been easy to make the leap to a CCG as another money-making opportunity, but the fact that the game can be expanded just by expanding the multiverse will hopefully help keep you in business.

And maybe one day we’ll even see actual comics. 8)

Many thanks!

We think that’s one of the best features too! All of us at Greater Than Games have played various CCGs, but we all stopped playing them after the cost became unsustainable. Thus, no CCGs (because we’re cheap), and a cooperative game where no one has to run the villain (because we always get roped into being the GM )
