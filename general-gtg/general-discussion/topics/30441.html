Hello Everyone!

My name is Lydia-Rae Wehmeyer (Lydia), pronouns are she/her/hers and I will be your new Crowdfunding and Customer Service Specialist for GTG!

I am so happy to be here! A little bit about me:

I’ve been in the tabletop industry for about 4 years now on the convention side presenting panels and workshops on how to incorporate tabletop gaming and RPGS with education, mental health, accessibility and DE&I. In addition, I am also a DE&I consultant for Stonemaier Games and have worked on many projects with companies such as Lucky Duck and Japanime.

I’ve always wanted to find a place in the hobby and didn’t get into it until I was 21. My entry way game was Lords of Waterdeep and my love for board games have been strong ever since.

I look forward to communicating with all of you and have a wonderful day.
