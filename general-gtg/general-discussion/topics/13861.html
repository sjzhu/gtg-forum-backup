Her's some spoiler(ish) stuff for you:

La Capitan messes with time, but is kind of new to it, so it has all kinds of awesomely crazy effects.  There were a lot of "WTF is she doing now" moments in playtesting, and often we left her out till last just to see what all would happen (although that didn't always work out well for us)

Citizens Anvil & Hammer are tough.  One of them has wings that are invulnerable and the other throws fire around.  They work together pretty well.  In playtesting they were brutal, and there was much begging and pleading with Christopher to make them less brutal.  After a lot of maniacal laughter at our broken spirits and teams of fallen heroes the powers that be relented and they are much more balanced now, but still really tough.

Plague Rat is my favorite of the new Villain decks.  It feels very thematic as a powerful beast that they are trying to wield against the heroes.  It is tempting for the heroes to try and turn the tables and use the beast-man against his controllers, but it is dangerous for anyone to try to control Plague Rat.

The Environments are really cool too.  My favorite of them is the Vampire deck, but then facing unholy things that hate light as my beloved Fanatic is exactly what I want.

The overall goals of the playtesters were to reduce drastically the amount of book keeping and chains of villain interactions that Vengeance lent itself to, as well as giving some less difficult villains to face and adding a lot of fun mechanics that wouldn't flood the table with cards.

I think we really nailed it, Christopher came up with great mechanics, and the decks are engaging thematically.  I loved working on Wrath of the Cosmos and the final result, but I think this expansion outshines it.  I really can't wait to get players reactions to it.
