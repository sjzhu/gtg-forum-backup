I'll use $50 as the cost of the core and uprising minis unpainted [1] and $170 [2] as their cost painted.

I'll use $20 as the cost of the add-on minis unpainted [3] and $80 [4] as their cost painted. 

Total for unpainted minis is thus $70 and for painted minis is $250. There are 53 minis [5]. 

 

That's $1.32/unpainted mini or $4.72/painted mini.

 

[1] I'm assuming that we're looking at the cost to _add_ minis to getting the core game, not to get them by themselves. Since $100 gets you the Core game and the Core/Uprising unpainted minis, but $50 gets you the Core game, $100-$50 is the additional cost for the unpainted minis. Total is $50

[2] Getting the Core and/or Uprising minis painted is $60 each, so $120 extra dollars on top of the $50 for unpainted minis. Total is $170.

[3] The Add-on including unpainted minis is $40. The Add-on maps alone are $20. The extra required to get the unpainted minis is thus $20.

[4] The Add-on including painted minis is $100, so that's another $60 above the unpainted minis. Total is $80.

 

[5] 53 minis: 

Core (19) - 9 characters plus 10 Citizens;

Uprising (17) - 5 characters plus 6 Proletariats plus 6 bots

Add-on (17) - 6 Blade Battalion soldiers plus 5 dinosaurs plus 5 underbosses plus the Reaper
