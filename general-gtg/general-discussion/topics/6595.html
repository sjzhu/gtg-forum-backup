I'd just like to point out that based on the majority of kickstarters I've been a part of, the funding followed a fairly specific pattern.  It would have a huge surge in the first 2-3 days as loyal/excited fans joined on.  Then it would have very slow growth for the whole rest of the project until the last 48 hours.  The primary reason for this is that when you "star" a project on kickstarter, you get a reminder 48 hours before it's over.  Many people (often including myself) like to wait until the last 48 hours because by then you have a pretty good idea what stretch rewards there will be, so you have a better idea overall of what (in total) you'll be getting.  I've seen a few projects literally double in the last 48 hours.

As a result, I wouldn't be too depressed that things have slowed down.  I'm not saying don't try to recruit more people; I've been posting on facebook and sponsoring international shippers myself.  I'm just saying, don't be too upset if we don't see MAJOR growth again until the last 48 hours.
