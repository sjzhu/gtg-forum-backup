There are 4 hero (Young Legacy, Rook City Wraith, Redeemer Fanatic and Ra, Horus of Two Horizons) and 2 villain promo card (Vengeful Baron Blade and Cosmic Omnitron) and 1 villain (Ambuscade) and 1 hero (Unity) promo decks.

The decks you can buy from the Greater than Games Store. The cards you'd probably have to go to a convention or get in on the kickstarter for the next expansion which should be starting 'soon'. The promo cards are often a reward level there. I believe the Young Legacy Promo is no longer available, however, but the front and back are on BGG so you could print your own and sleve it or stick it to card to make an acceptable substitute (which is what I did until I got it in the last Kickstarter).
