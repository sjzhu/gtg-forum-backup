I think it’s probably Visionary. Visionary just seems to have the exact right answer for exactly everything.

She’s the only character that can take a shot at controlling the villain deck (and can do a number on Environments too). She can bury tough or annoying villain cards, take things out of the villain trash to prevent ressurection (in the case of Voss or Omnitron, even vs Blade it has its uses to stop him from blowing up the world, and against Dawn it kicks, because you can keep her from flipping to her immunity side).

Visionary can buff or debuff with Twist the Ether. She can’t heal, but she can set up force fields to tank 5 damage or become immune to damage if things get really hairy. She’s great at helping everybody draw cards.

And just when you thought her main weakness was dealing damage… you run into Wrest the Mind. It’s all about target selection, a T-rex or Kraken is a good choice. We had some fun with her swinging a train around and hitting people with it every turn (plummeting monorail) but by far the greatest damage dealt was by Wresting an Electro-pulse explosive. Only a 3 player game, but redirected 45 damage back to Omnitron, instantly killing him at that point.

And honestly, Visionary is even good dead, able to retrieve good cards from the trash, or get rid of annoying ongoing cards or environments.

Even though it is hard to just pick one, I think I will say Bunker. Once you Heavy Plating, go into Recharge Mode and you’ll take no to little damage from enemies and environment cards. It will then you can stock your hand with your entire deck.

Afterwards you can slap down Upgrade Mode, allowing you to play two cards and still drawing one. Depending on who you are facing will play into the decision on when to play Omni-Cannon, such as a villian with plenty of cards to destroy equipment cards you may want to hold off on playing it so that way Omnicannon doesn’t get destroyed along with all the cards underneath. Or wait to more the end and lay it out and just do a weaker initial damage.

After you’ve got Omni-Cannon, one or two maintenance Units, Ammo Drop, Flak Cannon, Grenade Launcher, and maybe a Auxiliary Power Source, you then play Turrent Mode. You may not be able to play cards or draw, but you can now use two powers and a plus one to damage. Initial attack using Grenade Launcher to eliminate any minions that may be lurking about (especially against Voss) then use Omni-Cannon to cause massive (if charged) damage to the Villian card.

Next turn you can decide whether you want to use the Auxiliary Power Sources to grant an additional power or draw three additional cards. Ammo Drop wil be there to help you keep your hand full of cards since you can no longer draw regularly. Grenade Launcher for minions that may be around, if none then you can use both Omni-Cannon and Flak Cannon to do damage to the Villian. Also you can elect to use Maintenance Unit to restore your health by two if need be, or if you have two out you can be restored by four. Lastly if ever needed you can use Bunker’s power on his character card to draw another card if you needed one.

And thus is why Bunker is awesome, because of his high damage, damage reduction, and very well self healing.

I must be playing a different game than you guys…

Zero’s problem is yeah, it takes way too long to get his stuff out. There’s no way he can play more than 1 card a turn, and to be at all effective he needs both his modules, plus Thermal Shockwave, and for him to get to the degree you’re talking about, he also will need cryo chamber and focused apertures.

Even when he is at that point, there is so much that can screw him. Cramped Quarters Combat, for one, is terrible. Ok, suddenly Zero is worse than useless. Or Devestating Aroura, really anything that destroys all his equipment and/or ongoing cards and he’s back to starting from scratch, with a minimum 5 more turns to go before he can really do anything. Even Ra’s normally amazing Imbue Fire is REALLY frusterating when playing with zero, because it prevents him from healing.

He also can’t deal damage from the start. Which I hate. First turn vs omnitron: electro-pulse explosive. You need everyone to deal damage, NOW. Zero can’t help you, all he can do is sit there and cheer on everybody else, which inevitably ends with like 10-15 lightning damage to the face. He’s bad against omnitron because of this and because of the tendency for Omnitron to destroy stuff. He’s bad against blade because of Elemental redistrubutors (which you really want to avoid killing if possible, so he doesn’t blow up the world). Admittedly he might be pretty good against Voss, who 1. doesnt destroy stuff and 2. has a tendency to deal fire and cold damage. He’s allright against dawn, but not particularly amazing.

Even with his ultimate set-up, I don’t think he’s any better than wraith with her best stuff, or bunker with his best stuff, or Ra or Tempest or Legacy with their best stuff…

on a side note: Haka is pretty amazing. With multiple Dominions out he can draw so many cards, along with his card draw from Haka of SomethingorOther, and then you just heal, prevent damage, or deal damage as you see fit. Savage Mana is great, so is Mere and Tahia (?). Rampage is also a great card, especially with Savage Mana.
