If I could suggest one for Bunker that goes in a different direction:

Hard Light Interface: (Equipment, Limited)

Bunker may have multiple copies of the same "Mode" card in play at the same time.

At the start of his turn, Bunker deals himself 2 psychic damage or destroys this card.

When this card leaves play, destroy all mode cards.

If you played a new mode, then the normal condtions would re-assert themselves, and you'd get rid of both (or all three) modes in play. He can only have multiple copies of the same mode card- new ones still destryo all other modes cards in play.

Hard light interface is equipment, so it would stick around.  

Recharge mode reduces damage, even damage dealt to self, so you'd reduce it...to nothing if you had plating and 1 recharge, or 2 recharges out.

If it was too little reward, then you could make it so that it instead said:

 

 

Bunker may have multiple copies of the same "Mode" card in play at the same time.

Bunker may always play a "Mode" card, enven when he would otherwise be prevented from playing cards

When this card leaves play, destroy all mode cards.

 

With this configuration, he could give himself extra turret modes- so if he had the powers out, he could start using more and more every turn, at progressively higher damage.  He only has three direct damage powers (Grenade, flak, and omni-cannon), so with all three turret modes out, he'd be able to:

Use flak at +3 damage

Use grendes at +3 damage

Use Omni-cannon at +3 damage

Use 2 other powers (so, both repair modules)

 

and to even do that would require a LOT of setup time and a very, very lucky hand/draw of the cards.

In addition, the ability to always play a mode, even when the environment/villain/whatever would prevent card plays would reflect his tactical flexibility and ability ot adjust to any situation.
