So I did my first Guy's night game night with Sentinels of the Multiverse last night and it was a roaring sucess.  We played three games and I wanted to tell you how they all went.  There were four of us total.

First Game:  I played Chrono-Ranger, one of my buddies played Legacy, another played Ra, and the last guy REALLY wanted to play Absolute Zero.  I told him (tongue-in-cheek) under no circumstances would he be allowed to play AZ (first time for all of them).  He settled and played Tempest.  We fought Voss in Metopolis and destroyed him.  Fun and challenging, but not hard.

Second Game:  After that victory they all felt cocky and wanted to take on the hardest guy in the box.  I told them they were nuts and that we'd probably all die, but we took on Iron Legacy.  We fought him in the Final Wasteland.  It was Legacy, Chrono-Ranger, Haka, and Fanatic (the one guy was still really wanting to play AZ, but I refused to allow it).  It seemed IMPOSSIBLE to actually win, like we had no chance.  We were not inflicting any damage at all and getting kicked around like ragdolls.  We held on, and things started to turn around when we were able to start knocking out his ongoing cards.  He killed Chrono-Ranger and Fanatic, even after Fanatic resurrected once.  It was a bloodbath!  Haka had 6HP and Legacy only had 1, and on the final turn the good Legacy punched the Iron Legacy and defeated him.  We won!

Third Game:  At this point we were all reveling in the sucess of doing what literally seemed impossible (we actually googled something and were like, "No way, Iron Legacy is really THAT powerful???").  We had done it.  I said, "How about Cowboys and Robots in the wild, wild west?"  We all liked the idea and decided to take on Omnitron in Sivler Gulch.  My one friend was begging to play AZ, I told he was nuts and but decided to let him do it.  Then, for thematic purposes, I took Omnitron-X.  The other guys were Legacy (same guy played him every game) and Chrono-Ranger.  WE GOT DESTROYED.  AZ self-destructed and could never get anything running, Omnitron-X could never get anything going, and the other two were unable to stand alone.  It was bad, real bad.  But it was good to lose, because it was good to see that we were not invincible!

It was a ton of fun, and there were plentious LOLZ to go around.  The AZ player kept making the following jokes:

"You know how much I liked playing this character?  ABSOLUTE ZERO."

"You know what the chance of me ever playing him again?  ABSOLUTE ZERO."

"You know what good he is? ABSOLUTE ZERO."

Sounds like it went well!  I had a game night last night and really wanted Sentinels to hit the table, however, out game of Arkham Horror took 6 hours...so we didn't have the time.

Also, AZ players need to be extremely aware of card text, especially that which pertains to damage "targets" vs "non-hero targets".  A lot of AZ's One-Shots allow him to damage a "target" which can very well be directed at himself for further survivability.  They also need to pay attention to the changing damage type; if you take fire damage, which can be then dealt as cold damage, you can choose to target yourself a second time. With Focused Apertures in play, this can result in a +1 net heal.

So AZ is not just complex in terms of setting up, keeping track of damage, and letting your health go up and down; the player also needs to be incredibly astute when it comes to reading all the cards carefully, which is why newcomers aren't likely to grasp his full potential within their first 3-5 games.

It's come up before - with his NPCU, when Ab'Zero would be dealt cold damage, he heals instead. So if he's nuking, say, three targets for two points of cold damage each and chooses himself as one of those targets (and has his NPCU out at the time), he will only have dealt four points of cold damage, not six, because the two that hit him were changed into healing. Damage is only dealt if you lose hp - if they stay where they are or go up, you're not taking damage (which is why, under Rule 15, cards like Solar Flare and Pushing the Limits go away if you try to hit yourself whilst immune to that damage type).
