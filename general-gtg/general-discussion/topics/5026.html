Several people on the boards and at Gen Con mentioned forced deployment, so I figured I’d make a quick post about it since it is one of my favorite cards (in the sense that it is thrilling to play against).

One question that came up at Gen Con was how this card interacts with Voss’ alternate win condition, so I made a post about that over in Official Rulings. However, we were also talking about how Forced Deployment is much easier to deal with if a hero can destroy it before it destroys itself at the start of the Villain turn. That way, the heroes get to take shots at the minions before all of their “end of turn” effects trigger.
