 I'm not gonna lie - it feels like July. If you work at a tabletop game publisher, that means a near-constant panic attack for the 4 weeks before Gen Con (if you're lucky; if not, it is 8-12 weeks of panic). Here's some things that happened?

  1. We attended Origins.
  2. We received and shipped out a ton of stuff.
  3. We argued with several factories about massive production delays.
  4. We worked on a number of complicated business deals.
  5. We did a massive amount of Gen Con prep and planning.
  6. We inventoried all of the Cheapass Games products in our warehouse.
  7. I posted a post to the Cheapass Games Kickstarter! If any of you have any thoughts about any of the Cheapass Games line, let me know here on the forum, or via email! We are excited to see what fans are excited to see from that line going forward!

Here is the status of all of our works in progress.

In Stock Since March

  • Nothing

Shipping to Backers

  • Nothing

In Transit; On a Boat

  • Homebrewers
  • Microbrewers
  • Medium

At Factory; Pre-Press Work

  • Legends of Sleepy Hollow
  • Cheapass Games in Black & White

Current WIP Focus by GTG

  • Sentinel Comics: The Role Playing Game core rulebook (Christopher, Jenn, Critical Hits)
  • Prime War (Christopher, Chris Burton, Lore Forge)
  • Spirit Island: The Jagged Earth (Paul, Christopher, Jenn, Erik)
  • Sentinels of Freedom video game (Christopher, Adam, Underbite Studio)
  • The History of Sentinel Comics (Christopher, Adam)
  • Untitled Cheapass Games Project (Jenn, Chris Burton, James Ernest)
  • Milkman (Chris Kirkman, Chris Burton)
  • Fate of the Elder Gods: Agents of Arkham (Chris Kirkman)
  • Some other games (Chris Kirkman, Chris Burton)
  • A new and improved website (Jenn, Paul, Alex, Joel)

I'll see you next month!
