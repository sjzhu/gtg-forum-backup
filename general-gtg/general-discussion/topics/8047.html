The point of printing the keyword in bold and then printing the rules in italics is to make the ability easy to pick out when you're scanning a table full of cards (which is why I added names to all of the villain powers on my oversized cards).  When an ability takes an entire sentence to describe, your brain typically needs to get through a few words of the sentence before it can make the mental leap to "oh, I know what this is.  I can stop reading".  With a keyword, your brain makes that leap much more quickly.  And having the rules in italics makes it so that if your brain doesn't make the leap immediately, then that supporting text can help it along.  It's the best of both worlds, in a lot of ways.

That quick scan is why I think SotM cards need to have, at the very least, the word "start" and "end" in bold on cards.  That's one of the main things we do in the game, scan the cards on the table for start-of-turn and end-of-turn effects.  We do it all the time, in every game, but the dense wording on the cards makes it more difficult to do than it should be.
