The problem is that associations can be stronger than meanings.  Yes, the ad is using the literal meaning of "loser", and it's easy to recognize what they mean.  But that doesn't prevent the association "Playing SotM = Loser(at life)".  It won't be a strong association, but it will still be there, and it's not an association we want made at all.

 

That being said, if ThinkGeek has a deprecating sense of humor, then that goes a great way to breaking the association.  It's like calling your friend a jerk - you are familiar enough with each other that you know that term is meant in a meaningless fashion, and no negative connotation is attached to the insult.

It also helps that "League of Losers" feels like a reference, even if the readers aren't familiar with it, which also helps break the negative connotations.

 

I do trust ThinkGeek though.  They've had a lot of success selling to the geek community, which is a community with a particularly strong negative association with the term "loser".  It's not something that would slip under the radar.  If it's there, it's because they don't think it's a problem.
