I don't know if you knew this, but it is January already!

So, how are things going at GTG? December is an incredibly hectic month for us (as it is for many people), but we've gotten a number of things done, and a number of other things have progressed since last month.

  1. Our new warehouse management system implementation is coming along, and is on track for a mind-January roll out, as anticipated. We have a whole new shipping area setup that we are absolutely loving.
  2. Christopher and Adam have been hard at work on The Letters Page podcast, a weekly podcast about Sentinel Comics lore.  It is something that we promised years ago and are finally getting close to releasing. I'll let Christopher provide more details on that when he is ready.
  3. We've been working through cleaning up and figuring out how to best use our various mailing lists and social media accounts. We used December to experiment with "weekly deals" on the mailing list (don't worry, we won't send weekly emails every, or even most, months). We've also launched an email list for Citizens, as I mentioned last month, and one for distributors (not that most people reading this will care about that last one, but it is exciting for me :) )
  4. Jenn has been hard at work on our next Kickstarter project even though it doesn't launch until the end of February! She posted a teaser image today: https://twitter.com/GTGJennifer/status/816344465971486724
  5. Multiverse Month is coming up! I'll let Craig and Mara post more info about this, but like we have done the past two years, we are going to be sending promo cards and other materials to any stores that are interested in featuring specific games on specific weekends in January and February. If any of you have a local gameing store that is interseted, have them email craig@greaterthangames.com for more info!

I also want to update you all on our convention schedule for 2017! We've got that mostly ironed out, so here we are, in order!

  1. PAX South - Craig will be attending and will be running demos in a small booth with some Citizens
  2. NY Toy Fair - Jenn and Mara will be attending to see what that side of the industry is up to, but we won't have a booth.
  3. PAX East - Everyone from will be there! Our booth will be huge and big! Come see us! Except for Christopher and Craig, who will be on...
  4. JoCo Cruise - Christopher will be running demos on a boat!
  5. GAMA Trade Show - Chrisopher, Mara, and I will attend because our distributor and retailer customers say we have to
  6. UK Games Expo - We'll have a much bigger presence there this year, plus more people! Christopher, Mara, Jenn, Craig, and I will all be attending. We might even have time to do an event in London the week before the show! Stay tuned!
  7. Origins - Christopher, Jenn, and Chris Kirkman will be there, but we will not have a booth.
  8. Gen Con - The biggest every! Gen Con 50! Come see everyone in our booth and room! SO MANY EXCITING THINGS WILL HAPPEN!!!
  9. PAX Prime - Craig will run a small booth
  10. Essen Spiel - Paul and Mara will go, and Jenn and Christopher might, but we won't have a booth.

Phew... That's a ton of shows.

We've also been working on clearing our project backlog! Here is the status of all of our works in progress!

In Stock Since Last Month

  1. SotM: Enhanced Edition, Rook City & Infernal Relics, Vengeance, Omnitron-IV, & Wager Master.
  2. Belle of the Ball (This one is a cool old Dice Hate Me title that has been out for 2 years! Check it out if you haven't!)
  3. Great Heartland Hauling reprint

Shipping to Backers

  1. The Metagames KS (Time Management, Traitor Mechanic, and Trick Taking)
  2. New Bedford errata tiles
  3. Shattered Timelines & Wrath of the Cosmos to OblivAeon "Everything Ever" backers

In Transit; Scheduled Delivery late-January

  1. Some more SotM reprints.
  2. Deck Building: The Deck Building Game reprint
  3. Compounded reprint
  4. Brew Crafters reprint

In Mass Production

  1. Lazer Ryderz
  2. Exoplanets
  3. Viva Java
  4. Viva Java: The Dice Game

At Factory; Pre-Press Work

  1. Spirit Island
  2. Fate of the Elder Gods
  3. Bottom of the 9th: Clubhouse (also still waiting on a few late art pieces)
  4. SotM: Ultimate Collector's Case

OblivAeon is still in progress, but is coming along very well. I'm hoping to have news that it is being sent to the factory when I update you next month!

So, what are we going to be working on for the bulk of 2017? Here are the things I can tell you about!

Current WIP Focus by GTG

  1. Completing OblivAeon
  2. Sentinel Comics: The Role Playing Game
  3. A sweet Fabled Nexus title that will be on Kickstarter in late February.
  4. Prime War
  5. A new and improved website.

Anyway, that's all for this month! In future updates, I'll stick with the Works in Progress section, but if there are any particular questions or things you'd like me to talk about in addition to that stuff, definitely let me know!

I'll see you next month, if not before!
