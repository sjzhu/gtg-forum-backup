The only problems I can see are these: first, that the V5 don't scale with H; their attacks all do fixed amounts of damage and whatever else. It wouldn't be TOO hard to just put all the H values to H-2, but the other problem is that the Ennead are meant to be dealt with as a group. Their power comes from having a card play and having 3-9 auxillary effects trigger off of it, so having just 1 severely limits their ability to combo.

The problem is though that it results in double scaling.  Playing against the V5 already scales, because you're playing against (H) V5 decks.  If one of the members has damage that scales with (H), then that individual member becomes tougher or easier, in addition to having more partners.  Now, if you want to make the Ennead member harder, you can fix (H) as higher (always 4, always 5), but you really don't want it to actually fluctuate with (H).
