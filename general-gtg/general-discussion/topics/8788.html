The reasoning behind this reprint decision is as follows:

1) It will provide additional storage space for people who are interested in sleeving cards, as it will come in a box with the same dimensions as Enhanced Edition (for those who do not sleeve, Vengeance will provide sufficient extra space).

2) It will make warehouse operations much easier, since now Enhanced Edition, Vengeance, and the Rook City / Infernal Relics box set will all be the same size.

3) The combined MSRP of the separate expansions will wind up having been essentially the same (actually $0.05 less) than the MSRP of the new product.

4) Aside from some typos (which would be fixed in any reprint), the game content will not change at all.

5) The exact same number of copies of Rook City and Infernal Relics have been printed to date, so the number of people with one but not the other will be relatively small.
