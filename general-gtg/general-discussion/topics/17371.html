Not a lot has changed on the production front this month, though we've gotten a lot more information on the shipping process for OblivAeon stuff (Christopher will post a bit more about that on the KS today). The one exception is the RPG Starter Kit, which is already on its way! There are a lot of production and logistical advantages to making products that are just books. Here is a quick summary of things we accomplished in the past month:

  1. Sentinel Comics: The Role Playing Game Starter Kit finished printing!
  2. We had a giant meeting about the layout of the SC:RPG Core Rule Book!
  3. We began work on organizing and inventorying all of our convention supplies in the warehouse!
  4. We launched the Legends of Sleepy Hollow Kickstarter!
  5. Mara, Jenn, and I attended Essen Spiel in Germany!
  6. We reached an agreement with the German publisher Pegasus to publish Spirit Island in German!
  7. We finished planning for PAX Unplugged and printed a bunch of materials!

Here is the status of all of our works in progress!

In Stock Since October

  1. SotM: The Scholar reprint

Shipping to Backers

  1. Nothing!

In Transit; On A Train/Truck

  1. Spirit Island reprint
  2. Lazer Ryderz reprint
  3. Sentinel Comics: The Role Playing Game: Starter Kit

Completed Mass Production; In China

  1. SotM: Stuntman, Benchmark, & Void Guard
  2. SotM: Complete Hero Variant Collection

In Mass Production

  1. SotM: Ultimate Collector's Case
  2. SotM: 5th Anniversary Foil Villain Collection
  3. Various SotM expansion reprints
  4. Spirit Island: Branch & Claw reprint

At Factory; Pre-Press Work

  1. SotM: OblivAeon
  2. New Bedford reprint

Current WIP Focus by GTG

  1. Sentinel Comics: The Role Playing Game core rulebook (Christopher, Jenn, Critical Hits)
  2. Prime War (Christopher)
  3. Legend of Sleepy Hollow (Chris Kirkman + Jenn)
  4. A new and improved website (Jenn, Paul, Joel, etc)
  5. A cool Sentinel Comics related thing for 2018

I'll see you next month!
