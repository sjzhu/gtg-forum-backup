The normal card is the correct one in both cases. In Dreamer's case, it's just a simple typo. However in Matriarch's case, the original printing of Rook City had the "whenever this card is played, play the next card" on the Fowl Cards, but they decided to alter it in future printings, because cards like Hair Trigger Reflexes could stop the chaining of cards. They have not had the need to reprint over sized cards, however once they do reprint them there will be an "update" pack that will be sold that will contain the corrected cards.

On her front side, when you destroy a Fowl, Lilian will deal damage to the highest-hp hero unless they destroy one of their cards - so that person can choose which of the two effects happens to them, basically. But that's every time a Fowl is destroyed, so if someone's doing an AE that's gonna take out a whole load of them, you're either gonna lose all your stuff or take a lot of damage (unless you have soak in which case yay :D). More so if Muninn is out (and even more if Huginn's there too).

On her flip side, Lilian regains hp every time a Fowl enters play…so if she chains a whole bunch of them then yes, she will heal up a lot (unless you're in the Time Cataclysm with Oppressive Smog out - I think that's the only effect in the game currently which reduces healing).

I think a few typos and stuff may have been corrected in the new version (I have the old one, like you), like the Checmical Explosion and stuff. The main change to Lilian's deck, as far as I'm aware, was that the "When a Fowl card enters play, play the top card of the villain deck" text was moved from the Fowl cards to Lilian's, because otherwise you could prevent them chaining via Hairtrigger Reflexes (Expatriette).
