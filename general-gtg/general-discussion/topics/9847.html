Curious on what you (and your group) do when encountering 'cheap' overpowering situational combo that greatly benefit heroes and severely undermine villains, while doesn't make much sense thematicly such as:
 
* Insula Primalis' Volcanic Eruption + Legacy's Lead From The Front & Next Evolution OR Ra's all-hero-fire-immunity power = 7 Damage to all non-hero target EVERY turn. A hero can skip a turn to stop it but apparently villains are too selfish to even pay a mind to the raging volcano killing them.
 
* Omnitron's Electro Pulse Explosive + Visionary's Wrest The Mind = (15+12+9+6) = 42 or 45 damage to Omni, definitely worth the sacrifice of 12 Visionary HP while also removing the biggest threat from the villain. And makes me wonder why bother setting up the game and playing it in the first place.
 
* (To a much lesser extent) Plague Rat's Infection + Mr Fixer's Jack Handle, I don't think this is too bad but still I felt cheap exploiting it or solely picking Mr Fixer just because Plague Rat is there.
 
Those situation does happen in our game even though we don't plan and we are not looking to set it up at all, needless to say it trivialise the villains deck and I felt it ruined the game... What will you guys do in that situation? Personally I don't have a problem with powergaming and I welcome exploiting 'cheap' combo on a regular PvP game playing hard to win, but Sentinels is a co-op game, I don't think the mentality should be win at all cost. If it too much of an easy win with cheap combos then there is little satisfaction in that game.
 
How do you guys handle that? I'm thinking of just banning certain heroes from facing certain villains/environment, perhaps that's a bit much. But I also don't want to tell other player to not play a certain card from their hand just because it's 'too cheap' or 'game-ruining'.

If you feel a situation is too cheap, don't use it.  I don't play Plague Rat on Wagner Mars because I know it'd be lame.  But even if a hero contains the potential of the kinds of ubercombos that you describe, they don't draw into them every game, so I figure it's perfectly reasonable to have them in.

If Ra makes the heroes immune to the volcano (note: Legacy CANNOT do this, as Lead from the Front only lets him take damage from VILLAIN cards; Next Evolution can protect himself indefinitely but all other heroes will eventually burn), just think of it as the fire-god having led the villains onto a volcanic outcrop and then keeping them trapped there, letting the convection heat slowly roast them alive until they surrender or pass out.

If Visionary blows up Omnitron with his own bomb, well, she's telekinetic; she just threw it right back at him and put up a forcefield around him to contain the blast.  It's a bit weird that ONLY she can do this (Tachyon can redirect one blast with Synaptic), since other heroes like Idealist have similar powers, but meh.

And when Fixer is feeling a mite feverish, it just lets him get his Drunken Kung Fu moves going....presumably he trained to use the Jack Handle like a staff while fighting in this manner.

My friend chose Ra in his first game. The volcano came out, hit us once, then Ra read the card he'd just played. An expression of  "oh my god, I just realised something very cool" appeared on his face, and he asked me to confirm that the power did what he thought it did, I confirmed. 

The expression changed to one of genuine joy. he knew he could win the game for us. And he did.

Afterwards we talked for about 20 minutes about how the heroes heard ominous rumblings, the air filled with ash and smoke and the terrible heat of lava began to scorch both heroes and villain. Just as the heroes are beginning to worry about their impending fiery death, Ra steps forward with staff in hand, silhouetted against the forest fire that's already raging behind him. He holds the staff up to the sky, and the heroes begin to feel like this fire thing's not so bad really. One of them speaks. "Oh no, we're going to be killed by horrible burny lava! We should run away really very quickly!" Everyone grins. No one moves. They continue the fight knee-deep in the lava that is now reducing Omnitron to a puddle of molten metal and glass and plastic, with one hero or another commenting occasionally along the lines of, "Insula Primalis is pretty warm this time of year, huh? I'm glad we brought sunscreen..."

It was awesome.

And it makes up for all those times when you get beaten by someone who's usually easy, like Blade or Gloomweaver, in some hideously embarrassing way.

I enjoy "cheap" combos. But I play with random everything, so I never get them intentionally. However, a certain group of heroes basically thrive on being a "cheap" combo. Visionary, Horus Ra, and EW Absolute Zero.

A Demoralizing Cocooned Visionary will never take damage and will deal out 1 point every round. Horus Ra incapped can put a One-Shot that will do nothing to said Visionary. While a incapped EW AZ either shuts down the Environment deck or can do 1 damage to a villain. It works on just about all villains!

Another one is, Visionary Twist the Ethering the Villain Character card. Ra plays Flesh of the Sun God and uses it. While EW AZ continues to be incapped and does what he did before. It works great against single target villains that lack mass ongoing destruction. If facing Plague Rat, Imbued Fire will also shut down Infection damage. Visionary getting the Telekinetic Cocoon will free up Ra's power phase, throw in Demoralization, and woo! Visionary is helping Ra keep up with damage.

Visionary with Twist the Ether on the Villain Character Card plus a Cocoon, throw in a Demoralization for bonus. Horus Ra incapped, putting a damaging one-shot on the top of the deck. EW AZ with Null-Point, bonus for Cold Snap, Impale, Focused Apertures, and/or Cryo Chamber.

 

Reason why Visionary rather than Dark Visionary? As each hero needs certain cards to work, Enlighten helps a lot to get thost cards.

How to beat the Ennead Advanced, easy edition:

1.  The Scholar. (flesh to iron is sick, + alchemical redirection eliminates almost all damage they can do, his all target damage and heal is broken when you are low enough and have + damage.

2.  Legacy.  Here's that + damage, and take down is awesome.  Lead and fortitude can let you keep others from taking too much, just don't go crazy.

3.  Mr. Fixer.  Jack Handle with + damage, Grease Gun, redirection and pipe wrench.

4.  Haka.  Rampage, HoB, Ta Moke, healing, Ground Pound.

5.  Tempest.  Tempest is a boss.  

6.  Omni-X.  Defensive blast with + damage, elemental defenses.

7.  Nightmist.  Mistform, oblivion.

 

they all have -damage taken options, multi target damage or boosting.
