As an European backer, I have some frustrations on how my pledge fulfillment was handled. I would like to hear from GTG how we can move forward on this.

I know Darrell Louder, as the project creator, is the only person accountable for that project, but since GTG handled the shipping and my frustrations have something to do with the shipment, I’m posting this here.

I am backer ClémentXVII on Kickstarter for the Unpub 5 project. I pledged $60 for the Memory Box 1, as can be seen on the screenshot below (I just left out my Backer number):

However, when the rewards were shipped, I got the Memory Box 2 pledge level (i.e. Compounded and Mars needs Mechanics) instead of VivaJava. Also, no Compounded scientist was in the box.
This is frustration number 1. I quickly sent a mail to GTG in order to know what happened, but got a reply recently telling me that what I got matches what they got in their spreadsheet.

Then, some days later, I get a bill from FedEx. Since I’m in Belgium, I know that we have to pay 21% taxes if the shipment is valued more than € 20. This is why I contacted Darrell before he would ship the games, asking him to ensure the games would be labeled correctly, to minimize costs.

Some project creators write the actual cost of the game to their backers instead of the selling price, but here the content was labeled at $60. So, I was expecting to pay something like $12.6 in taxes. However, FedEx sent me a bill of EUR 42 (about $ 46 according to http://www.xe.com/currencyconverter/convert/?Amount=42&From=EUR&To=USD ). This is more than 50% of my pledge ! Courier services like FedEx and UPS are known for charging extremely high costs for the taxes. For overseas shipment, usually I prefer when USPS or FIMS (Fedex International Mail Service) are used, because they use the standard mail system. It might be slower, but we aren’t charged outreageous fees.

So my frustration number 2 is having to pay $46 in taxes to FedEx for… not getting my pledge.

I don’t know how to proceed from here, and I hope GTG can help me out. You may PM (or email) me.

  1. I would like to get what I pledged for (VivaJava and the Compounded Scientist promo), shipped using USPS, and labeled correctly.
  2. I would like to have a solution for the FedEx bill. I’m not happy to pay these costs, especially since I still don’t have my VivaJava…
  3. I would like to thank you for reading through this, and helping me through this unfortunate situation.

Thanks,
