I know it is too late for this on this print run, but for the future is there any chance of numbered hexes on the tiles and maps? 

The reason that I ask is that it makes it much easier to run play by email (or other types of remote) games as you can track the position of the characters by the number. For example "legacy moves from hex 001 to 005 and..."

This may be something not many people are interested in, but I thought I would throw it out there.
