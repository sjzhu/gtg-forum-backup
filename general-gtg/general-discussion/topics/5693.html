I don’t think that a promo is linked to a game/expansion. Otherwise, it would simply be part of that game/expansion.

I think you should rather see it as:

-Young legacy/Rook wrath were promos of the first kickstarter. Probably this second kickstarter is your last chance of getting one (first one costs 10 usd, second one 5).

-Ra, fanatic, omnitron, and baron blade will be given for free to all kickstarter backers (that pledge to the upgrade pack or any higher level). My guess is that if copies remain, they will be available either at the website, distributed at game conventions, … but this last one is a guess

-You can get Unity through kickstarter if you pledge an additional 5 usd. While copies remain it will also be sold at GTG website (price unaffected: 5$ + shipping). GTG has confirmed that she will never be part of an expansion

More importantly, up to now all promos were …well, promos. So GTG released the images and freely allowed fans to print their own copies. I do not know it the same will be true for Unity
