After getting my Infernal Relics expansion at PAX, I pulled the cards out, put them in the Enhanced box and just let the box sit next to Rook City and the original box for a while. I didn't want to throw them out, because I love the art on them, but I had no real use for them, either. As we all know, they're pretty terrible for actually holding cards. So, I came up with a solution. I drilled holes in the back of the box, put in some screws--held in place by nuts--and wrapped a short length of wire around the screws.

![Clipped Apostate's wing, too. Take that, guy.](upload://yOOxZSdPo31FQoMZGuQTxD3Wtva.png)

 

That way, I was able to hang them up on my wall with a certain amount of artful placement, just for fun.

![Rook City is farthest from the light. How appropriate.](upload://yOOxZSdPo31FQoMZGuQTxD3Wtva.png)

 

No longer taking up space, and I get to enjoy the art on them. Victory!
