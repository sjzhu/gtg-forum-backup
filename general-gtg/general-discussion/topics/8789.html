This thread is for people who have only one of the Rook City or the Infernal Relics expansion and are looking for someone else who has the other.

 

Since in the future the Rook City expansion and the Infernal Relics expansion will be published together in a single box, a pair of people who collectively have just one of each could collaborate to get the expansion they need using one of the following methods:

 

A) Buy and Divide -- The two people buy the combined box and then split the cards. One person or the other keeps the box.

B) Sell and Buy -- One person sells their existing expansion to the other and then buys the combined box.

 

Not sure if there's a need for a place like this, but in case there is, make a post saying what you have and how you'd like to collaborate (Buy and Divide or Sell and Buy) and hopefully you'll find someone willing to work with you.
