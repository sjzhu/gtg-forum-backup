#11

1) That is correct.

2) Since Tactics was released, the board game market has changed significantly. Going forward, Prime War products will include the miniatures in the box.

3) They are going to be somewhat different. Most notably, they will have a different sort of base and the sculpts will be better, more in line with the kind of miniatures we released with Fate of the Elder Gods.

4) I assume you mean easy to get the Sentinel Tactics miniatures? If so, yes! They are available for 50% off MSRP on our web store right now.

5) Before Prime War ships, we'll make an announcement that address changes are needed, and have you email us your new address.

6) The map tiles for sure, as well as the hex tokens for the characters (if you don't have the Flame of Freedom/Uprising miniatures).

7) We definitely hope to! We'll see how the connection is in the expo center; that's always the trouble with Gen Con streaming. The Letters Page and the like are all in attached hotels, so it is easier.

8) If you pre-ordered Broken City (without miniatures) and want to upgrade to a with-miniatures version, it will be the same price as if you had ordered miniatures with your Broken City preorder in the first place.

9) Possibly. As I mentioned earlier, the Tactics miniatures are already 50% off on our web store, and we intend to keep them there at that heavy discount.

10) Yes, but the details are a Christopher / Letters Page question.

11) Possibly at some point.

12) The gameplay is far ahead of the art, but he'll tease some once he has some to tease.

13) We'll go into more gameplay details at Gen Con and during our livestreams this fall.

Broader request, not necessarily tied to Prime War or any product.

More and more recently, I've been seeing a lot of comments on the forums to the effect of "Hey, where did <X> get announced?" "Oh, it was in a Letter's Page Episode/Christopher dropped a comment in the Patreon-exclusive Discord Chat/Check the Sentinels Subreddit/It was in a livestream." This poses a few problems:

1) Not everyone has the time/attention to follow every resource, and so it's likely announcements are going to slip through the cracks.

2) You rely almost entirely on word of mouth for information to get spread around, which has led to plenty of confusion and ire (see examples in the Sentinels RPG forums where people are still asking "wait, did the Kickstarter for the core book get delayed again? Where was that said and why?")

3) Over time, it leads to information getting lost in the ether due to fragmentation.

In an attempt to be polite, yet blunt, communication has been an issue that GTG has cited on several occasions that they need to improve on. And while some efforts have been made, it still needs iteration. 

 

This Prime War announcement was the first in the long time that used a) The GTG website, and b) An official post in the forums by someone in the GTG staff. And honestly, it's a nice breath of fresh air. Is there any chance you, Christopher, Maggie, or whomever can make some sort of commitment that, in the near future, there will be one centralized location to get announcments about GTG, their product line, and any changes therein?
