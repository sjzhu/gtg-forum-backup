So, we have a 4 hero team played by 1 hero, and it works quite well. I just relistened to the Pacific Rim Theme and I thought how cool will it eb to have a hero palyed by two people.

For those of you who never seen/heard of Pacific Rim, shame on you. 

Essentially, in a very very rough draft. Each player only has 2 cards each. and they can only play one per turn. But have cool ways to play the other half with toher cards or as a abse power.

Honestly, i took like 5 seconds to think this up and I really really like giant mecha. *prays-that-skyscraper-is-a-giant-mecha-but-know's-it's-probably-a size-changing-hero*
