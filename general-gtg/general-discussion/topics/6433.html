Played a game last night that featured AA, Unity, Haka, and Tempest vs. Akush'Bhuta in Pike Industries. Not the best environment for A'B due to its paucity of targets  (she actually milled both Mutagenic Growths while playing Limbs too), but that's randmoness for you. Besides, it was kind of thematic for a chaotic nature spirit to go all eco-teur on Pike's chemical factory.

Anyway, at one point we had a Vat Explosion flip up. I thought Unity's bots were gonna be toast – and she had quite a few, since we had the sweeping limb under control – although Stealth Bot was out so we could still save a couple. Then I realized that Bee Bot destroys an environment card when it gets destroyed…

So A'B took 3, then Bee Bot sucked up the whole explosion.

Two turns later this happened again.

Yeah, we won that one pretty handily. She never even flipped, although the Environment deck was exhausted on the turn we defeated her.
