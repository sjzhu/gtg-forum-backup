First off, I would like to apologize for missing February! I've been so busy that it completely slipped my mind, and I only realized that I had missed it last week. It is defnitely my intention and plan to do these every month, and I hope to avoid any more missed months this year. Anyhow! Here are the projects that we've been up to in the past two months!

  1. We approved the OblivAeon proofs.
  2. We sent over the pre-packing list for OblivAeon to the factory so that the ship out goes faster.
  3. We launched a Patreon to (hopefully) let us keep recording and releasing episodes of The Letters Page indefinitely!
  4. Alex fixed another set of bugs and inefficiencies with the web store.
  5. We started having Gen Con meetings. So, so many Gen Con meetings.
  6. We completed huge sections of the Core Rulebook for Sentinel Comics: The Roleplaying Game.
  7. Christopher and Adam moved all of the sticky notes from their office to another, larger room. They then covered every wall and table in that room with sticky notes, and then removed them all.
  8. Craig and Maggie attended and ran our booth at PAX South.
  9. Mara attended New York Toy Fair.
  10. Matt and Jodie filled our warehouse to 150% capacity, but then got it down to 105%.
  11. Matt and Jodie devised an algorithm to determine the number of pallet bays they could reasonably construct in a room of arbitrary dimensions with support pillars of various sizes spaced at various intervals.
  12. Christopher, Jodie, Matt, and I walked through the majority of the giant old warehouses currently for sale in the City of St. Louis.

Here is the status of all of our works in progress! Some of the production hasn't moved along as much as you might expect because all of the factories in China were closed for the month of February for the CNY.
