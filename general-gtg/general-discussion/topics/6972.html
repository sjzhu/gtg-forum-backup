This is my recipe for an ultra difficult Omnitron. Ingredients:

- 1 copy of "Omnitron, flipping machine"
-The start ingredient: a little bit of brain with a serious lack of reading comprehension skills. The key point is that your brain has to read this text in Omnitron:

"Reveal cards from the villain trash until 1 Drone or Component is revealed. Put it into play."

And decide that, actually, the text means this:

"Reveal cards from the villain trash until 1 Drone or Device is revealed. Put it into play."

- Add a couple of "Electro-pulse explosive" to the top of the villain deck.

- Shake well.

- Die quickly.

As a perfect aftermath, you can then reread Omnitron's card and realice you've been doing all wrong. Facepalm.
