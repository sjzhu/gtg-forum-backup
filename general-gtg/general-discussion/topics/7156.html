UXM266:

Does anybody know the figures offhand for the total number the Sentinels of the Multiverse has? This includes promos, expansions, alternate versions and Vengence?Regards

Up through Infernal Relics, there are 1104

Core - 578 (10 x 41 hero decks; 4 x 27 villain decks; 4 x 15 env decks)

Rook City - 222 (2 x 41 hero decks; 4 x 27 villain decks; 2 x 15 env decks; 2 extra cards for The Operative)

Infernal Relics - 228 (2 x41 hero decks; 4 x27 villain decks; 2 x 15 env decks; 8 extra cards for The Ennead's nine gods)

Mini Expansions - 68 (1 x 41 for Unity; 1 x 27 for Ambuscade)

Promos - 8 (Alt hero cards for Legacy, Wraith, Ra, Fanatic; Pairs of Alt villain cards for Blade, Omnitron)

 

For Shattered Timelines, there are another 326

Shattered Timelines - 220 (2 x 41 hero decks; 4 x 27 villain decks; 2 x 15 env decks)

Mini Expansions - 98 (1 x 41 for The Scholar; 1 x 27 for Miss Information; 2 x 15 for Silver Gulch, Final Wasteland)

Promos - 8 (Alt hero cards for Visionary, Freedom Six, Haka)

 

Vengeance I can't say for certain, as we don't know the exact makeup of what will be in it yet. My best guess is that there will be five hero decks, five villain decks and 2 environment decks, plus the alt card for Greatest Legacy. That would be (5 x 41; 5 x 27; 2 x 15; plus 1 for Alt Legacy) 371

 

Grand total would be 1801
