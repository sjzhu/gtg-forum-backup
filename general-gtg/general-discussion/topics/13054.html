Spoilers for all kinds of stuff hereafter, you have been warned!

 

 

 

 

 

 

The character designed was Akash'Darsha, Akash'Bhuta reawakened by the Oblivion Shards. Argent Adept is confirmed as being on the background Art of {oneof the tactics cards I can't remember the name of}. Story wise Akash'B is an avatar of Creation and Destruction, she wants to destroy so that new things can come up in it's place. Akash'D just wants to destroy.

Argent Adept is the first Virtuoso of the Void to face Akash more than once, every other time Akash has need centuaries to regain her power and be up and active again, and in the last battle before AA she killed the VotV, so AA has no teachings and is the least of the VotVs, so this is new territory for both of them.

Mechanically we currently have A HUGE Akash'D and 3 small ones, the huge ones take up 7 hexes and the smaller ones only 1 hex. Her mechanics revolve around a slow build, gain new types of tokens and changing the environment, raising elevation, adding lava and adding cover. Her three smaller forms are Rock, Magma and Arboreal.

There's going to be a part 4 scenario, one for each of the smaller ones and then one with the large and 3 smaller ones at once. The arboreal one flips tiles from Megalopolis to Insula, the Magma one is trying to gain power by walking in lava, and the rock one... I don't think was nailed down. The powers are 3 general ones and then 1 for each form, also scenario powers. All character panals have 3 power 'slots'

The smaller versions of Akash'D will be tournamnet legal and see can swap between forms 'somehow'.

Akash'D is planned to be an expansion by itself, with 2 version, painted and unpainted minis, no token version. Probably not a pre-order similar to the last one, just a boring old regular pre-order.

 

There will be a character who can generate portals in Tactics!

 

Art was made for 3 of the power cards, Rock Armour featuring the (all new, all differnt) Ennead, "get whacked by a tree" (with a "new" character) and "lava power I can't remember the name of" with just Akash'D on it.

 

We saw Adam create a new character, The Naturalist, standing upright like a human but with all beastial features, as if his powers had warped and he had little control over it. Different parts of animals were all over his body. He is confirmed for tactics, but will not be called the Naturalist. Primary guess in the stream was Chimera, secondary the Unnaturalist. There were others and Christopher said we got the name, no confirmation on which.

The Prime Wardens will be in Tactics! No specific characters were talked about so the roster could change.

 

 

Taco Bell was eaten.

Setback and Tachyon love Taco Bell, Expat likes it but also likes MRE rations soooooo...

Legacy prefers hotdogs and cheeseburgers to taco Bell, but he really loves his wife's Meatloaf, or even better his mothers. His wife is not a fantastic cook, she is too busy (we do not know doing what). But we do know her name now, Nancy Parsons!

The first Letters' Page will be all about Legacy, it will be recorded at some point between 6 weeks from now and the end of the year, so yay!

Oblivion shards are important, but they are not the most important thing.

All the Atlanteans are dead, they will not feature anywhere except as the attached pic.

Writhe is wearing a domino mask, like the other Sentinels, you just can't see it!

More characters from the past will be seen (like GI Bunker but not from that era), maybe even from an alternative past!

A question was asked about when The Scholar would be in Tactics, Adam said "i can answer that one" and then there was laughter, so it would appear (to my mind) the answer is never.

The next big character payoff (like the emotional beat of Legacy building to Iron Legacy) will be... all of them. With some having bigger parts than others.

 

Luther did a Q&A in the chat while Taco Bell was being eaten but I forgot to grab it, the only thing I remember is that the characters he is most excited for next is Catastrophe and Verge.

Oh! Renegade is like Doctor Who with all his want for justice with none of his compunctions about violence. I could not want this character more now!
