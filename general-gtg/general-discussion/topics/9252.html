I got the Enhanced Edition and  Shattered Timelines for my birthday this week. I sat down tonight and played a solo game to get a feel for things before I tried to recruit some other players.

 

I decided to keep it simple my first time so I took Ra, Legacy and Tempst against Baron Blade in Megolapolis.

 

I rolled him pretty easily. Once he flipped, he started dealing some heavy damage to my characters. But Legacy combined with equipment had Ra dealing at least 6 damage a turn. Tempest also chipped in and I was never in any real danger of losing. I think Legacy was down to 18 HP, Ra had 16 and Tempest had 14. I really dug using Legacy which surprised me. I'm not usually into buffing players but you could just see the impact he was having on the game as he started adding some ongoings. Tempest had a bad draw and was the least useful. Flash Flood did come in handy and he was helping to reduce some of the big damage that Blade was dealing out at the end. Ra was really fun too. 

 

Overall, I loved the game. I really liked the pace and the last couple of turns were awesome as the damage was just flying all over the place. I can't wait to get the other expansions and really dig into this game further.
