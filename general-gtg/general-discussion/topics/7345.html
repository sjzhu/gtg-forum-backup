Usually I try not to share my battle stories, but this one is a bit different. First, the setup.

Villain: Citizen Dawn

Environment: Ruins of Atlantis

Heroes: Bunker, Absolute Zero, Legacy, The Wraith and Tachyon

This was the first time playing with this specific group of people for my wife and I. The game had been going fairly well, our Wraith (my wife) had been using Infrared to make sure we didn't get a Devastating Aurora out to clear the board. CD was already invulnerable and we were waiting to get out enough Citizens to flip her back. Upon flipping her back, we had prepped my (Absolute Zero) equipment to dish out some major pain, Bunker's omnicannon was at 28 damage and Tachyon had every burst card from her deck in the graveyard. Suffice to say, when she flipped she was going to die.

Out comes CD's card that pulls out the Citizens from the discard pile. I clear up the unnecessary citizens and we wait for our environment turn. On the environment turn...out comes a Kraken. The Kraken smacks the lowest citizen, killing her. We drew another Citizen that turn, but our real delima was coming up. When the turn came back around to me, I was going to automatically generate four cold damage to all non-hero targets immediately. This would clear the board of Citizens again. Before me was Legacy and Bunker, neither of which could destroy my Ongoing. It was looking very grim for us as we were soon to get an Aurora and we might as well be hosed after all the setup.

My wife then pipes up. "Why not just kill him? Use the omnicannon on Absolute Zero." 

And so they did. I was sitting at 24 HP and our Bunker used Omnicannon for 28 damage. This obviously stopped my Ongoing card from going off and clearing the Citizens and allowed us the opportunity to get Dawn flipped again to finish her off with Tachyon. 

My wife had our team kill me.
