I play SotM solitaire. Ihaven't yet fought all the villains, but am getting there!

Grand Warlord Voss on the Wagner Mars Base was quite fun. The narrative was exciting. I grew up a Kirby fan and reading the Avengers and Legion of Super-Heroes. The game I played against Voss brought back memories of those comics' galactic tales (Kree/Skrull, Thanos, Darkseid vs LoSH, etc.). I'd played on Wagner before, but had not experienced the self-destruct countdown card.  In this game, I pulled it up and was floored with its creativity.

 

I'm in love with this game for its comic book roots, ability to play solo, strong narrative, and creative design. A big THANK YOU to the designers for creating such a fun experience!
