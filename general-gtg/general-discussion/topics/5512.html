""ketigid"":

Off topic a bit, will you give me a list of Legacy’s cards, using your interpretation, that affects both Legacys? Only 1 Legacy? I’m interested in running a 2 Legacy game but not quite sure about the cards effects based on previous discussions here on the forum.

Generally, we decided to play that anything that mentions Legacy refers to both Legacies. This is pretty overpowered, which is why maybe it’s not really a legit way to play. On the other hand, anything where Legacy dealt damage tended to only happen by the current Legacy playing.

We didn’t see every card on Tue, but the following is my current assessment of how to play each of Legacy’s cards:

No mention of Legacy, so no decision needs to be made:

[list]
[]Bolster Allies[/]
[]Inspiring Presence[/]
[]The Legacy Ring[/][/list]

Attack taken by and/or targeted to current Legacy only:

[list]
[]Back-Fist Strike[/]
[]Flying Smash[/]
[]Heroic Interception[/]
[]Motivational Charge[/]
[]Take Down[/]
[]Thok[/][/list]

Modifer that affects both Legacies:

[list]
[]Danger Sense[/]
[]Fortitude[/]
[]Lead from the Front[/]
[]Next Evolution[/]
[]Superhuman Durability[/]
[]Surge of Strength[/][/list]

A less broken way to play both of them would be to consider them completely separately, such that they could both play The Legacy Ring, but they wouldn’t both be strong just because one of them played Surge of Strength. My main problem with doing such is that thematically is seems wrong for there to be more than one Legacy Ring in play at a time.

Quick clarification: If something was Limited, we declared it Limited for both Legacies. This prevented us from getting out two Inspiring Presences, for example.

Really, I think we mostly obtained the flawless victory because we got very, very lucky in the cards.
