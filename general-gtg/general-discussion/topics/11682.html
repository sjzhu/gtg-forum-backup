I don't know how things work over the pond, but the thing that would concern me about it would be checking all the necessary child protection boxes - over here you can't just walk into a hospital or school with a bunch of people and some games and interact with children.

I needed a Criminal Records Bureau (CRB) check (which can take weeks to process) and had to attend child protection training and fill in a lengthy form to sing carols for a couple of hours with children in a hospital with parents, hospital staff, other members of my choir, and my choral director and his wife who are both teachers. Same for teaching children opera and African music.

This stuff's just not obvious to anyone who hasn't done official volunteering, so I thought I'd point out in case - apologies if you already know about it or this isn't relevant for the US!
