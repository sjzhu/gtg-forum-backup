So, I was having issues storing my Sentinels, when I had an idea to make a box that could fit all of it, from EE to Vengeance and about 3 expansions past it. (that was a grammatical cluster****. I don't care.)

 

So, basically, I wanted to show off my awesome box that I customized to fit Sentinels.

Here is the inside of the box.

I started with a giant sized card box (5 rows) then did a little surgery on 2 of the rows, to make room for my dice and tokens and the giant cards, which I have sleeved in 4x6 toploaders. According to my measurements, I should have just about enough room for all of the cards that are likely to come out, and a few more.

Here's the outside.

My main problem was keeping the lid on, since it's not very deep, so I fed a couple belts through the slots on the side, which can easily be adjusted to take the box off, and keep it on sturdily in the mean time.

 

So, yeah. I've heard a lot of questions on how to store the game, and I've heard many suggestions, but this was my solution. Hopefully you'll gain something from it. Also, tell me if the links don't work.
