Well, when playing Sentinels for a while, with the same heroes, and the same villains, some folks feel a bit burned out on doing the same thing.  And with the 2nd expansion soon in hand, we'll be able to get our energy back.  While in the mean time, me and my friends have been developing Video game-themed decks.  As the usual DM, I'm in the mest of creating a Villain Deck, from scratch, with it's own playstyle, that goes along with the Villain, who's based off of a popular videogame character.  My friends are each creating their own Hero deck, from different generes, again, with their own playstyles and powers.  I'm just about half way done with the concept and developing, all there is left is to playtest with my friends, and will soon showcase our efforts in the future.  It's fan-made, so not trying to claim original characters, just using them as themes.  Hopefully, it works out great, and will give folks something interesting to try if they want.
