Greetings awesome people!

Now that PAX East is over, we are looking for volunteers for Gen Con and PAX Prime! We are offering the same deal we have in the past:

What you do: Volunteer to teach games in our booth for half of the expo hall hours. Shifts run from 10-2 and from 2-6, so that means 4 shifts at Gen Con and PAX Prime.

What we do: Get you a free Exhibitor badge, 2 free t-shirts, and an invitation to the GTG Volunteer Dinner the evening before the show (Wednesday for Gen Con, Thursday for PAX).

If you are interested, send an email to me (paul@greaterthangames.com). We have a limited number of slots, so let me know as soon as you are certain you can make it!

Thanks!
