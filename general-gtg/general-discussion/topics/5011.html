Just wanted to say that I love Sentinels of the Multiverse. Within the first week I was playing it up to five times a day. Have became quite fond of Ra, Tempest, Absolute Zero and Bunker, not sute which one is quite my favorite since each one has their unique playing style. All the heroes also have a uniqueness to them but just haven’t got my attention yet. And then the villians, the first few times against Baron Blade I was beginning to think of him as a chump but then he got the right cards at the right time and it sure showed me not to under estimate him anymore. Citizen Dawn and Grand Warlord Voss are probably the toughest individuals to go up against, only beating Voss a total of twice and Dawn once. Omnitron too is a force to be reakoned with when him playing two cards each round, inless of course no components or drones have made their way to the trash. Haven’t had a chance to try out the advanced rules yet, mostly can only get enough for a group of three and that provides enough difficulties as is.And teamwork is a necessity after I played a few solo games and found out it does make it easier building strategies using all heroes rather then working solo. Now just have to transfer that into when playing with others. The quotes from the “comics” are also a very nice touch, adding more personality to the characters.

All in all, incredible game and totally pumped to see some expansions. Maybe the hippo costumed guy from Haka’s “Savage Mana” card? Or maybe it will be the lacky of the possible nemesis of Haka? I guess only time will tell, hopefully long before December 2012 so I can have some time to enjoy it.
