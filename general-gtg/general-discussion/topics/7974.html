I played though a game last night of Unity VS Mr. Fixer in the Final Wasteland.  It went REALLY quick, but it was satisfying.  It looks like it might be a great substitute for when you don't have a whole lot of time, but still want something to wrap something up.  

The enviroment deck usually adds a sort of timer to the game. It gives the players an incentive to not want to NOT be the hero with the lowest health. The only real modification the game needs is mentaly replacing the world "villian" on all the hero cards with "opponent."  

 

One on One it's a very quick game (which is what I tested) and seems far more dominated by the luck of the draw.  Two VS Two would be intruging.  One VS One VS One would be rather interesting too.
