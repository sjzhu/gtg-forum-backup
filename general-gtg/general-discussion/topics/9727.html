What I love about their world is that they show so much love to the every kind of comic book style, every character is either a loving homage to a popular character or a complete. The tongue-in-cheek humor, the shout-outs, and charm of the characters make the game so intriguing. 

To me the universe is more of a love letter to 75ish years of work done by 1000s of creators. I especially love that how much time was planned out since the original release those years ago!
