Generally, there is a post-kickstarter questionnaire sent out that asks you what specific items you want.  So you can increase the amount pledged, and then specify afterwards that it was for X shirt, Y pin, and Z hat.  They have to ask us, since they'll need to know about things like sizes.

They may also have a pledge manager afterwards that guides you through the process and gives you the option of buying add-ons.

Right now, just increase the amount to cover whatever you want.  For instance, add 22$ to your current pledge if you want a Mr. Fixer hat.  Then, after the kickstarter is over, you'll be able to get more detailed about the specifics.
