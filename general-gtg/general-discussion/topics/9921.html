There are some people who just don't care for the way Sentinels plays.  It can be frustratingly random at times; deep strategy players won't care for the way things can turn out, and may feel that the game (or at least some heroes) won't give them enough choices.  This is totally valid.  My last non-solo game featured not one but two players who, while they thought the game was okay, weren't big fans of it.  One spent the entire game bemusedly saying he couldn't get anything to work right and we were all going to die (which it turns out we did, though it was not inevitable, just a spot of bad luck).  The other one played a character picked more or less at random, appeared more than a little bored, and was less than amused when he got abruptly killed by The Grave Beckons just as he and the other survivor were stabilizing.  It's an excellent game for certain sets of values; not all players will share those.  I value the immense variety and replayability, but someone whose favorite game is, say, Chess probably won't go for SOTM.
