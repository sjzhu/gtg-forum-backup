Via 3-way Skype I and a nephew each in Colorado and Connecticut defeated Blade as Young Legacy, Bunker, and The Visionary today.

It’s harder to play over video than face to face, as each site has to manage cards and counters for the environment and the villain, or go without having accurate information. It’s also a bit frustrating to deal with the occasional lag or difficulty making out with what other’s are saying. While not ideal, however, it worked well enough for us to have a good time.
