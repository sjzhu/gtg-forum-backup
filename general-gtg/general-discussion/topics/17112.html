And, we're back! I apologize for the lack of a post last month; it was one of the things that unfortunately had to be triaged out during the massive pre-Gen Con crush of 80hr work weeks. Makes sense.  The RPG market hasn't been consistent about what books go in the "core" product and what go in supplements, and how many books the "core" product consists of.  Some games use the D&D approach of Player's Guide and GM's Guide, then Monsters/Enemies and Settings as supplements.  Some lump the Monsters/Enemies stuff into the GM book, and some lump the Settings stuff into the Players book.  Some just make one massive $100 book.

I would imagine that, given how crucial the setting is to the Sentinels game, and how focused it is on major enemies and disposable mooks and not random encounters with widely varying and fiddly monsters, that there won't be a setting or a "monster" book.  But I'd guess, with not much confidence, that there will be a separate GM book that will include how to develop and manage villains, other enemies, and environments, as well as campaign structure and rewards.

Unfortunately, I'd also guess that "weird stuff" that isn't part of the core Sentinels experience, like magic, will probably be in supplements.  I know a lot of people want to create Virtuosos of the Void, but I'm guessing that the core book will enable players to create just the hero types from the core SotM game and maybe a handful of others (non-super characters like Expat and Fixer).  But I wouldn't be surprised if they named and mocked up a magic supplement to wave at us for the Kickstarter, because that would help onboard a bunch of fans.

My imagnation on the book has been that it will be a big book similar to what I've seen for FATE or Star Wars, "everything you need to start goes here" and then the following products will narrow down on locations or specific events.

 

I feel like magic and heroes like Expat or Fixer are going to be possible with the base book. As I understand the system it is incredibly modular, with the specific things like "motivational charge" or "Lightspeed Barrage" being pre-made mods to the existing four or five actions in the game.

 

I'm not sure how many examples they will havem or how they will advise building these abilities, that's going to be the biggest kicker in how this game goes over in my opinion, but if the difference between the abilities is whether you attack with the highest and hinder with the lowest or vice versa, then saying your power source is magic vs guns vs martial arts vs flames isn't a huge deal. And that gets covered before you get to that point of the character creation, as I understand it.

 

Honestly, as excited as I am for the game, seeing and understanding character creation is going to be a massive challenge, in my experience. It's one of the reasons I'm twitchy about running everything, because as the DM/GM/ whatever you call it, you need to be the one who can answer most of the questions. And character creation is almost always the hardest part of the game.
