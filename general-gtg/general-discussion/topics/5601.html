Hey, just bought the game and expansion at PAX EAST, after a fun demo using Legacy.

I’m running a few games this Saturday, so I have not had a chance to do an official game yet, but I’m really looking forward to it.

That said opening a massive box of cards is a little maddening, and I’m still trying to decide exactly how to organize it all.

So I was wondering: how about for future “small” expansions putting out deck boxes? I dont just mean like a big box with a ton of tinnier boxes in it… but I mean micro expansions.

Something like a “Nemesis Pack” where you could have (as generic as possible) Captain Good (41 card deck) vs Major Evil (22 card villain deck) in a nice old fasioned cardboard deck box. Seems like a cheap and fun way to keep expanding the universe, and easier to store.
