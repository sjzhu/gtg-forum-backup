Wow are things busy. As predicted, more and more things are happening in an overlapping sequence of events that threatens to descend into chaos. On top of all this, we are doing a bit of hiring and reorganization here at Greater Than Games, which will help us handle the increasing load of projects and tasks in the medium and long term, but which adds to them in the short term! Here's all of the personelle stuff that has been going on:

  1. Mara, our Sales & Marketing Director, has taken advantage of an awesome opportunity with AEG and will be leaving us in July.
  2. After analysing our current workload and workload growth, we've decided to split the Marketing and Sales aspects of Mara's job among multiple people going forward.
  3. To that end, Maggie, our Social Media Manager, has been promoted to Marketing Manager! She has a strong marketing background and has been doing a fantastic job so far with all of those tasks.
  4. To help take some livestreaming-related tasks off of Maggie's plate now that she has more responsabilities, we have hired Trevor as a full time videographer! We've been working with Trevor as a contractor for years, but he'll be moving to full time now which is quite exciting.
  5. to take on the sales aspects of Mara's job, we are currently in the middle of the hiring progress for a new Inside Sales Manager to manage all of the interfacing with distributors throughout the world.
  6. For the past several months, we have also been in the process of transitioning Alex from a primarily warehouse-focused role to working more and more with other publishers for whom we provide warehousing and fulfillment services.
  7. To help with Christopher's thru-put on projects, we have hired Chris Burton (Braitwhite on the forum!) to be a Playtesting Manager and help speed up development time on all of our games.
  8. To help with the increased volume of games as well as marketing material that we need to create, we have hired Sarah Kelly as a new graphic designer to work with Jenn and SaRae. She started today!
  9. To help with my workload and help make sure that I keep up on all of my reporting and analysis tasks, we have hired Twuana Price as an Operations Manager. She starts on Monday!

As you can imagine, all of that added onto all of the conventions, upcoming Kickstarters, and OblivAeon release makes things a bit hectic. In addition to the above, here's some things we achieved in the past month:

  1. Craig organized the initial schedule and room layout for Citizens at Gen Con and Origins.
  2. Craig and I came up with some new training ideas for the Citizen program.
  3. Craig didn't attend ACD Games Day, but he spent the time organizing UK Games Expo citizens while Maggie, Jodie, Matt, and I all attended.
  4. Craig attended UK Games Expo with me, Christopher, Jenn, and Maggie, as well as Dave Chalker, one of the Designers of the SC RPG.
  5. Craig managed the UKGE booth, where we sold out of a ton of Spirit Island, and SC:RPG starter kits, and all around had a great experience.
  6. Craig, Maggie, and I worked out a few more key organizational details for our Gen Con booth.
