Presumably the five villains will each have a deck of their own, although possibly not the full 25.  But even if there was only a single 25-card villain deck, that plus two Environments and five 40-card Heroes totals 255 non-character cards (and seven character ones), compared to 210 (and ten characters) for the $20 expansions.  If all of the villains have full-size decks, that adds another 100 cards (plus eight more characters), and it's no longer remotely close.  The EE is $40, and it contains 500+ cards, but its price was probably low-rolled a little bit for the sake of getting people into the game; the expansions probably have a higher profit margin so that they don't need to sell in comparable volume.
