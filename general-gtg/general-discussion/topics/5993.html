Now, I don’t know if anyone knows about TV Tropes, but here goes.

Basically, it is a database/wiki/thing that is used to categorize things in the media – as well as certain “building blocks,” or tropes – by identifying characteristics that allow it to be compared and contrasted with other media.

I’m creating a Sentinels TVTropes page, but I need help. If any of you feel like helping, take a little while to familiarize yourself with the site (tvtropes.org) and then help me make the page better. http://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/SentinelsOfTheMultiverse
