It is a new year! We are wrapping up a lot of old projects, while reaching the middle of a bunch of others! Here's what we accomplished in December:

  1. Lots of going back and forth with the printer on OblivAeon proofs (there are so many cards!)
  2. We had a week-long intesive development session on Prime War.
  3. Maggie, our new Social Media Manager, started!
  4. We fixed a bunch of terrible bugs with our new web store.
  5. We had our annual GTG Employee Holiday Party.
  6. We wrote and edited more sections of the RPG core rulebook
  7. Christopher and Adam covered the walls of their office with sticky notes, which is a sign either of work getting done or impending madness.

Here is the status of all of our works in progress!

In Stock Since December

  1. Nothing!

Shipping to Backers

  1. Nothing!

  1. Spirit Island reprint (yes, another one)
  2. Spirit Island: Branch & Claw reprint
  3. Various SotM expansion reprints

Completed Mass Production; In China

  1. SotM: Stuntman, Benchmark, & Void Guard
  2. SotM: Complete Hero Variant Collection
  3. SotM: Ultimate Collector's Case

In Mass Production

  1. SotM: 5th Anniversary Foil Villain Collection
  2. Spirit Island: Branch & Claw reprint
  3. SotM: OblivAeon
  4. The giant OblivAeon KS shipping cartons.
  5. Compounded: Geiger reprint
  6. Compounded reprint
  7. Bottom of the 9th reprint

At Factory; Pre-Press Work

  1. New Bedford reprint

Current WIP Focus by GTG

  1. Sentinel Comics: The Role Playing Game core rulebook (Christopher, Jenn, Critical Hits)
  2. Prime War (Christopher)
  3. Sentinels of Earth Prime (Christopher)
  4. Legend of Sleepy Hollow (Chris Kirkman + Jenn)
  5. A new and improved website (Jenn, Paul, Joel, etc)
  6. A cool Sentinel Comics related thing for 2018

I'll see you next month!
