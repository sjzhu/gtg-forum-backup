#11

Iron Legacy is quite possibly the single hardest villain to beat in the game. Sometimes I've played games where he takes each hero down to half health in the first villain turn.

 

Miss Information is not a 2; no way in hell. She's at least a solid 3.

 

Kismet is a low 2, but with the right combination of cards (Karmic Disjunction, Lady Luck, and Inconceivable Obstruction) she can pack quite a wallop.

 

La Capitan can be pretty hard. Her crew is all very powerful and annoying, and especially on advanced she takes a while to whittle her health down.

 

Dreamer is probably a 3. Some of her projections are downright brutal, and you can't even START to win until you clear the board, which can take a while with certain setups.
