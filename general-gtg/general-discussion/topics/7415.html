I have an intersting relationship with that set, I consider the villains and enviroments to be punishingly tough, but in terms of flavor I love the set and I dont know what the artist would think but I consider the best set in terms of artistic experimentation. There are several nods to Frank Miller in the art, Mr. Fixer has those cool "style" cards and every card back has a lot of flavor, like the tabloid cover for Plague Rat or the dossier on Spite.

 

As for Spite he is a real challenge that requires a lot of teamwork and control, and his whole victim angle is really interesting.
