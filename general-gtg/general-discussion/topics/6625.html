I posted a couple of pix to my randomizer site last night, and looking over the list of all heroes in the game, I noticed a trend.  If you look at the hp of the original heroes, you'll see plenty of 30s and many high-20s. Rook City heroes were still relatively robust as well.  Argent Adept seems to be the first victim to whatever is slowly killing the heroes though, because his hp is a low 24, and the trend worsens the closer we get to the shattered future:

Omnitron-X: 25 hp. Dark Visionary: 25 hp.  Freedom Tempest, Price of Freedom Wraith, Golem Unity, all 25 hp.  If the trend continues, Chrono-Ranger will barely be able to stand!

In all seriousness, I'm assuming that the hp have been dropping as the base powers have been getting better, which isn't a bad thing.  Or maybe over time, the game designers have decided that the starting heroes had too many hp?  I wonder if we'll ever see any true 30+hp tanks anymore.
