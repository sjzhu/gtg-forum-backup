Yeah, that's a bummer, man

Same thing happened to me when I was adjusting my pledge, but I noticed it (and wasn't backing at a limited level, so it doesn't matter). The problem, I believe, is that the whole area with the description in it is an active element, rather than just the radio button. Therefore, if you click on the window to get control in order to, for example, scroll up and down in your browser, you will have inadvertently changed your reward level. The same thing happens when doing it on a tablet - a stray finger or a lazy swipe can accidentally switch the radio button.
