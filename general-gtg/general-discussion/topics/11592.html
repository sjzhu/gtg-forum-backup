GTG just tweeted about this, so I wanted to post it here for the non-Twitter folks:
 
Fantasy writer Patrick Rothfuss (author of the excellent Kingkiller Chronicles) runs an annual charity drive called Worldbuilders in which he gets lots of authors, artists, and game publishers to donate items which the fund drive either sells, auctions, or raffles off with the proceeds going to support Heifer International.
 
It appears that Greater Than Games has gotten into the WorldBuilders fundraiser and has donated a set of limited edition Sentinels of the Multiverse promo cards to the fund drive.  They're up for sale now at the Woldbuilders' Tinker's Packs site.  
 
It's a clever bit of partnering actually.  The hero of Rothfuss' Kingkiller Chronicles is Kvothe, a mystic and the greatest lute player in the fantasy world in which the series is set, so it's only natural that Kvothe becomes an alternate version of the Argent Adept.  Photos of the card, showing AA's new power, can be seen at the above website.
 
Rumor has it that there are only 150 of these cards, so move quick if you want one and as the money goes to a good charity too, that's a bonus!
