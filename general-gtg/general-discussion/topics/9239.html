Lost in Time is an alternative lose condition. It gives the heroes a ticking clock to deal with as well as the villains.

When it enters play, you discard the top two cards of the environment deck. Then, every start of the environment turn you take the top card in the discard and return it under the environment deck.

if, at the end of the Environment turn, you have no cards left in the environment deck, the protal to the past is closed and the heroes are trapped in the American West forever. This is the equivalent of losing.

It's similar to the Self-Destruct Sequence in Wagner.

The mechanic also includes a "mixed blessing" in terms of the Explosives Wagons.  They provide a flat +1 to all damage buff (to all sides), though you may have to end up destroying them to keep the portal open, or by accident (i.e. effects that deal damage to all targets or all non-hero/non-villain targets).  If brought to 0 hp, they explode, dealing tons of damage to everything in the game!  Ouch!
