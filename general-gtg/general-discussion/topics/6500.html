Enhanced edition = 10 heroes * 41 cards + 4 villains * 27 cards + 4 env * 15 cards = 578 cards

Core = same as Enhanced edition, 578 (mostly the same) cards

Rook City = 2 heroes * 41 cards + 4 villains * 27 cards + 2 env * 15 cards + Operative card = 221 cards

Infernal Relics = 2 heroes * 41 + 3 villains * 27 cards + 2 env * 15 cards + 35 Ennead cards = 228 cards

Promos = 1 hero * 41 cards, 1 villain * 27 cards, 4 hero promo cards, 4 promo villain cards = 76 cards

That makes 578 + (578 +) 221 + 228 + 76 = 1103 cards (1681 cards)

Also, 16 oversized villain cards have been released.
