It’s up to you, really. The designers really didn’t focus too much on balancing (except when they noticed something broken during play testing). More they wanted to focus on making the cards play the way they wanted the character to act. E.g. Tachyon is very fast, so her deck is designed to play multiple cards at once; Absolute Zero has a fire/ice thing going on, so cards are designed to allow reacting to fire and cold damage; Legacy is a leader looking out for his team, so he’s designed to help other heroes by increasing the damage they deal or decreasing the damage they take, etc. Basically, create a hero, give him a style, create cards for him that follow that style, test them to see if they are too broken, and, finally, DO NOT POST THEM HERE!! You can if you want, but it will likely end in your posts being deleted (I know first hand… poor Phoenix).
