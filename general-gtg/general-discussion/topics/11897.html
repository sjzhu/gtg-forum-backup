Email from Paul below...

"Greetings!

 
I would like to apologize to all of you for the delay in shipping Wrath of the Cosmos preorders to EU backers.
 
For the past several months, we have been working with FedEx and some European shipping companies to set up a more efficient and cost-effective method of importing and sending games to the EU. The simplified version of how this will work is that we will ship everything to the EU in bulk, pay taxes and import duties once at the point of entry, and then have the individual parcels sent out throughout Europe. This will ensure that all of you receive your items without having to pay any taxes or duties.
 
One of the key parts of this setup is that we will be handling EU customs clearance ourselves instead of relying on FedEx or another company, which means that we need a VAT number somewhere in the EU. Because of how we are setting this up, it happens that we are getting a VAT number in France. We have been waiting for this number for months, with regular assurances that we would be getting it by the end of 2014. As you might have guessed, we still do not have a VAT number. As of Monday I was told that we would have it "by the end of next week".
 
In summary, in order to ship these orders EU friendly, they are delayed until we receive a VAT number from the French government. As soon as we do, we will be sending them out. I definitely realize that this is frustrating. There is nothing we can do to speed it up at this point, but we have decided that, as a small token of our appreciation for your patience with this order, for our next pre-order shipment we will ship to EU backers before anyone else, including US customers.
 
I apologize again for the delay, and will keep you all informed as more information comes in. At this point, I anticipate sending your orders within the next two weeks."
