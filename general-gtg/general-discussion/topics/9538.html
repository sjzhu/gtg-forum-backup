Well, here's a sample storage solution for sleeved cards that is less fancy, but seems to be working for me.  Attached some pictures as well.

Base Storage box is a plastic Sterilite file folder box.  I picked up the pictured one in a Target store for $10.  Sufficient depth and width (barely) as will be shown, and the lid has some storage as well.

(See Attachment 1)

The bottom of the storage box fits the Rook City/Infernal Relics expansion box.  The RC/IR is wide enough to fit cards sleeved with Dragonshields - the EE box is not.  Therefore, the RC/IR box holds all the villain decks and all the environment decks but four.

(See Attachment 2)

On top of the RC/IR box go two layers of smaller boxes - 12 boxes per layer.  These are empty Dragonshield boxes being repurposed for each holding a hero deck.  Plus one box holding four environment decks.  There is enough room on the top left for a small notebook to lie flat as well.

There is also a large manila envelope that slips down one edge of the box.  In this are the rules pamphlets from EE, RC/IR, and Vengeance.  Plus the over-sized villain cards and the hero divider cards.  I use the last with new players as a way to introduce the various heroes to them.

(See Attachment 3)

Last of all, the clear plastic flip-top storage on the box is used to hold status, markers, HP tokens, and a few miscellaneous items like extra dividers.

(See Attachment 4)
