So I got my copy of Vengeance (Hooray!) and I got to try a game against 4 out of 5 Villians (poor Proletariat got the short straw) as well as trying out all the new heroes (accept The Sentinels) when something occurred to me.

 

The Turn Order Chart isn't newb friendly.

 

Now I know what you're thinking. "TEGRE WHAT'S WRONG WITH YOU! WHY U TRY TO TROLL US!" but hear me out.

I pick up the manual and I set up my table. I also am very familiar on how to play (Well I should be considering this is expansion number 4) However the anal retentive side wants to make sure there is some sort of timing chart.  You know. When playing regular Sentinels it's the whole:

Start of Villian Turn do start of turn events, Draw Villian card and play it, End of Villian Turn and do end of Turn effects.  

Followed with Start of Hero Turn, Play Card, Play Power, Draw Hero Card, End of Hero Turn

Than it's the Start of Environment Turn, Play a card, End of Environment Turn.
 
Ok so we all know this (and by we I mean all of you who started with the Core set and expanded from there.)  However it occurred to me if I were to give this to a friend as a gift, who has never played, and he or she was just as anal retentive...at most they'd be lost unless they called me up and asked me... how to play.  If someone where to pick this up...they don't have a specific page to refer to.  They at least know it's Villian 1, than Hero 1, Villian 2, than Hero 2 and so on...but not exactly how to play.  When reading just the Vengeance Manual alone...what exactly constitues a Villian and Hero (and yes an Environment turn?)  Yes I know they could come here to the forums or even the Greater than Games website...but that's not my point.
 
 
I know it may seem like common knowledge, but I'm just pointing out as someone who were to pick this up by itself...they wouldn't have a clear picture of how all the moving parts are supposed to move. Am I mistaken in thinking this?
 
 
I think the only reason why this comes to mind is that it's been stated as being a stand-alone expansion.  While I can see it, I'm wondering if someone new would be able to see it.

My first Vengeance game was a bit of a chore, even with two other experienced SotM players.  The reason is that in a regular game, there's usually one person (me) who handles the villain and environment in addition to their hero.  But with Vengeance, each player has a villain to manage, and my players weren't mentally prepared for that.  They don't have the habit of checking for interactions between villain cards and tracking all the minion hp and whatnot.  It ended up working out fine, but it was slow going.

Of course, playing all-new heroes against a super-complex all-new villain for our first Vengeance game probably didn't help either.  In retrospect, a game with new heroes against old villains would have been a better warmup, followed by a game with old heroes against the new villains.

One other thing that didn't help, I'm still waiting for the rest of the Vengeance art, so I don't have accessories completed for most of this set, which meant that we were using just the cards and token that come with the game, no custom oversized stuff.  I don't know how you people do it!  The hero cards are so tiny!  The oversized villain cards were better than the old two-card models, but they're still slow to scan during a game.  And keeping track of hp by shlepping tokens around and making change everytime someone gets poked?  Fuggetaboutit!  I can't wait until I can finish my Vengeance set of accessories and play a real game again. :)

Even with all of that though, my wife announced after the one game that she has a new favorite hero in The Sentinels.  We still had fun. :)
