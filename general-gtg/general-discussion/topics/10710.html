So just so everyone knows, the voice of Baron Blade was directed by Christopher himself, and he said "I think that's pretty much as close as anyone could get who isn't actually baron blade himself". :)

regarding the name - we also had a similar discussion about "digital game" vs "digital board game" vs "video game" and we landed on "The Video Game" mainly as a way to differentiate it from "The Card Game". Any game that is played on a computer is a video game as far as I'm concerned but really, when you are playing it on your tablet, you are playing "Sentinels of the Multiverse" period, full stop.

When we work with GtG to come up with the next great adventure in the multiverse (finger's crossed), maybe it will follow the "Sentinel Tactics" naming convention and have some other title entirely.
