I would think you could do a little better. For example:

First guess: 1-1-1-1 = 1/0

You know exactly one of them is a 1. You'll never have four 2s. You could carry one of the 1s over to see if you can get a correct placement.

Second guess: 1-2-2-2 = 2/0 (the 1 is in the correct place and there is one 2; next guess is 1-2-3-3)

or

Second guess: 1-2-2-2 = 0/1 (the 1 is in the wrong place and there are no 2s; next guess is 3-1-3-3)

This would get you some more information about placement and still give you the quantity information you were originally getting.

Matchstickman:

To divert slightly, is the 'first best five guesses' in a d6 mastermind game going to be

1111,2222,3333,4444,5555

I always lead 1111 and then try a variation on the above - i there is one 1, my next guess would be 1222. I know there can't be four 2's and you can get a lot of position information from that guess: 

  1. One right, wrong position: 1 is not first, no 2's
  2. One right, right position: 1 is first, no 2's
  3. Two right, wrong position: there is one 2 in first position and the 1 is elsewhere
  4. Two right, right positions: the 1 is  in first position, one 2 elsewhere
  5. etc.
