I picked this up on Steam Early Access yesterday; I had wanted to Kickstart it, but I was unemployed during the drive, so I was unable to.

Darkest Dungeon is a Lovecraft-tinged medieval Rogue-like with a really unique "Stress" mechanic--in addition to managing your party's hit points, your heroes gain stress from crawling through undead-infested dungeons, which can lead to inter-party conflict and even total breakdowns.  

I've done pretty well thusfar--I played last night for about 4 hours total, with no fatalities and no failed quests.  Party balance is incredibly important, as is position within your party.   It's a real challenge, but a ton of fun with great atmosphere; the voice-over alone is spectacular!
