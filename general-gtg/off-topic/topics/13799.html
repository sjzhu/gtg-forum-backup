I'd also suggest keeping the updates on the 'Updates' page. The first real information on the Campaign page is the post about Silhouette, which entirely fails to introduce your premise or setting, and makes a bunch of references to things we don't know yet, because we've just got here and have no idea what's going on. It is unreasonably difficult to find what your books are actually about, and why we should care about them. That info needs to be front and centre.

EDIT: I just watched some of your campaign video; you don't even really introduce the comics until two minutes in. The campaign video is your hard sell, and you need to take advantage of that. To compare to another comics project: https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/sonson/kuruz-volume-2-one-by-one?ref=category_recommended - I watched 30 seconds of this campaign video and I now know what the premise of the guy's comic is and have seen some of the art to boot. People have short attention spans, and you need to hook them sharpish.
