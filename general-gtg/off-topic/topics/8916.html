A "Let's Play" is where someone plays a game and basically reports back their progress. In the case of a YouTube Let's Play, the person is recording themselves playing the game and commentating over it (either taking as they play the game, or as a commentary edited on after the gameplay is recorded). In a written version, I think the person basically plays the game (usually taking screenshots as they go), and then writes up what happened in that session, putting in appropriate screenshots (and sometimes embedded videos of cutscenes) as they go. So it's like a post-by-post walkthrough of that particular person's run through a game.
