Ok, it’s kind of a plug, but I’m not associated with the company in any way. Evil Hat has released their Kickstarter project for Race to Adventure, as of a couple days ago. It’s a competitive modular board game involving a race through various exciting locations. It’s set in their Spirit of the Century world, so pulp adventure to the max. Since superhero comics are an off-shoot of the pulp genre, it seems that I may not be the only here with similar interests.

This is Evil Hat’s first foray into the board game market, after successfully doing RPGs (FATE system, Don’t Rest Your Head) and launching a fiction line. They’re definitely worth checking out.
