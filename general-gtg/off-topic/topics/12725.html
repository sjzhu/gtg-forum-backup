Today is my birthday, and every year I always do a year in review kind of thing, and thank those that made it great,

So what brings me here? Well, it's simple: You all. Sentinels of the Multiverse is probable one of my favorite universes, games and just experiences. The excitement of new releases, the promos, and especially the secrets of who is doing what, why, and what's to come next is always a fantastic experience! Everyone on the forum has been a delight to talk to, your stories, experiences, help and suggestions to deal with gameplay issues, or team dynamics is always wonderful to read.

I know I have said some things here that are just plain ignorant, or riffled with spelling mistakes, but I always make sure I do my best to catch them right away, or make amends afterwards. All of this leads up to my big thanks. Thanks to the forum for tolerating and encouraging me and other gamers. Thanks to all my friends who through this game, have given us a stronger bond, and thank you to Greater Than Games, Dice Hate Me, and Handelabra Games for bringing everyone here together.

You are all awesome.
