Matchstickman started this - a while back he posted a thread celebrating some large number of users (I forget how many) and as a result many forum members sported party-hat-flavoured avatars for a while.

Despite considerable pressure to conform, I resisted joining the ranks of the behatted.

But eventually I promised that I would wear an avatar hat of the forum's choice for some arbitrary length of time (likely a week, but I'll say a month now to be on the safe side) if I successfully completed 300 online games in 2014.

And thanks to a bunch of awesome people I hit my goal at 10.30pm on New Year's Eve, so now I need to let you guys choose my hat. I'd like you to post images for your collective consideration, and I'm imposing the following rules, because I'm mad with power.

  • Each forum member may submit only one image, to stop things getting crazy. Post your image in this thread.
  • Usual forum rules apply - nothing offensive, no nudity or profanity, and nothing that will get any of us into trouble in any way.
  • I reserve the right to veto any image that endangers anyone or misrepresents me in a way I find morally unacceptable. I'm perfectly happy to look silly though!
  • Images should be of appropriate dimensions and file size to work as an avatar - whatever the forum software demands. And of course they should include a hat in some way!
  • The deadline for image submission is 2.00am (GMT, work out your own time for yourself) 9 January 2015.
  • Any registered forum member may then vote for the hat they want me to wear. I'll organise a voting system once I know how many entries there are, and I'll allow a one week voting period.
  • I'll wear the winning avatar for at least one calendar month, and the winner gets the satisfaction of knowing that the forum thinks their hat is the best!

This is going to be awesome. I have faith in you guys. :)
