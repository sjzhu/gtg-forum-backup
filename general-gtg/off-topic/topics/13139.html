I'm not sure how much I can agree with this given the large fanbase Seven has managed to acquire. The use of cutscenes was definitely a factor in Seven's success, but part of it is also how those cutscenes were combined with the gameplay to create an epic experience. Now, not everything about the story is perfect, Disc 3 is just mostly filler, but it was good enough to keep people engaged. The game also still has one of the best introductions I've ever seen. You instantly know the genre, the main character, and the world aesthetics and conflict all while learning how to play the game in a way that's intuitive, fun, and doesn't feel overwhelming. 

 

While the graphics are dated, people's love for the game isn't. Fans of the series hold on to it just as much as Six or Five. As far as the remake goes, the only main thing I hope they change is to make it so that your Limit command doesn't replace Attack. I'd actually like to see elements of Ten's overdrive system incorporated.
