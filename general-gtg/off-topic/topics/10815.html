I'm thinking that One Night Ultimate Werewolf will be a little easier to run as a first-time experiment.

Rabk, I know you said Resistance, but would you be willing to give that a try?

The idea of the game is that everyone gets a secret role. All of the roles are either on the Werewolf team or the Villager team. During the Night Phase of the game, the roles perform their actions (e.g. Werewolves finding out their teammates, The Seer peeking at someone's role, etc.). After that is all done, the Day Phase begins. This is where all the players discuss one person they want to accuse of being a Werewolf and lynch. After a limited time of discussion, a vote is held. The opposing team of the player voted for wins! It's pretty simple. I'll be handling all the stuff done in secret through private messages, then I will be setting an amount of time for discussion. Sound fun?
