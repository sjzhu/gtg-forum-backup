A bunch of my friends jumped on this quiz when I posted the link on FB, and the consensus there seems to be that the quiz gives a much higher Tactician rating than expected.  Looking at the explanation of how the questions are weighted, the quiz' definition of Tactician seems to be "Someone who likes to be prepared for different possible obstacles" as opposed to "Someone who can come up with specific plans to overcome a particular obstacle given known assets and liabilities."  The former can certainly lead to the latter, but the quiz appears to weigh the desire to be prepared more than any actual skill at making use of the preparations.
