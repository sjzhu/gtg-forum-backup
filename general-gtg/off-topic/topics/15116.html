If you can get at least 3-4 people to play with, I can always recomend The Red Dragon Inn, a game that's something like a grown-up version of Munchkin.

It's a game where all the players are characters from a D&D style dungeoneering party that have rested up and are now partying at the Red Dragon Inn(hence the name). There the players gamble, rough-house and drink, all the while trying their best to get eachother's share of treasure from the previous adventure.

What's great about this game is that there's so many different character's/decks that you can play, each unique! It verges on Sentinel's levels of variation! I'd start with the base set; it has a human female fighter(Fiona the Volitile), an elf preistess(Deirdre the Preistess), a halfling rouge(Gerki the Sneak), and a elderly human wizard with his Monty Python-esque rabbit familiar(Zot the Wizard and Pooky).

As far as I know there are at least 5 base sets to get with a mess of single deck expansions, but even one 4-deck set is enough to have quite the time with friends!
