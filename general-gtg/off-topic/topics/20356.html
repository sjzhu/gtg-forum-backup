So long story short: Brawl is one of the very cool games in the Cheapass Games catalogue, a real-time card game styled after fighting games. I got into it because of the Dicebreaker video on it: https://m.youtube.com/watch?v=sL2y8BCgZwU

Anyway, now that GtG are handling (or will be handling?) the Cheapass catalogue, it seemed appropriate to make some decks for Sentinels characters. So, I drew up the following:

Fanatic:
Bases x4
Clear x2
Press x2
Red Hit x4
Blue Hit x9
Green Hit x5
Blue 2-Hit x2
Green 2-Hit x1
Red Block x1
Green Block x2
Freeze x3

Tachyon:
Bases x6
Clear x3
Red Hit x6
Blue Hit x7
Green Hit x8
Blue Block x2
Freeze x3

People who know Brawl: any thoughts? Have I made Tachyon ludicrously OP?
