He was, at one point.  The story goes that the 2003 Special Edition had to be re-rated because it had changed so much from the original, and that caused some problems.  When Star Wars came out, there was no PG-13 rating, only G PG and R.  PG was somewhere in between PG and modern PG-13, while G was for any clean movie, not just kiddie fare.  In order to keep the PG rating under the new system, some changes had to be made to make it more acceptable to younger audiences.  Apparently Lucas wasn't too happy about it… but he still did it, and he's said repeatedly that the Special Editions are the only versions of the films that he wants preserved.  It's not just that he wants additional director's cuts to be added to the films' history, he actually wants to get rid of the theatrical versions entirely.  So one photo of him wearing a t-shirt doesn't help much.

This is all irrelevant, though, since everyone knows Alderaan shot first.
