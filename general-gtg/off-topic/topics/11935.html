Sadly, what people crave is fat, salt and sugar. Plus there are sometimes technical reasons to do with stability and finished quality, as well as a whole raft of other tricks that cut costs by mixing in cheaper ingredients, even including water and air (commodity prices are always rising but nobody wants to pay more, and static promotion price points put further pressure on suppliers to cut corners. I have an interesting tale about the whole horsemeat scandal regarding just such a situation). The last few years working the food industry have really opened my eyes.

We crave fat, salt and sugar because these things were scarce in our evolutionary past and so we needed the motivation to scarf them down whenever they were available.
