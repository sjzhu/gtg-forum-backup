So after months of looking and trying to move some things around, I was FINALY able to secure travel and lodging to get to Gen Con! Needless to say, I was very excited, as it was getting down to the wire and I really wanted to get a game of GSF in with the >G guys.

But life has a funny way sometimes. Not 24 hours after I had finalized plans for Gen Con, I was invited to play at the Peach Music Festival at Montage Mountain in Scranton PA. How could I turn down the oppertunity to share the stage with the likes of the Allman Brothers, Grace Potter and Lotus amongst others? 

So, I guess the point of this post is, if any of you forumites are festival goers or music lovers from the greater north east and are attending the Peach Music Festival, come shout Foote chants Saturday night as I attempt to burn the place down to the ground through sound waves. Where are those Sonic Mines when you need them.
