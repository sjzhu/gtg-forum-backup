This is One Night Ultimate Werewolf. If you would like to read the full rules, check here: http://boardgamegeek.com/filepage/96373/one-night-ultimate-werewolf-rules-english

The basic idea is that each player will have a secret role allied either with the Villagers or the Werewolves. During the Night Phase, players with a Night action will do that action in secret. Then, during the Day Phase, players will discuss who they would like to "kill," with the goal of selecting someone on the opposing team. After a pre-determined time for discussion, players will vote on the one to "kill." The team whose player "dies" is the losing team.

All secret and Night actions will be handled via PMs with me, the moderator, as I will not be playing a role myself. The Night Phase will conclude when all actions have been taken, and I will announce the beginning of the Day Phase. The Day Phase will last for 24 hours. After that, all players will post a single vote; a vote that is subsequently edited will disqualify the voting player from the game.

The roles being used in this game are as follows.

Villager x 3 (No Night actions)

Werewolf x 2 (Night action: to discover who the other Werewolf is, if one is present)

Seer x 1 (Night action: discover the identity of one player, or discover two of the untaken roles)

Robber x 1 (Night action: may swap roles with one other player, then act as that new role)

Troublemaker x 1 (Night action: swap the roles of two other players)

Tanner x 1 (No Night action. Wins if killed)

The players in this game are BlueHairedMeerkat, Silverleaf, Nielzabub, Rabk, McBehrer, and Humble-Knight. Expect a PM soon with your role. I will announce when all roles have been distributed.

Night Phase has concluded. Voting will occur at 12 Noon Eastern on Friday 01 August.
