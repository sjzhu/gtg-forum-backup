FreeRPGday is a day when shops and game companies try to entice people into playing RPGs they might not have tried before, both people who play RPGs but might not have tried their particular product or people who have never played an RPG before.

The companies who participate make a specific product for the day and then it gets distributed to all the shops participating, worldwide. Typically on these days shops also 'open their tables' so that they have GMs to run those games or whatever else the shop wants.

http://www.freerpgday.com/

That's the official site with a list of all the stuff being offered up this year, and a search tool to help you find your closest participating shop.
