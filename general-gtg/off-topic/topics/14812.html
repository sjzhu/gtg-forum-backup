Just today, friends and neighbors, I launched my second Kickstarter for Cold Steel Wardens: Roleplaying in the Iron Age of Comics.  This one, following the successful Kickstart of our corebook, is for Cold Steel Wardens: Rogues Gallery.  It's a bevy of mooks, made men, metahumans, maniacs, and masterminds, ready to drop into any Iron Age superhero game.  

CSW: Rogues Gallery builds heavily on the world set down in the CSW corebook, providing detailed writeups on Stonegate Federal Penitentiary and Lincroft Asylum, as well as how to use prison and asylum settings in such a game.  The majority of the book, however, is written from the perspective of the phase-shifting vigilante, Sawbones, whose notes include in-character evidence pieces, including police reports, psychological evaluations, interviews, and more.  

Don't have Cold Steel Wardens yet?  Pick up the corebook as part of our Kickstarter drive!

If Rook City is your favorite Sentinels expansion....if you can't wait for Daredevil Season 2...then CSW: Rogues Gallery is waiting for you!

Thanks again for your support!
