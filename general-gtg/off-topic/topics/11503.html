Thanks Rabit!  I appreciate the personal feedback.

I don't think you're an anomaly.  I've read a number of reviews of people who got frustrated with the game, or felt that it was poorly scaled between starting and advanced characters.  From what I've seen of it, it seems very much catered to the fans of the Shadowrun game setting, and not so much to people who enjoy gaming for the sake of the game itself.  I don't know if either of those apply to you, but I suspect that most of the people who do enjoy it are there for the thematic aspect.

When I get a copy and get some friends together to play it eventually, I'll likely recommend that we add a storytelling component to it.  For example (I'm going to steal the obstacles from Rahdo's YT video) rather than just saying "Okay, for the first scene of the mission we've got a Light Combat Drone and a Gang Leader as obstacles...the LCD has 5 health levels that consist of four generic damage, one green, one generic, one black, and one generic...the GL has 3 health levels that consist of one red, three generic, and one red" we'll say something like "Okay, we've done the datasteal and now we're trying to sneak out of the corp site, but a Light Combat Drone is blocking the hallway up ahead and one of Joe's contacts, a Gang Leader, chooses NOW to call him up and bend his ear over turf negotiations.  We're going to need to keep that drone busy while our decker can disrupt its connection to the corp rigger, then open it up and shoot out the power cell.  Joe's gonna have to do some fast talking and maybe drop some threats of personal violence to get that guy off the line and let him focus."

Also, one of the mechanics of the game is the idea of your character gaining Karma (like experience) and getting upgrades for future games - if you only played it once, you're not going to see any of that at all unless you decide to start out at a higher power level just to try the upgrade traits.  I don't think that would've made a huge difference in your enjoyment of the game, but if you ever give it another shot, that might be something to try out :)
