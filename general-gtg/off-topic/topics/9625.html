As a few of you might know, I've been pseudo-unemployed since June of last year, when my previous school declined to give me a long-term contract.

Since then, I've been hunting for jobs, in addition to running my currently--totally unprofitable, as I have yet to have my flagship product released by my publisher--RPG company.  

In January, I was hired by Kaplan as an SAT/ACT prep coach, but only on a part-time basis.  My first (and only class) with them was cancelled, leaving me high and dry for both hours and pay.  In fairly dire straights, I applied at a local Donato's Pizza as a delivery driver and was hired.  I started there this week.

However, today, I was tendered an offer by another local school district as a long-term substitute teacher.  I accepted the offer and would start on March 2nd.

The issue comes in what I do next.  Between my few Kaplan hours and my subbing, we'll be fine financially through the rest of the school year.  However, come June, I'll lose my primary method of employment, even if the district picks me up for the vacancy I'm subbing for (the prior teacher is retiring, so there's definitely a vacancy, for which I'll have the inside track).  There's no way that I could potentially keep all three jobs, as Donato's has me primarily closing (which means being at the store until 1am most nights, making it unsuitable when I have to wake up at 6am to go teach).  However, if I drop the Donato's job, I may be high and dry come June.

Thoughts?  Ideas?  Advice?  I'll be working at Donato's tomorrow, so I will talk to them then, but what do I even say in this situation?
