When your expectations do not match your results, then either your expectations are wrong, or your model is.  Assuming that either one is correct is foolish.  Find why there is a descrepancy (or at least why there might be a descrepancy), and make your decision based on that.

 

For example:  dclietz expected AZ to improve in H=5 games more than he did because he benfits from pumping.  One possible explaination is that there are few enough pumping heroes that 5H doesn't significantly improve AZ's chances of being pumped.  So now you can do math to investigate whether this is likely, or if the source of the discrepancy is elsewhere.
