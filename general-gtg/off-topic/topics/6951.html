You could go to my SotM site (www.spiffworld.com/sotm) and read the Rules & Clarifications PDF.  It's a collection of all the official rulings that have been laid down by the game's creators here or on Boardgame Geek.  It'll give you a deeper look into card interactions and maybe head off any misunderstandings about how some cards work.

There's also a bunch of accessories you can print and put together, like hp trackers and card boxes and such.  That should keep you busy until your package arrives. :)
