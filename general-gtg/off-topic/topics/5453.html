Basically, it’s a thing to encourage folks to exercise. You log workouts and get points for it. There are also “quests” that you can do, too, such as learning a new lift or running X amount of miles or something.

What do you get for the points? Absolutely nothing as far as I’m aware. The idea is that when you go to the gym you don’t see immediate results, so this shows your progress in a more quickly quantifiable way.

It’s in beta right now, by the way.
