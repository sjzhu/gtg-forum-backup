This will be a roleplaying story game, I don't want to do turn based, so we'll let things go organically.  If you can't post for a while you might just get dragged along, or we could all end up sitting around waiting for you.

 

The Characters:

We can have up to four players, those players can pick from the below characters, all characters get four things to start with, all get two personality traits, the rest depend on what you are:

A Prince or Princess:  You have to be nice, polite and you get to be a leader.  You get an item and a spell, and you are a pathetic human.

A Cat:  You are a cat.  You will get some basic cat abilities to choose from and a cool spell, but you are not a fighter, you don't hurt things that much with your claws, you can be brave but you are not a ninja.

A Bear Cub:  You mean well, and you are a bear, but you are not a big bear.  You like Princes/Princesses and try to get along with everyone.  You have great common sense about the outdoors, and have an idea for just about every situation, at least the less strategic ones.  You get bear sense and an item.

A Bird:  You are small and bright, you sing happy ditties and are upbeat, except when you are scared.  You get 2 bird traits.  Flying you get free, it is a nice one.  You are not fast though, you are a pretty little songbird.

A Dragon:  You are big and tough.  You have a cave and treasure, but you never-ever take your treasure out of your cave.  Ever.  You like Princesses and small animals, you might like the taste of a Prince, who knows?  You are great at dealing with big problems, but sadly the little ones are often the key.  You get two Dragon traits.

A Pony:  You are loyal and brave, you love your Prince/Princess and follow with them wherever they go.  You have secrets, but you never let them out.  No one must ever know you are more than you appear.  That is the way of Ponies.  You get a magic ability and an item.

 

Tell me what one you want, I'll respond with choices for you to pick from.

How to play:

Like an adventure RPG, but no dice or anything.  No fighting to win, sometimes you have to fight but that is just a step you don't get to win or lose fights.  (fights kind of go on until other stuff stops them)  Use your creativity, be silly, help people.  I will answer any questions and post relevant details when you act.

I'll post a start, then relevant characters take it from there, sometimes there is a choice, other times you just get to try stuff until something happens.

 

Go ahead and sign up.  Four person limit.

It takes Dragons a while to wake up.  One part of them wakes up first, it wakes up another part, that part wakes up another until finally the whole dragon is awake.  Dragons have lots of parts, so they take a while to wake up.

This evening the Dragon's fifth scale to the right of his belly line woke up first.  It was itchy.  It was very itchy.  The itchy scale bumped its neighbor, who bumped its neighbor, who was also itchy, and woke up the skin underneath it.  That skin twitched and woke up a muscle.  By the time the sun had started to set two thirds of the Dragon's belly was awake, one of his horns, his fire pouch, his front right leg and half of his tail.

All of them were itchy.

Now a half awake Dragon with the itches is a funny sight, but even more so when the Dragon's brain is still asleep.  There was much rolling and twitching and half of an awake tail sending the sleeping half flying all about.  There were even some fire snorts.  By the time the Dragon's brain woke up the sun had nearly set.

The twitching stopped, the Dragon thought, and felt.  Through the itches he could feel something was off, over near his left rear leg.  A small indentation about the size of a walnut where there shouldn't have been one.  The great beast roared and swung around, almost fully awake now.  Something was missing.  One of his treasures was missing.

Now you may wonder how a great beast covered in scales would notice a misplaced treasure the size of a walnut, esp. when he had been thrashing about for almost an hour.

Well Dragons, you see are very particular about their treasure, and even when they have stomping fire snorting fights they never upset their treasure.  It simply isn't done.

The great red Dragon cast a dragon snort spell, and in a little puff of smoke a dragon book appeared before him, and he started blowing through the pages.

There it was, item 8703Y Division A4, sub-division TT5, small objects.  One night stone, purple and grey with a hint of green deep inside it.

He looked around and sniffed for clues but even with his sharp eyes he was just too big to be of any use searching for clues.  He would need help; and a bath.  Boy did he itch.
