Casino Royale was an exciting movie and a great kickoff into Craig's "prequel" trilogy.  People complain that the movie didn't capture the full spirit of Bond, but I always argued it was because Casino was supposed to take place when Bond was still fresh as a 00 agent; that spirit of Bond didn't exist yet and what is left is something more raw than suave.  I thought it fit perfectly.

Quantum of Solace was, unfortunately, a product of the writers' strike that took place a handful of years ago; the production team basically admitted that fact, apologized, and promised that Skyfall would make up for it tenfold.  And they definitely delivered.

Overall, I have to say Craig is my favorite Bond.  I know the general population's opinion is eager to dismiss him, proposing that the classics are irreplacable.  But personally, I'm not that nostalgic.  I love Connery and Dalton; From Russia with Love is among my top movies of all time.  But, when I look at what the Craig trilogy accomplished (despite Quantum of Solace's shortcomings), I can't help but be completely enthralled with it.
