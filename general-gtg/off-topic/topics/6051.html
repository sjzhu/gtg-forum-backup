Anybody else play this?

It’s a pretty standard Co-operative FPS where England has been overrun by zombies and you and your mates shoot them all.
There are different classes who have differing guns but each game you all start off with just a pistol and need to earn money to buy those guns by killing the zombies and then head to the shop at the end of the wave.

They’ve just started their Summer sideshow where they reskin the enemies, add new maps and you have the opportunity to do the achievement thing and unlock a new character (no stats or anything, just a reskin too). They have run different events during Summer, Halloween and Christmas each with their own theme.

As far as I know it is online and pc only. I love the game and boot it up every once in a while but I’m waiting for a sequel… the only thing that holds back the fun is the relatively old technology that makes for bad lighting in a really dark game. Otherwise in terms of teaming up to survive it is an awesome game. I love the formula of the game… it is very realistic (welding doors shut, limited ammo) and also very gameshow like (buying weapons between rounds) so you feel like you are in some bizzaro reality show.
