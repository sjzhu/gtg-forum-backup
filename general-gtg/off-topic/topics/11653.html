Firstly, if there is objection to me posting about another game that is currently on Kickstarter then please delete this thread, I'm not in any way related to this project just a keen backer of a game that I believe a lot of you would also get a kick out of, and a small publisher who I believe to genuinely deserve a bit of support, hence why I'm specifically posting here.

Too Many Cinderellas is a Japanese micro game that the small Australian publisher Grail Games is bringing to the English language. It's a quick filler which also packs in enough game to keep it replayable (think micro games along the line of Council of Verona or Coup), and has a cute theme of the prince searching for his mysterious CInderella. It's also affordable at only £8.50/$13.00/e10.50 including worldwide shipping, and curently hanging at just a hundred backers short of hitting funding.

If you would like to know more then why not mosey on over to their KS page and maybe consider helping the project to hit funding.
