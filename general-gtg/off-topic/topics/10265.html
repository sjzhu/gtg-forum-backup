So really more of a demo/teaser for the analog game, then?

 

One of the things I like best about BCon is the strategic depth.  Similar to Chess/Go/Checkers, there is no element of luck.  Everything that happens in the game is a result of a player decision.  There is minimal hidden knowledge (the only thing hidden is what attack each opponent is playing right before revealing).  You always know exactly what cards your opponent has in hand and exactly what their capabilities are.

 

There is a really deep mind-game involved as well.  

Player 1: "I have this awesome attack"

Player 2: "I know you have that awesome attack, but I have this perfect counter"

Player 1: "Yes, but I have this different attack that could counter your counter"

Something of an "I know that you know that I know that you know…", but it really allows for a lot of strategic and tactical play, and even defeats are very satisfying.

 

Often, when I lose a competitive game, it is because of a bad dice roll, or a poor card flip (can even happen in Sentinels).  However, a loss in BattleCON is either because I made a mistake or because my opponent outplayed me.  Each defeat makes me a better player.

 

BattleCON cannot replace Sentinels, but in our house it has fired every other competitive game.  (BattleCON and Sentinels are the only two games we regularly keep in an easily accessible space in the living room.  All other games are in the closet in the back with the Hard Hat sign on the door).

Well, I don't like fighters either (except Smash Bros) and I LOVE it. The huge differences to me are:

1.) there are no button combinations to memorize, so there's 0 chance you won't know HOW to pull off the attack you want. It's all about knowing WHICH ONE to use.

2.) The turns are simulaneous, so you don't have to worry about not having quick enough reflexes to land a hit. It's PURE strategery; no speed, finesse, or random luck involved.

3.) each attack has a 2-turn cooldown, so you won't get unfairly beaten by some noob spamming one really good move the entire time.
