Just thought I'd share this with you all. I know GTG had some con-exclusive Sentinels button set, which was great and I thought was neat. But it wasn't enough for me.

These are the first 11 in a set of what I'm planning on being a set of buttons for every hero! And maybe some villains. I'll just have to wait until they post his character card art with just the art and no card text.
