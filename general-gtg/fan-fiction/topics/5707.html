Oh the font is smaller, ergo it is not as big as it was in word Alright, time for some assumptions I made.

  1. Baron Blade was imprisoned for the death of Legacy Sr. This would mean he only fought the current Legacy once and found him to be bullet proof.
  2. Baron Blade would have an escape plan in case he did fail and get caught
  3. Baron Blade escapes immediately, and Legacy would be able to find out that the man who killed his father is free relatively quickly
  4. Baron Blade believes everyone is intellectually inferior
  5. Baron Blade would have some sort of way to get data on his enemies (in the form of nano-bots in this story)
  6. Baron Blade is emotionally stressed because this new Legacy is much stronger than the old one
  7. Baron Blade knows that the Parsons are Legacys
  8. Baron Blade is smart enough, even with his sanity strained, to know that he would not survive the impact if he stayed on Earth
  9. Baron Blade experiences mood swings.
  10. Baron Blade sees no value in human life other than his own.

That’s about it! I hope you all like this little story. I have to applaud whoever did the art for his HP tracker, because that is what really got me thinking about it. And also thanks to whoever runs their twitter fo allowing me to use their characters and setting. My teacher is worried about stuff like that for some reason. And finally, thanks to the enire theam at >G for making a stupidly fun game!
