Eugene dreamed,

The Entity the shadow demon, looming over the land.

With Eugene's heart in his throat,

He stepped forward

The Void Entity leaned over and ripped

Into a small pale body.

It transformed into a familiar figure

with a small shadowy body

The Void Entity looked up and smiled 

He stepped away 

With Eugene's Flesh, covering its body 

The Entity reached out blue bloody mask in hand 

Eugene screamed.
