1) The title

"Lead by Example"

2) The type

One-Shot

3) The full game text

Each other player may choose one of the following:

Deal yourself and up to 2 targets 1 fire damage and 1 cold damage.

Draw 2 cards. You may discard up to 2 cards.

Put an Equipment card from your trash into play.

Discard 1 card. Deal 1 target 4 projectile damage.

4) The flavor text

"How would you like a job saving the world?" - Legacy, Freedom Four Annual #1

 

"Lead by Example" stays within Legacy's core theme of being the selfless leader of the Freedom 5. He heads the FV not just because of his strength and ability to lead from the front, but he also strives to set the bar high for his teammates and will lead by example. With Legacy's leadership to guide them, his team rises to to meet the occasion by performing at their best. 

The card effect represents the fruits of Legacy's efforts as leader, and how his team grows around him. Each team member other than Legacy gets to choose from 1 of 4 unique effects directly inspired by one of the Freedom Five. Each one is reminicent of an effect found in their deck and built specifically around them, though anyone Legacy is teamed up with will find the choices very useful as well. This Legacy does not rule with an Iron Fist like his timey-wimey counterpart, so providing his teammates with a choice of how to proceed was important. What kind of example would he set ruling like a tyrant instead of promoting democracy? This was my line of thought anyway.

The flavor text choosen embodies everything that Legacy and his team stands for. Saving the world. As long as there is evil, Legacy will be there, showing the entire world that he will not back down or be intimidated. Like the Lantern on his chest, Legacy serves as a shining moral beacon for everyone to follow. Legacy will always strive to lead by example.

Card Name:  Rush of Memories

Card Type:  One-Shot

Card Text:  Each player may discard 1 card.  Legacy deals one target X + 2 melee damage, then regains X hp. X = the total number of cards discarded.

Flavor Text:  "My friends, my allies are my greatest strength, Blade!  Until you understand that, you'll never win!" -- Legacy, Broken Blade mini-series #5.

--------------------------------------------------

Legacy, much like Superman, is the sort of character that grows stronger when he has something to fight for.  He gains strength not just from his own unique genetics, but from the relationships he's fostered over the years.  His friends are what inspire him to keep fighting, to keep forging on, even when the odds are against him.  Superman on his own?  He's strong.  Superman with the rest of the Justice League?  He'll never be defeated and he'll never give up.

To reflect that, I thought it made sense for his friends to push Legacy on towards a great, victorious smash.  Knowing what he's fighting for, it makes Legacy fight all the harder.  Plus, this card represents that "second wind" Legacy gets when he pulls inspiration from his friends.  After getting beaten and battered, taking punches for his friends as he Leads From The Front, Legacy remembers why he fights and draws strength from it.  Hence, the hp gain. 

Mechanically, Legacy is all about helping his allies--Bolster Allies, Inspiring Prescence, Motivational Charge, etc--but we rarely see Legacy ever in a reciprocal relationship with his allies.  This card represents how Legacy benefits from his relationship with his friends, gaining strength from them just as they gain strength from him.

This card is meant to represent an early point in the heroes' career, they've trained together long enough to know how things work, but not neccessarily long enough to know what they all can do.

------

By the Numbers

One-Shot

You may change the order of the heroes, once this is done the game continues as normal. If this card was played on Legacy's turn then the game continues from Legacy's new position. This may result in players missing turns or having a second turn during this round.

"OK Team, just like we practiced..." - Legacy, Freedom Five #12

------

Messing with the Metagame is something we haven't really seen before, and who better to inspire the players to be better at the game than Legacy?

As a quick example of the idea of how the card works, currently the play order is

  • Villain, Bunker, Tachyon, Absolute Zero, The Wraith, Legacy, Environment

and Legacy plays the card, he changes the order to 

  • Villain, Legacy, Tachyon, The Wraith, Bunker, Absolute Zero, Environment

which means that Tachyon, The Wraith, Bunker and Absolute Zero would all get a second turn this round! Sounds like a powerful effect but you can really only do it once per game, unless you can get an out of turn card play to swap back to the last hero spot again (otherwise everyone else loses their turn when you play the card to move back), though you could use it to move say Haka from after you to before you so his Ground Pound stays around longer.

I had to account for effects that would flip a card from the top of Legacy's deck (like Don't Dismiss Anything or Timeshift) which is why there is a "If Legacy's turn" clause.
