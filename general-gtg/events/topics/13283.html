The tournament is complete and here is the report!

We had 6 players and so we split up into 3 teams of two players each. We still played 3 vs 3 in the normal format however.

Round 1

  • The Caffeinators drafted The Operative, The Wraith, and Baron Blade
  • Team Ginger drafted Unity, Legacy, and Citizen Dawn
  • Winner: Team Ginger 3-1

Round 2

  • Arnolabra drafted Tachyon, Citizen Dawn, and Beacon
  • Team Ginger drafted Unity, The Operative, and Legacy
  • Winner: Team Ginger 2-1

Round 3

  • The Caffeinators drafted The Wraith, Tachyon, and Citizen Dawn
  • Arnolabra drafted Bunker, Ambuscade, and Omnitron
  • Winner: The Caffeinators (default)
  • Note: Due to a heinous rules screwup at the beginning of the game that we only noticed at the very end, Arnolabra forfeit the game rather than play it all over again. For some reason we thought Rigged Explosives get placed when the card enters play, and took advantage of that in a way that should have been possible.

Final Round

  • The Caffeinators drafted The Wraith, Tachyon, and Citizen Dawn
  • Team Ginger drafted Baron Blade, The Visionary, and Legacy
  • Winner: Team Ginger 3-1
  • Note: We only played one game in the finals due to time.

Congrats to Team Ginger on the win! And much thanks to Greater Than Games for the tournament pack with info & prizes, and to The Board Room Game Cafe for hosting the event.
