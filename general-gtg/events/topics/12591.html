We realized it would probably be easier to know what’s going on if everyone could see the nominations as they are happening.  (Plus it will eliminate the “Oh, someone probably nominated them” issue. ) We’ve created two threads for folks to post nominations in: this thread for nominating forumites and a second thread for nominating posts/threads.

The awards for forumites are:

  • Inspiring Presence (Morale Booster) – The forum member who, through positive attitude and kind heart, did the most to make the Greater Than Games forums a positive experience over the past year
  • Bolster Allies (Funniest) – The forum member who was best at making people laugh and providing humorous moments over the past year
  • Truth Seeker (Lore Master) – The forum member who has done the best job answering questions (rules, facts, mythology, etc.) and otherwise providing useful and accurate information over the past year
  • Harmony (Top Debater) – The forum member who is best at rationally debating positions (without turning it into a fight) over the past year
  • Exceeded Expectations (Top Contributor) – The forum member who contributed to the Greater Than Games community with tools and/or accessories the most (including wiki contributions) over the past year
  • Unique Capabilities (Top Artist) – The forum member who created the best/most Greater Than Games-related art or fanfic over the past year

If you want to nominate someone, you can either post here with their name and the award you're nominating them for or use the original form for anonymous nominations. For posts here, you can also include a link to an example of their contributions and/or a quick blurb explaining why you're nominating someone. Such a blurb shouldn't be more than a sentence (to avoid it turning into campaigning). If you want to nominate someone who's already been nominated, feel free.

And feel free to include multiple nominations in each post.

Someone nominated for an award for different specific reasons will still only be listed once in the voting - we won't be splitting votes due to multiple contributions.

Rules are posted in the original nominations thread.

We'll be posting the nominations that have already been made anonymously, and we'll continue to post those that come in for the next couple of weeks.
