July 4, 1944

London, England

Supreme Headquarters Allied Expeditionary Force

 

Anthony looked around, the park was overrun with soldiers, barricades, tents, jeeps, you name it it was there.  Otherwise, it would probably be very beautiful.  If Tyler Vance was correct, General Eisenhower would be in Normandy and Paul Parsons VII will be here for a secret meeting about some suspicious happenings that would turn out to be the launch of Fyodor Romonat's War-Zeppelins.  This is where Ivan Romonat, mistaken to be Fyodor's cousin would murder Parsons, and supposedly create the world he had grown up in.  If Parsons lives, Romonat is stopped, and Parsons' son is around to lead them against Voss.

The mood was noticably lax on the base.  Anthony looked around, trying to get an eye on Parsons.

Instead he saw approaching one of the smaller tents three men, one in some kind of shiny metal suit, another one, he was huge and wearing some serious armor, even if it did look pretty beat up, the last one was a freaking cowboy, with one arm?

"What the heck. . ." Anthony got up and started to follow them.

Looking around the edge of the tent he could see them, and what looked to be Parsons, they were arguing.  None of them looked like Romonat.

Suddenly he heard a yell "You won't live that long old man!" and then an explosion.  They were attacking Parsons, Anthony ran around the tent, trying to get over on Parsons side, as he arrived a man in an armored suit ran by him to check on Parsons.

He ran up behind him and as he did he could feel the air around him feel warp, things seemed really wierd, like it had in the portal.

"The generator is operational, this time will be fixed." the shiny metal man stated.

"What?" Parsons asked as he got up.

"He means that when you die here, now, you die everywhere." The cowboy added.

"And with you dies your son!"

Number of Heroes:  6

Team 1:  Omnitron-X, Crono Ranger, Bunker EOW

Team 2:  Greatest Legacy, GI Bunker, The Argent Adept

Environment:  Time Cataclysm

How the round works:

Omnitron-X and Greatest Legacy are captains, after all heroes have players they will report the order of play to me through a PM.  The Environment will go first, Team 1 will have their first hero play, then Team 2 will have their first hero play, then Team 1 2nd, Team 2 2nd, T1 3rd, T2 3rd, start over.

How the mechanics of PVP will work:

The team you are on count as hero targets for your cards.  The other team count as villain targets for your cards.  Cards that destroy villain ongoing cards may destroy equipment or ongoing cards.  Cards that affect the environment affect the environment.  Environment cards treat both teams as heroes and villains.  Cards like Crushing hallway can be destroyed by anyone from either team.

If a hero plays a card that destroys a card, the hero playing the card chooses what to destroy.

If an environment destroys a card your team chooses, anyone can choose for their team if others are taking a while to get back on.

Further environment card clarifications:

Main computer room will play the top card of the environment deck instead of the villain deck.

Marsquake will destroy one card from each team, and one from the environment (instead of one villain, one hero, one environment)

Other Issues:

Don't feel that there is a right or wrong side to play on, this match doesn't make a huge impact in the story line, but it brings two of the original six possibilities back together and lets us rebuild a team.  Do your best to crush the other team, and don't take it too seriously, PVP is not balanced.

This hopefully will go quickly, gang up on people, fight as dirty as you can, and be polite.

Any issues/questions as to how things work will be resolved by me.

So, first come, first serve, lets see what the future holds!

If you don't think you would like PVP play anyway, I need six people to play this match so we can get back to the normal games and standard matches.  This is the only PVP match we will see.

If you want more on your characters rational and backstory let me know, I'm happy to share.

Feel free to communicate by PM, there are spies from both teams watching this thread.
