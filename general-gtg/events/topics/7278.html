Here is an post-con report for those who are interested.  I ran this event 3 times and everyone seemed to have a blast.

 

This event took our heroes from the streets of Megalopolis, to the Ruins of Atlantis, and ended up on Insula Primalis, where they faced Citizen Dawn.

 

Everyone seems to love Tachyon, as she was the only hero to be picked at all three events.  She was also responsible for defeating the krakken in two of the three events.  After the heroes learned there was simply no way to beat the beast down, she used her super speed to get the Krakken to attack her and then using here lightning fast agility and relexes to make the giant beast tie its tentacles into knots, taking it out of the fight.

 

Game session two was awesome with Unity riding on the back of her massive T-Rex bot, eating and stomping on Citizens of the Sun minions.

 

Session one featured the Wraith, who was instrumental in defeating the forces attacking the city while never even leaving her base.  She coordinated via communications satellites, and used her tech skills to cause havoc with the Citizens of the Sun gadgets and vehicles, making it easier for the rest of the team to take them out.

 

Thanks to all who participated.  I look forward to running this again in the future and I have already started on a new event.

 

Stay tuned all...  :)
