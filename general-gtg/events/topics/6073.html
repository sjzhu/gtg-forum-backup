Gamer’s Guild

""Koga"":

Gamer’s Guild of IUPUI meets every Friday in the IT building, room 167, from 12pm to 6pm, though we usually stay later. The doors lock at 6pm, but that only prevents people from entering, not from leaving. During the summer it doesn’t really get going till around 1 or 2pm. And if there isn’t a meeting it will be posted on our facebook page.

We usually play Sentinels every meeting, with me bringing the cards, though another person has a set as well. Anyone is allowed to come, the guild’s population just has to stay mostly IUPUI students, which is not a problem so far. We also have many other games to play, which are mostly provided by me with new games getting added all the time.
