The manager at GP Bloomington is Wendy and I'd be happy to offer an introduction, but you probably don't need it. If you're setting up a regular get together, they care much more about whether or not they can trust you to show up and whether or not anyone else will show up than whether or not they are introduced to you by me. Also, despite exchanging email w/ Wendy and talking to her on the phone once, I've never met her in person.

 

I've heard there are other game stores in Bloomington, so I recommend you shop around and find the one that works best -- they're happy you are there, they have good hours, they have good snacks, they have a good location, whatever. I think there's a store called something like Common Room Games.

Arsenal is unique compared to any other game store I know of in that it's business model is as much or more about selling food then selling games, so they are really keen to have groups in. Now, every game store likes to have players in the store, because there's a chance you'll buy product, but some games are more lucrative than others. People are always buying more kit for Magic The Gathering or Warhammer, so having those games in the shop is great. People want to load up on their arsenal. Sentinels? Not so much. There might be the odd  purchase of an expansion or something, but the market gets saturated much more quickly.

 

Still, the stores are probably happy enough to put you on the schedule as long as you aren't interfering with anything else.

 

I think you'll find that the most important thing is to have a core group of people who enjoy playing and enjoy being with each other enough that they want to keep doing it over and over again.
