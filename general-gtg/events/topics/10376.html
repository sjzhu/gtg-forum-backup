Greetings, fellow Craftinels!

This is the first ever Sentinels Craft Swap. The idea of this swap is to make something Sentinels themed for another forumite and then to swap them. Once a partner receives the item, he or she posts a picture of it here on this thread so we can all admire it!

Rules (well, technically, it's more like guidelines...):

  1. Signups are from the 25th of May - 1st of June, and partnering will be done on the 2nd of June or thereabouts. You should have sent off your crafted item to your partner by July 1st.
  2. The item that you craft must be Sentinels themed in some way.
  3. You may not send anything that is not legally shippable or that is illegal in the country of origin or destination. Also, please check postage to other countries before agreeing to send to a partner abroad. You don't want to be surprised by crazy shipping fees.
  4. The time you should put into creating your item should be somewhere between two days and a week. If you item takes considerably less time to create, you may also make a set of items to comply with this rule.
  5. Note to Playtesters: Obviously, we are not crafting and swapping anything that uses playtesting material. That'd be wrong.

If you are up for this swap, fill out this questionnaire and post it below or message it to me, if you prefer. Feel free to adjust the spelling to American English for your post. ;)
