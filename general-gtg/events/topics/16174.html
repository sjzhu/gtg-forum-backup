It is awesome that you are going to Gen Con! The last time you were at Gen Con, I was part of the group that won the costume contest :)

Greater Than Games will have a huge presence at the show this year. In addition to our booth, we've had a dedicated demo room in the concourse outside of the main expo hall for the past two years. In addition to short demos in our booth, and long demos in our room, we'll be doing a bunch of Sentinel Comics RPG demos in other spaces at the show, and be running a number of panels on board gaming.
