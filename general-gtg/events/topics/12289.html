Hey, all!

Back when I was in college, I started a little group called the Wittenberg Role-Playing Guild.  The Guild is now in its 13th year and is now holding its 12th consecutive convention:  WittCon XII--The Dirty Dozen!

If you're around the I-70 Corridor on March 28th, come spend the day gaming with us!  Starting at 9am and running till midnight, there are tons of rpg sessions, as well as board games and card games (including SotM, GSF, and Tactics), a costume contest, and tons more.  WittCon is known for featuring regional indie-game creators, including Conjuring Seasons by White Wizards Workshop, Grisleigh End by Jon Anderson, Era of the Ninja from Mythical Eras of War Games LLC, and the Shuffling Horror system by GameWick Games.  I'll be running demo scenarios of Cold Steel Wardens, as well.  

$5 gets you into the convention, gets you access to events at the con, and buys you a pizza-and-pop dinner, courtesy of Beavercreek Pizza Dive.  

If you'd like more info, feel free to ask away or check the link here:  WittCon XII--The Dirty Dozen!
