So, I am very excited that soon we will be welcoming some new Heroes and facing new villians in new locals…

So I had the idea to throw a party to celebrate. Here are my ideas, would love to here what other ideas people may have to mark this momentous occasions!

Environment themed tables where people play through a set up scenario with a little story to set it up, and lower stakes akin to a demo game (less HP for Villain) for a quicker game (30 minutes) and people meeting all the characters as they rotate through the tables.

Custom invitations with a costume element thrown in with perhaps some sentinels related door prize, or some variation on the Sentinels Points idea from Gen Con with different prizes…

Love to hear other’s ideas or how they are marking the great occasion!
