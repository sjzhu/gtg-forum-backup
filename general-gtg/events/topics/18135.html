So how was everyone's Origins?  

What'd you do?  What'd you play?  What'd you eat?  What'd you buy?

 

As mentioned earlier, I spent most of my Origins running Cold Steel Wardens and the rpg I currently have in alpha, De Civitates Dei.  I did manage to play a few games:  a Quantum Black game (which uses the Ubiquity mechanics), an ICONS game, and a Savage Worlds Stargate game.  I had two others scheduled, but I was the only person to show for the first and my wife and I skipped the second in favor of some much-needed downtime.

I also had a pitch meeting with Indie Boards and Cards for a new card game I've been working on.  While the original person I'd been talking to wasn't particularly interested, the fellow he brought along (who happened to be their acquisitions manager) was really enamored with my design and wants to see more, as I continue playtesting.  Small victory, definitely!

I actually made a point to not buy anything this time around, as we've been trying to cut back on unnecessary spending.  I was tempted by a few items, but nothing blew me away to the point where I "had" to have it.  Was hoping that Green Ronin might possibly have Faces of Thedas in, but it's been pushed back until after GenCon.

While Columbus' storied North Market is always great, we always try to get to some of the new restaurants in the Short North.  The Eagle (north as you walk up High Street) may well have the best fried chicken I've ever eaten; get it with the white cheddar grits, which are just phenomenal.  The Flat Iron Bar & Diner (near the Red Roof) has a pretty solid pulled pork, but their jalapeno cornbread--which comes sliced like a massive piece of sheet cake, with a giant dollop of honey-butter like icing--really stole the show.  Other highlights included the massive quesadilla from Fuzzy's Taco Shop and the duck-fat frites from Taste of Belgium.
