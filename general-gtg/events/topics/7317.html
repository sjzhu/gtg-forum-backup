Ten people at Comic Carnival and Tim was able to give out some promos and art prints as door prizes.

 

I don't normally enjoy playing with non-standard decks, but our friends brought their bespoke villain, who is hilarious and fun, so we gave it a go. After winning handily, we looked at our heroes and decided that The Scholar, Legacy, Dark Visionary, and O-X were pretty high-power heroes, so how would we do if we took supposedly less powerful heroes -- Expatriette, Mr. Fixer, Bunker, and, for lack of an obvious fourth choice, Absolute Zero.

 

Not so well, as it turns out. We had to call the game due to time, but Bunker was incapacitated, the other heroes were pretty beat up, and the villain was at max HP.

 

As it turns out, that was probably due more to the vagaries of the villain's deck, though.
