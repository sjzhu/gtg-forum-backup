I have no idea what the official >G Gen Con events are this year, but I think it would be fun to try something, now that all things will be revealed once Oblivaeon is released(which is supposed to happen before Gen Con).

What if we could play through the entire multiverse over the course of the con?

I'm thinking of a marathon/legacy style series of games, as had been suggested elsewhere on these fora, but in-person for whoever can attend Gen Con.

We would play each villain in sequence, roughly in order of the events of the "prime" timeline. People would sign up for the villain they wanted to play, and can choose whatever heroes they wanted to, as long as that hero has not been defeated previously. That's right: if a hero is incapped during a fight, that Character Card is out, and that hero can only be played with a promo character card in future fights!

I think we could have a rough estimate of how long each fight would take, and have time slots for all of the villains, so that people would only be taking a small part of their con experience to help out. I know that at least the first eight or nine villains have a ton of play within the community, so we could get a fairly good estimate on the time it would take for those.

Would anybody want to take part in an event like this? I'm not sure how environments would be determined, and I'm a little fuzzy on how the Vengeance/Villains fights would go, as they are pretty variable in length, but I think it would be fun to have people drop by and help contribute to the preservation of the Multiverse!
