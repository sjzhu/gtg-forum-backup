Allied Command was scurrying.  Intelligence reports being analyzed, maps being updated, everyone trying to find a way to get a small team into Paris as fast as possible.

There were other things going on of course.  The push the coast was going slowly, but not badly, and reports of soviet activity were being carefully monitored.

When Command requested that instead of a “wild goose chase” into enemy territory his efforts might be better spent investigating reports of a mysterious fleet of Zeppelins heading East from Southern Russia, Legacy politely replied that the entire future of the world rested on this mission into France, and besides Zeppelins vs. the new fighters, they were of minimal threat.

It was now July 7th.  Omnitron’s History had Blade first spotted in Paris on the 8th.  By the 11th he would be gone, and they would have a very hard time preventing him from damaging the timelines after that.  The best plans they could come up with had them in Paris on the 9th, but with only a 60% chance of success.

“It’s the best chance we have team, I know we’ve only known each other a short time, but you’ve all shown you can handle yourselves, and we all share the same goal:  Preventing this madman from changing the results of this war and wrecking your world.”  Legacy was just getting warmed up, when a blonde woman approached, MP’s on either side of her.

“Hello there gramps, I’m sorry to do this but I’ll be taking over this mission.”  Paul looked at her, she looked familiar, her face was hard, but honest, looked a bit like the photos of his mother when she was younger.

“I’m sorry miss, but I don’t know. . .”

“Gentlemen, I need to speak with Mr. Parsons here for a minute, if you could give us some privacy.”

Everyone obeyed.  Whoever this lady was she had an aura of authority about her.  Anthony liked her immediately, he couldn’t state why, but he trusted her.  He was pretty sure she knew what she was doing too.  It seemed that she had the same effect on Legacy, that man towered over everyone else, but this lady, she was more than his match, he watched the two, he couldn’t imagine Legacy standing down from this mission, not now, not when he was . .  hugging her?  “What?” he spoke out loud.

The others looked, Legacy saluted the young woman, with tears in his eyes, then turned and walked the other way.  The woman turned and walked towards them, drying some tears from her own eyes.

“Lieutenant, you have orders to accompany Mr. Parsons, he will need your assistance.” she had tear stains on her face, but her voice was nothing but confidence and authority.

“Wait a minute,” Jim Brooks interjected, “Just who are you?”

The lady unfastened her coat, underneath she was wearing a white shirt and skirt, with a lantern in the middle, a very familiar lantern.

 

“You can call me Legacy.”

Her Story was crazy, almost unbelievable.  She was Paul Parson's Daughter; yet only Stephen Graves knew of her.  "The girl who died," is what he called her.  In her time her father disappearred, he went off to fight Baron Blade and never returned.  After several years of searching she rebuilt the Freedom 4, but with a secret agenda.  She was going to find her father.

Find him she did.  Scattered throughout time and space, diferent versions, different fates.  She fought Legacy the tyrant, tore through prisons and secure laboratories to find corpses and broken shells of the once great hero, but not one of them was her father.  Not one.

Then she found Atlantis, and a small band of old superheroes struggling to survive, trapped beneath the ocean by an explosion of temporal and mystical energy.  She didn't find her father there, but she found a friend, an old woman who knew her, and knew what had happened to her father.  Most importantly, she found out how to save him.

Now, 15 years after his disappearance they were landing in Paris, and her chance was at hand.  Anthony was nervous, as much as he believed Pauline Parsons was up to the task ahead of her he had doubts about the rest.  The Robot and the cowboy kept to themselves, and Graves, that man was just scary.  He was at his best when he was with people he knew, ones he could easily help achieve their best.  He would have to learn fast.

"How do you know we are walking into a trap?"  Graves asked Legacy.

"It's too easy, when Blade covers his tracks no one finds him, that he was recorded being here so consistently throughout the timelines makes it pretty obvious he wants us to find him here."

"Then why did we send gramps and the armored guy away?" Jim asked, as the conversation drew everyone's attention.

"Because that's who the trap is for." Legacy noticed she had everyones attention and turned to face them, sitting up in her chair.

"Baron Blade doesn't have the knowledge we do, he is hedging his bet on destroying my Grandfather by also trying to draw him away from the War Zepelins of Fyodor Ramonat.  If My Grandfather is in Paris, he isn't stopping Ramonat's fleet, and even if Blade failed his father would succeed."

"That's where we come in," Anthony got up and moved toward the window, "we don't belong here, even if we die there's still a chance your Grandpa sets things right."

She stood up, her face as serious as he had ever seen.  "You're here because you know what is at stake.  No one from this world has any clue what the future holds if Blade wins.  You are here to right a multitude of wrongs, you are here to kill Ivan Ramonat."

. . .

Paris was crazy.  They moved quickly, trying to avoid drawing attention to themselves, moving into the old industrial sector, looking for the boarding house Blade stayed at.

They turned the corner and saw him.  He laughed.
