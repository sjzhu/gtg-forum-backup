Sorting out some rules and such with Fido:

The effect on the first side goes off even if you don't take the damage, it goes off before redirection takes place, or if the target is immune.  If Ambuscade cannot deal damage it will not trigger, which will cause the start of turn effect to take place at the start of the next round.  If Ambuscade targets a non-character card it does not trigger, but if that damage is redirected to a hero character card it will trigger on that hero.  If Ambuscade damages a non-character card hero target, then targets a hero character card it will trigger on the character card.

The hero who is targetted gets until the start of their turn to decide, they can post their decision and action(s) at that point.  They can't draw a card on someone else's turn and then discard it to fulfil requirements, but if they do met the requirement they could then draw cards they would have drawn on other player's turn.

On the second side:  I will announce on the environment turn what card Fido finds, the very first responder will decide what happens to that card (in play or under Fido) then I will continue on to the villain turn.
