For a variety of reasons, we decided to make things simpler this year: Everyone nominated will be getting a badge for the respective award! The new badges will go live... Uh, any time now. ![](upload://jmG3LQlF5SuLT7HrfVzYrp9QfUn.gif) (EDIT: Hey, look - new badges!)

Congratulations to everyone who was recognized for their contributions this past year, and thank you to all of you who have contributed to the community. Greater Than Games greatly appreciates everything you do here - and we Mods can't thank you enough for making our jobs simple and enjoyable. ![](upload://v8HPFTp4mIGfU9rdG54voZZlPnh.gif)

We won't be doing rolling nominations for awards next year, but we are going to look at other ways to help strengthen the community. We'll definitely be talking about it, soon!
