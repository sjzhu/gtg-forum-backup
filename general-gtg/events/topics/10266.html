As previously mentioned, we wanted to get some feedback on the awards, especially as this was our first time. This thread is dedicated to the award categories, themselves; we've created a separate thread for thoughts and feedback on the process, how we did things, and the rest of it.

The award categories we used this year were:

  1. Inspiring Presence (Morale Booster) – The forum member who, through positive attitude and kind heart, did the most to make the Greater Than Games forums a positive experience over the past year
  2. Bolster Allies (Funniest) – The forum member who was best at making people laugh and providing humorous moments over the past year
  3. Truth Seeker (Lore Master) – The forum member who has done the best job answering questions (rules, facts, mythology, etc.) and otherwise providing useful and accurate information over the past year
  4. Harmony (Top Debater) – The forum member who is best at rationally debating positions (without turning it into a fight) over the past year
  5. Exceeded Expectations (Top Contributor) – The forum member who contributed to the Greater Than Games community with tools and/or accessories the most over the past year
  6. Bring What You Need (Top Wikier) – The forum member who contributed most to the Greater Than Games wikis over the past year
  7. Unique Capabilities (Top Artist) – The forum member who created the best/most Greater Than Games-related art or fanfic over the past year

 

What did you think of these award categories? What thoughts and suggestions do you have for future awards? How many do you think are appropriate? How many is too many?

Looking forward to reading your thoughts!
