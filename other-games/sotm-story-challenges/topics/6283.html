There's a thread somewhere on the forums about someone who tried to do exactly what you're talking about - stringing together fights where the heroes get a slight rest between fights, maybe heal a bit, but you have to keep going with whoever survived the last fight.  I think the consensus was that it was a fun idea but still needed some tweaking because for every fight after the first, the heroes will not have to go through their normal build-up phase of getting cards into play and so are extraordinarily powerful from the first instant the new villain walks onto the stage.  Tachnyon specifically can be extra powerful in later fights since her trash will be full of Burst cards.

I also dabbled in something similar to his.  I've got a series of scenarios that link together individual fights to tell a story like a comic book arc.  It's different, though, in that each fight is a regular fight.  You can find my scenarios on my website: www.spiffworld.com/sotm.
