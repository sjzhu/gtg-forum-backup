I had an Idea for a mini-game yesterday: minion showdown.
 
Though this has probably been done before, I'll still say it.
 
Here are the rules:
 
-Gather all minions from all villain decks (see below for more detail)
 
-Shuffle them into 1 giant deck
 
-Choose a team of three heroes
 
-Star by playing three cards from the deck
 
-Then play the hero turns.
 
-After that play 2 cards from the deck each turn
 
-Repeat previous 2 steps until you die, or you run out of cards in the deck
 
-If, at any point, there are no villain cards in play, next time the deck plays 2 cards, play 1 extra
 
-If a villain card refers to a villain, use the special ruling on itself
 
-If a Hero card refers to a villain, play it against any villain card
 
-If a card relies on a destroyed environment card, you may use it any ways (eg. Expats RPG)
 
-Post your score (total cards in the deck's trash) in this thread along with what heroes you used and an analysis of what happened (if you want)
 
-Compete for best score
 
Include:
 
-GWV's Minions, Flagships, Dreadnaughts and Thorathians (no  devices or himself)
 
-BB's Blade battalion (nothing else)
 
-All Citizen Dawn's targets
 
-All Omnitron's drones except the repair drone
 
-All Matriarch's Fowls and Cohorts
 
-All Chairman's Thugs and Underbosses
 
-All Gloomweavers Zombies and Cultists minus himself
 
-All Apostates Demons except the. Relic Spirit
 
-All Dreamer's Projections
 
-All La Capitan's Crew
 
My results:
 
(This doesn't technically count because I didn't use GloomWeaver, Matriarch, or Chairman because I haven't played them yet and I want to play them normally first)
