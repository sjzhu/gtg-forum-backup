The Hive Master would come out with 6 weaponry and 8 defense.

16 weaponry would deal 8 energy damage to it (16 - 8 = 8), which would reduce it to 6 weaponry and 0 defense. It would not be destroyed. Though you would most likely have won on the next turn.

Ok I'm missing an important rule here … 16 weapon only does 8 damage? why is this halved? I've totally missed this … please explain!

Damage Dealt = Your Weaponry - Their Shields

16W (vigi) - 8D (Hive) = 8 Energy Damage dealt. (Hive would deal you damage in the same way simultaneously)

You always remove energy from shields first. Since Hive started at 6W/8D, it would end with 6W/0D
