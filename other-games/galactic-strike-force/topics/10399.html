Tried to play our first game last night.  After one round we had quite a few questions, some of which I've been able to find the answers to.  We decided to hold off playing any more until we could get some clarification.

1)  When resolving phase effects does it matter which side resolves first?  

2)  When you put an opposition card into play, it goes above the station card?  That is the oppositions play area?

3)  When station cards get flipped, do they stay on top of the station deck they came from blocking it, or are they moved above the station cards with  the opposition ships?

4)  If Grey Hammer engages an opposition ship during the battle phase then scraps a bomb to destroy the opp ship he was engaged with, does he then get to re-engage another opp ship?

5) The Bareg's panel has a Travel phase icon.  That effect takes place immediately during the first round of play correct?

edit:

6)  Can a GSF ship be a secondary attacker against someone else's engaged opp ship and a primary attacker against another opp ship in the same sector?

1) No. They resolve in player choice order.

2) Pretty much

3) They become opposition cards and either activated and scrapped (Boosts) or go into play as a conventional Opposition Ship (Ships). Note, ships over the Fleet Limit in a sector are scrapped instead.

4) No. You only engage with one ship in a given battle.

5) I can see no reason why it wouldn't.

6) No, you can only engage 1 ship per battle phase.
