No. If you play an SF Boost from their starting deck, it goes to the bottom of your deck. This is called recycling. When you requisition cards, they go on the bottom of your deck. There is no discard pile.

When an Opp Ship Deck card or Mission card is scrapped, it goes to the bottom of those decks. No discard pile.

The only scrap (I.e. discard pile) is for Station cards. Once a Station card is scrapped, it goes to the top of the Station Scrap, Opp side up. When a Station deck runs out, you shuffle the Station Scrap, and deal five cards for a new Station deck.
