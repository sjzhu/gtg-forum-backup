I shuffle everything at the start but only shuffle the station cards in game.

No, it does not discard the cards. Showing is up to you as it's a co-op but I always do out of habit. Once you've got your money, you can then play the cards for effects if you wish. When teaching I keep hands open so that it's easier for people so see what I'm doing. when not, I prefer closed hands, to prevent me going Alpha player and running everyone else's game for them.
