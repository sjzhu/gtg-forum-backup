Hey lxcelbrien! Let me see if I can deconstruct your questions and help answer it for you.

Temporary Buffs: IIRC, the only temporary buff boost I know of comes from Conclave of Niphon and the card Inspired Shield. (If there is another I am not remembering, please correct me). It explicitly uses the term Temporary in the card text.

Lets work with an in-game example Lets say you are in the [B] phase. You just ended the damage calculation subphase and can play boosts again before the next subphase happens. You see that you will take 3 total energy damage and decide that boosting your shields is the best play, so you play Inspired Shield giving you two temporary shielding. You can place those 2 shields on the panel if you wish, but I personally set them outside of my Shield Tech stack as to better identify them. So once everyone has played boosts, you move on to the next subphase of damage calculation. That 3 energy damage will come off of your temporary shielding first. So you would remove both of the temp shields and 1 additional from the outermost shield tech (or panel if no shield tech is present, or from weaponry if there are no more shields).

Lets change the above scenario a bit. In the damage calculation stage you find that you will be taking 1 energy damage from battle. But you have no Shields left on your ship and you want to preserve your weaponry. You decide to play your boost card before damage calculation begins. That 1 energy damage will come off of your temporary shielding first. Now you have 1 temp shield left over. At the end of the [A] phase during cleanup you will remove any remaining temp shielding you gained that round.

General Buffs: For all the other cards that grant your ship extra Weapons or Shields, those extra tokens get put directly onto your ship panel and are protected by your installed tech. So when you are playing Vigilance for example (on of the better FlagShip killers in the game), her weaponry buffs from things like Redirect Energy and Cargo Container will go straight onto the panel. Shields from APS also go directly onto the panel. These are considered permanent until removed.

Energy Removal: Energy gets removed in this order. Shield Tech -> Shields on Panel -> Weaponry Tech -> Weaponry on panel. Your Weapons do not get touched unless all your shielding is down. You might have already known this, but from your post it read as if you had this mixed up a little.
