  1. Converter only converts shields from you Ship Panel, not your total defense value. Just clearing that up now. Generally though you are correct, if you had 8 shields on your ship panel, your weaponry on your panel would change to 4. But this conversion only happens during the Icon activation of the Battle Phase and works just like every other effect with a phase symbol in the game

  2. Converter only converts during the Icon activation of the Battle Phase. Damage Calculation has nothing to do with DrurSkjyld

  3. If you had 4 weaponry on your ship panel while activating Battle Icons and a defense of 6, yes your weaponry on your panel would be lowered to 3.

  4. Again, this ability only activates during the Icon activation phase, just like every other Icon ability in the game. Converter is no exception.

Does that help any? Again, the card only deals with energy on your ship panel and not from any techs. And its an Icon activation, meaning it triggers once at the start of every battle phase with the other icons. After that, any change is permanent until the next time the ability triggers.

It says it only changes the ships panel. So shield tech doesn't increase it, but weapon tech would as long as it doesn't add to the ship panel's weaponry total.

So Apt Countermeasures would increase the panel's shields and hence, eventually, the panel's wepaonry via DrurSkjyld Converter, as it increases the ship panel's shields, but DuraCycling Plating wouldn't. Likewise, Master Crafters could add to the ship panel's weaponry, but it would be overwritten by the Converter. If you'd installed the Colossal Pulse Cannon, however, this would be unaffected as it's not on the ship's panel.
