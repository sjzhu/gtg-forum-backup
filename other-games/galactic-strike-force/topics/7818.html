I don't think that the $40 is meant to make GtG money. I think it was setup in order to separate the really serious playtesters from the poeple that just want a sneak peak. If GtG made it free (or even a small fee), many people would sign up just for the sneak peak with very few of them really interested in putting in the time and money (you have to print everytihng yourself) to help make a better product.

The $40 price mark was also set that high in order that people really take it seriously. You always treat something better and with more respect when you have to pay a significant amount for it. At that price, few people will be a playtester just to have a sneak peak, but rather you have to really consider whether or not you have the ability and time to contribute something meaningful.

As an example, I started giviing CPR classes at the school I work at. The first class was free (the school paid for it), but the rest cost the students money. The first class had the most students, but it was the students in the other classes that really took the class seriously.
