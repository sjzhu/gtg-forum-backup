Why I'm not a playtester:

1) I generally prefer to play a game that has the kinks worked out.  I don't have time to (and my family/gaming group doesn't have the patience for) playing a game that isn't really ready yet.

2) It cost extra money during the SotM Kickstarters to become a playtester and I didn't want to pony up. ;)

This worked out fine for me for SotM, because the only things to playtest were expansions.  I didn't have time to play lots of games to see if cards were balanced, and it was easy enough to wait for the release of the actual expansion to have my "Christmas morning" moment when the box arrive.

However, for GSF, my curiosity/interest in getting a look at the game before it came out was very high.  I even tried a couple of times to get ahold of Christopher to see if he'd let me become a playtester even if it just meant that I'd be a fly on the wall to read the chatter and examine the rules, without the expectation that I'd be able to participate with actual gameplay reports.

Christopher's a busy guy though and he never replied, so it never happened. That means you all get to read my Monday-morning quarterbacking feedback now instead. ;)
