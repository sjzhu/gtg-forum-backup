We are going to be holding another Official Sentinel Tactics Tournament at PAX East in Boston next month. If you will be there, we would love to have you enter the tournament! If you will not, we would love to have you watch live at twitch.tv/greaterthangames! Here are the details:

  • Admission: Free with PAX Badge
  • Start Time: 11am, 7 March 2013
  • Location: Booth 401 in the Tabletop Hall at PAX East
  • Format: 3v3 Skirmishes. Register with a team, or as an individual and we will assign you to a team! Same rules as our PAX East tournament, different map.
  • Prizes: $600 to First Place Team, $150 to Second Place Team, $75 to Third and Fourth Place Teams. First and Second place also qualify for the World Championship Sentinel Tactics Tournament on 1 August 2015 at Gen Con in Indianapolis, IN and earn a free 4-day badge to Gen Con.

We hope to see many of you there, and even more on the Twitch stream!
