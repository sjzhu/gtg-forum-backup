Since getting into Tactics, I have been introducing it to my gaming circle. Most of the time it is hard to get 6 of us together to play standard tournament format, so we have been doing something that seams to work well for us. Thought I would share.

2 vs 2 Format
Works the same as standard, with just two on each team. Each player controls one character, play to 3 encaps. Play is quick and we have not gone the full 50 minutes yet.

If you want to make an afternoon/evening of it, after the match set the charaters used aside and draft another round. Repeat until you have drafted all the characters. Forced players to learn the other characters and how the play. Who knows, you might find a new favorite.
