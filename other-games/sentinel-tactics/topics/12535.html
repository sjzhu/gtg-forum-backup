I've updated the scenario a little bit.  I fixed some formatting and how the rules sectioned out for hopefully more clarity.

Mostly, though, I pretty completely redid how the villians move and act.  They each have a different 'flavor' now.

The T-Rex goes after the biggest target he can see.
The Raptors now use pack hunting and go after whomever hurt them last.
Thugs pick off the weakest targets around, usually Civilians.

The idea was to give the villain targets some differentiation and more character.

If you have a chance to give this scenario a try, please let me know how it goes for you!! :D
