Short answer: Proletariat is essentially the Absolute Zero of the Tactics set, a high complexity character who is not as instinctively easily to "grok" as many of the other characters.  Also, because he is so positioning dependant, he is at the mercy of the map setup more so than most other charcters since he is melee only. 

 

That being said, he's extremely powerfull when played by someone who knows how and I expect we will see him more in tournaments that have more experienced players

I think the meta and maps have a lot to do with prole not being used.

What we need to see is one really good Beacon-Prole match, but I don't know if anyone is going to be able to pull it off in a big match on the current map style.

Proletariat is a great stall and finish character, but in wide open maps his natural tendency to cluster makes him pretty easy to control.

He is also pretty easy to counterpick, Baron, Operative and AZ all can just crush Prole through defensive sacrifice or shared burden.

I wonder if we'll see Prole at all really, because we are going to add a ton of characters to the roster, and Prole is already limited in scope of use.
