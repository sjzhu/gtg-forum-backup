Yay, there's an official ruling on hero destruction/flipping over on BGG:

Essentially, consider the Hero Incapacitation rules to read: "If a hero is destroyed, by either being reduced to 0 HP or otherwise, then flip their Hero Character Card and remove all their cards from the game."

I could have gone either way with this ruling, based on the letter of the law, but given the option between, "Look, you just die when you reach 0 HP, regardless of indestructibility," and "Certain specific situations can make you go into negative HP temporarily and perhaps recover," the second is obviously more awesome and fun. So! If the above wording is the official ruling, (which it is), then that makes both Fixed Point from the Time Cataclysm environment AND Unforgiving Wasteland from The Final Wasteland environment much more exciting cards.
