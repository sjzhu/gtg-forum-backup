Just a quick question.... This occured in a game we played earlier and would like some clarification if possible...

It was 4 players (Legacy, Unity, Bunker, The Sentinels). Legacy had 4 cards in play. I was playing The Sentinels who had about 8 cards in play. Unity and Bunker had about 3 i think (but definitely less than Legacy).

Then Citizen Dawn drew this card.

Citizen Summer: At the end of the villain turn, this card deals the 2 heroes with the most cards in play 3 fire damage each.

So how does the damage work in this case? Is it...

1) All 4 members of The Sentinels take 3 damage, then Legacy takes 3 damage.

2) Two members of The Sentinels take 3 damage. No one else takes any damage.

3) I get to choose one member of The Sentinels to take 3 damage. Legacy takes 3 damage.

I'm not exactly sure what the ruling would be in this case, but we went with option 3 after some debate on rule interpretation.
