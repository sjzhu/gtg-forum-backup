Basically, at the beginning of the turn, you do anything that says at the start of the villain turn. In Omnitron’s case, you flip his cards (but only once). Then you simply play a card. After that, you do anything that says at the end of the villain turn. In Omnitron’s case, that is either play another card, or reveal from the trash, depending on which side he is on.

If a card from the Environment or Villain deck is played during the play step of the turn, it takes effect during the current turn unless it is a start of turn effect, then it will take effect the next turn, as it was not in play at the start of the Environment/Villain turn.

Example:
Start of Environment turn: Do all Start of turn effects on Environment cards in play.
Play an Environment card from the top of the Environment deck.
End of Environment turn: Do all End of turn effects on Environment cards in play.

Also all effects should happen in the order in which they were played (at least that’s how we play it).

Basically, you do read and follow all text when the card comes into play. However, “At the start of the environment turn, this card deals the 2 targets with the highest HP 5 melee damage each,” actually sets up a trigger. The inner effect is then only caused by the start of environment event. You still follow all directions on the card, it’s just the directions set up a trigger rather than have any actual effect. Also, Omnitron and Megalopolis are of the easiest setups, thus explaining why you seem to have lack of a challenge.
