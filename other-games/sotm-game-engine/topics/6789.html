Interesting.  I had been playing more or less that way, but never followed it to its natural conclusion.  I think that's how to solve Ambuscade's "Infinite Card Combo".

The combo in question relies on Quick Stim Patch, Run and Gun, or Armed and Dangerous to keep drawing each other.  If the card doesn't go into the trash until each part of the card is done, all of those cards could theoretically play themselves in order, but none of them would go to the trash until they are all done, thus indicating that Ambuscade can't just keep playing cards until everyone dies.

So, I think you're right, Spiff, but I never considered exactly what that meant. 

You can really see this principle in full effect with Akash'Bhuta's Disrupt the Field.  That one really causes headaches.
