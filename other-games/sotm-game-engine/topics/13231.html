Ok I can understand that.

But on thinking about it now you hit another problem. If the check occurs after the One Shot enters play but before you perform any action written on the card itself then every card that is played will play every card that comes after it (because none of them use their innate "play a card" text before the manifestation check plays a card).

The last card played from Infinitor's deck (Card Z) it will do its thing and then try to play a card from Infinitor's deck, but because they are all in play it  will have nothing to play, not even from his trash. It will resolve and then enter the trash.

Card Y will then resolve. It will have a card to play, Card Z, once that is played there will be nothing to play so cards Z & Y will enter the trash.

Card X will then resolve. It will have a card to play, Card Z, which will itself have a card to play, Card Y. Once that is played there will be nothing to play so cards Z, Y & X will enter the trash.

etc etc.

 

I guess it's not an infinite loop but it will play Card Z 10 times, Y 9 times, X 8 times, etc, more than enough to finish the game one way or another!
