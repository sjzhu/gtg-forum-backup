You keep going until you stop getting birds, but it seems like you’re doing something wrong. The birds you mentioned deal damage during the “Start of Villain Turn” phase. At the time you’re playing cards, it’s the “Play a Villain Card” phase. They won’t deal damage until the next turn.

Yeah, the birds come “for free” since each one makes you draw another card. The thing is, all the birds do damage at the start of the villain turn. If you have someone like tempest, all birds will die before they do damage…so the only source of damage from the Matriarch is her Brood mother’s revenge power (when a bird is killed she does H-2 damage)
