phantaskippy:

I am not too thrilled with it because it can target Villain decks, and discarding the top card of the villain deck is pretty dang powerful in many circumstances.  To do that and put a hero card in play is really strong, even for Adept.

As far as I can tell, those circumstances are limited to the following:

a) The Wraith, The Visionary or Nightmist saw multiple horrible cards on top of the Villain deck and had to put one back on top.

b) Tempest used Into the Stratosphere to put a card on top of the Villain deck, and you want to get rid of it.

c) You are playing against Spite and want to get more Victims on the table.

 (c)(i) The Visionary put Thiago on top of Spite's deck with Suggestion.

d) You want to trigger a reshuffle.

 

In any other circumstance I can think of, you would prefer to reveal the top cards of two Hero decks so that you have a choice about which Hero card to put into play, and don't get randomly hosed by having to decide whether you would prefer to put the Electropulse Explosive or Inventory Barrage in play.
