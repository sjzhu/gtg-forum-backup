Because I simply cannot comprehend the awesomeness of Guise, I have a question about how he takes some of Naturalist's powers and makes them better (by making them magenta).

The card in question is, I believe, "I Can Do That, Too." If I recall, the card allows Guise to use a power located on another hero's character card. In this case, because I thought it would be funny, I used Naturalist's power to shapeshift into a Guiselle.

I assumed that the following things were now true:

  1. Guise is a gazelle until he uses Naturalist's power again to become something else (say, a Guisenoceros or an Crocoguise).
  2. Guise has access to the Gazelle's self-healing power, even if the Naturalist is no longer in that form and the Gazelle card is in the Trash, since thanks to Nat's base power, Guise is now 100% being a Guiselle.
  3. Being a magenta and purple gazelle was pretty fun at first, but then it got lame, and then it was fun again, and then lame again, and it evened out to "meh" after a few rounds of cavorting about while the Naturalist breaks stuff in crocodile form.
  4. The Earth is 6000 years old and made entirely out of spackle.

Did I handle that right? The power's shapeshift effect doesn't seem to end until Naturalist uses it again to shapeshift, and since Guise can't do that without copying the power again (or doing Oh Yeah I'm That Guy), then he's a gazelle for life, pretty much, right? And since he's a gazelle, he has access to the gazelle form's power, correct?

The mental image of Guise being a Guiselle and then being stuck that way for the rest of the game is so hilarious that I shot milk out of my nose. I just want to make sure that I carried out the rules correctly.
