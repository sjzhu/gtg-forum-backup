Some cards mention powers that deal damage. For instance, Unity's Hasty Augmentation or the title Living Weapon. In most cases it's clear what they mean but what about when a power lets a hero play a card, and then that card deals damage? 

For example, suppose Unity plays Hasty Augmentation, and then lets Omnitron-X use a power. Omnitron-X Timeshifts itself, revealing Sedative Flechettes. Do the Flechettes do 2 damage, because the damage comes from playing the card, or 4 damage, because playing the card was part of using a power?

More complicated still, what if Omnitron-X Timeshifts Ra, who reveals a Fire Blast? Or if it Timeshifts Wraith, who reveals an Impromptu Invention and uses THAT to play a Throat Jab? In some sense, all of the events trace back to Omnitron-X's power. But in another sense, each new card seems like a new source.

Is there an unambiguous answer?
