Not only that, but even the cold damage to himself would be increased.

 

Per the Vengeance Rulebook:

Only damage dealt by targets with a nenesis icon to another target with the same nemesis icon is increased by 1.
So if Atum decides he wants to burn Geb's biscuits he would get a +1 to his damage, because it is a target dealing another target damage when they share the same nemesis icon. However if Atum wants to burn his own biscuits for whatever reason, there would not be a +1 since it is a target dealing itself damage and not another target.

So I can't see how an agruement saying that dealing themself damage would be increased, when the rules are very clear on how it is intended to work.
