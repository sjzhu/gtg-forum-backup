mmm… not quite.

Citizen Dare doesn’t count as a citizen during setup, therefore he is not played. Set him aside without effect, just like you would if you drew Devestating Aroura or something, and draw more cards until you have 3 Citizens out. Then shuffle Citizen Dare and all the other cards that were not citizens back into the deck and begin play.
