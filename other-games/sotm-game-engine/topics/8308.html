My criticism is not that the game has errata; all games do, it's pretty much inevitable.  My problem is with the idea that we're using the same word for both an action and its result.  "Dealing damage" in almost any other gaming context, refers to a total output by the actor, as distinct from "taking damage", referring to the amount actually incurred after defensive effects are applied.  

This is the context in which the vast majority of us interpret the rules and cards, as evidenced by the most cursory scan of these forums.  The overloading of the verb "deal" merges these concepts and causes more confusion than it solves.

In no way am I trying to be "mean" or say that the game sucks, or anything of the sort.  I've stated that I love the game, and I do.  I love my dog too, but that doesn't stop me from protesting his behavior on occasion.

My intention with these cards is that damage must be dealt to the hero in order to get the benefit. If I were to reword them to make them more explicitely clear on the cards, they would look a little different. Let's take a look at Fanatic's "Embolden":

Current Wording:

Play this card next to a Hero character card. That Hero may use an additional Power during their power phase. At the end of that Hero’s turn, either Fanatic may deal them 2 radiant damage or destroy this card.

So, to rephrase this to make it clearer, I need to make sure people know that you MUST take damage in order to keep this card around.

Proposed Rephrasing:

Play this card next to a Hero character card. That Hero may use an additional Power during their power phase. At the end of that Hero’s turn, Fanatic may deal them 2 radiant damage. If no damage is dealt to that hero this way, destroy this card.

So, in order to make this work elegantly, we're saying that at least some damage has to be done, but not necessarily a total of 2 damage. So, if that damage is redirected or prevented entirely (or if Fanatic chooses to not deal the damage at all), the card will be destroyed, but if the damage is reduced by one, the card survives. This is a bit different than my original intent, but I feel that it satisfies the thematic intent, and also is probably not quite as rage inducing, as a bit of DR won't destroy your cards. The phrasing gets a little wonky with the "no damage is dealt to that hero this way" part, but I want to make it clear that the hero benefitting must pay some cost. There is a price for greatness.

Let's give Pushing the Limits the same treatment. Someone (I think Matchstick Man, maybe?) said something about not considering Tachyon a cards for damage machine. I absolutely consider her that way, both mechanically and thematically. In Pushing the Limits mode, she is running SO FAST that she hurts herself. For her to be getting all these extra resources, she has to be getting the negatives as well. If she manages to throw that damage to someone/something else, well, good, but that means she's not getting the resources - she's getting an alternate benefit (specifically, dealing damage).

Let's see how this goes:

Current Wording:

You may play an additional card during your Play phase and draw an additional card during your Draw phase. At the start of your turn, either Tachyon deals herself 2 sonic damage or this card is destroyed.

And now, let's make it clear that there's gotta be damage or this card goes away.

Proposed Wording:

You may play an additional card during your Play phase and draw an additional card during your Draw phase. At the start of your turn, Tachyon may deal herself 2 sonic damage. If no damage is dealt to Tachyon this way, destroy this card.

Ok, so, you probably saw that coming. Same thing as the last time, but this time Tachyon herself is called out as the one having to take the damage. Embolden has to be a bit more versatile in its targeting, since it can be played on any hero. Same thing here, though: Tachyon is getting extra stuff. She's gotta take the extra damage to keep getting extra stuff. Not necessarily all two of it (and this also frees us of the damage type requirement), but she MUST take SOME damage.

Does that help? I realize it's a bit of a revision of Ruling 15, but it also nicely bridges the gap between my original intent and the desire for making these cards still "viable". That said, I've been playing it "right" from the start (go figure), as have all of my in-person playtesting groups, and Legacy and Fanatic are still great friends. Tachyon is almost always Pushing the Limits. Ra is absolutely a damage-crazed powerhouse. I'm not too worried about anyone being broken by this ruling. But I do apologize for any confusion. That is no fun at all, and I totally get the upsetting nature of having a core understanding broken like this. Thanks for sticking with me through this! Hope this write-up helps!

  • Takes/taken = damage after modifiers/reduction effects.

At the end of that Hero’s turn, either that Hero takes 2 radiant damage from Fanatic or destroy this card.

Again, the damage would be modified by Fanatic's damage modifiers as it's coming "from" her, but since the damage is being "taken" by the other Hero, it's already beyond any damage reduction effects.
