the wraith deals 1 target 2 melee

Coupled with the clarification of Tachyon's Hypersonic Assault card in the rules FAQ, it now seems rather clear that the intended effect would be for Wraith to deal 2 melee damage to Baron Blade, at which point Blade can no longer deal damage until the start of Wraith's next turn, so the backlash field would be prevented from dealing any damage in response.
