Comparing Parse to Adept or Visionary I think of them like this:

Visionary hanging back a bit, but still in the midst of things, subtly manipulating everything that happens, laying to waste the best laid plans of villains with a subtle tweak here and there, and an occasional misdirection.  They can't understand why that Flagship is so late, or why that EPE backfired, but we all know.

Adept is hanging back for the most part, directing the battle with a timely card play here, an extra power there, some DR where needed or a timely boost to damage.  Occassionaly he moves up front and gives his allies a much needed break, but mostly he's at his best making everyone else get more out of each round.

Parse is all attack, she's focusing on the badguys and showing everyone the things they need to know to win the fight.  Often that means firing the first shot herself, or the first 3 or 4 even.  She's actively going after the Villain, Visionary might confuse the Flagships orders so it shows up late, Parse is crashing it into the ground as it arrives.  The heroes are laying seige to the fortress and Parse is sapping the walls.  She isn't there to patch up your wounds, or take a shot for you, she's there to make sure the badguys are exposed and defenseless, their toys broken and their hiding places flushed open.

I still prefer playing Visionary, sitting back and laughing maniacally as you steer the entire multiverse to your desired result, only occasionaly having to step forward and reveal the true nature of your power, but Parse is a lot of fun too.  She's the support version of Fanatic.  That's how I'd describe her.

And thats what took /me/ a long time to realize. You play a CCG by looking for the best card for the situation - usually with consistancy. I need this card against this matchup, so Ill use this card to go get it (or maybe already have it in my hand) and I want to dig for this card if I can, because once I get it it's an auto win. But the leson I learned was to forget there is the rest of the deck. Forget you have any cards that exist /except/ those in your hand. And find the best situation that fits the cards, not the best card that fits the situation. Its a subtle difference - and in the end, neither way is a bad/wrong way to play at all - certainly the CCG way will win you games and will give you victory.
