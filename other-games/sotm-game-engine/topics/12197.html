First of all, welcome to the forums!

Second, where did you read that his tools can be in play simultaneously? His styles definitely can, because their condition is destruction. And actually, once Fixed Point leaves, he would STILL be able to keep them, since the destruction trigger took place upon each tool entering play. So together you get 2 attacks at +1 damage that deal damage to all non-hero targets.

1. pick a non-hero target, then deal 3 damage to all non-hero targets twice.

3.  repeat step 2 until there are no targets that haven't been picked already.

After thinking for a while, I’ve come to think about the destruction like this:

  1. Target takes damage, reduced to 0 HP.
  2. Check if it should be destroyed.
  3. Fixed Point is responsible for the card to not be destroyed.
  4. Fixed Point is destroyed for whatever reason.
  5. Fixed Point ceases keeping the target from being destroyed.
  6. The target is destroyed.

When I look at it that way, I think there are 2 cases I could believe about who detroyed the target (and I prefer the first one):

  1. Fixed Point is responsible because it was previously responsible for the target staying in play.
  2. Nothing is responsible. No effect that would normally cause a state change to destruction was affecting it.

I never thought about this before. Thanks for the opportunity to expand my thinking.
