Fixed Point makes cards indestructible. Oneshots though are not destroyed when they resolve; they are put into the trash, so how they work is unaffected by Fixed Point. That cuts on a lot of shenanigans.

As for the difference between oneshots and ongoings, and static effects on them, there isn't any. Oneshots are just cards that happen to be put into the trash as part of their resolution. So if a hypothetical one shot had a text reading "Hero deals 1 melee damage to target. Damage dealt by Hero is increased by 1 until the start of his next turn." then Hero would end up dealing 2 damage with that card, regardless of the order of those two sentences.
