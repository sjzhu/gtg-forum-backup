If I am remembering it correctly extra power granting in the online game works as so:

1.  Granted extra power.

2.  If the hero has a power phase coming this turn, resolve extra power(s) then.

3.  If the hero does not have a power phase this turn they must use the powers immediately or not at all.

 

This works really well within the intent of the cards.

As to the "may" in Overdrive, I can see that argument, but the key is that the granted power wording has no relationship with your power phase or normal power use, it doesn't work at all like Pride and Prejudice, which requires a power use first to grant the second power.

If Overdrive was worded

If you use Strike this turn you may use it again for free.

Then the choice would be use strike twice or use a different power.  It would also not grant out of turn power uses.
