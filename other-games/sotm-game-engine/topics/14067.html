1.  Play area is an old term (you can find it by clicking on the Gameplay tab on the Sentinels of the Multiverse tab {page 2})

Play area is just a term for the area where your cards go.  Cards that go next to cards in your play area are also in your play area.

2.  If Chiquito is still worded "The first time a player discards a card each turn" then yes, he gets all three.  Ermine and La Capitan are all the fun.

3.  The problem is you went full circus.  Don't ever go full Circus.  You have to keep that thing in check.  (Isn't it awesome though)

A card played on someone else may be in their play area, but it's still your card, just as if you get a villain card played on you (such as Infection or Isolated Hero) it's still a villain card and belongs to the villain in question :).
