Those are two separate actions. First, you draw three cards. Once you have drawn your cards, they are a part of your hand. The second part is then to discard two cards. Discarding cards is defined by choosing cards in your hand to be placed in the trash. As these are two separate actions, there is no requirement for any cards discarded to be part of the cards drawn. (I believe the only time more than one action can be grouped as a single action is if a single action has multiple targets defined (e.g. all non-hero targets, or two targets with the highest HP, etc). Even target lowest HP and highest HP are two separate actions.)
