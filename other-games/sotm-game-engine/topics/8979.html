If you play an ongoing or equipment card, it is now "in play". It doesn't matter where it is (ie it could be a Compounded Bow out in front of you or a By Any Means on that bad guy over there), it's still your card, belonging to you. If you become incapacitated, for example, that card will go away just like all the rest of your cards. Therefore, any card from your deck which you have put into play, regardless of where you put it (if it was a card that could be played against someone else), counts as "one of your cards in play". Conversely, if a villain (eg Kismet or Plague Rat) plays one of their cards on you, it's still their card which they have in play.

Basically, a card from a deck will always belong to that deck, regardless of who it's played against. So you can't have, for example, Freedom-6 Tempest use his base power to destroy that Jinx that just got put in front of him, because it isn't his to destroy.

To pick the obligatory nit, one-shots would probably also count as "cards in play" while they're still resolving...if you played a hero card such as Fanatic's Brutal Censure ("Fanatic deals one target 2 radiant damage.  You may draw a card", and the 1 fire damage triggered effects which resulted in a villain card being played, and that card was Scattered Mind, the one-shot might still count, since it doesn't trash itself until after you draw your card (or choose not to). I can't think of a way for this to come up when playing against Kismet, nor am I entirely certain it can happen at all, but there's precedent for similar things - Akana in the Tomb of Anubis plays an environment card whenever a villain target is destroyed, so if there were a card (let's call it Anaka) in a future Environment which did exactly the reverse (and the Environment contained at least one Target), you could conceivably be fighting Kismet in that Environment (the "Womb of Hathor", perhaps?), blow up an environment target with Brutal Censure, and Anaka would put Kismet's top villain card into play, which could conceivably be Scattered Mind.  So in that case, Brutal Censure could still count as a card in play, even though it won't be in play as soon as "the stack" clears and the unwritten final line of "text" on all One-Shots, "put this card into the trash", occurs.
