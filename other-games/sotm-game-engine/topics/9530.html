#1
In conventional SotM, the H mechanic allows villains to scale to the number of heroes opposing them. Vengeance offers a cool new mode of play where the number of villains scale to the number of players instead of the potency of the villain cards. As such, all the Vengeance villain decks and character cards lack the H mechanic. Is there a good way to convert the Vengeance villains to adopt the H mechanic so you can play them like a conventional, stand-alone villain?
 
 
 
Example: at the end of his turn, the Fright Train character card says he does 3 damage to the hero with the highest hp. Converting that to H-1 seems like it'd do the trick, because the default H assumption is 4 (with a minimum of 2 and a maximum of 5). However, Proletariat (and his clones) does 2 damage, which would convert to H-2 which would be zero damage in a 2 player game. Clearly this isn't as simple as replacing all damage values with H + (OriginalValue - 4).
 
 
 
 
 
Failing that, is there a number of players the Vengeance villains would be effective against (e.g. you can play against Proletariat as is provided you play against him with no more than 2 heroes... any more than that and he becomes too easy)?

Well, seeing as the idea of Vengeance is that you play against H of them, this mean that H villain cards get played each turn.  Thus, while you would still need to do something to their hp (multiplying by H might be too much, but scale it some), you could fairly easily make them scaling by playing them like a full Vengeance team.  Just let the specific villain play a card between each hero's turn, and it would be similar to playing against the V5, but just with one.

 

Edit: To be clear, you don't need to adjust their damage to scale with H, as they'll be playing H cards each full turn.
