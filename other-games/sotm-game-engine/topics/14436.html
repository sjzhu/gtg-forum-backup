Villains adds 10 Vengeance style decks that you can use to play the vengeance format, you don't need the Vengeance game to play it.

It also comes with 4 environments that can be played in any game, in any format.

The mini-expansion Villain is a solo villain, it can be played with any other sets, and the Mini-expansion environment as well.
