Because Wrest the Mind redirects damage, putting that sort of power on the Villain Character card neuters the Villain. It can be redirected at a minion, at an environment target, at a hero who has immunity to that sort of damage… even back at the villain itself!

Vicious Cyclone is ‘just’ a damaging card.
