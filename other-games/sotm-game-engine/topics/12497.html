General agreement.  Here's what's currently written in the (entirely unofficial, fan-created, but based on official rulings) Damage Dealing doc: " If there are multiple damage negation effects, players choose the order to apply them. In almost all cases, only the first one chosen will matter, as it negates all the damage, and there is no damage remaining for the other negation effects to work on."

Also, the "destroy this card" on Neutralizing Resonator (or any other card) refers only to the specific card it is printed on.  "This card" does NOT mean "all cards with the same name as this".
