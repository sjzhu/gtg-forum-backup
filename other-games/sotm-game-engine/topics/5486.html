Per original ruling, if there were two Ongoing cards in play, each target would get hit for 1 fire damage + modifires twice (i.e. 2 + 2 fire damage if Staff of Ra is equipped). If I’m not mistaken, new rulings with Rook City expansion will state that “for each” is enumerated for a grand total before applying modifires for a single attack, though that may have changed since I last spoke with Chris.
