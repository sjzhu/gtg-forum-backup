It took me a couple of games to see KNYFE go crazy. Now that I have, I have a reasonably good handle on how do treat this Complexity One hero. Was it obvious to everyone else that she’s like a console fighter game  character?

Here’s what I mean. (I’m omitting draws for clarity) During my mostly weekly game night, we were playing Vengeance, and I had Focusing Conduit-Blade and Battlefield Experience out. I played For the Greater Good, which played “Energy Burn” for 7 and used Battlefield Experience’s power. Net 13 to Baron Blade. This destroyed BE so I drew a card and played Amplified Combatant to get to 17 to the Baron. That was the play phase. Then I used my regular power to net 20 to the Baron and drew a card.

While this will almost never come to pass – too many cards lined up nicely – I can see how it could go on through most of the deck. I felt like I was hitting “Punch punch kick, punch punch kick…”

Does this line up with everyone else's experience with the character?

I didn't get the console fighter feel from her, but I did get a solid feel for her 'chains' of play in the few times that I've played her.  For the Greater Good and Battlefield Experience combine beautifully, particularly if your Conduit Blade and Servo Gauntlet are already in play.  

I find that KNYFE may be my favorite of the Vengeance heroes, actually.  She takes a bit of forethought to build up chains of attacks, giving her a touch more complexity than, say, Ra or Haka.  But, she's effective straight out of the gate and really only gets stronger the more she has in play.  Plus, her ability to kill a target and and ongoing in one shot is pretty awesome.
