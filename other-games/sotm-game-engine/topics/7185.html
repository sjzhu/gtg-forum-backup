For several villians there are mechanics that make the first turn confusing.

1) Omnitron: He is setup factory side up.  Does he flip to rampaging robot on his first turn immediately or would that happen on subsequent turns?  My inclination would be to do exactly what it says and flip immediately.  This also makes thematic sense since he has just become self aware and goes berserk.

2) Citizen Dawn: She has several citizens in play as part of her setup based on the number of heroes.  Does she still play another card on her first turn or is the the setup her first card play phase?  Again, my inclination is to simply have the standard villian play phase meaning she would in fact on round 1 also play another card beside what was setup.

3) Grand Warlord Voss: The same as above for Citizen Dawn.

I guess the ultimate question is, do villians still take there standard turn even after setup on the initial round?
