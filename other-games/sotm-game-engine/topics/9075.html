One day I hope to live the dream of having Argent go Polyphoric {Harp - Supertonic - [Bell - Syncopated - Subdominant (Vocalize - Syncopated)] Subdominant - [Pipes- Scherzo - Scherzo ]} - [Lyra - Supertonic - <Drum - Syncopated - Scherzo> -Syncopated] }.

Polyphoric plays Harp which plays Supertonic which plays Bell which plays Subdominant which plays Vocalize, which resolves, destroying one Subdominant.  Then Harp plays the other Subdominant which plays Pipes, resolves and destroys.  Argent then strums the Lyra during his power phase, and the Lyra plays Supertonic which plays the Drum.  You thus get to stack +2 to damage onto yourself, then deal 3 fire, 3 cold, 3 fire, 3 cold, 3 sonic, 3 fire, 3 cold, and 3 sonic, for a total of 24 damage, all of which can be to the same target or up to eight different ones (say hi to Warlord Voss for me).

With this kind of damage outlay, you can kill Lt. Tamar with the first three blasts, then take out two Gene-Bound Frost-Hounds, two Firesworn and a Banshee, even though all of the Minions are immune to some of your damage.  Sure, it takes twelve cards (trashing three of them) and 2 damage to yourself, and Mr. Fixer will make fun of you for having to work that hard (when all he needs is Charge and a Jack Handle plus Grease Monkey Fist and Harmony), but it's still totally worth it.  The best part is that the 2 damage Argent deals to himself comes right before he starts getting +2 to damage.  Oh, and you're also giving the +2 to one other hero, or +1 to two of them.

Now that's what I call a pipe dream....
