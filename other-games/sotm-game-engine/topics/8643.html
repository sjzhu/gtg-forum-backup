I'm doing a solo game against Grand Warlord Voss right now, and it's not looking good for the heroes.  But I've come up with a hilarious situation which is only slightly different from what's actually in play.  Haka's Savage Mana can store all the minions he kills out of play, and Meteor Storm can make it impossible to deal damage, while Forced Deployment makes it almost impossible to keep any of Voss's minions dead unless they're stored in Savage Mana.  So if Haka killed and carried away all of Voss's minions except two Gene-Bound Guards, who never deal any damage, they'd flip Voss to his Conquering Alien Warlord side, so he wouldn't deal damage himself, but he'd no longer be capable of winning the game through an overrun.  Thusly, if the heroes never got rid of the Meteor Swarm, no damage could be dealt on either side, Voss couldn't get his alternate win condition, and the game would never end.  The heroes would have to shut down the Self-Destruct Sequence every few turns, but otherwise the four heroes on one side, and Voss and his Guards and liutenants and two spaceships on the other side, would sit there staring at each other forever, never daring to set foot outside their respective meteor-proof domes, eternally stalemated.

EDIT - On further examination, you'd also have to have Haka destroy the TCF Conqueror, which is nearly impossible since it's immune to melee damage and he deals nothing else; this is probably only possible with the aid of other heroes (or an Environment effect, not that that applies here).  So it's a spectacularly unlikely scenario - but if Ra or Visionary were helping Haka, it could still happen.  And the idea of a Maori warrior and an Egyptian god single-handedly thwarting an alien invasion, at the cost of being stuck in a technological Purgatory with the invaders until the end of time, is perhaps even funnier than if it had just been Haka.

What other hilariously improbable combinations have you seen come up in play, or theorized about in some vaguely realistic fashion?

This is mildly silly, tell me if I've got the rules right:

Omnitron puts out an Electro-Pulse Explosive.  Visionary, who has a Decoy Projection out, puts Wrest the Mind on the bomb.  There are two other heroes out, one of which is Unity, and she has one bot out.  So when Omnitron's turn begins, the Bomb starts to explode, targeting the first hero target chosen - let's say it's Visionary.  Visionary Wrests the damage to Omnitron, causing him to take 15, while she and the bomb each take 3; she has the Decoy take the 3 damage instead of her.  The bomb now uses its new HP to try and damage the next non-hero target it didn't already try to hit, which we'll say is Unity.  Visionary Wrests that too, so Omnitron takes 12 more, and Visi and the bomb take 3 more; even though the Decoy has only 2 HP, it sustains all 3 of the damage in Visi's place and is destroyed.  The Bomb then tries to hit the third hero for 9, Visi Wrests it again, takes 3 damage herself, then the Bomb explodes and tries to hit a Bot, but Visi Wrests it one more time.  So the Bomb has now "counted" all the hero targets which are still in play (the Decoy having disappeared), and stops exploding, staying in play with 3 HP, while Omnitron has taken 42 damage from his own bomb, and Visionary is out just 6 HP and her Decoy.
