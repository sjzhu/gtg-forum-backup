I love when two heroes really, really work well together.  What are your favorite pairings?  I'm not talking two hero teams, but two heroes that paired up really boost the team and get wins.

Mine:

Omnitron-X and Unity:  Obvious pairing, Omni puts bots in play, Stealth bot saves Omni's components, Omni can heal stealth bot.  My favorite part of their duo is Hasty augmentation and defensive blast, and the sheer lack of cards Unity has that you don't want in play.

Visionary and Mr. Fixer:  They don't have awesome combos like Unity/Omni but they have great synergy for a team.  Vis has sustained ongoing destruction, Fixer has grease gun, Twist the Ether lets Fixer hit with riveting crane much more reliably, or on a villain can make sure driving mantis is most effective.  A slow starting fixer can benefit from Vis base power, or he can team with dark vis to really shut down a villain (knowing exactly when to grease gun in a good thing).  Mostly though Vis and Fixer set up your other two heroes so they don't have to worry about anything but laying down some pain.  Another big bonus is Vis can bring back destroyed ongoing cards, and Fixer can bring back destroyed equipment, really helping the team when that is needed.  Vis/Fixer/Expat/Bunker is a favorite team with my group, and it works really well.

 

Those are my favorite two pairings, and their win percentage in my games are ridiculous (Omni and Unity have only lost to Iron Legacy, Fixer/vis have beaten everyone advanced, with only a few losses)
