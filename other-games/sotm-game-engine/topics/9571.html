FAQ'd.

Here's the text I'm adding to the FAQ, in case that helps with the confusion (or in case I'm totally wrong):

some cards have effects which trigger when a card is played (example: the chairman’s “informant” card, which says “when a hero card is played, play the top card of the villain deck”).  because the playing of a card necessarily happens before the effect of the text on the card occurs, the triggered effect would happen after the card had been played but before it can be affected by the card’s text.

so in our example, the hero card would be played, the informant would cause the top card of the villain deck to be played, and then the card’s effect would be able to destroy the informant.

Christopher originally ruled that when HtR killed a Fowl card, The Matriarch still got to play another card. He was later convinced that the text on the cards did not support that ruling [1], and thus the latest version of the Matriarch's deck has moved the text about playing another card from the Fowl cards themself to the Matriarch's gameplay. 

 

Here's Christopher's original ruling on HtR vs. Fowl cards: https://greaterthangames.com/forum/topic/hairtrigger-reflexes-vs-fowl

 

[1] Order of operations – both HtR and card play triggered on the Fowl coming in to play, so in the past you would do the trigger from the card that was in play first, which would be HtR. HtR would destroy the Fowl and thus the trigger for another Fowl would be gone. Even with the more recent ruling on order of play, players could choose the order. With the text for playing another card moved to the Matriarch's setup, players can no longer destroy the trigger for playing another card. So, even with the more recent ruling on order of play, although players could choose to destroy the Fowl card before another card is played, the text for playing another card is still valid and the other card still gets played.
