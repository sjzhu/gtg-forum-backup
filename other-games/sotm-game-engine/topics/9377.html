No. In the case of a tie, the heroes get to choose. So, one of the two highest takes the 3 damage.

If the heroes were all close in HP and the first hit causes the hero that takes it to be the lowest HP, they would then take the 2 damage as well.

For each instance of damage you determine who is what 'position' (e.g. who is highest and who is lowest), so when 2 heroes are tied for highest and damage hits the second highest only one of them is highest, heroes' choice. The other is second highest and takes the hit.
