Being dealt damage and destruction effects are not the same trigger. Triggers can also interrupt other triggers (note End of Days with Bee Bot). If Cryo Bot is dealt damage, he would typically trigger. However if that damage caused him to go to 0 or fewer HP, a new trigger occurs after he was dealt damage, that being him being 0 or fewer HP. The destruction trigger interrupts the damage trigger, moving Cryo Bot to the trash. You would go be to the being deal trigger, but he is no longer in play, thus not being able to do anything and that's the end of it.

This is how it works for me:

1.  Destruction is triggered, and checks for things like indestructible status, triggers that would stop the destruction via the word "instead," and "When this card is destroyed" text.  I do it in that order.

2.  If destruction is not prevented by indestructible status or "instead" effects you resolve the text on the card that triggers when it is destroyed.

3.  If destruction is not prevented and after any on destruct text from the card itself is resolved it leaves play, the card leaves play and any effects on other cards that do not prevent destruction by the word "Instead" or other means, now activate.

4.  If the card has not been placed elsewhere by card effect, it goes to its associated trash.

 

I believe this is the proper order.
