The difficulty is that he has soo many targets PLUS the other villains and nemeses, and if you don't keep the clones under control, they can quickly gang up on you.
 
Fright Train hits hard, but also takes extra damage at the start of the game, so if he's the only target, you can whittle him down quickly as well. Minor changes for him could make him more difficult pretty easily, but it's usually the other villains that would shut us down.
