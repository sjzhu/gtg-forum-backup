Just ran across a scenario I haven't seen addressed here yet.  It was Spite's turn, with 4 drugs in play.  Absolute Zero had Sub-Zero Atmosphere in play.  Following the order of the card, at the start of turn (because of S-ZA) Spite played a card which just happened to be the 5th drug, and then dealt damage to the highest HP hero.  Then, since the last thing listed on Spite's card is to flip Spite at the start of the turn if 5 drugs are in play, he flipped.  Is that correct?  Spiff's FAQ for Sub-Zero Atmosphere says that "Each villain’s start-of-turn action is always listed before their end-of-turn action on their card," but Spite appears to be the lone exception to this rule.

So is the ordering of actions of Spite's card intentional, or is it just an anomaly?
