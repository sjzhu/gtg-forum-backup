Although I've been playing SotM for years, I still consider myself a noob.  Yesterday I decided to try advanced mode for the first time and figured I'd wade in against Baron Blade for nostalgic reasons.  What ensued was an embarassing beatdown of BB that I had to share:

Basically, we fought in the Ruins of Atlantis.  I don't even remember who all the heroes were because it really came down to Visionary and Legacy.  Pillars of Hercules came out and I managed to hold out against all the extra villain plays until Legacy's turn.  I had two Takedowns in hand, plus Next Evolution.  I played Takedown, used Galvanize and Next Evolution to make myself immune to the energy damage that BB would deal me for playing Takedown.  For a full round I got an extra power, play, and draw with no drawback because BB couldn't play cards.  One Takedown left play and I played the other and used the same two powers as I described above.  On Visionary's turn, I used Mental Divergence to put Takedown from the trash back on top of Legacy's deck.  Lather, rinse, repeat.

For four or five turns, I kept Pillars of Hercules in play while I pounded on Baron Blade.  At one point I actually debated stopping the combo because I had absolutely zero risk of losing, but I decided to see it through to the end.
