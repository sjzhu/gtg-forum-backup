If it says something like “All heroes take 2 cold damage”, you could use it to change damage to all heroes to fire (or whatever else you choose). If it says something like “All heroes take 2 cold damage and 3 fire damage”, you could change it to 2 lightning damage and radiant damage. However, you wouldn’t be able to split it up by hero and say, “Ok, Ra takes 2 fire damage, and Absolute Zero takes 2 cold damage, and Fanatic takes 2 radiant damage, and etc…”
