As you say, no shuffling of the trash into the deck, since it doesn't say to do that.  There's just no Infection coming into play.  However, I think it says to shuffle the deck after you search, right?  If so, then you'd still shuffle the deck even if you didn't find (or didn't bother looking because you knew it wasn't there) an Infection card.  That could be important if some hero or effect had allowed you to look at the top card of the villain deck, for example.  Since you have to shuffle it, that card you were counting on being next would now be roaming freely in the villain deck again.
