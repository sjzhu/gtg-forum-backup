#3

A card's effect will only stay in effect after the card is gone if the effect has a specific duration which hasn't been met yet. For example, I think the power on Ra's Flesh of the Sun God makes all heroes immune to fire until the start of Ra's next turn. If Ra used that power on his turn but was then incapacitated, everyone would still be immune to fire until Ra's next turn came around. The same goes for effects caused by a target (such as Akash'Bhuta's Ensnaring Brambles) if the target is destroyed between turns.

A reason for Ground Pound to be worded the way it does is Omnitron-X's Timeshift power, which puts the top card of a deck directly into play; if you TS a Groundpound, you've wasted your power unless Haka has the cards to discard.  (Combine this with his Haka cards, Punish the Weak, and the multiple copies of Mere and Taiaha, and Haka is generally not a good target for Timeshift.)  Similar things happen if you fight in Atlantis, where the Font of Power plays the top card of everyone's deck, or against the Ennead if you kill Set early (not generally recommended, by the way).  All of these can cause Haka to play Ground Pound even if he's not quite ready to.
