Greywind:
  1. Yes.

To clarify, I'm fairly certain that the revealed card is not on the top of the deck while you're Enlightening yourself.  Effects such as Infrared Eyepiece say to "put one on the top of the deck (and the other on the bottom)", which would be redundant if they stayed on the top of the deck after being revealed.  So I think they go to a sort of "in-between zone", not in play or in a deck or trash, while the effects of a card are resolving.  You should then always have instructions telling you what to do with the card…if no such instructions existed, I don't know what would happen.  If anyone can think of a card that doesn't provide any, it might serve as a counterexample to what I'm proposing.

La Capitan's "Boarding Party" has cards revealed until a condition is met, some put into play and the rest discarded.  "A Motley Crew" has the villain trash shuffled, cards revealed until a condition is met, some put in play and the rest put back into the trash.

So it seems that "revealed" is a sort of temporary limbo space; not in the deck, in play, or in the trash.
