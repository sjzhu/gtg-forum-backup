Right then, something unusual that came up in a game we played earlier...

Scholar played DDA. When this happens, we normally just go around each hero in turn order, so as Scholar was first, he revealed his top card. DDA again, so this interrupted the first DDA and Scholar revealed his top card again. Know when to Hold Fast - Draw five cards and end your turn.

So...then we were wondering whether the "End your turn" part cancelled the two unfinished DDAs because the Scholar's turn had ended. We decided that because the two DDAs were still in progress, they would still finish. So basically we skipped to the Scholar's End phase (as per Hold Fast), then finished off the two DDAs (both other heroes playing their top cards since rarely do we choose to put a card on the deck from the trash ;)), at whicpoint the Scholar's turn ended.

Did we do this right, or should the Hold Fast in fact have prevented the DDAs from finishing because the Scholar's Play phase had been abruptly ended?
