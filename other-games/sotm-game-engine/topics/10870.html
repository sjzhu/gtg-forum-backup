So, on G+ tonight I tried the new promos (sans Cosmic). In general I really liked them. I only got to use Tempest and Haka, but I saw the other 2 played and got to think about how I would have played them.

Haka gets set up really fast this way. We were fighting Voss admittably, but I had every ongoing and equipment card out except Punish the weak, and that was purposeful. He's fun and he combos well with the Tempest. Ultimately, it didn't feel all that different from regular Haka.

I also played Tempest, and while Tempest himself was a beast, several times this power was a lot less useful than his normal one or any of the ones in his deck. Except when I needed to kill something or at the very beginning of the game to set up a bit, I never used this power. His deck doesn't have enough draw to support that power. I used it a couple times to play an extra one shot, and once to get a piece of equipment out, but other than that I just didn't have cards that justified the damage or the cards I would take away from vicious cyclone. I like this power but to me it seems a bit too open. You almost need localized hurricane to use it effectively, and I think that late game as a set up would be great, but early game it seems more useless. 

Adept's power I don't think got used properly so I want to see it again, but in general it's mostly a different way to play him, neither better or worse.

Fanatic's power is great. She gets a lot more one shots out, which boosts her damage a lot, it combos well with sacrosanct martyr, wrathful retribution and zealous offense really well. It also makes her a bit into a support, which is interesting. The randomness is the only draw back, but it's small. The biggest fear is a poor timed end of days, but the likelyhood of that is low.

In general, I like them and want to play with them some more
