In the case for all decks (and is stated in the rulebook) whenever a deck runs out of cards, the corresponding trash is shuffled and replaced as the deck. Also, this action does not occur until a new card is needed but does not exist (this effects cards which depend on certain attributes about the trash, such as certain cards being in the trash or amount of cards in the trash, etc).
