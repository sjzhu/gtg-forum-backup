Really this is almost the same question as Brain Burn. What he is asking is if having no ongoing cards in play count as no damage at all (and no damage can’t be increased because there is no damage to increase) or if the damage is combined as a single point of damage (which would be zero damage, increased to whatever amount).

Basically, if you’d play three ongoing cards as giving a total of six points of damage when increased by one, then zero ongoing cards results in no damage; if you’d play three ongoing cards as giving a total of four points of damage when increased by one, then the zero ongoing cards would result in one damage. The same effect would then consistently have to be used for non-hero cards that do the same, such as Pterodactyl Theif.

pppery:

If a card does x damage where x is… it does one instance of damage. if aa card does 1 damage for … it is many instances?

Yes.

To use examples in the cards, Visionary's Brain Burn does X damage, where X is the number of cards removed from the villain trash.  So if 3 cards were removed, she deals herself 1 instance of 3 damage.  Tempest's Vicious Cyclone deals 1 projectile damage for each card discarded.  If he discards three cards, he will deal 1 damage 3 times, each one being a separate instance of damage.
