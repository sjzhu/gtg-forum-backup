It sounds like you didn't make any rule errors. That's sometimes a reason a new villain cleans your clock.

Rook City really doesn't help. That place is a ****hole.

I would like to note that AZ should not be doing much healing against either Baron Blade. He ramps up fast. This ain't no Dawn fight. AZ should tear through his 29 HP, dealing it right back to Blade. If you do that then Tempest and Legacy would only need to deal around 40 to win.

Cosmic Omnitron is just hard. It is one of the few villains that is harder with 5 heroes than with 3. I have a short guide to beating it here:
