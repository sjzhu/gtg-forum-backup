If you have a card that allows you to destroy an environment card, then it doesn't matter if it has HP or not.  It gets destroyed, no saving throw.

Characters with environment destruction are wonderful for getting out of tight spots, or for eliminating high HP enemies that you don't have the firepower to take down (or you need it down RIGHT NOW).  A good example would be the T-rex or a Kraken, since they do lots of damage and have enough HP that low-damage teams can have difficulty taking them out.

As for the Argent Adept, he is fantastic, but not without his own drawbacks.  His low HP and setup-inensive nature can sometimes lead to trouble- we had a game once where La Capitan (and her crew) had him down to 14 HP before he could even do anything, and he spent the entire game trying to stay alive while she stole all his stuff (though in fairness, she stole everyone's stuff).
