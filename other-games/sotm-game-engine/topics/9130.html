Yeah, the damage is still coming from the same thing - Nightmist (or anyone else, for that matter) redirecting the damage isn't "picking up" that damage and then throwing it at someone else - she's putting up a big arrow pointing in a different direction so the damage goes over there instead - it doesn't make it her damage. Nemesis bonus is also recalculated if the target changes - Gloomy tries to hit Nightmist so the damage is increased as he's her Nemesis, but she uses the Amulet to redirect. The damage is no longer hitting Nightmist, so the Nemesis bonus goes away.
