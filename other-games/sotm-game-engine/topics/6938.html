I just completed a storage solution of my own.  The Hobby Lobby box suggested by jagarciao in this thread is quite roomy.  The internal dimensions are 15 5/8 in x 12 in x 2.5 in (approximately) for the bottom half, and 15 5/8 in x 12 in x 1.5 in (approximately) for the top half. Link to Hobby Lobby box:

http://shop.hobbylobby.com/products/wooden-artist-case-125005/

You have to unscrew the easel tray inside (standard philips screwdriver will work) and take out the organizer within.  I recommend keeping the screws for the next part.

Rolodex has a desktop organizer tray (The Rolodex 22131 Deep Desk Drawer Organizer) that fits within the dimensions of the artist case.  It is made of metal mesh, and has 3 rows with an internal width of 3.75in each, perfect for Spiff's sleeved boxes.  Here's an amazon.com link to it:

amazon.com

Amazon.com : Rolodex Deep Desk Drawer Organizer, Metal Mesh, Black (22131) 11.75...

Amazon.com : Rolodex Deep Desk Drawer Organizer, Metal Mesh, Black (22131) 11.75 inches long by 15.25 inches wide : Office Desk Organizers : Office Products

You can use the two screws from the easel tray and screw them through the wire mesh and anchor the tray to the sides of the box.  The screws fit pretty easily between the mesh wire, and will drill quite nicely into the wood on the bottom half of the box.  If you don't want to use screws, you can probably just use double sided tape or glue.

This still leaves some "headroom" on the top half of the Hobby Lobby box, which may or may not be desired.  You can tape a mesh bag to the top half to store instruction manuals and incidentals (like the oversized villain cards), or just put some padding up there.  I put a couple of sink/bath corner splash guards (which had peel and stick adhesive, so they were easy to mount) and just mounted a piece of wood paneling there (truth be told, I'm currently using the easel from the Hobby Lobby box at the moment).  If you are using Spiff's card boxes, you won't need to worry about cards getting loose, however.  Even without the card boxes, the cards don't seem to scatter inside the case and tray, even with significant jostling and bumping.

The entire process took less than 30 minutes, with all the materials available.

I'm using Mayday 63.5mm x 85mm sleeves, which have the perfect vertical height for the cards with no plastic extending above the edge of the card.  Not all of my cards are sleeved, currently… just the most beat-up ones (Argent Adept is the character I use the most, and his cards are pretty beat-up).  I'm also still in the process of creating all of Spiff's sleeved boxes for all of my decks (they take a bit of time to construct).
