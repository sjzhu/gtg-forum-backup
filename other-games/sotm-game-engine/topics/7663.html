For Bunker's card which says "a second target" and "a third target", they have to be different because otherwise it isn't a second and third target, it is just the first one again.  Most cards are written like this.

There are a few cards (Argent Adept and Chrono Ranger) which just say deal a target some damage, deal a target some other damage.  This can be the same target both times.

#3

It has to be different targets. Certain cards can hit the same target twice, but they're worded differently; The Argent Adept's Scherzo of Frost and Flame says "AA deals 1 target cold damage, then deals 1 target fire damage", rather than "deals 1 target cold damage, then deals a second target fire damage", or "deals 2 targets cold damage." The former can hit the same target twice, the latter two cannot.

+damage boosts do not effect healing. There are specific effects that boost healing, such as Ruins of Atlantis enviroment and the Scholars Liquid forms.

Argents card that heals 5 targets for 1hp, you can apply what Dypaca and Meerkat have stated above. All 5 must be different targets
