Yes, Decoy Projection is absolutely a Hero Target. Think about it this way: The Enraged T-Rex is an Environment card, as it comes from an Environment deck. It is also a Target, as it has HP. Therefore, it is an Environment Target.
Blade Battalion is a Villain Card as it comes from a Villain Deck. It is also a Target, as it has HP. Therefore, it is a Villain Target.
Thus, Decoy Projection, a Hero Card from a Hero Deck that also has HP, is certainly a Hero Target.

So, when the Gene-Bound Frosthound deals 2 cold damage to each Hero Target, the Decoy Projection takes 2 damage from the Frosthound AND 2 damage redirected from The Visionary. Of course, if it only had 1 or 2 HP when this happened, it would be wise to have it take damage from The Visionary before it took its own damage. But that’s more strategy and less definition.
