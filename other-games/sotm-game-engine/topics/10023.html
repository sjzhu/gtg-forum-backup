Unfortunately, Arcane Cadence doesn't have you draw 5 cards, it has you reveal 5 cards.  But you only shuffle your trash and make it the deck when drawing or discarding from the deck—revealing cards doesn't cause you to shuffle your trash into your deck.  So if you use Arcane Cadence when you have fewer than 5 cards in your deck, you just reveal however many cards there are.  And you process the text of effects in order, so if you've only revealed 4 cards, that should just mean that first you put 1 in your hand, then 1 on top of your deck, etc., in that order, until you run out of revealed cards.  Playing a card is the last part of Arcane Cadence's effect, so if you reveal 4 or fewer cards you won't get to play 1.

Unfortunately there are several problems with your Arcane Cadence loop

Arcane Cadence is not a draw, it is a reveal. If you run out of cards in your deck you stop revealing cards and deal with just the ones you have revealed.

You do the things in order, so you would not get to the "and play 1." instruction if you only had 4 cards left in your deck.

Also, the Arcane Cadence is not put into the discard pile until it has finished resolving, which includes the card you put into play resolving too. As an example if you had play an Arcane Cadence and it plays Vernal Sonata the Arcane Cadence is still in play, and not in the discard pile to put back on top of your deck.

Parse is cool for damage, yeah :). But you can technically get infinite damage by other means - the wonderful game-changer that is Fixed Point. With this, Ab'Zero can continually hit himself over and over again with er...some kind of power setup that I can't remember specifically...but you hit yourself, which means you can hit yourself again, which means you can hit yourself again, etc (I suppose you could also do this with the Isothermic Transducer and a Twist from Visionary, so you deal yourself fire damage with your base power, then Twist turns the Transducer's cold damage into fire and you hit yourself again, repeat until bored). You can go as far into the minus figures as you want but you won't die because of Fixed Point. Eventually you build up a massive charge (it's either Thermal Shockwave or Coolant Blast, I can't remember which) and unleash it for many many many damages and the villain turns into a little burn mark on the wall :). Fanatic can build up some hefty damage by similar means if everyone just gangs up on her deliberately to get her as far into negative hp as possible, then she lets loose with a Wrathful Retribution for All of the Damage :D.
