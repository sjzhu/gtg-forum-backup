I was wondering how people play negative HP when targets are not indestructible.

A minion with 1hp is damaged for 5 hp, do they:

1) take 5 damage, drop to -4 HP and then are destroyed?

2) take 1 damage, drop to 0hp and then are destroyed?

This is important for AZ and Thermal Shockwave, but even for Fanatic (since she can Martyr herself to death if she has Aegis).

 

The original ruling that involves the idea of negative HP was in response to Fixed Point, and it reads:

Certain specific situations can make you go into negative HP temporarily and perhaps recover.

I therefore play it that only when a target is indestructible can they enter negative HP.

But does hitting 0hp actually stop any more damage from being dealt?  Or can you overkill and get benefit for it?

If Fanatic has 1hp and Aegis, can she drop herself into negative HP before recovering to 10, or can she only take 1 damage?

I don't know of any official confirmation, but forum consensus seems to be that the amount of damage dealt is not constrained by the HP of the target, so Shockwave and Martyr both count overkill.  Granted, the person who I remember most recently pointing this out was Foote, and I seem to recall you two tending to disagree on pretty much everything.  But in general, the safest assumption seems to be that there are no "invisible rules" other than those in the Glossary (eg handling of duplicate Limited cards) or on the official website (eg shuffling your deck)...if you're mentally inserting a rule such as "damage dealt to a target cannot exceed its current hit points", where no such rule is explicitly stated, you're probably doing so in error.
