Shock Rounds is worded differently to the others. The others explicitly modify the damage of the power. shock Rounds adds a rider to the power, so you'd resolve the power first, then Shock Rounds would go off and destroy itself.  The power is not used until it is finished, so shock Rounds, uniquely among the ammos, goes off after the power.
