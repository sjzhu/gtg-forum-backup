Investigate has two steps: deal damage and then draw cards. They happen one after the other, so since the Amulet discard is in response to the damage, it has to happen before the draw.

#3

The discard for redirection via the amulet happens right at the moment when Nightmist would be about to take damage, so you'd go "Right, I'm gonna hit myself", then choose to discard two to redirect it to something else, then continue once that was resolved and, in this case, finish off the "Investigate" power by drawing two cards :). Note also that as the two parts of Investigate are listed separately (there isn't an "If you do…" phrase at the start of the second part), you don't have to have dealt or taken damage in order to draw cards - if you're immune to damage or have been whacked by something like Ensnaring Brambles or Ethereal Bonds and can't deal any, that's fine - you basically get to draw two cards for free :).
