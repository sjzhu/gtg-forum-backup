2a) The revealed card is discarded, because it cleary doesn't share a keyword with <null card>.

I can point at Spiiff's FAQ were it talks about

What causes a deck Reshuffle

2) revealing cards does not trigger a reshuffle. you only reshuffle a
deck when you need to draw a card from it, and revealing cards is not
the same thing as drawing. so if you are triggering an effect which says
to reveal the top five cards of a deck and there are only three cards
in that deck, you may only reveal those three and then you’re done.
[br]and

Searching decks/revealing cards

Effects which direct you to “reveal” cards are slightly different from effects  which direct you to “search” for a card. When “revealing” cards, you must  pull the cards off the top of the deck in order until you find the card you’re  looking for. When you “search” a deck for a card, you are allowed to  choose which card you want as long as it satisfies the criteria you’re searching  for; you don’t necessarily have to pick the first one you come upon.

W hat if an effect tells you to search the deck/reveal cards until you find a  card and then do something once you’ve found it, but you don’t find the card?

Answer: Nothing.
 
example: plague rat’s “noxious bite” card says to “search the villain deck
for a copy of the card ‘infection’. Put it into play. Shuffle the villain
deck.” If you search the deck for “infection” and there are no infections
in the deck, then just shuffle the deck and proceed with the game.
 
[br]
[br]Which [s]implies[/s] outright states that you reveal when instructed even if you know you can't fulfil the condition.

Nah, you're not misremembering it. (I'm not a good judge of crazy, so I'll leave that to others. )

Christopher has always (in the past) said you do as much as you can of an action. You can see this in all these examples of previous rulings.

I can understand why Christopher ruled it differently here, though. This is a very different situation for one reason only: the cards revealed are then compared.
