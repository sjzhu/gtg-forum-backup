Here's what I have in my FAQ, based on this:

the sotm rule book says “when a hero is reduced to 0 or fewer hp, remove all of that hero’s cards from the game, leaving only the hero character card, flipping it over so their incapacitated side is up”. this has been officially amended so that it should be read as “if a hero is destroyed, by either being reduced to 0 hp or otherwise, flip their hero character card and remove all of their cards from the game”. 

That looks to me like they aren't actually destroyed since you're told to do something else in the instance when they would have been destroyed.
