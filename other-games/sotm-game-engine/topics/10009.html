This came up with The Sentinels vs. Miss Information

 

Medico's Signature, Hippocratic Oath, states "Whenever Dr. Medico would deal damage, prevent that much damage and instead 1 hero target regains that much HP."

Mainstay had used Block (damage dealt to the Sentinels is reduced by 1)

Miss I. caused each Hero Target to deal themselves 2 damage.

 

Does Dr.. Medico heal someone for 2, or just 1 (because Block would have reduced the damage before Medico would have dealt it)?  What about if Galvanize was also active (Hero damage increased by 1), Would Medico heal someone for 3, or 2?

 

My understanding is 2 health restored in both cases.  I could see 1, 2, being an option (both DI and DR affect before it becomes healing), but not 1, 3 (DI affects it, but not DR).
