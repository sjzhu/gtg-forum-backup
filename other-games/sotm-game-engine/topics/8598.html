To add to the Nemesis comments, another way to tell is to check for a Nemesis symbol - if one target attacks another target and both have the same symbol, the Nemesis bonus applies. Doesn't count for a target hitting itself, though. Also, note that it's not just villains who have Nemesis symbols - Anubis has Ra's symbol, and Rat Beasts have Chrono Ranger's. Finally, the Ennead all have Ra's symbol, and the Nemesis bonus will apply if something causes them to hit each other (eg someone redirecting their damage) - just because they're working together doesn't mean they like each other ;).
