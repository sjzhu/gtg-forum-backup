Yes, and if you're playing the Adept and you bring one of his song ongoings into play (Harmonies, Melodies, or Rhythms), you wouldn't activate their texts either as they can only be activated by stuff that causes Perform or Accompany texts to activate (his base power, instruments, or Silver Shadow one-shot). Note that in this case the song effects aren't powers so if you have the ability to use more than one power in a turn and use two different powers which can activate the same song, you can do this :).
