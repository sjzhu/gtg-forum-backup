Let's break it down in pieces classic play was apply the cards in the order they are played thus the damage type used to always be projectile if Tire Iron came out in second and your choice if Grease Monkey Fist was out second. But that's all in the past.

Now, both of those cards change damage type and well one would argue that trying to change the damage type occurs at the same time, thus by the OoO (Order of Operations ruling) you get to choose wich happens first. You now get to imagine your strike going from melee to range as it flies with the tire iron and the burst in flames if you like classic types or simply go with Rainbow Unicorn Lunar type of damage if it floats your boat. You also get the option of simply applying it in the order they fell on the table if you feel better this way cause in the end the choice is yours. The key component is to have fun...
