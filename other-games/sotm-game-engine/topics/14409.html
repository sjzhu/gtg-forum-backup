Sounds like both are incorrect. You do End of Turn First, so anything that activates at the End of Turn will trigger, such as Raptor Bot, Platform Bot, Embolden, etc. You then move to your Draw phase, so you draw a card. Then you go to your power phase, now using a power if desired. Then you will be able to play a card for your Play Phase. Finally, you go to your Start of Turn phase, so anything triggering then will go like Turret Bot, Divine Focus, Tool Box, etc. It will then go on to the next hero's turn, who will do the same.
