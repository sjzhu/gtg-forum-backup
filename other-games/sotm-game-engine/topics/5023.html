The “10” in the corner the card is Hit Points. In fact, any number in the upper right hand corner of a card is always hit points. In the case of traffic pileup, the heroes have to destroy the wreckage of all of those cars before they can effectively reach the villain. Once “Traffic Pileup” is reduced to 0 HP, it is destroyed.
