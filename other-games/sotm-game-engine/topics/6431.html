I noticed that Backlash Field has been significantly nerfed in the enhanced edition by removing the 2nd paragraph. In any case, how is the original version intended to work? For reference: "Whenever Baron Blade is delt 5 or more damage in a single turn, put the top card of the villain deck into the villain trash".

What I'm wondering is exactly when and how often is the effect triggered, for example, if I deal BB 3 packets of damage during my turn (5 - 1 -1):

(1) It triggers once, after I deal the first packet, because I've delt 5 damage this turn.

(2) It triggers once, at the end of my turn, since I've delt 7 damage this turn.

(3) It triggers three times: after I deal the first packet (because I've delt 5 damage this turn), after the second packet (because I've now delt 6 damage this turn), and after the third packet following the same logic.

I always implicitly assumed (1), although now (3) seems more likely after thinking about it. I guess (2) is a possibily (in this case, you would only check if something happened on a turn when that turn ends) but it seems unlikely.

After thinking about each case, I would say that the first case is the most likely.  It triggers once 5 or more damage has been dealt.  After that, the condition has been met, and has no reason to keep firing (dismissing the third case).  Also, it is stated that it occurs whenever 5 or more damage has been dealt, and not as at the end of each turn, if 5 or more damage has been dealt (dismissing the second case).

The difference between Backlash Field and Lead from the Front is that Backlash Field was worded to trigger once the point of damage has increased to become 5 or more damage, whereas Lead from the Front is worded to trigger whenever a hero target would be dealt damage.  Backlash Field was not worded such as "Whenever Baron Blade is dealt damage, if he was dealt 5 or more damage this turn."  Basically, there is no wording to say it triggers when damage is dealt, only when a certain amount of damage has passed, which can occur at most once (in this case, it states per turn, thus it can occur at most once per turn).
