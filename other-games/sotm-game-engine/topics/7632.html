Cameo: Whenever a character whose card is in play appears on the artwork of a card played from another deck, everyone takes a drink. (Mostly stuff like Heros and Villains, but sometimes you'll get cases like Macbre Spectre on Throat Jab or whatnot)

 

Waste Not: Whenever you don't use a power, OR don't play a card, but not when you skip both for the extra card, take a drink.
