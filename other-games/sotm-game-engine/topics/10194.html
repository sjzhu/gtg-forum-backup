I want to make sure that I'm using the card correctly, so I figured I'd ask the community for some clarification.  Relevant portion of the card text:

The first time a hero target would be dealt damage each turn, redirect that damage to Setback, or remove X tokens from your unlucky pool to redirect that damage to a target, where X = the amount of damage.

In a situation where something is dealing damage to multiple hero targets, I assumed that the first time each of them was dealt damage in a turn, that instance of damage would interact with WT&P, like how if multiple targets do damage in a turn to Ra with Flame Barrier up, the first damage they do to him that turn causes an interaction with Flame Barrier for each of them.  A post from February indicates that WT&P is only triggered once per turn.  Looking at the collected errata put together by Spiff, 

There are some cards which are triggered the first time a target does something each turn. for example, Wraith’s “Combat Stance” will trigger "the first time Wraith is dealt damage by a target each turn”. Similar wording is on Ra’s “Flame Barrier” and on Miss Information’s “Revealed Saboteur” side. Does this mean that the effect will only trigger the first time any target does xyz per turn, or that it will trigger the first time any particular target does xyz per turn?
 
 
It will trigger the first time any particular target does xyz per turn. So, if two different targets damage Wraith in a turn, “combat stance” will trigger both times, but if a single target damages her twice in the same turn, “Combat Stance” will only trigger once. 
https://greaterthangames.com/forum/topic/combat-stance-0

Why would WT&P be handled differently?

 

Interaction between WT&P and another of Setback's own cards - High Risk Behavior.  Relevant card text:

Increase damage to and by Setback to and by villain targets by 1 for every 3 tokens in your unlucky pool.

If 1 damage from a villain target is coming in to one of Setback's teammates for the first time this turn (and let's say it's the only instance of damage occurring at all this turn), and Setback has 9 tokens in his unlucky pool, that's 1 damage to the teammate but it'd be 4 damage to Setback, unless he redirects it (let's say back to a villain target) which would make it 1 damage to that target.  I assumed that he'd remove 1 token from his unlucky pool to redirect the damage, since damage modifications specific to a target don't get carried through redirection.  Is that correct, or would he have to remove 4 tokens from his unlucky pool to redirect what would be 4 damage to himself, but would end up causing the villain target to do only 1 damage to itself?

 

Lastly, interaction between WT&P and a teammate's card - something like the Naturalist's Indomitable Force, or the Scholar's Alchemical Redirection.  Relevant card text:

Whenever damage would be dealt to a hero target, redirect it to the Naturalist.

Redirect all damage that would be dealt to hero targets to the Scholar.

First off, I'm assuming that those two cards, despite different phrasing, function identically.  If I'm wrong, please correct me.  For the interaction with WT&P, since these redirections are not part of the "At start/end of turn..." set of effects, the "simultaneous" redirections can be handled in order of player preference per posted errata as far as I understand.

So, let's say Parse, Setback, and the Naturalist are squaring off against Baron Blade, whose Powered Remote Turret is doing 2 damage to each hero target.  If they all get redirected to the Naturalist first, would that mean that only the first redirect to the Naturalist would be able to be redirected to Setback (or a target of his choice if he removes 2 tokens from his pool)?  If they all get redirected to Setback first and he removes 6 tokens from his pool, would he redirect them all to Baron Blade (assuming WT&P is allowed to interact with them all, per the first question) and thus have no damage going to a hero target so nothing for the Naturalist to have redirected to himself?  Or if they all get redirected to Setback first, would they then all be redirected to the Naturalist without Setback having a chance to remove tokens from his pool to redirect them elsewhere even though he could have enough to do that?  That last one doesn't seem remotely likely; I'm just including it as a possibility to make sure I'm missing as little as possible.

Thanks!

It's different because of the wording. Flame Barrier counters the first damage Ra takes from each target. It's not the first time Ra is dealt damage; it's the first time that target is the source of the damage. WTAP is just "the first time a hero is dealt damage." It's not referring to a specific source or target; grammatically speaking, it cannot be interpreted the same way as Flame Barrier.

 

For the second question: I'd say you only spend 1 token. It would only be increased if Setback were the target, but since it's "spend tokens OR redirect it to Setback," it never touches Setback until he chooses not to spend a token. However, if Setback were the original target, it would be increased, so he'd spend 4 to redirect it, at which point it would become 1.

 

For the third; you've almost got it, except that you can only spend tokens once a turn; either way, you couldn't spend 6 tokens to redirect all of them, and Setback would only ever take 2 damage, if any. So all of it would get redirected to the Naturalist, and the FIRST one would be redirected to Setback or a target of your choice, but both effects must happen. So for the first one you can either choose to

a.) Send it to Setback (or target of your choice) and then to the Naturalist, or

b.) Send it to the Naturalist and then Setback or a target of your choice.

For the first question, it sadly has not been officially stated if it's only for the first hero or each hero. Which is why you were provided two different answers. I will point out that I am in the opinion that it triggers for each hero, since prior to being the "first time" it triggered for every instance of damage a hero would be dealt. Same question for Silverleaf and Rabit—I get your reasoning in a vacuum, but not when we already have the rulings on Flame Barrier and Combat Stance.  The Spiff clarification on Combat Stance tells us that "the first time Wraith is dealt damage by a target each turn," triggers not just for any target, but for each target.  So the ideas that "a target" inherently means just one, or that otherwise they'd have said "each target," don't seem persuasive to me in light of precedent.
