As my boyfriend and I were playing Vengeance yesterday, we noticed a marked lack of reliance on "H," beyond how many villains were in play. Therefore, we *gasp* tried an experiment tonight of the dreaded, unsupported un-house-ruled two player game.

It wasn't bad. The Sentinels and KNYFE took on Frighttrain and Proletariat in the Mobile Defence Platform. A very tight game with little time for building up. Everything came down the wire with lots of tension and no "weird" moments that you can run into with two players in a standard game. KNYFE and two of the Sentinels were knocked out right after Frighttrain went down. A little lucky psychic damage from The Idealist took out Proletariat with only about 6 HP left between Mainstay and the little girl.

All in all, I would probably try it again.
