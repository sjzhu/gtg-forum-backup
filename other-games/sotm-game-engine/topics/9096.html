Anyone who deals multiple instances of damage will have every instance boosted by a damage buff - Fanatic has been mentioned, of course. Another good example is Tempest's Vicious Cyclone, where you would normally deal one damage for each card discarded (up to a maximum of three cards), but say he has a total of +1 damage, that means two damage for each card discarded. If the Cyclone is on the villain with the highest hp (usually the main villain, obviously) and he's wearing his Shackles and is buffed by, say, Galvanise and Inspiring Presence, that'll be five damage per card. Six if the target happens to be Voss. So that's an automatic eighteen damage (assuming Voss has no soak) at the start of Tempest's turn, before he's even played a card, provided he discards three :D.

Someone like Char, the Abominable Snowman, or the "Diplomatic" Envoy, already nasty as they deal two lots of two damage to a target, are made all the worse if they can get their damage buffed by even one point :(.
