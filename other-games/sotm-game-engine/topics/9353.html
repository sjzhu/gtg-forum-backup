Essentially, on your turn you draw a card, then play one. Cards can give you points, take away points or do damn near anything else. The unique bit of the game is that all the cards are player-created; everyone makes some cards before the game starts, and these are shuffled together with blank cards that can be made into new cards during the game. (There's a completed forum game in the Off-Topic section if you want an example.)

So yeah, a Sentinels version would presumably do similar, but using Sentinels as its framework rather than the point-based game. And it would be majestic.
