It is. If you have a means of redirecting (eg Nightmist's Amulet), you can only choose to use it the first time something tries to hit you in any turn. So if the villain is going to attack you multiple times on his/her turn (whether directly, via minions/weapons, or both), you only have the option of using your redirection on the first of those attacks. Whether you choose to redirect or not, you can't then choose to use it again that turn, even if the first hit was for a crappy 1hp and the next one for, say, 5hp. Once it's no longer the villain's turn, the count resets, so if it's then another hero's turn and they have some kind of multi-hit that will include you as a target (eg Haka using Rampage), you can choose to redirect that. Once it gets to your turn, many of your cards involve hitting yourself so if you play one of those, you can redirect that damage. If you then try to use your Investigation power and hit yourself again, you've already been attacked once that turn (whether you redirected it or not), so the Amulet isn't an option. And so on.
