So both Visionary and Tempest have cards that let them convert the damage from an attack to any type. Does this damage have to be a type that exists somewhere on a card already or can they make up any type of damage they want? Like can Tempest use his Elemental Sub-wave Inducer to inflict Care Bear Stare damage and hug the bad guys to death?

I know it’s kind of a pointless question as the only application I can think of would be to force Omnitron into being immune to a type of damage that no one inflicts, but you never know how future cards might relate.
