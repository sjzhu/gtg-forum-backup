I just found myself thinking about how I was looking forward to the putative future electronic version of Sentinels of the Multiverse because then if I was uncertain what the resolution would be to some part of the game I could just see how the electronic version handled it. It then occurred to me that it would also be really cool if the putative future electronic version kept a log of the game state such that the log could be loaded in at some point in the future and one could scroll forward and backward in the log and see everything that happened. So, requests:

 

1) Make the game such that one can specify a game state (decks, cards in hand, cards in play, cards in trash, HP, etc.).

2) Make the game such that there is a log of what happens in the game, that the log is saveable, and that the log is loadable.

3) Make the game such that one can step backwards and forwards through the log of a game.

 

Are these the most important things for the game? No. Much more important would be making sure that the game is correct, easy to use, and fun to play. Still, the items above would be really cool to have.
