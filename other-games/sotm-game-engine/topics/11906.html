Each turn, hero, villain or environment, if an Environment card comes into play, play the top card of the Villain deck.  If a second target comes into play that same turn, you don't play a card.

 

the first time each turn is limiting the effect to only play 1 card per turn.

So when Chupacabra comes into play:  play a card.

The next Environment turn when chupacabra #2 comes into play, play a card.

If Con's Bunker causes more than one target to enter play that turn, you only play 1 villain card.
