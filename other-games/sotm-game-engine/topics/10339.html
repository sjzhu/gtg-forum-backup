You can also stack it - if your chosen target hasn't been attacked by the next time your (Bunker's) turn comes around again, you can apply the damage prevention again to the same target, meaning the next time they get hit, they instead get to ignore (up to) four points of damage. Note that if they were going to get hit for less than four, you prevent all of it but don't get to keep any spare "prevention points" (as with Wraith's Stealth, though that's only reduction, not prevention). Also, it's only one instance of damage - say they're going to be hit by Apostate's end-of-turn damage (H-2 Infernal and H-2 Mélee I believe), the prevention would only apply to the first hit, not the second.
