tzgardner:

Has anyone come up with a good time to ever use Fanatic's Divine Focus or RA's Solar Flare.  I don't ever find myself in a situation where I feel compelled to use cards like these.  Playing a card that deals more damage to self than the villain just doesn't add up for me.

Divine Focus allows Fanatic to toss tons of cards, and then play a Prayer of Desperation to draw a ton. In a three hero game, she will be able to deal 10 damage to the villain target with the highest HP if she discarded every turn, sounds awesome to me!

As for Solar Flare, on the turn you play it, you will deal 2 extra damage. Take the 4 damage from Solar Flare, then on your next turn you will deal 2 extra damage for the one-shot played and 2 extra damage for the power. That's 6 extra damage for the price of 4 damage. Toss in a Flesh of the Sun God and Flame Barrier, which allows Ra to trigger the extra damage anytime he his hit, without triggering Flame Barrier on himself.

Edit: And if the one-shot you played is Flame Spike, you will get an extra power usage, so even more damage!

In my opinion, Divine Focus can be a game-changing card seeing as that you can discard at the start of every turn.  So in a four hero game, once around the table Fanatic can discard at the start of each hero turn and the villain turn and the environment turn for a total of 12 damage, and even more if you have some damage boosts out.  It can also be done for a time and then sacrificed to ongoing destruction or to hero actions that can destroy and hero ongoing (such as Dark Watch Mister Fixer's base power).  Also, if you have Wrathful Retribution around, hurting Fanatic isn't the worst thing.

Because Divine Focus deals self damage at the start of her turn, there is nothing wrong with only keeping it out for a single round. You still get to discard each and every turn for damage (even more with +damage buffs), and there doesn't have to be any draw backs if you dont want to take the damage. Its not very common to have enough expendable cards in hand to fuel Divine Focus for 2+ full rounds. And with Fanatic, maybe you WANT to take that self damage? If you have Wrathful Ret. in hand, then there is little reason not to burn yourself to the ground to make good use of it.

Solar Flare is kinda the same way. Personally I use Solar Flare as a sort of one-shot damage boost, and usually opt not to take the damage to keep it in play. But as Ronway pointed out above, even by taking the full 4 damage you are still way ahead of the damage curve by the end of your 2nd turn with Solar Flare out.
