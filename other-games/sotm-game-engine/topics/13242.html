So, just out of my own curiosity, what do you all consider the worst card for a given villain to flop on the first turn? The one that comes down and immediately signals a game that's going to be, at best, hard as hell on you and at worst, all but unwinnable?

The second is Ravage from Plague Rat -- having four out of five heroes punching themselves in the face from the start before you have the cards out to mitigate it is rough.

What about y'all?

The classic combo is for Citizen Dawn to start with Citizens Sweat & Tears, and follow that with a Blinding Blast.  Congratulations!  Now nobody has any cards in hand, and you'll be losing your card draws every round.  Have fun!

 

For the Chairman, it's hard to match a Contract + Enforcer opening.

For the Matriarch, a double Cohort opening means you're probably screwed, especially if you're playing Advanced.

For Baron Blade, Backlash Field is probably the worst card unless you have ongoing destruction on hand.

Raiding Party is probably the nastiest La Capitan opening.

It's probably a toss up which of Voss's ships is the worse opener.
