I have to disagee. Cards under cards are considered out of play and cannot be chosen as the targets of any effects. Since the Room is hidden, it cannot be destroyed.

Ruling otherwise allows cards under Sudden Mana or La Capitan to be targetted again and destroyed, with ambiguous results. How the cards got under other cards is irrelevant. They are hidden, so not in play, even if still on the playing field. Cards under Sudden Mana are destroyed, but on the playing field and out of play. They are destroyed a second time when Sudden Mana's Power is used, so there is there is precedent to allow them to be destroyed again by another effect.

The only consistent resolution to all three effects is for all cards hidden under other cards to not be targetted by any effect. You can't hit what you can't see.

Basically, you can destroy the cards under Self-Destruct Countdown before you destroy the countdown itself, but if you do then you will lose when the countdown notices that it's pile is empty and blows up.

It's even worse if you're destroying the environment using Terraforming, because even if you don't blow up (say you noticed at the last second, and destroyed the countdown before its last card), then Omnitron will play a card for every card it destroyed.  If you destroy the countdown first then it's not a problem because it wasn't terraforming that destroyed the other cards, it was the countdown.  But if you go the other way, then watch out!
