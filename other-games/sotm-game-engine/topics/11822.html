The problem for playtesters is those definitions have been known in the forums for a long time now.  Us playtesters see those wordings and largely know what they mean, and how to use them, so there really isn't a lot of confusion, and when there is confusion usually someone knows the answer, or eventually Christopher smoke bombs in and gives us a ruling.

Mini-Expansions play around with the rules a lot more than regular decks, and Guise and Wager-Master much more than others.  They weren't very easy to balance and they aren't clean cut when it comes to having them be easy to pick up, read cards and understand the nuances of rule interactions.

 

Also from eveything we've seen I'd say following official rulings and playing "the right way" isn't a big priority of the game's creators.  So go play and have fun.  We can give you rulings and arguments about them, but your group your game your rules.  Wager Master is intentionally Fiddly and game-breaking.  That is the whole schtick behind the deck, so don't be too worried about it.
