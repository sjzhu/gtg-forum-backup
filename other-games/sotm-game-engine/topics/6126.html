Greetings, Sentinels fans!

I’ve made you a set of documents! When we launch our new site in a couple weeks here, these will be available to anyone and everyone as downloads, but we wanted to preview them here first. Most of this stuff the playtesters have already seen, but we love all our forum-goers and wanted to get this out to all of you so you’ll have an idea of what’s coming.

Here are the changes for the Sentinels of the Multiverse: Enhanced Edition heroes!

And here are the changes for the Sentinels of the Multiverse: Enhanced Edition villains!
And also there are changes to the Sentinels of the Multiverse: Enhanced Edition environments!
These documents have 1-to-1 ratio copies of the text portions of the changed cards, so you can print them out and put them in sleeves with your original edition cards to update them. If you do so, make sure you set the printer to print at 100% of the size of the document.

Anyway, enjoy! And feel free to give feedback in case I (GASP!) did something wrong!
