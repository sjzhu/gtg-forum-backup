I'm sure we played this right, but we had an interesting situation last session:

The Tachyon player wanted to play Supersonic Response on the first turn.  The villain had damaged her previously.  However, the card wording is "If that target dealt Tachyon damage since your last turn, she deals that target 2 sonic damage."  The problem is that even though the villain had damaged Tachyon, she hadn't had a turn yet, so the exact condition of the card wasn't met.

We played it by what we assume was the intent of the card and went ahead with the 2 sonic damage.  What does everyone else think?
