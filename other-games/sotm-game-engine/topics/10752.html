For a 3 player team I feel like you need a Tank / controller,/ Healer but with one or more acting as a dps… Id say the best 3 person team would be : Team Leader Legacy, Visionary, Tempest

Legacy has the ability to stop villans from playing cards and will give the team much needed buffs to have greater attacks. Also he has a few dps cards as well not to mention he can take some attacks off the other team members

Visionary should act as a pure controller. With her looking at all the cards in the villain deck and environmental deck you will always know what is coming next not to mention she can destroy ongoing cards and give players a chance to get back an ongoing card that was destroyed

Tempest is your Healer and main dps. Use his downpour to heal the team and then attack with chain lightning and other cards to finish off the boss. If you play your cards right you could easily defeat any boss within 4 rounds or less

I’d be willing to substitute Argent Adpet for Tempest only because he can use multiple powers and he also has the dps cards handy and can destroy environment cards.

Well, I'll start with the one on my list that's not going to get any arguments, and say "Team Leader Tachyon". Extra draws for the entire team? The only power she'll ever need being her innate? The ability to destroy ongoing and environment cards, along with great heavy hitters, multi-hitters, and one of the best villain debuffs in the entire game? Yes please.

My second choice would be Dark Visionary; she's usually just there to make sure that the big cards from the villain deck don't come out too soon, but Twist the Ether is also one of my favorite cards, sometimes played on the villain (especially on either version of Spite or on Gloomweaver's alt), other times played on my third choice to empower all their attacks... And that's a lot of attacks generally.

And finally, Omnitron-X. Seems almost every list on this page has an all-purpose hero, and most of you seem to pick Tempest for this... Personally, I prefer X. His components just allow him to do so much in a single turn, and his plating will usually prevent him from taking enough damage in one turn to destroy them. And then there's Disruptive Flechettes... If the villain has out multiple ongoings, there's no better card to play. On top of that, if you have both Twist the Ethers out, he'll be dealing 14 damage to the villain card each turn- potentially more with more villain targets out. Top it off with one of the best environment destruction cards in the game and an innate power that allows you to catch any double jeopardies that Dark Visionary spots, and I'd have to say Omnitron-X is my favorite go-to hero.

 

Of course, I might be biased about that last one, considering Mega Man is my favorite video game series.
