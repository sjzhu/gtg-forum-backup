If something targets a player or character card, such as Primordial Plantlife from Insula Primalus, then you choose which Sentinel takes the damage. If something damages all hero targets, all the Sentinels take damage. If A Sentinel is the hero target with the highest HP or lowest HP, then they're affected just like everything else.

The Sentinels, as a group, are one hero. However, they are, individually, four hero targets. If something affects the hero with the lowest hp and any one of the Sentinels fits that definition, then you can choose which one gets hit. If it says hero target, then it is the specific Sentinel with the lowest hp.

They count as one hero because then H isn't affected (just think, H would be four before anyone else has even chosen a hero, regardless of how many other players there actually were!). Also for stuff like Plague Rat - he flips when every hero is Infected, but only has five copies of Infection in his deck. So when it's the Sentinels' turn to be Infected, you choose which individual one of the four will have the card attached to them.
