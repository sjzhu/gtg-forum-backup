#2

Each Sentinel counts as a hero character card but they collectively only count as 1h. They still follow the normal rules in terms of play power and draw. The character cards all count as cards in play. Anything that targets the player hits all of them. Anything that targets a hero or hero target hits one of them. Cards like infection have their own rules detailed in the rule book.

Yes, there are ongoings and oneshots in the decks. The primary difference is in how they set up which is one villian then one hero and so on and so forth until the enviornment.

Does that help?
