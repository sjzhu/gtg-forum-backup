Not sure if this has been asked before but, I find the wording on Omnitron character card a little odd. It states: “When Omnitron is destroyed, remove Omnitron’s villain character cards from play. The heroes cannot win until there are no devices in play.” However, the act of removing Omnitron’s character cards from play also removes the line “The heroes cannot win until there are no devices in play.” from play. Hence, the condition fizzles and the heroes win?

Other examples, Kaargra Warfang’s character card reads: “The Heroes cannot win the game.” Once she flips, the text is no longer in effect, and the heroes can win.

Your thoughts?

This is the difference between:

Until the start of next turn, Effect A happens.

vs.

Effect A happens. Destroy this card at the start of the turn.

 

In the second case, if the card is destroyed early, Effect A stops.
