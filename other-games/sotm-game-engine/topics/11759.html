Cards under other cards have no text but still belong to their deck and can be re-destroyed - Haka re-destroys cards under Savage Mana (letting Bunker's Ammo Drop go off a second time per card), Freedom Six Tempest can destroy his own cards that've been stolen by La Capitan, and so on.
