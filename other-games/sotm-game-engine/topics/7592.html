I was trying out the new Shattered Timelines villains without looking through their decks or reading their flipped side. I tried fighting Iron Legacy on advanced vs Scholar, Unity, TL Tachyon, and Fanatic. Things were going well, until I flipped him (whoops!). Holy !$%^, redirect the first damage dealt to him each *turn*!?. Apparently my strategy of steady damage isn't going to work. Realized I need to flip him back and then unload in one turn to beat him. Spent the next few turns biding our time until he healed back over 25. Didn't make it though; too much damage too fast. It's clear that the strategy for advanced mode is to get him to just above twenty, then blast him down in a single turn (hard for three players). I like this villain a lot: he's tough, the games are fast and intense (low life total, lots of damage), and it requires teamwork to beat.
