I see The Matriatrch consistently listed as one of the hardest enemies.  Either my group is playing her wrong, or y'all are, as we've only ever lost to her once, and that was when we got cocky and left the Pillars of Hercules in play.  Most games, we overwhelm her decisively.

She has some nasty cards available in her deck, but so much of her deck (60%!) is filled with the chaff of 1-HP Fowl that (in our experience) she never works up any momentum.  [We typically read card text aloud when they come into play, but for Fowl cards, we just say: "At the start of the Villain turn, this card will already be dead."]  If she does manage to play something nasty, we almost always just focus fire on it, and it goes down in a round or two, before it can form an evil combo (this, of course, includes the Mask that she starts with).  Horrid Cacophony is unpleasant, but she only has two of them; compare against Sedative Flechettes, which is objectively worse in 95% of games, and we don't rate Omnitron as a hard villain.

I suppose if you're allowing her retaliation strikes to destroy hero cards, then she'd give you a harder time.  Don't do that!  Just take the HP damage.  You'll kill her far faster than she will kill you that way.

Rook City was the first Expansion. There was still some learning going on. Consider that the core game was in development for a considerable period of time before it was released (Aug, 2011), and then fans immediately wanted an expansion. The Kickstarter was launched just two months later and they had proofs back from the printer by early January. Sleulth. Checmical Explosion. 

After all the in-depth discussions we've had over the years, it's sometimes hard for me to remember that there was a time early on when some of the meaning of the text and how things worked just wasn't as pinned down. I think towards the beginning there was more general acceptance that as long as you had fun playing, it didn't really matter so much how some of the arcane interactions worked. If it comes up while playing, make a decision and move on.
