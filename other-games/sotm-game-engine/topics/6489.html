Quite honestly, I don't think it was intended this way, as, originally, villains had nemisis icons, and heroes had hero icons, and if a hero and a villain were to deal damage to each other, and the nemesis icon matched the hero icon, damage was increased.  But now that an environment card was added which had a nemesis icon, the nemesis relationship had to be redefined (Christopher did confirm during playtesting that Anubis was nemesis to Ra as well as nemesis to all of the Ennead).  Since later editions allowed multiple villain card being in play having the same nemesis icon, and the tricky ways for allowing one of the villain cards to deal damage to the other villain cards, Christopher decided to keep everything consistant with the "new" ruling stating two cards having the same icon get nemesis bonus.

Of course, this could easily be avoided while remaining consistant by stating that nemesis bonus only applies to cards from another faction (such that heroes only get bonus against non-heroes, villains only get bonus against non-villains, and environments only get bonus against non-environments).  I actually thought that was the conclusion we came to on another thread in this forum. Of course, I don't read BGG at all, so I never noticed Christopher's post about this until now.

The official ruling is that whenever a target deals damage to a different target that shares the same nemesis icon, the damage is increased by 1. So, if you're playing with Legacy and Young Legacy, and Felicia punches her dad, the damage is increased by 1. But when Legacy playes Heroic Interception and damages himself, that damage is not increased.
