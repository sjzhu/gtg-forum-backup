Since Blade was the source of the damage Blade would actually hit himself with Backlash field also. Redirecting doesn't change who is dealing the damage, it'll just change who is taking it.

 

Yes t'll get reduced to 0, and since he didn't actually take any damage he will not strike himself with Backlash Field.
