Voss laughs heartily as he orders a Forced Deployment.  The heroes then immediately destroy and trigger it!  What do they have up their sleeves for dealing with the minion pile that has just appeared?

(Note: I don't have the Rook City and Infernal Relics heroes - so someone feel free to finish this.  )

Caveat: This is sort of assuming that any villain damage reduction is due to the Gene-Bound Guards only, if they are even there.  A "*" next to a card name indicates that 1-2 minions may survive due to being immune to that card's type of damage.)

 

The Sure Thing

Fanatic nukes the pile (or probably the Forced Deployment and the minions) with End of Days.

Haka does a Rampage.  Bonus points if he has Savage Mana out for minion storage and eventual toxic unleash.

Good Enough with no Gene-Bound Guards about

Bunker fires up some External Combustion*

 

The Stars Are Right! (might need damage dealt help to handle Gene-Bound guards)

Omnitron-X unleashes a Defensive Blast (Note 1), or declares a Singularity* destroys a pile of equipment.

Tachyon stirs her trash and hits them with a Sonic Vortex* (need enough burst cards in trash)

Absolute Zero stops them cold with a Fueled Freeze* (needs to destroy three ongoing cards)

Unity's Bot-pack help her fire a Powered Shock Wave* (damage based on # of golems in play)

Ra draws upon the environment to cause Scorched Earth* (damage based on # of environment cards in play)

 

Need A Little Help Over Here! (+1 damage dealt needed for minions with no damage reduction)

Omnitron-X bombards them with Disruptive Flechettes*

Tempest hits them from the skies with a Grevious Hail Storm*

Visionary catches them in a Psychic Maelstrom*

 

Need A LOT of Help Over Here!! (we're into the 1 pt area effect attacks now)

Tempest dampens them with a Squall* (only default power on the list), or calls upon a handy Electrical Storm*

Absolute Zero breaks them with a Cold Snap*.

Fanatic gets stellar with a Holy Nova.

Visonary causes a sad with Demoralization*.

The Scholar tells everyone but the minions to Get Out Of The Way*!

Tachyon punches their ticket with either Hypersonic Assault* or Accelerated Assault.

Someone shoots Cryobot*!

 

Wait, That WORKED?!?

Ra has them Drawn To The Flame* (Note 2)

Wraith goes toe to toe with the whole set using Combat Stance. (Note 3)

 

Sadly, Legacy and Chrono-Ranger get left out of the fun. 

 

Note 1: The Defensive Blast option is very conditional since it's a 1+1+1 or 1+1+1+1 attack of different types.  Gene-bound guards will essentially negate it, but any sort of damage dealt bonus makes it obliterate the whole pile.

Note 2: Drawn To the Flame damage is based on Villain ongoing cards.  The only ones in the Grand Warlord Voss deck are the Forced Deployment cards themselves.

Note 3: Needs some damage dealt help and would only result if every minion damaged her on the Villain turn.

I actually just ruined Voss's day myself last night.  I recently acquired all the various Promo cards and decided to take them for a spin.  Normally I like a four man team against Voss, but I decided to try Young Legacy, ExPatriette, and Absolute Zero (I forget the name of their promos, I'm still kinda new to the game).  Deal of Deals, (I might get a few names wrong) I got out Inspiring Presence for YL, Hair-trigger Reflexes for ExPat, and SubZero Atmo for AZ.  Effectively, this meant that none of Voss's Minions could do anything until their second round in game (since all End-of-Turn effects are now Start-of-Turn), and everyone was taking 2 points of damage as soon as they came into play, meaning all Minions were 1-HP. 

After that AZ got into his usually Cold-Fire-COLD damage-dealing-self-healing mode, Ex-Pat got her Assault rifle and cleared off any minions still standing, and YL pounded Voss all day long.  With the occasional RPG to blow up any annoying Enviro cards, it had to be the easiest time against Voss I'd ever had.
