I think you're confusing morals with morale - morals are like ethics - if you have morals, you're probably going to be unwilling to hurt/kill people, stuff like that. Morale is confidence, hope, etc. If you're demoralised, your confidence and will to go on fades. If morale gets too low, you end up just thinking "what's the point?" and not bothering at all, or whatever. On a ship you'd demoralise the crew (assuming there is one) so that they might give up on following orders and stuff - someone like Legacy would boost morale. That's what Inspiring Presence does, and is opposed by Iron Legacy's Demoralising Presence, of course. I suppose demoralising an actual ship would be abit difficult, unless it has some kind of AI you can confuse or something, which isn't quite the same thing.
