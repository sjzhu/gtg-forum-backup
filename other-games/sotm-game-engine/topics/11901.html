Chaos Lord is devastating to have on Kaargra.  The only ways to clear it are flipping her, End of Days, or Adept's destruction that costs an instrument.

How I normally deal with Kaargra is get her down low in health, usually 5-10 based on environment damage and leave her until we can finish her off and reach 20 in the same round.  There are times you have to go away from this, but you don't want to get rid of her and then have Bloodsworn Judgement hit right before you would win.

When she gains a title like Chaos Lord or The Seeker it changes the game drastically.  Or the one game when she picked up Mindbreaker, and then was hit by the environment and gained the Indiscriminate, which with Captain Cosmic in the game soon led to Champion.  That was a rough game, and we did not win.

We have 2 house rules we use with Kaargra Warfang:

1.  The only title that does nothing under Kaargra Warfang is Living Weapon.  We house-rule it to increase the first damage dealt by her each turn by 2.

2.  We put titles back in the title deck once (H) are in the title area.  We line them up in order they entered play, and when a new one comes into play (or returns from a destroyed target) if more than (H) are in play we put the oldest one back in the deck until only (H) are in play.  Makes it easier to manage and makes Bloodsworn Judgement more threatening later in the game.
