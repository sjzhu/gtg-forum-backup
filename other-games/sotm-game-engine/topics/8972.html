I would say so - as far as I'm aware, "dealing damage" means "making something lose hp". Therefore if Tempest were to, say, Squall and everything had soak, he would have dealt no damage therefore wouldn't have to discard anything. But if he hit five targets, that's five instances of damage, therefore bye bye five cards (assuming he has that many).
