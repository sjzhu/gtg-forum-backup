It depends.

Any start of turn and end of turn effects happen in the order they were played, so if Self-Destruct Sequence came out and then Haka played Ground Pound you choose whether or not to skip your turn for SDS and if you don't you go on to look at any other start of turn effects.

If Ground Pound is played first and then SDS comes out, you destroy Ground Pound first and then get to decide if you want to skip your turn for SDS, not as beneficial that way around.

 

Things can still mess up your plans, villains can destroy ongoing or environment cards, which would stop your choice, Environments can also do this (though not in Wagner Mars Base IIRC) or other heroes might destroy the Environment, also Haka might decide that skipping their turn every round is boring and stop doing it. Just because it's good for the team doesn't mean you have to do it, the primary rule here is "Have fun saving the Multiverse".
