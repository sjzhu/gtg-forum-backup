First off, welcome to the forums, hope you survive the experience.

 

As to your question, all damage done has a type but the type itself has no different effect on a villain, it is just an idea of how the hero is dealing damage (e.g. Tachyon does Melee damage because she punches people really fast). But some ongoing/equipment cards can increase or decrease specific types of damage, or certain targets can be immune to specific types of damage, it's all detailed on the cards themselves.

 

If you look at Wraith's deck you can find a card called "Micro targeting computers" which increase any projectile damage she does, if you were to play a oneshot card does melee damage then Microt argeting computers will not increase the damage done but most of Wraith's powers do Projectile damage so that damage would be increased.

 

Another card you might want to look at is Ra's "Flesh of the sun god", it makes him immune to Fire damage, so he would ignore any Fire damage but all other types of damage would still reduce his HP normally.
