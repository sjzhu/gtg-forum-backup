After omnitron is hit wih damage, you run through all the effects of the subroutine. If the attack was melee, the first card would make him immune. Then the second one would resolve and also make him immune to melee. It just means you’d have to destroy two cards before the effect goes away.

You’re welcome. In most situations like that, just remember that each card’s effects are resolved separately based on the conditions of the game when each card is reached. Resolve the effects of the first copy, then evaluate the conditions of the next copy and act based on that. IE if two cards that target the hero with the highest HP (let’s use Legacy as an example here) are out, and the first one makes it so that a new hero has the highest HP (for example Legacy is lowered below Bunker’s current HP), the second card would target that Bunker instead of Legacy.
