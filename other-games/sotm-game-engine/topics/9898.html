Nope, you can only hit a particular target once.

There are ways to deal that damage to a single target, but they generally involve redirection shenanigans.  For instance, if Mr. Fixer has both Dual Crowbars and Driving Mantis, he can hit one target for 2 damage, then hit himself for 2 damage but redirect that second instance of damage to the same target as the first.

Yup, works well against Gloomy if you whack a cultist with a multi-hit (killing it) and then the resultant Zombie with another hit of the same attack. The destruction of the cultist, in this case, technically interrupts your damage-dealing…so say you were playing Bunker and using a Grenade Launcher, you hit the cultist for the first hit and it dies (because it only had a couple of hp left). It then interrupts you from attacking a second thing to go "Hang on, I have to turn into a zombie now…" and said Zombie comes out of the trash. Then you go "Okay, now where was I…okay I can hit another thing…ooh look, a zombie >:)" and said Zombie goes bye-bye as well. Then I suppose you can plink Gloomy for the other hit (assuming no other viable targets in play of course ;)).

If an attack says "you may deal X targets X damage", then you have a choice as to whether to do the damage at all. If it says "you may deal up to X targets X damage" then you can choose anywhere from zero up to that number of targets. If it says "deal X targets X damage" there is no choice - you must hit that many targets…though I can't think of any multi-hit attacks which force you to do this. You need to be careful with stuff like Mr Chomps, though, who will always attack one thing at the end of Unity's turn. If you're fighting an unflipped Miss Information when there are no environment targets or targettable Diversions in play, you're gonna have to hit a hero target…
