Question 1--You nailed it.  Shuffle the trash and that becomes the new deck.

Question 2--You have to resolve all the text on the card before putting a One-Shot into the trash.  Omnitron-X's "Reset" works the same way.  After playing Reset (or a similar card), it should be the only card in the trash pile.

Question 3--Yes.  It's still the "Start of Villain/Hero Turn", so the effect goes into play during that phase.

To add a few extra points…

To the second part of your first question, the deck only shuffles if you need to draw or play from it (and haven't got enough cards). If yu need to reveal, then you only reveal as many as you can. In your example with La Capitan's Raiding Party, if there weren't enough/any Crew cards left in the deck you'd just end up revealing the whole deck and chucking all the non-Crew cards in the trash. On a similar note, something like the Arcane Cadence (one-shot belonging to the Adept) says you have to reveal your top five cards and then do various different things with them - if you played Arcane Cadence and had fewer than five cards in your deck, you would still follow the instructions but would have to do so in order and stop when you ran out of cards (so if you only had four cards left, you wouldn't get to do the last part - "play one").

To clarify a little on the start/end-of-turn things, all cards which specify a time of activation like this are activated in order. So if a villain has "play a card" as part of their Start phase (on their villain card) and the card they play is an ongoing which has a Start phase effect, you would put it into play at the end of the line of villain cards currently in play, and check through the rest of the in-play cards, in order, to see if any of them have start-of-turn effects. Then you'd get to the new card, and activate that because it's still the Start phase. Similarly, you can destroy some environment cards before they get a chance to do anything, as they come out in the environment's Play phase but give you a chance to destroy them during the End phase ("at the end of the environment turn, discard X hero cards to destroy this card", for example). Cards from different decks also activate in order - if the environment plays a card with a Start phase action, then on her turn on the following round Fanatic plays End of Days (activates at start of environment turn), the environment card was in play first so the next time its turn rolls around, its card does its thing before End of Days activates.

To further add to the second part of your first question, discarding from an empty deck will also cause a re-shuffle. So, if Akash'Bhuta  (on her front side) plays a target and the environment deck is empty, you would reshuffle the environment trash, creating a new environment deck (flipping Akash'Bhuta), and then discard the top card of the new environment deck.
