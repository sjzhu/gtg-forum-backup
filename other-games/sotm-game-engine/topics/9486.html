Here is the announcement of the rule change.

With the old ruling by order cards entered play response triggers and card text were tiggered simultaneously when the card entered play.

Since it was simultaneous the order of resolution was determined by order the cards entered play, which meant the newly played card went last.

 

Now that order is up to the players.  If you want to keep it the old way (my group does) that is your decision.  But if you want you could let the newly played card go first, or even decide each time in the way that will benefit you most.

I understand the need for this ruling, and pushed for it, but I liked the interaction before the rule change, so we've kept it timed that way, now because we want to instead of because it is the proper way to play.
