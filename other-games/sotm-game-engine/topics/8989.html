Yes, you would be able to destroy Impale.

 

Impale destroys itself when the target you set it next to leaves play.

We know that cards with effects that happen on their own destruction stay in play until that effect is completed, so Impale and Illusory Demon will both be in play when Illusory Demon destroys a card.

The new ruling doesn't apply to this one.
