Nojh:

Can someone explain the first sentence of Setback's "High Risk Behavior" card using one sentence per +1 being assigned to a target (or in any way that makes it easier to parse)?

Basically, when Setback deals damage to a villain target, he increases it by 1 for every 3 tokens he has in his pool. So 9 tokens will give a +3. It also increases damage dealt by villain targets to Setback by 1 for every 3 tokens in his pool.

Don't think of it as rounding. You are just counting the groups of 3 that you have. You would get +1 for every complete set of 3 tokens that you have. Partial sets don't contribute anything. So:

Tokens / Boost
1/0
2/0
3/1
4/1
5/1
6/2
7/2
8/2
9/3
