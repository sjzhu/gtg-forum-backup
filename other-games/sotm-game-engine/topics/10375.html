#2

1) Damage reduction only applies to that specific hero. So if Fanatic is no longer the final recipient of the damage, her damage reduction doesn't apply. Damage can be redirected more than once by different sources, but the consensus is that a particular redirection effect (e.g. Smoke Bombs) can only be triggered once, and therefore, the damage redcution only triggers once. Now, whether or not the reduction applies if the final target isn't the one that Smoke Bombs originally redirected to is up for debate. Unless there was another thread that clarified it.

 

2) A generic +1 damage will add the damage to each instance of damage. So, Fanatic will do 2/2, and Tempy will have the +1 to each of the targets of Chain Lightning and Squall. If a card only increases the next damage, only the first one will get the increase, not the entire power.

 

3) Nothing in SotM is simultaneous. Each instance of damage is dealt with individually before moving onto the next. So with Stealth Bot, it won't be 1 instance of 10 damage, but little pockets one at a time, and it will destroy Stealth Bot before it gets to redirect everything.

tenk51:

When exactly does damage reduction apply?

When the specific effect granting damage reduction says it does. In the example you provided of Fanatic with Undaunted in play, it applies when five or more points of damage are going to be subtracted from Fanatic’s current HP.

tenk51:

If Legacy increases all damage by 1, am I dealing 2 melee and 2 radiant damage?

Yup.

On the downside, if something reduces all damage by one, Fanatic’s base Exorcism power deals 0 melee and 0 radiant unless buffs are also in play.

tenk51:

For example, if Stealth Bot is in play with 3 health, and something like Baron Blade’s Hasten Doom gets played (with 5 players), can all 10 (5 with reduction) damage be redirected to Stealth Bot?

Nope. On the plus side, though, since damage isn’t dealt simultaneously, it can also be interrupted under the right circumstances. Say in your example Baron Blade only has 2 HP left, and Unity has Volatile Parts in play. You can choose to have Stealth Bot be hit first so the explosion will finish him off before he has a chance to inflict damage on the other hero targets.
