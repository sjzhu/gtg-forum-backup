For reference:

[quote=““Focused Apertures””]
Increase cold damage dealt by Absolute Zero by 1.
[/quote]

[quote=““Modular Realignment””]
Select 1 Equipment card from your Trash and put it either into your hand or into play. Absolute Zero deals himself 1 fire damage and 1 cold damage.
[/quote]

[quote=““Null-Point Calibration Unit””]
When Absolute Zero would take cold damage, regain that many HP instead.
[/quote]

So, in the example cited above, the following events would happen in the following order:

  • The player would remove Null-Point Calibration Unit from their Trash and put it into play.
  • Modular Realignment would deal Absolute Zero 1 fire damage.
  • Modular Realignment would deal Absolute Zero 1 cold damage, which would actually be 2 cold damage because of Focused Apertures. Because Modular Realignment is in play, Absolute Zero would regain 2 HP instead of losing 2 HP

The sequence of events gets even more complex if Isothermic Transducer is in play. In that case:

  • The player would remove Null-Point Calibration Unit from their Trash and put it into play.
  • Modular Realignment would deal Absolute Zero 1 fire damage.
  • Absolute Zero could then deal the target of his choice 1 cold damage, which would actually be 2 cold damage because of Focused Apertures.
  • Modular Realignment would deal Absolute Zero 1 cold damage, which would actually be 2 cold damage because of Focused Apertures. Because Modular Realignment is in play, Absolute Zero would regain 2 HP instead of losing 2 HP

Cryochamber makes this sequence of events even more thrilling… and that’s why I love Absolute Zero
