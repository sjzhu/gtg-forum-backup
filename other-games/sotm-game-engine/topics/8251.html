BlueHairedMeerkat:

Sounds like you played it pretty much right; I think you put the fowl into play one at a time, but once they've been knocked back out they don't get a second in.

 

I disagree, because destroyed cards go to the trash.  So if you pulled each fowl out and put it into play individually, it would trigger Shopping, get destroyed, go to the trash and then come back out.  That would trigger the infinite loop.

The only way to avoid that is to follow the "put all Fowl cards" into play but doing it all at once.  Then that card is done.  Then Shopping Trip deals a one damage attack for each Fowl card that came out.

This is the same loop as Darken the Skies & Hairtrigger Reflexes, I believe we're still waiting on an official ruling on that too.
