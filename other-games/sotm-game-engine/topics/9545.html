Voss in particular is harder for 3 heroes than for more. I believe he hardly uses the H mechanic. He also has no destruction in his deck, so if you can survive until you get set up then you can often take him out in short order. Because of the lack of destruction, it's worth prioritizing long-term development over one-shots if there aren't super-damaging minions out. If you take a look at almost any heroes deck (especially Chrono-Ranger's), you can easily come up with combinations that lead to massive amounts of damage with just a few cards out.

Villain deck manipulation is also key, to avoid cards like Forced Deployment (or else get them out early when they aren't so damaging).

Was Tempest not using his Incapacitated powers? He has one of the best such powers in the game, although I guess it's less useful against Voss than many other villains.

You'll also get better about seeing synergies between different decks as you play more. You may want to browse some of the play-by-forum games here; the level of play is pretty high there, and think about why people played the way they did.

EDIT: Also, Flamethrower has some good strategy guides: https://greaterthangames.com/forum/topic/flamethrower49s-strategy

elias4444:

 it's frustrating for someone to die and no longer be involved for the rest of the game

 

You know that incapacitated heroes get to use an incapacitated power on their turn, right?

 

You're playing with three heroes. Though the game has the 'H' scaling mechanic, it only goes so far. Most of the villains are considerably harder with only three heroes. Try having someone place two heroes so you can have four.

 

Voss' big gotcha is Forced Deployment. You may be able to play for the eventuality that it's coming.

 

La Capitan is swingy. In some games she gets a ton of minions out. In others, not as many.

 

Try this team against Voss: Legacy, Wraith, Ra, Tempest. If Wraith gets the Infrared eyepiece, use it to try to control Voss' deck to bury Forced Deployment and the ships. Hopefully Legacy and Ra will be able to boost Tempest so that Tempests' base power can kill all the minions.
