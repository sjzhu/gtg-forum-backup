This is the ongoing card that reverses the order of the hero turn.   If you are under the effect of this card, and you play a card that destroys ongoing cards during your "play a card" phase, does the turn reset at that moment?  The "play a card" phase is the second-to-last phase while under the card's effect. 

Which of these would happen?

1) You finish your turn as if the card were still there, and the next player takes there's in the proper order. This is the least likely as the SotM mechanic doesn't tend to ignore things like that.

2) The turn resets.  Since you were on the second to last phase, you now go to the last phase which would be "end of turn" again, and any "end of turn" effects go off again.

3) The turn resets.  Instead of looking at your position in the turn ordianally, the game looks at it by position name.  Since you just finished your "play a card" phase, you are now on your "use a power" phase again.  You would get to use a power, draw a card, and trigger end of turn effects again.
