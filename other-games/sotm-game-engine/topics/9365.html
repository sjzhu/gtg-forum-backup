The confusion between "schizophrenia" and "split personality" is understandable, given that "schizo" means "split".  But schizophrenia really refers to a "split" between the 'phrenic's internal reality and the external world, which can be virtually anything - the classic "hearing voices" is only one example.  In all cases, they have nothing to do with multiple personality disorder.
