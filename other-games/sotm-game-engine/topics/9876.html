Wanted to post a short report of a solitaire game that I played that ended in a stalemate (I would have eventually won, I think, but I was unwilling to put in the hours of gameplay to see the end).

 

GAME: FORCES OF NATURE

 

VILLAIN: Akash'bhuta

ENVIRONMENT: The Final Wasteland

HEROES: The Argent Adept, The Naturalist

 

First and foremost, I ruled (to myself, which left little debate) that any damage that was H-2+ had a minimum damage of 1.  I felt that things dealing 0 damage without any damage reduction was a little broken in my favor.

 

The game went along normally, with the heroes destroying Akash's limbs and with the Adept taking loads of damage when the Naturalist wasn't able to tank (due to being in crocodile form, mainly).  Eventually, Adept goes down.  At this point the Naturalist is at full HP, so I'm not terribly worried.  With the extra power use from the dead Adept, I am tearing through limbs (and the environment cards are helping, because the limbs have the lowest health and nothing meaningful is getting through Resilient Hide) at a steady pace, and  seem to have the game well in hand.  Then Ensaring Brambles (I may have this card name slightly wrong, as I have packed up the game already and am unwilling to fact check right now) comes out.  Suddenly, the Naturalist cannot deal damage.  OK, I think, I can just turtle up in rhino form and ride it out until the environment takes out the Brambles, and then I can go croc and win.  This....never came to fruition.  About 60-90 minutes later, Akash had all 12 limbs out, I was at full health, and I was dealing about 10 damage per hour to Akash from incidental enviro-kills.  I called it when Akash healed all of her limbs up (again), destroyed all of the environment cards (again), and stretched the game out even farther (again).

 

The moral of this tale: When it's nature vs. nature.....nothing really gets accomplished.

 

In my previous game (same heroes/enviro, against Vengeance Baron and Ermine) the environment performed a board sweep, killing the last villain AND the last hero (in that order, so technically a hero win, but I'm declaring that an environment win).  Nature won that fight HARD.  Fear the wild...

 

-DubTak
