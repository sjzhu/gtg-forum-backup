Since I love the video game's new opening pages, I wanted to see what all of the characters had to say.  Collected here are all of the standard and nemesis quotes for anyone who's interested:
 
VILLAIN QUOTES
Baron Blade: Tremble, fools! The moon grows closer with your every breath!
Baron Blade (Mad Bomber): My bombs are scattered everywhere! You cannot win.
Citizen Dawn: The world belongs to the Citizens of the Sun!
Grand Warlord Voss: Cower before the might of the Thorathian conquering fleet!* 
Omnitron: Offensive Programming Online. Destroy Humans.
Omnitron (Cosmic): Power Source: Unknown. Directive: Destroy.
 
HERO QUOTES
Absolute Zero: Tempers are running hot. Time to cool things down!
Bunker: Work together, team! Think tactically and protect your teammates. 
Bunker (GI): I may be just a soldier, but you’re still going down!
Fanatic: Repent, evildoer! You cannot stand against divine might!
Haka: Haha! Battle! This will be fun!
Legacy: We fight this day for liberty and for justice!
Legacy (Young): We fight for what’s right! Gotta make dad proud!
Ra: You shall burn under the baleful gaze of the sun!
Tachyon: No time to waste! Let’s see how fast we can make this.
Tachyon (Super-Scientific): A quandary! Time for some scientific action!
Tempest: You face the strength of the storm. Face the maelstrom!
Visionary: I have foreseen the consequences of this conflict.
Wraith: The shadows strike as The Wraith! Stand down, villain! 
Wraith (Rook City): Preliminary investigations have shown this will be a tough fight.
 
NEMESIS QUOTE PAIRS:
Baron Blade: At last! The destruction of my greatest foe is at hand! Legacy shall fall!
Legacy: Stand down, Baron! The price of your hatred should not be the entire planet!
--
Baron Blade: Yet another blasted Legacy! Will there be no end to your terrible line?
Young Legacy: You couldn’t defeat my father, and you’ll find me just as tough!
--
Mad Bomber Blade: If I cannot destroy you, Legacy, I will destroy all that you love! 
Legacy: Stop the madness, Blade! You threaten the lives of the innocent!
--
Mad Bomber Blade: You think yourself so clever. Your father knew to fear me. You shall learn...
Young Legacy: You’re crazier than you are dangerous... and you’re really dangerous! 
--
Grand Warlord Voss: Ah, the puny being who escaped me on Vognild Prime! Ready for your shackles? 
Tempest: You shall not ruin this planet the way you did my home! Your tyranny ends here!
 
 
* There's a typo in this one: conquering is spelled conquerering
