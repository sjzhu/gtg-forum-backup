To clarify on TheJayMann’s point, the starting condition only takes effect at the start of the game. If The Chairman runs out of cards, the Thugs cards stay wherever they are. If they’re in play, they remain in play. If they’re in the trash, they get shuffled, too. And if they’re under Savage Mana or something similar, they stay under Savage Mana.
