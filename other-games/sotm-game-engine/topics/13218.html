Yes. Individually. So Squall isn't one 5 Point attack, it's five 1 point attacks (if there were five targets in play for example).

Ronway

#3

It should also be noted that you choose a target, then redirect if needed, choose the next target, redirecting if needed. If you have 5 relics in play, with Orb at 3 HP, you could hit it first, then target two other relics, causing it to be destroyed. That will allow the other 2 relics to be hit.

Another note of importance, if a target has DR that would reduce the damage to 0, then no redirection would occur. So without damage increases or irreducible damage, targeting Condemnation will not trigger a redirection.
