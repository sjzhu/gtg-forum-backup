  1. Both Promos are situational and depend on how you like to play those heroes.  I prefer Eternal Haka since his card drawing ability is amazing for getting a powerful hand, though if he's paired with TLT, he probably doesn't need it; he's going to have a hand full of cards anyways (since Haka has no way to play multiple cards or use multiple powers on his own), though having plenty of extra fuel for Haka of Battle/Restoration is never a bad thing.  

Dark Watch Nightmist is a much more careful, deliberate approach to using Nightmist as it seeks to take the "randomness" out of her Spell damage (since you can arrange which numbers come up first).  This could be very powerful with TLT as well, since you can set up the next 3 cards coming to Nightmist, one which she'd draw at the end of her turn, the second she'd draw on TLT's turn, and third waiting to be "revealed" if she wanted to plan ahead for a spell use on her next turn.  I prefer original Nightmist since I like the high risk, high reward, game ending damage that can come from her once she's set up, but I have not tried DW NM with TLT as a teammate, which could be quite amazing.  I say test it out!

  1.  Vanilla Visionary certainly helps Nightmist.  If you're going with DW NM, you'll need to get as many cards in your hand as possible since you don't have Investigate to fuel your Amulet.  Visionary's Enlighten + TLT's power can help NM get the hand she needs.  That being said, Dark Visionary is pretty OP.  Turmoil is one hell of a power that any team could benefit from.  Argent Adept also benefits from being able to choose which heroes he buffs, which can be a godsend to Nightmist (who doesn't want to hurt herself more than she has to).  And Tempest is…well…Tempest.  He's awesome.

In the end, you're only going to know by toying around with it!  Experiment, play more games, switch the teams up, switch up the villains.  There is never going to be a "best" team as each villain/environment brings a different scenario to the table, allowing each hero to shine in a particular situation.

Legacy has pretty good synergy with anyone who deals damage (especially Ra in Insula Primalis - yay for the Volcano :D). The Adept has pretty good synergy with anyone who does...anything. Unity does well with people who let her play/draw more cards (especially if they let her play out of turn, since she can put bots straight out - Adept is good here, for example).

I would say Wraith is another good all-rounder. She's kind of equipment-heavy (unlike Tempest), but there is a wide variety of things she can do - damage, deck control, ongoing/envinromnet destruciton, damage nerfing, etc. And she has a "finishing move" in the form of Inventory Barrage.

Mr Fixer is another versatile hero, but tends to be a bit slow to set up - if he can get extrra draws/plays, this can help him get more of his stuff out and get into a decent combo. DW Fixer can benefit more from cards like Bloody Knuckles, since he can whack something for a load of damage and then destroy it and not worry about getting hit for extra damage.
