I think that, barring an errata, it's clear that the active players may all draw without destroying the Arsenal.  Not only is that what the plain wording of the card means, but it even fits thematically. 

An incapped hero is, despite greatly reduced options, still doing what he can to help the team.  A player who deliberately foregoes a draw from the Arsenal is voluntarily reducing their own options to help the team.

Or, looked at another way, In a 4-hero game, the Arsenal can withstand up to 3 draws per turn without being destroyed.  If a player is incapped, then Arsenal will never go above that limit, so is fine.  The Arsenal itself doesn't care about the fact that a player has been incapped.

Final argument: "H" doesn't change based on incapped heroes.  While I don't think this card actually uses "H", it seems pretty analagous to me.
