Also, if you want to go really awesome AZ get a Visionary to put Twist the Ether on you (Thermal Shockwave can fully heal you turning the fire damage to cold), Fixer with Crowbars and Grease Monkey fist is fun too, for healing with cold damage or to double up on a bad guy with fire damage.

The biggest things is to remember:

That Thermal Shockwave (AZ's Main big damage power) adds up all the damage you do at the start of your turn, so Impale and Cold Snap get added in.  With TtE adding one to those the total can get really high.

Coolant Blast counts all fire damage dealt to AZ since your last turn, great for Weapons platform AZ (hitting him with fire from other heroes to deal cold or heal himself).

AZ is a hero to build a party around, he's good by himself, but with a team built to feed him he's incredible.
