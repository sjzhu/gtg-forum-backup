The only time you can lose "nothing" of something to avoid/trigger an effect is discarding your hand when you have zero cards, because a hand of zero cards is still a hand (Toxic Seaweed in Atlantis is an example of this). Otherwise, you must always discard/destroy the specified number of cards in order to achieve whatever it is.
