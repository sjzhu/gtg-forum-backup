Think of it this way; anything that does not say "One-Shot" stays on the field; this can include Ongoings, Equipment, Components, Relics, Modes, and many are combinations of such.  Anything that destroys these will specify in the card text.  If a villain card says "destroy an Equipment Card" or one of Omnitron-X's cards say "Destroy a Component card", Omni-X's Innervation Ray can be destroyed by either as it's title is "Equipment, Component".

 

Any card that has Ongoing in the title can be destroyed by Ongoing destruction; anything with Equipment in the title can be destroyed by Equipment destruction.  In the end, it all comes down to the text of the card.
