This is only half on-topic because it pertains to art rather than gameplay, but I just noticed for the first time where exactly the pupil of Wraith's right eye (on the left side of her face as we see it) is in the picture for Grappling Hook.  I always saw this picture as showing her looking kinda googley-eyed or cross-eyed, because I thought the dark shadow in the lower-right corner of the eye was her pupil; instead, it's her nose, and the pupil is red-tinged from backlighting and visible at the upper-left edge of the eye, making it look like she's sighting a far-distant object with intention to grapple-hook it.  This obviously makes much more sense than my previous interpretation, but makes the card as a whole somewhat less hilarious to me.
