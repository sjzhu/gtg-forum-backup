I recently put together some very brief summaries of the SotM heroes for when I had new players over to try the game.  My goal was to capture the general idea of the character in less than 110 characters, so that I could print them all out cleanly -- one line each on a single page.  New folks could then look through the list to figure out which hero they'd like to try out. The heroes are grouped by complexity, mostly to let players know which ones benefit from having more knowledge of the character deck. Since I've now used this a couple of times when introducing people to the game, I thought I'd share it here in case outhers might want to use it. [Thanks to AlexxKay, for giving me some helpful suggestions with this.]

Charley Sumner / @SeeSome

[Edit: per suggestions, I moved Argent Adept to the 3rd grouping]

--

SEESOME'S SUMMARIES

One-Line Guides to the Sentinels of the Multiverse Heroes for New Player

 

Mostly Straightforward Heroes:

EXPATRIETTE - An expert sharpshooter. She loves guns, especially her custom handguns, Pride and Prejudice.

HAKA - An immortal brawler. His abilities can prevent damage, heal himself, & boost his attacks.

KNYFE - A secret agent with energy blades. Her military & espionage training give her tactical advantages.

LEGACY - A patriotic hero. He has flight & super-strength, but his greatest power is inspiring his team.

RA - An archaeologist who wields the Staff of Ra. His firebolts deal large amounts of damage.

TACHYON - A speedster. She starts weak, but her attacks get more powerful as her velocity increases.

TEMPEST - An aquatic alien. M'kk uses weather-control powers to damage enemies and aid teammates.

THE WRAITH - A dark vigilante. Her gadgets deal damage, shield herself, and control the battlefield.

 

Somewhat Trickier Heroes:

BUNKER - A military tank-suit. He builds up devastating weapon combos, but they can take time to deploy.

CHRONO-RANGER - A time-traveling old west sheriff & bounty-hunter. He always gets his man... or thing.

FANATIC - An angelic warrior. She wields divine powers, some of which damage her along with her enemies.

MISTER FIXER - A master of martial arts, and a damn fine auto mechanic. He has a versatile toolbox.

THE NATURALIST - A shifter. He can become a gazelle, rhino, or crocodile; each with different abilities.

OMNITRON-X - A robot hero. It can upgrade its body with a multitude of offensive & defensive modifications.

PARSE - An archer who can sense hidden connections. Her powers detect and exploit enemy weaknesses.

UNITY - A technopath. She can turn scrap metal into robot golems with a wide variety of powers.

THE VISIONARY - A psychic. She can control environments and enemies, or just hit them with mental blasts.

 

More Complicated Heroes:

ABSOLUTE ZERO – Thermodynamic Hero. His cryo-suit keeps him alive & lets him manipulate cold and fire.

ARGENT ADEPT - A bard. He knows songs for every occasion, but having the right tune at the right time is tricky.

NIGHTMIST - Mistress of black magic. She dishes out lots of Infernal damage -- to herself as well as others

THE SCHOLAR - A mystic. He uses the Philosopher’s Stone to alter his body, heal, and redirect damage.

THE SENTINELS - A mini-team of four heroes. Weak individually, together they have a multitude of abilities.

SETBACK - An unlucky hero. His risky behavior can turn misfortunes into boosts when the odds change.

--
