Damage dealt means lose of hp. If no hp is lost, no damage was dealt. Therefore, if the damage was reduced to 0, no damage was dealt, and the card wouldn’t activate.

Yes, Char would be KOed and not deal the other damage. Nothing in SotM is simultaneous. After the initial damage, retaliation damage kicks in before the second instance of damage.

When dealing multiple damage from a single source do this

  1. Pick a target
  2. Inflict the first instance of damage
  3. Check for triggers (incap, reactive plating, things coming back from the trash etc)
  4. Repeat steps 2&3 for each instance of damage
  5. Pick the next target
  6. Repeat steps 2 through 5 for each target the card/power says you can hit*
  7. Smile as all your foes fall before you.

 

*If something says hit "all targets" (or variations thereof) and new targets enter play as a result of your damage (e.g. zombies coming back from killing Cultists) those targets are part of 'all' even though they weren't there when you started dealing damage.

With regard to stuff happening in order, this only applies to stuff which occurs at a specified time (start/end of turn). So all of Omni-10's components will activate in the order in which you played them because they all activate at the start of his turn. Villains and environments obviously have many more start/end-of-turn actions to perform than heroes, so this will come up more for those decks. If a card from one deck affects another, you still go in play order. So say Apostate plays an Apocalypse, activates at start of the environment turn, when that point comes you first check any environment cards which have a start-of-turn action because they'll have entered play before the Apocalypse did. So you activate those, then the Apocalypse nukes everything.

With regard to applying effects to multiple targets, the order doesn't matter. You don't have to attack stuff in the order it entered play. This is worth noting against someone like Voss - say he has a Gene-Bound Guard out (gives all villain targets one point of soak) as well as a one other minions and you're Tempest and you play Chain Lightning. This hits three targets - the first for four damage, the second for three, and the third for two. So you hit the Guard first, and the four points is enough to get through the soak and kill it, meaning the other minion now has no soak. So you choose it as your second target (assuming it isn't a lightning minion), hitting it for three and killing it. Then you choose Voss as your third target, who's now lost all his soak (his last minion just got killed) and gets hit for an extra point thanks to the Nemesis bonus, so takes three points instead of two (or five if you have your Shackles) ;). And then you still have your Power phase in which to hit him so more if you so wish.

As mentioned, multi-hit stuff works well against Gloomy too, as you can kill both a Cultist and the resultant Zombie during the same attack if it's a multi-target one, because a target entering play during a multi-hit attack isn't automatically exempt from that attack.

Anything which attacks stuff twice (eg Char, the Cursed Acolyte) will attempt to deal all its damage, in the order listed on the card, to one target before moving on to the next one.

You can find plenty of ways to exploit the order in which stuff gets hit. We had a good game once against Gloomy which involved Ra and Expatriette (and possibly Legacy). Expatriette was buffed from somewhere (either Ra had Imbued Fire out or Legacy was Galvanising) and had Hairtrigger Reflexes out. Ra had his Flame Barrier. A Cultist would enter play and immediately get damaged by Expatriette. Then the Cultist would attempt to attack someone - in the case of the Cursed Acolyte, he attacks all hero targets, so we had him attack Ra first. I think Ra had Flesh of the Sun God out so the fire damage did nothing, but then the infernal damage hit, causing Ra's Flame Barrier to go off, finishing off the Acolyte and bringing him back as a Zombie…who promptly got insta-killed by Expatriette. Yeah, Gloomy didn't do too well that game ;).
