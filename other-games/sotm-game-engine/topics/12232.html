The more I look at this, the more it doesn't make sense.  Spiff's clarifications state that Play, Draw, and Discard always trigger a shuffle.  Just because it says "Play the top card", that doesn't mean it's not playing a card.  If there is no top card, shouldn't there be a shuffle in order to play a card?

 

This language has been around since the beginning.  Citizen Dawn's Channel the Eclipse has "Play the top card of the villain deck".  Atlantean Font of Power -  "At the end of the environment turn, play the top card of the villain deck.  Play the top card of each hero deck in turn order.  If the played card is a limited card already in play, discard that card and play the next card.   At the start of the environment turn, destroy this card."  So the way it is worded, play the top card is the same as play the next card.  And both of those are synonymous with Play a card.  For the heroes, it is worded that way in order to distinguish playing a card from your hand. 

So unless someone can point to a clarification somewhere that states that playing the top card is not the same as playing a card off the deck, then I'll have to go with Spiff's clarifications.  Thanks in advance.
