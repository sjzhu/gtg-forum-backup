Encountered a new wrinkle when playing against Kaargara Warfang last night.

 

End of days was played by Fanatic. Several titles were out. We played it as follows...

1) Title cards are "Cards in play" since the colosseum specifically states that they are "put into play". Therefore, they are vulnerable to End of Days.

2) Title cards are not indestructible. The colosseum and crowd favor cards are, but the titles themselves do not appear to be.

3) We therefore destroyed all the title cards and created a title card trash

4) If we needed to draw a title card and none were in the deck, we would then reshuffle the trash as normal and play from there. (Kaargra saved us this trouble by beating us into submission first. How kind of her.)

 

Does this follow with the way others would play End of Days against her?
