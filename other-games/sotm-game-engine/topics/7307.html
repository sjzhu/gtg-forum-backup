 Reckless is correct.

 

You have to understand that damage buffs/debuffs apply *any time*  damage is dealt.  So Martyr deals 5 damage (But damage is +1. So she deals herself 6 damage).   She now attacks a target for an equal amount.    She attacks Baron Blade for 6 damage  (but damage is +1, so she deals 7 damage). 

 

Same logic still applies with Counterpoint.

 

Damage self for 5 damage:   +1=6 damage.  -1 =5 damage.

5 damage attack vs Baron Blade,  +1 damage=6 damage.

There's a thread with a second-hand Christopher response stating that Dual Crowbars don't get to double dip on damage boosts; the "same amount" wording is meant to denote that.

The difference here is Sacrosanct Martyr actually uses a different wording. It reads: "POWER: Fanatic deals herself up to 5 Radiant damage. Fanatic deals 1 target X Radiant damage, where X = the amount of Radiant damage dealt to Fanatic this turn." Fanatic unambiguously gets both packets boosted separately.

https://greaterthangames.com/forum/topic/mr-fixers-dual-crowbars-and-damage-modification

is the thread if you wanna check it out. There's still wiggle room on how it works with reduction, but the second attack definitely is not double boosted.
