I'm not sure if anyone's tried this before. Probably; it's not a particularly revolutionary idea. What it is, is this: fight multiple villains, one after another, with the same team, without resetting them. So they keep their equipment and hands, but also keep their wounds. You could do it with a specific pair of villains you wanted to fight in sequence, or you could take a team and fight their nemeses one after the other. As for me, I want to fight them all. The plan is to take on every villain, one after the other, in the following rough difficulty order:

Baron Blade, Omnitron, Gloomweaver, *, Akash'Bhuta, Kismet, Apostate, Mad Bomber Blade, Plague Rat, Spite, La Capitan, Grand Warlord Voss, Cosmitron, Citizen Dawn, Miss Information, The Ennead, The Dreamer, The Matriarch, The Organisation, Iron Legacy

*I don't own Ambuscade.

I'll die, of course, I just want to see how far I'll get (and invite others to give it a go as well). As for the environment, whenever I need to reshuffle it I'll swap it out for a new one, leaving any environment cards in play (though removing them from play when they're destroyed). Here's an order for them, too:

Insula Primalis, Silver Gulch, Time Cataclysm, The Final Wasteland, The Block, Realm of Discord, Ruins of Atlantis, Pike Industrial Complex, Tomb of Anubis, Megalopolis, Wagner Mars Base, Rook City

So Legacy, Team Leader Tachyon, Tempest, The Scholar and Omnitron-X started off against Baron Blade. It was a pretty tough fight, but the team came out with a decent selection of cards in play.

Conclusions: Do not do this. Once you're set up, it is boring. Or, alternately, don't pick so many heroes with healing, or who can pour cards into your hand, and play on Advanced, and maybe don't start easy. The idea may be workable, but this is not the way to do it.
