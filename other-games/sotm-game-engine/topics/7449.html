This is an ongoing debate, but my stance is that this is not a role-based game.  Most heroes easily fulfill more than one role, and you don't need to have roles covered in order to succeed.  The only thing you need, in most cases, is damage, which all characters can do.  (Some better than others, but the characters that don't do as much damage contribute in useful, often unique ways.)  I also find it helpful to have a character or two that can remove ongoing and environment cards.  Other than that, though, it's all good. 

Learn what options the characters have, learn what you need to watch out for from the villains, and you will improve.

well, if you want a quick archetype list:

 

Legacy: support/buff, damage control

Tachyon: buildup/ping damage, damage control

Wraith: literally everything

Bunker: tank, low consistent damage/buildup (omni-cannon)

AZ: berzerker/ALL THE DAMAGE

Tempest: crowd control, damage, some healing

Unity: lots of damage here too. some alternate effects, but not many

Fanatic: crash-and-burn (takes and deals damage; loses health to do more damage)

Ra: ALL THE DAMAGE

Visionary: support (control, as opposed to buff)

Haka: tank, low consistent damage with some really strong attacks here and there.

Scholar: tank, heal, damage, support (pretty complex, though)

Mr. Fixer: support (cover fire, crowd control, damage control, punches through shields, etc.)

ExPatriette: ALL THE DAMAGE

Argent Adept: ALL THE SUPPORT

Nightmist: see Wraith

Chrono-Trigger: damage, some damage-related support

Omnitronks: utilitarian; combine Tachyon (plays lots of cards) with Bunker (tanky, automatic attacks), with some slight elements from Tempest (crowd control, healing).

My classifications for this game are not the classics. 

 

Instead:

 

Damage Dealers

 

Ongoing Control

 

Environment Control

 

Villain Deck Control

 

Tanking

 

Most heroes do many of these things, but nobody does them all. Villain deck control is probably the rarest, with Damage the most common. You can run into trouble if for example you have a team of Legacy, Ra, and Haka. None of them can destroy ongoings.
