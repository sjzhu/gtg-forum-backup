Here is how I see it. When O-X uses Timeshift, he is forcing you to perform some random action. It is if he uses the power, rolls a die, and you perform some action of yours. However, you get no prep time or a choice as to what yo do. When MoM is in play, it is like a metamagic skill. Whenever you cast a spell, you can add in this extra word, gesture, etc. in order to also gain some health. At least to me, it doesn't happen automatically, only when you have a chance to add in the extra things. When O-X uses his power, you don't have that extra time, it just happens and forces you to cast the spell. If it was an equipment card, I could see it activating automatically, but it isn't. I am not saying that this is what actually happens, just what makes sense to me. I also wanted to show that even thematically the current rules can makes sense.
