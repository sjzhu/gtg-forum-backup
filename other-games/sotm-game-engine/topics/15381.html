This is one of my favorite SotM sequences:

  • You have America’s Greatest Legacy (AGL) and Argent Adept(AA) in play.
  • In AGL’s hand you have
  • Motivational Charge (power: 1 target 2 melee dmg and heal all heroes for 1 health)
  • The Legacy Ring (AGL may use an additional power)
  • AA has:
  • In play: Alacritous Subdominant (Harmony perform: 1 player may play 1 card now)
  • In play: Telamon’s Lyra (Power: Activate 1 Harmony perform and 1 Rhythm accompany)
  • In hand: Inventive Preparation (Rhythm accompany: 1 player (not AA) may play 1 card now)

 

It is AGL’s turn. The sequence:

  • AGL plays Legacy’s Ring
  • AGL uses his own innate power to heal AA for 1 health and then let AA use a power
  • AA uses the Lyra’s power to
  • Use Alac’s perform to play a card himself, playing InventPrep.
  • The use the accompany of the newly player InventPrep to let Legacy play a card
  • Legacy chooses to play Motivational Charge
  • …and since the ring is still saying he can use another power, he now activates Motiv.Charge

Love that sequence.

Also love this one:

  • AGL in play with Takedown out from his last turn.
  • Dark Visionary(DV) in play with Mental Divergence also in play.
  • AA is also in play in sequence after AGL. (AGL plays before AA every round.)
  • AA currently has the ability to let another hero play a card, either through
  • AlacSubdom
  • Or InventPrep and the Lyra/Xu’s Bell/SilverShadow

Here is the sequence:

  • Start of AGL’s turn. Takedown gets moved to Trash
  • AGL plays any card
  • For AGL’s power, he uses his innate to let DV use a power
  • She uses Mental Divergence to put the Takedown from the Trash back onto the top of his deck.
  • AGL’s turn end, therefore he draws a card – Takedown!
  • During AA’s turn, AA uses one of his methods to have Legacy play a card – Takedown!
  • Repeat each round

Using this method, AGL can prevent most villains from ever playing a card! Ever! And since Takedown is being played out of turn, it’s cost (2 energy damage) never has to be paid.

What especially super awesome is playing in the Ruins of Atlantis when the Atlantean Font of Power or Pillars of Hercules comes up – which usually means that the heroes gets LOTS of extra card plays while the villain typically gets NONE. Good times.

I've always been fond of the Isothermic Transducer + Twist The Ether + Fixed Point combo, which is pretty much the only way to deal an arbitrarily large amount of damage in the game.  

But my true favorite combination is the one I used in the Max Damage thread.  Isothermic Transducer + Coolant Blast x2 + Dynamic Syphon  + Fixed Point + Elemental Redistributor.  

Every time AZ uses Coolant Blast on Baron Blade the damage is redirected back at himself, doubling the amount of fire damage AZ has taken this round.  This means that the amount of damage AZ takes increases expontentially, that is A * 2^n, where A is the amount of damage you started with, and n is the number of times you use the power.  Two power uses each turn gets you an n of up to 13 before your last use, which is a cool A*8192.  Or you can do what I did and reflect the damage a few more times with Isothermic Transducer.  3 extra reflections per Coolant Blast effectively doubles n, giving you damage around A*67,108,864.  If A is around 50 (pretty easy with Legacy and AA in play), then that is more than enough to break the app's ability to track how much damage AZ has taken.
