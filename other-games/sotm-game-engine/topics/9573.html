I shouldn't have used this example - I meant to use it to point how all character cards are undestructible, but the rules don't state that.

My home rule is :

  • a: using a "destroy" effect on a character card with HP left just means reduce it to 0 HP

  • b : heroes with 0 Hp flip

  • c : villains with 0 HP are removed from the game as normal except when they have a special text saying otherwise.

As points b and c found page 7 of the rulebook, all it does is give a specific definition of what destroy does to character cards. In fact, it was how I intuitively played untill I visited these fora and learned about the debate, long ago, with the Baron Blade not flipping problem.

It probably would be better if I chose another homerule, much simpler : ALL character cards are indestructible as long as they have Hit Points, and so immune to destroy effect, like the Talisman, rather than point a. It would make a lot of useful cards innefective (as well as a few villains attacks), but can prevent lots of problems if ever we find a way to destroy a full HP character.

In both cases, the advantage is that you don't "break" the normal villain/character behavior by using a "destroy" card, and it is easy to remember and apply.

For instance, The Grave Beckons destroy each non-villain target with 3 or fewer HP - but the rules only talk about flipping hero character card when reduced to 0 HP, and that a destroyed card is put in the apropriate trash pile. Same problem as with the Chairman above.

Anyway - if the official ruling is that destoy cards circumvent the normal gameplay of character cards, leading maybe to the Chairman being shuffled in his deck, good. I just won't apply it as it leads to more and more problems and breaks the natural flow of the game (at least as I feel the game naturally flows).

In all cases except The Chairman, the Vengeful Five, and the Ennead, and Omnitron, destroying a character card specifically would be the end of that game for the villain, and remove that hero from the game...

 

However, Given that Christopher specifically designed the incap abilities because he doesnt like people getting kicked out of a coop game, I would hazard that the /intent/ of destroying a card is that it flips (for the cases of when a Hero Character card is destroyed)

 

The Chairman goes into the trash because you can't destroy himi on his front side... - you'll never get him to the point where a destroy effect could actually trigger before he flips, because he's invulveranble to damage and will never be that low. And he only goes into the the trash IF the operative is still active because the game cannot end, as per her specific rule (and Cannot trumps Can) So he has to go somewhere, and it doesn't say remove from game, so he goes into the trash. Plus he cannot flip back to his front side at 0 hp. He is not Apostate.

 

Omnitron has a specific condition on being destroyed, if devices are still inplay. It actually does say remove him from the game.

 

I would hazard that Destroy would flip Ennead and Vengeance characters based on the same /intent/ we know of Incap abilities for heroes - that nothing really stops playing, no one really gets removed. Apply that same /intent/ from hero character cards to villain character cards and destroy should only flip them (except in the two above mentioned cases, one where a Cannot flip and Cannot win, and the other specifically mentions what happens when you destroy them)

 

 

So... my logic states that Destroy effects flip all character cards, and if they cannot flip again at 0 HP (ie, not Apostate) the game ends as they are treated as 0 hp. This is with the exception of Omnitron, because he has a provision, and the Chairman who has a provision about the game NOT ending if the Operative is still in play.
