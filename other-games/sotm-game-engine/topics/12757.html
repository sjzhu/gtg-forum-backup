Looking at it from a game designer's perspective, there are clear signs that Omnitron-X and Omnitron-IV were both built with the explicit intention of having interesting interactions with other Omnitrons.

See also Grand Warlord Voss and the Dok'Thorath Capitol.  The keywords Minion and Thorathian suddenly become much... denser when both are in play.

By contrast, the game designer does not sanction having multiple variants of the same hero deck in play at once.  If you want Legacy, Young Legacy and Old Legacy all in the same game, you need to use house rules (and have extra copies of the Legacy deck!).  You can, of course, have any one version of Legacy go up against Iron Legacy, just like you can have Visionary fight The Dreamer.
