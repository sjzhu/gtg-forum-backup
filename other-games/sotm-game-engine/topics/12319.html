Cards under other cards (like Omni-Cannon) are considered in play (but have no text) unless the card on top says otherwise. AlexxKay:

Do the cards under Savage Mana count towards Haka's "cards in play"?  Omni-Cannon cards obviously are Bunker's no matter how you look at it, but Savage Mana cards at least used to belong to other people.

No, unless they happen to be cards from his deck (via Imbued Vitality). The cards remain "Baron Blade cards" or whatever deck they came from. They do not count toward Haka's cards in play, because they are not his.

Haka's cards under La Capitan would count towards his cards in play.

Note: for similar reasons, Infection and the Rod of Anubis do not count as cards in play for the hero they are next to.
