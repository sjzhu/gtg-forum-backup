phantaskippy:

It can't stack.Or at least can't be allowed to stack.Because if you resolve it triggering twice you could cycle an equipment card into your hand, then into play whenever one was destroyed, making an all-equip wipe an infinite damage loop as long as you have 1 in play and 1 in the trash.

The "play a card" only applies when you use the power – it doesn't happen when the equipment is destroyed. The only thing that stacks is the damage dealing.

There's two components to the power:

The ongoing effect of doing damage when a card is destroyed.

A one-shot effect of playing a card or putting an equipment card into your hand (and, as a note, this is not optional – if there's no equipment in your trash, you have to play a card).
