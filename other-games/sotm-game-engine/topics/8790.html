When I first played the Adept, I found that he seemed rather complicated, and mentally grouped him with Ab'Zero as one of the heroes who'd need quite a bit of brain-work to get my head around. However, after a few games with him I started to learn the cards in his deck and the combos that could happen with them - what each song does, what kind of song(s) each instrument can activate, and so on.

He can take a while to get set up, if you plan on getting out most/all of his stuff. This can take quite a few turns (even if you use his songs on himself to let him play extra cards and stuff to get set up evern quicker), but you really don't need a whole load of cards in order to be effective. It can depend on who you're playing with, of course, but just having out, say, the Lyra (or the Bell) and Counterpoint Bulwark, in a turn where you also play Vernal Sonata during your Play Phase, can let someone take a card from their trash and put it on top of their deck, then draw it ready to play it on their next turn. Something as simple as the Pipes and both copies of Sarabande can be pretty epic to take out two ongoings/environment cards at once. And never underestimate the value of Cedistic Dissonance to insta-kill absolutely anything that isn't a character card (oh dear, Voss, was that your spaceship? Aww, Apostate, you were anjoying those Runes giving your relics all that soak, weren't you?), plus as long as you have a copy of Instrumental Conjuration on-hand, getting your lost instrument back again is no really major trouble.

I find Arcane Cadence useful in the early game to hopefully hunt down some useful cards to put in a suitable position ready to be activated or put into play on this turn or over the next few turns. Once I've got a few things out, I tend to favour Silver Shadow during my Play phase since by then I've usually got a few nice songs out so the Accompany use is good, plus I then get a free draw or play anyway :).

One combo I like to set up is the Harp, the Lyra, Inspiring Supertonic, and any other stuff I can get my hands on, because of the way you can combo them - the Harp activates Supertonic, which you use on yourself to activate the Lyra, which activates Supertonic again so you can use it again on yourself to activate another instrument in order to do some more stuff :). Of course, while this set-up is certainly nice, it by no means happens every game. But that's the thing with the Adept - unless you get very unlucky with your card draw (or at least your starting hand), there always seems to be something you can do, even if it's only putting stuff out to set up for next turn, or something. One important thing to remember is to, if you can, get a song in play during your first turn so that you can actually make use of your Powr phase. If you can't, it's a bit of a shame, but that's better than forgetting about it completely until you get to your Power phase and go "Oops, I forgot to put a song out" - I've done it plenty of times myself ;).

In terms of working with other heroes, often I just look at who else is in the game and ask whether they could do with anything in particular right now. For example, Expatriette really likes extra card draw to find all her guns and stuff. Unity gets on amazingly with those who can let her play cards out of turn, since this means she can get her bots out faster. Of course, pretty much any character will like extra chances to play, draw, or use a power, but some can really make amazing use of it. Were you playing in a game with, say, Legacy and anyone who deals plink damage (ie little hits of one damage, such as Chrono Ranger, Tachyon, or Fanatic), as long as Legacy's turn is before the Adept's, you can have him use Galvanise again and it'll stack with the one he used on his own turn, giving everyone else +2 damage until the start of Legacy's next turn (obviously this is utterly useless if Legacy's turn is right after the Adept's). Using the Vernal Sonata/Counterpoint Bulwark combo (mentioned above) can be good for letting Fanatic retrieve Wrathful Retribution for a second blast, for example.

Really, the amount of possible things the Adept can let his allies do would probably be far too long a list to write here, because he can help pretty much any hero do pretty much anything, since he's capable of letting them use any action (apart from start/end of turn stuff, obviously). But that's why I like him so much - because I don't think he works better with some heroes than others - he can help all of them!

The main down sides I see when I play him are that he can take a while to get set up, so in a short game he may not get much of a chance to do anything. He also has the lowest starting hp of any hero. He does have plenty of ways to regain said hp, of course, but naturally you aren't guaranteed to be able to get hold of them when you need them. Having all your stuff destroyed can be a pain as well…but that's pretty much the same for every other character, and some of them have no personal means of retrieving any of their own lost cards, or any means of searching for new ones (Legacy or Haka, for example).

There's probably more I could say about the Adept, but I can't think of anything specific right now ;).
