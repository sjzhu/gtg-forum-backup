#2

1) Start-of-turn effects played during the start-of-turn phase take effect that turn. End-of-turn effects played during the end-of-turn phase also take effect that turn. You process start- and end-of-turn effects in the order in which they entered play. Any new effect necessarily enters play after the card that caused it to enter play, so it still has a chance to be processed. The only exception (and this isn't really an exception) is start-of-turn effects that enter play after the start-of-turn phase; at this point, start-of-turn effects are no longer being processed, so the new one won't take effect until the next turn.

2) One of them will take the damage/healing. Players choose how to break the tie.

1) Yep, you're still in the Start phase, so if your new card says to do something during the Start phase, you have to do it.  It's more common for this to happen during the villain's End phase, but it's the same thing.

2) The players get to choose in a tie like this, so you'd just pick which one you want to take the damage or the healing.
