A question was asked in this thread about the interaction between Savage Mana and Ammo Drop; in particular:

  • Does Bunker draw a card due to Ammo Drop when cards are placed beneath Savage Mana?
  • Does Bunker draw a card due to Ammo Drop when a card beneath Savage mana is destroyed?

The answer is “yes” on both counts. Villain Cards are always Villain Cards, so in this unusual case, a given card can actually be destroyed twice, and make Bunker draw twice with Ammo Drop.
