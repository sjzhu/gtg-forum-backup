Greetings Everyone!

I want to take this opportunity to announce that we have made the excellent Sentinel Comics wiki maintained by lynkfox into an official wiki for Sentinel Comics! That means that we'll be sending official corrections and information to lynkfox from time to time, and would encourage anyone who has interest to check out the wiki and help keep it up to date! We'll also eventually be helping to get more official art on the wiki.

Lynkfox is still the main wiki admin, but he's now maintaining it as a contractor for Greater Than Games. There is also a cool new link to the wiki on the main menu bar of the website!
