In a 2-player game, let's say both players are doing growth and pick a growth option that involves gaining a power. I do that all the time so I certainly hope it's okay ;). And all too often I compare my four cards to those of the other person and grumble about how we should've drawn our four cards the other way around so that we each actually got options that involve our Elements and stuff :P.
