A "order of play" question/situation, this time involving Keeper of the Forbidden Wilds, and specifically, these two growth options:

  • Growth Option (2): Add Presence (3 Range) to Land with Wilds or your Presence/Gain 1 Energy
  • Growth Option (3): Pay 3 energy/Gain Power Card/Add Presence (3 Range) to Land Without Blight

In all, that's five different "things" going on during growth: 

  1. (2)(a) adding presence at 3 range to a land w/ wilds or Keeper's Presence;
  2. (2)(b) gaining 1 energy;
  3. (3)(a) paying 3 energy;
  4. (3)(b) gaining a power card;
  5. (3)(c) adding presence (3 range) to a land without blight.  

If you choose these two growth options, can you split up/reorder all 5 "things" in whatever order you like, or do all of the "Option 2"s have to take place together, and likewise with the "Option 3"s?  For example, could Keeper:

  1. Gain a power card (3B)
  2. Add Presence (3 range to a land without blight) (3C)
  3. Add Presence to land w/wilds or Keeper's Presence; (2A)
  4. Gain 1 energy (2B); then
  5. Pay 3 energy (3A)?

Or how about within the Options themselves -- can you re-order the "things" within each Growth Option?   For example, on Keeper's board, the "Cost 3" looks like it's attached to the Gain Power Card part; could Keeper first place the Presence, *then* pay the cost/get the power card?

Choices ("pick two") are distinct from individual actions ("Add a Presence at Range 3"). You can make your choices in any order (when you have more than one), and within each choice you may perform the actions in any order, but you perform all actions in one choice before making another.

https://querki.net/u/darker/spirit-island-faq/#!.bu6od05

https://querki.net/u/darker/spirit-island-faq/#!.bu6od06

When a choice has a cost, it must be paid in order to make that choice, before performing any actions therein.
