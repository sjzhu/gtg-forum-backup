Our copy came a day early! Happy Jagged Earth to us!

I can't help but notice that last expansion we got a major power that destroyed an island board. But now we have one that creates a new board. Much insanity will ensue, I'm sure....

Now that this box is in my hot little hands, let me tell you all right now that you've got to start thinking about your storage solution. The stacks of cards that come with Jagged Earth stand substantially higher than the top of the plastic inserts that they come in, which means that once you break the plastic wrap, cards are going to go everywhere if you try to keep Jagged Earth stuff in the Jagged Earth box. I was hoping to figure out *something* for full game storage once this expansion came, anyway, but now I've got to do it sooner rather than later, I guess.
