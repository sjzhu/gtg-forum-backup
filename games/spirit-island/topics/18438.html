Incoming reprint is a straight reprint.

A kickstarter campaign is scheduled to launch on Oct. 16 for a new expansion. Publicly revealed info on said expansion:

  • At least 8 spirits
  • Two of those spirits were revealed at Gen Con to have the names Grinning Trickster Stirs Up Trouble and Burning Plague Seeks Vengeance
  • A goal is to provide support for 5-6 players.
  • Another goal is to provide more of existing types of content: spirits, adversaries, powers, events, scenarios
  • It has been said publicly that goals include having some content compatible with core game only, some with the core game and new expansion only, and some that requires all three. Eric has said at BGG that right now he has three different icons on them to indicate which playtest material falls into which category.
  • It can be speculated that there will be rules for 0-2 of the token types shown on the thematic island playmat and not currently in the game.
  • Said playmat should receive a color rebalance and reprint.
  • Plans include a retailer tier
  • It is somewhere between possible and likely that there will be the option to order the core game and current expansion as add-ons
