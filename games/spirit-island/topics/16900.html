#2

You activate every line that you qualify for, in order from top to bottom. The exception is spirits like River, where later lines say "Instead", meaning that the later line replaces the previous line.

 

For the first Sharp Fangs question: yes, you have it exactly.

For the second question, yes, you have to do them in order.
