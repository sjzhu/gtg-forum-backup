You have three questions there:

(a) I believe you're correct, that the card from Aid from Lesser Spirits can be targeted by Repeat effects. I'm not 100% positive.

(b) The card remains in play next turn and all future turns.

(c) No, the card is not one of the Spirit's powers; you can't forget it.
