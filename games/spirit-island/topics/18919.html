My friends and I aren't hugely experienced, but we're working our way up the adversary levels, one at a time.  Haven't lost yet, except for our first game when we had no idea what we were doing.  Some have been close, but it's always been by running out the Invader deck.  We've never flipped the Blight card, which makes it supposedly a "dominating victory," but when you're on the very last Invader card, it hardly seems that way.  I do see that the scoring method does heavily weight invader cards over Blight (2/card remaining vs. 1/(blight / # of players)).

How about everyone else?  Anyone else have the same experience?  Or is flipping the Blight card and have everything go to pot from there the more normal path to loss?

I usually end up flipping the Blight card, which oddly takes a lot of pressure off as you get a big infusion of blight usually when you need it.

Generally, I lose to blight. Don't think I've ever run out the Invader deck, which is another option for loss, and definitely haven't had anyone run out of presence before.

One thing I heard someone say recently is that victory and defeat in Spirit Island dovetail, which is why sometimes a win can feel anticlimactic, when you're so close to destruction and suddenly realize, oh! That's it, then!
