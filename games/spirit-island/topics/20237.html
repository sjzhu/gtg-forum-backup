On Q1: "destroy" can be voluntary or not, but in any case is different from "remove". It only counts as "remove" if it literally uses that word, as on a card like the Major power Bargains of Power and Protection. A power like the two you mention (Flowing and Silent Forms Dart By or Hold the Island Fast with a Bulwark of Will) can prevent anything that says "destroy" (like Draw Down into a Consuming Void), but not something like Bargains of Power and Protection.

 

Q2: Yes, you can prevent the Ravage by (attempting to) Destroy Presence, then prevent the Destruction with Flowing and Silent Forms Dart By.
