If I understand the question here, the key distinction you're missing is between a Build Action (which is what is affected by Avoid the Dahan), a Build Card (everything caused by a single card) and the Build Step (which is what Habsburg refers to).

The Build Step always happens, regardless of whether there was actually any building going on. And lands still match the Build Card, even if the Build was prevented for one reason or another. So you would still do the gathering as normal.

The Action/Card/Step distinction is laid out on p. 27 of the Jagged Earth rulebook.
