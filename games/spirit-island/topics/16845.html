For targetting, you can only use presence (and sacred sites) that is on the map at the time you are activating the power. So if your presence gets destroyed during Ravage, you can't use it to target slow powers.

Glad you're having fun. Shadows and Thunderspeaker is a fun combination, particularly with Shadows' ability to protect the Dahan (thus Thunderspeaker's presence) during ravage.

""lynkfox"":

So… say you got a Slow, Range 0 from Presence power. Yet during Ravage, a Blight gets placed down where you were going to use it. You don’t have any presence there anymore. Does that power no longer work there (and could you, theoretically, just use it somewhere else you have presence if you wanted to?

Correct, the power no longer works there.

In general, you don’t decide where slow powers are going to be used until you activate them. The game will flow much more smoothly when you get in the habit of not pre-planning everything in detail; like, there’s likely to be someplace with an Explorer to destroy, I don’t need to decide exactly where that will be.
