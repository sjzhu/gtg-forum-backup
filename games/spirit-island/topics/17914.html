The Champions of the Dahan Token Pack is billed as providing you everything you need to upgrade the game from 4 players max to 6 players. While it does provide extra invader figures and two more sets of presence tokens, it does not provide additional reminder tokens for the two extra players, nor does it provide any more fear tokens or energy tokens (which run out quickly with only 4 players). Also, this was sold separately from the 6 player island mat that you also need if you want to play with more than 4. What was the point of selling these things individually, and what was the point in not having any more fear tokens? I feel like most people who love this game enough to buy the 6 player upgrades would be fine with spending another couple bucks to have the fear and energy tokens you need. It seems lazy and, as indicated in the title, disapppointing. How do those of us who have purchased the Token Pack and 6 player mat go about getting the missing fear tokens and extra energy tokens?
