How does the Defend mechanic work when you have multiple instances of damage in a given turn?

1. For example, if you play a "Defend 4" fast power on a land, then an Event is triggered which does 2 damage, does that leave only 2 "points" of defense for the Ravage phase, or is it at the full 4?  

2.  Does this matter if the Event *only* damages the Dahan?  Take the same "Defend 4" example from above.  If an Event does 2 points of damage to the Dahan only, and the rules consider 2 points of defense to be "used" defending the Dahan, does that leave the full 4 points of defense available for the land during the Ravage phase?  Is it 2 for any remaining Dahan and 4 for the land?

Defense is never used up, it lasts for the whole round. So you're defending 4 for each relevant hit.

#3

Also, Defend reduces damage dealt by Invaders, it does not reduce damage dealt to the land/Dahan by Events unless specifically stated otherwise. So the Disease event that deals 2 damage to Dahan bypasses Defend.
