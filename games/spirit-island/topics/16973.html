So, this just came up while I am playing my first 2 spirit solo game.

 

I goofed and Ocean doesn't have the energy to pay for both the cards I want to play this turn, however, River is going to be playing a card to give Ocean energy this turn. As I can determine the order and both things are fast, I can gain the energy before I activate the card.

Chaosmancer:

My question, is that a legal move? Can you play a card you cannot afford if you will gain the energy to use it before the card activates?

No, you cannot. You pay for cards during the Spirit Phase. The Spirit Phase needs to be entirely completed before the Fast Powers Phase starts; that Fast Phase is when River would be gifting you the energy. (So generally gifts of Energy will only be useful on the following turn, unless you need to pay Energy in the middle of the turn for one reason or another.)

And, if you do play such a card, but something happens and you cannot gain the energy, what happens?
This is exactly why it's not allowed…

 

I added a FAQ. Thanks for the question–I'd seen this issue before, but forgot to make a FAQ entry.
