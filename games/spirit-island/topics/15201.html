Well, I can ramble a little bit. Feel free to ask specific questions if you have them. It's all made out of cutoffs from other projects and found scraps (I even "found" the candle in a cupboard).
  • The minor power icon is plasma cut from a sheet of mild steel. The coloration is a heat effect applied with an acetylene torch (when steel is heated to ~300-600 degrees F, a thin oxidation layer forms; the color indicates the temperature reached).
  • The supporting elements are all forged mild steel. Some of them are welded to the top piece and some just wrap around it. I was going to epoxy them into the holes in the wood, but the fit is tight enough that the epoxy is unnecessary.
  • The wood is a chunk of hard maple. Maple is pretty light in color and I wanted the base to have more visual weight, so I scorched it in the forge. There was also lots of sawing and sanding involved.
  • The candle is a candle.
