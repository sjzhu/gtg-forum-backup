Uh, Days That Never Were is a Special Rule, not an Innate Power.

Anyway, it reads . . .

Days That Never Were:

Your 3rd Growth option lets you gain any one Power Card from a special set you create during Setup. When you gain a Power Card any other way, you may add one unchosen card to this set.

The usage of “set” rather than “deck” or “pile” and “gain any one Power Card” implies to me that it is a collection of face-up cards that you may freely choose from, like @MindWanderer said.
