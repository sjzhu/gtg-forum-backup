Nope - from the Repeat section:

"If a Repeat specifies where to use it, obey those instructions (instead of the normal Range and Target restrictions)."
and Insatiable Hunger of the Swarm explicitly says "an adjacent land". (Thematically, the swarm is moving.) So you ignore normal Range/Targeting.

But if it were The Land Thrashes In Furious PainWinds of Rust and Atrophy, which simply says "Repeat this Power", then you'd choose your target again from scratch and could choose to use Shadows of the Dahan - no need to  pay Energy; Shadows of the Dahan is a modification to the Power (specifically, its Range), which is kept when the Power is Repeated.
