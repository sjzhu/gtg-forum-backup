So, this is one of the reasons I asked MigrantP about boundary conditions earlier. In a nutshell, things that you do before an unlock is created have absolutely no bearing on progress towards that unlock.  

In other words, if (months from now) we get a variant where step 1 is "Defeat Baron Blade"... Then it won't matter if you defeated him a long time ago when the game came out.  You'll need to do it again to "count" towards the Unlock.

As for why I think OmniX needs to be incapped through hero damage, it's canon that it sacrificed itself for the greater good, and (as far as I can tell) all of the data on the steam forums and here suggests that it NEEDS to be incapped, and that it involves being incapped by itself or possibly a hero.  I'm not dead certain that the incap has to be the result of Self Sabotage, though, as it seems like there have been others where Omni X was eaten by golems or killed using reactive plating.

I'll be sending this to Handelabra (unless MigrantP chimes in at some point, since I know he reads these), but one of the reasons that I think that this kind of feaure is getting more and necessary is because:

1. Unlocks aren't necessarily going to become more complicated as time goes on- but we're steadily getting more and more content, which increases the variables.  Even in cases where we have some idea about what needs to be done, by the time Oblivaeon rolls around, we'll have LOTS more villain and team configurations, and the addition of the V5 style stuff is just going to ratchet up that kind of complexity.  I can easily see how an unlock could involve having multiple villains and multiple heroes with the V5 style, all opf which have mini-nemeses within their decks that could ALSO be a factor...

 

2. There is always an initial spate of interest when a new variant comes out. Theres a brief flurry of activity, then it trails off as more and more of the people who are inclined to try variation after variation get the unlock.  Once they get it, some still compile data, but you can't do nearly as much to pinpoint a solution once you've stumbled into it. Its kind of hard to do further experimentation once you've unlocked a variant, since the only way to actually know that you've done the job is for that variant to pop up.  So unless you've got the game on more than one platform, then theres no way to collect any additional data.  

 

3. I can't emphasize how frustrating it is to do something over and over with absolutely no knowledge if you are accomplishing anything.  This is a VERY subjective thing, as I know that for some people, doing things over and over just means amassing more and more data about what DIDN'T work.  Those people are spectacular human beings. They can probably play the Argent Adept really well, or take immense satisfaction in spending an entire game soaking up damage with Bunker in recharge mode with a heavy plating.

I am not one of those people.
