I Minted, with Bunker incapaciated, the others in single digits. Bunker got incapacitated because I miscalculated an External Combustion on the penultimate round. It was an unnecessary play anyway, but Citizen Dare was in play and I took the dare. :P

A few things to note:

  1. Chastise can help with getting Zealous Offense out later. That 2 points of self-damage counts towards the 3-damage condition, and allows for one of the damaging Citizens to be neutered for a bit.
  2. Other than End of Days, the first hero to be able to destroy Return with the Dawn is, somewhat ironically, Bunker. Destroy it when you can.
  3. Velociraptors are your friends. Let them eat the Citizens for you!
  4. Infrared Eyepiece should be played and used as soon as you get it. When exactly you get it comes down to deck shuffles--I had played Impromptu Invention at some point to get Utility Belt into play, so it might be possible you won't see it. However, a certain nasty card is somewhat buried in her deck, so you have quite a bit of time to worry about it.
  5. Try to prioritize damage on Dawn, especially once both Obsidian Fields are in play. Fanatic's Zealous Offense helps stop Citizens from harming you, anyway (outside of Tears, of course).
  6. Since H=3 in this game, Citizen Dawn flips back to her front side if there are 2 Citizens in play. Since Dawn is herself a Citizen, this means you need only one other Citizen in play to flip her back. Thus you can get it set up so she flips and flips back immediately, allowing you to destroy all future Citizens indiscriminately. (E.g., if there are 4 Citizens in the trash, and Dawn and 2 other Citizens in play, you can have a Velociraptor eat one Citizen, and then on Dawn's turn she will flip and flip back.)
