The unlock conditions are typically related to what made the character change enough to deserve a variant.  In this case, the formation of the Dark Watch, and specifically Setback joining it.

The other DW unlocks (summarized) are:

Expatriette: Shoot Baron Blade in the face a bunch in Rook City

Nightmist: Deal lots of Obliveon damage while Expatriette is around in the Realm of Discord

Fixer: Get killed by the Operative in Rook City

 

We know that the Dark Watch was formed to protect Rook City, and figure out what happened to Mr. Fixer.  Setback himself is strongly motived to protect his teammates from harm, especially Expatriette.

 

My guess is that Setback needs to redirect damage away from Expatriette, possibly with Nightmist on the team, and possibly with Mr. Fixer barred from the team, in Rook City (or possibly the Temple of Zhu Long, but that seems less likely).
