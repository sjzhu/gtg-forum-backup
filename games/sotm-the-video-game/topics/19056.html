All five of the new heroes test their mettle against one of the toughest heroes in the game, Iron Legacy? How will they fare?

 

I don't know about you, but I got MINT BAY-BEE!

 

It's a pretty rough setup, but as far as IL is concerned, it could be worse. Focus on eliminating the ongoing cards, and it should be all right. Also, use Weigh Anchor immediately. Then, you should be able to use Harnessed Anomaly to hit a home run in the Bottom of the Ninth on the first try, which gives you a solid advantage.

 

Once he flips -- which should be ASAP -- play As the Earth Turns and make sure Luminary has Healing Nanites out, and he can't hurt you. Then he won't get another turn.
