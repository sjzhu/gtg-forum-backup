Powerhound_2000:

I've done this with Wager Master a few times when he has "Who Are You Really Fighting?" out to destroy him with Tire Iron without bringing to zero and so I won because he was destroyed not brought to zero HP.  

 

Slightly different case there, I think, because "Who…?" doesn't have a condition that triggers on destruction.   In fact, it would be interesting to see if cards like Tire Iron, Final Dive, and Wrathful Gaze weren't exactly what Christopher had in mind when writing "Who Are You Fighting?"  – it seems tailor-made for those cards that destroy targets without reducing their HP.

 

Killswitch has a real point, though – does Flawless Execution act as a 'cardus interruptus', and so the Gauntlet's power isn't "finished" yet when the target is destroyed?    I mean, Flawless Execution reads:  "Whenever damage dealt by KNYFE reduces a card to 1 HP, destroy that target."  Unlike cards that add damage, it in effect changes the conditions for destruction of a target.  Normally, it takes X points of damage (where X is the target's current HP) to destroy a target.  Flawless Execution essentially changes those conditions, stating that a target is destroyed when it takes X-1 points of damage from KNYFE.  

 

This also addresses, to a certain extent, Matchstickman's argument above that the Gauntlet has to be read as not including Flawless Execution because otherwise it would mean that the Gauntlet's effect occurs anytime a target is destroyed sometime after KNYFE damages it with the Gauntlet.  Flawless Execution only takes effect when KNYFE does the damage that takes the target out.  It wouldn't activate if (ex:) KNYFE destroyed the Explosives Wagon with the Gauntlet, which then did damage that killed other targets.
