Apparently Mr. Chomps got hungry and ate my post from earlier :(

Near Mint because ow. Second game I played to what I knew and did better, ending with Omni around 5 and Benchmark around 10, with Unity and Bunker down. Strategy below for those that need help, but spoilers.

Order of the nasty cards is Terraforming early, Singularity, Singularity, Flechettes, Flechettes, Terraforming. They are unfortunately fairly spaced out, so you can't just eat them all and rebuild. You need to build up enough to survive the drones but not too much to get destroyed by the Singularities.

My main strategy was stay defensive, with minimal Equipment out until both Singularities hit. Ongoings will be safe for some time, and Unity Bots are good environment fodder. Bunker Grenades and Omni Beam are pretty necessary to keep the environment down, and I used a couple of Bee Bots to kill persistent ongoings.

Be sure to keep enough Equipment out to control the drones though, as they can't hurt you if they're dead. After the second singularity hit, I built up as much Equipment as I could as quickly as I could to mitigate as much of the painful Flechettes as possible. I also brought out Stealth bot to eat one of the flechettes for Omni-U to keep him alive, though I lost Unity and Bunker to the second one.

Once the Singularities were gone, building up Benchmark became the priority. Reduced damage, increased healing, heal every turn + discard for healing make him extremely hard to bring down. After the second Flechettes, I could start the Software build up, including Intervening Path to keep Omni alive and healing. Once I was there, I could survive anything the bots threw at me and Benchmark started doing 10+ damage a round. Omnitron I was slowly plinked away over the course of a few rounds.

Although I did have 1 scary part right at the end. I knew Omni I couldn't be defeated as long as a device was in play. I also knew that Omni IV had weird interactions with Omni I. What I didn't piece together was that I couldn't win with the Defensive Grid in play, and that I had to deal with that full health Explosive that was there O.o

Mint with everyone in low single digits. I made sure to take out all cards, whether they be environment, components or drones. Which is easy with this team. Omnitron-U is great for getting rid of components on the turn when Omnitron has damage reduction, and he gets to do damage to boot, thanks to Self-Sabotage.

I had Omnitron down to 65 before the first Technological Singularity came out. At least Omnitron-U got some good counter damage and Benchmark tanked a lot of that damage using Intervening Path Calculator. After that, it was a slog where the heroes raced to get out gear and use it and Omnitron raced to destory it. Unity was great as Platfrom Bots and Turret Bots will stick around for the entire fight and that makes a lot of difference.

Bunker's strategy is the same I always use: put out a piece of equipment I don't want then destory it using his main power. This lets you cycle through the deck and find those Adhesive Foam Grenades while still firing away with the Flak Cannon. Omnitron's plating never really came into play. The main damage source he took was from Technological Singularity so he had no protection. The only thing plating was good for was doing 2 fire damage after it was destoryed.

I never worried about not putting equipment out. I just went for broke and kept plugging away and it did the trick.
