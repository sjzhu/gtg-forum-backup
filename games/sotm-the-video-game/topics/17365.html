It’s mostly Steam.  I think you might be able to find it on Android but I’m not sure. Anyways, this was posted on Steam forums on how to gather them:

[quote="Deej-O-Matic"]
  • I saw this asked in a thread.  Rather than have new users need to dig through threads for it, I figured it'd be easier to just have a thread dedicated on how to do it.  I only know how to do it on Windows, so if anyone would like to post a tutorial on other OSs, I'll gladly update this.
  • Your log can be found by punching in "C:\Users\(YourNameHere)\AppData\LocalLow\Handelabra Games Inc_\Sentinels" to your File Explorer.  At the bottom there will be a text file called "log" and another called "multiplayer_log."  Open it then copy the whole thing.
  • Go to pastebin.com and paste your gamelog, then click "Create new paste."  Once it's done, just copy the url url for the paste and post it where you need.
  • Here's a practical example.  I wanted to have a short log, so I fought Iron Legacy as 5 of the Freedom Six at Wagner Mars Base.  Here's the log:  http://pastebin.com/rUH5KU07
  • Let's say I want to find out which round F6 Tachyon was incapped.  I do a search for "Iron Legacy's Start" to narrow down when each round starts.  His third turn is line 277.  So I scroll down from there until I find "Tachyon was flipped" (345).  And yes you don't need to tell me that getting rid of Meteor Storm was a terrible idea.  I said I wanted a short log.
  • The game only saves two logs and that's the last game you played solo and the last one you played over multiplayer.  The biggest benefit is for the discussion of variant unlocking; but don't forget that these can also be sent with bug reports for Handlabra to help track down the exact causes of bugs.

[/quote]

Steam logs are in: C:\Users\(username)\AppData\LocalLow\Handelabra Games Inc_\Sentinels

Android logs are in: [internal storage] > Android > data > com.Handelabra.Sentinels > files

I have no idea about iOS.
