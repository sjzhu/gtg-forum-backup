I played La Capitan vs. Scholar, Omnitron-X, Chrono-Ranger, and TL Tachyon today in the Time Cataclysm.  I was falling way behind and La Capitan got almost all of her crew out (everyone but Siege Breaker I believe).  I almost quit, but then Fixed Point came out.  I had one plating in play, played another on OX's turn, and topdecked the last one with his power.  Very shortly after that, I got both Adaptive Plating Subroutine and Defensive Blast in play and it made the game a real laugher.  I cleared the board with Defensive Blast and did 22 damage to La Capitan with one attack a few turns later (thanks to one of Chrono-Ranger's bounties).  OX was -2 damage on all types and everytime he took an odd point here and there I was dishing it back with Adaptive Plating Subroutine.  

It went from a seemingly unwinnable game to a route in a matter of turns.  That's one of things I absolutely love about this game and what gives it that authentic comic book game feel.

From the rulebook of the core game:

Indestructible: If a card is indestructible, it cannot be destroyed or removed from play. If a target is indestructible, it stays in play even if it is reduced to 0 or fewer HP.

Emphasis mine. Returning it to your hand would count as removing it from play.
