Oh My God. That was difficult. If you've done that achievement (and Steam says only .88% of all owners of SotM have it) how did you do it?

 

TLTachyon, Grandpa Legacy, Wraith, Fixer, Sky Scrapper against Blade.

 

Flip him first, then get an elemental redistributer out.

 

Put a Cortex Hyperstimulator on him from Sky.

 

He deals himself one Lighting damage, redirects with Elemental Redistributer (1). 

Fixer is the target. Alternating Tiger Claw + Damage Boosts (Legacy, Pipe Wrench, Obsidian Fields) boosts it above 3 and redirects it to Tachyon (2)

Synaptic Disruption redirects it to the lowest hero HP target (3)

Smoke bombs tosses it to the highest (4)

Lead from the Front takes it from that target (as long as its not Legacy) (5)

and Thorathian Monoloith takes it again if Legacy is the higest (6)

 

Oh dear that was hard. I spent most of Blades flip side just trying to get enough setup to get it to work. I burned through three Thorathian Monoliths to get it to work too!

I think when I did it, I used a combination of Legacy's Lead From the Front, Unity's Stealth Bot, Nightmist's Amulet of the Elder Gods, Tachyon's Synaptic Interruption and The Wraith's Smoke Bombs.

 

I think I set up Tachyon with the highest HP and then the order was something like:

  • Villain attacks one of the heroes with an attack of 4 or more damage
  1. Legacy redirects to himself with Lead From the Front (IIRC, this had to be first due to not getting the prompt back again later)
  2. Stealth Bot redirects to itself
  3. Smoke Bombs redirect the damage to Tachyon
  4. Synaptic Interruption redirects to Nightmist
  5. Amulet of the Elder Gods redirects to a Villain Target.

I think my Villain was Ambuscade (as he doesn't destroy stuff) and I waited for one of his Traps shuffled into the deck to trigger it all.

It seems that some of these things (e.g. Lies of Shadows) only trigger the first time a particular hero is targeted.  Likewise, Elemental Redistributor only triggers the first time a specific instance of damage hits it.

So here's how I finally did it: Tachyon, Legacy, Wraith, Nightmist vs Baron Blade in Enclave of the Endlings (thought I might use Bloogoo or Immortus, but didn't need them).

  • Baron Blade plays Slash & Burn, and targets Tachyon (highest HP) with fire.
  • Synaptic Interruption bounces it back to Blade (1st redirect)
  • Elemental Redistributor bounces it to Legacy (lowest HP; 2nd redirect)
  • Smoke Bomb bounces it back to Tachyon (3rd redirect)
  • Synaptic Interrpution bounces it to Nightmist (4th redirect)
  • Amulet bounces it back to Tachyon (5th redirect)
  • Synaptic Interrpution sends it back to Baron Blade where it belong (6th redirect)
