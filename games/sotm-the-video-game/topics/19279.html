In this fur-flung adventure to the pilose past, 5 hirsute heroes assemble to try to trim Baron Blade's scruffy schemes.  Set in shaggy old Silver Gulch (presumably because Insula Primalis lacks any wooly mammoths), the fuzzy fivesome of Ra (Horus), Mr. Fixer, Captain Cosmic, the Scholar, and Mainstay (Void Guard) attempt to depilate the villous villain. 

I won't beat around the bush, this one shot was neither hairy nor a close shave.  These piliferous powerhouses gave their flocculent foe a beat-down(y), turning the bewhiskered baron into a lanate loser..  Easy mint with the pileous protaganosts at 26/20/19/21/18 HP respectively.  Though that may be due to the power of my own bristly beard; those gamers who are more glabrous may find it more challenging.

EDIT: So sad that nobody enjoyed my write-up enough to comment on it.  I literally slaved for minutes with dictionary.com and its thesaurus equivalent to come up my best Stan Lee impersonation...
