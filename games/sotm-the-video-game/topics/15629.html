Easy Mint, with everyone at 6 or 7 hp. Flesh of the Sun God was huge this game, as was AZ; she kept bringing back Hammer. Summer would hit AZ and Ra. AZ would heal, and Ra would laugh. Then Hammer would hit AZ, and AZ would kill him, saving everyone else.

Another game-saver was using Fanatic to play Zealous Offense first turn, and figuring out how to maintain it no matter what. First turn it was hitting herself, with Dare’s buff, for 4 with her power. Turn 2 it was blowing up Return with the Dawn, and hitting herself and Ra (then the highest) with the splash from that, and skipping her power. One turn it was using Holy Nova, which only actually hit the environment target (the “can’t draw” one, I think) and then using her power on it for the last 2. She only ever let it drop when she got down to Prayer, Wrathful Retribution, and another copy of Zealous Offense. She chose to draw the cards instead. This was also the turn Dawn flipped, and the turn right before Blood came out. (leave Spring alive, btw. It will make her flip back that much faster)

Once she flipped and flipped back, I tore into her. Fanatic took a turn to set up Absolution and Zealous Offense again, but then dropped Retribution with 6 hp, dealing 24 damage.

AZ only ever used his power to heal, once he got the card, but Cold Snap and Impale really helped. A well-timed Hoarfire (immediately the turn you first draw it, and after Apertures) took out an early environment threat, and a lot of his health, but he was never really at risk. Just as long as you get rid of every environment card ASAP. Except the good one.

Ra got to use a lot of his powers; I discarded Drawn to the Flame, Fire Blast, Scorched Earth x2, Imbued Fire, and Excavation (in no particular order) over the course of the game. I did use a Flame Spike, though, to clear out Blood and a Monorail.

For Tempest, ignore his defensive cards, except his Fortitude clone. Start with Hail Storm, then do Lightning Slash next. Then Chain Lightning a couple times, and eventually Localized Hurricane for better damage and card draw. A late game Electrical Storm is pretty great too.

Mint.  Played this one side-by-side with a coworker who recently picked it up on the phone.  We took different approaches from the beginning and he ended up with Near Mint instead.  I spent a long time staring at the board at the start before enacting my plan...
 
Fanatic - I'd never burned Wrathful Retribution in round 1 before, but it was really tempting with Anvil on the board.  I went ahead and did it and was rewarded with her first draw being Prayer of Desperation (apt!).  From then on, Fanatic dealt damage where she could (Final Dive, Absolution, Smite the Transgressor were all helpful) and also came back from the dead thanks to Aegis.  Definitely wouldn't have won without that resurrect.  Finished with all of 3 HP.
 
Absolute Zero - Poor guy had literally nothing to do for the first half of the fight, but he took over when Ra went down.  He was consistently at or near the highest HP because I kept healing for 2 HP per round (Null-Point + Focused Apertures).  Fueled Freeze came up just in time to wipe all three of Dawn's Ongoings at the time (Return / Return / Channel the Eclipse), then AZ kept healing up until he got Thermal Shockwave to go along with a hilariously-timed CQC. Why, yes, I would love to smack you all around and not kill myself, thank you.  Finished with 4 HP.
 
Ra - Only used his base power once thanks to Flame Spike.  Otherwise, I kept spamming Flesh of the Sun God every round.  It burned me in round 1 (*thanks*, CQC) but otherwise was a total lifesaver.  On one hand, it prevented AZ's usual shenanigans; on the other hand, there's no way I would have survived without it, because Hammer Returned with the Dawn at least 3 times.  Ra went down about 2/3 of the way through the match after being quite helpful (Scorched Earth in particular was a nice couple of 3 damage AOE hits).  His incap power to put a Villain card from the trash back on top of the deck sounded great until I looked at Dawn's 11 cards in the trash and realized I didn't want her to play any of them!  Instead, I blew up an ongoing, then Luminous Leadership came out, so I cycled that one a couple times.  I could live with her regaining 1 HP a round given most of her alternatives.
 
Tempest - Consistent damage from Grievous Hailstorm and one-shots.  Shielding Winds saved us from at least 6 damage from the Plummeting Monorails, while allowing them to stay alive to smack Dawn around.  A++, would use again.  Finished with 1 HP.
 
Environment - This environment spread wasn't as bad as "When it Rains", but it was still pretty bad.  Back to back Hostage Situations came up right around when I got hit with a Blinding Blast and Dawn flipped, bringing out Blood & Tears, so for a couple turns, Fanatic was the only one with any cards in hand, and I felt pretty helpless.  Things turned around after that, though.  I won on the turn when Targeting Innocents came out – had just enough damage to finish Dawn off before it triggered.
 
Dawn - Even without ever seeing That Card, this was really tough.  I can't imagine what would have happened had I not had Hammer come back repeatedly - if Truth or Dare had been cycling in and out like for my coworker, I would have been toast.  I was trying not to flip Dawn, but it happened when she had 36 HP, and it was horrible - she got out Channel the Eclipse and two Return with the Dawns, which made her go from 0 minions in play up to 4.  :\  Luckily I still had some sweet AOE damage from Tempest and I could soak Hammer's damage for a round.  Once she flipped back and I was able to blow up Ongoings, her deck slowed down considerably and I chipped her down from 36 to 0 without taking significant damage (though I would have been in serious trouble if a Plummeting Monorail hadn't been in a position to hit itself).
