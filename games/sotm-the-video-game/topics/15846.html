Yeah, get out as many copies of Dominion as you can and attack with Mere every round (you can and probably should attack your allies).  Don't play cards that don't advance your cause--other than the above, Savage Mana, Vitality Surge, Ta Moko, and Haka cards were all I had him play.  If you have nothing else, you can have him play Haka of Shielding or Haka of Battle and discard zero cards (net gain +1 card).  Other heroes can reduce damage to him or help him draw cards.  Healing him mid-game is only moderately helpful, since it makes him the highest-HP hero, so he ends up taking more damage.  I got out two Stun Bolts and the Utility Belt on Wraith (no Micro-Targeting computer, so you don't kill Ambuscade early!), and Shielding Winds on Tempest, and at that point it was simple--I actually over-healed Haka for about 8 points in the last round.

Used Team Leader Tachyon,  Haka, Wraith,  Youngest Legacy.  There's plenty of damage mitigation and card drawing there.  You can put out the Mega Computers to help with environment damage,  and there's plenty of things (Throat Punch,  Hypersonic Assault,  Take Down,  Heroic Interception,  Ground Pound, and especially Lead from the Front) for Villain damage.  This is basically to prep Haka - he'll want lots of cards,  a Haka of Restoration and maybe a Haka of Battle.  Dominion helps with cards, and you can play Haka of Shielding and Ta Moko to help mitigate damage.  Spend most of the time hitting Ambuscade to get him to about 10 HP.  Once Haka had a good-sized hand,  put Ambuscade under 10 HP, and put out Ground Pound while you finish off the heroes - remember they can hit themselves for to suicide.  Ground Pound will mean no damage except some self-inflicted damage.  On the final round,  use incap powers to play Haka Restoration and heal to max,  then play Haka of Battle to boost a final attack via a power usage or elbow smash to finish off Ambuscade.  You just need to kill Ambuscade - things like Reactive Plating won't trigger if he's dead.
