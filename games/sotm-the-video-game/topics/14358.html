Something I was thinking about while compiling all the start screen texts.

How will the promos be distributed across the remaining updates for Handelabra. Below are my thoughts.

 

Minipack 3 (Guise, Wager Master, Omnitron IV)

Promos:

Santa Guise, following the Scholar's pattern of introducing the promo with the minipack character.

Chrono-Ranger: The Best of Times, because frankly there isn't anyone else available at that point. I'm not sure Omnitron-U will be in existence when this gets created and the others are all part of defined groups which Handelabra quite reasonably seem to want to keep together for thematic reasons.

 

Wrath of the Cosmos

Promos:

The Prime Wardens (Argent Adept, Captain Cosmic, Fanatic, Haka, Tempest): This one seems obvious It's a big team of promos to distribute and until Cosmic arrives, there isn't much point to doing so.

 

Season 2

Vengeance

Promos:

KNYFE: Rogue Agent, Hunted Naturalist, Dark Watch Setback, Adamant Sentinels: Mostly this is just because these are available now and Handelabra probably want to release more promos with the larger packages.

 

Minipack 4 (Chokepoint, Celestial Tribunal)

Promos:

Termi-Nation (Absolute Zero, Bunker, Unity). With Chokepoint being introduced, it seems aposite to introduce her nemeses' promos.

 

Villains of the Multiverse:

Promos:

Heroic Infinitor, Trickster Kismet: What better point to add the last two villain promos than in the expansion that's all about the villains?

 

OblivAeon:

Promos:

Omnitron-U, unrevealed Parse promo, unrevealed Sky-Scraper promo: Just to clear up the list. If lots of promos are associated with OblivAeon, as is entirely possible, these may be moved to another set.

 

Unknown:

Kvorthe Sixstring Argent Adept: Given his status as a charity reward promo, I'm somewhat doubtful he'll appear in the video game. If so, no idea which set is most appropriate for him.

 

Antyone have any thoughts?
