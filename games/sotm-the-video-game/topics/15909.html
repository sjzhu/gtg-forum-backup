Near Mint.  Played the first game carelessly and got wiped quickly.  Second game ended with all heroes active, with 14 hp between them.

 

Keep Cosmitron on his front side as much as possible, as his energy damage really hurts.  The mecha-accumulators make it difficult, but if you kill them as they come out then eventually you will manage it.  (If you have only one component out, destroy that component as the mecha-accumulator's equipment destruction).  You will want to take the time to deal with the smaller drones, rather than focusing all fire on Cosmitron.  Also, nuke Omni's Defensive Plating Subroutines.  Two of them will come out, so don't wait.  There are also two Fletchettes in a row, so wait a turn after the first nukes you, if the second doesn't come out on the same turn.  Chrono-Ranger should grab his hat, then By Any Means and Ultimate Target.  Drop them fast - you'll get them back with Ranger's Marks (although your mileage may vary on that one, since CR shuffles so much).  Once the Fletchettes are past, drop Hunter and Hunted and start whaling.  Keep AZ as healthy as possible, and get your hits in where you can.  Draw lots with Unity.  Be cautious using her base power, but don't be afraid to drop a Platform Bot early to get some hits in.  Once Hunter and Hunted is out, Stealth Bot is brillant for helping CR live longer.  Bunker is useful for getting rid of Defensive Plating Subroutines, but mostly he just Flak Cannons.  I used a Temporal Grenade to get rid of the second Plating Subroutine.

Here are my recommendations for how to win this one. 

 

never, ever let Cosmitron flip. You can still beat it if you do, but it is much harder. the key (I've found) is to purposefully leave Cosmitron's components in play, so that the mechanical accumulators will destroy them (as opposed to destroying Omnitron-U's components. 

 

I liked using chrono's displaced Armory to get the neurotoxin thrower ASAP.  It's a little counterintuitive if you (like me) prefer to grab the hat or bow as early as you can, but it is amazing for nullifying the interpolation beam... and you don't want to get rid of those components by doing too much, too fast.  That ensures that the initial Accumulator is stripping components from Cosmitron (I prefer the disintegration ray), as opposed to Omni-U.  Turn 2, I really liked putting Kill On Sight on the Accumulator, to give chrono more options.

Don't sacrifice Omnitron-u's components to the accumulators. I like to use Bunker, since he starts with Decommissoned Hardware so you can recover.

 

Unity is more useful incapped. Ideally, you want her to go down after her second turn, but before turn 3. I tend to get out a platform bot and then a bee bot on turn 2 to help clear away the adaptive platings (using Bunker to remove the other one ASAP).

This is because...

Round 3 is CRUCIAL. If you keep Cosmitron from flipping, you are going to get hit with back-to-back sedative flechettes. It's horrible... but you CAN recover. Here's how to do it. 

Omni-U will lose its components, even if you have a plating out. Use the damage from their destruction to help incap Unity, who will likely be on her last legs. 

Omni-U, use your power to get a component in hand ASAP! 

Chrono, get your hat out.  If Unity is still up, you need to take her out.  If she is already down, start piling bounties on Cosmitron- you'll be spending the putting Hunter and Hunted out next turn, and escalating with more bounties whenever possible.

Unity... I'm sorry, but it was for the Greater Good (the greater good).  You are going to help people play cards off-turn to help set up (in this case, get that recovered component out ASAP!!! DON'T let Cosmitron flip!!!)

Bunker, build your arsenal back up, shoot stuff, and soak damage.  Rely on the others to help increase you damage, help you set up (let Unity play your Gatling gun), and use a battery once you've got your gun and heavy plating to get more options.

AZ, hopefully no one sacrificed your equipment to the accumulators.  Get yourself set up and tank.  Use yiur Thermal Shockwave carefully- I don't know about you, but I'm always paranoid about my calculations!
