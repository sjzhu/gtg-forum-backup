Does anyone have a good strategy to achieve the unlock? I've tried several times so far, but something always pops up and screws up the end or I get obliterated before I can get Progeny down to 1. My team was Prime Wardens Argent Adept, Parse, Dark Visionary, Hunted Naturalist, and Greatest Legacy. The Adept was there for ongoing destruction, card plays, card draws, and power usages; Parse was there because I needed her, but also to make sure via “Extrasensory Awareness” Progeny didn’t play any card I didn’t want him to; Dark Visionary was there to manipulate whichever deck had the effect I most needed next; Naturalist was there to provide immunity to vampires via “Environmental Allies;” Legacy was there to divert damage when necessary (though that never ended up happening) and to give the Adept more powers.

I got lucky with my starting hands. The Adept started with a copy of “Sarabande of Destruction” and “Instrumental Conjuration” to be able to make use of it; Parse started out “Extrasensory Perception.” This allowed me to make sure Progeny played useless ongoing cards more than anything, and never played a card that destroyed any of my heroes’ cards. Dark Visionary was the one whose turns took the most thought, choosing between manipulating the Court of Blood or doing whatever I could to get first “Environmental Allies” out, then “Twist the Ether” (the Court of Blood mostly wasn’t worth manipulating because you know “Unhallowed Halls” will come out pretty soon). The Hunted Naturalist was probably the MVP, because repeated use of “Indomitable Force” kept Progeny from flipping.

All in all, the most important cards to get in play are “Twist the Ether,” “Unhallowed Halls,” “Environmental Allies,” and “Extrasensory Awareness,” in that order. They will guarantee neither the villain nor the environment can do damage to anyone, leaving you time to get Parse’s cards into play and to beat her up carefully, as to knock her down to that final HP.

Very slow/drawn out build up - but strategy a friend an I came up with.

Dark Visionary, Haka (now thinking I should have used PW Haka if I had had him unlocked), Parse (in that order) on Wagner.

Beat a bit up on Progeny, and let him beat someone down until he flips - just don't let Parse fall below 3 hp.

Use Visionary's power as well as Prophetic Vision to get Meteor Storm into play.

After that - use Visionary, Precognition and Parse's Extrasensory Perception (if you have it out) to lock down Progeny (Top priority is to keep Beginning of the End from coming out, secondary would be to keep Scion of Storm from coming out so that Progeny doesn't get to play an unknown card that could pull out Beginning of the End).  All of this is just to keep Meteor Storm in play as you set up.

Then it's all about grinding until you are set up.

1.  Get the 10 ongoings out for Parse (hopefully including multiple Critical Multipliers)

2.  Get Haka a couple of Dominions out to keep his hand size up.

3.  Power up Parse's next damage with Critical Multipliers (discarding cards to destroy other Wagner environments as they come up, using Snap Decision, etc).  If using PW Haka, can also use Haka of Battle on Parse.  The goal is to get Parse's next damage to the point that you can one-shot Progeny.

When you are ready -

Dark Visionary skips her turn to destroy Meteor.

Haka uses a Haka of Battle on himself if necessary so that he can attack Parse for exactly the right amount of damage to put her at 1 hp.  This is why you need her at a minimum of 3 - since Mere (or base Haka's built-in) can do a minimum of 2 damage.

Parse then kills Progeny.

Unlock.
