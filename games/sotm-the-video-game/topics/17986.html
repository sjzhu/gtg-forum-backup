Mint due to a favorable deck.

Sometimes I do practice games before trying the One-Shot. It gives me a chance to get a feel for the cards and possible strategies, especially if the villain or some of the heroes aren't ones I play often.

All three of my practice games kicked my ass. In the first one, he got a Terraforming Card on his flipped side that drew two Sedative Flechettes (12 damage to every hero) and then a drone explosion (six drones, including several of the "damage is proportional to the number of drones" ones). My heroes dropped from mid-twenties to an average of 5 HP in a single round. The second was a little better, but again a drone explosion and a Sedative Flechettes combo did me in (this time aided by a Electromagnetic Pulse). For the third practice game, I tried to keep him from flipping to avoid the drone explosion and he just beat me down with components.

So I was very nervous about the actual One-Shot until I read everyone here having an easy time. And--voila! No drone explosions. No Sedative Flechettes at all. A very straightforward beatdown because of a favorable deck.

Finally got to play.

Near mint for me. First game I did what I usually do with Cosmic Omnitron - try and keep it on the front side to avoid a drone apocalypse. Cosmic Omnitron is really tough without either some way of faking out the “component” card text with Omnitron X or the Omnitron IV environment, or serious villain deck management. This team has neither, so they all died.

Second game I came back here and read how other people do it. And yes, a straight damage race works here, even though it’s high risk and a bit suicidal given Omnitron can easily play 5-6 drones in one turn on the flip side. This team has lots of damage, particularly since we’ve got 2 variants that trade standard powers for straight damage (Legacy and Absolute Zero). Imbued Fire bumps that up, though I used Fire Blast on turn 1 instead of Imbued Fire because I needed the extra bump to get 7 damage to destroy the components.

I had no need to Chastise the Electro Pulse Explosive, because Omnitron was down to 8 HP by the time it came up.

It’s still a strategy that could have resulted in a fast loss. It only works because Omnitron has a relatively bad start, playing Technological Signulartiy and Terraforming on turn 1 when they don’t do anything, only has a single Assault Drone in the early deck, and doesn’t play Sedative Flechettes or the other Singularity until fairly late.
