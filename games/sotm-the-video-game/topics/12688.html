Mario, you're conflating two concepts here.

What the posters above are talking about is quality, which is very important to us, and is what I'm talking about when I say it'll be out when it's ready.

The other thing is features. We're not restricted to the old console model where the game has to be "finished" before it is released. We prefer to work with an agile model where we can get a fun quality game out to players relatively quickly, and improve it over time based on their real feedback and not just what we imagine to be important. It's how most web services are developed these days, and is finding a place in game development too, particularly in the mobile space.

We aspire to be like Valve in the first way, but not in the second way =)
