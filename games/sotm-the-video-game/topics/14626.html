Ameena:

What is a "mint" in the context of Steam? I have Steam but I've never ever seen that word come up anywhere…

 

So Handelabra has implemented weekly one-shots, which are specific games (heroes + villain + environment) set up every week. If you play the game and win on the first try without using rewind, you get "mint," which is the best possible. If you win on the first game and use rewind or win the second game, you get near mint. If you win on the third or later attempt, you get fine, and if you win a previous week's weekly one-shot, you get good.

Mint, near mint, fine, and good are also quality labels for comics. A mint condition comic is in perfect condition. Near mint is nearly perfect, fine is in good condition but clearly seen some use, and good is also good condition but not as good as fine.
