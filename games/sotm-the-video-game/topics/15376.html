Wager Master is one of those villains that doesn't see a lot of play among my friends, mostly because tracking all of the conditions and rules alterations can get tough. 

Playing against him in the video game was a LOT more fun!  I've always enjoyed having the video game take care of the bookkeeping- in addition to revealing new rules quirks (like how Fixer's crowbars work), it lets me enjoy playing instead of trying to make sure that everyone at the table is aware of all the modifiers, rules alterations from the environment, etc.

Wager master kicked that enjoyment to 11. Instead of a team of people all trying to figure out what the rule changes meant, with screw ups, rewinds, forgetting about things like indestructible keywords... I had a great time playing and letting the game tell me when I goofed (in the case of forgetting a condition was indestructible), and a very tense couple of turns when I was focused entirely on figuring out how to beat the odds by anticipating getting hit, or altering damage output.
