Santa Guise, the Scholar, Redeemer Fanatic, the Argent Adept, and Ra of the Setting Sun against the base Dreamer at the Temple of Zhu Long.

Credit where it's due, the scansion on the rewrite of "A Visit from St. Nicholas" is pretty good. You've no idea how often that isn't the case...

Near Mint. Lost the first game to being overwhelmed by VIolent Nightmares on the Dreamer's flip side.

 

Notes:

Fanatic gets an End of Days in the first three cards, and between Santa Guise and the Scholar's flip cards, it can be her first-turn play. Great way to flip the Dreamer early.

Destroying the Illusory Demon last with End of Days means you can stop it from destroying anything else (because the Demon destroys EoD). But the Dreamer's flip is going to wipe your board of Ongoings anyway.

Setting Sun Ra + Flesh of the Sun God can be very strong indeed. But heal the Dreamer regularly.

Mysterious Ceremonies is worth keeping around. Especially when the ninjas and even The True Form can do as much to help you (by attacking the Projections) as hurt you.

The Argent Adept completely failed to get off the ground in my games. Mostly he healed.

I still can't stand the "can't deal damage/can't play cards" cards, like Whipacorn. Argh.
