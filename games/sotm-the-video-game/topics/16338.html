Yoshi348:

…I experimented on Tachyon rather than the Sentinels, and I feel like it paid off in her big burst-fueled cards. Really, though, this team is ridiculous…

Played a practice match to get the feel of it, and yep, this team is ridiculous; the combination possibilities are endless.  Re: Experiment, 32 out of the 40 cards (80%) in the Sentinels deck are one-shots, so Experimenting on them with SS Tachyon is about as guaranteed a double-card-play as you're going to get.  With the potential to use Experiment 3-4 times a round to fish for Team Communication and Unique Capabilities, you can get out all of their Signatures/Ongoings in no time flat.  

 

UPDATE:  Mint.  The God of Random Seeding was against me, so Experiment didn't work as well on the Sentinels' deck as it did on my practice game, but still no sweat.  Once Human Shield and Sentinels Tactics are out, the Ennead's piddly little instances of 1-3 AOE damage can't hurt the heroes, and all you need to watch out for are Ancient Magicks and Mass Overheating. 

 

P.S.  This team was so ridiculously fun that I used them to take on Challenge Mode Spite and then Challenge Mode Voss.  Again, once Human Shield and Sentinels Tactics came out, neither of them had a chance.  Spite couldn't do any damage, so his Challenge condition (allowing him to heal beyond his base HP) was essentially a nullity.  As for Voss, he has a plethora of minions that all do various kinds of 2-3 AOE damage, so after taking a few hits, the heroes were essentially invulnerable from him.  Flipped him once and then kept killing his minions keeping him from ever flipping again, making his Challenge condition (playing an additional card when he flips) also useless.
