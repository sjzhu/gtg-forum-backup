For once I had a mighty struggle with this one. Ultimately I got the Mint, with an incapped Captain Cosmic, 2 HP Sky-scraper, and 3 HP Guise. My first Thorathian Monolith got played right before Obvious Futility got played, so Sky-scraper took a lot of damage I hadn't intended on. Cosmic Crest didn't last, but it entered play long after Progeny had flipped and so wasn't that useful (though it would've helped with various environment things...). Ultimately I threw in a bunch of damage in the last couple rounds, thanks to the fun combination of Time of Tribulations and Compulsion Canister on Progeny.

I probably would've had a better time if I had destroyed Scion of Blight instead of Scion of Frost. This team is very good at drawing additional cards, so having to discard a card for Progeny's first turn-damage isn't as bad as it could be otherwise. It would have enabled me to do better work at hitting Progeny and keeping his HP low.

In all honesty, I'm very bad at playing Progeny. I've heard a lot of people say Progeny is the easiest difficulty 4 villain (and some say he shouldn't even be considered difficulty 4), but I still struggle with him a lot. Granted, I struggle with all difficulty 4 villains (except Matriarch, for some reason), but I've yet to figure out good strategies for Progeny.
