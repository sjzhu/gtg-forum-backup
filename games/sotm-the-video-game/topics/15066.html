Perfect lockdown. Take Down happens to be an Ongoing, which Mental Divergence can infinitely recycle. Not only that, but Argent Adept helps Legacy to draw AND play Take Down out of turn. Legacy becomes immune to Melee damage, Wraith reduces all the damage the environment deals to 0, and then picks them off with some heavy damage powers. So the Villain cannot play cards or deal damage, and the environment cannot deal damage. In the Final Wasteland, with 4 Heroes, the maximum damage they could deal is 3, making 3 Mega Computers comfy.

 

It's normal to accumulate ~7-9 tokens per round using this strategy, though once the combo is set up, make sure you turn off automatic drawing, and only draw when Legacy needs to get his Take Down; if you draw your whole deck, you can't use the Library's effect to play more Environment targets for Wraith to mow down for Favor.
