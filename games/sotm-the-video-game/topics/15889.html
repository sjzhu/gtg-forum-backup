Some general hints for people who are struggling:

remember that you get to choose the order in which you hit things with AoE damage. It's not always crucial, but sometimes (such as using cryo ammo in a SMG), you'll want to micromanage the order to make sure to hit things like poisoners, carrion fields, and the matriarch before killing off any birds. 

Shinobi can be incredibly useful in killing off the cohorts. Just remember that they always go after the lowest, so sometimes it can be really useful to wipe the field, even if it means taking a hit. 

Cryo ammo only works if you do damage with it. So you'll need to aim if you want to punch through both cohorts to the matriarch with a pistol or assault rifle. 

Salvage yard retrieves all equipment. Useful for getting back ammo!

Mitigate removes a token, but if you don't have one, you still get the bonus. Cash Out's healing effect works the same way, as long as you have the cards to discard. 

Ammo doesn't destroy itself until after the power is fully resolved. So you can sacrifice it midstream to prevent damage from the matriarch... just remember to micromanage your damage resolution to get the most out of it first. 

Nightmist works MUCH better in a support role with occasional damage in her Dark Watch incarnation.  Take shameless advantage of your base power to set up heedless lashes to get relics and ongoings out faster!

once mysterious ceremonies comes out, stay focused.  It accelerates the environment deck, but you can pull a LOT of clever tricks by having Setback and Nightmist destroy "Master of the temple" before it can activate... or draw cards for nightmist to scout out mistform for tanking purposes, or set up fixer/setback for damage.

hope this helps!

That's why I didn't say "all" =).

Most of the time, I set up a game and press play and see how it goes. If it's good, I use that random seed.

Sometimes I am looking for a few specific things. Like the villain starting with a certain card, or heroes having (or not having) something in their hand. I programmatically create thousands of games to find a seed that generates a game with the conditions I want. The more complicated the conditions, the harder it is to find a random seed, so I try to keep it simple.

Last week's would be an example of that, Baron Blade starting with Hasten Doom, Hasten Doom, and Consider the Price of Victory. Everything else in the game was whatever it was. I still play the game and see if it's OK, because 99% of the setup is still unknown to me.

Of course, that won't stop people from claiming that every little thing that happens is a devious trap and I'm the worst person in the world for it =)

I think it's worth reiterating that nothing that happens in a Weekly One-Shot game is any less likely to happen than any time you press "New Game" with the same team. It is literally exactly the same chance.
