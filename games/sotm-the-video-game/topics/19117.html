I have never liked people who brag about achievements in games much. I think it’s a kind of arrogant thing to do.

With that said... I just beat OblivAeon, AND Voss, with the four canonical casualties of OblivAeon (DW Nightmist, Setting Sun Ra, Scholar of the Infinite, and Akash’Thriya), and I didn’t lose a single one!

https://i.redd.it/xn1064wf2fs21.png

I tried to save every canonically wrecked environment, but gosh darn it, Fort Adamant, you had to ruin my day.

The shield was Arc of Unreality. Progeny was awful the entire time up to when we took him out. Major props go to, of all things, Flesh of the Sun God and Imbued Fire, for preventing the massive amounts of self-damage these heroes all do. The Scions were Progeny, Empyreon, Aeon Master, Sanction, and of course Voss who just had to show up right near the end, the jerk. Of those, I never actually stopped Sanction. Oops.
