In my opinion, this game is… Fine. The heroes are fun. The villains are challenging. The environments are creative. But there’s something… Missing. Maybe it’s the Great Value justice league. Maybe the card art. Maybe the variants (Captain Thunder with a mask, a male version of a female version of Batman, and the hideous art on One with Freedom Dr. Metropolis all come to mind). But no. I think it’s the presentation. The graphic design? Boring. The music? Cliche ( Except for Argo’s theme, which is great). But most of all: This is a super hero universe, and not a comic book one. Will I buy future expansions? Of course. But I hope that these problems get fixed in the near future.
