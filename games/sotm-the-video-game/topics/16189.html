Yeah, that one involves a lot of luck. If you're really lucky, you can Risk into having both Silver Lining and Friendly Fire out in turn one, which makes the combo sustainable and repeatable. The Amulet can also be a lifesaver -- since you don't have vanilla Nightmist's drawing, you'll want to use it sparingly, but if you can, say, get the Chairman to hit himself (and then counter himself) or get the Hired Gun to whack himself out of action, it saves you a lot of pain. Going into Mistform can also help, if you can get Nightmist in a place where she's drawing the most heat (highest HP is most likely).

But yeah. It needs a lot of luck no matter how you go about it.
