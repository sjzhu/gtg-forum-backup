Mint. This was easier than I expected, given the setup.  I just kept knocking everything out round-by-round until her megaturn.  The most critical decisions I had to make were these:

- At one point, I had to choose whether Tempest or F6AZ would take a hit, making them the lowest HP, making them the target of the Brambles. I chose Tempest; despite the fact that he had two Lightning Slashes in hand, I figured keeping AZ around with Cold Snap + Imbued Fire would be worth more through the course of the game.  Plus, Tempest could actually do stuff without dealing damage (Aquatic Correspondence, Subwave Inducer, Ball Lightning).

- I had 7 Ongoings out when AB decided she was destroying 5 Ongoings.  I kept Cold Snap and Impale out, destroying everything else (Imbued Fire, Battlefield Experience, Flawless Execution, Grievous Hailstorm, and Living Conflagration.)  Basically, I had another copy of three of those in hand already, and blowing up BE made me draw another one anyway, so no big loss.

Akash'Bhuta's mega-turn ended up being painful, but I still had Grease Gun, so I threw that out and used the round off to chip away at Mountainous Carapaces and recover my Ongoings (and Isothermic Transducer) that got destroyed.  Once Imbued Fire was back out, I went berserk in the final round and took down her entire board, including a glorious Imbued Fire-boosted Incidental Contact that caused AZ to deal out an additional 6 damage.  We finished the job on Ra's power usage before she could start going through her freshly reshuffled deck.

Mr. Fixer (12 HP) - Could have been a lot more useful, actually, but having so many copies of Charge and Overdrive was enough.  Never played any equipment (only saw Tire Iron), only got to play Grease Monkey Fist right at the end.

KNYFE (10 HP) - Punch stuff with base power, used Energy Burn ASAP when it came up, threw out a couple Ongoings that didn't help much.  Incidental Contact was her finest moment.

PW Tempest (7 HP) - Lots of Hailstorm until it got blown up, used Ball Lightning to knock out an Entomb despite being unable to do damage.  Never used his base power.  Somehow never got around to using Lightning Slash, which felt weird. I did use a Chain Lightning though.

F6 Absolute Zero (5 HP) - Pulled Transducers with each Onboard Module Installation I got. Cold Snap first, then Impale, then just kept chipping away at things with his base power. Used Fueled Freeze as soon as I got it, destroyed one of KNYFE's ongoings to make it 2 damage (in addition to destroying the Allies of the Earth). Only hit him with fire twice (Incidental Contact). Guess I could have done it again, but I didn't know I'd have the HP to spare. I got Subzero Atmosphere one turn too late for it to have done any good.

Ra (9 HP) - Imbued Fire, Staff, start burning things.  I threw out Conflagration as a one-shot but never used the power. Good thing, too, as I only got A'B dead by a margin of 2 HP on Ra's last turn, and who knows what that next turn would have been.  Anyway, he finally got to throw out Inferno on the last turn.
