We have some steps to try on our FAQ:

The first thing to check is whether you’ve disabled the expansion packs using the Decks button in the New Game screen. It can be easy to forget!

If you’re on iOS or Android, open the Get Expansion Packs screen and make sure you’ve downloaded the DLC pack.

On Steam, there is an issue with Steam itself where this can occur. Quitting and restarting Steam fixes the problem in most cases. You can also try these steps:

  • Restart your computer.
  • Right click the game in Steam, click Properties, click Local Files, then click Verify Integrity of Game Cache.
  • If you are playing offline, use the “Go Offline” feature of Steam.

You may also try contacting Steam support here: https://support.steampowered.com

Note that when you buy the season passes it won't show you as owning the individual expansions - make sure to look for the season pass (these are the first two items in the expansions list)  and download those.  You'll have to redownload when there are substantial updates (e.g. when Vengeance released,  when the Termi-Nation variants released), so if you downloaded the Preview pack but didn't redownload the Season pass after Vengeance came out that could be why you can't access that content!
