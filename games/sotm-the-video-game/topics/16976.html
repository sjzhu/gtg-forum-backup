From what I've found, the four versions of Captain Cosmic have very different uses. Here's my experience with them:

Base: You like playing cards and don't mind if they don't stick around. You'll slowly fill the board with Constructs unless something is targeting them. You've got the best chance of any Captain Cosmic to set yourself up for a long game.

Prime Wardens: You hate losing your Constructs and want to punish anyone who dares touch them. Your play flow is considerably slower than any other Captain Cosmic, except for that one turn when a Construct dies and then sho golly nelly in the fritter I reckon

Requital: You like playing cards. You aren't too concerned about which ones. You aren't too concerned about taking damage. You will quickly flood the board with Constructs, only to lose them all the round after. You aren't too concerned about that, either. When you finally succumb to your own hubris, fun things happen.

Xtreme: You want to get some use out of your Constructs right now. You're more interest in dealing damage than supporting your team, though you can do that too, if you're a nerd. Mostly, you enjoy showboating, and you got stuck playing a hero who otherwise can't really showboat.

Me, I prefer either of the Prime Wardens versions because I like showboating. :V
