Dark Conductor is the official replacement for Kvothe Six-String, as

1) Kvothe is a character owed by someone else (He's from Patrick Rothfuss' Name of the Wind) and

2) it was a special, limited-edition promo card created specifically for Rothfuss' Worldbuilders charity fundraisers.

As such, GTG pledged not to use that card in other things, but created Dark Conductor to replicate the functionality of it for those who didn’t get Kvothe through the fundraiser.
