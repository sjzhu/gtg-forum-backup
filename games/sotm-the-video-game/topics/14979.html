Chrono Ranger, Greatest Legacy, Omnitron-X, Freedom 6 Bunker and Freedom 6 Unity vs. La Capitán in the Time Cataclysm.

 

This is another damage fest, and you can take down Maria (Helena Tereas etc.) pretty quicky if you concentrate damage on the ship, then her.  I went in blind and got a Mint without too much worry.

 

The Environment is kind of nice to you here, and I suggest you let the deck play as many cards as possible.  I made sure to take out The Amazing Mable, but I left Trueshot in play for the whole game as there was not much point in getting rid of her.  Make sure you take out the ship quickly to slow down her plays, and then pile damage onto the captain.

I had Omnitron-X playing Unity's cards for her, which was quite useful, especially with Greatest Legacy giving him 2 chances of freee bots per round.  Bunker's shuffle is pretty poor, so I suggest using Auxilliary Power Source in the first round to speed his deck up a bit.

The captain did flip once for me, as she plays an early Plunder that can take out a few cards, but Chrono Ranger can easily get rid of all her armour if he's played Displaced Armory to get his hat into play beforehand.

 

Overall, I found this one to be very fun.  Not too difficult, and it's nice to have a mostly friendly environment to play with.

I mostly used Legacy > Omni-X > Timeshift Unity (I tried timeshifting Chrono-Ranger twice and got a Terrible Tech-Strike and a Sudden Contract while both damage bounties were already out, and while it wasn't a terrible play it seemed using it on Unity was better). Didn't get a single piece of equipment out for Chrono-Ranger, but between Inspiring Presence and his two damage increasing bounties he was still doing 4 damage a pop with Quick Shot.

Did a first-turn Heroic Interception, which allowed Unity to get two bots out without taking any damage. When Surprise Shopping Trip came out I used Legacy > Unity to get Stealth Bot out, which made it a fantastic shopping trip - between that and the Heroic, I took very little damage from that.

Only crew I saw were Mabel, Trueshot, and Final Breath. Mabel died (By Any Means, Chrono-Ranger, and Disruptive Flechettes) first  round and got shuffled back into the deck by the ship, which died second round (Chrono, Shopping Trip, Unity bots); Final Breath died to the shopping trip and the smaller hit on a Tech Strike and Trueshot died to Unity's bots.
