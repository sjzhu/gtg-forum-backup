SPOILER:

Here's how I did it. The first three turns are basically devoted to (a) killing the Crooked Cops first so you can kill the rest of the henchmen/underbosses more easily, with crucial help from the environment; (b) keeping the underboss/henchman population under control until Parse can play Extrasensory Awareness and control the Chairman's deck; (c) trying to keep Naturalist in Rhino Form/Resilient Hide/Blend With the Pack as the highest HP hero, so he can tank the Operative's retaliation damage when henchmen are killed, as well as absorbing any other damage directed at the highest HP hero:

 

Turn 1:

Chairman:  Undivided Attention, Deputy, Crooked Cop

Fixer:  Play: Pipe Wrench  Power: Strike (Crooked Cop,2HP) Draw: Overdrive

Parse:  Play: Exploit Vunerability; Power: Shot (Crooked Cop-2HP).  Draw: Targeting Arrow

Naturalist:  Play:  Resilient Hide.  Power:  Transform (Rhino). Draw: Blend Into The Pack

Bunker: Play:  Grenade Launcher. Power: Same (kill Crooked Cop, Deputy-2HP, Operative-1HP)

Environment: Korrupton (1+1 AOE damage)

 

Turn 2:

Chairman:  Fence, Muscle, Thief (destroy Rhino); Enforcers (Bunker discard Maint. Unit); Crooked Cop.

Fixer: Play Overdrive, (kill Crooked Cop);

Parse: Play Snap Decision, Power-Shot (kill Thief), Draw-Ex. Awareness

Naturalist:  Play-Blend Into the Pack, Power-Transform (Rhino), Draw Natural-Born Vigor

Bunker: Play Flak Cannon, power-Grenade Launcher (Fence-2, Enforcers-2, Deputy-1) draw-Flak Cannon

Environment: play Venox (Venox/Korrupton will kill all henchmen/underbosses except Muscle)

 

Turn 3:

Chairman:  Prison Break+The Contract. Enforcers (Bunker discard Flak Cannon); Thief (destroy Rhino) Lots of damage spread all around -- HP: Fixer-18, Parse-15, Naturalist-18,Bunker-14

Fixer:  Play/Power: Overdrive (kill Crooked Cop) Draw-Meditation

Parse: (skip Snap Decision), Play Ex Awareness, Power-ExAw (discard Broker, Human Specimens on top)

Naturalist:  Play IndomitableForce, Power-Transform (Rhino), Draw-Blend Into The Pack

Bunker: Play:  MaintUnit, Power:  Grenade Launcher (2-Deputy, 2-Contract, 1-Fence. This is important; you want the Contract and the Fence low enough so the environnment will kill them.)

Environment: Hall of the Terminarch;**IMPT**--assign all Environment damage to the Contract first when possible.  That way, once he is killed, his +1 damage buff goes away, and all of the Operative’s retaliation damage can be safely tanked by Naturalist with Rhino/Resilient Hide. Environment should kill everything except the Deputy and Enforcers, who will have about 2HP apiece. Bloogo will then come out; put him next to Enforcers.

 

You’ve now survived the first Prison Break, Parse has ExAw out so you can avoid the other one, and the Chairman will flip once you kill the Deputy .  It should be more or less straightforward from here.  What you’re shooting for now is the following setup:

  • Fixer with Pipe Wrench and Driving Mantis -- this will allow him to hit the Chairman, and redirect the retaliation damage to the Operative. Once the Operative is dead, switch to whatever weapon/style combo you want.

  • Keep Naturalist in Rhino Form and keep using his power to draw cards (playing Indomitable Force when necessary), building towards the following setup:  Gazelle Form, Natural Born Vigor, Natural Form’s Power, Blend Into the Pack, Resilient Hide, and Environmental Allies.  Naturalist will now heal 3 HP per turn, and let someone else draw two cards.  He’ll always be the highest HP hero, and given the number of environment targets, BlendWTP will make him more or less immune to damage, even from Undivided Attention.  EnvAllies will mean that all of the environment cards damage the Operative, but not your heroes.  

  • Parse:  Do whatever you like, but once you have gotten rid of the second Prison Break, DON’T use ExAw any more, since it churns through the Chairman’s deck faster, and with the underbosses and Prison Breaks gone, there are no cards left that can really hurt you, and the longer you can put off putting Underbosses and Henchmen back into the deck, the better. You should have 3-4 turns to kill the Operative before the trash is shuffled back into the deck and she can start pulling Underbosses again.  Just make sure you have Buffer Overflow in play before the Chairman shuffles the trash back into the deck.

  • Bunker with Grenade Launcher, Flak Cannon, Maintenance Unit, and Turret Mode.  

  From this point, it’s all mathematical.  Fixer can hit the Chairman every round with impunity, redirecting the retaliation damage to the Operative.  Bunker can hit Operative 2X, or hit the Chairman and heal the retaliation damage with his second power use. Regardless of whether you killed the Operative before the deck reshuffle, Parse's ExAw and Buffer Overflow should keep the Chairman under control.   You never need to kill the Endlings, since they can't hurt you (and will damage the Operative every round) although you'll need to save a Data-Mining to get rid of Immutus when he becomes inconvenient. Lather, rinse, repeat.

I just replayed it again to test and ended up with no heroes incapped, and the Operative dead before the deck reshuffle; Fixer-11HP, Parse-11HP, Naturalist-28HP, Bunker-11HP.   YMMV.
