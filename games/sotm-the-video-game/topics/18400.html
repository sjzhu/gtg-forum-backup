Blackfang108:

Question: why does End of Days remove cards from under the dreamer, but not from under Bomber Blade?

Because destroying ALL cards other than Indestructible, Relics or Character cards is what End of Days does, doesn't matter where they are or what side up they are, End of Days destroys them.

However, Bomber Blade specifically says  "Whenever a Villain Target enters play, put it under this card and play the top card of the Villain deck. Cards under this card have no game text, are indestructible, and are not considered in play." The Dreamer does not have any text like this.
