Biomancer, Baron Blade, The Operative, La Capitan, and Bugbear vs Chrono-Ranger, Expatriette, Mr. Fixer, Tempest, and Argent Adept, in the TIME CATACLYSM.

 

Well, this is pretty intimidating, but I managed to come out on top.  Solid Mint, with everyone a little over 10 hp.

 

I did what I always do when I can and banished Chiquito to the bottom of Helena's deck using No Executions.  Atlantean Throne Room slowed the entire game down, except for Mr. Harmonious Tiger Claw Jackhandle Fixer, who doesn't care about royalty, or other defenses.  It did however, make everyone immune to the Surprise Shopping Trip.  Everyone, that is, except for Bugbear, who we had been instructed to take down By Any Means.  It turns out that Surprise Shopping Trip triggers a great deal in a five hero team villain game.  That took away over half his health.  Then La Capitan's Chronological Hot Spot tried to make Mr. Fixer deal himself 3 damage.  Or in this case, blindly lash out at all the villains for 4 irreducible.  Tempest managed to chain his Gene-Bound Shackles, AA let Mr. Fixer play another Overdrive, and the only villains left were Biomancer and Baron Blade, each at 1.  A little Opressive Smog meant that they stayed at 1, and Chrono-Ranger kept his Eyes on the Prizes, and finished them off.
