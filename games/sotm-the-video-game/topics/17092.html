bolnerap:

I was in the mode of "if burst, discard" since I wanted to boost up those Lightspeed Barrages as much as possible. It may not have been the best play, but it didn't end up hurting my options at all. The other two both had plenty to do anyway.

The thing is, you end up with the same number of cards in the trash regardless.  Either you discard Fleet of Foot and draw the next card (we'll say Sucker Punch in this case), or you draw Fleet of Foot, and then the next turn you play it, draw the Sucker Punch, and you still have the same number of plays as you did before, and once Fleet of Foot resolves you have the same number of cards in the trash.

 

The only case it's different is if you plan on playing Lightspeed Barrage as your first play next turn, because the Fleet of Foot won't have left play yet so it won't be counted.  But that's only if you plan on using the Lightspeed Barrage on your very next play - in all other cases it's advantages.

 

Like you say, it doesn't really matter for this particular game, though.
