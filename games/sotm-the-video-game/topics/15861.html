Hello everybody!

I am uploading a Sentinels database that I painstakingly put together over the course of 12 hours. It is an autofiltered Excel spreadsheet that records every comic, issue number, attributed quote tied to a specific comic issue, first appearances (from bios), and the actual card from which all of this information is retrieved. Because it's autofiltered, you can bring up tons of information with just a simple click. For example, you want to know every single instance of a "Fanatic appearance?" Click and BOOM it's there. You now know every comic (and issue) she appears in based off information from actual cards. You want to see all of the "Mystery Comics" in numerical order, along with who appears in each issue? Click and BOOM it's there. 

A couple of things: 1) the database is current up to the most recent Prime Warden unlock; 2) I deliberately chose not to record environment "quotes" because they are not tied to a specific issue and therefore cannot be used to speculate on chronology; 3) I'm pretty faithful to update the database as new things come out, so I'll do my best to release current versions as time moves forward.

Hope people find this useful. Maybe it can even help with unlocks (since Chris tries to make his unlocks story-driven).

Cheers!
