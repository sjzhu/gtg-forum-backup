Yeah I think you're right. Apostate appears first in Fanatic #25 and runs through 3 issues. At the same time he appears in 4 continuous issues in Mystery Comics, and actually appears one issue before Fanatic appears. I've been trying to figure out the placement of Absolution Comics and Fanatic Comics and I'm thinking that Absolution takes place chronologically after Fanatic because earlier Fanatic Comics detail a fight with the Dreamer, and I think storywise Fanatic holes herself up in that bell tower after losing to Apostate, emerges as Redeemer Fanatic (Fanatic #30), and then beats Apostate. That'd result in a nice thematic ending because the final issue of Absolution (#19) is mentioned by Wrathful Retribution. 

tldr: The chronology of Sentinels is tough to line up across all the comics.
