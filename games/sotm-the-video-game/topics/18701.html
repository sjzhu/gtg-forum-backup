Just unlocked it in a single game.

Ambuscade, Proletariat, The Operative, Biomancer, Miss Information vs. Grandpa Legacy, Benchmark, Dr. Medico, Dark Conductor Argent Adept, and The Idealist in the Realm of Discord.

I chose villains that I felt didn’t hit particularly hard and could be stalled, Legacy for damage reduction, Benchmark to do damage, Argent Adept to give Medico additional plays, and The Idealist just because. I never deliberately hit Dr. Medico - he just ended up taking a fair amount of damage, both from villains and self-inflicted, and used Regeneration when his health got particularly low. Regeneration is significantly more powerful in a team villain game. I didn’t track exactly how much damage he was taking, I just played to win and let the damage and healing flow naturally.

Glamour came out a couple of times, but Benchmark has a fair number of ways to hit multiple times in one turn. The Inferno Missile Pod, boosted by Multi Point HUD and Inspiring Presence, is practically tailor made for taking her out in a single turn. It’s a pretty good anti-Biomancer weapon too. Base Benchmark is slightly bettter for this kind of thing than Supply and Demand because he can use his base power to put a 1 point ping on Glamour to trigger her redirection, and then hit her with something else (assuming he has an extra power use, which he often does).

At the end it was just Ambuscade by himself, down to 5 HP. At that point I stopped hitting him and waited for ReVolt to come out. When he did, Benchmark softened him up, and Dr. Medico used Second Opinion to take him out. Which is highly appropriate given the card art. That unlocked Malpractice. If it hadn’t, I was prepared to beat up Medico some and wait for ReVolt to play again.
