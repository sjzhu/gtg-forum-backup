Easy mint, but since it’s a random start YMMV.

I went for a pretty straightforward beat down. Haka did most of the damage because he drew Taiaha early, and Unity and Benchmark kept giving him extra uses. Hasty Augmentation makes for 2 hits of 5 damage each. Ambuscade did have a sonic mine out, but when I got a chance Haka did a ground pound and then smashed it.

Bunker went into Recharge Mode with heavy plating and the Omni Cannon in hand. He kept drawing cards and feeding them to the cannon, but never ended up firing it. Haka took down Ambuscade via the aforementioned Hasty Augmentation before his turn came up.

Unity was a near second in damage output. An early Flash Forge let me put Swift Bot and Hasty Bot into play via a pylon, and then she put Platform Bot, Raptor Bot, and Cryo Bot into play. Ambuscade likes to rely on his toys, and Cryo Bot is good for whittling them down (along with incidental damage to the hordes of Endlings that showed up due to Hall of the Terminarch.)

Ambuscade only got his cloaking device out once, and I smashed it the same turn.
