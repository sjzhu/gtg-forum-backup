Yeah I got some pointers. See below for spoilers. Anyone who wants a clear start, ignore this

Near mint after a long first game where I wasn’t able to make any progress. Redirects and damage reduction helped keep things from going too quickly, but I was doing almost no damage to the main villains while trying to keep the minions down. Eventually got to ~3-4 hp on all heroes, then Car Chase started taking people down left and right.

Second game was 26/13/23/18/18 and was much more comfortable. I was able to ignore the minions pretty much completely and slowly beat down the villains, using the below cheese. Kill order ended up being Ambuscade, Sgt. Steel, Proletariat (who, funnily enough, I never actually did any damage to and killed himself by having too many clones), Baron, and finally Greazer.

Nightmist starts with Mist Form, Sky-Sky starts with Micro Assembler, and Unity starts with Stealth Bot. Mist form, put Micro Assembler on Wraith, and get Stealth Bot into play somehow. I actually discarded Stealth Bot then brought it into play with Unity’s base power. Wraith uses Micro Assembler to discard Infra-Red and pulls out Smoke Bombs. Get Stealth Bot to the lowest HP target, always redirect damage to Stealth, then Smoke Bomb it to Nightmist. Boom, invincible heroes.

The rest of the game is managing troublesome minions (Manta, Infiltrationist, Glamour) and leaving the damage dealing ones to impotently beat on Nightmist who stays in mist for 90% of the game. Keep enough fodder out to deal with ongoing/equipment destruction to keep the above going, leave Pratfalls and Car Chase in play to help with minions, and slowly beat down the villains.

~Komori

So, I'll toss a tip out for the crowd!

Field Inventor. Field Inventor is your friend. Field Inventor flips Ambuscade's squad straight into the trash, and if you've used the Debilitating card from Skyscraper on Sergeant Steel, he doesn't even get the damage boost. 

I won the game on Mint at the Deadly Choice, with only Skyscraper down. I burned through some folks, then used Oblivion to wipe the board of bad guys while Car Chase was active, and then quickly killed Steel and Proletariat, before finishing everyone else off with the Choice.
