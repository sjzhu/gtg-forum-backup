If a card has text that activates while it is being destroyed, it does not count as being Limited while that text is in effect. However, if this would cause any effects to double-dip (+2 energy from the conduit blades, etc.) only one of them may apply. Ideally, the one that isn’t being destroyed, although it doesn’t really matter.

That isn’t to say that they lose their text entirely – it would still retain its on-destruction effects, after all – but it would basically be like equipping multiple pieces of armor in Pathfinder; if they provide the same type of benefit, the one that is stronger wins.
