Mint with everyone low teens except Fanatic, who was at 1 but had an Aegis ready. 

Nothing terribly bad happened in my game. Cards played are going to be different every game of course since he shuffles his trash into his deck with several cards, but for me it was mostly devices and Vanish. Seriously, after the first turn where he flipped, I destroyed the Cloaking Device and then he would play Vanish on his next turn three rounds in a row. I had Stuntman ready to go several times, but he was always invulnerable. I just spent the time building up and destroying devices. 

I got Next Evolution and Lead from the Front out on Legacy, so traps were no longer scary. That combined with Updated Intel and a careful reading of the trap cards revealed that I could use UI to safely peek at the bottom of his deck and put several more devices on top. I believe I got Next Evolution by playing Legacy's bottom card btw.

Anyway, once I finally got him to stay visible, things did not go well for him. Fanatic Wrathful Retributioned for ~20 damage on her turn, then AA put it back on her deck with Vernal Sonata and a base power use to put it in play again. Ambuscade went down without Stuntman doing much at all to him.
