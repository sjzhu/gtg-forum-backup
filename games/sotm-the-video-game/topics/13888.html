There have been at least two unlocks now.  Here's the link to the first screenshot, and my own is posted in response: https://twitter.com/esaka_jiro/status/667862847641161728

I went with the same line-up, environment, and villain as the first person did, for consistency.  Scholar, Nightmist, Haka, Ra, and Argent Adept vs. regular Gloomweaver in Realm of Discord.  The flavor for Scholar of the Infinite describes him as a gateway between reality and the void, which might indicate Realm of Discord is important.  From his screenshot and his comment that the unlock came after the end of match, Gloomweaver's turn must have just started and triggered a relic victory.  Second screenshot was also an end-of-game unlock, but otherwise did things a bit differently---brought Gloomweaver to 0 health during an incapped Scholar's turn, letting Haka use a power to fire a Savage Mana.  (Even with the other screenshot showing it wasn't necessary, it seemed like a very flavor-y way to do it.)  Since the Scholar of the Infinite text indicates says he "cannot protect himself," him being incapped is probably a necessary part of the unlock.  I also had two other heroes incapped, so even though there's flavor about Scholar protecting his allies, it seems like you don't need to keep everyone else alive.

So my current guess---beat Gloomweaver in Realm of Discord while Scholar is incapped.  Method of winning and whose turn it is don't seem to be important so far.  As for what cards are otherwise in play---looks like neither of us had any pins or Ophidian in play, so that could be relevant again, but it's hard to say from two examples whether any particular cards need to be absent or present.  Obviously, Scholar himself was incapped, so nothing of his was in play, for whatever that's worth.
