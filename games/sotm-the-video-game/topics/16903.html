I never faced any of these villains before in their team villain mode. Despite seeing A World Improved, I still used Bunkers first turn to set up and launch a Grenade Launcher to no effect, which slowed down Unity.

I started beating on Miss Information following my normal strategy of focused fire on the on the weaker targets. After Plague Rat's next turn beating on Absolute Zero, I realized that was not going to work. First, the other villains would get brought down easily enough by Plague Rat, and second, if left unchecked, Plague Rat was going to demolish the heroes.

I switched gears to attack Plague Rat. Realizing that Unity wasn't going to stumble across golems on her own, I used the two Mysterious Ceremonies to cycle through her deck. With Absolute Zero and Unity sitting in single digits, two Master of Temples and an Apprentice Poisoner were drawn. Unity used the first Mysterious Ceremony to draw 3 cards- no help. She used the second one to play Inspired Repair, with which she drew and played Stealth Bot in the nick of time. Stealth Bot was the MVP from that point on as the environment wrecked the villains while keeping the heroes safe. It often kept the end of turn damage from Plague Rat in check as well. Greazer took double damage as his Pompadour was often destroyed by the environment with no backlash to the heroes.

Absolute Zero had been useless for the most point as Violent Shivers was too risky to play. He managed to get both his modules out, but not much else. Finally, on the last turn, he used Violent Shivers and then used the two Mysterious Ceremonies during the environment turn to play two boosted HoarFires which took out the villains in a massive blaze of glory.

Mint (Bunker 9, Unity 1, Absolute Zero 1)
