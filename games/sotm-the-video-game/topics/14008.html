So this week's challenge is Mad Bomber Blade vs Young Legacy, G.I. Bunker, SS Tachyon and Rook City Wraith in Megalopolis, a relatively easy challenge to start us off.

Mint!

No damage from Bunker

The Wraith proved her usual self, setting up with Razor Ordnance and Micro Targeting Computer on the first and second turn, then losing both to Blade's flip and setting back up with Throwing Knives & MTC on her third turn and Razor Ordnance on her fourth, leading to her getting the kill.

Huzzah for (my) heroes, how did yours do?

I can see why you would want Fixed Decks. This particular One Shot (if using Randomised Decks) could prove incredibly luck-based issues. 6 Targets go under Bomber Blade in the first turn for one player and none for another player? You're gonna see some resentment from the completionists and collectors out there.

It does have the little problem that people can log on, see the deck order and use someone else's strategy to get Mint without posing a challenge, but seeing as you highlighted in your article that this was to "build the community" then I don't see that as TOO big an issue. If too many people are using online posts to get Mint Comics that means that your plan worked well. The community is working together to figure this stuff out.

Personally, this wasn't really a challenge with the cards that came up, but the Theme was fantastic. I'd prefer that these One Shots remain less difficult than these games have the potential to be as it will help players who are new to the game be able to collect the Mint Issues without HAVING to look up a guide and will be less likely to make the Community not risk losing their Mint because they didn't look it up! If they chose to play a certain character aggressively instead of defensively that one hypothetical turn where it mattered a lot.
