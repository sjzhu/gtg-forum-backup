Fine. As in, “Fine, be that way.”

Lost game one when Iron Legacy wiped everyone but his daughter out on turn 2. She couldn’t really do anything to him, but could hold out due to Next Evolution immunity to melee. So the game dragged out until he drew enough projectile damage.

Lost game two when I simply didn’t do enough damage, and lost Chrono Ranger to Hunter and Hunted boosted Demoralization. Moral: don’t play Hunter and Hunted, even though this is a damage race. Chrono Ranger’s bounties are required to bring the damage up to the point where you can overcome Armored Fortitude, and once he’s down, the game is lost.

Won game 3 during round 2. Basically, I didn’t play On Board Installation on Absolute Zero turn 1, because I knew Legacy would blow it up on his turn 2, and he drew a Fueled Freeze. On turn 2 Bunker’s power lets Tachyon play Blinding Speed to get rid of Armored Fortitude, and a turn 2 Fueled Freeze on Absolute Zero’s part brings Iron Legacy down to 1 HP.

Also, Tachyon’s Blitz power is a lot more effective if Armored Fortitude is out of the way, and boosted by By Any Means. While the Fueled Freeze was the near-finisher, Tachyon doing 4 x 3 = 12 points of damage on round 2 had a lot to do with it as well.

It's actually super easy.  Sudden Contract is the third card down Chrono-Ranger's deck, so if he plays Eye on the Prize, Bunker can play it for him immediately.  Tachyon starts off with a Hypersonic Assault and draws a second one immediately, so she can shut down IL's damage for his second and third turn (if he even lasts that long).  All those discards also help feed Tachyon's Blitz; I managed to get Blinding Speed on top of her deck in round 2, so Bunker played that for her, letting her do 3 damage 4 times, and then I won with the Impale that I'd had AZ dig for.  Normally I focus on destroying IL's ongoings, but you don't have to here--only one of them really makes any difference, and even it just delays the inevitable.

I tried to be super careful to eke out all the damage I needed before IL got to deal damage again, but there's a lot of wiggle room--AZ and Young Legacy only got one turn each and IL was still looking at being unable to do damage on his next turn.
