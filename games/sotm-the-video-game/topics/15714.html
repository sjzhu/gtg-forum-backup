Lady Luck finally fell, and I could get rid of the jinxes crippling setback (who had been getting absolutely pummeled).    the combination of 2 left feet and a weak heart made it difficult for him to DO anything until either Kismet's damage was mitigated or the jinxes went away. 

the fall of Lady Luck was the turning point. Setback cleared away his jinxes, then used the mysterious ceremonies to cash out 3 times and heal up. We were back in the fight!

we went fully in the offensive. Kismet was mist bound, then hypersoniced, then mist bound again (and then hypersoniced again). We had the eye of the tiger and decided to risk it all on one huge offensive while the luck lasted.  

Setback pulled out all the stops with high risk behavior, and used the mysterious ceremonies to pile on the tokens.  Captain disrupted everything he had. OX exploded and Tachyon desperately shoveled bursts into the trash to fuel the light speed barrage. Nightmist emptied her hand to keep kismet tangled and redirect everything she could. 

 After 3 turns of her getting to have her fun, and 4 of us rampaging, we ran out of tricks. But by then she was in the single digits and the exhausted heroes (except for Setback, who was doing great) could let Tachyon get in the final blow with her charged up light speed barrage.
