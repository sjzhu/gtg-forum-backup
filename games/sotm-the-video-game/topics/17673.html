And the man in the back said everyone attack
And it turned into a ballroom blitz
 
Bugbear, the Operative, Friction, and Greazer against the Scholar of the Infinite, Freedom Six Wraith, Freedom Five Tachyon, and Prime Wardens Argent Adept (because you can't keep a musician away from "Ballroom Blitz").
 
Easy Mint. Everybody was in double digits.
 
Notes:
 
Tachyon opens with two Hypersonic Assaults, and the Scholar has an Offensive Transmutation; you can get Bugbear out of the fight really quickly when he can't heal. Do that.
 
Mayor Overbrook shows up early in the Operative's deck, but I didn't find him that much of a problem since the Villain cards he flipped were usually damage dealers that didn't fire.
 
FV Tachyon's nomenclaturally appropriate power is OK when it's a one-damage plink, but if you're aiming at Friction, 2 points per really adds up.
 
Greazer mostly gets in his own way with his hair obsession, especially if you time it so that it's Hypersonic-blocked when you hit it but it can blast Greazer n his turn.
 
AA was probably MVP with the out-of-turn-play cards, though.
