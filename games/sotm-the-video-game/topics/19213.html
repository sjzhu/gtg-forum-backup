Actually, that's precisely what happened to me and I still won, unlocking Completionist Guise with a Mint. :)

If you haven't played Ultimate Apostate, this is like a textbook-perfect game against him. His advanced rule isn't particularly scary (especially since you can kill the Book with no problem right off the bat), but those extra Demon plays are intimidating. But then he starts doing nothing out of the ordinary for a while... until you drop the Runes. Then you suddenly understand that Apostate is a force to be reckoned with.

Back to back Apocalypses used up my single Consecrated Ground and lost me everything but Absolution and the Aegis. But honestly, that was key. Everyone was in single digits by the end, but no one died mostly because Aegis. :B Once he shuffled his trash into his deck (that has never happened to me against Apostate before), it became a matter of taking out the last few relics while bouncing Apocalypse onto his deck with Into the Stratosphere. Finished him off on Argent Adept's turn with a 28-point Wrathful Retribution, and it felt so good. :D

But yeah, things were hairy for a while there, and I had a number of highly technical Argent Adept turns, trying to maximize chances for everyone to do what they could to help out.

Relatively easy Mint, final HP 17 / 14 / 6 / 19 / 14.

Haka nuked the initial field with Rampage and did plenty of damage by recycling Elbow Smash. Only saw Taiaha on my last turn.

Tempest was MVP, dropping tons of damage and at least three uses of Into the Stratosphere to stunlock Apostate into playing Apocalypse over and over. By the time I was out of ITS recycling, Apostate was dead. He did heal a couple times too, but it wasn't necessary.

Fanatic used her base power and Absolution a bit, but also got a couple turns' mileage out of Zealous Offense after Apostate's one big turn, paralyzing the Pugilists. Key for Fanatic was passing the first power use to Argent Adept, which saved him a play on his first play phase. 

Argent Adept got Bulwark out off the initial power usage from Fanatic, then quickly got the Bell to reduce damage to Fanatic and whoever was the highest HP. All card draw was passed to Tempest to make sure he got to Into the Stratosphere as soon as possible, then by the time he had it, I was able to recycle them, play them off-turn, and draw into more at will.

Captain Cosmic was almost completely worthless and I would have won easily without him. Every time a construct got out, it was immediately nuked by Apostate AOE or the Block.
