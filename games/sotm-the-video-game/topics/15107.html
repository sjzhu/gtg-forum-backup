Jackhandle rewrites the targetting of the attack, it doesn't actually cancel the original attack, just alters the wording of it.  At least that is my understanding.

With Jackhandle Strike becomes deal all non-hero targets 1 melee damage, so I would appy it to the whole thing the same as Squall.

Crowbar responds to damage Mr. Fixer deals by then hitting a different target for the same amount (+ modifiers)

Crowbars + Strike + Living Weapon should result in (1+2+1) 4 melee, and then (4+1) 5 melee.

It should not gain the effect of Living Weapon on the second hit anymore than Cosmic's Autonomous Blade should if you had that on Fixer and used strike.  They both respond to damage being dealt.

 

Thinking this through the Video Game resolution of Crowbars + Jackhandle makes sense to me, as Jackhandle is changing the targetting of Crowbars' damage from "he may deal that damge to one other target as well" to "he may deal that damage to all non-hero targets as well"

Although following that logic it would seem that Crowbars should only respond once to damage dealt by Squall (if Guise borrowed crowbars and used Squall)

We will have to wait and see.

Either way once they sort out exactly how they want it to work I really hope we can get errata for it.  If not printed at least posted and changed in the Video Game.
