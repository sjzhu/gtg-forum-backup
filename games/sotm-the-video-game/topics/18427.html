It is correct. Challenge mode says "Villain relics are indestructible." It says nothing about GloomWeaver doing anything different when reduced to 0 hp. So as normal GloomWeaver goes kaput when you get him to 0 hp, so does Challenge GloomWeaver.

Challenge mode is harder because you can't get rid of the relic effects which are quite nasty. Also, it makes GloomWeaver easier to flip as you can't prevent 3 relics going into play, and flipped GloomWeaver is much nastier than front side GloomWeaver.

 

None of those heroes have Relics. The only heroes with Relics are Fanatic and NightMist. However, that Madamme Mittermeir card does not change the owner of the card, just changes villain to hero on hero cards.

 

Confratulations, you beat Challenge GloomWeaver fair and square.
