This got me thinking of the unspoken etiquette/rules of the game. Things like.

1. If there's a choice of who gets damaged, the person with damage reduction is the first target. Failing that, give it to the person with healing cards/powers. Exceptions can be made if said heroes are at extremely low HP.

2. When there's a choice to be voted on (to discard or destroy a card or to take damage, for example) and you aren't directly involved (none of your cards are options or your hero isn't in the mix) give the heroes who are involved a chance to decide before you vote. Its only fair to give one of them a chance to volunteer first.

3. If you have a way to get rid of a pesky card (environmental or ongoing, for example) say so in the chat as soon as possible. That way someone who goes before you doesn't waste a play, power, or entire turn to get rid of the card.

4. Order of effect matters. Don't just click on the 'choose for me' button without thinking it through. Sometimes you need to take out the card that's buffing other cards or that will damage you if other enemies are hit first.

5. Be patient. People are playing on different machines and on different platforms. Some people read at different speeds (and actually take the time to read the cards). Some people consider their moves before making them. Some people even like watching the game's animations as part of play. Respect each player's play speed.

6. Unless someone asks for it, suggesting every move for someone can be quite annoying. Especially when there's only one move available. Give people a chance to play their own character.
