Unity, Void Guard Writhe, And Prime Wardens Tempest must recapture that merc-with-a-pain-in-the-derriere-cloaking-device Ambuscade, who has escaped his cell and is running wild in The Block! Hijinks ensue!

Easy mint, with the outcome never really in doubt. Ambuscade never got most of his damaging devices in play, other than a couple of Sonic Mines, easily neutralized in the first instance by Writhe’s Lies Of The Shadows by turning its own damage debuff against it, and in the second, by the Block Guard responsible for its destruction in the first place. His Reactive Plating could do little more than plink at Unity’s Stealth Bot, while the heroes hit him with everything but the kitchen sink!

The environment turned out to be pretty benign in this game - Tempest was able to play Flash Flood to remove nasty cards like Char (ugh!), and Defensive Displacement hampered Ansel while the heroes inflicted irreducible damage courtesy of Writhe’s Nowhere To Hide.

A Hasty Augmentation to give Writhe a big boost on his Concealed Assailant, a few rounds of Platform Bot damage, and Gene Bound Shackles coming out early to let Tempest really bring the thunder spelled quick incapacitation, and hopefully more secure accommodations, for Ambuscade!
