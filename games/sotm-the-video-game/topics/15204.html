Price of Freedom Wraith, Omnitron-U, Darkwatch Mr. Fixer, and Unity Classic vs The Chairman at Pike Industrial Complex

 

Whoo boy, this one was a doozy.  I managed to mint it, but barely.  Everyone was in single digits by the end of it.

 

[table][row color=#FFFFFF]The first play is a Broker, followed by both Prison Breaks, then The Muscle.  Operative pulls out Crooked Cop, The Contract, then The Fence.  All five Underbosses WILL be in play before you can flip the Chairman.  At that point, it's just damage mitigation.  I very quickly realized that I needed to solve this with a Hasty Augmented Defensive Blast, so I was kicking myself when I didn't grab a Plating with Omnitron's Technological Advancement.  Wraith and Fixer are purely on Thug control, and are you main nethod for clearing Underbosses with Omnitron-U's base power, which you should be spamming every turn.  Pike is actually pretty helpful, giving a Chemical Explosion right off the bat, then a Trisolvent Cooling Vat, a Biomemetic Plasma Vat, a Rat, then another Explosion, and another Plasma Vat.  Wraith gets her Grappling Hooks early, so take advantage of them to clear the vats before they cause too much damage.  Fixer should be detonating Omnitron-U's components each turn to get that free damage in, and Unity should be making her Bots.  Fixer has Overdrive and two Salvage Yards in his first draws, so you can dish out a LOT of damage rather quickly.  Unity starts with a Supply Crate and Cryo Bot, and will draw a Platform Bot and Raptor Bot early.  Her next bot isn't until Stealth Bot, however.  Cryo Bot is a champ, though.[/row][/table]

 

Sorry about the blatant spoilers, those who've already seen it.  I've got them edited out now.  You can highlight if you want to read them.

This game wasn't too challenging.  O-X grabbed his Ablative Coating, and with all the major damage types mitigated O-X makes a great tank.  Throw in his Reactive Plating Subroutine, and O-X is dealing way more damage than he takes.  The reaction damage (and Cryo's too) can help deal with thugs on the Chairman's turn as well, making things especially easy.  And there's no danger of Prison Breaks.

I managed to squeak by with everyone in the low single digits. Here's how I pulled it off, and a few of the mistakes I made. 

Omnitron's Ablative Coating is fantastic. I made the mistake of destroying it with Bitter Strike to kill off a thug, which led to a lot of damage that could have been avoided. 

The Hoist Chain is a great way to keep the Operative's retaliation damage under control. 

Sedative Flechettes are great for clearing out (or at least putting the hurt on) a field of minions, as is defensive blast. Just... Be careful. I did it before I really had any damage mitigation in place, because I was a bit intimidated by the prospect of dual hitmen boosted by the contract... But I'm pretty sure the Operative's retaliation did more damage than they would have, since I hadn't planned ahead on how to tank it. 

Seriously-Omnitron U's Ablative coating makes everything much easier. 

The Vats are nasty, but you can occasionally turn them to your advantage- make sure that when you are choosing damage orders to get rid of people like the contract first, to reduce retaliation a crucial point. 

Unity wont be cranking out a ton of damage, but her hasty Augmentations really turned the tide. Letting Fixer use that Hoist chain as often as possible went a long way. 

I tended to sacrifice the Wraith and Unity's gear over O-u's components. The damage from breaking them is nice, but healing a few HP from the Innervation Rays were definitely worth losing a few knives or supply crates.
