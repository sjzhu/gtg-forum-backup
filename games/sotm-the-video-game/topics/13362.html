Just discovered another fun trick against Akash -- Visionary + Wraith + Tempest + SST + Unity at Wagner base.

Use Visionary and Wraith to stack the top and bottom of Akash's deck with the limbs while Meteor Shower is out. SST forces them onto the field while Unity builds up her bots. Tempest uses Reclaim from the Deep to keep cycling Visionary's deck control cards, Unity's Construction Pylons, and Reclaim from the Deep itself.

Then take out the Meteor Shower (either by skipping a turn or with someone's environment destruction cards since by now you'll have several out). Unity gives Akash a double dose of Powered Shockwave, leaving Villainous Weaponry for last.

And then Platform and Raptor bots will usually finish her off.

Just as fun as End of Days with none of the bitter aftertaste of nuking your own stuff.
