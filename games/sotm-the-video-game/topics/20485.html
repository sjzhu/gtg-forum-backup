This might be the first one-shot I completed with all heroes at full hit points.  It was all over before the end of round 5.

Early on, I concentrated on giving Guise lots of extra powers to get lots of gift cards in play.  In fact, by Guise’s turn on round 1, he let Legacy “open” 3 gift cards.  In later rounds Guise let Parse open 4 gift cards and then next round open 5 gift cards himself.

By that time the heroes were fully set up and shut Plague Rat down hard.

Had one surprising result.  Mr. Fixer played “Grease Gun” to cancel out all non-hero damage.  Guise on his turn played “Lemme See That!” (From opening his gift cards), so Guise took “Grease Gun” (not wanting to take Mr. Fixer’s “Pipe Wrench”).  Here’s the neat thing: at the start of Mr. Fixer’s next turn, “Grease Gun” did NOT get discarded, since technically it was in Guise’s play area.  On Guise’s turn “Grease Gun” moved back to Mr. Fixer’s play area.  Net effect: the heroes got two rounds of no damage from one play of “Grease Gun”.  I just sorta accidentally stumbled into this.  After many (many, many, many) games of SotMV, I am still finding new tactics.

Good fun game.  Every hero played their part.
