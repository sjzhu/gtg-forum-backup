I have heard that sometime (this year?) we will have multiplayer available on the various SotM video games. I have also played a number of times on the Google Hangout forum. Who would like to join me in some simluated multiplayer combining these two?

I guess what we need is someone who has the Steam game and then they would set up a google hangout. In the hangout, they would screenshare the game. Then each player would be allowed to make their decisions like we usually do in the hangout. The lead player would take each persons turn and input it into the computer. This should make it much easier for players to play with lower player counts and for more people to be the lead player, since it gets complicated to keep track of your hero and the villain, etc. Players could keep their decks nearby if it helps them, or just ask the lead player to zoom in when they need to.

So, who is in?

PS. If a player does not have the Steam version, I am not saying this is a way they should play for free. Any player could be expected to play heroes that they own, which for now, is still a limited pool to mostly the base game.
