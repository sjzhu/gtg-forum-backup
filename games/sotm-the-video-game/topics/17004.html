Comfortable Mint for me here, with everyone up and in good condition (not counting the fact that Mainstay had been down three times but kept getting back up, of course!), though it certainly took a while. I always love a good La Capitan fight, and this one certainly didn't disappoint. Make sure to drop Mabel immediately, and the ship right afterwards and you should be right on track. Also, get Hippocratic Oath out as soon as possible, if you're going for the long slog, you'll definitely appreciate all the healing. Unity also really gets to shine in this one, as using Reconfigured Beebots to deal with La Capitan's horrible ongoings is the way to go.

I didn't actually use any of Parse's deck controlling powers, since Fugue State Parse's base power is just so incredibly good, especially once she has snap decision out.

Belatedly... I got a near-mint that shoulda been a mint (mis-clicked and rewound because it was a pretty crucial mis-click), but yes, this one is a bear.  This was the key for me:

I'd suggest having Parse switch to deck control as soon as possible and prioritizing destroying Mable and La Paradoja Magnifica first. Also, I got a lot of use out of Writhe's Caliginous Form, Sentinels Tactics, and Exploit Vulnerability.

Generally, the first few turns were just holding on for dear life and burning down Mable and then the ship with extreme prejudice.  I didn't switch to deck control until I'd stablized because I was trying to get the team set-up.  At some point early in the game, the Sentinels have that Mainstay ongoing that tanks damage - you should be able to keep it up for two turns:  that, in addition to his DR power and his signature should buy you the time you need to stablize.  Unity mostly recycled Bee-bot, which was helpful.  I got a bit lucky when the environment played everybody's cards - Maria got the guy who zaps at start of turn for X = number of hero toys, which at that point was 13+, but Expatty got Flak Jacket in the same play, which absorbed the hit.

Hairtrigger and Exploit Vulnerability were the low-key MVPs but you gotta survive the initial blitz of all the pirates first.
