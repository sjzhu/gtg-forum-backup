Despite my sense that the same game "should" cost the same on different platforms, I have disparate impressions of how much games on different platforms "should" cost: My intuitiion for PC is that $15 is relatively INexpensive, but my intuition for an iOS or Android app is that $10 is pretty expensive. 

 

Economics suggests that the best price is the one on the demand curve that maximizes profit, and maybe that's what is going on here. They've priced the game differently on different platforms because the demand curves on those platforms are different.
