#14

Yeah,  the Ennead are tricky - things that discard villain cards to the trash are dangerous to use because they will trigger them!  But I didn't realise that actually made Buffer Overflow stronger against challenge Ennead - since all the symbols are guaranteed to trigger,  you can at least suffer two less card play effects (if you are fighting those Ennead) since Buffer overflow can keep canceling cards until it gets destroyed - it does the discard first (and then discard effects resolve) and then it will do the destroy effect.  Technically it should attempt to destroy itself three times,  once for each discard,  but once it is destroyed the other attempts won't happen.

For non-challenge Ennead you usually want to try avoiding trashing cards to reduce the number of symbols being triggered. Cool interaction!
