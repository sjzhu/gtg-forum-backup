I think it's more: With the current setup, the Cauldron people do the production of the cards themselves, since Drive Through Cards is apparently inexpensive enough for that to be viable.

Whereas developing a video game is shall we say more complicated logistically. You either need to be able to do all the work yourself, be independently wealthy enough to hire people to do it for you, or show to prospective outside funders that the project would be worth forking over enough dimes.

So I feel hoping for eventual Steam Workshop support is the best option as it makes the first two options more viable, since I'm afraid I just don't think the last option would be viable.

Or to sum up, I think folks making the request may possibly not be taking into account that "getting permission" is only half the matter; the other half is "finding the resources with which to do it once you have the permission".
