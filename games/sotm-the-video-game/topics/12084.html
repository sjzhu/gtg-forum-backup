I haven't tried to unlock any of the variants in the digital version -- I was having fun just playing the main game and jumping through hoops to unlock things didn't seem fun.

It was a super fun surprise, then, when I incidentally unlocked Mad Bomber Blade.

 

Today, though, I felt like playing and didn't feel like just a regular fight, so I thought it would be fun to see if I could destroy both of Voss' ships in the same turn, as I vaguely recalled that doing so was a way to unlock something.

 

I dutifully picked a team of heroes based on dealing a lot of power and controlling the villain deck: Legacy, Visionary, Wraith, Tempest, Ra. I focused first on getting lots of cards for Wraith. I wanted to get out both of her Infrared Eyepieces and her Utility belt.

 

You see where this is going, right? I incidentally unlocked the Rook City Wraith promo by using both of the Infrared Eyepieces. So much fun! I also successfully unlocked Cosmic Omnitron by destroying both ships in the same turn. Still fun, but not the same sort of joyous surprise.

 

Still, if I hadn't been _trying_ to destroy both ships in the same turn, I have difficulty imagining that I ever would have done it incidentally.
