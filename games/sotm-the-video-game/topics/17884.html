I found it a relatively easy mint, mainly because I dug for Telekinetic Coccoon with Omnitron, and got it right away. I knew Visionary would rapidly end up with the most HP, which meant that flipping Kismet twice meant that she’d end up with the Talisman, a bunch of Jinxes, and she’d tank all the damage provided Kismet didn’t play too many lucky cards.

Secondary was getting damage reduction on Kismet. To that end, I got out Neurotoxin dart gun for Chrono Ranger, Twist the Ether (before the Coccoon went up), and Stun Bolt. That’s -3 to all Kismet’s damage, which meant her “attack all heroes” effects fizzled.

Chrono Ranger and Wraith took a ton of damage before I got this set up, but didn’t fall down. The first-turn Prison RIot was bad, bringing out Char, Time Crazed Prisoner, and a Rogue, but it was followed by a Lock Down so I didn’t need to worry too much about the environment.

Once Wraith had an Infrared Eyepiece out, I just made sure that Kismet played nothing but Jinxes, which all piled on Visionary one way or another. Chrono Ranger put down bounties to boost damage, and Omnitron got a bunch of weapon components out since he wasn’t taking any damage. I probably should have prioritized the Eyepiece over Smoke Bombs and Stun Bolt, since in practice I never got a benefit from Smoke Bombs - Kismet was only attacking Visionary.

By the time I was done Chrono Ranger and Wraith were back in the mid-teens because of a Omnitron’s healing rays. Chrono Ranger’s relatively low health did make me delay putting out Hunter and Hunted, but even with By Any Means and The Ultimate Target out, he was doing 6-9 damage a turn to Kismet. 3 damage from the dart thrower, plus 3 damage any time he played something like Eye on the Prize.

Near Mint.

The first game was BRUTAL. I managed to end up with five jinxes out, the talisman in her hand, and her playing cards that let her play other cards in addition to doing damage. Each hero took 15+ damage in that single round, which killed everyone but Omnitron and left him at 1 hp.

Along the way to getting my butt kicked, I realized that Decoy Projection is not your friend when you have Smoke Bombs out. They do a great job of keeping it alive. I also made a mistake of having Wraith churn way too many cards digging for Mental Divergence, which never came up. I also realized that Demoralization is not your friend as it hits the talisman when it's in your own hand. Similarly, Omnitron's Defensive Blast needs to be done judiciously.

The second time around, I took out jinxes and unluckies whenever I could so they wouldn't build up. Twist the Ether, Stun Bolt, and Neurotoxin Dart coupled with Omnitron's plating kept the damage more reasonable. What I'm proud of is dropping Wrest the Mind on the Time Crazed Prisoner when the environment trash was full. 

A strategy I thought of but didn't try would be to use the Talisman's ability to rearrange the first three cards on the Environment on the first round. That'd move the Prison Break back. Of course, that's non-intuitive so it only looked like a good strategy in hindsight.

So the second time around, I finished with Wraith at 3 (where she sat for a few rounds to my surprise) and everyone else in their mid-teens. That was a definite improvement over round one.
