First off, hi.  Didn't see an "introduce yourself" thread if there is one.

Second, wasn't sure if this was the place to discuss the PC port of the card game, the one I just Kickstartered, or both.

So, a couple thoughts after playing the game for 50+ hours now.

  • This game and the setting are totally badass, and I want, if not the real card game, the Tabletop RPG at least.
  • Don't know if it's in a future expansion, and don't have the real card game to know if there's one in it.  We've got Wagner Mars Base, and we've got Omnitron IV, but there's no "Just Somewhere Out In Space Fighting Aliens and Spaceships" environment that I'm aware of.  If it doesn't exist, the game needs one.
  • Naturally, one of the things that happens getting into a game like this is Headcanon; you start making your own issues of Sentinel Comics in your head, and your own superteams and story and such.  I notice that it's hard to put together 5-man groups for such stories.  So, if I want to do a 5-man "The Deep Space characters have a story together" or "All the Time-Travelers," or "Batman-level types," I generally end up having an Odd Man Out.  Or something really detailed, like, "The immortal characters face a problem at the end of time that ends up reverberating into the past, so the one who goes back in time has to go get help from the past and solve the problem in the past and the future together." Can't expect the devs to think of everything, naturally; and that 5th spot is a good place for Guise "just show up, for some reason," so it works well with him, but I can't find any good 5ths to round out a team like that.  I've got a Justice League of Ame, here.  One Avenger who's gotta Assemble all by himself.
