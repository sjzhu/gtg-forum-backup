Near Mint. Argh, I really do not like the Matriarch. Not simply because she’s a difficult villain (though she is), but because the game can get very slow. Especially if I have to make decisions, like whether to engage a damage redirect card, for everything.

And Madame Mittermeier’s just compounds the problem.

Near Mint, because the first time I had no idea what I was up to.

Tips:

Get damage redirection/mitigation out early. After a couple of turns, the Environment is going to start making its presence known in a big way. Be ready.

Speaking of, there’s only one (well, three copies of one) Environment-destruction card in all three of the decks you’re playing with, and Mme. M’s deck has probably the most annoying card in the entire game. (Hall of Mirrors.) Be warned.

Don’t even bother with the flock. See point 1 about the Environment.

Mainstay for MVP.

The rule that the four Sentinels count as each other’s nemesis is really annoying, but not a big enough deal to make or break the game.

After a certain point, while you’re technically fighting the villain, it’s more like you’re competing against her to survive the carnival.

For those having trouble with this one here is a guaranteed way to win although long.

In the beginning just focus on defense you don’t even have to deal damage. 

Never deal damge to Carrion Fields and be careful that Unstable Midway does do get them below 1 HP before step 4

Step 1: Get Durasteel Chains out with Unique Capabilities and use block every turn.

Most importantly Muninn must not destroy Durasteel Chains.  Use Unity equipment as fodder.

Step 2: As soon as Huginn comes out kill him.  This is important because Muninn comes out right before the deck gets reshuffled. 

Step 3: Get Muninn to exactly 1 HP and as many heroes as you can to below 5 HP.

Be careful of Unstable Midway dealing damage to Muninn.  Use Bee Bot to protect him if necessary.

Step 4: Once Gone Without a Trace is active use it to remove Muninn from the game.

Step 5: Let all your heroes and sentinels other than Mainstay die.

Step 6: Wait for the environment to kill Matriarch for you.

 

With Muninn removed and Durasteel Chains out with block there is no damage except for self-inflicted environment damage that is more than 2 so Mainstay is immune.  With Freak Show from the environment out and Mainstay at lowest of all hero targets he is immune to self-inflicted damage.  Eventually You Won't Believe Your Eyes will kill Matriarch without you ever having to lay a finger on her.
