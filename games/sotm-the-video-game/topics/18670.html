Fairly easy mint, though the Adamant Sentinels weren’t so adamant, and took a lot of damage. Nobody went down, though.

Despite the title, I went with More Is More, and played lots of equipment and ongoing cards. The advantages more than outweighed the drawbacks.

Chokepoint does a ton of damage on her 2nd turn by playing Stripped Resources and destroying 4 environment cards. Turn 1 Durasteel Chains + Human Shield + TK Thump absorbed all of that. It didn’t last, though, she took away the Chains her next turn and after that it wasn’t worth maintaining Human Shield.

Yes, I flipped Chokepoint, but I was focused enough on damage that her damage reduction didn’t matter too much. Legacy’s Galvanize meant that even low-damage attacks like TK Thump were landing. He got out Inspiring Presence soon enough, which meant +2 damage all around.

I did use Mr. Fixer’s Strike power, after building him up with a style, tool, and Harmony. Even used Overdrive. I chose Grease Monkey Fist for the extra damage instead of Driving Mantis, because I figured that it’d be over long before damage to Mr. Fixer mattered. By the time he started attacking he was doing 6-7 damage per hit, which was worth taking Chokepoint’s counter-damage and her healing for destroying a card.

Mass healing between Cleansing Downpour and Dr. Medico’s base ability was mostly enough to offset those AoE attacks. TK Thump helped significantly cutting with all that AoE damage.
