Mint. It started out decent, but a turn with a lot of card draws took out everyone but Haka and Argent Adept. Argent Adept lasted another few turns and then Haka stood up to the monster by himself. And then it got easy. First, I used all the incap powers to draw Haka's deck into hand, using Vitality Surge and Ground Pound to survive the damage. Then:

Tempest incap to remove bad ongoings or put "Time of Tribulations" out to destroy a Scion. (I made sure to keep Haka at 9 hp exactly)

Argent Adept incap destroyed an environment card to have Haka draw 3 cards thanks to Dominionx3.

Captain Cosmic incap let Haka discard 1 to draw 3.

Fanatic incap let Haka use his base power to play extra cards.

Haka played Ground Pound to stop all damage and then played Haka of Battle just enough so that with all the card draws he got, he always had his other Ground Pound in hand and could continually cycle through his remaining deck.

Haka kept the Ground Pounds going for the whole game. Even with Beginning of the End coming up, I made sure all of Haka's ongoings were out so Ground Pound would never need to be sacrificed. Eventually, the damage and Haka of Battles added up and brought down Progeny.

I had a shockingly easy Mint considering the hot mess of starter hands I had where the only hero who could do damage was PW Captain Cosmic, of all people. Thankfully, Argent Adept started with Bulwark / Instrumental Conjuration, so he got set up with the Bell quickly and was directly responsible for Tempest and Fanatic surviving the fight - a triple dose of Bulwark on Tempest and a double dose on Fanatic protected Tempest from death to Progeny's flip side, which as soon as he flipped was augmented by Time of Tribulations, and saved Fanatic from taking 12 damage from Elemental Outburst immediately afterward (instead, she took a cozy 6). 

Everyone contributed, but the biggest factor in the victory was probably Progeny's deck order. He opened Scion of Blight (helpfully countered by having a bunch of relatively useless cards in hand and extra draws from Bulwark) / Elemental Outburst. Round 2, Scion of Flame. Round 3, Time of Tribulations, which I promptly Stratosphered back onto his deck. Round 4, he flipped because Tempest was under 10 HP and played Time of Tribulations into another Elemental Outburst... and then he went down like a sack of potatoes from 50 to 0 thanks in part to a fun Reclaim from the Deep:

Tempest: Reclaim from the Deep to put Lightning Slash back on top. Base power plays Lightning Slash for 5.

AA: Syncopated Onslaught on Captain Cosmic and Fanatic.

Captain Cosmic: Reclaim from the Deep put a Construct back on top. Harsh Offense for 5.

Fanatic: Reclaim from the Deep put Smite the Transgressor on top. Wrathful Retribution for 20, then base power plays Smite the Transgressor for 3, which lets Haka hit for 3, and also lets Fanatic use Absolution for another 4.

Haka: Haka of Battle dumps his entire hand to boost a Taiaha to 10, gg.

Games like this - where I have a ton of fun and get to do cool stuff despite being sick of fighting Progeny thanks to the Bunker variant hunt - are why it's so hard to put this game down.
