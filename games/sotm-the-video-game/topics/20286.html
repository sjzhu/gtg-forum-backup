Maria Helena (Both of them? More?) is causing fractures in the space-time-quantum-jelly-ness! The heroes must stop her before time implodes like an overstuffed sandwich! Or something.


Advanced La Capitan (Destroy 1 ongoing/equipment per turn / First time she would be dealt damage each round, redirect it to highest HP non-villain) is in the Time Cataclysm, but an older and wiser La Comodora has recruited some Dark Watch to help stop her(self?). DW Nightmist, DW Harpy, and DW Expatirette have answered the call.

Good luck heroes!

Spoilers: Near Mint after a first game where I couldn't seem to keep the crew under control fast enough to not die. Nightmist even tanked for a lot of damage in Mist Form but it just delayed the loss. Second game, I used Astral Premonition to mess around with La Capitan's deck and alter the shuffles. This meant that much less dangerous cards came out throughout the game and the heroes won, though at low hp.


Harpy and Expatirette can turn into damage powerhouses, but its all plink damage. I couldn't seem to find a Figurehead to boost damage until way late, but its probably just a matter of getting the right shuffle with Comodora's base power. Harpy Hex + Direct Strike or an Unload with 4 guns, either with a damage boost means lots of damage. One way to get a boost is to get Temporal Rigging out and allow Expat to use her base power, then pew pew her next turn. Also, I actually could get some use out of Brig Teleporter since La Capitan has a lot of low hp minions.

Near Mint on this one. The first game, I let La Capitan get too many crew out. Second game was all about deck control.

Once I had Mabel down, La Commodora played Timeless Treasure. Then I made sure the other characters kept playing enough equipment and on-goings to ensure it wouldn't get destroyed. I used the extra card draws for herself, Nightmist, and whichever third character might need it.

Then Nightmist went hunting for Mistbound and once she'd gotten it, I used La Commodora's character ability to keep cycling it out of the trash. Dark Watch's character ability guaranteed she could draw it every time it was cycled back in. I did use Nightmist's Astral Premonition at times, but once I had the cycle with Mistbound, that was preferable. No cards from La Capitan beats picking her card.
