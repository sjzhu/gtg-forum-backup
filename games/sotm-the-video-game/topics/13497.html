So, one of the things I really like about the game is the ability to really experiment with characters in somewhat controlled circumstances, so I'm curious which villains and environments you guys go with when you want a safe-ish place to try out some combination or set-up for a character (working out the Argent Adept's labyrinthian combos, seeing what all of Unity's bots out at once can get done, etc.).

For me, this basically means villains and environments with no -- or easily avoidable -- ongoing and equipment destruction and minimal mass damage. Only playing one card a round normally helps, too.

For me this usually means someone like Apostate, whose continuous cards tend to target the person with the highest HP, and whose ongoing/equipment destruction is either one at a time or the result of an ongoing card you can nuke before it's a problem. Ambuscade is also good, since I don't think he has any ongoing/equipment destruction at all.

Akash'Bhuta is also a good one for this, mainly because she has so much HP that you'll definitely have time to do the set ups, even if you have a bunch of mandatory-damage stuff out.

For Environments, Megalopolis tends to be good, Paparazzi and Hostage Situations aside, for its lack of equipment/ongoing destruction. Realm of Discord is also pretty neutral in that regard and easy enough to navigate.

What about you guys? When you want to try something out with little pressure, where do you go to?

My default when I'm trying for max damage Absolute Zero runs is Voss in Insula Primalis.  The only destruction on the island is Thieving Pterodactyl (easy to kill before it destroys anything), Primordial Plant Life (optional), and River of Lava (optional).  Voss's only destruction is one of his ships.  The ship is a bit harder to kill, but it only destroys one thing a turn and there's only one copy of it in the deck.

Mass damage is a bit more plentiful, but Insula Primalis' mass damage (other than Volcanic Eruption and Thieving Pterodactyl, both of which are easy to get rid of) is all per hero, not per hero target, keeping Unity's bots safe.  And while Voss's ships are painful, the mass damage minions are easy to kill.
