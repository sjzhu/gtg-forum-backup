I've been working on it for a few hours, and here are some tips that may or may not be helpful.

For Expatriette: Consider starting a new seed if she starts with no guns.  Things tend to kick off very fast, and you really can't wait around.  IF you start with hair trigger reflexes, but no gun, then you are actually in a great place- she can spend tons of time aiming and picking off thieves and informants as soon as they show up.  

For Fixer: The jack handle is a godsend for when things pile up, but crowbars are fantastic for amping up your damage and a hoist chain can shut down the operative... until the environment or the contract amp her back up again.  If you start with no tools, then its going to get dicey.  You really need that multi-target damage ASAP, or the ability to cut down on the operative's retaliation. 

For Nightmist: I've yet to really come up with a good strategy for DW Nightmist. You can set up your spells for maximum effect with attunement, but the self harm aspect gets out of hand so, so fast. You rarely have time to set up, what with the thieves lurking about, and your teammates just won't have the HP to spare to really let you cut loose with Oblivions and the like. Mist form can be fantastic for tanking... but only if your teammates have the AoE damage that they need to clear out enemies. If fixer is stuck hitting one target at a time, and expatriette is plinking away with an SMG, then going into mistform is just prolonging the fight and letting the chairman build up the army.  Really, the only thing I can say is to HAVE A PLAN when going into mistform- I tend to do it as a panic measure when I think that things are getting grim, and it hasn't turned out well.

For the Chairman: If they start out with the Contract, just reset.  The entire pace of the fight is accelerated, and that is NOT to your benefit.  The ideal start, such as it is, is one where you get informants out ASAP- you actually want to let the chairman play cards so that you can shuffle those thugs into the deck.  the onyl trick is knowing when to really go all out- the key is to wipe the board entirely (or mostly) of thugs, but NOT underbosses- then have the deck shuffle.  If you can do that, you might actually pull off a win.  Might.  This is a hellish fight, and I fully expect to spend several days trying (and I still might fail).

Good luck!

Here is what helped me, but so much of this just comes down to having the right tools at the right time. 

Expatriette: in your opening hand, look forHair Trigger Reflexes. Aimed HTR is the single best protection the team has against thieves and informants, so getting that out immediately is incredibly useful. Hopefully you get some Cryo ammo later, for when the chairman flips  

Fixer: having a jack handle to start is amazing. The ideal is to start with the handle, get out mantis style, then switch to crowbars once the Chairman comes out. 

Nightmist: starting with a mistform is amazing. It helps absorb so much damage. Oblivions are just as good, unless the contract is out. I really like using her base power to arrange it so that a heedless lash will play a necklace or stabulet. 

In the later game, an Astral Premonition can help you control the deck  

rook city: hope that you get tremata and Tony out ASAP, and try like hell to survive. There's no good strategy here that I've found. 

The Organization:

so much depends on what comes out when. 

In general, you need: Prison Break to be played ASAP (preferably first turn), then the second one to be played either immediately thereafter, or uselessly. 

It really, really helps if the first thing out is the broker- you can accelerate the deck and try to kill everything except the underbosses right before the shuffle (Jack handle/bloody knuckles/grease monkey, with most form works well for that).  Alternatively, if you have an early oblivion and the crowbars, go hyper aggressive ASAP and try to get the chairman to flip ASAP... Though you still really need those prison breaks to come at the right (for you) time. 

be SHAMELESS about using the rewind. Oblivion fizzles because of revealing dual 1's? Rewind. Did you forget retaliation damage and end up dead? Rewind!  Did a prison break come out and undo everything? Rewind!

in the end, it is all about luck and getting things to align. I'll readily admit ragequitting when the contract was the first out and expat started with no guns. Or just plain restarting again and again until the heroes started with a loadout I thought might work. 

Good luck!  I hope you get it.
