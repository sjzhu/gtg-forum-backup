More lore-based conjecture:

The quotes on the Ennead cards "Rise to Power" and "Sun's Fury" are from Ra: God of the Sun #289, while the quote from Ra's "Solar Flare" is from #290. Ra is not yet Horus of Two Horizons on Solar Flare, so this must be during the "massive battle" mentioned. 

Apart from Anubis' nemesis symbol, the only card linking the Ennead and The Tomb of Anubis might be "Taste of Immortality," which depicts Osiris and a beaten Ra (pre-Horus) inside a tomb-like room of some sort. However, the grail theme of "Taste of Immortality" isn't very Egyptian.  This may have been before Ra encountered the full Ennead.
