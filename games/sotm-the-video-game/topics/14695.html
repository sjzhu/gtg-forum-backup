My default unlock team is Team Leader Tachyon, Dark Visionary, Argent Adept, Grampa Legacy, and whoever you're unlocking for.  Legacy or AA can be substituted for other heroes (Probably Nightmist) if you think that the redirection will be handy, but they'll work fine.

Once you have Ra completely set up (Barring Solar Flare) use Visionary to determine what is on top of the Villain deck, and once it's something that can kill Ra you can play Solar Flare.  Ra should go last so that you can confirm that everything else is set up ahead of time.  Don't be afraid to deal damage to Ra yourself.  Twist the Ether is excellent here, as it will allow Ra to damage himself through Flesh of the Sun God (and help manage the damage so that you deal yourself the right amount).  A judicious Twist the Ether on the highest HP Ennead member can allow Ra to be killed by Sun's Fury or Mass Overheating (as well as boosting the damage to make it easier, and reducing the damage to everyone else in case you want to try winning once Ra is dead)

 

Make sure that the environment is something harmless that is unlikely to kill Ra unexpectedly.

This variant has similar challenges as the DW Fixer one (although I struggled much more with the DW Fixer, partially because of the lesser number of heroes to choose from at the time).

For such unlocks I suggest Dark Visionary as first hero followed by Ra, Argent Adept, Nightmist and a nuker hero (Wraith is pretty good, for its directed damage + utility/control potential).

Get Ra set-up with everything except Solar Flare. Dark Visionary scout and stack Ennead’s deck, keep Ra’s HP somewhere safe but not too high. Keep environment play area as clear as possible.

Set Nightmist up to take the final damage, with amulet ready.

When Dark Visionary sees the right card is ready, Ra damages himself to the point of almost-death, Argent Adept plays Solar Flare for Ra and Nightmist Mistbinds the environment. Final hero to do other things (e.g continue damaging Ra) if required.

Ennead hits Nightmist, redirects to Ra.
