Heroic Infinitor vs. Argent Adept, Captain Cosmic, Sky-Scraper (her image isn't shown on the cover) and Chrono-Ranger in the Time Cataclysm.

 

My goodness, this one was insane!  Argent Adept has an absolutely attrocious deck order (I suggest using an early instrument card to shuffle it) and it will be a brutal slug most of the way.

I got Mint, but only barely.  I had to tank for several turns at the end with lucky draws from Sky-Scraper and even had Constructs in play as extra targets to absorb damage towards the end.  I had to wait for the final card of Infinitor's deck (Whispers of Oblivion) to get the win (as it is the easiest way to take out all the Manifestations in one foul swoop on his starting side).

Cosmic tank the dudes that deal 4 energy damage to the highest HP hero.  Keep it alive at all costs.  Argent Adept made me goof up when Surprise Shopping trip came up (was trying to get Skyscraper to play a one-shot to nuke SST), but refrain from playing cards if you have to because if you keep Cosmic Crest and its buddies out, it makes things super easy.

- Immediately after Fixed Point shows up, Heroic Infinitor plays a one-shot that plinks everything for 1 damage.  This is your best window to win:  If the one-shot resolves without burying all manifestations he will play a few more dudes and you're going to have a bad time.  Therefore, the turn BEFORE is when you gotta get all the manifestations below zero.  If Sky and Chrono are doing their thing you should have enough damage to make that happen.  Where I goofed was there was so many manifestations that I didn't scroll all the way to the right and missed out on a 4 hp manifestation that I could easily have hit.  Also, don't be afraid to buff Heroic Infitor with Syncopated as well to make sure the manifestations go under him.  (I can't remember offhand if it was Throne or Shopping Trip that's your cue that Fixed Point/Whispering Madness(?) is next)

Arcanist Lupus:

Hey, does anybody think that it's possible to win before Infinitor plays his last card?  I've played around a little, but I haven't managed to do it.

It's possible. You have to use Sky-scraper's Neutralizing Resonator in order to do it, though. The bottom of Infinitor's deck is stacked like this (bottom card listed is last card played):

Crazed Artifice
Whispers of Oblivion
Crazed Artifice
Lambent Reaper
Recalescent Hellion
Whispers of Oblivion

What happens is Crazed Artifice reveals Whispers of Oblivion and the next three cards, discarding the Whispers of Oblivion and playing the two Manifestations. In order to get Whispers of Oblivion to be played instead of discarded, you must discard Crazed Artifice, the only means of which is via Neutralizing Resonator on the proper turn. (The turn in question is the round where he just plays Ocular Swarm, fyi).

I managed to win by Infinitor's fourth turn by utilizing Neutralizing Resonator twice, on rounds three (discarding Lambent Reaper) and four (discarding Crazed Artifice). If you were to play Neutralizing Resonator in the first round, then theoretically it's possible to win on Infinitor's third turn, since his card then would be Whispers of Oblivion, but I'm not sure whether that's possible.
