This week is another Ultimate matchup of Ambuscade vs. Luminary, Super Scientific Tachyon, Omnitron-X, Rogue Agent K.N.Y.F.E., and Freedom Five Bunker fighting in the Omnitron-IV facility! As suggested by the title, lots of Devices here from many sources. I recommend fishing for All According to Plan as soon as you can, even though it’ll make thing hurt worse for the heroes. Luminary can probably cycle through his deck a couple times with all the deck manipulation powers here. I got off a Death Ray shot for 24, and had a horde of boosted Devices that could slash and burn right through the 1 DR on Ambuscade’s front side. I think it ended by about the 3rd, maybe 4th round.

Yeah, the trick with Ultimate Ambuscade is that you never really know if one of his turns is going to be “play a gun that deals 2 damage” or if it’s going to be “hit everything on the board for 10-15 damage, play seven devices that will hit you all for another 5-10 damage, heal 10 HP and turn invisible.”

I lost my first round, with two times that it could have changed. I had fifty damage queued up, blew up his invisibility suit, and he promptly played Armed and Dangerous, shuffled his deck, detonated a trap in my face and then played Vanish to go invisible again.

Then I managed to get him visible, hit him for 40 damage (he’d destroyed too much of Luminary’s stuff in the mean time), and then he chained enough damage to take everyone on the team from 6-10 HP down to 0 in one round.
