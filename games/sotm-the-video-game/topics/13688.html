Hey Sentinels Fans!

Jean-Marc Giffin here, programmer and music composer at Handelabra Games!

Not sure if you saw the post yet, but we're celebrating our 1-year anniversary on October 16-18, and part of that will be featuring YOU as arrangers and performers of the Sentinels of the Multiverse Theme Song!

I've made sheet music, which anyone can download, and you can do whatever you'd like with the music! You can record yourself playing it as-is for the instrument of your choice, or make an entirely new style of it and record that. Whatever you'd like!

You can get a PDF file here, and the Finale MUS file here.

If you're interested, feel free to post in this thread, and then submit your music or video file when it's ready. We'll be sure to highlight and feature the submitters, providing your name and links to your other work (if you have some you'd like to share!)

Hope to hear from you!
