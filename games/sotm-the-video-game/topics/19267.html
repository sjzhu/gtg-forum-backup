Fine after two rough games. 

Focusing down the gladiators seem to be the way to go for me. Lifeline getting the Seeker first turn helps with that, and it slows down the favor gain for each that you drop. I used Comodora to damage boost Fanatic and discard the one gladiator that redirects damage, which helped. Side effect from that is Fixed Point will then save your ongoings and equipment. Another fixed point is you can use concealed assailant without destroying the cloak and without discarding for comodora. Ignore the Pangolin, as he gets chaos lord and will play cards. 

If you can survive to round 3, you get a Fickle Fans, which brought it from 19-5 to 14-10 for me. If you can keep surviving to round 4, you get another Fickle Fans, which brought it back to 12-15 I think. From there, I flipped Kaargra and wailed on gladiators enough for 5 more points and won, with very low hp all around.

I did a few similar practice games to get the hang of the idea, which helped. Won the game and got a Mint.

It is not as bad as it looks, even when it looks like you are losing and hopeless!

Fickle Fans really saves you: I got 10 tokens to my hero favor pool from Fickle Fans. Lifeline cann play Leyline Shift and sometimes get lucky and get Fickle Fans.

Pay a lot of attention to which TItles are currently available and try to get those fast. Make sure that Lifeline doesn't get Seeker because that Title is disastrous for him. When Kaargra is out, you have to focus fire on her (or focus fire on Provocator Tarnis first if he is in your way.) 

Good luck folks! This one can be done!
