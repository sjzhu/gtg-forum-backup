I’m really not seeing how this one is done.

I’ve lost two games now, and came here looking for insight or tricks. No clues in this thread, unfortunately. The obvious order seems to be Freight Train (because he dishes out damage and is vulnerable), Ermine (because she’s a pain), and then La Capitan. In my games I can manage the first 2, but La Capitan is never in danger from this team afterward.

This is a remarkably weak team, particularly with La Capitan on the opposing team because everyone, and I mean everyone on the hero team wants to discard cards. Guise’s signature Blatant Reference combo has no effect against her since he has to discard a card every turn he uses it.

I’ve come to really like Guise, but there’s just nothing here for him. No ongoings to copy, no equipment to use, and very few innate powers he wants to copy. He never wants to copy Scholar’s self-harm power, and Sky Scrapper’s normal size power is of no use to him, since he can’t play links or draw links from his trash. Her Tiny power is good, but I never seem to draw any cards that let me switch to Tiny. Her Giant power damages the heroes too much unless she’s got Thorian Monolith up, and she usually doesn’t.

I’m not seeing how Guise does a lot of damage from one-shots here. Except to Freight Train, of course, since he takes extra damage from everything.

EDIT: Game 3 went worse than games 1 and 2. I didn’t even really hurt Ermine before everyone went down.

EDIT: Won game 4. Barely. Guise had 2 HP, everyone else was down. Temporal Hotspot killed both Scholar and Sky Scrapper, and I had no way of removing it. The only reason I won was I put Total Beefcake up on Guise’s last turn, and re-directed La Capitan’s attack to herself.

I wish I could provide some strategy, but I feel that I did exactly what I did in games 1-3. Obviously I did little variants or I’d have gotten the same result each time, but I wasn’t employing different tactics, just varying slightly the order of my plays. Mostly it’s an approach that got me killed, but squeaked by in game 4.
