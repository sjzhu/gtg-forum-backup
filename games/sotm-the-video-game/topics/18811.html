The Freedom Fighters got knocked down to 2 hp. On the environment turn, they let lose with their energy rifles and Benchmark’s Countermeasrues activate and destroy them. I was expecting that Rebel Headquarters would have stopped this from happening.

The card says “would be dealt damage” not “takes damage.” It triggers when the attack starts, and doesn’t care how much damage would be dealt (including 0). To prevent destroying the freedom fighters, Countermeasures would have to say “when Benchmark is dealt damage by a target with 2 or fewer HP, prevent that damage and destroy that target.”

I had the same thing happen in that one-shot, incidentally.
