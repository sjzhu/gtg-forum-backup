In the video game, it can become tiresome to repeat the same choice over and over. I'm thinking in particular of situations like putting Twist The Ether on the Matriarch and then having to choose for 15 Fowl cards that you want to set the damage to Fire and reduce it.

 

Perhaps it would be useful to have a button pop up the first time a choice is made for some things in a turn allowing one to temporarily tell the game that you want to make that same choice for the rest of the turn.
