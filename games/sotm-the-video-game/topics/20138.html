Mint with everyone near 20 hp, though the danger here is of course the discarded cards, not hp loss.

I don't really have any advice beyond "Do damage" really. First round I beat down the MDP, with Bunker playing his grenade to keep the environment from playing. After that, I tried to get both Wraith and Tachyon ready for big damage turns while wailing away as much as possible on Blade. Legacy just played damage cards and Galvanized. Bunker did what he could with a Flak cannon. Wraith did drop a MDP to the bottom of the deck which helped. I flipped him the turn he had 16 cards in discard (after he discarded 3 at the end of his turn), and Wraith did Razor/Stun bolts for 5+3 and tachyon did 2x Lightspeed for 11 each to drop him.

Of course, if he plays the Blade Battalions, don't kill them as they just add to his trash.

Very hard one, but I got it with a near mint.

My spoiler free hint is to play adhesive foam grenade turn one. Other than that it's your classic Barron fight where you ignore your own HP in favor of dealing as much damage as possible. There may be an RNG component when baron flips which can change the outcome of the battle though. I was able to beat him on turn 4 and made him flip on turn 3. If you adhesive foam grenade and use all of your "party draw card" abilities you will end up with wraith getting a free sun bolt from the enviroment on turn 2 which allows her to have her utility belt, razor ordinance, microtargeting computer, and stun bolt out on turn 3 so she outputs 10 damage a turn thanks to galvanize. In the end tachyon got 12 burst cards in the trash + two hypersonic assaults in hand.

Looks like the trick here (other than Adhesive on turn 1) is that Tachyon should always use her Quick Insights and discard with Rapid Recon even when you normally wouldn't do that, and should be Pushing the Limits for almost the entire fight. You can't get the cards you need unless you dig really deep into Tachyon's deck, which requires everything you have.  If you use a Vortex on the MDP in round 1 instead of Quick Insight, you won't be set up in round 3 to get Wraith's stun bolts and get positioned for a multi-Barrage turn, and then you're screwed.
