The community has discovered the official unlock condition for Heroic Luminary.

First, defeat Baron Blade in the Realm of Discord without Luminary on the team. Then, win a game in Freedom Tower with the Freedom Five (any variants). Then, in a game in Megalopolis with Luminary on the team, use the power on each Doomsday Device while at least 15 cards are in Luminary's trash.

Possibly the fastest way to charge up a doomdsay device: Spite's Compound Xi (the tentacle one) makes Luminary discard 5 cards per power use.  With a well chosen team, you can have your weapons fully armed every turn or two, to the point that the difficulty is more often getting the doomsday weapons in play before your trash reshuffles.  (I recommend adding in Argent Adept, who can give Luminary extra power uses to speed-charge him, and give him card draws or plays to search for the various cards that will let him fish his doomsday devices out of the trash. Or you can do that with Verdant Sonata, in a pinch, but that's riskier.)
