It’s Trickster Kismet vs. TermiNation Unity, F6 Wraith, SADmark, Visionary Unleashed, and DW Harpy in Insula Primalis. Random start, as the name suggests, so good luck to all and share your experience if you wish!

It wasn’t too bad for me, got Mint on round 6 I believe. Benchmark was lowest at 6 HP after being beaten on by Kismet’s front side form until I decided to flip her. Others were at 10 HP or higher. Environment just did a couple plants and lava rivers, never any targets. Pretty much directed everything at Kismet the whole time. Wraith eventually got two Stun Bolts, Visionary destroyed Jinx cards, and Unity fished for Stealth Bot to keep all the heroes safe. A well-timed Precognition let me bury Lady Luck, probably my most hated card in Kismet’s deck. She only played a couple Lucky cards, so didn’t have to deal with that much healing. Fun game overall, that team worked rather well together.

That went very badly, but not so badly that I lost.

My opening hands were pretty garbage; no Hardware for Benchmark, all damage cards for Visionary, a hand full of unplayable golems for Unity, and a so-so hand for the Wraith. As a result, I got blown up pretty consistently; Visionary managed to get control of the Talisman and then hop into a psychic cocoon to draw a lot of Kismet’s fire, but Benchmark went down early.

That actually helped, because once he was dead he could start popping Jinxes. I stumbled along for a bit longer, and then Wraith died and things got better, because she could keep tossing Jinxes back to the top of Kismet’s deck, where they would be played, and then Benchmark would kill them.
