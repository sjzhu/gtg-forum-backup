Grand Warlord Voss crosses dimensional boundaries to invade Freedom City. It’s up to Daedalus, Siren, Captain Thunder, Dr. Metropolis and Star Knight to repel the Torathian invasion.

image828×1792 138 KB

And repel it they did. Siren and Star Knight ran the show here with the others providing damage and clean up. Siren developed a storm that involved enough weather effects to fully flummox the low level minions and provide support for the rest of the party. Star Knight’s gear provides a lot of damage opportunities that when boosted start to chunk out Voss’s health quickly. In the end, a Ravenous Whirlpool swallowed Voss and his army beneath the waves, never to be seen again.
