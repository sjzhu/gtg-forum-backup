Iron Legacy just wrecks everyone - whenever you fight him, the game will be short. It's just a matter of who dishes out enough damage first. Fanatic can be very useful if you get the right cards in time(first-turn End of Days, then Wrathful Retribution a couple of rounds later when you're nearly dead).

Kismet I find can be tricky if she gets too many Jinxes out. Lady Luck and Inconceivable Obstruction are both pains in the arse, especially if they both come out at once. The latter is much worse in a high-target environment (and no trouble at all in the Freedom Tower ;)).

La Capitan is another one I find tends to be a bit of a pain, especially if she starts getting too many crew cards out (this can happen fairly quickly).

The Dreamer is similar to Miss Information in that she's easier with a smaller group of heroes. I think she's probably the villain I find most fun to fight from this expansion. And one of the more unusual out of all the villains because technically, she isn't one.
