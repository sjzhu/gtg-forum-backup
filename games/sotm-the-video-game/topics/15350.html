This is on our list to do in the future, but there are some ways to do it within the existing system that don't take many (or any) more clicks than a "locking" feature would.

You want to take on Voss in Megalopolis, but wanted a randomized team: Choose Voss/Megalopolis then press randomize, and the heroes will be filled out. Or if you want to choose from various teams: Press randomize as many times as you want, then replace the villain & environment.

You have the Prime Wardens set up to go, but want to roll the dice on the environment and villain: Do that with no environment and villain set up, and press randomize, and they will be filled out. Want another combo? Remove the environment and villain and press randomize again.

You want to randomize two or three heroes in multiplayer. Select any two or three heroes and press randomize, and you will get the same number of heroes randomized.
