Very rare case of a Spite game that was almost fun, though it still suffers from the “ignore all victims completely” problem. Normally I don’t even try to punch through Spite’s healing, but with him having only one Drug for a long time, and playing some extremely powerful and aggressive heroes, I managed to outrace him. He didn’t start to bounce back until getting a big Drug Raid which let him heal for like 20, but the beatdown continued even after Powers started to fuel his lifetapping. I would have won before he could flip, except that Skyscraper picked the wrong card for her deathblow… Catch A Ride and probably several other cards would have done it, but instead I picked a Compulsion Canister, not realizing that Rebounding Debilitator would prevent it. And then I was stuck in Huge without any Environment card to throw at him, so he survived an unhelpful Endling play, flipped, and did a fistful of extra damage before being utterly pasted by Idealist. Thus did I miss my chance to see whether Spite has a different loss screen if you beat him pre-flip, the way Kaargra and Dreamer and Baron Blade all have unique pictures depending on how they defeat you.
