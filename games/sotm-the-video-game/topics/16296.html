Hello. Sorry for Necro-ing a dead thread but I discovered something about unlocking Termi-Nation Unity that I thought worth sharing. I’ve already posted this on the Steam forums but it makes sense to post it here, too.

I unlocked Termi-Nation Unity without doing what the official unlock posted to this thread (and other guides I’ve found online) tell you to do. I did something similar but not quite the same and it worked.

I didn’t mean to do it, I was trying to do what the guide said.

Let me explain.

I read the guide and saw that I needed to keep 5(H) Hero Targets in play for an entire round. I didn’t read any advice on how to do it; I enjoy seeing what I’ve got to do and then figuring out the How by myself. So the conclusion I reached was to use TLTachyon in the first slot, then Unity then Luminary. Just the three heroes, I planned to use Luminary’s Doomsday Device to get back loads of his devices from his trash all at the same time. I figured 15 would be easier with Unity and Luminary than making it 20 with any other Hero also in the team.

So the game proceeds.
And I reach the point where I manage to get 15 targets in play at the end of the round. Great, now to survive the next round …
Bad luck! Omnitron just did 5 damage to all Hero Targets, all Luminary’s devices die. Never mind, I still have his Doomsday Device and can get them back and try next round. Which is precisely what I do in Luminary’s turn.
At the end of the Environment Turn … I unlock Termi-Nation Unity.

So just to be clear …
I did NOT have 5(H) Hero Targets in play for an entire round.
But I DID have 5(H) Hero Targets in play at the start of the round and 5(H) Hero Targets in play at the end of the round, too.

And that was enough to get her.
