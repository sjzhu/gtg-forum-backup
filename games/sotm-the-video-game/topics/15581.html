There are other methods that could damage the heroes too.  Impossible Quandary+Losing to the Odds is also a possibility.

One other thing to note is that Wagelings needs to be played before Losing to the Odds and The New Deal can't be between them unless there is a second Wageling after it.

Probably your best chance of getting the insta-win is to use 4-Heroes with odd starting HP, as that makes Impossible Quandary safe, and then you are just waiting for the correct line-up of Wageling and Losing to the Odds.
