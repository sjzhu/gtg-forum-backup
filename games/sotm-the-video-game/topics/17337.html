Absolute Zero, Freedom Six Unity, Dark Watch Mr. Fixer, and Santa Guise vs. Gloomweaver (on Ultimate) in the Realm of Discord. Randomized start. 

I had a relatively easy time with this one, final HP 10/19/23/20, and that's mostly because AZ did one of his patented 15-self-damage Thermal Shockwaves.

Gloomy opened with the Grimoire and got the Pouch of Bones out in round 3, but otherwise, he whiffed on a couple of Vast Followings and played out a couple pins and Profane Zealots before he went down.

AZ was fully set up with Apertures, both modules, Thermal Shockwave, and both Impales in short order thanks to presents from Guise and an early Time Flies. He did the lion's share of the damage.

Unity had very poor draws and was paralyzed by the Grimoire, though an early Stealth Bot did serve us well and saved us from 1 discard per round. By the time Fixer pulled out Salvage Yard to help Unity get her hand together, Gloomy was already mostly dead.

Fixer opened Jack Handle / Grease Monkey Fist and ended up with three Bloody Knuckles by the end of round 3, so he played crowd control while AZ focused on Gloomy.

Guise passed out presents quickly in the first two rounds thanks to Hasty Augmentation and I Can Do That Too! AZ's presents were the only really useful stuff, though. He got to copy Glacial Structure and draw a bunch of extra cards, but it didn't amount to much.
