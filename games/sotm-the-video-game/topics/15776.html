I've found that the difficulty on this one depends largely on how you choose to employ Visionary.

SPOILERS below for the fight...

 

OK!

so, Visionary starts with two prophetic visions. And Atlantis is exactly the sort of place that likes to screw with you. I've found that using one of those visions ASAP saves you from a world of hurt by letting you get the pillars of Hercules out of the way and giving you something you can work with... like a kraken. Sure, it will go after you that first time, but once the drones start appearing, it's nothing but helpful. Even more so once you wrest the mind. 

Heres the thing though. You are GOING to get sedative flechetted at some point. Probably twice. You've got a brief window between them, and that's when the Kraken can really be used to your advantage. After both flechettes, there are probably enough drones that you can just let the Kraken do it's own thing. 

In terms of powers, you want to get yourself a twist the Ether ASAP. Save it for after both flechettes, because it is going to be the key to a LOT of damage. See... it's tempting to put it on Omnitron, but it can do a LOT more damage if you put it on Ra. Since he does nothing but fire, twisting him allows for him to play a lot more damage dealing one shots and still use his powers. Focus fire (or whatever you twist it to) on Omnitron, and he'll start to melt fast. After that, I like to use my base power on Ra or legacy, depending on what they have at hand.

Once that's done, you can start using Visionary to screw with Omnitron's deck.  You start out with a suggestion, and once Ra is twisted, feel free to use it to keep Omnitron from playing anything useful, at least for a turn. I like giving Omnitron back the disintegration beams, since you are likely doing more than 7 damage a round (honestly, once twisted, staffed, and inspired, Ra tends to do that much in a single turn).

By now, someone has likely lost their hand to rid the party of Toxic Seaweed, so help them stay on the offensive with your power while continuing to screw with Omnitron by wresting the Kraken (if you haven't already), or joining the damage train.

heres a few tips for the others:

Fanatic is solid.  If you can finagle it so that Zealous offense is out, go you. It's tricky, what with the singularities, but useful. Mostly she just does a marvelous job tanking...and once the phosphorescent chambers come out, her healing gets much nicer.  Holy novas are great with damage and healing boosts!  Be really careful with divine sacrifices. If you know Legacy is going to do an intervention, go for it... but it can still backfire.

Legacy is amazing, as always. Get that inspiring presence out ASAP... and don't worry about being hit with flechettes and losing it. You've got a Visionary to help look for another one. Since your one shots do melee, and your base power energy, feel free to go on the offense and pummel the big robot without worrying that you are going to be stymied by the adaptive subroutine.

Ra is your heavy damage dealer. Get a staff, burn things. If your staff gets busted, get a new one (or hope the environment is kind enough to play one for you when the font of power comes out).  Once twisted, I found that the Living Conflagrations could be used like one shots due to the fact that they do damage on being played.   Then, of course you can use them to scout for even nastier one shots like flame spikes and fire blasts. 

Go all out out on Omnitron. The drones are a nuisance, but only take them out if you have an AoE attack, or if you absolutely need to.  Just keep hitting that big robot, and you'll be fine.  

Hope this helps!

Braithwhite:

 You are GOING to get sedative flechetted at some point. 

 

Fun fact:  It's actually possible to win this without seeing the Sedative Fletchettes even once!

 

Doing so (at least the path I used, there might be others) pretty much requires exact knowledge of the villain and environment decks and several non-intuitive tactics, but it can be done.

 

Details below in white

Round 1:  Visionary plays Prophetic Visions and puts Pillars of Hercules (yes, really) on top.  Visonary passes her power draw to Legacy.  Legacy plays Inspiring Presence.  Do not deal enough damage to destroy the components.  You really don't want Cosmitron chaining drones with Pillars of Hercules out.

Round 2:  Leave Pillars out.  They'll play a few drones, but it won't be too bad.  Don't play the second Prophetic Visions.  Pass your power draw to Legacy again.  Legacy will play Bolster Allies and draw Take Down, which she will then play.  You can destroy all of Cosmitron's components now - there won't be any drone chaining ever again.  Wail on the evil robot as much as you can.

Round 3: Play Prophetic Visions to dodge the Seaweed and the Leaking Room.  Use Suggestion to put a component on top of Cosmitron's deck.  Start blowing up the Adaptive Platings.  Legacy should skip her turn to destroy Pillars of Hercules.  This will preserve Take Down for another round.  Continue wailing on Cosmitron.

Round 4: More wailing.

Round 5: Kill Cosmitron.
