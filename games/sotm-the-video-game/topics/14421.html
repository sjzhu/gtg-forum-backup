Used Ultimate Target on GloomWeaver, despite not actually intending to attack GloomWeaver, in order to get the extra power users throughout the game. Put By Any Means on the relics to focus fire one each one in turn.

http://oi67.tinypic.com/i78weq.jpg

 

Basically, use Chrono-Ranger and Wraith's "Search for Equipment" cards to grab a Neuro-Toxin Dart Thrower and two Stun Bolts. Throw out the damage reduction items like Plating, Recharge Mode, Fortitude and Ta' Moko.
