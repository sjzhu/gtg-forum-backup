Yes, but that text doesn't have to exist anymore. That text triggers the damage action. The damage action is currently happening, not the EOT effect. The EOT effect is "over" when the damage starts. If there was something that were to happen after the damage is done, in the same EOT block, that secondary effect shouldn't happen, but that's not represented here.

[End of Turn:Start Damage action] => (Damage:Do some damage)
