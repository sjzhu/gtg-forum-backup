Yeah this fight is not fun at all.

Other then the Crowbar thing. Cards to keep an eye out for include speed loading and a ammo as that allows you to keep Mr. Fixer Stocked. SMG or Assault Rifle helps Expat keep the Thugs down so does Mr. Fixer and Jack Handle. Aim and Hairtrigger Reflexes should keep the Thiefs and Informants under Control.

 

Looking up and Suprising Fortune provides a useful engine to get tokens and restore health. Also with the Dual Crowbars and Friendly Fire. A Silver Lining will help undo some of the damage Setback took killing the operative.

 

Nightmist has tons of utility but requires some consideration for when you use her abilities. She has some pretty useful spells and the Amulet can be used to either double up on Crowbar Damage by bouncing an attack of her. Or you could use to make the gun man finish himself off if you shot him on his way in with HairTrigger and Aim.

 

If you check the Ridiculous Challenge one shot thread you might be able to get some additional suggestions.

I got really lucky and finally managed to pull this off today, so here's my log for whatever it helps: http://pastebin.com/VfZwb8bE

Basically:

I got ridiculously lucky and started off with Friendly Fire as a Risk flop, two sets of Double Crowbars, and Amulet of the Elder Gods. I also got Tome of Magic and Jack Handle quickly too, and the environment served up allies soon as well.

So I offed the Operative and poor Setback, Setback mostly milled the villain deck, Expatriette fed Fixer guns (the only really unlucky aspect was that Expatriette was often a bit starved for guns to sacrifice), used Fixer to get rid of mooks, and Nightmist pulled cards out of decks and sometimes helped out a bit in killing things.

I eventually ended up with everyone incapped except Nightmist and the Chairman worn down a bit to 22HP. Setback swapped between milling cards and letting Nightmist draw spells from the Tome, Expatriette was just Tome-drawing, Fixer swapped between killing environment cards and even more Tome drawing. Nightmist mostly used the Amulet to redirect damage to the Chairman, occasionally stopping to feed herself cards with Call Forth or Enlightenment or take out mooks with Oblivion or Scouring Mists when they got too numerous. An off-turn supercharged Mist-Fueled Recovery also helped.

Also I cheated a lot with undos. Mostly in the form of: Setback didn't mill a Prison Break? Undo.

But Mr. Adorable is now mine!
