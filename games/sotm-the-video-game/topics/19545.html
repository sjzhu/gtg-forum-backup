Inspired by Lodo the Bear's collection on reddit, I set out to form my own.

  • 67 unique collectible heroes (including yours, truly! Twice! A Christmas Miracle!)
  • 39 Guise cards, except 1 Lemme See That (including copies of what's only my Best Card Ever!)
  • 39 Idealist cards, except 1 card destroyed by Form the Head (the imagination of some kid!)
  • 23 constructs (the imagination of some bearded guy!)
  • 14 mechanical golems (Such adorable Mr. Chomps!)
  • 12 links (More toys!)

Total: 194 cards

Forming the Head: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1911310883 (I'm #1! That's what the token means, right?)

Completionist Guise has 10hp while adding himself to his own Impressive Collection. I'm assuming this is because base Guise previously had 10hp from Representative of Earth: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1911311521

The collection: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1911311849 (so bitey!)

 

The real fun was when I gathered up the last few cards (Energy Bracers, then bots, the Retcons, and finally the Focusing Tiara and Bright Idea), because the team suddenly stopped being able to do anything other than Guise vaporizing 1 target with Form the Head.

CC deals 1 target 1 damage. Unity gives a Hasty Augmentation so that Guise can reset the Idealist and deal 4 damage. Idealist looks around in confusion. Skyscraper puts up Monolith and uses Mainstay's Block with Character Witness.
