I don’t really see much of a point playing with closed hands.  In my experience, people end up just telling everyone else what they have practically all the time. Oh I don't tell people what to play. If someone is puzzling over their options I'll instead point at whatever the villain/environment decks currently have out and say stuff like "Well, this thing is a pretty major threat right now - is there anything you can do to help with that?". Also, if I'm teaching and someone asks me about a specific card, it kind of helps if I can see it, in case I need to double-check the wording or something (many of the older decks I've got memorised pretty well by now, but by no means every deck in the game ;)). Also, if people can see my hand, it better helps me explain whatever I'm planning for my next turn, or whatever, should I need to go into any more detail than "I'm gonna deal with X on my turn so don't worry about it", or "Don't worry about not being able to do that thing along with the other thing because I can let you do that when we get to my turn", etc.

I dunno, it just feels...wrong (if that's the right word) for people to be keeping their resources from each other when the whole point of the game is that we're working together and need to know what each other is capable of.
