Akash'Bhuta threatens the Maerynian Refuge, but PW Tempest, Unity, Hunted Naturalist, and XPW Captain Cosmic come to the defense.

Despite what felt like a slow setup, things went fairly easy for a Mint.  There are some fun interactions here, such as:

* Naturalist with Natural Form's Power (x2), plus Augmented Ally (x2) means lots of power uses and card draws, especially with Primal Charge.  And then he can feed Unity some cards (because it seemed like she took forever to get much going.

* Captan Cosmic, with a lot of constructs in play, using "Play With Madness" -- plus Naturalist's "Predator's Eye" -- from Unity's "Hasty Augmentation" -- wooh, that's a lot of damage.

Just make sure to get a Bee Bot out in case an Entomb drops...
