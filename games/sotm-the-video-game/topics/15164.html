Well, here's what I did in what became my Near Mint:

Round 1

Voss: Ion-Lancer targets Wraith, Shock Infantry targets Bunker

Tempest: Chain Lightning (4 -> TCF Conqueror, 3 -> Frosthound, 2 -> Ion-Lancer), skip power

Wraith: Razor Ordnance Psi-Weaver

Tachyon: Nimble Strike

Bunker: External Combustion, skip power

Absolute Zero: Isothermic Transducer, Elemental Wrath Infantry

Round 2

Voss: Both of Zero's reactions on Targeting Innocents

Tempest: Shielding Winds, Sacrifice

Wraith: Smoke Bombs, Razor Ordnance TCF Conqueror

Tachyon: Fleet of Foot, HUD Goggles, Research Grant, Team Leader

Bunker: Adhesive Foam Grenade, skip power

Absolute Zero: Coolant Blast Targeting Innocents

Megalopolis: Target Absolute Zero first, reaction damage on Targeting Innocents

Round 3

Voss: Destroy Research Grant, Conqueror hits Tempest first, Zero second, reaction damage on TCF Conqueror, Shock Infantry targets Wraith, Soldier targets Tempest

Tempest: Cleansing Downpour, Cleansing Downpour

Wraith: Micro-Targeting Computer, Razor Ordnance Infantry

Tachyon: Quick Insight, discard Accelerated Assault & Sucker Punch, Fleet of Foot, Nimble Strike

Bunker: Recharge Mode

Absolute Zero: Onboard Module Installation x3 (Null-Point, Isothermic, Null-Point), Focused Apertures, Coolant Blast TCF Conqueror

With Conqueror and Targeting Innocents gone and Cleansing Downpour out, you can steadily regain HP while getting your team setup. Don't flip Voss until you're ready to take him out in a round or two; double card plays sucks tremendously if you're not ready for it.

With the help of Dolphin's excellent opener, and the general advice of the others here, I finally managed to beat it. 

Once the ship was down and people were a bit more set up, the fight felt a LOT more equal. I focused on healing with Tempest, bolstered by one-shots for direct damage and some control.

Wraith scouted for forced deployments and kept surprises to a minimum (and did damage where surprise was unavoidable). 

Tach kept everyone in cards, destroyed pesky monorails, hypersoniced, and generally was a jack of all trades.

Bunker tanked with Smoke Bombs and Plating, doing a lot of grenade launching and combusting when a Forced Deployment brought out minions. 

AZ did a lot of Isothermic retribution in the early game, and in the mid to late game did a lot of cleaning up stragglers and focusing on Voss.

I wished I'd been a bit more careful about SubZero atmosphere though- there were a couple of turns where a Voss's end (start of turn) card play brought out a deployment, which then popped and brought out all minions, who then all went.  Given that he had only a few cards left and I'd been relentlessly putting the Deployments at the bottom of the deck, it shouldn't have been such a surprise!  Still, we weathered the onslaught and managed to take them all out... and prepare better for the next time. 

I'm glad I finally beat it. I've got some new respect for Advanced Voss... and a bit more for F6 Tempest as well.
