Ultimate Cosmic Omnitron on Mars, with Horus Ra, Prime Wardens Haka, Team Leader Tachyon, and Freedom Six Wraith.

Cool, minty cheese. I thought I was toast when Ra went down on turn 2–that opening salvo from Cosmitron is a doozy, and forgetting the Interpolation Beams didn’t help. But I did manage to flip him on turn 1 thanks to a Fire Blast and another trick or two, and once everyone was incapacitated but Tachyon, I managed to assemble a steady-state condition–behind a Meteor Swarm, using the Wraith to put Adaptive Subroutine on top of the villain deck every turn, building up Tachyon’s damage thanks to Haka. When I had enough boosted, not to mention all 19 Burst cards in her trash, Ra dropped the Swarm and a huge set of damage dealings took out Cosmitron.

Not how I’d recommend the battle, it was pretty boring and, as I said, all cheese. But it did work.

Looks like my game was the same as everyone else's. First game I tried to lock down Omnitron with Ground Pound and Self-Destruct Sequence, but I turtled too long and it was only a matter of time before Sedative Flechettes came out and the drones killed everyone.

Second game, I realized that incap abilities were needed to lock down Omnitron, and Ra got to 1 all on his lonesome, but I just have a hard time offing my own teammates so I let him live and even healed him to 7 using 2xStaff of Ra. And then Omnitron took out everyone but Tachyon who had 3 hp left. Ra took Adaptive Subroutine from the trash and put it on top of the deck and Wraith kept it there for the rest of the game. I buried Fire in the Bio-Sphere under Self Destruct Sequence and had Haka and Ra skip a few turns to ensure it stayed buried. Tachyon used Blinding Speed to destroy some of the other environment cards under Self Destruct Sequence to make sure these damage dealing cards were put right at the bottom of the Self Destruct Sequence pile. I threw Pervasive Red Dust under there as well so I could keep Tachyon's HUD goggles out. Ra skipped his turns for the rest of the game to keep the Self Destruct Sequence pile constant, which kept the environment in check.

With all the card draws from Haka's incap ability, Research Grant and Fleet of Foot, Tachyon easily kept a supply of Hypersonic Assaults and Synaptic Interruptions on the board. With the villain and environment decks kept in check, there was nothing left but to beat on the oversized trash compactor until the end.

Near Mint.
