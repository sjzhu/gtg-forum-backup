Oh, another thing I wanted to mention: Tony Taurus came out early (round 1 maybe) from the environment deck, and I didn’t notice right away that he wasn’t taking damage. I suspect that’s because the card says the strongest villain does damage to him, and I guess gladiators don’t count as villains. That was a nice surprise and something I’ve never seen happen before. I don’t know how many hundreds of games I’ve played, but it is nice that I keep getting surprised by interactions I didn’t expect.
