Just an FYI: I was trying to unlock Road Warrior Mainstay this afternoon while fighting in the "Freedom Tower" environment deck and ran into something odd.

VG Mainstay got his three equipment cards out for the requisite number of rounds (moreso since I am paranoid), Dropped the Hammer to destroy his three equipment cards and... nothing.  So I rewound, looked at things, and realized the enviroment card "Caspit's 'Playground'" was in play, which makes hero ongoing cards into equipment cards.  So I kept playing and in the next round had Tachyon destroy "Caspit's 'Playground'" and then had VG Mainstay Drop the Hammer again and now destroy his three "true" equipment cards and BAM!, variant card unlocked.

While "Caspit's 'Playground'" was in play I thought about just destroying all of VG Mainstay's ongoing cards as well, but thought the path I took was 'safer' and closer to the stated unlock condition, so that's what I did.

Just wanted to mention this in case others take this path and see the same thing.
