Just encountered a bug with Shocking Animation where the animated equipment remained a target after shocking animation was gone. 

Chokepoint vs. F6 Wraith, GI Bunker, and Scholar of the Infinite at the Tomb. Shocking animation was in play on bunker's grenade launcher. I made the grenade launcher destroy itself on Bunker's end of turn phase, sending it face down to Chokepoint's play area. Choke flipped and I had her destroy the grenade launcher from her face down cards, sending 8th to Bunker's discard pile. It was the only one of Bunker's cards she had, so I know it was that launcher.

On Bunker's next turn, I fished the grenade launcher out with a Decommissioned Hardware,  and when it hit play, it was a 5 hp target!

On a later turn it was destroyed (from damage!) and I got it back again with another decommissioned Hardware, and it was still a 5 hp target.

Android version, if it matters.
