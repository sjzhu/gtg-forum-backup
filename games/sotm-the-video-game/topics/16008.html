My understanding is that the Sentinels count as "one hero" for the purposes of determining H at the start of the game, but otherwise, the four characters themselves are indeed "heroes" in the gameplay sense.

So, any effect that damages "all heroes" would indeed hit all four Sentinels. The difference between that and "all hero targets" is that the latter also includes cards such as Unity's golems and Visionary's Decoy Projection.
