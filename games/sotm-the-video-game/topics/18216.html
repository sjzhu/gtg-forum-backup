Near mint.

First game I went after Bugbear first, and he kept healing about as fast as I was doing damage. By the time I took him down, everyone was down to single digits, and the game didn’t last much longer after that.

Second game, I decided to copy QuirkyDM, and went after Miss Information first. It worked for him, so maybe it would work for me. Really, though, what made the difference was putting Twist the Aether on Bugbear, and then Flesh of the Sun God on everyone with Ra. If Bugbear can’t do damage, he doesn’t heal, and there’s enough damage here to take him down. KNYFE in particular was doing more than anyone else.

Target order thus was Miss Information -> Bugbear -> The Operative -> La Capitan. Taking out the monkey permanently with the Sky Deck neutralizes much of La Capitan’s threat. I think it’s probably smarter to go The Operative -> Bug Bear -> Miss Information -> La Capitan, since The Operative is really the 2nd biggest damage dealer here. The main thing is not bothering with Bugbear until you either turn off his healing or at least you tone down his damage (and thus healing) somehow.
