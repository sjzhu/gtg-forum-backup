In this utterly bizarre one-shot, Wraith, KNYFE, Sky-Scraper, and Setback go to the Block to batle Kismet in a randomized start.

What makes this so bizarre?  That it has been up for nearly a week, and nobody has started a thread about it!  I wonder why?  Maybe because people don't like randomized starts?  Maybe people just don't like Kismet so nobody has played it?  Maybe a glitch with the website?  Maybe everybody's just busy this week?  Who knows?

As is the case with any randomized start, your mileage will vary.  I got a mint at 19/19/21/12, then played again to win with 11/24/25/20.  If you can get an Infrared Eyepiece, you can try to avoid some of Kismet's more annoying cards, like Lady Luck and Inconceivable Obstruction; and if IR reveals two annoying cards, a Wrecking Uppercut or Neutralizing Resonator can help.
