Whew got the mint!

Lucky start with Inspiring Presence, Electrical Storm, Submachine Gun so lots of area damage right away.  Too afraid to get Unitys Bots out without any Stealth protection.  Got Amulet right away, but lost all equipment to Rejuvenating Entropy and didn't get another one til way later.  Luckily, had a second Submachine Gun to replace the first and got Hairtrigger Reflexes.  Tempest eventually gets Hailstorm. So Expat and Tempest were doing great area damage on their own and got her to about 118 pretty quickly.  

Unlucky break: double Primeval Eruption so there were 3 Brambles, 2 Rockslides, 2 Phalanges and 2 Carapaces out.  Brambles always seemed to target Nightmist.  Kinda lucky actually; without the Necklace, could hunt for cards with Enlightment or Investigation without hurting herself.  Cleared the Rockslides and eventually the other limbs the following round.

Another unlucky break: Earth's Sacrifice takes out Inspring Presence, Hairtrigger Reflexes, Electrical Storm, Hailstorm. But Akash is down to around 60 HP.  I start playing Tempest defensively and do Cleansing Downpour.  But for the others I go for broke with all the Thokks, Backfisted Strikes, Shotgun, Chain Lightning.  I finally get 2 Raptors but they die to New Jersey Devil right after. Platform and Cryo do come out and help a little and a few Brainstorms.

Final blow: Shotgun kills Rockslide ->kills Akash.

She flipped once in the middle of the game and then one more time just before she died.

Final Wasteland did not appear til late and no cards were lost to the Wasteland.

With Cleansing Downpour, everyone was at approx 10 HP.

I have mixed feelings about Random Start.  I did play duplicate bridge in my day, so I actually like everyone having the same initial conditions.  I feel there is a better common talking point and also a little more meaningful to me if someone else gets a mint, but I lose: since it was the same seed, I'd more feel like I could've made better choices vs. blame the loss on bad card shuffle and draw.

However, with enough strong familiarly with all decks, I believe good players can also play more robustly than others, i.e. try to anticipate certain bad case scenarios and make choices to accomodate; be flexible and adapt, etc.  Hence, Random Start can still be a good way to measure players.  Although extreme bad luck will still feel disappointing.

Overall, enjoying the One-Shots a lot!
