So: FF5 Wraith's power says "Draw a card.  Until the end of your next turn, whenever you draw a card, you may discard a card.  If you do, another player draws a card." If you activate Clandestine Funding multiple times in a round, she gets multiple opportunities to discard and give a draw to someone else each time she draws.  2 Clandestine Fundings means 2 chances to discard, 3 is 3, and so on.

Malpractice Medico says "Until the end of your next turn, the first time each hero target would regain hp each turn, Dr Medico deals 1 target that amount of energy damage instead."  Activating this power multiple times does not trigger multiple instances of damage the first time each hero is healed in a turn.  

Why is one one way and the other the other?  I can't parse it from the rules.
