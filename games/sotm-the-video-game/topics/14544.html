Zero is tricky to use, but if you get him going, he has some of the best damage output in the game. My run-through on this one shot had Zero doing 20+ damage to Spite each turn at the end of the game.

Galvanize used, Inspiring presence in Play

Start of turn - Impale for 4 to Spite (-2 from the drug)

Hoarfire: 4 cold to Spite, 4 fire to Zero, transducer reacts so 6 cold to spite. 3 cold to zero for heals, 3 fire to zero, transducer reacts for 5 more cold to spite. 

Power: thermodynamics for 3 fire to zero, transducer reacts for 5 more cold to Spite. 

Was able to do that two turns in a row.

When we first got the game we said he was called Absolute Zero because he did absolutely nothing. We then figured out how he could tank so he became a damage sponge.
