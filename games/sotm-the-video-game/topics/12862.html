Save up your Bloodied Knuckles.  Visionary can help you get them quickly.

Use normal Tachyon because SST is unpredictable.

Haka helps as he can nom all the people you don't want coming back out.  Just make sure to leave someone like the Broker around for the Operative to pull out for retaliation damage.

Wraith is still good because she can help avoid Prison Breaks and also kill small guys.  Concentrate more on getting Infrared Eyepiece than Razor Ordinance.  Remember that using Infrared Eyepiece also gets you more cards, which will lead to being able to play Razor Ordinance later.

It is better to depend on retaliation damage than on Undevided Attention.

If you put Wrest the Mind on the Operative you can increase how much damage she deals to Mr. Fixer while reducing her retaliation damage.

Never play a tool for Mr. Fixer.  This will avoid the issue of having one in play when you don't want it anymore.

If you want the scenarios to go faster, play on Advanced.  It flips The Chairman and increases how much damage everyone takes, getting to that key low health point very quickly.  However, it can get out of control and wipe you before you can get Bloody Knuckles in play.

The Operative deals H-2 retaliation damage.  That means you'll want more heroes, not less, on your team to help get the Fixer kill.  With Bloody Knuckles, the Nemesis Symbol, 5 heroes, and Wrest the Mind, she can do 6 damage to Mr. Fixer in one hit, so you want to get him to that or less before trying to kill him.  5 or less if you don't have Wrest the Mind.

Make sure you're fighting in Rook City.  And don't destroy Blighted Streets.  It will increase the Operative's counterstrike damage.

It may help to have Legacy on the team if you can get Lead from the Front and Next Evolution.  That can help you get the time you need to get the cards you need.  And it'll help Haka use Savage Mana on the pesky thugs.
