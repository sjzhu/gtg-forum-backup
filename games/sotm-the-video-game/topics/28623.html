#2

Mint with everyone around 20, which surprised me a little. Usually Iron Legacy fights are a bit more brutal, but H=3 helps.

Luminary begins with Timely Disruption which can take out all 3 starting Ongoings, which helps a lot. From there, I flipped him quickly so he would start focusing the devices rather than AoE’ing everyone. This does mean he has his damage reduction which makes things slower, but it also opens him up to the environment’s True Form if the HP balances are right.

After that, Mystical Ceremonies add a LOT to the heroes’ power. Drawing or playing 3 extra cards per round is really strong. I left the Ninjas out once they came out because otherwise they tend to target the Devices.

End game had several options. First is the Death-Laser, which blows through his DR and with enough cards discarded can one-shot him. Second was Guise’s combo of Blatant Reference + Guise the Barbarian + Inspiring Presence to do a lot of damage quickly. Lastly was the one I ended up using, just whittling him down to the point a Triple Cross could take him out.
