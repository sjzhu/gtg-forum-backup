Mint.

Dark Visionary focused on Baron Blade's deck to avoid cards I didn't want to see. An early Trust Fund from Wraith set up Improptu Invention into Utility Belt so she could work on the environment and still help out elsewhere. Haka pulled out his big clubs and merrily pounded away. Bunker pulled out a Flak Cannon to add more damage. Baron Blade quickly was the lowest hit point character when Wraith pulled out a Kraken. When he flipped, there were only 3 cards under him.

On the flip side, it was more of the same. Tachyon got a Hypersonic Assault off to keep the Kraken under control and slow down Baron Blade. Dark Visionary stacked the deck with minions so his deck slowed down considerably. I got a little too eager using Tachyon's power when I should have waited for Phosphorescent Moss to come out first, but Baron Blade was only at 4 hit points by then, so there was no point in waiting. Everyone was in teens, low 20s.
