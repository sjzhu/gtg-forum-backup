Ra, Argent Adept, Fanatic, and Scholar against the Ennead in the Tomb of Anubis.

I took some advice from the forum and played through it once outside the challenge to see if there were any surprises. Nothing too suprising (tank with Scholar and hope that Ra gets Drawn to the Flame early) so I went in.

My starting Ennead was Nuit, Set, Osiris, and Shu, I think. They pulled a first term Taste of Immortality for Atem and Isis and Nepythys showed up at some point.

It was a pretty straightforward damage fight with no particular twist. Ra eventually buffed up with Imbued Fire and his Staff and got the majority of extra plays from AA's Inspiring Supertonic. Never got out Drawn to the Flame so everyone had to be knocked out one at a time. Scholar used Don't Dismiss Anything a couple of times to keep Fanatic and AA in the fight and I did manage to get off a Verbal Sonata during AA's play after Set got incapacitated, which was pretty helpful.

So that's my sixth mint. How'd everyone else do?
