Fine.

I lost several times, because I couldn’t build up as long as Carbon Adept and Ray Manta were in play, and by the time I managed to take them out, Sargent Steel had agents doing a metric buttload of damage to all my heroes every turn. Miss Information’s -1 debuf neuters most of the team, particularly Tachyon, and Steel and Biomancer both heal their minions every turn so it’s difficult to wear them down if you don’t kill them outright. Or rather, Steel gets a medic out early who does the healing.

So I finally gave up and looked here. Pete’s claim that he managed to BONK the Carbon Adept before he triggered led me to re-examine what I was doing. Heroic Interception is the obvious play turn 1 with Legacy, since the Net absorbs the self-damage, and Carbon Adept will probably eat Inspiring Presence, but it’s the wrong play. With Inspiring Presence in play, there’s enough damage to take out Carbon Adept, except that Miss Information will re-play World Improved if you’ve eliminated it, so Tempest can’t do damage on his turn. Still, External Combustion + Tachyon’s Sucker Puch will still take out Carbon Adept.

After that, it’s easy. 2x Shivers + Inspiring Presence + Cold Snap will wipe the minions, Supersonic Assault eliminates 90% of the damage, and Take Down prevents the other 10% and prevents the villains from building up. I had a couple of each of those cards, which meant I took virtually no damage until the end.

I had the same problem Pete had, I accidentally took down Ambuscade a bit earlier than I intended via those supercharged Cold Snaps, and Glamour did a bunch of damage to my team. Even so, I was able to put down The Operative for a second time immediately afterward, and that ended the game.

Target order was Sargent Steel (because I had such bitter memories of what he does if he builds up) -> Miss Information -> Biomancer -> Ambuscade -> The Operative. I intended to take down Ambuscade last, but as I said, I knocked him down accidentally while I was working on The Operative’s second life. Biomancer at #3 was a bit of an attack of opportunity, when Absolute Zero was in a position to take him down, after Cold Snap had eliminated his “first attack each turn” damage resistance.
