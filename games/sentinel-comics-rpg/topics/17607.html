As I suggested earlier, I think a great number of house rules will be necessary for this to work.  When I did D&D, if someone knew they wouldn't be able to post for a day or so, they'd state their intended action and the DM would adjudicate as best they could based on how things had changed.

Here, as I suggested before, I suggest a first-come, first-served method, where initiative just goes to whoever posts next (and not who the previous character chose to go next). Players should also have increased privilege to come up with their own twists and just run with them without GM approval (although the GM could veto and retcon if they felt it was necessary).

Most likely the need for more house rules will become apparent as the game goes by.  However, while having everyone be in compatible time zones would be a plus, it's hardly necessary (I played D&D with several Europeans).
