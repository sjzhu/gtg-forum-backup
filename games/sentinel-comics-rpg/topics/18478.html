I'm running the quickstart issues and next session we'll be tackling Tome of The Bizarre #86. I've readthe adventure and there are some details I want to change, but there's two major ones:

-I want the events to be set on the real Tomb of Anubis. 

-But it's still connected to Baron Blade's plans to distract the heroes (while gathering forces for issue 6). 

 

So instead of robots and Miss Information's traps the characters will be facing real mummies and genuine dangers from the temple, but my problem is how to fit it in the major plot. Here are some ideas I''m playing with: 

-Baron Blade found a way to open a portal to the temple in New Vegas, risking letting out all the monsters and dark energies into the city if the players can't close it from the other side (there would be a machine inside the temple keeping the passage open, serving as a clue to Baron's identity). The new Anubis went to investigate the portal but got captured by the temple's traps. 

-Miss Information, hired by Baron Blade, used her powers to open the New Vegas portal as a way to lure the heroes in and let the Temple itself deal with them. Maybe she planned to capture Anubis to serve as bait. 

-Blade made a deal with Ammit? Maybe he gave her the new Anubis in exchange for dealing with the heroes, but I'm not sure if it would make sense for both to interact this way. 

 

Anyways, what are your thoughts?
