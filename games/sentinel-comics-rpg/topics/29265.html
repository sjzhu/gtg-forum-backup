fjur:

Why is there a picture of Dr. Broussard in a bikini?

I claim no responsibility for that. Ernest found those pictures on the internet and insisted that’s what Dr. Broussard looks like. Honestly, in my mind I was picturing a more solidly built woman. But I always view world-building as a consensual activity, so I let it ride. I’m pretty sure Ernest snuck in after Bruce completed the session summary and pasted those images in there.

fjur:

Wait a minute, is it possible that Nightstalker chose his hero-name in emulation of Ambuscade’s Night Hunter persona?

Well, Ambuscade never became Night Hunter in my setting, he took a different path. The name Nightwalker is one I came up with after considering many variations of names with ‘Night’ or ‘Dark’ or ‘Shadow’ in them. It’s tough because there are already so many characters in comic fiction with names like that. I expected ‘streetwalker’ jokes and of course it was one of the first things the players said.
