MindWanderer:

Not sure we're talking about the same thing then. Whoever made the bonus/penalty chooses what it applies to, as also defined by what the bonus or penalty actually is in the game world. It's applied to one effect die - a bonus or penalty isn't ever going to be used on a non-effect die. See page 9 of the gameplay guide.

However, if you have a bonus that you use on an attack that affects multiple targets with your Min die, that bonus is only applying to that die, so the damage to each is increased accordingly. 

Say you're taking the "lose health equal to the Max die" twist and you used a boost on a "Principle of X" ability (and thus, you boosted the Max die).  Do you take more damage?

Ah, I see. Largely this would be the GM's call based on what the boost was and how the twist was created because they're the one choosing the twist, though in general, I'd say losing health equal to Max die from a twist isn't an effect die and so mods don't really apply.
