I'll bite on a little bit:

Principle of Obsession: 

You are a known supervillain, sworn to tear down America's Finest Legacy.

Minor Twist:  How does your tenuous alliance with the Freedom Five inconvenience you? 

Major Twist:  How do you resist taking the opportunity to slay Legacy once and for all?

 

Principle of the Baron:

You are the benevolent leader of Mordengrad, the land of your father and a realm of technological marvels.

Minor Twist:  How do your duties as Baron of Mordengrad interfere with your current schemes?

Major Twist:  What threat to your homeland has emerged, which requires your undivided wrath?
