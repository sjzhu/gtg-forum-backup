It's starting to become clear that there's a lot in the starter kit that's either vague or just plain wrong.  I'm starting a thread listing as many of these things as possible, in hopes that this will become a stickied reference.

I'm not including typoes and other errors that don't change the meaning, only things that are missing or incorrect.

Please feel free to reply with anything additional you find, and I'll add them to this post.

Errata

  • Some hero abilities listed as "I" ("Inherent") are mis-labeled.  Usually, if it involves the player making a roll, it should be an Action or a Reaction.  Specific abilities we know are wrong:
    • Absolute Zero's Modular realignment should be a Reaction
    • Bunker's BUDDABUDDABUDDABUDDA should be an Action
  • The "gameplay example" on page 2 has Legacy taking damage equal to the Min die as a minor twist.  While the GM is of course free to do this, it's a poor example, since the default minor twist is to lose health equal to the Max die.  (The difference between "takes damage" and "loses health" is also relevant--in this case, Legacy's Fortitude would have reduced "damage taken" but not "health lost.")  https://greaterthangames.com/comment/183576#comment-183576
  • Page 16 suggests that "the scene" takes turns.  This isn't correct.  The environment takes a turn (even if no environment is described), and the scene tracker advances on the environment's turn.  https://greaterthangames.com/comment/183523#comment-183523
  • Minions created as a threat by the environment act on the environment's turn, the round after they were created.  https://greaterthangames.com/comment/183523#comment-183523

Clarifications

  • Unless otherwise explicitly stated, Actions always use three dice (Power, Quality, Status).  Reactions and Out abilities always use one die (specified in the ability).
  • When new minions enter a fight, if there's an existing group of that type of minion, they join the group and act when they do.  If there aren't any existing minions of that type, they form a new group and go for the first time in the next round. (Minions created by the environment are an exception, see errata above.)  https://greaterthangames.com/comment/182808#comment-182808
  • When you cause a twist, you choose the twist first and then roll.  No rolling first and then choosing a twist that's weaker based on your roll!  https://greaterthangames.com/comment/183208#comment-183208
  • Whoever creates a bonus or penalty chooses when that bonus or penalty is to be applied, before the roll.  This should be appropriate to what the penalty or bonus was supposed to be, although the GM should interpret this generously (e.g. if Wraith was suffering from "I dropped all my knives -2," that wouldn't do anything to her doing detective work).  https://greaterthangames.com/comment/186384#comment-186384
  • Bonuses and penalties apply to an "effect die," after min/mid/max is determined.  Most actions have only one effect die, but abilities that cause more than one separate effect have multiple effect dice, and each mod applies to only one (again, determined before rolling).  For instance, if Tachyon wants to deal lots of damage with a Hypersonic Assault, she can declare before the roll to add a bonus she has to the damage effect of that ability (i.e. whichever the min die ends up being).  https://greaterthangames.com/comment/186384#comment-186384
  • If an ability says that a thing happens sometimes, that's in addition to what happens normally.  E.g. Bunker's Grenade Launcher always attacks up to 3 targets but also damages Bunker or causes a twist on doubles.  https://greaterthangames.com/comment/184938#comment-184938

Actually, and I made this argument in the other thread, I think that all the minor twists are excessively harmful.

  • The hero is hindered using Max+Min.  That's probably a -3 or -4.  Not too bad if your next action was a boost or hinder, but if it was an attack, you're almost guaranteed to fail, and an overcome will be downgraded a whole step, resulting in a chaining twist at best, a wasted turn more likely, or possibly the dreaded "spectacular failure".
  • The scene tracker advances a step.  This is frequently horrible; most of the failures and near-failures I've seen have been because the scene tracker ran out, and losing 1/8 of it is a big deal.
  • The hero is separated from the group.  Being unable to assist or be assisted by the other heroes can be crippling, depending on the situation.
  • Spawn a new minion.  Congratulations, your action probably wastes the action of another hero--depending on what you achieved, things might have been better had you done nothing.  If your teammates are unlucky, your one action might waste the actions of several teammates and might cost your team some health as well.  It's a good one if your team has mass attacks, but right now I'm of the opinion that unlimited mass attacks are overpowered anyway.

There's no objective way to balance story stuff.  They can be the most lenient (they change the story rather than just "making things harder"), but can also be the worst (like creating a new challenge that requires additional Overcome actions... which in turn trigger more minor twists... creating a spiral that causes you to fail the whole scene).
