The way I see it, you get 3 Qualities from the Struggling Background.  Then you assign 3 powers in the Mystical Power source and 1 more quality.  Finally you have 3 dice to assign in the Elemental Manipulator Archetype... one of which must be assigned to an Elemental/Energy power (though you can opt to switch one of the dice you have for a die that is already assigned to an Elemental/Energy Power).  Assuming you didn't switch a die with an Elemental/Energy power you already had, you only have 2 dice left to assign, one to a Power from the list in Elemental Manipulator and one to a Quality in that same Archetype.

This will grant you a total of 5 powers and 5 qualities... or 4 powers and 6 qualities (depending if you switched a die in the Archetype step) not including the RP quality you create in the Personality step.

Elemental Manipulator is one of the few Archetypes where specifically states to select only 1 power (after making sure you have an Elemental/Energy Power), where most state to assign one or more dice to a list of powers before assigning any remaining dice to qualities.  The key however is realizing that one of the dice you bring into this step has to go to an Elemental/Energy power before you choose from the list of powers... even if you are just switching a die you have with an Elemental/Energy power you already have.

The easiest solution is not to choose any Elemental/Energy powers in the Power Source step or have a character that can manipulate multiple energies (such as a Weather Manipulator who has both Weather and Electricity or Cold powers... to create lightning storms or blizzards for example).  Of course, if you really want to maximize the number of qualities you have, you can always put your RP Quality in an empty space in the Power section (the bottom slot would keep it separated from your powers if you need the reminder).
