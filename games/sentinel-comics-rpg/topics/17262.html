I'd go starter kit for a few reasons.

 

1) We're like 85% sure it is going to hit in December, and the core book isn't going to be for a while

 

2) The system looks easy while I was playing it, but there is a lot of nuance between the different characters. Particularly Bunker is almost a different type of character sheet compared to the others, and I'm not sure if the core book will include the FV plus Unity since they come with the Starter kit. Having these characters to study might help Parse aspects of the system.

 

2.5) Playing the game and seeing how it works when all put together is going to make it much easier to have answers when your players come up with crazy concepts, and it will also give you experience running the game so when you look to the rules things are concrete rather than abstract.

 

3) Lore. The Starter kit is going to have a bunch of lore stuff for us to go through. And we know it tells the story of the last major run by the FV, of how Legacy becomes Heritage and of the Rise of Daybreak and who all is on that team. Even if I was not going to be running the RPG ever, the starter kit tells the story of how we get from Oblivaeon to the RPG and that is super cool for me. Also, some of that art is amazing from what little I was able to sneak a peek at.
