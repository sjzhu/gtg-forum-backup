Yeah, when the Reds kick in (especially Red combos) that’s the heroes’ time to shine. If your players are fairly new to the game I find it can be helpful to remind them that they can opt to take a minor twist (or burn a collection) to use an ability one GYRO zone early. It’s costly and sometimes won’t work well (like when your Reds are very combo-dependent) but popping a Red ability while you’re in Yellow has been decisive in more than a few scenes I’ve seen.

And if the baddies roll hot on you round 1 it can become an option while the scene is still in the Green, even. “I told you you wouldn’t like me when I’m angry!”
