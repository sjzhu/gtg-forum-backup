“The ropes bite into your skin, and the discomfort distracts you, making it a bit harder to concentrate! Roll with -2 Hinder! Good luck, hero!”

Any bonus or penalty can be justified using story flavor.

Using up the penalties on the next roll and declaring bonuses before rolling also helps with bookkeeping, too. There is no need to try to remember which penalty/bonus has been used or not. It shows up, gets used, and then on to the next thing.

You also have to describe what the boost or hinder is, in the fiction of the game, when you create it. And the person who created the bonus or penalty decides when it gets used (before the die roll.) So if you make a bonus of "Really Big Sword +3" for someone else and their next roll is to try an overcome a psychic challenge, you can have the bonus apply to a later roll of theirs (or make a strong case about how that Sword helps you be psychic.)
