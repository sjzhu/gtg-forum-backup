As this is an RPG I can point at one company that does not do a kickstarter until the product is ready to go, you get the PDFs of the books a couple of weeks after the kickstarter ends.

More and more these days Kickstarter is not about getting your dream out there but about seeing the market for a product you have already finished and getting hype for it.

 

 

Also >G, doing your kickstarter over  nerd Christmas (GenCon)? Why would you do that to yourselves?
