That is the rule. If you read it on page 86, you are allowed to upgrade the minion to the Max die size rolled. It's not the same as the making a minion rules. Its a nice little trick for a minion maker to build around actually (build a boost combo that uses d10 and d12 to hopefully improve on the average minion size)
