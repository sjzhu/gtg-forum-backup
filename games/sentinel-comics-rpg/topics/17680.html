I’m wondering if there is a specific difference between the phrasing in the thread title. Specific example would be Legacy’s Danger Sense ability which says, “Defend using Awareness” and his Heroic Interception ability which says, “Defend by rolling your Leadership die.”

My interpretation is that the former language–“X using Y”–means assemble your dice pool as normal but you must include, i.e. “use”, the named die, which in this example is Awareness. The latter language–“rolling your Y die”–means to roll only the Leadership die alone and do not include any other dice with it. Does that seem correct?

The fact that abilities that use the “X using Y” sometimes refer to Min/Mid/Max die and “X by rolling your Y die” refers to one die only is where I’m drawing my interpretation from. At the same time I know this is a starter product and there may be certain types or templating issues that might have snuck through.
