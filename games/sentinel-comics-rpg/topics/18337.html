It takes a while to teach the game and getting the first scene finish. Mostly getting the flow of the game and soildtifing rules. Even though the 2nd scene has a lot more going on, most of my player have the flow if and can play mostly with out my help. 6 players does push the game well over 2 hours. I have been known to ignore something in Scene two to fit it under the 2 hours I get to demo at a con. Anytime I was able to finish my games it took 2 and a half hours. (I have demoed this game well over 2 dozen times)

 

If you are playing with less than 6 (Like 2 or 3) I would cut some of the overcomes in scene 2 (Instead of 2 overcome need just one). As to not overwhelm the players. Action Economy.
