How are powers chosen/balanced between characters?  What other system is it closest to? How does luck and unlucky work?  How does super speed work?  How hard is it to get time travel powers?

Those ARE a lot of questions! The character generation is based on the stories of the characters, and the balance comes from the story and the GM. The system is very flexible and allows for great storytelling both on the part of the GM and the players. From what I've seen, I'd say it's closest to Apocalypse World, but still very different from that. And that's just my impressions: Cam, Dave, and Phillipe might feel very differently! As for luck, it is a dice-based game, so there is that. And powers are all specific to characters. Can't go too far into that without just laying out the entire system.

Do you used pre created villains and stories or will it have the ability to create custom villains? Also will it feature all of the characters of the SOTM world or just a select few?

You can totally create your own villains, environments, and stories! The game will feature a TON of characters, but perhaps not every single one. But still a lot, and all the tools necessary to make even more.

I tend to prefer middle of the road stuff myself.

Hi folks,
