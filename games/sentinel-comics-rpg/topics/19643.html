Here's mine.

Here's Sylphid's backstory, which the other characters wouldn't know, but anyone is welcome to use this as a springboard for figuring out how they might know her.

Mindy Price came from a wealthy, well-connected family.  Her father was an executive at a large Silicon Valley tech company.  Her mother was a partner in a large, successful corporate law firm.  Between them and their extended families, they know people in business, politics, Hollywood and more. 

Mindy herself tried to be a conscientious "granola girl" but did so with all the privilege of class.  She loved to hike, bike, and rock climb, and had expensive equipment for all of it.  She ate vegan and organic.  She did interpretive dance and plays guitar, and did both really well because she's had private tutors.  She's traveled to famous landscapes all over the world.  She went to protests for all kinds of social justice issues, but never anything dangerous.

As a freshman in college (a small liberal arts college, of course), Mindy and a half-dozen girlfriends went on a wildrness bonding retreat.  There were music and dancing, and later wine, and later a pipe of some sort got passed around.  The rest of the evening was lost in a haze of sound and motion and strange, fey voices and laughter.  When she woke up, she was lying on a dewy hilltop, wearing little more than flowers and a pair of diaphanous fairy wings.

Minutes later, Mindy heard dogs barking and the voices of men.  She panicked and ran, not wanting to be discovered like this.  She hoped to find her friends before anyone found her, but failed.  The day went on, and grew hungry and thirsty.  She tried to forage but couldn't find anything to eat or drink.  As she grumbled to herself, the bushes next to her started spontaneously growing juicy berries!  She ate her fill, then began experimenting with her new powers, spending the next several days surviving in the wild, avoiding rescue parties, and trying to figure out what to do next.

Mindy discovered that she could cause living plants to move and grow, and even dead plants (like the cotton in her friendship bracelets, which she still wore) to move around somewhat.  She could talk to animals and understand them empathically, and sometimes even command them.  She learned to fly with her new wings, which had great speed but little stamina.

The final piece came when Mindy began to feel weak from her limited diet.  She'd even consider eating meat at this point!  As she imagined biting into a thick, juicy steak, she found her body changing shape, with her teeth elongating, and claws sprouting from her hands.  Moments later, a great tawny cougar raced through the forest looking for prey.

This new power was the toughest of all to master, but eventually Mindy learned to assume the cougar form at will, as well as a wheat-colored wolfhound and a huge golden eagle.  Most importantly, she learned to make her fairy wings disappear!  She was finally able to sneak back to her dorm room.  Once here, she refused to tell her friends anything about what happened, rebuffed their attempts at continued friendship, and focused on finishing the semester.  She then spent a year traveling the world, telling her parents she needed to "figure out who she was."

When she went back to school (a new school, wherever the campaign takes place), she was no wiser about the origin of her powers but resolved to make the most of them.  She put aside her neo-hippie persona and became a serious, somewhat antisocial student... when she wasn't being a superhero, still dedicated to the protection of the downtrodden and the environment.  Rather than settling on a single costume, she put together a wardrobe of fairy outfits (all made of natural, organic fibers, of course), with matching half-masks.  The only consistent pieces were a rainbow-colored scarf, assorted wooden jewelry, and the cloth bracelets made by the friends she abandoned.
