So I am so stoked for the RPG and I am eagerly awaiting its release.  Here's the thing Major and Minor twists seem to require a lot of creativity.  So as a resource, I thought we could list all the major/minor twists Christopher has used in the lifestream.  That way we can get an idea for what the general power level is.  I'm just re-listening to the first session, so if people want to jump in with ones from the other sessions, that's fine.

 

Major:

Take away a major resource from the player for the session (Dynamo-on his first roll, has a major twist and ends up loosing use of his recon bots for the remainder of the session.)

A power source of a player starts making (unreasonable) demands of the player. (Starblight-His sword starts demanding he attack the dragon that is immune to damage.)  He also (somewhat) looses use of his left hand as his sword won't 'sheathe itself,' so there's that too.

You (Or someone else) takes dmage equal to the highest die on the roll (Stella Nova hits Starblight for 10 when dispeling her accidental shield)

Summon some minions (Chris mentions that he would have done this if Dynamo had rolled a major plot twist when taking out his sigil.)

Minor:

Succeed, but add an additional overcome action that needs to happen. (Stella Nova-Accidentally does too good a job cutting a rune off from its power source, and cuts it off from the PCs.)

Damage equal to min die on the check (can't remember where that one happens.)

Damage or in some way hinder or annoy an ally (Stella Nova rips into Dynamo's mind and pains him, but doesn't cause damage.  Possibly removed memories from him?)

The devil is in the detail here: a twist doesn't deal damage, it causes you to "lose Health."  Unless the GM says otherwise, of course.

 

The Gameplay Guide also says this:

A successful Attack deals damage to the  target, either reducing their Health, or forcing them to roll  a save to survive.
And, two paragraphs later...
• Damage: A successful Attack deals damage to the  target, reducing the target’s Health by the amount  shown on the effect die. In the case of a minion  or lieutenant, damage forces them to save to stick  around.
Neither of them say that it can be reduced by defends nor damage reduction abilities are applied. That's in Defend.

So, on to defend…

When you Defend, you describe what you’re doing to  avoid an Attack or threat. After you roll, note the number  showing on the effect die—write it down so you don’t  forget. Before your next turn, when you would next lose  Health you reduce the amount you lose by that number.
Key wording for Defend: "..., when you would next lose Health...." Not, "When you next take damage." It does, however, go on to say, in a self-contradiction, "If you are not Attacked before your next turn, the effect is wasted."

Now, Bunker's Armor Plating says

Reduce physical damage by 1 while you are in the Green zone, 2 while in the  Yellow zone, and 3 while in the Red zone.
For this, you MIGHT be right... but it feels wrong when Defend clearly is detailed such that it doesn't need to be an attack.

I really hope they clear this up in the corebook.
