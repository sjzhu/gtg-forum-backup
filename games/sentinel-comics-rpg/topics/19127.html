Glad people are finding them useful! Hope the GTG crew are happy for me to share them.

A word on how I've used them, just in case there is any confusion (they are a little more complex now than the first iteration). The idea is that the minion/lieutenant tokens (except d12) are double-sided, so when they step down a die you simply flip them over (or replace them with the next lowest token). The modifier (and yellow persistent modifier) tokens are likewise double-sided (not because they flip in play, but just to cut down on the sheer number of tokens), plus they have a space at the bottom to write the name of the modifier (see the spider bot webs tokens for a pre-filled example).

The PC (and villain, when they actually exist) tokens are doubled sided too, so that once a PC has acted you flip the token to indicate that. The yellow "acted" tokens can be placed on minion group or lieutenant tokens to indicate the same (as these are already flipped to indicate the current die).

I've generally been handling locations with half-size index cards (I write the name of the location on the top of the card), which the relevant tokens are then placed on top of, though they could also be used for a more standard battlemat if desired.

Finally, the bunker mode cards can be used both to indicate his current mode, and to have a reference which can be layed on top of the left hand column of the second page of his character sheet, so you don't have to flip to his secondary mode reference sheet all the time.
