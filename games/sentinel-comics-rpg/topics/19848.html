You partially correct.
Even if your mode does not allow you to take the Boost action, you can still use Switch Mode to enter a different mode. You simply will not get a Boost action while doing so. If a villain removes all of the bonuses you made, then you will be unable to use Quick Switch due there being nothing available for it to eat.
