We have decided that doing finales at conventions is suboptimal, because live-streaming from shows is always hard, and because it puts the final episode of a season at a different day and time from the other episodes, making it hard for everyone to watch it who had been enjoying it up to that point.

However, we do plan to do one-off adventures at shows going forward! We currently plan to at Unplugged, but haven't worked out all of the details yet.
