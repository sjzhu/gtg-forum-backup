Unlike some Archetypes, the Cursed power source doesn't restrict your choice to only powers that you chose or ones from a certain list.  Nothing in "Defining Abilities" says your choices are limited this way.  We do know that one of the SCRPG Season 2 heroes had Immunity from the Accident power source without having an Energy/Element power, although it's not phrased quite the same way.

For Physical Powerhouse, indeed, the Yellow ability isn't restricted to a power or quality from the Physical Powerhouse list.
