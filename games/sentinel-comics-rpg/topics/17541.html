I'd have a hard time justifying "the heroes cannot win this fight."  She's tough, but standing up the the whole Freedom Five+Unity at once?  I don't think so.  Especially since we'll have full stats for her when the full game comes out.

I'm thinking what I'd do is make her a "phantom" full villain with "real" HP.  Except that she just soaks the damage, takes out Tempest at the earliest opportunity, and then the next scene begins with her still hostile.  If the heroes attempt to defend her at any sort of risk to themselves, that counts as her "convince her to listen" the first time and then "convince her the heroes aren't villains" the second time.  If, after the battle, the heroes still persist in attacking her, she tries to flee.  If the heroes pursue her... well, only Legacy and Tachyon have a chance of doing so, and that fight she can win, especially when they're weakened by the spirit battle.
