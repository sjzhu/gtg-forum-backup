Hi Everyone! For the last month Ive been working on and planning a Sentinels Cinematic Universe!

To explain- I plan to have several GMs, and upwards of 8 players, playing in several parallel campaigns set in the different areas of the Sentinels universe-

Megalopolis, Rook City, The Cosmos, Adventures around Earth, and so on.

Campaigns would connect with eachother while maintaining distinct identies as separate hero teams- think of Marvels Infinity War-

You had the Thor Team tryign to make the ultimate weapon, the Iron Man team tryign to stop Thanos, and the Captain America team trying to defend Vision.

Big events would be handled this way, and smaller events would happen to individual teams

Characters could crossover and connect through separate OneShot sessions.

 

At the moment I have 2 GMs and 7 Players ready and we are goign to start scheduling and creating characters this coming week.

I would like more players and at least 1 more GM however, and thats why Ive contacted you all here!

If youre interested, please drop by our Discord, you can leave again at any time!
Spectators to the Cinematic Universe are also welcome ^^
