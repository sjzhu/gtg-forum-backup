I can help a little here:

1) We have heard of multiple supplements in the works. Currently it sounds like a mix of villains, pre-made characters, locations, and possibly story arcs. I would also believe that there would be additional and new character creation options.

2) I will say yes? I am not completely familiar with the character but if you look to Bunker, you can see that he can move between different forms (Tyler Vance on foot, Recharge Mode, full Assault mode, etc.). Using that as a basis, it would seem that there would be the possibility to do so and that transformations would be tied to health zone. (I can now see how a Power Ranger mod could be possible where you play as teens in the green zone who would only morph once health or scene tracker hit the yellow zone, maybe zord summoning in the red zone...but I digress)

3) Maybe? For one, I can see the community really supporting this. C&A have also been putting out several cool one shots over the last 14 months or so involving Daybreak. I can see that continuing.
