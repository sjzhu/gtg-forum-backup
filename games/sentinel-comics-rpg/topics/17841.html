Well, that was insane.

We got through issue 1.  Every. Single. Attack. From Legacy emulated a port in some fashion.

His first attack: Can I throw one spider-bot into a second, banking the second into a third? 

His second attack: Bowling for Spiderbots!

His third: Can I Slide one of these on the ice AZ just created and then race ahead of it with a broom?

 

Other standouts: Unity rolling a 3 trying to crash an impromptu conveyance bot into the Spider Mothership.

Tachyon wiping up the entirety of the first wave with Hypersonic assault after some tips from Wraith.

Absolute Zero Saving a bunch of Cocooned peeps with a very creative usage of ice.

Somewhere in these threads is one where someone suggested using index cards to track the bad guys and the various tasks that are on the table for the PC.  The idea is to literally put them on the table.  "Three spider bots for you"...GM lays card on table with 3 spider bots...etc.  Gosh that seemed like a good idea to me.  I plan to do that when I run it.  ;)
