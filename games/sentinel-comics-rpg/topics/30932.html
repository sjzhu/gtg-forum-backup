Minor correction, though:

Exclusive means that if you use it you can only use it, it won’t stack with anything else.

Exclusive mods won’t stack with other exclusives, so you can only use one per roll. They can be combined with non-exclusives to your heart’s content. Usually that means you can’t apply multiple persistent mods (which are almost always exclusive as well) but it also prevents you from using more than one Hero Point bonus at a time (which are exclusive, but not persistent).

The idea of a bow that can shoot snakes that is also a snake that you could use as a whip or (stiffened, I suppose, and biting with the snake head) as a fighting staff is very original. Clever.
