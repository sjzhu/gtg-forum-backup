Best expansions for:

Villain Decks: Shattered Timelines:

The roster here has a great range of interesting mechanics and difficulties and offers a great range of fights. Iron Legacy and Kismet give you interesting single target fights (with IL being one of the hardest bosses around). LaCapitain scratches the swarming minion-fest itch with all her crew members, and Dreamer also provides a target rich encounter but with the twist of needing to save her instead of defeat her. When I compare the villain rosters in each (currently released) expansion as a whole, Shattered Timelines offers the most well rounded group if you are looking to add variety to your base set.

Hero Decks: Vengeance:

When we are talking about Hero decks, there might be fan favorites scattered throughout all the many expansions, but its hard to argue against the bang for your buck in the staggering 5 new heros that come with Vengeance. They are all powerhouse characters that span almost every playstyle, difficulty, and role you can think of. It offers the first level 1 complexity hero since the base set in the damage focused KNYFE, the high risk/reward playstyle of Setback, hyper control/support in Parse, the Jack of all trades Naturalist, and the very unique hero team deck of The Sentinels. If you are looking to add more to your hero roster, Vengeance is the clear winner to add to your base set.

Difficulty: Rook City:

Is your base set getting a little too easy even with Advanced Modes? Don't consider anything else other than Rook City. It offers a whopping two villains (Matriarch and Chairman) rated at a 4 difficulty and they consistently beat the pants off even the most veteran players. Plauge Rat is brutal single target slug fest and Spite offers a very different (although predictable) playstyle than any other villain deck. The hero decks, although not very complex, are not effected by the power creep of later expansion decks and really need teamwork to make shine. On top of all of that, you have some of the most brutal and unforgiving environments SotM has released. If you need to up the difficulty of your base set, adding Rook City to your arsenal is the best way to go.

Foote's Choice: Infernal Relics:

Even though it doesn't edge out any of the other expansions in the above catagories I laid out, Infernal Relics still might be one of my favorite overall expansions, and I'd do it a disservice not to mention that here. Both heros are high complexity and very rewarding and the villains (save for Gloomweaver) all see regular rotation (even Gloomweaver was saved with his Dark Watch Promo which still makes him very valuable). The environments are varied and interesting and there is just too much awesome magic goodness out of the overall theme of this expansion to not get special mention. Infernal Relics also has the leg up on being included with Rook City these days, which makes the RC/IR package way too good to pass up. Overall, if you are looking to expand outward from the base set today, there would be no better choice than the RC/IR bundle
