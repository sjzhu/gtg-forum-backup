In your own personal Sentinels of the Multiverse game continuity, however you keep track of things - video game stats, paper tally sheets, scratches on the wall, anecdotes - do any of you have an undefeated hero?

I ask because we keep a tally sheet of wins and losses for each hero, environment, and villain, and we have ONE undefeated hero: Fanatic. She has 26 recorded wins, and no recorded losses. We don’t really have a great idea how this happened, except that she usually appears in our games as her Prime Warden variant, and she goes bananas with her powers. Even if she goes down before the end of the game, she’s blasted the villain and cranked up her heroic teammates.

(Nightmist is next, with only one loss out of a similar ~25 recorded games. Then we come to Tachyon, who has about 6 losses on record out of almost 45 games.)

At this point, we take special note when Fanatic comes out of the box at the beginning of the game: Her reputation is on the line! We’d better make sure to try extra hard to win!

What other undefeated heroes are out there in the many multiverses of your game tables, iPads, and Stream accounts?
