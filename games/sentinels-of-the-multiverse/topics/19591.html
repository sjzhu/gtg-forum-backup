I have a question about what happens when OblivAeon's countdown reaches 0.  His card says "An environment is removed from the game as per the rules on the Scion Reserve", but it does not specify which environment.  Does this mean the players choose which environment he destroys?  That seems to be the implication, since the Inevitable Destruction card specifies to "remove the environment in the battle zone with OblivAeon from the game".  Since OblivAeon moves to the battle zone of the environment he's destroying before he deals the 20 damage, it seems like you can use this to direct that damage to whichever battle zone you want when the countdown reaches 0.  Is this the way it's supposed to work?

OblivAeon is actively in Battle Zone 1 or Battle Zone 2.   If the Countdown reaches 0 in phase 1 or 2 then the environment of the Battle Zone OblivAeon is currently in has all of its cards removed from the game.  Step 2 on the Scion Reserve just mentions to move OblivAeon to that Battle Zone he removed the environment from incase something triggered which caused him to move inbetween before another environment deck is put into play, you're in phase 3 which has a hero removed in addition to the environment of the Battle Zone that the hero was in, or more likely because a Game Over condition of an environment occurred in a Battle Zone OblivAeon was not in.
