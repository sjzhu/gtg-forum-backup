What specifically are you confused about?

Basically, at the start of each of his turns, count how many of his cards are in play that have "Manifestation" in the orange box beneath the card art. Examples of Manifestations are Ocular Swarm, Twisted Miscreation, and Lambent Reaper. So, if you have H or more of these in play, you flip his cards to reveal the other side, the Unstable Despoiler. And just a note here, "H" represents the number of heroes you are taking into the fight. So if you use Sky-Scraper, Guise and Captain Cosmic, "H" equals 3.

If you reveal Insanity B with Insanity A, insanity A only plays B, and nothing else happens, because it is a one-shot. However, if Insanity B reveals a manifestation, you copy only Insanity B again.

To Clarify:

 

If Insanity plays Insanity, the first insanity will not act anymore, it put a card into play, that card was not a manifestation, Insanity will not repeat.

If the Second Insanity plays a manifestation, its text will repeat, but the first Insanity's text will not.

 

So if a chain of Manifestations played by Insanity leads to a second Insanity, you will continue to play the top card, but it will have nothing to do with the original Insanity, because it will not act again, but simply wait to go to the trash.
