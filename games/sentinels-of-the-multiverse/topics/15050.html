So, here is what I was thinking when I made that comment about healing being rare:

Certain heroes, such as Absolute Zero, The Scholar, and Nightmist are generally good at healing themselves. They are not so good at, or are simply incapable of, healing others.

A few heroes like Tempest, the Argent Adept, Omnitron-X, and Legacy can heal others. However, that generally (a) requires drawing and playing certain cards, which might not even happen, and (b) comes in increments of 1-2 HP.

Overall, healing is much more rare than damage, such that heroes generally end games with much less than their max HP. (I think this is good; it puts pressure on the heroes to finish the game with what they have instead of spending all their time going for the most powerful setup every time.)

So, anyway, Medico’s power to increase the next amount of HP recovery by 2 seems like it only does about as much good as one of those standard 1-2 HP heals - EXCEPT that is is also contingent on having one of those instances occur separately. If Tempest never draws his Cleansing Downpour, then Medico’s power would be near useless in that game. So I figure Medico himself must have some additional powers/cards to chain up with his base, allowing him to boost his capability. Maybe he has multiple power uses, or maybe all his one-shots have a Chrono-Ranger-like “one target regains 1 HP” on them.
