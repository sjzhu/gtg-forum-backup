It allows Bunker to cycle through his deck without forgoing his power phase, the caveat being you have to destroy a card. Consider this example:

 

Base Bunker

Play a card, use base power. Net result is 1 card in play, 2 cards drawn.

Termination Bunker

Play a card, use base power, play another card and use new power. Net result is 1 card in play, 2 cards drawn, and whatever the power was.

 

As you can see, Termination Bunker can accomplish more on his turns without sacrificing card draw, however his power has 3 caveats.

1. You end up with 1 less card in hand than Base Bunker

2. You have to be able to play a card with a power on it to fully utilize his base power.

3. You have to be wiling to sacrifice a card, but in my opinion Bunker has plenty of options for this.

 

He's not as good as hoarding cards for Gatling Gun and Omnicannon, but he is good at finding the cards you're looking for while still contributing to the fight.

TermiNation Bunker can move through his deck to find damage dealing powers at the same speed as base Bunker. However, relative to base Bunker he effectively trades 1 card in hand for an extra power use.

Additionally, by letting you play, power, or draw in any order it allows Bunker to get the around the drawbacks of Mode cards.
