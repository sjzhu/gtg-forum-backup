The Aeon men, Scion, OblivAeon, and Mission decks all have 25 cards.

There are thirty variants that come with OblivAeon, if you count Completionist Guise, who comes with the collector's case.

There are 67 cards in the complete hero variant pack.

I don't tend to track the number over oversized cards, so let me think about the foil villain cards... One for each villain, so 4x5 for the four villains that come in each of the core, RC,IR,ST,WC boxes, plus four more for Ambuscade, Kismet, Wager Master, and Chokepoint, plus an additional one, I'd imagine, for The Operative, plus 15 for the Vengeance and Villains decks, plus the ten scions, plus the OblivAeon booklet. 50 plus the booklet?
