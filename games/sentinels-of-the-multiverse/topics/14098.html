I keep all my non-GTG printed promos with the heroes, because I don't really care if they get damaged. They're replaceable.

I keep all my "official" GTG promos in a sleeve, as well as a toploader, with my leftover hero/environment box, which aren't replaceable, unless I want to spend tons of cash.

I started preordering before Wrath of the Cosmos came out, so I don't have many of the previous ones. Although I did get a set of the Freedom Six, thanks to a good friend of mine.

My setup is a bit cumbersome, since I use premium Mayday sleeves, so I need all 4 large SOTM boxes to store everything nicely.

I treat them like the original character cards. The hero character cards go with their respective decks while the villain character cards (I only use the oversized cards, myself) all go in a stack in the Vengeance box (including the V-format character cards).

For a little while I started taking all of the character cards away from their respective decks and gave them to players to see what gauged their interest, but that never flew well with me. As such, I'm back to the tried and true method of keeping every hero's Hero Card(s) with their deck, usually ordered in date of release. When someone grabs a deck, if it's their first time through it, I urge them to select the topmost card as their avatar.

 

I tend toward the oversized villain cards, but since I lack large cards for most of the promo villains, those just hang out with their respective decks. To save on space, I store the normal sized cards in one of the small expansion boxes.
