The biggest reason why 3 heros is generally easier has a lot to do with how damage numbers scale down while damage mitigation stays consistent. In addition to that, damage mitigation is far more valuable with lower damage numbers than higher ones. For example, the difference between taking 0 or 1 damage is more significant than the difference between taking 6 or 7. Damage reduction of 1 is fairly common, even in random groups of 3 heros. With H-2 being by far and large the average instance of damage Villains generally deal, it's a lot more common to completely nullify damage instead of just mitigating it a little bit.

3 Hero games tend to be much longer on average as well compared to 5 hero games because of the lower damage output on both Hero and Villain side while Health Points stay consistent (which works very much in favor of the heavier setup reliant heros such as Absolute Zero).

Because of all of these things, group composition doesn't suffer nearly as much for having some holes as it does in 5 player games. You can get away with not having Ongoing Destruction against Iron Legacy with Bunker, Sentinels, and Legacy (for example), because the sheer amount of damage mitigation they all bring to the table is far more valuable with less heros. You can play AbZero more consistently against more Villains because games are longer and one board wipe does not hurt as much.
