At some point, they just have to stay within the confines of the game they defined. Part of the great thing about Sentinels is that - even without Vengeance style - it allows tremendous variety within its set format. The more any particular villain deviates from that format, the more that villain might as well just be part of a different game entirely. So I don’t see the final villain requiring tons of heroes or anything like that.

EDIT: In the interest of contributing something more substantive and fun to the discussion, what about a villain that acts like a more extreme version of Plague Rat? Say, the villain deck has a few of these in it:

TURN
Ongoing
When this card enters play, select a player through some largely random mechanism.

Treat all of that player’s cards as if they have the keyword Villain.

Treat all other players’ cards as Hero cards.

That player would then face a dilemma…continue to help the heroes, even as they take damage as part of “all villain targets,” or try to force a condition that causes the heroes to lose, thus winning themselves.

I will call this hypothetical villain “Palpatine.”
