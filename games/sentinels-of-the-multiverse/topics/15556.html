Blitz:

If you do, use this power again now.

PL626

Whenever a hero uses a power,

So if you used Blitz, then moved a Burst from your trash to the bottom of your deck to use it again, and used it a total of 13 times, each power use would resolve last to first, and Spite would deal you 2 damage, then discard the top 5 cards of your deck 13 times.

Similarly, if you use Prejudice and then Pride against Spite as Expatriette, he should wait until Pride is finished and then hit you for 2 and discard cards twice.

I assume (but could be wrong) that this also means that if Fanatic put embolden on Tachyon and Tachyon used Burst, putting 1 burst card under to repeat the power (repeating any number of times) she will have used at least 2 powers and be done.  But if she uses esearch Grant first then she could use Blitz as many times as her stock of trashed burst cards allows still.  Just like Expat and Unload.
