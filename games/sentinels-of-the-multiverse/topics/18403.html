I'm not sure what counts as "spoilers" anymore.  C&A were holding back on Tactics and RPG info for a while, but not anymore.  Some big reveals I can think of which still have some mystery around them:

Mist Storm:

  • Prime War takes place after the "end" of the Mist Storm timeline; presumably the Prime Aspects yanked some survivors of their choice out to fight in their games and left the rest to die.  We still don't know how the Mist Storm universe manages to be completely destroyed; presumably it's the Mist Storm itself, but we don't really understand what that is or how it works.
  • Captain Cosmic somehow defeats Galactra and absorbs her powers.

Sentinel Comics:

  • The Citizens of the Sun have disbanded and are doing their own thing.  Notably, Blood, Sweat, and Tears are still hanging out, under new names, and are up to no good, though their intended endgame is still unknown.
  • Akash'Flora seems to be some sort of transitional state.  It's unknown what it will become afterwards.
  • Proletariat is part of a new villain team, Perestroika, along with the new Iron Curtain (the daughter of the original), the Marxman, and an unnamed fourth member.  They can be seen on lanyards given out at GenCon.  It's been hinted that they'll be in the Sentinels of Freedom video game.
  • Captain Cosmic's encounter with Galactra, instead of making him stronger, leaves him weak and stranded on an alien planet.  Presumably he'll get rescued at some point, but no one knows he's there.
  • Sky-Scraper and K.N.Y.F.E. have new names and a new villain, Malyce (of whom we know nothing).
  • Gloomweaver is legitimately free in the universe, but strangely absent from stories so far.  Why hasn't he taken over?
