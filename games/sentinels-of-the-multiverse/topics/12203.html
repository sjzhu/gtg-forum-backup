As part of my quest to get better at using various post-production software, I decided to make a trailer for a Sentinels of the Multiverse movie. It was a great choice, since there are so many different heroes with different powers it will give me a chance to work with a lot of different special effects. 

I wasn't planning on posting anything here until it was complete, but some people suggested that the community might like to see my progress as it unfolds (since I had just been uploading clips to my social media sites, so the actors could see how cool I was making them look).

So, here is a playlist that I will be updating as I work on more shots.

Bear in mind, these are first drafts so far, so  don't be too harsh, but I would love some feedback from people on how eveything looks, and what you'd like to see. 

http://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLqUArjqK6fsdxYDFAP-UiAK_1yzN56COS

Also, these shots are really short, since they will be a part of a trailer. There will be a short scene with LEgacy and Baron Blade at the beginning but most of the shots after that will be quick ones like this set to typical action movie trailer music.
