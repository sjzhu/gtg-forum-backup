I just had to share the tale of the epic beating I handed to Checmical Immortality Chairman last night. Happened across a team that's just insanely good: Grandpa Legacy, Omnitron X, Scholar, The Sentinels, and Chrono-Ranger. My plan was that I could set up Scholar so that healing him would let him hand out damage, and then let him simultaneously tank and beat on enemies while he was healed by Legacy, Omnitron X, and Doc Medico. But that didn't even happen.
 
Legacy got out Inspiring Presence pretty quickly, and kept using his power to let Omnitron use his, which let someone else play a random card. The real damage-doers turned out to be Chrono-Ranger and The Sentinels. It got very difficult to track whose turn it was sometimes when Legacy would use his power on Omnitron X, who'd let Scholar play a card that let everyone else use a power. But by the end, Chrono-Ranger had Hunter and Hunted out along with a few bounties, and was doing upwards of 20 damage per turn, and every time The Operative would retaliate, he got to use another power because of a bounty. Legacy protected him two rounds in a row with Heroic Interceptions so he didn't get killed from the effects of Hunter and Hunted, and nobody (except Legacy) took much damage from the Chairman's retaliation. We ended the game with all heroes at close-to-max HP.
 
It was glorious.
