What you seem to be ignoring, and I keep trying to emphasize, is that the face down card is already in play. Therefore, if Fixed Point is in play, it is indestructible. The only action that satisifies both of these existing rules is to flip the card back face down, keeping the card in play but making it no longer have a limited keyword. There is no "trumping" involved.
