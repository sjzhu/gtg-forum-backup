Several forumites are in the game, so I came up with some challenge modes specifically for their cards.

Hope you enjoy, and if you are in the game let me know and I'll get you one.

 

Rabit:  Look at the Bones!!!

Imprisoned Rogue treats all non-inmate, non-character targets as if they had the keyword agent.  If there are no targets to destroy, Imprisoned Rogue treats all Heroes as if they had the keyword Agent.  

Arenson:  Police protection.

The Informant is indestructible while The Deputy is in play.

Or:

Not on my watch!

The deputy gains the following text:

Redirect damage dealt to The Broker to the Crooked Cops with the highest HP.

Ronway:  Heroes must die!

Cursed Acolyte gains the following text:

Increase damage dealt to hero targets by 1.  If this card would be dealt damage redirect it to the Zombie with the lowest HP.

If Cursed Acolyte reduces a Hero Character Card to 0 or fewer hp, Ronway wins.

Pydro:  [REDACTED]

Whenever Seige breaker damages a hero target, increase the next damage dealt to that target by 2.

 

Hope you enjoy!

Anyone else?
