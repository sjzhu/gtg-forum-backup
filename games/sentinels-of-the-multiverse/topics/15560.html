I seem to recall someone asking for the order of the foil cards, although I forget who. In any case, I know Christopher carefully picked that order, and thought I'd record it before my cards can played with. So here you go.

  • Legacy: Base (Finest), Newest, Greatest, Freedom Five (4 cards)
  • Bunker: Base (Indestructible), Engine of War, G. I., Termi-Nation, Freedom Five (5 cards)
  • Tachyon: Base (Quickest Woman), Super-Scientific, Team Leader, Freedom Five (4 cards)
  • Wraith: Base (Mystery Comics), Rook City, Price of Freedom, Freedom Five (4 cards)
  • Absolute Zero: Base, Elemental Wrath, Termi-Nation, Freedom Five (4 cards)
  • Unity: Base, Golem, Termi-Nation (3 cards)
  • Omnitron-X: Base, Omnitron-U (2 cards)
  • Visionary: Base, Dark, Unleashed (3 cards)
  • Parse: Base, Fugue State (2 cards)
  • Mister Fixer: Base, Dark Watch (2 cards)
  • Ex-Patriette: Base, Dark Watch (2 cards)
  • Setback: Base, Dark Watch (2 cards)
  • Nightmist: Base, Dark Watch (2 cards)
  • The Scholar: Base, Infinite (2 cards)
  • K.N.Y.F.E.: Base (Agent of F.I.L.T.E.R.), Rogue Agent (2 cards)
  • Argent Adept: Base (Virtuoso of the Void), Prime Wardens, Dark Dynamics, X-treme Prime Wardens (4 cards)
  • Captain Cosmic: Base, Prime Wardens, Requital, X-treme Prime Wardens (4 cards)
  • Haka: Base (Savage), Eternal, Prime Wardens, X-treme Prime Wardens (4 cards)
  • Tempest: Base (Inhuman), Sacrifice, Prime Wardens, X-treme Prime Wardens (4 cards)
  • Fanatic: Base, Reedemer, Prime Wardens, X-treme Prime Wardens (4 cards)
  • Ra: Base (Mighty God of the Sun), Horus of Two Horizons, Setting Sun (3 cards)
  • Chrono-Ranger: Base, Best of Times (2 cards)
  • Sky-Scraper: Base (Normal/Huge/Tiny), Extremist (Normal/Huge/Tiny) (6 cards)
  • The Naturalist: Base, Desperate Prey (2 cards)
  • The Sentinels: Rules, Base (Dr. Medico/Mainstay/Idealist/Writhe), Ft. Adamant (Dr. Medico/Mainstay/Idealist/Writhe) (9 cards)
  • Luminary: Base, Heroic (2 cards)
  • The Harpy: Base, Dark Watch (2 cards)
  • Akash'Thriya: Base, Spirit of the Void (2 cards)
  • La Comodora: Base, Curse of the Black Spot (2 cards)
  • Lifeline: Base, Blood Mage (2 cards)
  • Stuntman: Base, All My Own Stunts (2 cards)
  • Benchmark: Base, Supply and Demand (2 cards)
  • Dr. Medico: Base (Void Guard), Malpractice (2 cards)
  • Mainstay: Base (Void Guard), Road Warrior (2 cards)
  • The Idealist: Base (Void Guard), Super Sentai (2 cards)
  • Writhe: Base (Void Guard), Cosmic Inventor (2 cards)
  • Guise: Base, Santa, Completionist (3 cards)

Whew!

Seems to be by team and other close connections.

Starts w/ Freedom IV/V, in the order they joined, then their intern, Unity, then O-X, whom Unity is very close to. Not quite sure Visionary and Parse are grouped with them.

Next come Darkwatch. Mr. Fixer is first, which makes sense as I think he formed Darkwatch. Then Scholar. I vaguely recall that he has a connection to Setback, but I'm not sure I'm remembering that correctly. I don't know why KNYFE is grouped with them.

Then we get the Prime Wardens. The order is interesting here. I don't know much about what drew the Primer Wardens together. I'm not surprised that Argent Adept might have organized them. Fanatic is listed last and is followed by Ra, who she's close to. Not exactly sure how Chrono-Ranger, Sky-Scraper, and Naturalist relate to the Prime Wardens. I vaguely recall something about the Prime Wardens being on Dok'Thorath in order to help out Sky-Scraper.

Next are the Southwest Sentinels.

Following them are the various antagonists who fight with the heroes vs. OblivAeon, followed by Benchmark and Stuntman who join the fight from other realities.

After that we get Void Guard -- the Southwest Sentinels as individual heroes. The fact that they are separated from the Southwest Sentinels suggests to me that maybe there's a chronological component to this order, but that raises other questions, like Ra showing up as late in the order as he does.

Finally, Guise. Who, of course, would be first or last.

Got a card binder with 4 cards on a page, works great because you get a lot of heroes on one page, and the 9 on a page don't look as nice.

Had to redo the order a bit, put Bunker last of the FV, his last card sharing a page with Unity, had to manipulate to get Dark Watch and Sky-Scraper visible at once, but also re-ordered a few to get a better look to the pages.
I also oriented the 2 card heroes vertically so the variant is below the base.

It looks gorgeous, and will definitely make it easy to let people pick the heroes they want to play.

In case anyone wants to do something similar here's the order I used:

page - heroes (ones next to each other are visible together)

1.  Legacy

2.  Wraith
3.  Tachyon

4.  Absolute Zero
5.  Bunker

6.  GI Bunker and Unity
7.  Omnitron-X, Parse

8.  Expatriette, Setback
9.  Mr. Fixer, Nightmist

10.  The Harpy, Chrono Ranger (considered KNYFE or Naturalist here)
11.  The Argent Adept

12.  Captain Cosmic
13.  Haka

14.  Tempest
15.  Fanatic

16.  Ra, Dark Visionary
17.  Visionary, The Scholar

18.  Guise, Sentinels Set up card  (considered switching Guise and Visionary)
19.  The Sentinels

20.  Ft. Adamant Sentinels
21.  Void Guard Medico, Mainstay

22.  Void Guard Idealist, Writhe
23.  KNYFE, Naturalist

24.  Sky-Scraper, Extremist Sky-Scraper Huge
25.  Extremist Sky-Scraper, Benchmark.

26.  Akash'Thriya, La Comodora
27.  Lifeline, Luminary

28.  Stuntman

That leaves 50 card spots open for custom hero character cards, which I can fill up.

Really happy with how it looks, and would advise this method to anyone looking for an attractive and functional way to store the foil cards.
