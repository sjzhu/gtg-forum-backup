Welcome, all, to a most thrilling spectacle!  11 teams, representing among them all the heroes of the Multiverse, will face 15 battles with nefarious villains.  Only time will tell who can survive this devastating gauntlet and claim incredible victory!  

The teams are as follows:

Team 1: The Harbingers- Road Warrior Mainstay, Dark Watch Expatriette, Void Guard Idealist, The Sentinels, KNYFE

Team 2: The Prophets- Omnitron-X, La Comodora, Lifeline: Blood Mage

Team 3: The Fateless- Malpracticd Medico, NightMist, Dark Watch Setback

Team 4: The Keepers- Spirit Akash’Thryia, Legacy, Freedom Five Wraith, Heroic Luminary

Team 5: The Elements- Prime Wardens Xtreme Tempest, Harpy, Freedom Five Absolute Zero, Haka

Team 6: The Third Wheel- Ra, Fanatic, Requital Captain Cosmic

Team 7: All Creatures Great And Small- Void Guard Writhe, Naturalist, Sky-Scraper

Team 8: The Rebels- Supply and Demand Benchmark, Unity, Freedom Six Tachyon

Team 9: The Stubble- Best of Times Chrono-Ranger, Prime Wardens Xtreme Argent Adept, Action Hero Stuntman

Team 10: The Santa Clause 4: Who The Dependent Clause Is- Santa Guise, Visionary Unleashed, Bunker

Team 11: The Altered States: Scholar of the Infinite, Dark Watch Mr. Fixer, Fugue State Parse

 

Each team will face off in a series of 15 matches against the same villains and environments.  The last three matches are Vengeance mode.  In both the solo and Vengeance segments, the difficulty will increase, match by match.  Any loss will result in elimination.  Round 1 is Apostate at Dok’Thorath.  Stay tuned for details, and speculate on the winners below!
