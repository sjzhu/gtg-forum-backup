Hello,

With the very last set of Sentinels of the Multiverse being Kickstarted right now- the story of the Multiverse will be coming to an end. I figured a fun and low key way to celebrate the end of an awesome game would be to campaign the whole thing. I spent the last week compiling a list of everything in the game- so we can campaign the whole story chronologically.

I looked through a few other groups campaign and decided to keep it somewhat simple as far as general rules.  I noticed alot of them had away rules and return villain strikes- but I decided to bypass those and use Unlocked Variants as return villain strikes instead.  We are going to use the concept that first game is on normal difficulty- if the group wins the next villain begins on advanced difficulty.  If the fight is lost than the next battle is reset to normal- including a direct re-match.

We will have to defeat all of the (core) villains in a set to unlock the next set, variant characters, and environments. Every currently unlocked villain must be defeated to unlock the next set.  We will try to capture all of the villains right up and into the release of the final set. Every defeated villain is captured and cannot come back unless it has a variant form left. When a villain is selected- their hero nemesis is automatically drafted into the battle. If we lose the battle that hero is lost and cannot return unless in variant form. If there is no nemesis available- open select the heroes for the game- just know if the group loses a dice roll determines the captured hero.  Defeat all of the villains and win, lose all of the heroes and it's game over!

I am attaching the list I compiled - the list is an attempt to approach things chronologically overall- but that is easier said than done. I integrated The Cauldron and some of the Yoshi material into the list as well so I just chose points in where those characters would be introduced into the Multiverse.  If you choose to use the list- just know it is not fully finished pending future information that may come out in OblivAeon and in The Cauldron Storm Fall- so you will have to update those yourself.

I just wanted to put this out there if anybody wanted to put it to use / continue it on their own.  I appreciate any input but please know that I will not be updating it further on here.  Enjoy!
