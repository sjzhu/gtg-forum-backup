There was a time when I couldn't play against Spite. There was just too much to remember with all of the drug affects. I'm past that now, but feel similarly towards Kaagara Warfang. If I play against her enough, I'll eventually get the handle of what all those titles do, but for now they are nearly impossible to keep track of. The video game version doesn't help as much as I'd like, as I have trouble figuring out _why_ various effects are occuring. It would be awesome if there was an in-game log that not only listed everything that's happened but explained why it had happened. A lot to ask, perhaps.

 

So I was going to vote for Kaagara, but, yeah, a five villain V+ game would probably be a lot more taxing. 

 

Honorable mentions to The Ennead and to Akash'Bhutah, particularly if played with an environment with a lot of targets.

 

Heroes: The most taxing ones are the ones that give powers and plays to other heroes on their turn, so Argent Adept, Scholar, GI Legacy, Captain Cosmic come to mind. Guise is his own kind of taxing, as one tries to understand just what the effects of his cards are. I'm actually quite unfamiliar with many of the more recent variants, so I can't properly note exactly which variants might be the most taxing for me.

 

I actually have little problem with Smoke Bombs. It just hasn't been that hard to remember to invoke it or to figure out who is the highest and lowest -- we're constantly having to determine highest and lowest anyway for various damages being dealt. What _has_ been tricky with Smoke Bombs is remembering that it's only villain damage that gets redirected.
