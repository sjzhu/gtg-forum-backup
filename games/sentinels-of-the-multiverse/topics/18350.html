A question cane up in a game tonight that I couldn’t quite answer. We were fighting in the Bloodsworn coliseum, when Nightmist cast oblivion. She flipped two fours. The question is this, she gets one favour for hitting multiple villains for more than two. 

But how many does she get for hitting for four or more damage?

She did six separate instances of 4 damage. The hit multiple for 2+ specified in a turn, but nothing on the card said anything about the high damage only triggering once. 

So, 2 favour (1 for hitting multiple, 1 for a 4+) or 7 (one for multiple, 6 for 6 4+s)?

You get a favor every time a hero target deals a non-hero target 4 or more damage, so 7 is the correct answer in that case.

Also, both of those fours count, since she essentially hits the villain targets twice. I'm assuming there were only three, or some combination of destruction and/or Dymkharn.

Also, she can pick up the Indiscriminate easily, if that's an option. :B Just for the sake of completeness.
