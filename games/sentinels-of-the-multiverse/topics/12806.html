With Naturalist's promo, if you have a form card in play you can activate a second form with your power.  That activated form lasts until the end of your next turn, so your next turn you can choose the third form and play Primal Charge using all 3 abilities.

Bestial Shift is actually not as good with the promo most of the time, because you really want 2-3 powers in play, and that isn't as easy to pull off with this Naturalist.

Promo is fun, but you lose a lot not being able to pull a damage taken -1 or +1 damage dealt from your deck or trash.
