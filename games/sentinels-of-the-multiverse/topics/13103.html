I recently beat Advanced Spite with Expat, Mr Fixer, Unity, and Absolute Zero.

Fixer was MVP with his Hoist Chain and Riveting Crane.  I never drew Salvage Yard, but with the milling drug it would have been fantastic.

Unity was the second MVP.  Stealth bot can absorb Spite's mass damage (although once his damage boosting drug comes along, you'll need Hoist Chain or Stun bolt or Liquid Nitrogen Rounds to completely block it), and she once her bots get out she can stop using her powers while still dealing massive damage.

 

The only problem I had was with the environment.  I was fighting in the Checmical factory, and I lost Stealth bot at one point protecting my Raptor Bots from a Checmical Explosion.  The first explosion I dealt with using a Bee Bot, but I didn't have another one ready in time for the second explosion.  That could have gone badly, but I had enough DR on Spite that turn that none of my Raptors died, and I was able to resurrect my Stealth Bot next turn using Robot Reclaimation.

 

Play with Fixer, Unity, and Wraith against him and you'll crush him easily.

#14

Using a combo of Visionary and Ra, you can make even Advanced Spite a complete joke. Just get out Twist the Ether on Spite and have Ra constantly activate Flesh of the Sun God; this will make all of Spite's damage into fire damage and your team immune to such. This also does wonders against Plague Rat, but doesn't work quite as well since he causes heroes to damage each other.

I also find Tachyon really helps; when he gets out his "return H-2 hero ongoing or equipment cards", I often have her play HUD Goggles and Push the Limits, and let them return to her hand. Sure, it limits what she can do, but since she can play them "for free" each turn, it's often worth it. Especially Push the Limits, since it gives an extra card draw, and since it damages Tachyon at the beginning of her turn, Spite is almost helping you in a way when it returns to your hand.

Chrono Ranger is always great against villains with few targets in their deck, and Spite is no exception. Once you get a few bounties out, he'll really start to deal damge, especially with the Masada.

And as a general tip, don't bother saving victims until Spite is about to flip; since they all take an investment to save, it's essentially wasted if Forced Entry comes out. Once the last drug comes out, you may well want to consider saving them for the extra damage against spite.

Pulseglazer:

Advice? Play a better designed villain. That means literally anyone else.

While I understand your point I think it's important to clarify a bit. Spite's mechanical frustrations can stem from a lack of (H) involving one of his big mechanics: his Victims. Forced Entry puts ALL Victims into play, and blows up a Victim after they're all put into play. Is Good Samaritan the only Victim in play? If he triggers a Forced Deployment he is dead and nothing you can do can save him. His drugs are impossible to avoid with enough time and some are absolutely dreadful to deal with. After he flips he basically becomes a damage dealing escaped animal you need to zookeep back into his cage since he doesn't play cards and blows up the environment every round.

I definitely have some frustrations with Spite, but I think it's important to provide some context and conveyance of why those frustrations exist. For me, anyway.

Like others said, damage reducing or preventing effects are really important. Deck manipulation can help a lot to make sure his pace doesn't get too crazy by avoiding Drugs and Lab Raids when possible (shunting them to the bottom of the deck with Visionary or Parse, for instance). Victims are a distraction. Don't be a hero. They will more than likely hurt you more than help you in the long run. If he flips be sure to remember he increases his own damage but also prevents some of it between all of his Drugs when he takes damage through the Safe House.
