I would strongly encourage the Greater than Games team to consider this. Both are perfect games and should be shown and shine on Tabletop and deserve the honor of being on this show. Plus both fit in the qualifications for season four according to Wil Wheaton's blog:

http://wilwheaton.net/2015/08/tabletop-submission-criteria/

1) Must be in retail - Check and Check

2) optimally four players - for SoTM this is perfect, for Tactics this is perfect if they do a secnario, which is probably the best part of this game

3) no Simutaneous play - check and check

4) balance of strategy and luck - I would say both games have this. SoTM with forming the right team and getting the right cards, both hero and villian wise. Tactics has this with the dice rolling and knowning what powers to have down when.

5) Game must look good and produced with quality materials. - SoTM check, tactics has this but they will probably need to  be played with figures since it is a tv show

6) 90 minute game - Never had a 90 minute game of sentinels if I dont play any of the more difficult ones. Sometimes tactics can draw out but not by much. also needs to be cut down to 30-45 minutes. should be doable.

7) season 4 is more family gaming - Lots of familes on here play both from what I can tell.

All it takes it looks like is an email and then possibly later down the road sending 2 copies of the game to them. But getting that Wil Wheaton bump that a lot of other games has produced a lot of sales so it is worth it.

Also based off a tweet it looks like he likes the deck building game too so that could be a good option as well. oh well just do all 3 :)
