The way you'll have them is in the oversized pack: on one side of the Infinitor variant, it will say something like "Tormented Hero"; the Kismet variant is something like "Unsubtle Trickster."

They were originally only released in the (updated) oversized pack; Adam eventually did small art for them to match the other "normal" villain character cards. If you've got small character cards matching those descriptions,  you've got them.
