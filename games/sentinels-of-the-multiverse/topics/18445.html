So I didn't think much of this when I got my game- I was mostly just excited to have everything. The Oblivaeon booklet and Scion cards are identical to the versions found in the Oblivaeon expansion, only with the foil effects added. At first I assumed that I had just missed or forgotten a mention that the scions would be the same as their non-foil counterparts, and they do look pretty cool still, so I wasn't really worried about it. But then I was going through the Kickstarter updates again, and I found this:

https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/gtgames/sentinels-of-the-multiverse-oblivaeon/posts/1512909

Any of the cards that can be found in the Oversized Villain Card Pack on our website will not have new art - Adam just redid all the arts for those cards in the last year. However, Adam WILL be doing unique art for all of the other villain character cards. Which means 10 new arts for Vengeance, 22 new arts for Villains of the Multiverse, AND 4 alternate arts for OblivAeon and 20 alternate arts for Scions. (Those numbers might seem twice as high as what you might expect - I'm counting two arts for each character card: front and back.)

Emphasis added.

I'm not seeing this discussed anywhere else, so... Did I miss another post later that rescinded this? If not, does anyone else have this problem with their foil set?
