@Skippy: Here's a weird challenge I've been wanting someone to do for a while: Traitor Sentinels: The Traitor Sentinels Game


Choose 5 heroes and a villain/environment of your choice. At the start of the game, shuffle the heroes and pick one at random. That hero must spend their turns dealing as much damage to their team as they possibly can. The other heroes play as normal; they can attack that hero or ignore them, they can assist or resist him. That one hero, however, must treat the other members of the team as if they were the enemy (not mechanically; they still have to follow the rules of the game), and cannot deal ANY damage to the villain.

Report back once you win a game this way!

Traitor Sentinels, the Sentinels Traitor Game!

Word had come in, Haka was awake, and while he couldn't talk, he was able to get a message through, "save her".

The Freedom 4 had never seen Haka like this, Fanatic and Tempest where nowhere to be found.  Maia was able to track their movements, it seemed that Haka, Tempest and Fanatic had travelled to Atlantis.  Captain Cosmic and The Argent Adept were still involved in negotiations off world, and could not be reached.

"This is it team, we don't know what we are facing down there, but our friends are in trouble, and we are going in."  The Freedom 4 was ready for anything, or so they thought.

Upon arriving at the underwater ruins they were greeted by a familiar face, Helena seemed a bit off, but was inviting, and assured them that Tempest was okay, but when she led them to him, they found him in chains, wierdly runed devices causing him pain.

"What is going on here?" Legacy asked.

"Let the master explain," Fanatic answered.

"Yes, let me explain," came the voice of Bezeliel, the demon known as Apostate.

"Well, now we know what happened." Wraith said matter of factly.  

"We're going to pass on that explanation," Tachyon yelled as she zoomed towards Tempest.

 

Apostate in Ruins of Atlantis vs. F5 LEgacy, Wraith, (traitor) X-treme Prime Warden Fanatic, Super Scientific Tachyon, and GI Bunker.

 

I thought this would be a lot harder than it was, F5 Legacy started with 2 Bolster Allies and by turn 3 got the third one.  We got amazing cards and shut down Apostate.  Fanatic got 2 chastise, which were immediately destroyed by Tachyon (chastise on orb is not cool) and Martyr, and got to use Smite twice.  Hypersonic Assault and Throat Jab crushed Apostate's damage while 2 Kraken and Panzer Buster took care of all the relics.

Once Legacy got Superhuman/Fortitude/lead (I was counting Fanatic as a villain target because otherwise things broke) the danger was pretty much over.

Final health:  Legacy 22, Wraith 18, Fanatic 22, Tachyon 15 and Bunker 17

The battle started quite poorly. Kismet's opening turn involved two Karmic Disjunctions, each of which revealed at least 4 Jinxes. So, thanks to the Talisman, her damage output increased ludicrously. The first few turns were a slog. While I did play the Caliginous Form on the first turn, three of the Sentinels got almost immediately incapacitated. Mission Control was the first Room to appear and it played an inappropriate Mists of Time that swapped Nightmist's 2-card trash with her all-the-cards deck, stalling her card draw further. About the only bit of luck was that Expatriette's opening weapon was a Submachine Gun, which specifically aims at Non-Hero targets, and she had Hollow Points in hand.

Then Kismet's luck began to turn around with, appropriately enough, Surprising Fortune. Setback accelerated Nightmist's card draw and she was able to put on her Amulet and thoroughly lock down Kismet's deck with three Mistbounds in a row. Mister Fixer started laying on a reliable beat-down, and the Sentinels brought out a number of cards that aim for Villain targets. Or they gave Mister Fixer Dark Delusions - whatever worked!

In the end, I got this challenge on the first try, though I reallllllly did not think that would be the case!
