Sometimes a villain just has to go down.  What team do you use for each in revenge games?

 

Akash - Argent, Fanatic, Haka, The Scholar - Argent and Fanatic take care of enviornments, while Haka and Scholar deal with limbs.  If it goes long and something goes wrong, you want the Scholar all set up to tank the game to victory.  Fanatic tossing limbs early can be huge.

Ambuscade - TL Tachyon, Parse, Chrono Ranger, Knyfe - hit hard and focus fire.  Tachyon gives cards to the big guns.

Apostate - Parse, Young Legacy, Ra, SS Tachyon - This has enough damage to eat through the early trouble.  SS Tachyon can whittle down anything with really fast, big hits, while this game ends up being about ignoring relics for a timely Sucker Punch to finish things off.  

Baron Blade - Visionary, Wraith, Tempest, Haka - Hit fast and keep the wrong cards out of play/have a way to mess with his discard pile.

Bomber Blade - TL Tachyon, Wraith, Tempest, Ra - Hit hard early, stop him from playing the wrong card or dealing damage with Tahcyon and Wraith, destroy ongoings, and if all else fails, kill Tempest and you win.

La Capitan - unsure yet!

The Chairman - Visionary, Mr. Fixer, Nightmist, Haka - This team solves most problems. Visionary can throw out the Chairman's discard pile.  Nightmist can redirect damage really constantly, especially with Vis' help and Haka can eat the nastier problems.  Fixer? He just solves whatever other issues you happen to have.

Citizen Dawn - Visionary, Wraith, Captain Cosmic, The Naturalist - Visionary and Wraith kill ongoings and control when you see a Devestating Aurora.  Cosmic ignores an Aurora that gets through, and Naturalist... well, he hits stuff as a Croc mostly, but I like that he recovers his stuff fast and has the option to tank.

Deadline - unsure yet!

The Dreamer - Visionary, Parse, Mr. Fixer, Haka - just handle problems, kill stuff, and be patient.  Argent fits really well here,too.

The Ennead - Parse, Wraith, Tempest, Ra - Try to control how many Ennead you see, damage them all at once or have the option to focus fire HARD.

Gloomweaver - Unsure yet!

Voss - Wraith, Visionary, Omnitron-X, Ra - Since he won't destroy your stuff, you might as well take advantage.  His damage is predictable enough for Omnitron, while Ra's fire immunity can be huge.  Wraith and Visionary ensure the timing of Forced Deployments is to your favor.

Infinitor - Tempest, Visionary, AG Legacy, Nightmist - Lock his deck up, hit everyone thing you need, and whittle away.

Iron Legacy - Parse, TL Tachyon, Fanatic, Tempest - All ongoing hate and two alpha strikes to make him dead. If you're in trouble, kill Tempest and be immune to melee.  Parse can be replaced with Nightmist.

Kaagra - Legacy, SS Tachyon, Haka, Sky Skraper - lots of 4 damage, lots more multi-hit. The crowd loves it.

Kismet - unsure yet!

The Matriarch - Legacy, TL Tachyon, Wraith, Tempest - 4- ways to stop the psychic damage and plenty more to just punch Lillian to death.

Omnitron - Parse, Haka, Ra, Fanatic - Damage boosts, ongoing hate... this shouldn't require a special team.

Cosmitron - Wraith, Legacy, Ra, Haka - A bit of damage boosting for the early game, a bit of immunity from late for Legacy.  Wraith slows down some cards that can really hurt.  Ra and Haka kill things.

Plague Rat - Visionary, Tempest, Fixer, Sentinels - Tempest heals, Fixer redirects badness to villains, Sentinels with a signature turn infection to healing, and Visionary makes sure the worng person isn't infected and gets folks the cards they need.

Progeny - unsure for now.  

Spite - If waiting him out - TL Tachyon, Scholar, Nightmist, Absolute Zero - Get Scholar and A/Z optimum builds with Tachyon who is set up for a Barrage upon flip.  Nightmist should have a million cards at her disposal, too.

If not waiting - Parse, Omnitron-X, Wraith, Chrono Ranger - Smash face.  These guys set up fast.  Parse and Wraith help to avoid bad drugs while still bringing the pain.  Omnitron gets the right plating and the right components and he should be safe to beat down.  Chono just makes a lot of damage happen, including some out of turn.

Thoughts? What are your teams?
