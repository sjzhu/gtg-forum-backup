I'd say people are on the right track looking for nothing but "just damage" characters who can't manipulate decks/destroy non-Targets.  However, some characters in that category are amazing - and Legacy, in particular, may well be the most powerful in the game.

 

I'll try to avoid any "control" effects as well, but my real priority is to find heroes who are setup dependant - I've had really effective games with Unity and AZ (I've had each of them win games near-singlehandedly, in fact), but they're also more inconsistant, since they need to draw the right cards, in the right order, without the villain breaking those cards too soon.

Bunker (Freedom Six)

Expatriette (Dark Watch)

Absolute Zero (Termi-Nation)

Mr. Fixer (Dark Watch)

For the last slot, I can't quite decide between Chrono-Ranger (base) and Unity (Termi-Nation).  They each have a tiny amount of stuff destruction, and they both tend to be better at their jobs once they get going than the other four.  Even without setup, Ranger is actually pretty good at single-target damage if there's no reduction about, whilst Unity has some of the highest damage in the game on a good day.  Even still, these characters have much more limited options than many others - any team made using these six can break things, but lacking so many options, they'll lose more Advanced games than they win.

 

Hurm.  I guess I should try a few villains with them, see what I can do.  Let's start with... Citizen Dawn (Advanced).
