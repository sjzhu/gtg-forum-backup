For completeness I'm copy/pasting my answer from BGG too.

Yes it's correct. Yes it makes sense.

There was a big debate in the playtesting forum on the wording and that is what they have gone for. The debate was about who did the discarding to power the Haka card, the recipient of the bonus or Haka himself, the current wording makes it clear(er) who discards.

Example

Prime Warden Haka uses his Guardian power to play a card. The card he plays is Haka of Battle

Haka of Battle reads "Draw 2 cards, then discard 1 or more cards. Increase the next Damage dealt by Haka by 1 for each card discarded this way."

Prime Warden Haka draws the 2 cards.

Prime Warden Haka discards 1 or more cards.

Prime Warden Haka can chose any target, Hero, Environment or Villain, to receive the bonus damage from the discard on his Haka of Battle card.

Example 2

Prime Warden Haka uses his Guardian power to play a card.

The card Prime Warden Haka plays is Vitality Surge

This is not a card with Haka in the title so only the hero Prime Warden Haka can gain 2 HP and draw a card.
