I'm a huge fan of SotM, ever since discovering it, it has rapidly climbed the ranks to sit as my second favourite board game right behind BSG. I've bought every physical release except villains, and have always had fun playing it with my friends.

 

However, recently the majority of my gaming group all moved, in most cases out of the country. I've found there is a Vassal module for it, but it's stripped down and doesn't include a lot of the most fun decks. The steam version appears to be single player only.

 

I really want to be able to keep playing this with my friends, and I really, really want to be able to keep playing in a way the devs are happy with.
