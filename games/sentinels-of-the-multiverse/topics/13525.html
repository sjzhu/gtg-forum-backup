I 'beat' Advanced Ennead here with Legacy, Parse, and Wraith.  It's possible, although improbable.  And all other characters can deal non-projectile non-melee damage.  (Well, Setback can't except to himself, but he can redirect villain damage, which works just as well)

 

I just did the math for the AZ, AA, Bunker vs Matriarch matchup mentioned above, and it's easy.

 

Round one:
Horrid Cacophony x2, Impale, Syncophated Onslaught (Bunker & AZ), Flak Cannon [4], Toxic Sludge [5].  Matriarch at 61.

Round two:
Darken the Skies x2, ([3] from first Impale), Impale, Xu's Bell, (onslaught Bunker & AZ, [1]), Grenade Launcher (cannon [4]), Second Toxic Sludge [10].  Matriarch at 43.

Round three:

Carrion Fields x2, ([6] from Impales), Cold Snap, <some ongoing or equipment>, (onslaught Bunker & AZ, [1], Turret Mode [9], Dr. Tremane ([10] from sludge).  Matriarch at 17.

Round four: Darken the Skies, Hugin (destroy AA's extra card), [8] from AZ's start of turn, [1] from AA, [9] from Bunker.  Matriarch is at -1.


And I didn't even bother to try complicated shenanigans with AA.

Round four is the sweet spot for the Matriarch.  If you deal 70 damage to her before round 5, then (as demonstrated) you can basically ignore her deck.  Infinitor is probably going to be harder.

Wow.  I tried this a couple of times, and it is brutal.  I'm pretty sure that it is winnable, though.

 

Assuming all decks are stacked to make this winnable:

The simplest way to do it would be to bring out all the underbosses as fast as possible (Using Informants, Rook City is Mine, and Scum and Villany), and get the Chairman to reshuffle while all the Underbosses are in play.  Then Oblivion them all to hell, and let Nightmist sacrifice herself (doing as much damage as she can on the way).  Use Grease Guns to mitigate damage while the underbosses are in play.  When the deck reshuffles, put both Thieves on top.  One of DW Nightmist's incap abilities is to put the top card of a trash on top of that deck.  So, an Aimed Hairtrigger Reflexes, combined with that ability can completely lock down the Chairman.  While Expat is constantly Aiming, she's dropping guns for a finishing Unload, and Fixer is beating on his nemeses.  You're limited by the size of the environment deck, but you should have plenty of time to take down the Chairman and the Operative.  The only concern is the Operative damage from killing the thief over and over, and the Chairman's reaction damage, but those are both small enough that you should easily outpace them.
