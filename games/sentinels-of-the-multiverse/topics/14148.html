I've finally had to accept reality - there are just too many cards in this game for me to store them all in my cutesey custom card boxes.  The amount of room they took up was acceptible before given how much I loved the boxes, but it's been reaching the straining point for a while, and now it's broken through.  So this weekend I got a 4-lane white baseball card box and all the cards in the game fit nicely in there stored vertically, separated by the custom vertical dividers jagarciao and tracerbullet have created, which I've got hosted on my site.  They actually only take up three of the lanes so I can fit some tokens and oversized cards in that third lane, at least until the final expansion comes out.  No idea how many/what types of cards will be coming in that.

I'll still keep posting templates for card boxes (when I get the art) for people who still use them, and I still think they're the bee's knees, but now I have a box full of unused boxes in my den.  It's a fitting meta-tribute to a bygone era when decks had boxes and the living was easy. (sob)
