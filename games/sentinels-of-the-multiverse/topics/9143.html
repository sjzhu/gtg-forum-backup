I just got my package from >G today (by the way, thanks guys!).  While Jules will be happy with the Mr. Chomps, I'm more interested in the Dark Watch promo cards.  So, I'm going to do a little thinking-out-loud...

Mister Fixer

Okay, I love Bitter Strike.  It gives Fixer a great boost in offensive capability, at the cost of keeping his stuff around.  I can see all sorts of combos with Scholar and Argent Adept, letting him get fully set up before unleashing massive damage all over the place.  

The front art reminds me of some of the 90s Daredevil or Iron Fist comics, which fits well.  I'm wondering where that golden skull-temple is in the SotM universe.  On the back, who is that?!?!  I don't recognize this fellow at all.  I thought he was Spite, at first, but the red mask isn't the same.  Maybe we're seeing how Fixer got that scar in the first place?

Nightmist

I like the look of the art on this one particularly:  Nightmist looks exotic and powerful on the front, while looking utterly hopeless on the back, alone against Gloomweaver.  I'm on the fence about her power:  it'll mean a genuine shift in the way I usually play her, which is pretty awesome.  I'll hopefully give her a shot within a week or so.

Expatriette and Setback

Oh, man!  These two have to be talked about together, given their incapacitated side.  Are we seeing the first ever in-team hookup?!  I think we are!  Also, is that Argent Adept on the back of Expatriette's incap side, fighting some flying villain (with red energy around his hands)?  It looks like AA just jumped off of a Mobile Defense Platform...  More details are necessary!

I like Expatriette's new power.  While it slows down her set-up time, it definitely increases her overall damage output and makes SMG so much more useful in the long-run.  Hollow Points + Aim + SMG?  Whooo....that's some pain.  I can't speak much to Setback's power, as I'm not a playtester, but it definitely seems more defensive than offensive, so that might be a thing...

Spite

This is definitely a neat take on Spite, with lots of opportunities for players to get the jump on him on the back, if you manage the risk-reward ratio well.  He hits like a Mack truck on his flipped side, made especially rougher for the fact that he goes after the lowest HP hero.  That definitely makes Wraith's Smoke Bombs useful in a fight with him!

Gloomweaver

Holy crap on a crutch!  People who are asking for a harder Gloomweaver, your prayers have been answered.  While his front side isn't hateful, you almost want to keep him there while you build up to deal with the utter devastation of his flip side!  H+2 damage per round!  H AND H-2 damage per round for everyone?!  Ow ow ow!  Very cool!

I've gotta say, though.  Both the art on Gloomweaver and Spite look decidedly creepy.  Spite looks like a combo of Ghost Rider and Solomon Grundy on his flip side, while "The Rotting God" looks suitably gruesome.  Awesome job on these!

Heres the way I interpret his (Setback's) incap side, and some guesses about what the future holds.

Setback is crippled emotionally, rather than physically.  What we are seeing is a combination of guilt and self-blame that is leading to an inability to do anything.  He's more seasoned as a hero (thus the costume and attitude change evidenced from his previous character card), but with that he's seen how badly things can go wrong around him.  He's trying to control his powers, and prevent them from hurting others (probably by putting himself at risk).    I'm thinking that he blames his lack of control for Expatriette getting shot (and killed- more on that later).  In his mind, that blast was meant for HIM, and his powers made it hurt the one he cared about most.  If he'd been faster, or had more control over them, or hadn't hesitated- she'd still be alive.  But she's dead now, and it was all because of his powers.  

On the back of his card, he's in that space where he thinks that if he moves, if he does ANYTHING, someone else is going to get hurt. He can't trust himself, and he can't trust his powers, and the love of his life is dead, and its all his fault.  He's his own worst enemy, and always has been.

 

NOW- here's what I think about Expatriette's incap:

I've thought for a long time that Expatriette has some sort of resurrection-based component to her powers (I had a whole theory based around the flak vest soaking up infinite damagbe meaning that she died and got back up while thinking that it was super effective).  

I think that she took that hit deliberately.  She knew it was going to kill either her or setback, and she made her choice.  I think that at this point in th estory, she suspects that theres a chance (maybe she believes it fully, or maybe she hopes, or maybe she knws exactly how it works) that she'll recover.  But I don't think that she's told that to Setback- maybe because she's scared, or because she doesn't fully believe, or maybe because she doesn't want to be judged by her powers. Or maybe she DID tell him, and he's out of the fight, desperately hoping that it comes true.

She'll get back up.  Maybe the trigger is moonlight (since a lot of her stuff that shows her getting shot takes place at night), or maybe its dusk. I think that she'll get up stronger and faster than ever before, and her powers will finally awaken.  Maybe this is the first time anyones actually manage to KILL her instead of grieveously injure her, and that was the trigger all along.    Once she's back up, Setback will be right alongside.  His life is a series of ups and downs- and while this is certainly a low ebb, he just needs to see that it wasn't his fault.

I think that Expat's story is a trilogy, and that her incaps are her defining moments- the  places where the story breaks and the next part begins.

So her first incap likely marks the beginning of her transition into DW Expatriette- the place where she starts to step back and think instead of rushing in guns blazing. Maybe its where she lost her eye, maybe its just a defeat by a gang of citizens.  Either way, I think it marks a place where she stopped being a loner, and started being a part of something larger.  

Her second incap marks her transition into someone new, and I think that it heralds her rebirth as someone of equal power as Citizen Dawn.  

I think that the third part of her story is about confronting her mother on equal terms.  I don't think that Expatriette is going to be inclined to accept any offers from Dawn. Dawn was shaped by prejudice, and surrounded herself with an army.  Dawn's entire life has been shaped by prejudice, and she has embraced it's reflection by hating the normals.  Exaptriette could have gone the same route (hating superpowered folks), but I think that her story is all about acceptance.   She starts out getting a rush from taking down supers, but over time she starts to abandon that attitude and take a much different path from her mother.  She has friends instead of followers.   She falls in love and doesn't kill the guy when things don't go according to plan.

 

As for Setback (and yes, I totally see a Spidey vibe), I think that his story is one of acceptance as well.  Its about accepting that he HAS powers, and ultimately that he can exert some control- but not total control.  Once he accepts that there is always going to be an element of chaos in there, he's going to be at his strongest.  He and Expat are linked in a lot of ways, and they are traveling down the same road.  fortunately, I think that they bring out the best in each other.
