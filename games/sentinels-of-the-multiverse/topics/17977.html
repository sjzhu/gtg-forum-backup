I've been working on a spreadsheet to generate random games for a line-up of 10 heroes (choosing 3 at a time) and figured I'd share it in case anyone else was interested in trying it. (I hope it's okay to post here.)

 

The idea is that if you choose 10 heroes, you end up with 120 possible combinations of 3. I'm trying to win a game with each of these combinations, going up against each of the 24 villains currently in the iPhone app 5 times each (3 on standard, 2 on advanced) and using 20 of the 21 environments 6 times each. (I ditched Megalopolis, partly to give all locations an equal amount of usage and partly because I find it super-frustrating.

 

I've tried to give clear instructions on the spreadsheet itself as to how to use it. (If anything is unclear, please let me know and I'll alter.)

 

Probably going to post session reports in this thread, assuming that's an okay thing to do. I'm currently 4 games in, having gone 3-1, and it's been really interesting trying different combinations of some of my favourite heroes.

Unity does some damage with bots, and gets Stealth Bot in play, Setback uses Exceeded Expectations to hit Akash’bhuta, knock Turret Bot down to no longer be highest non-villain HP, and get rid of the raptor pack - unfortunately bringing out Ensnaring Brambles and Living Rockslide.y
