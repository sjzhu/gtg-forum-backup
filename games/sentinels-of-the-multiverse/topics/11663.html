My groups tend to use similar logic for choices.  Some people I play with prefer to stick to only 2-3 decks that they really like.  Others like to try ones that they haven't played recently.  Some folks just want someone they can use to cause straightforward destruction after a frustrating day at work.  I tend to choose based on what I think the group needs after others have picked, though there are times when I've chosen decks because there was a particular character I really wanted to play.  Occasionally we've gone with a randomizer app, but we generally prefer to go with player choice.
