Looking at Akash's deck again, Primordial Seeds can't be a means to get Primordial Limbs into play, ala Unity.  Her variant has her Primordial Seeds in play do damage, so they have to stay in play.

Also, I strongly suspect that Akash has some way, probably multiple ways, to shuffle or stack the environment deck.  Otherwise, if you have to put Seeds into the environment trash, it would take far too many turns for anything worthwhile to result ( waiting for the entire environment deck to cycle ).  Either the ability would be too weak for a base power, to the point of uselessness; or else the Seeds are so awesome  that a ten turn delay is "balanced", and the game completely breaks if you have any means to speed the process.  Either way, not good design.  So, she either has other ways to get Seeds into play, or she has ways to cycle/shuffle/stack the Environment deck.
