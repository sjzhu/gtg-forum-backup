Infinitor vs Young Legacy, O-X, Tachyon, and Nightmist in the Block

 

Close game, overwhelming victory?  

 

By the time we had removed half of Infinitor's health, he had decimated our team.  Legacy, O-X, and Tachyon were dead, and Nightmist was at 1.  But then Nightmist went into her Mistform.  And at that point, we were all but guarenteed victory.  I could have just stayed in Mistform and let him kill himself, but that wouldn't be fun.  I healed back to full (courtesy of Starshield Necklace), and drew my entire deck, and stacked my trash with 4s.  The Block guards were out in force, preventing the inmates from getting any toeholds.  A few rounds later, and Infinitor was dead as a doornail.

 

Favorite moves:

There were two this game.  The first was that the Block and Infinitor managed to work together to flip him back to his front side while Nightmist was in Mistform.  That was entirely without the help of the heroes.

Secondly, the game ended after a massive Oblivion.  I'd stacked two 4s, Elder Ring was out, and Young Legacy's incap had boosted my damage.  Infinitor took 18 damage and nothing else survived (except Nightmist, who took no damage).  Then Nighmist played Mistbound, and proceeded to kill Infinitor on the next round.  Lockdown was in play, and the game ended with Infinitor totally locked down and controlled.
