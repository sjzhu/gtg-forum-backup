After playing around a bit in the videogame, Nightmist loves the Realm of Discord. Consider:

Mistbound can keep a helpful Distortion out an extra turn or two -- alternately, it can bind the villain deck and make Time Flies a huge boon for the heroes. Even better, if you have two copies in your hand, you can do both -- give yourself a guaranteed extra round of double plays and draws, and keep the villain in the lurch.

The vast majority of her cards are One Shots, so Ghostly Images is all but guaranteed to let her play something useful -- and even if it doesn't, she can turn that self-damage on someone else.

With her healing, she's got a really good chance of being the hero target with the highest HP, so the Ethereal Bonds will go after her -- meaning she can either neuter the villain target of her choice or spend a round playing utility cards like Enlightenment or Planar banishment without the self-damage and Investigate for free cards. The latter is especially useful if there's something boosting damage, like Legacy or Imbued Frailty.

The same applies to Portal Fiend, which can become a huge attack toward the end of the game.

The one card she really doesn't like is Negative Energy Field, since it usually means that 1 hp of damage hits her before she has a chance to reflect away the self damage of cards like Heedless Lash or Oblivion. But aside from that, she synergizes really well with the environment's cards.
