Christopher mentioned that you cannot defeat OblivAeon until you figure out how to do so. I know that the whole Legacy thing has been kind of a buzz word lately in the gaming world, but I think it would be really cool to "unlock" certain hidden packets in the game based on what you've done so far in your plays, a la Risk Legacy. Could you just tear into all of the envelopes straight out the gate? Of course, but where would be the fun in that! 

Examples for those who don't know Legacy games would be along the lines of: Open this packet when you defeat your second Scion; or, Open this packet after your first loss; or, Open this packet immediately when a Hero target would do more than 12 damage in a single hit. In each envelope would be a little story card, but several new surprises, like maybe an entirely new deck.

I really, really don't want the final expansion to be a Legacy expansion.

I loved Pandemic: Legacy. It's maybe the best game I've ever played. But it's over now. I'm probably never going to play it again. Sentinels, on the other hand, is a game that I play over and over again, and to have the final expansion, the thing we've been building to for years, be something that we can only play a certain number of times, or even just get the most out of a certain number of times, would be deeply disappointing.

That said, the superhero genre is ripe for a Legacy game; heroes and villains change and evolve over the years, new threats appear, et cetera. It's something I've considered trying to design myself, though it's got such a large scope that I'd inevitably mess it up...

EDIT: Fortunately, OblivAeon: Legacy is very unlikely, not least because the idea for this predates Risk: Legacy.
