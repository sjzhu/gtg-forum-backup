You want a team that can do a lot of damage very quickly so that you can get rid of the boat as soon as possible. Characters that stop the villain deck from playing cards such as Nightmist and Legacy are also incredibly useful. Deck manipulation is also great against her, but only after you've destroyed the boat. Characters that consistently play multiple cards for multiple hits in a round like Chrono Ranger and Tachyon can also be very useful as a way to get rid of the cards under her when she's flipped.

La Capitan has limited destruction, she doesn't wipe the field, deal with that by getting a lot of cards out, ones you don't even need.  Protect the ongoings/equips you really want.

Get rid of the Boat immediately.  If you can hide it out of her trash so she can't get it back.

Prioritize the Crew.

In (H)=3 fights Siege Breaker and Trueshot are easier to ignore than take out.  Mabel is awful, you must destroy her.  Try to deal with others in ways that take less effort, cards like Chastise or Zealous Offense, Wrest the Mind, Smoke Bombs can take the bite out of her crew and let you focus Cap down.

 

My favorite hero to face her with is Visionary.  Suggestion to bury the boat, wrest for L'epieste, and her ongoing recovery and ongoing cards to sacrifice make her great.

If La Capitan started with about 45 HP, you could probably ignore her crew and just beat on her directly and win the damage race. The question then becomes how to get her down to that amount without losing too much HP yourself.

 

So, you slow her down by taking out her ship as quickly as possible and you prioritize the crew as mentioned above. If you have heroes who hit all non-hero, then you can focus on keeping the crew pain down while slowy whitling La Capitan to a low enough point that you can pivot and attack her while ignoring the crew. If you have heroes that build up to big attacks, then you focus on the crew until you can get a couple of big attacks that bring La Capitan to the point that you can pivot and focus on her.

 

If you've got deck manipulation, you keep her from bringing out the ship, the cards that bring out lots of crew, and the worst crew members. As mentioned above, you get out as many non-essential cards as you can so you can sacrifice them and keep your best stuff. When she flips, you flip her back as quickly as possible by pinging her with lots of small shots.

 

La Capitan is one of the most swingy villains. A bit of luck and or deck manipulation and she can seem too easy. A bit of bad luck, though, and you get swamped with crew and extra card plays.
