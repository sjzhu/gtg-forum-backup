Having completed my "Multiverse Saving Contest" I found it interesting to play a specific series of games to evaluate the effectiveness of the various teams of heroes.  I felt that experiment helped me determine which team is the most effective (at least the way I play them). So I figured why not run a similar experiment for the villains?

Here are the rules:

1. Video game decks only.

2. Other than the villain matches will be randomly generated.

3. Each villain will play 6 games: 1 regular and 1 advanced of: 3 Hero, 4 Hero, 5 Hero combinations.

4. The outcome of each game, heroes, and environments will be tracked.

5. Once I go through the 6 games against each villain once, I will decide if I want to repeat this process to increase the sample size.
