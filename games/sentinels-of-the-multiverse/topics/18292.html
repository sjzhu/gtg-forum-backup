It depends on the wording of the individual cards.

The Cosmic Weapon will work for the hero who has it because it's the weapon itself that gives the power. Destructive Response however would not "see" the Construct's destruction if Cap was in the other battle zone (even though it would end up in Cap's trash when destroyed).

Similarly Chrono-Ranger's By Any Means would increase damage dealt to the target no matter where Chrono-Ranger was, but The Ultimate Target would try to trigger the extra power but if Jim isn't in the the same battlezone then it wont "find" him.
