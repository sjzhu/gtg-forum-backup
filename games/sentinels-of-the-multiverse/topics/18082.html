What card, how, and why?

I would change Setback’s Uncharmed Life to:

Whenever a hero target other than Setback would be dealt damage, you may redirect that damage to Setback.  If he takes damage this way, gain an unlucky token.

Power:  Setback regains 5 health.  Destroy this card.

 

The normal version of this card has two issues.  The first portion is too niche and too expensive to be used most of the time. The second portion is too expensive for its effect.  With the above changes , Setback has another token generator (something his promo card could use!) and when the redirect is too painful to maintain, you can recover a good amount of health.

This card would combo well with Surprising Fortune, especially at low incoming damage values.  It also would make Friendly Fire a friendlier option, as Setback could use Friendly Fire twice, then activate this card for a net gain of 2 health and 4 tokens for two plays and a power.

Thematically, Setback getting hit instead of someone else is still bad luck for him, and he should gain unlucky tokens from it.

I'd like to take a moment to speak up in defense of Devastating Aurora.

Having this card in one deck in the entire game is great. Make it an Ongoing, and it's Apocalypse, which I generally laugh at.

But That Card? It's That Card for a reason! It's the kind of card legends are built around!

You know what you're getting into against Dawn. Maybe she'll wreck your crew in the middle of the game, when you were just about to turn things to your favor. Maybe she'll play both in the first two rounds, setting you scott-free for the rest of the game. Maybe you'll never see it all... except for that one time Setback discards one off the top of her deck and you all breathe a sigh of relief and shake his hand for being so darn clumsy.

Devastating Aurora is punishing, yes, but it's exciting. Plus, it's thematic that she destroys more stuff than Progeny because who won in the Cosmic Contest? That should've been our clue. :V

Granted, if there's a card I could change, it'd be Sedative Flechettes. Tons of damage on top of a board wipe is just not fun. :/ Technological Singularity is nasty, but at least it's thematic. I'd rather the Flechettes did, say, (H)-1 to all, or 3 to the (H)-1 highest, and destroy one ongoing for each hero dealt damage. A little bit of setup destruction keeps a villain from being, well, Ambuscade, but that card's ridiculous and I hate it.
