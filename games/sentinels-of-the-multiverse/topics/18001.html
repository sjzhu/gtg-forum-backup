I play Sentinels at least once a week on the videogame, but for Tabletop Day, I wanted to get out the old cards.  I was playing with my mom, who hadn't played in a while, and I had just listened to the GtG origin story extrasode where Christopher said everyone's first game should be against Baron Blade.  So even though it wasn't quite her first time, I decided we should fight the Baron.  Should make for a fun, simple game, right?

My mom decided to play as Ra, because she sort of remembered using him before and chose Atlantis as the environment.  I played as Mister Fixer and the Scholar, because she wanted to see how the Scholar worked.  Normally I don't think Atlantis is as difficult an environment as people say, but today it was pretty rough on us.  But the real killer was Blade's two Living Force Fields and a Backlash Field and realizing we had picked a team with no ongoing destruction between them.  We managed to get Blade down to 1hp on his front side before we lost.
