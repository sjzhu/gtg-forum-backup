We've been playing Sentinels over lunch here and there, and we've finished maybe 1/3 of them before the "time limit" hit and we had to go back to work. I've been trying to figure out what matchups we can plan for that might make for shorter, but still fun, games.

We took down Mad Bomber Blade in 30 minutes with 4 players, but we couldn't finish regular Blade in an hour with 3. 4 players couldn't finish Kismet, but then, that was two of the players' first game.

I know some villains seem to take longer than others. Matriarch, Chairman, Ennead, etc. But if there are some villains or villain/environment combos that are more likely to finish in roughly an hour, it'd be awesome to start out with those.

We can leave the game up if we don't finish, but if we're not guaranteed to finish it the next day, things get awkward, as people are more likely to need that table space for something else.

And I know Iron Legacy can be super-quick, but I do want a few villains to fight that we could win against from time to time. ;)
