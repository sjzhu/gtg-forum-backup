Akash'Bhuta has grown more powerful of late.  So Visionary, The Wraith, The Sentinels, The Scholar, and Guise banded together to protect humanity.  They were not afraid.  Even at her Ultimate power, Akash'Bhuta can still be stopped!

Or can she?  Our fair heros were met with a Primeval Eruption.  But this was no ordinary eruption - on and on it went.  Three times did the tangled limbs of the dread avatar emerge from the ground.  And then our heroes felt the earth grow fast around them.  They were Entombed!

But the heroes stood fast.  They had chosen the ground for this fight carefully, and they had prepared well.  Hope was not lost!

 

Dark Visionary, Rook City Wraith, The Adamant Sentinels, The Scholar, and Guise must face Ultimate Akash'Bhuta.  After a triple Primeval Eruption, all limbs and Entomb are in play.  You may choose which environment you use, and stack the hero and environment decks as you will.  You can stack the Villain Deck as well, because it makes planning exact sequences easier and this team has too much deck manipulation for me to stack it against you.  You may use as many rounds as you want, but unless I screwed up the math it is possible in one.  Yes, really.

Have fun!

ogseamus:

Every time Guise regains 1 HP, 3 targets regain 1 HP, every time Scholar regains HP he deals 3 targets 1 damage.

As long as Guise uses every third heal on himself he can go on until he reaches max HP. As long as for each time Guise heals himself Scholar targets Guise and each time Scholar heals he damage himself neither will ever reach max HP and the loop can continue.
