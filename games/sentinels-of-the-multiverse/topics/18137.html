In the most recent Extrasode of the Letters Page Podcast, Christopher and Adam opine that Akash'Bhuta is a fight that cannot be rushed.

I say nay!

What is the fastest you forumites can come up with to defeat Akash'Bhuta? Assuming any H, any environment, Regular Akash'Bhuta, and the perfect deck ordering/shuffling you desire. (Note, Yes, I know about the Fixed Point/AZ or Mr. Fixer and Setback shenanigans could get it in one round, but how else would you do it?)

My first thought would be something along the lines of Legacy, Rook City Wraith, FV Tachyon, Chrono-Ranger, and Scholar of the Infinite at Insula Primalis. First round Akash'Bhuta plays Allies of the Earth, Legacy plays Inspiring Presence, Wraith plays lots of Impromptu Inventions and pulls an Obsidian Field from the environment, Tachyon plays lots of bursts to get ready, Chrono-Ranger gets some Bounties out, and Scholar plays lots of Don't Dismiss Anythings to further set up the heroes.

Environment plays another Obsidian Field, Akash'Bhuta plays Phlanges. Second Round Legacy Galvanizes, Wraith starts unloading, Tachyon starts unloading, Chrono-Ranger starts unloading (literally), and Scholar does some shenanigans with Forms.

Environment plays another Obsidian Field, Akash plays another Phlanges, and the Heroes proceed to mop up with a lot of +5 damage hits, espeically from FV Tachyon Blitzing.
 

Can't do the exact math at the moment, but I am pretty sure the environment wouldn't get a third turn. Thats pretty fast :)

For non-challenge mode:

Hunted Naturalist, PW Fanatic, Chrono-Ranger, Fixer, Science Tachyon in the Celestial Tribunal.

Akash begin with an Eruption, gets some boring Phalanges.  Naturalist slaps her with 2 Eyes (1 from play, 1 from power).  Fanatic plays Focus and powers into EoD.  Start of Chrono’s turn: 4 damage.  Chrono hits her with a bounty and another 4 (8 total).  Start of Fixer’s turn: 5 damage (13 total).  Fixer plays Crane Style and uses power for another 4 damage (17).  Start of Tachy’s (22).  Fanatic has 1 card left.  Tachyon chains 2x Lightning reflexes into a Nimble Strike and 2 Supersonic Responses- 14 damage (36).  She sciences on Akash, revealing 2 more eruptions.  Between the two, all limbs are now in play, as are 2x entomb.  Fanatic’s focus triggers before EoD so another 5 damage (41).  Then, Akash hits herself for 174 (12,12,12,13,13,13,14,14,14,15,15,15) and dies so hard that Akash’thyra is put into play by the Celestial Tribunal.
