I know. I know this won't work. You don't have to tell me. But I'm going to beg anyway.

The world we live in is filled with people and actions that are, on their best day, morally ambiguous. And it's incredibly draining. Sometimes its hard to live in that world. For years now, I've loved the Sentinels universe because, at least the way I've read, watched, interpreted and loved the universe, it's not that way. The good guys are good, and the bad guys are bad. Except when the bad guys are REALLY bad, and then even the bad guys are good ;) Nobody has embodied this for me more than Legacy. Somehow the >G crew has managed to stuff all of the good-heartedness, earnestness, do-the-right thing-iness of Captain America and Superman into Legacy, with (at least for me), none of the campiness, cheesiness, or outright jingoism that either has, at times. Maybe it's the fact that Legacy is a Dad, and so that makes some of the earnestness, patience and desire to see the world as a good place a bit more understandable, I don't know. Seeing Legacy art just makes me think that the world could be a better place.

But, for me at least, no art that I've seen in Sentinels more embodies this than Legacy on "America's Cleverest Legacy". I can just see Legacy sitting down to do the crossword in the living room, still in his costume, but having put on a shirt and his glasses to minimize the "Legacy-ness" a bit. In walks a teenage Beacon, who earnestly (she is a Legacy, after all, even if she is a teenager) rolls her eyes. "Daddy! Can't you change out of your costume? I have FRIENDS coming over! It's EMBARASSING!" Legacy looks up, confused for a second as to how this could possibly be an issue, and then says "But, sweetheart, I'm SUPER at crosswords, I have to wear my costume!" All while smiling that America's Cleverest Legacy smile, with you left wondering if he is just taking the chance to good-heartedly needle his beloved daughter some, or if he could really, actually believe that.

Maybe it's just me - but I love seeing Legacy this way. That's why I'm asking, even though we've already been told "no", no, begging that you reconsider making America's Cleverest Legacy a real-life promo. Even if you have to change his (admittedly pretty over-the-top) power. I'd just love to see that part of Legacy, the part that I've always imagined, actually be a part of the in-game universe. Even if he just showed up with this art in some other card, not in a promo. Please, please use that art - for me it just sums up all of Legacy.

Thanks for listening,

BLM
