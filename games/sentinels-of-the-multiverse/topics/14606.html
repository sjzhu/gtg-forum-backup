Ironic:

Why those six?  No love for the Naturalst, the Scholar, or Chrono-Ranger?

 

Lots of love for all 3 of those heroes plus Sky-Scraper, Omnitron-X, the Southwest Sentinels and Parse (my favorite hero.)  I picked these 6 because:

  1. Ra is the only hero from the base game with only 1 promo, he is central to the OblivAeon story and Christopher answered to a question in the Q&A about Ra promo in the Q&A with " Perhaps. If you're good."

  2. The Dark Watch Heroes because giving each of them a promo would mean each hero from Infernal Relics or earlier would have 2. Setback's only current promo was released before his deck and I'm guessing Christopher might want to make a new one now that he has the deck to go with. Plus Setback's mechanic is so unique and awesome if I were a game designer I'd want to find a fun way to change his game up (same holds true for NightMist and Mr. Fixer).

  3. That leaves KNYFE which is basicly a shot in the dark. My reasoning was she's the hero whose goal was the most closely tied to stopping OblivAeon (other than Lifeline but he's 1. new and 2. already getting a promo with this expansion.

  4. The heroes you listed all got promo's with Villains. Chrono's sparked lots of debate and its pretty hard to come up with another idea for what a Nataralist power could be be thats not similar to his existing 2.

 

Of course, I would never have guessed additional Prime Wardens promos or a Heroic Akash'bhuta so psychic powers aside I don't really put too much stock in my own insites into the mad genius that is Christopher.

edit: Also I would love those "Be The Gate" Heroes but there are 7 of them which makes them seem less likely to me.

  • Ambuscade added face-up trap cards.
  • The Ennead added villains with multiple character cards.

  • The Tomb of Anubis added the prizes to give players extra abilities from outside their deck.
  • Khagraa added a whole extra deck outside the usual Environments/Heroes/Villains types. (I don't know if the Title Deck technically counts as a villain deck, but still, you get the point.)
  • Spite added the Safehouse and Drugs.
  • Sky Scraper added swapping character cards.
  • Unity added a hero based around cards she couldn't directly play.
  • Chrono-Ranger and Captain Cosmic both added Heroes based primarily around playing cards into other areas (bounties for CR, constructs for CC).
  • The Temple of Zhu Long added the idea of an environment hiding cards in your deck.
  • Celestrial Tribunal added bringing in cards from the box to the current game.
  • Completionist Guise in the box looks like adding specialist tokens, along with whatever the heck they're used for.

That last one is key here, because swapping cards within a deck is basically promos plus. It's one of the few mechanics they haven't toyed with yet.
