I also haven't listened to her episode yet, so this is me spitballing based on the Chairman's episode.

If I recall correctly, the Bear was a more hands-on boss, and the Chairman didn't emerge to take over directly until the Bear fell. So as an Operative, the Bear needs to be more in control. So here is my thought:

The Operative [Bear] - 15 HP

"At the beginning of the game, place an Underboss under the Operative. Cards under the Operative are indestructible, are not targets, and have no text except for their end of turn text, which still activates as normal. Cards under the Chairman are indestructible and have no text."

If an Underboss comes into play, place it underneath the Operative and play the top card of the villain deck.

Whenever a Thug is destroyed, the Operative deals the target who destroyed it H-1 Melee damage.

If The Operative is reduced to 0 or fewer HP, place a random Underboss from beneath the Operative to beneath the Chairman. Then, if there are (H) or more Underbosses beneath the Chairman, flip the Operative and the Chairman. Otherwise, restore the Operative to 10 HP, reveal cards from the top of the villain deck until an Underboss is revealed, and place it beneath the Operative. Place the other revealed cards into the villain trash. 

At the end of the villain turn, the Operative deals one hero target X melee damage, where X is the number of Thugs in play. 

FLIP SIDE - THE END OF THE BEAR

When this card is flipped, place all face-down cards under the Chairman into play.

Reduce damage to Underbosses by 1.
