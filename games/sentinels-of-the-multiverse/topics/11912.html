The goal of this puzzle is simple.  Destroy as many targets as possible in 1 turn.  You may choose any legal setup, but you get to stack every deck the way you choose.  (I recommend that you avoid shuffle effects, however I'm no longer sure that the max is possible without reshuffling, so stacking the deck when you reshuffle is now allowed).

 

I haven't actually solved this, but if I've done the math right then I know the max possible, and I think that it's achievable.  EDIT: actually, I misread a card.  I'm no longer sure that the max is achievable, but you can still get pretty close. EDIT II: Now I think that it's achievable after all.

 

All answers and hints should be labeled as such, and then hidden as white text.  They should not be quoted, as that will reveal them.
