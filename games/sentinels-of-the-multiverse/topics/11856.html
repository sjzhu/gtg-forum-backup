So the Prime Wardens vs. advanced Akash.

It might have been a challenge except that opening hand Tempest had Resilience, his armor, and hurricane.  By his second card play of round 2 Tempest was at -2 damage (Energy Bracer, Hurricane, Otherwordly, Bulwark) taken and -1 to lightning.  He had Embolden, Dynamic Syphon, and Augmented Ally.

Adept was feeding him cards, and everyone was working to keep his cards safe.

Hurricane, his base power, 2 cleansing rain and Hailstorm saw a ton of use in this game, and he played 3-4 cards on almost every one of his turns.  It was insane just keeping track of what was going on.

His Syphon lasted the entire game because it was constantly healed by Cleansing Rain, and Captain got so many constructs in play (almost anytime the constructs took damage Syphon triggered and the rain fell) and the battle I think ended on round 7?  I can't remember well because it was crazy.

The best is using Reclaim and then playing the car you reclaim, then doing it again.  In the same turn.

Most rounds Tempest was getting in 7-8 powers and playing a total of 6-10 cards.  It was crazy.
