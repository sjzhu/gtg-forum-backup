I think that I've got it.  With a round to spare, actually!

 

The important thing is not how you kill them, but who you kill.

 

I've included the general idea below in white. 

The key is to kill Set as soon as possible.  Set's incap effectively gives you an extra half-turn every turn, and helps you really pump out the damage.

 

If you want more advice, this is what I did.  Other routes will give you victory, as long as you're getting those extra plays off of Set:

 

Don't worry about Legacy's ring and charge - just get out inspiring presence and then Smash, Strike, and Thokk your way to glory.

Wraith can use her three Inventive Preparations to get almost fully set up in the first turn.  I went with Razor Ordinence, Throwing Knives, Microtargetting Computer, and Utility belt the first turn and dropped Combat Stance on the second.  Then I just Throat Jabbed until those were out, and then dropped random equiptment in anticipation of an inventory barrage at the end that I didn't actually end up needing.

Parse also sets up quickly with a double Syntactic Analysis, which I used to get Snap Decision, a Critical Multiplier, and Recompile.  Then I Recompiled twice more, then and started wailing with Between the Lines (for Wraith) and Impossible Shot.  I also tossed in another critical multiplier, which probably should have gone before the second two recompiles, but whatever.  I was hoping that with Isis's Incap I could draw out Parse's entire deck and force a reshuffle, which would have given me back the Syntactic Analyses and the Recompiles (which would have been 1 play for 27 damage if the Critical Multipliers were all out), but they turned out not to be necessary.

[color=white] For Villain plays, I started out with The Grave Beckons and Death's Grasp, with the occasional Sun's Fury thrown in for maximum Combat Stancing.  None of them do very much damage, none of them harm your board, and purple tends to trigger the least harmful Ennead effects.

[/color]
