Modulize, if it does Destroy a card in play to let you play draw and use a power in any order, isn't that powerful.

  1.  "If you do" means you have to destroy a card or the power does nothing.  No freebies.

  2.  The cost of the power's effect is a card in play and a power.

  3.  The power's effect lets you play a card, replacing the one you destroyed at the cost of a card in hand.  It also lets you draw a card, replacing the card in hand loss.  It then gives you a power to replace the one you spent on Modulize.

So at the end of using the power you have the same number of cards in play, in hand, and can use a power you would have used instead.

So it essentially lets you swap out a card for a different one for free.  There aren't a lot of times you want to sacrifice one of Bunker's cards.  Gatling Gun is an easy target if you are running low on cards to feed it, or Ammo Drop when you have a full hand, but often the cards you have in play are there because you want them there.

(copied from playtesting)

You have 5 power cards that will help you get Golems into play, both are awesome, as 1 let’s you discard to put one into play, and the other gets you 2 into play.  You have 2 cards (flash forge) that will let you search for those equipment, it will also let you discard a golem and put it right into play that turn with Reconfiguration.  So that is actually 7 cards that will let you get a golem into play turn 1.  (Flash Forge is actually the best card in the deck for this promo)

At 7 target cards your odds of having a card that lets you play a golem are 55% first turn, 71% at 6 cards, and 91% at 11 cards.

Original Unity had 12 cards that would let you get a Golem into play first turn, giving you: 78% first turn, 90% at 6 cards. (not counting other player's equipment)

Ouch. That is a big difference. (all odds assume Golem in hand, since innate powers do not affect those odds)

 

You lose a good deal of cards that can get you that initial Golem, and gain the awesomeness that is Flash Forge with this power and a fallback of draw a card if everything else on the power fails.

Also any other effect that can make you discard early can help you get going.

I like that Golem Unity gives you the easiest path to putting golems in play, but her sustainability and recovery aren't good, Regular Unity has good odds of getting golems into play, better sustainability and the same recovery, Termination Unity gives you the worst hand to play odds, but with crazy good recovery and improved sustainability.

Something non-obvious about the new Unity - if you don't have any golems in your trash and don't have any golems in play, you can still activate her power to draw a card. As people pointed out, the draw is not conditional on the destroy succeeding, but it's also not conditional on the "move into play" succeeding.
