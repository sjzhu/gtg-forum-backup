Sorry I'm coming late to the party, but as a previous owner of an earlier version of the Enhanced Edition, I wanted to add a few things that caused me to drop $40 on this third printing.

1. The third printing's cards are cut to the correct length.  Earlier cards were cut just a bit longer at the top than they should have been.  I've heard this has made issues with sleeving when using "perfect fit" sleeves (although I don't sleeve so I can't verify this).  The difference was also noticable when making the support deck of un-used hero character cards for the two-player variant that included hero cards from expansions.  Also, I ordered replacement cards for the re-printed errata cards (see point 3), and I couldn't use them with my earlier version as the lengths were different.

2. The box of the third printing is of the correct width.  Earlier boxes were made just a bit more narrow than the expansion boxes, which I heard caused issues with storing sleeved cards (again, I can't verify this)

3. The errata cards are included in the third printing.  About 4 of the cards, which were re-printed with errata (ex. Fanatic's "Chastise"), are included in this version.

Just sharing in case anyone else with an earlier version might benefit from this info.

Thanks
