As I mentioned in another thread, I've been interested in playing around with Fugue State Parse's logo a bit.  I don't love the design I came up here with as much as the Luminary one I did, but I like it enough and think it's an improvement over what's shown on the Kickstarter currently, so I thought I'd share it.

I tried various ways to incorporate the wall of code we see on her original card's incap side into the design but found that in just about every case all it did was muddy things up rather than improve it.  I finally settled on having it in the background of the top of the card.  I think it delivers the wall-of-code effect I was looking for without being too obtrusive back there.  It also helps to hide the bad clone stamping I had to do in Photoshop to remove the text from the art available on the Kickstarter. :)

I also wanted the "fugue state" tag line to focus on the overlapping nature of how I think Parse sees reality now, as if she were Neo viewing the code underlying the world at the same time she views the world itself.  That was hard to do without making it unreadable, but I think what I ended up with does what I was looking for well enough.

I didn't incorporate arrows into the design at all.  That would have felt too literal and I don't think it needed it.

Hope you like it.
