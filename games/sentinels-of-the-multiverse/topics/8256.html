Some of the best moments in SotM is when the cards just seem to keep coming, everything lines up, and you nuke the enemy into oblivion. This got me thinking: what is the absolute maximum damage a character can deal to any given generic opponent without help from anybody else? This is the result.

I simulated the conditions as follows:
No help from teammates. Any increase in damage must come from themselves, not teammates or the environment, or even the Nemesis Bonus.
The villain may oblige by hitting or damaging the hero as the case may be, but the damage type will be nonspecific (I'm looking at you, AZ), as well the amount of damage itself.
I counted damage dealt to only ONE villain target. No minions, just the big bad. I also didn't count damage dealt to self.
I used only the base card, no alternate arts, for simplicity's sake.

 

I, of course, recognize that some heroes are not meant to deal damage, but I included them in the experiment anyway, just for fun. I am also, of course, only human, so any comments, suggestions, or changes are welcome :) If you will notice, I don't have any of the ST or Promo heroes, so I couldn't do this for them. Anyone who has them is welcome to do it. Additions are awesome!

Assume that La Comodora has two cards in her deck, a copy of Run Aground and both Flintlock and Cutlasses in her trash, and two Harnessed Anomalies as well as the second Run Aground in hand. She also has a copy of Maria Helena’s Plot, Chronological Sweetspot, and Combat Timing, along with ONE Concordant Helm and all her other equipment (15 cards) with the Figureheads last in line. The other Helms are in the trash.

Start of turn: she lets all the portals on her ship close and fuels Chronological Sweetspot. All Equipments except for the Concordant Helm are destroyed. For each of the first 12 equipment destroyed, she deals 1 + 2x Figurehead boost damage to one target. The first Figurehead then causes 2 damage, and the last causes 1. Total: 12*3 + 2 + 1 = 39.

Draw: La Comodora draws the two cards in her deck.

Play: she plays Harnessed Anomaly, shuffling Run Aground into her empty deck and playing it. She shuffles 15 equipment cards into her deck, and deals one target 15 damage. (Leave two Concordant Helms, to prevent the limited keyword from interfering with the next step.) Total: 39 + 15 = 54.

For the card play on Harnessed Anomaly, she plays Weigh Anchor, putting all her equipments back in play and emptying her deck again.

Power: using her base power, La Comodora puts that copy of Harnessed Anomaly she played earlier from her trash under the top card of her empty deck, making it the only card in her deck.

End of turn: first, the Chronological Sweetspot goes off. La Comodora reveals her Harnessed Anomaly, choosing Flintlock and Cutlass from her trash. She deals one target 2 + 2 and 2 + 2 damage. For the extra card play, she plays the second copy of Harnessed Anomaly from her hand, choosing another Flintlock and Cutlass from her trash. She deals one target 2 + 2 and 2 + 2 damage. For the second Anomaly’s extra play, she plays Run Aground out of her hand, shuffling the last 2 Concordant Helms into her deck and dealing one target 2 + 2 damage. Total: 54 + 20 = 74.

Then La Comodora’s equipment effects trigger. The two Cannon Portals do 2*(3 + 2) damage to one target and the two Brig Teleporters do 2*(1 + 2) damage. (Subtotal: 16.) The two Temporal Rigging cards let her use the powers on the two Future-Tech Deck Guns, dealing one target 2*(1 + 2) damage. For one of the Deck Guns, she targets herself and would do 3 damage, but Combat Timing prevents it and La Comodora deals one target 2 + 2 damage instead. (She can also throw some damage at other targets here, if she wants. Subtotal: 10.) Total: 74 + 26 = 100!

For some extra support to other heroes at this point, she can now have Timeless Treasure award extra card draws or move the Paradoja Figureheads around. And the best part is, this being the expert time traveler La Comodora, she should be able to repeat the majority of this on her very next turn.
