I'd highly recommend (basing off of running VotM demos at Gencon):

You: run all the villains. This lets the players focus on their heroes.

Villains:

Plague Rat (the indiscriminate damage makes for some very interesting choices)

Greazer (Unique mechanic, lots of style, puts the pressure on a hero... Also has some VERY funny interactions with Plague Rat if the rat touches the hair!)

The Operative (goes after lowest, very easy to run, lots of style)

 

for 4-5 player games, add in:

Miss Information (screws with heroes in fun ways, and everything she does tends to have some sort of positive tradeoff- she takes away stuff, but usually gives something else)

Citizen Hammer and Anvil (Super easy to run and keep track of- you might want to pull Bastion position from their deck if you don't want to use an attack-boosting hero like Legacy. Bastion position can slow down the game, but it also shows off how well the villains can sometimes work together.)

environment: I'd recommend Magmaria- it tends to be kind of neutral, and it's one of the new ones that lets the heroes do some cool things.
