So, my friends and I played a game against a Cauldron villain (fan made expansion) called Gray, who was basically radioactive. We were in Insula Primalis, playing the Prime Wardens. After a round or two, I realized we were basically playing the Planeteers. 

Haka was Earth, being strong, durable, but still has a good heart. Like Kwame.

Fanatic was Wind; basically she can fly and is a bit temperamental. Like Linka.

Argent Adept was Heart; though he is not a physical in nature, is there as moral support, making others feel better. Like Ma-Ti

Captain Cosmic was Fire, creating great forms of energy , which can get out of hand with an explosion. Like Wheeler. Which I find funny that one is American and the other is British. Close enough?

Tempest was Water. I don't know why. It was the only one that didn't fit. :P

We won, but we were missing a green-mulleted blue-metal skinned speedo wearing eco-wizard.
