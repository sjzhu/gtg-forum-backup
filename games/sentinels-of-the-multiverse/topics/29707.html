Hello. I’ve been playing DE games with friends, and then trying the same game on my own in classic Sentinels to see how different it feels. All of the games we’ve been playing have been on advanced.

In general, the DE games have all been really close, and we only win due to a lot of ingenuity and a little bit of luck. The classic games have been tricky, but much easier in general…until today.

Advanced Akash’Bhuta vs Legacy, Haka and Bunker (in that order) on Magmaria is really hard. In DE heros were down to roughly 15 hp after the second villain turn. We pulled out a victory, but primarily by taking advantage of the environment and doing legacy/haka combos.

In classic, it seemed really easy at first. Akash didn’t seem nearly as threatening, especially after Legacy blocked all the melee and projectile damage. I got her down to low 60s, and then she played Allies of the Earth. In one turn she healed herself to mid 90s, killed Bunker, and used ensnaring brambles to keep Haka from fighting back. She’s healing faster than I can damage her, and we have no ongoing destruction.

To make things worse, the environment wont let me keep Legacy’s ring out, so I can’t block Entomb.

Can anyone else beat her with this setup?

Also, I like games that look like they will be easy that turn out to be really tough. Does anyone have any suggestions for these types of games?
