Hey, gang!  Long time, no see!  The summer's been kind of busy, so I haven't been available as much as I'd have liked.  However, a friend of mine came up with a bit of a challenge.  You see, he hates Absolute Zero.  Won't play him, doesn't like him.  Loves complex characters like Argent Adept, Nightmist, and The Naturalist, but he just doesn't care for AZ.  And, while I'm more of a fan than he is, I did have to admit:  "If I could make one change..."

So, that got me thinking:  if you could change ONE CARD from each deck, what cards would you change?  I'm not arguing that the decks aren't great as they are; just a matter of, if you were to "revise history", how might you do so?  To get us started, here are my thoughts so far:

Absolute Zero and The Wraith:  Quite simply, I'd swap the text on Impromptu Invention with that of Onboard Module Installation.  The Wraith is already absurdly versatile without Imp Inv, and Absolute Zero's greatest weakness is his long set-up time.  I'd love to be able to see him operate in a world where he could get down his starting modules in one turn.

Legacy:  I'd make Fortitude (rather than Next Evolution) unlimited.  Let him be the tank we all know he can be.  

Bunker:  I'd alter the Mode cards, such that you'd only lose out on one phase--Recharge Mode would let you *either* Play or Power, Upgrade Mode would let you *either* Power or Draw, Turret Mode would let you *either* Play or Draw.

Fanatic:  I'd add the following text to Undaunted:  "While this card is in play, Fanatic deals +1 damage to all targets."

Haka:  I'd alter Enduring Intercession, such that Haka would take -1 damage from Environment Targets while in play.

Tachyon:  I'd alter her base power:  "Reveal the top card of your deck.  Play that card, place it into your hand, or discard it."

Ra:  I'd add to Wrathful Gaze:  "If you destroyed a target this way, Ra deals +2 damage to all targets until the end of your next turn."

Visionary:  I'd love to see her have some kind of damage buff, to make Demoralization and her damage one-shots more appealing.  Perhaps add it onto Foresight?

Tempest:  I'd probably nerf Localized Hurricane, such that it'd only draw 1 card, if not 0 cards.  

 

And, for good measure, a few of the expansion characters:

Expatriette:  I'd revisit Reload to make it more attractive with Speed Loading:  "Take all Ammo cards from your trash and put them in your hand.  You may play a card."

Mister Fixer:  So much of his deck is built to work well together, it's hard to isolate a single card.  Riveting Crane Style, however, always seems to be particularly situational.  Adding a damage buff to it would make it a much more attractive option.

Argent Adept:  Scherzo of Frost and Flame gets so little love.  Why not make it "All non-hero targets take one fire damage.  All non-hero targets take one cold damage."?

Nightmist:  I don't think I've ever seen anyone use Mists of Time.  I'd love to see it become something like a rotation:  "Shuffle your Hand.  Your hand becomes your new deck.  Shuffle your Trash.  Your trash becomes your new Hand.  Place your old deck in the Trash."  That'd be really hard to word, but it makes it a lot more interesting as a choice...especially if casting it can get your "desirable" cards back.
