Doing arithmetic on whether or not my sleeved cards will fit.

I have been using Fantasy Flight Clear Sleeves for Standard Card Games. These are listed as being 63.5x88 MM, but in fact vary. Still, measurements show that they will have no problem fitting into the 70x100MM space.

The remaining question is whether the four drawers will be long enough to accommodate them. For me, a hero deck takes up 27 MM, a villain deck takes up 18 MM, a V-style deck takes up 13 MM, and an environment deck takes up 10 MM.

Before OblivAeon, there were 26 hero decks. We are so far set to get 11 more, for a total of 37 hero decks. Each drawer is 435 MM long, so can hold 16 hero decks. If I'm willing to compress them to 25 MM, I could squeeze 17 hero decks in one drawer. There are two hero deck drawers, so I could get 34 hero decks into them. So, three short, but maybe there's room in the other drawers.

 

There are 23 villain decks and 15 V-style decks. I could fit 24 villain decks into a villain drawer, so the 23 villain decks would fit with 21 MM left over. The V-style decks would go in the 4th drawer with 240 MM to spare. There are 22 environment decks before OblivAeon and two more announced. Those 24 environment decks would take up ... 240 MM.

 

SO ... As of right now it looks like the box would hold everything but three heroes.

That also doesn't count the two spare copies of the Legacy deck I like to keep around just in case.
