Here's some numbers I did during playtesting.

Reconfiguration:

Using this promo you obviously have two problems:

  1. Getting your first Golem into play.

  2. Getting Golems into the trash.

You have 5 cards with powers that will help you get Golems into play, both are awesome, as 1 let’s you discard to put one into play, and the other gets you 2 into play.  You have 2 cards (flash forge) that will let you search for those equipment, it will also let you discard a golem and put it right into play that turn with Reconfiguration.  So that is actually 7 cards that will let you get a golem into play turn 1.  (Flash Forge is actually the best card in the deck for this promo)

At 7 target cards your odds of having a card that lets you play a golem are 55% first turn, 71% at 6 cards, and 91% at 11 cards.

Original Unity had 12 cards that would let you get a Golem into play first turn, giving you: 78% first turn, 90% at 6 cards. (not counting other player's equipment)

There are some allies that can work specifically well with this power.

1.  Argent Adept, Inventive Preparation:  can let Unity discard top of her deck.

2.  Captain Cosmic, Augmented Ally:  Discard a Golem to use Reconfigure to put it into play.  This one is awesome.

3.  Chrono-Ranger, "Just doing my Job.":  Let's you discard a Golem.

4.  Guise, Gimmicky Character:  works well for both.

5.  Setback, Cash Out, Fumbling Fool:  Good for both.

6.  Sky-Scraper, Micro Assembler:  Is amazing with this Unity.

7.  Super-Scientific Tachyon, Experiment:  if they don't enter play you still can get a Golem in the trash.

With regard to the wording of the power (or, indeed, any power), if the second part of the power was reliant on you having been able to do the first part, there would be the phrasing "If you do..." present. So something like "Destroy a bot. If you do, play a bot from your trash", in which case you would need to have destroyed a golem in order to put out another one. And as there is no use of the word "may" (as far as I'm aware), if you choose to use this power you must do all parts that apply, so you must destroy a golem if any are out, then you must play one from your trash if any are there, and then you must draw a card (unless you've somehow managed to get all of them in-hand/in play, probably unlikely with Unity ;)).
