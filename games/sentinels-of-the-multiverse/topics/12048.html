#1

Two teams of players (or 1 vs 1)

Winner of coin toss decides if they will choose a villain first or heroes first.

First team to choose a villain does so, specifying whether or not the match is on Advanced (probably always yes). The second team then decides if they want to choose the number of heroes or which environment will be used. The first team chooses the other. The second team then chooses heroes to use and attempts to defeat the chosen villain in the chosen environment.

If the second team loses, they lose the match. If they win, then the process repeats, but the second team now chooses the villain. Villains, environments, and heroes can not be repeated.

If enough games are played that villains, environments, or heroes run out, then the match is a draw.

 

What do you think?
