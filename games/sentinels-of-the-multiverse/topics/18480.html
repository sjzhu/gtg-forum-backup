Unfortunately, Greater than Games (as far as I know) has been a little unclear on what is canon in the 'main universe'. The trouble is, there are so many alternate dimensions (just like in comics), that EVERYTHING is canon. What's uncertain is what's canon in the universe where we start in the basic game. 

The Sentinels wiki has this site, but it's a little sparse: https://sentinelswiki.com/index.php?title=Timeline

Overall, I wouldn't play in that order. It starts with the Matriarch, one of the most difficult villains in the game.

 

Here are what I believe to be the 'easier' villains in the game, where you should start (basic versions only):

Deadline, Ambuscade, Baron Blade, Gloomweaver, Chokepoint, Apostate, Kismet

 

The easiest Environments in the game (can vary greatly depending on villain used):

The Block, Final Wasteland, Mobile Defense Platform, Dok'Thorath, Freedom Tower

 

The hardest villains, which should be saved until later:

Miss Information, Progeny, Vengeance 5, Matriarch, Infinitor, Kaarga Warfang, Dreamer, Iron Legacy, the Chairman, and Oblivaeon

 

The hardest environments are:

Rook City, Ruins of Atlantis, and Omnitron 4

 

Everything else is in-between. Also, I would avoid both Spite and Metropolis, not because they're the hardest, but because they can be the most frustrating. Depending on what order their cards come out, players can spend most of the game unable to either play cards, draw cards, or use powers without terrible consequences. Not fun.
