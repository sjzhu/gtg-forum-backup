Ok so, if there are only cards of which there are 1 or 2 copies, then there must be exactly 12 unique cards and 14 duoque cards (121 & 142).

If there's the option of 3 copies of a card then here's a table:

W is number of unique cards, X is number of duoque cards and Z is the number of trioque cards.

W |X | Y

13|12|1

14|10|2

15|08|3

16|06|4

17|04|5

18|02|6

19|00|7

Adding in the possibility of 4 of a kind (a quartique, if you will) means generating multiple tables, one for each value of Z, the number of quartiques:

Z=1

W |X | Y

15|09|0

16|07|1

17|05|2

18|03|3

19|01|4

Z=2

W |X | Y

16|08|0

17|06|1

18|04|2

19|02|3

20|00|4

Z=3

W |X | Y

18|05|0

19|03|1

20|01|2

Z=4

W |X | Y

20|02|0

21|00|1

(If Z>5 X or Y must be negative to compensate and you can't ship negative cards)
