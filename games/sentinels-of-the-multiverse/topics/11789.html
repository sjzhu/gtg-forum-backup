We've all had those games that have gotten totally out of control.  You feel like you're struggling to keep your head above water and meanwhile the villain is still at full HP.  The attached file contains a midgame set-up for a game such as this with Visionary, Absolute Zero, Legacy, and the Wraith vs. Advanced Citizen Dawn in the Ruins of Atlantis.  It is currently the start of the Wraith's turn.  Your mission (should you choose to accept it) is to defeat Citizen Dawn before the beginning of the Wraith's next turn.  In other words, you have 6 turns (Wraith, Environment, Dawn, Visionary, Absolute Zero, Legacy) to deal 80 damage to Citizen Dawn.

 

Designer's Notes:  I remember when I was younger doing these Magic the Gathering puzzles in Inquest or Scrye magazine where you have to accomplish some goal (usually winning the game) with the cards shown in play and in hand.  I've always wanted to see something like that for Sentinels and just finally found the time and energy today to make that happen myself.  I'm tentatively calling it #1 with the intent to maybe do more, but I'll admit that it was very mentally taxing and I'm still not confident that I didn't miss something in my solution that makes the whole thing insoluble.  If this is well recieved, I will certainly try to do more (maybe on a monthly or bi-weekly basis).  

I would rate this puzzle a 5 out of 5 in terms of difficulty.  I could probably have started with an easier one, but this is the one that came to mind.  The whole time I was coming up with the solution, I found myself wishing I had access to the Mega Computer that SotM: VG playtesters use.  If you don't have access to that, the next best bet is probably to set up the actual cards as I describe unless you are much better at keeping track of things in your head than I am.  

As a final note, there is certainly a way to do more than 80 damage to Citizen Dawn in the one round I've allowed, but my solution (again assuming I've done it right) leaves Dawn with exactly 0 HP.  

I'm going to press "Save" now.  I really really really hope I didn't miss anything in my solution...

The heroes roles in this puzzle are very similar to their roles in the actual game.  Wraith is needed for a few choice utilities, Legacy for damage boosting, the Visionary for villian control and Twist the Aether, and Absolute Zero is for crazy comboing for ridiculous damage.

 

The key here is understanding how ridiculous AZ can be with Twist the Aether and coolant blast.  Once you've wrapped your head around that, it's mostly a matter of fueling his shenanigans as much as possible without killing anyone, and juggling the numbers to maximize his effects.

Getting all the fuel sources for the combo nessesary to deal 80+ damage requires some creative juggling.  If you need help with that, go back up to Humble-Knight's hints.
