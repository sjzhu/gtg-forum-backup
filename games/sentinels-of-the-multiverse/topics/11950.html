What we know for definite:

A) SotM should be finished in about 2 years.

B) Everything should fit in Enhanced Edition-sized boxes (although we don't know how many).

 

So based on this and the fact that Villains of the Cosmos seems to be an EE-sized box, I'd say there won't be another large box.

Right now everything fits (unsleeved) in two boxes. A third box, apart from its own contents, could contain another two or three small expansions or maybe a small one and a large one. That sounds just about reasonable for 2016.

There just isn't time for a fourth large box in the two year timescale, especially as I believe that they won't release a large box without subsequent expansions planned to fill the empty space.

 

My prediction is that 2016 will bring us two small standard expansions or a single large one (in a small box), plus another set of three single-deck mini-expansions (hero, villain and environment). Everything will fit in three large boxes.
