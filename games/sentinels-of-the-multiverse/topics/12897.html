I had a conversation with a friend, and we came up with something interesting for entertainment. I was talking with my friend and we talked about who would win in a chairman vs matriarch battle. What I thought of is pit two villians against each other. All opposing targets are treated as Hero targets for the character, H is equal to the difficulty of the villian +1 (so H for baron is 2, while H for chairman would be 5, etc.). Take turns playing the top card of each deck and go until one villian is standing or meets any alternate losing conditions. I'ts not tested, so I don't know how well it will work, but it seems like something fun to do when I'm bored. I haven't worked out any kinks for advanced rules yet, so feel free to give me ideas. Environments is also not out of the question, just because of the villians that have cards that play around the environment.

 

Feel free to tell me any feedback, or if my idea is dumb. I am writing this while tired, so it could sound better now than when i wake up tomorrow.
