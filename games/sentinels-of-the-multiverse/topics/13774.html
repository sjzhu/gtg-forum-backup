When he builds up a certain number (3, irrc) of special Ongings (cataclysms, iirc), he flips, destroys the ongoings to deal damage and removes environment cards from the game. Then, as long as another cataclysm isn't played that turn somehow, he flips back the next turn. 

He tends to throw out cataclysms pretty quickly, so AZ is actually pretty good against Deadline for both the self-healing and his card that destroys up to 3 ongoings for damage.
