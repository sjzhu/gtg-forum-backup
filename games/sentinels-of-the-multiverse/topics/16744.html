While we have not seen all their cards yet, nor gotten complete oficial confirmations on all of these (I think), I believe this is the comprehensive list we have thus far. 1. Progeny (Kickstarter Update 6)

2. Aeon Master (Kickstarter Update 37)

3. Dark Mind (Kickstarter Update 37)

4. Sanction (Kickstarter Update 37)

5. Borr (Kickstarter Update 39)

6. Empyreon (Kickstarter Update 39)

7. Nixious (Kickstarter Update 39)

8. Faultless (Mentioned in several episodes of the letters page podcast, including Expatriette, Setback, and Mr. Fixer)

9. Voidsoul (Art done on Twitch Stream, also mentioned in the F.I.L.T.E.R. episode of the letters page podcast)

10. ???

 

So by my count, if we consider Voidspawn a confirmed Scion (please correct me if I'm wrong on that one), we only have 1 scion still unaccounted for. So my question is, who is this mystery Scion? Christopher has said the scions will be about half old characters and half new ones, so he/she could by either one (depending on how you count Voidspawn, since as far as I know he's never been named in the card game).

So who do we think this last Scion is? Let's try to figure this out!
