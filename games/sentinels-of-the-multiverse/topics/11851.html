Unless you get into some kind of tanky stalemate as the Scholar or something, games against Iron Legacy are generally over pretty quickly (usually around ten minutes when we play). If you're having major troubles coming up with a winning team, try and include Fanatic - End of Days can deal with most of his horrible ongoings and once you've been battered around a fair bit you can often one-shot him with Wrathful Retribution. Having someone like TLT along to help with card draw would be useful (though she'll go down pretty quickly due to the Nemesis bonus), and anyone else with ongoing destruction in case Superhuman Redirection (is that what it's called, I can't remember) comes out at a really bad time (like, right before you plan to go all Wrathful on him).

The important thing vs. IL is to buy rounds with cards like Mistbound, Throat Jab, Ground Pound, Grease Gun, etc.

Then find ways to turn damage around with redirection, counter-damage, and getting some irreducible damage on him.

He doesn't have much health, so getting his defense cleared and throat jabbing him can be a win.

The problem with IL is you have such limited time you need a few key cards or heroes that can pull what you need.

I love Sky-Scraper vs. IL, esp. paired with Fixer so you can hunt for Grease Gun and Jack-Handle and then IL is pretty much toast.

Jackhandle/Riveting Crane + Compulsion Canister is awesome.
