So we finally finished painting all the miniatures last week and they were beautiful. We took them down to so cal to play but when we opened the box they had kind of jumbled together. We picked them up and they were sticking to each other. After pulling them apart the paint came off of one and stuck to the other. So I had to touch them up and re seal, they don’t look as good anymore but still ok.

We used krylon gloss sealer and it was hotter when we were in so cal. Do you guys have any ideas about how to prevent this? We were thinking about making a foam tray thing for the box but don’t know if that will take paint off too.

Elmer's glue. Mix 1 part glue with 1 part water, then paint on after the paint is all dry. It goes on white and gross, but it dries clear, and makes a nice, solid shell that protects the paint nicely.
