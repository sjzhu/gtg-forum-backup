I teleport a lot, too, but not really to run away.  Instead, I tend to leave in Teleporter and Battlesuit and just try to remove scenario markers as fast as possible.  The scenario is basically a race, and your reward for incapacitating heroes isn't as valuable as the actions it costs to do so.  The exception is if I start the turn knowing that I can either get to the next marker with a move action rather than a teleport, or that I won't be going to the next marker this turn anyway, I'll put in kinetic redistributor in place of teleporter in hopes I might get an incap at some point without having to spend actions on attacking.  The way I see it, you just have to live long enough to remove your marker quota.  You can try to achieve this by either living longer, or removing markers faster.  I think the latter is more fun.
