When we demo it we have a coin toss to see who places first and the other goes first.  If you go first, you place second. 

For the tournament the starting locations are set up already and you do a coin flip for place first or go first, where place first gets first ban (I think).

For starting powers you get to choose two of them and put them into play.  During "Power Up" you can swap out one of your power cards in play for one in hand or if you aren't at your max you can put a power card into play.  (Main reasons for not being at max are:  Playing AZ, Playing Omnitron-V, or you were incapacitated recently).

At the start of the game, after you place, you roll your movement.  Then, during the "To Be Continued" phase you re-roll your movement.  If you are incapped, you re-roll your movement when your turn begins.
