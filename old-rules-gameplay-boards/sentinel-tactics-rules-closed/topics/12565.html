Q: Unity and 2 of her bots are in legacy's line of site when he starts passing out attack +1 tokens. Do the bots get tokens?

A: Yes

Q: Also do the bots Aim or does Unity aim and use it with a bot's attack?

A: The bots must Aim to use an Aim token. Specifically, Unity must use the Go Bots action to make the bot take an Aim action, and then must use the Go Bots action to make the bot attack, at which time it may use the Aim token. If the Aim token is not used, it goes away at the start of Unity's next turn.
