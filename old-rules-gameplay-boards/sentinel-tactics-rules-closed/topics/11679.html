For your very first match I would suggest a 1v1 with characters like Wraith, Bunker, Ra, and Omnitron-V.  Use Megalopolis, and get things like Line of Sight, Turn order, and how Aim/Dodge/Attack and Defense tokens work.

Note that the game isn't balanced for 1v1, but keeping it simple while you learn the basics of the game is a good idea.

From there move on to a bigger skirmish, 2v2 skirmishes are much more balanced, 3v3 are the best; or try a scenario.

Scenarios are almost never a straight up fight and involve different strategies than skirmishes, they are more objective based than fight based.

Baron Blade and Omnitron Scenarios are great once you have a pretty good idea what you are doing.
