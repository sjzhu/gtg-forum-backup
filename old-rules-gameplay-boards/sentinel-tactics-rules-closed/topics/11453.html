Living Force Field protects allies, but not itself. Two Living Force Fields can protect each other though.

 

Active player makes timing decisions when simultaneous events occur. If the active player attacks (and kills) the Living Force Field before attacking the Blade Battalion, the Blade Battalion won't have the defense bonus.
