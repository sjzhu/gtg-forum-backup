I don't think there are official rankings, but I'll take a stab at it.

This is based solely on my impression of new players being able to handle playing the character competently, mostly the more complex the options are the higher they rank.

FYI: I think Wraith and Bunker are the best two characters to learn the game with:  Between the two they cover the whole gambit of mechanics really well.  I like to give a new player one, then have them play the other next.  If they understand both of them they are pretty well set.
