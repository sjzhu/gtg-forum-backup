Reach does not increase Origin, nor does it increase Radius.

For vertices you count hexes, then a vertex of that hex.  It works like Omni-tron moving.

 

So Grenade Launcher with Artillery you would:

Count up to three hexes from Bunker, then pick your Vertex.

When you calculate range to the targets if your initial selected Hex was elevation 1, and a target on one of the other two Hexes was at elevation 4 they would count as being range 4-3=1.

 

If you counted Vertices it would be impossible to calculate range to the targets, esp. if the elevations for each hex was different.
