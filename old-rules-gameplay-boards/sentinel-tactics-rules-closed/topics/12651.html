1.  The shard moves to the hex with the target that was carrying it, and they are no longer carrying it.  They have to pick it back up.

2.  The next time that citizen takes a turn they stand up.  Not before then.

3.  That is a hazard space moving to a target, just like if Ra ran up to the target, it won't trigger that way.

4.  "On that line" refers to the one of sight from Unity to the bot.  The line of sight between 2 targets ends at the target.  Arcing shot will not extend past the bot.

morph147 said:

so on 3. If I thrown down a hazard on a space like living pyre the second it goes down it doesn't activate if somone is standing in it?

That is correct! Hazard spaces only trigger 1) When a target enters them or 2) When a target ends its turn in them. Causing a hazard space to be made manifest (like by playing Living Pyre), or moving a hazard space onto someone (like by running up to them with Turret Bot) does not cause it to immediately trigger.
