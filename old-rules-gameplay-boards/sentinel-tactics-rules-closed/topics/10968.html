#2

1. Correct.

2. You just know because it's next to and on the Tornado.

3. No, they did not enter the Hazard Space, so they are not instantly attacked. They will not be attacked until the move onto a Hazard Space or end their turn on it.

4. Yes.

5. It disappears when the card leaves play. If you play Blazing Tornado again later (note you cannot remove it and play it again on the same turn) it will once again be placed somewhere in Ra's current Line of SIght.

#4

1,4&5.  Blazing Tornado enters play when the card enters play, so you can spawn with it.  (really great map control to start)  If you swap out Blazing tornado, or the card leaves play the tornado goes away.  It comes back when the card is played, once again anywhere in Ra's line of sight.  As soon as it enters play the hazard spaces are active.

2.  Hazard spaces are unmarked, just have to remember to check what cards are in play, and remember that Tornado will burn you.

3.  No.  Only when they enter a hazard space or end their turn on it.

 

Tornado is mostly map control, it is great for stopping Vis or Unity from hiding out in advantageous places.  It also is great for melting AZ's Structures.

Spawning it on someone would seem to be legal.  I can't remember if that was a thing.
