I've been ruling that it did. It triggers off of entering a hex, or ending your turn on the hex. Being placed on the map is entering the hex just the same as moving onto the hex is. In my games so far, it's only been in the case of Drone Deployment onto Impaling Structures, but it should apply the same way.
