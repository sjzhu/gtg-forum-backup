I think Ronway's right, but that's my interpretation - it'll be good to get an official answer on this one.

Unity can use Go Bots to allow a bot to use an action. By definition, the common actions (Move, et al) are limited, so the bot can't do one of those more than once. 

What is sometimes missed is that Swift Bot can use it's attack action which allows it to move and then execute an attack, which can look like it just moved, again. That might be part of what folks are seeing.

Christopher and I had a big discussion about this back in January at or right after South. The conclusion we came to at that time is that, since Go Bots is Unlimited, you can have bots take the same action multiple times. Our reasoning is that:

1) It is simpler to explain. "The action is Unlimited, so you can do the same thing multiple times." is much simpler than "The 'Go Bots!' action is Unlimited, so you can do it multiple times, but since each Bot's actions are not Unlimited, you can only have a specific bot do a given action once per turn."

2) It means that, if you are doing it wrong and learn the right rule, your character is now more awesome, not less awesome. The experience "I enjoy playing this character and just discovered that it is more awesome than I thought" is much better than "I enjoy playing this character and just discovered that it is less awesome than I thought"

3) It is not overpowered. You can do some cool stuff with it (like use Champion Bot + Raptor Bot for a giant attack), but bots are fragile and that situation is easy to disrupt for a careful opponent.
