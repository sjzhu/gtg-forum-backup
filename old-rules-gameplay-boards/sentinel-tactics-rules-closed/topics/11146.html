So let's say you're using Grenade Launcher (automiss 1,2) against Ra and Legacy. You have an Aim token and 3 +1 attack tokens. Before the attack roll, you say you're aiming at Ra. You then roll 1,3,5,6. Now you can spend +1 attack tokens. You decide to spend 2 at Ra and 1 at Legacy. Against Ra you get 2,4 and against Legacy you get 3. Then you remove Automisses.

Ra has to defend against 3,4,5,6 aimed, so effectively 6,6,6,6. Legacy has to defend against 3,3,5,6. (Note that this example ignored the whole range part of calculating, since it wasn't in question.) Generally for bookkeeping aimed multi-target attacks, instead of physically flipping the dice, we put the aim token with the dice to remind us to treat those dice as the highest die result.
