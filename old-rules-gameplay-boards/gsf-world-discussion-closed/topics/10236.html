One thing I really have been digging is the interplay between the Opposition forces and the SF races that they correlate to, which got me thinking about which sectors would bring out the most flavor of our maches.

Hopefully I'll be writing more indepth faqs on the Strike Force and Oppositions before KS orders start shipping (and the Pax Demo write-up which is in the works), but for instance, did you know?:

The Sulf Orcs are incredible exterminators for the Technovores. Both Claw and Warbeast can both function at a high level with very little tech installed, and when playing with Elite Mode activated (really the best way to play imo!) the SF will be running on fumes for the majority of the game. Hastily Welded Turrets, because they recycle instead of scrapping, are great choices for the Hive-Master to destroy as they wont go under his card. Claw can Snipe off Technovore Swarms to buy you time and neutralize sectors you are not primarily operating in.
