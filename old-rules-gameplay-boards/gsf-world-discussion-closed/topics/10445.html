Just received the box and my global impression is rather mixed.

 

Manual and counters looks good, plenty of space to store the cards.

I have serious concern about the cards. 

1- There is a lot of wasted space with extra large border wich make the image looks tiny, meaning the cards will be harder to recognize.

2- The font used is VERY unsatisfying : it is far too thin and small to be easily readable from about a foot away, definitely not from across the table. The narrow font tends to blur with the card background. And given the large empty space on the cards you could have used a police at least 2pts bigger.

3- First card I look, first line and I find a typo :"Accelerated Deilvery", that dont make for a good first impression.

The minis are nice but look very fragile and the dragon is disapointing compared to what was posted during the KS campaign. I think I will use the tokens, they are more distinguishable.

The expansion is a big box for very small content. Doesnt really justify the cost of the heavy cardboard box, could have been sold in a plastic wrapper.

I will try a game tonight to see if things get better playing it as that is the most important thing, but this is a uphill battle due to thoses box opening concern.
